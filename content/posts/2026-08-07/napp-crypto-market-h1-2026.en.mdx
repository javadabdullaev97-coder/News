The National Agency for Perspective Projects (NAPP) [published](https://t.me/napp_uz/2355) data on Uzbekistan's crypto asset market as of July 1, 2026: 21 licensed providers operate in the country, with 702,532 registered clients between them.

## Residents and non-residents

Of the 702,532 clients registered by providers, [697,873 are residents of Uzbekistan](https://t.me/napp_uz/2355), and 4,659 are non-residents. Non-residents account for less than one percent of the client base.

NAPP does not provide comparisons with previous periods in this release — the publication is limited to the snapshot as of July 1.

## How licensing works

NAPP serves as the regulator and licensing authority for the entire crypto asset industry in the country. The agency [issues licenses](https://napp.uz/ru/pages/service-providers) in four categories — crypto exchange, crypto depository, crypto store and mining pool. Applications are reviewed within up to 20 business days, licenses are issued indefinitely, and applicants are required to host their trading platform on servers located in Uzbekistan.

Companies [listed in the agency's license register](https://napp.uz/ru/pages/erlsp) include COINPAY, ASTERIUM and PAYNET CRYPTO. At the time of publication, the licenses of COINPAY and ASTERIUM are active, while both PAYNET CRYPTO licenses are suspended (the suspension period runs from June 1 to August 1, 2026). NAPP periodically suspends or revokes the licenses of individual providers; the current status of each company is available in the agency's public register on its website.

## Tax benefit for a mining zone in Karakalpakstan

In a separate message published in the same period, NAPP [clarified](https://t.me/napp_uz/2354) the terms for miners: residents of the special mining zone "Besqala Mining Valley" in the Republic of Karakalpakstan are exempt from fees for crypto asset circulation activities until January 1, 2035. The changes were made to the regulation on the procedure for establishing, paying and distributing fees for activities in this sphere.

The agency did not publish data on crypto asset trading volumes for the first half of 2026 or a breakdown of the 21 providers by license type (exchanges, depositories, stores, mining pools) in this release.
