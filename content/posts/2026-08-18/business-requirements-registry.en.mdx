President Shavkat Mirziyoyev on August 17 signed law [ZRU-1168](https://t.me/huquqiyaxborot/20269), which elevates to law the ban on penalizing businesses for requirements not entered in the unified registry — a ban that previously existed only at the level of a Cabinet of Ministers resolution.

The full text of the document was [published](https://t.me/huquqiyaxborot/20269) by the Ministry of Justice's Telegram channel Huquqiy Axborot. It also states that the law introduces amendments and additions to a number of legislative acts of Uzbekistan.

## What changes

The Ministry of Justice operates the unified registry of mandatory requirements for entrepreneurship. Government agencies are required to enter into the registry all requirements they impose on businesses.

If a requirement is not entered in the registry, an entrepreneur cannot be held liable for failing to meet it — this is explicitly stated in the [text of the law](https://t.me/huquqiyaxborot/20269).

A similar rule had already existed in Uzbekistan. In November 2022, the president ordered the creation of a unified system for tracking requirements imposed on businesses. The Cabinet of Ministers carried out the order with [resolution No. 385](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/6572266) dated August 14, 2023. The registry itself appeared at that time, and the Ministry of Justice became its operator — but at the level of a subordinate legal act, not a law.

The new law entrenches the same ban with an act of higher legal force. A Cabinet of Ministers resolution can be changed by a government decision; a law can only be changed by a new law.

## Who it affects

The changes affect all business entities in Uzbekistan and the government agencies that impose requirements on them.

The law also introduces a new obligation for government agencies. Draft regulations that introduce new obligations, bans or liability measures for businesses must be discussed directly with entrepreneurs and their associations. This provision is [set out in the law](https://t.me/huquqiyaxborot/20269).

## When it takes effect

The law takes effect three months after official publication, [the document states](https://t.me/huquqiyaxborot/20269). The exact publication date had not been named at the time this article was published.
