The Cabinet of Ministers of Uzbekistan has adopted Resolution No. 234 of May 11, 2026, on a new procedure for state environmental review — the document takes effect on August 14 and moves the process to an electronic format.

## What's Changing in the Procedure

The resolution, ["On Measures to Introduce New Mechanisms for Environmental Impact Assessment,"](https://lex.uz/docs/8193120) moves state environmental review to the Unified Interactive Public Services Portal (my.gov.uz) — applicants submit documents and receive findings electronically. This was [reported](https://t.me/ecogovuz/49459) on the Telegram channel of the National Committee for Ecology and Climate Change of the Republic of Uzbekistan (Ecology Committee).

The resolution reduces the number of facility categories by degree of environmental impact from four to three — facilities have been reclassified by type of economic activity, according to the [Ecology Committee's statement](https://gov.uz/ru/eco/news/view/200812).

The assessment introduces the concept of "cumulative impact" for the first time: experts must account not only for the effect of the project itself but also for its combined impact with existing and planned facilities in the same area. The requirement to apply "best available technologies" in project design is also formally established.

## Who the New Procedure Affects

State environmental review applies to developers, industrial enterprises and investors whose projects fall under the list of activities subject to mandatory review — the list is given in an annex to [Resolution No. 234](https://lex.uz/docs/8193120). The exact review timeframes in days and the fee amounts for each of the three categories are set out in the annexes to the document on lex.uz.

The new procedure replaces Resolution No. 541, in effect since 2020, "On Further Improving the Environmental Impact Assessment Mechanism" — the [previous act](https://lex.uz/docs/8193120) has been declared void as of the date Resolution No. 234 takes effect.

## New Requirements for Experts and Designers

The resolution introduces qualification requirements for environmental experts: a higher education degree, at least three years of work experience, and mandatory certification. Certificates are issued by the Ecology Committee, according to the [agency's statement](https://gov.uz/ru/eco/news/view/200812).

Design organizations that prepare environmental impact assessment materials must have at least two certified specialists on staff — a rating system is being introduced for such organizations. Mechanisms for public review and public hearings involving citizens and non-governmental organizations are being expanded. A separate institution for strategic environmental assessment is also being created — it will apply to government programs and territorial development plans, not just individual projects, the [Ecology Committee notes](https://gov.uz/ru/eco/news/view/200812).

## When It Takes Effect

Resolution No. 234 was adopted on May 11, 2026, and, according to the [text of the document](https://lex.uz/docs/8193120), takes effect three months after official publication — on August 14, 2026. Until that date, the procedure established by Resolution No. 541 of September 7, 2020, remains in force.
