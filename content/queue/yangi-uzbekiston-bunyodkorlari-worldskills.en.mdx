Tashkent [awarded](https://t.me/eduuz/50271) 120 winners and runners-up of the first National "Creators of New Uzbekistan" Contest at Humo Arena on August 8 — they will join the national team preparing for WorldSkills International.

## How the Contest Was Held

The contest, established by [Cabinet of Ministers resolution No. 152](https://lex.uz/uz/docs/-8126334) of April 8, 2026, was held for the first time in three stages — district/city, regional and national. It was open to college students, young people, university students and unemployed citizens, as well as foreigners residing in Uzbekistan, aged 16 to 30. Participants were assessed in 10 trades based on WorldSkills standards.

More than 124,000 young people took part in the first stage, according to [data](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9490) from the president's congratulatory message. The regional stage had about 7,000 participants, including 3,500 self-employed young people. Following all stages, 22,000 participants received employment and education opportunities. Of them, 8,900 people received job placements, 4,470 received recommendations for admission to colleges, and another 8,340 were referred to international selection contests for employment with foreign employers.

At the national stage, the jury selected 120 winners and runners-up in ten categories, who received gold, silver and bronze medals.

## President's Congratulations and Link to WorldSkills

The [president's website](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9490) published Shavkat Mirziyoyev's congratulatory message to the contest winners. The publication states that the 120 winners and runners-up of the national stage will join Uzbekistan's national team, which will prepare to compete in the WorldSkills International professional skills championship.

The Ministry of Higher Education, Science and Innovation also [reported](https://t.me/eduuz/50271) on the ceremony at Humo Arena.
