Rasul Jurayev, a patrol police officer with the Yakkasaray District Internal Affairs Department in Tashkent, saved a 13-year-old boy who was drowning in the Burijar canal, the [Telegram channel of the district hokimiyat](https://t.me/yakkasaroy_hokimiyati/100066) reported on August 6.

## How the Rescue Happened

According to the hokimiyat, the boy began drowning while swimming in the Burijar canal. Inspector Jurayev noticed what was happening and jumped into the water. Local resident Zukhriddin Eshkuvvatov, who was also in the water near the teenager, helped with the rescue. Together, the two pulled the child to shore.

The hokimiyat did not report on the boy's condition. The rescue was captured on video, which the official Telegram channel of the Main Internal Affairs Directorate (GUVD) of Tashkent city [published](https://t.me/iibbuz/48326).

The agencies did not name the teenager — LEAP News also does not disclose the identities of minors affected by incidents.

## Award Ceremony

The inspector and his helper were honored at a formation ceremony at the Yakkasaray District Internal Affairs Department. The ceremony was attended by the Head of the Main Internal Affairs Directorate of Tashkent city, Ravshan Sultankhodjaev, along with Yakkasaray District hokim Nilufar Allabergenova. Jurayev and Eshkuvvatov were presented with letters of commendation, a cash reward, and memorial gifts — the hokimiyat's statement did not specify the amount.
