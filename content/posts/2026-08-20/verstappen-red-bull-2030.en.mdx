Max Verstappen extended his contract with Red Bull Racing through the end of the 2030 season on August 20 — the team [announced](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/verstappen-signs-contract-extension-with-red-bull-until-the-end-of-2030.4gUd8JEkYnKmD6bNMS5syw) the deal a day before the Dutch Grand Prix in Zandvoort, the driver's home race.

The previous agreement ran through the end of 2028 — the new contract extends the partnership by two more seasons.

Red Bull Racing team principal Laurent Mekies called keeping Verstappen [“fantastic news”](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/verstappen-signs-contract-extension-with-red-bull-until-the-end-of-2030.4gUd8JEkYnKmD6bNMS5syw). The driver's impact on the team and on Red Bull's history in motorsport "has been transformative," the team said in a statement.

According to [Sky Sports](https://www.skysports.com/f1/news/12433/13574907/max-verstappen-red-bull-driver-signs-new-contract-extension-until-2030-formula-1-season-ahead-of-dutch-grand-prix), the contract included a clause allowing Verstappen to move to another team starting in 2027. The clause would have taken effect if, by the summer break — the mid-season pause between race weekends — he was not among the top two drivers in the championship standings by points. Verstappen did not exercise that right. Earlier this year his management held talks with McLaren about a move for the 2027 season, the outlet reports.

Verstappen attributed the decision to his attachment to the team, where his entire Formula 1 career has unfolded. "The team is like a second family to me, and I feel at home here," he [said](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/verstappen-signs-contract-extension-with-red-bull-until-the-end-of-2030.4gUd8JEkYnKmD6bNMS5syw).

In a separate comment, the driver added: "I don't need to drive in another colour" — a reference to Red Bull's signature blue livery. He said that even while the team's co-owner Dietrich Mateschitz was alive, he had told him he wanted to finish his career at this team. "I never felt the desire or the need to change teams just for the sake of change," Verstappen [said](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/i-dont-need-to-drive-in-another-colour-verstappen-explains-why-he-chose-to-stay-with-family-at-red-bull.4z0k66NtGxU2V8WlKEEg7N).

Over his career at Red Bull, Verstappen has won 71 races and four championship titles, [Formula1.com notes](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/verstappen-signs-contract-extension-with-red-bull-until-the-end-of-2030.4gUd8JEkYnKmD6bNMS5syw).
