Crypto exchange Kraken announced on August 18, 2026, that it launched the Krak debit card in the US with up to 2% cashback — in dollars or bitcoin — issued by Lead Bank under a Visa license.

The card is available in almost every state: Kraken is not launching it in New York, Maine, Massachusetts and Indiana, [the card's page states](https://krak.app/card). Users pay for purchases from more than 600 crypto and fiat balances — the system converts the needed asset into dollars at the moment of payment. The order in which balances are drawn down can be customized, and a single purchase can be split across several sources, [CNBC reported](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/18/kraken-launches-debit-card-in-bid-for-consumer-wallets.html).

The maximum 2% cashback and the metal version of the card are available to clients who hold an average of $50,000 across their Krak, Kraken and Kraken Pro accounts. The card has no monthly or annual fee, but a variable spread applies when converting assets, along with third-party ATM fees, [according to Krak's website](https://krak.app/card).

## The US — a new market for the Krak card

Krak — Kraken's consumer app — started with more modest cashback in the UK and EU markets, where it launched a debit card earlier. In March 2026, the company added a metal card with up to 2% cashback for clients with large balances, [Kraken's blog reported](https://blog.kraken.com/product/krak/metal-debit-card). In July 2026, a Mastercard-network card with the same rate came out, [Kraken's blog reported](https://blog.kraken.com/product/kraken-card/introducing-the-kraken-card). The US launch is the first outside the UK and EU — there, the card runs on the Visa network rather than Mastercard.

A survey of more than 2,000 adult Americans, commissioned by Krak itself, found that 63% believe they are falling behind on personal finances. Another 60% are ready to switch to a card that offers tangible rewards without debt, [CNBC reported](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/18/kraken-launches-debit-card-in-bid-for-consumer-wallets.html), citing the company.

Payward — Kraken's parent company — co-CEO Arjun Sethi told CNBC: "People have stopped trusting how the financial system treats them — the rates they're paid, the fees they're charged, and how rewards programs actually work."

## The card is part of Kraken's banking strategy

The US card is issued by third-party Lead Bank. Kraken itself also holds bank status: several years ago, Wyoming granted the company a special purpose depository institution (SPDI) charter. On March 4, 2026, subsidiary Kraken Financial became the first crypto company to gain direct access to the payment system of the US Federal Reserve, [the company's blog reported](https://blog.kraken.com/news/federal-reserve-master-account).

Issuing the card through Lead Bank is not directly tied to that status. But both facts fit into the same Kraken strategy — offering the market infrastructure for everyday payments, not just a platform for trading cryptocurrency.

CNBC names Block's Cash App, PayPal's Venmo, as well as SoFi, Robinhood, Chime and Coinbase as direct competitors to the Krak card.
