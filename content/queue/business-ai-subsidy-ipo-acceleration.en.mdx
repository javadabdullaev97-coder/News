President Shavkat Mirziyoyev on August 18, at the sixth open dialogue with entrepreneurs, announced the «AI-hamkor» program and an annual selection of 50 companies to prepare for entering the capital market.

The state will compensate businesses for half of the costs of adopting artificial intelligence under the «10,000 Enterprises — AI-hamkor» program, [the Ministry of Digital Technologies said](https://t.me/mitcuz/24176) in a statement. At least $100 million will go toward the program's first stage, which includes creating an open platform of ready-made AI solutions for businesses, [the president's press secretary's Telegram channel said](https://t.me/Press_Secretary_Uz/7717).

Companies developing their own AI models for new products will get free access to the first supercomputing cluster of the Digital Government Project Management Center. Related research costs will be covered from the budget. Next year, the cluster's capacity will [triple](https://t.me/mitcuz/24176).

The program is backed by statistics on companies that have already adopted modern management and artificial intelligence. Demand for products sharply increased at 54% of them, and production costs fell at 25%, [the president's press secretary's channel said](https://t.me/Press_Secretary_Uz/7717). At 40% of companies, revenue growth allowed raising employee salaries by more than 10%.

## Selection of 50 Companies for IPO Preparation

Every year, the state will select 50 private companies with revenue above 1 trillion soums to enter the capital market through an acceleration program, [the president's press secretary's channel said](https://t.me/Press_Secretary_Uz/7718). Revenue of 1 trillion soums is about $84.3 million at the central bank's exchange rate [as of August 18](https://cbu.uz/ru/arkhiv-kursov-valyut/).

The state will compensate companies for half of the costs of IPO preparation and transitioning their reporting to international standards. Authorities expect the program to allow private businesses to attract at least $1 billion a year from abroad.
