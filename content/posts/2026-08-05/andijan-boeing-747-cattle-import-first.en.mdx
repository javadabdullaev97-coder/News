Andijan International Airport on August 4 received its first cargo Boeing 747-400F operated by Challenge Air Cargo — the aircraft delivered 160 head of pedigree cattle from Germany, flying in from Belgium. State-owned airport operator Uzbekistan Airports [reported](https://t.me/uzbairportsuz/9276) the flight.

## What was delivered

The flight departed from Liège Airport in Belgium and landed in Andijan at 03:13 local time, [according to](https://t.me/uzbairportsuz/9276) the Uzbekistan Airports statement. On board were 160 head of Simmental and Holstein breed cattle with a combined weight of 83.3 tonnes.

Unloading the animals, veterinary inspection and loading onto specialized vehicles were carried out, according to Uzbekistan Airports, "in strict accordance with applicable veterinary and biosafety requirements." Specialists from Uzbekistan Airports and Andijan International Airport handled the receiving and processing of the cargo.

The Boeing 747-400F is a wide-body cargo aircraft, one of the largest types used to transport live cattle. Before this flight, Andijan Airport had not received such aircraft carrying live cattle.

## Why this became possible

Andijan Airport was closed for several years for a large-scale reconstruction. President Shavkat Mirziyoyev reviewed the progress of the work on April 15, 2025 — at the time it was [reported](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/8055) that a new runway had been built, 3,300 meters long and 60 meters wide, equipped to ICAO CAT-II standard. The runway allows for large Boeing and Airbus aircraft, including night flights and flights in difficult weather conditions.

According to plans announced during the visit, after the reconstruction is completed the airport's capacity should grow from 584 to 3,700 flights per year.

"The successful receipt of a Boeing 747-400F cargo aircraft confirms the growing capacity of Andijan International Airport to handle oversized and special cargo," [the Uzbekistan Airports statement said](https://t.me/uzbairportsuz/9276).
