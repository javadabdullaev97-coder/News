Starting September 1, 2026, passengers on city buses operating under the gross-contract system — currently in use in Tashkent — will be required to pay their fare at the moment of boarding, under a rule introduced by Cabinet of Ministers Resolution No. 276 of June 3.

## How the New Rule Will Work

Resolution [No. 276](https://lex.uz/ru/pdfs/8244019) itself has been registered and, according to its record card on lex.uz, took effect on June 4, 2026. The provision on payment at boarding — a separate subclause of its appendix — takes effect later: a note to the document states explicitly that it enters into force on September 1, 2026.

The resolution amends the Rules for the Carriage of Passengers and Baggage by Road Transport, approved by Cabinet resolution No. 482 of November 4, 2003. The new wording of clause 28 of these rules applies to regular city bus routes organized under the gross (bruto) contract system. Such buses are equipped with automated payment terminals — validators. This is exactly the scheme operating in Tashkent's bus network: LEAP News [reported](https://leap.uz/en/2026/08/08/tashkent-bus-fare-evaders-80k-fined-aug2026) on fine statistics for fare evasion on the capital's buses.

When boarding a bus equipped with a validator, a passenger must pay for or confirm the fare using one of the following methods:

- tap a transport card on the validator and complete validation;
- pay with a bank card, smartphone or another contactless method — NFC, QR code, biometrics, BLE, UWB and other technologies being rolled out;
- tap a social or transport card on the validator to confirm eligibility for a discounted or free ride.

If payment or confirmation of a benefit is not completed at the moment of boarding, the ride is considered fare evasion — this is stated explicitly [in the resolution](https://lex.uz/ru/pdfs/8244019).

In a separate clause, the resolution also classifies as city bus routes those extending beyond city limits by up to 5 kilometers, or up to 12 kilometers where general plans for transport infrastructure development apply.

## Fine Stays the Same, a Tougher One Is in the Works

Resolution No. 276 does not introduce a new fine specifically for failing to pay at boarding. The new clause 28¹ of the Carriage Rules refers to "administrative liability under the established procedure" — that is, to the rule already in force. Fare evasion is currently punishable under part 5 of Article 144 of the Code of Administrative Liability of the Republic of Uzbekistan by a fine of 1/10 of the base calculation value — 41,200 soums (about $3.4 at the Central Bank's exchange rate on July 30). Under this article, the Ministry of Transport has already [drawn up](https://t.me/Mintrans_uz/28419) almost 80,000 citations against fare-evading passengers since the start of 2026.

At the same time, the Cabinet has separately instructed the Ministry of Transport, together with the Ministry of Internal Affairs, to draft within three months a regulatory act toughening administrative liability for fare evasion on trolleybuses, trams and city buses, and to submit it for government review. In other words, a tougher fine specifically for the new boarding-payment rule effective September 1 has not yet been adopted, [the document shows](https://lex.uz/ru/pdfs/8244019).

## What Else Is Changing

The Cabinet has instructed the Ministry of Economy and Finance, Uzbekiston Temir Yo'llari and the operator of the automated payment system to implement, by September 1, 2026, an integrated fare payment system for public transport in the capital and for suburban rail service. The document provides for unified "electric train–bus" and "electric train–metro" tariff plans, [the resolution states](https://lex.uz/ru/pdfs/8244019).

In addition, the document zeroes out, through December 31, 2027, the recycling fee rate on imports of new buses, electric buses and trolleybuses of category M3 (60 or more seats, including the driver) with an environmental class of at least Euro-5 — this measure concerns vehicle procurement, not passenger fares.

## What's Next

The hokimiyats of Tashkent, the regions and the Republic of Karakalpakstan, together with the National Television and Radio Company and the National News Agency, are required under the resolution to ensure broad public information about the goals and procedure of the new fare payment scheme. The Ministry of Transport and the Ministry of Internal Affairs must submit to the Cabinet a draft on tougher administrative liability for fare evasion. Oversight of the resolution's implementation has been assigned to Deputy Prime Minister A. Zh. Ramatov and Minister of Transport I. R. Makhkamov.
