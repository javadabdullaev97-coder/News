The unified public services portal my.gov.uz [reported](https://t.me/MyGovUz/3974) on August 8 that since the start of 2026, the most requested service for drivers has been issuing an electronic power of attorney to operate a vehicle — used 1.04 million times.

## Top 5 Services for Drivers

The portal published an infographic showing the five most popular driver-related services by number of applications filed:

1. **Electronic power of attorney to operate a vehicle** — [1.04 million applications](https://t.me/MyGovUz/3974).
2. **Permit for window tinting** — 898,000 applications.
3. **Transfer of liability for traffic violations to the actual driver** — 412,000 applications.
4. **Environmental stickers for vehicles** — 251,000 applications.
5. **Issuance of a driver's license** — 33,000 applications.

The portal recorded the highest number of applications for driver services in January (580,000), April (450,000) and June (352,000) 2026, [according to the infographic](https://t.me/MyGovUz/3974).

## How to Get a Power of Attorney and What It Costs

The service "Issuance of an electronic power of attorney to individuals for operating a vehicle (without the right to sell or take it out of the country)" is provided by the Ministry of Justice through the [my.gov.uz portal](https://my.gov.uz/ru/service/640) under Resolution No. 613 of the Cabinet of Ministers of the Republic of Uzbekistan dated November 20, 2023, [according to the service page](https://gov.uz/ru/advice/72/document/2774).

To obtain the power of attorney, users must log in to the my.gov.uz portal, submit an application and pay the issued invoice — the document is generated automatically, and processing takes about 7 minutes, [my.gov.uz states](https://my.gov.uz/ru/service/640). No in-person visit to a notary is required — the power of attorney is issued electronically and carries a QR code.

The state fee depends on the validity period of the power of attorney:

- up to 1 month — 74,160 soums (~$6 at the Central Bank exchange rate as of August 7);
- 1 to 6 months — 370,800 soums (~$31);
- 6 months to 3 years — 741,600 soums (~$62).

The conversion is based on data from the Central Bank of Uzbekistan — 11,915.64 soums per dollar as of August 7, 2026.

## What the Statistics Show

The five services in the my.gov.uz infographic cover the full cycle of car ownership — from transferring driving rights and legalizing window tinting to reassigning camera-recorded traffic violation fines to the actual driver. The portal did not provide data for previous years, so it is not yet possible to compare the growth in demand for the power of attorney with past periods based on the published materials.
