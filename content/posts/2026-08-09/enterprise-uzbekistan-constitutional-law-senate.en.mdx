The Senate of the Oliy Majlis on August 8 approved the constitutional law "On the International Digital Technologies Center," giving the Enterprise Uzbekistan center full legal grounds to operate, the IT-Park Uzbekistan Telegram channel [reported](https://t.me/itpark_uz/20592). The next step is the president's signature.

## What the Law Changes

The law establishes a separate legal regime for the center — based on elements of the common law of England and Wales — with simplified registration, licensing and business procedures aligned with international standards, IT-Park Uzbekistan [said](https://t.me/itpark_uz/20592) in its statement. Once the president signs the document, Enterprise Uzbekistan will be able to operate fully as a platform for international technology companies.

Enterprise Uzbekistan was established by [presidential decree UP-233](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/7865800) on November 26, 2025, as a separate territory (jurisdiction) with a special legal regime operating on the IT-Park site. The center's governing council is chaired by the president.

## English Law, an Independent Court and Incentives Until 2100

Within the center's boundaries, the norms and principles of the common law of England and Wales will apply directly — modeled on the standards of leading international financial centers, the presidential decree [states](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/7865800). Residents will be able to resolve commercial disputes with each other and with other businesses through the center's independent specialized court, bypassing Uzbekistan's courts of general jurisdiction. This is possible with the mutual consent of the parties, IT-Park [notes](http://www.it-park.uz/en/itpark/news/uzbekistan-launched-the-international-enterprise-uzbekistan-center-english-law-innovation-and-a-world-class-ecosystem).

A one-stop shop is being created for residents, with a separate tax office operating in English. The center's special legal regime will remain in effect until 2100 and provides tax and customs incentives for residents, IT-Park [reports](https://www.it-park.uz/ru/itpark/news/v-uzbekistane-zapustyat-mezhdunarodnyy-centr-cifrovyh-tehnologiy-s-lgotami-dlya-kompaniy). In particular, residents will benefit from simplified import of technology, equipment and materials. Even at the first-reading stage, the bill provided for a flexible tax and customs regime and protection of investor and intellectual property rights, the UzA agency [noted](https://uza.uz/uz/posts/raqamli-texnologiyalar-xalqaro-markazi-tashkil-etiladi_847435) in April.

Center residents are permitted to engage in software development, IT consulting and outsourcing, scientific research, video game development, digital content hosting, work with artificial intelligence technologies, cloud services and educational programs in digital technology.

## What's Next

The law has been sent to President Shavkat Mirziyoyev for signature — the special legal regime will not take effect until he signs it and it is officially published on lex.uz. IT-Park has not yet named a signing date.
