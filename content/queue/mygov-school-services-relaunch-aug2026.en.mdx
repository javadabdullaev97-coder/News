The government services portal My.Gov.Uz resumed accepting online applications for three school-related services for parents and students on August 1, ahead of the new school year, [the portal said](https://t.me/MyGovUz/3963) on its Telegram channel.

## Which services are now available

The post lists three electronic services that are again available on my.gov.uz:

- [transferring a student from one public school to another](https://my.gov.uz/uz/service/660);
- [enrolling foreign nationals' and stateless children in public schools](https://my.gov.uz/uz/service/665);
- [enrolling children of Uzbek citizens who received their education abroad in public schools](https://my.gov.uz/uz/service/1276).

All three services are provided by the [Ministry of Preschool and School Education of the Republic of Uzbekistan](https://my.gov.uz/uz/service/660) — it is listed as the implementing agency in the cards of all three services on the portal.

## How to apply and what documents are required

To [transfer a child to another school](https://my.gov.uz/uz/service/660), no additional documents are needed — the applicant enters the child's birth certificate data into the form. The service is free, the review period is 3 business days, and filling out the application itself takes about 5 minutes.

To [enroll children of foreign nationals](https://my.gov.uz/uz/service/665), applicants must attach a copy of the child's grade and exam records, a notarized translation into the state language, and a health certificate for the child. If the documents are submitted by a representative, proof of their authority is required. The service is free, and processing takes 7 business days. The service's legal basis is [Cabinet of Ministers resolution No. 295 dated June 1, 2022](https://my.gov.uz/uz/service/665), on improving public services in the general education system.

To [enroll children of Uzbek citizens who finished school abroad](https://my.gov.uz/uz/service/1276), applicants need the child's foreign education certificate and its notarized translation. For grades 10–11, confirmation that the foreign certificate is recognized in Uzbekistan is additionally required. If no education documents exist, a decision by the district commission for minors' affairs on compulsory enrollment of the child is required. The service is free, and processing takes 3 business days.

All three applications can be submitted without visiting a school or education department: the applicant logs into my.gov.uz with their account and tracks the application status in their personal account.

## Why this matters now

The new school year in Uzbekistan traditionally begins on September 1. My.Gov.Uz's post ties the relaunch of the three services to that date — the portal reminds parents that questions about transferring or enrolling children in school can be resolved online in advance, without leaving home.
