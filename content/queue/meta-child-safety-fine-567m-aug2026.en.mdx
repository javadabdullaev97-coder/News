New Mexico state judge Bryan Biedscheid on August 7 ordered Meta Platforms to pay [$567 million](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/07/new-mexico-court-orders-meta-to-pay-additional-567m-in-child-safety-case/) into a fund to address harm caused to children by the Facebook and Instagram social networks.

## Second Phase of the Trial

The ruling is the outcome of the second, "remedial" phase of the trial brought by the New Mexico Attorney General's Office against Meta. The judge [found the company to be a source of "public nuisance"](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/7/new-mexico-court-orders-meta-to-pay-567m-over-harm-to-youths) under state law: the platforms created a risk of child sexual exploitation, interfered with minors' education and harmed their mental health by fostering addictive use.

Of the [$567 million](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/7/new-mexico-court-orders-meta-to-pay-567m-over-harm-to-youths) awarded, $420 million will go toward treatment services for youth harmed by social media use; the remainder will fund public awareness campaigns and screening services over the next five years.

The judge also ordered Meta to introduce safety measures for underage users: limit teenagers' monthly usage time on Facebook and Instagram and tighten controls over notifications.

## First Phase and Total Amount

In March 2026, a jury [awarded](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/07/new-mexico-court-orders-meta-to-pay-additional-567m-in-child-safety-case/) $375 million in civil penalties in the same case. With the August 7 ruling, the case's total has grown to $942 million.

New Mexico Attorney General Raúl Torrez said Meta knew about the harm its platforms were causing to the state's children but chose user engagement and profit over their safety.

Meta disagrees with the ruling. Company spokesperson Andy Stone said Meta is "confident in its work to protect teens online," and that the company intends to appeal the decision.

## What's Next

Meta has announced it will appeal the ruling. A date for the appeal hearing has not yet been set.
