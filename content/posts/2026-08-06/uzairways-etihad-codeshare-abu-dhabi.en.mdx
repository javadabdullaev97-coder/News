Etihad Airways and Uzbekistan Airways will launch a codeshare flight on the Abu Dhabi–Tashkent–Abu Dhabi route starting August 9, 2026: Etihad operates the flight on an Airbus A320, while Uzbekistan Airways sells tickets under the codeshare agreement.

## Who Operates the Flight, and How

The codeshare agreement between the two airlines took effect on May 15, 2026, and the first codeshare tickets became available for travel starting August 9, [Etihad Airways said](https://www.etihad.com/en/news/etihad-opens-the-silk-road-with-new-uzbekistan-airways-codeshare) in a press release. The [Uzbekistan Airways Telegram channel](https://t.me/uzbekistanairways/8767) confirmed the same start date: "Direct flights on the Abu Dhabi – Tashkent – Abu Dhabi route will begin on August 9, 2026."

Flight EY830 departs Abu Dhabi at 21:10 and arrives in Tashkent at 01:35 local time the following day. The return flight EY831 leaves Tashkent at 03:50 and lands in Abu Dhabi at 06:35. Both flights operate daily on an Airbus A320, [according to Etihad's statement](https://www.etihad.com/en/news/etihad-opens-the-silk-road-with-new-uzbekistan-airways-codeshare).

## What Passengers Get

Under a single ticket, Etihad passengers will be able to fly from Tashkent to eight more cities in Uzbekistan — Samarkand, Urgench, Nukus, Termez, Fergana, Namangan, Andijan and Bukhara. Uzbekistan Airways operates these domestic flights, [Etihad's press release said](https://www.etihad.com/en/news/etihad-opens-the-silk-road-with-new-uzbekistan-airways-codeshare).

The [Uzbekistan Airways Telegram channel](https://t.me/uzbekistanairways/8767) adds that the single ticket gives "freedom of movement across the Emirates" and allows flexible trip planning, while UzAirPlus program members will earn premium points for flights on the new routes between Tashkent and Abu Dhabi. The airlines also said they are working on a partnership between the Etihad Guest and UzAirPlus loyalty programs — no details are available yet. Neither airline has announced ticket prices for the codeshare route.

## What the Airlines Say

Etihad Airways Chief Revenue and Commercial Officer Arik De said the agreement gives passengers "easy access to eight Uzbek cities on a single ticket with seamless service" to Abu Dhabi, [the press release said](https://www.etihad.com/en/news/etihad-opens-the-silk-road-with-new-uzbekistan-airways-codeshare).

Uzbekistan Airways Deputy Chairman of the Management Board for Commerce and Tourism Shukhrat Yadgarov called the partnership with Etihad "another strategic step in expanding the carrier's international network," [the same statement said](https://www.etihad.com/en/news/etihad-opens-the-silk-road-with-new-uzbekistan-airways-codeshare).

Tickets are already available for purchase on the official website uzairways.com and in the Uzbekistan Airways mobile app, [according to the airline's Telegram channel](https://t.me/uzbekistanairways/8767).
