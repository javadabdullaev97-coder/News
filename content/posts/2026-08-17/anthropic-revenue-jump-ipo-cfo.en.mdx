Revenue at Anthropic, the developer of the Claude chatbot, jumped 14-fold year over year to $11.5 billion in the second quarter of 2026, according to investor documents, [CNBC reports](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/15/anthropic-revenue-jumps-to-over-11point5-billion-in-q2-report.html).

The company's revenue was $787 million in the second quarter of 2025 and $4.73 billion in the first quarter of 2026. [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/anthropic-touts-14-fold-jump-in-revenue-for-would-be-investors/) cites the same figures, based on the same documents. That means revenue more than doubled in three months. The data is preliminary and could change, Bloomberg notes.

Anthropic also posted positive adjusted operating profit in the second quarter — a rare result among leading large language model developers, [PYMNTS notes](https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/anthropic-touts-14-fold-jump-in-revenue-for-would-be-investors/). In May 2026, the company said its quarterly revenue run rate had exceeded $47 billion, according to [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/anthropic-touts-14-fold-jump-in-revenue-for-would-be-investors/). [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/investor-pressure-forces-nvidia-to-shrink-its-openai-bet-just-as-anthropics-numbers-defy-bubble-warnings/) cites a different figure — about $45 billion.

## CFO Holds Investor Meetings but Won't Discuss Valuation

Anthropic Chief Financial Officer Krishna Rao is already holding early meetings with prospective investors ahead of a possible IPO, [CNBC reports](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/13/anthropic-cfo-early-ipo-meetings-valuation.html), citing people familiar with the matter. According to them, the conversations remain general for now — covering Claude models, the Claude Code product, and the management team. Specific financial metrics and the company's valuation have not been discussed. That approach is standard for the weeks before official pricing and underwriter selection, CNBC's sources say.

LEAP News [previously examined](https://leap.uz/en/2026/08/14/anthropic-ipo-valuation-2-trillion) investor expectations for the timing and valuation of the upcoming IPO — up to $2 trillion for a possible October listing. Anthropic has not confirmed that figure.

Investors are anticipating a possible listing in October 2026, but Anthropic has not officially announced a date or IPO price.
