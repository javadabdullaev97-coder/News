In the city of Urgench, Khorezm Region, a BYD Chazor driver deliberately rammed several cars on August 4 during a conflict with another man — the Internal Affairs Directorate of Khorezm Region [reported](https://xviib.uz/uz/news/1197) on August 7.

## What Happened, According to Police

The conflict took place between Sh.B., born in 2000, a resident of the Istiqlol mahalla (neighborhood community) in Urgench, and Kh.B., born in 1999, a resident of the Shermatlar mahalla in Urgench district, [the police statement said](https://xviib.uz/uz/news/1197). According to the department, during the mutual dispute Kh.B. deliberately rammed a black BYD Chazor into a Chevrolet Cobalt belonging to Sh.B., and also damaged two more cars belonging to other residents — a white BYD Chazor and a Damas.

All the collisions were intentional, the department noted. No one was injured — only the vehicles were damaged.

## Police Response and Status of the Inquiry

Internal affairs officers who arrived at the scene [stopped](https://xviib.uz/uz/news/1197) the BYD Chazor driver's unlawful actions. All parties to the conflict were taken to the internal affairs department. According to the Internal Affairs Directorate of Khorezm Region, a pre-investigation inquiry into the incident is being conducted by the Internal Affairs Directorate of Urgench, following which a legal assessment will be made of all participants' actions.

As of the publication of the official statement, no decision to open a criminal case had been announced. The specific article of the Criminal Code or the Code of Administrative Responsibility was not specified in the police release.

## Timeline

According to police, the incident occurred on August 4. According to the Internal Affairs Directorate, video from the scene spread on social media, and official confirmation [appeared](https://t.me/xorazm_iib/26997) three days later, on August 7.
