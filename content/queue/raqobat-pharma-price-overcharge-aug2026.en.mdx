The Competition Development and Consumer Rights Protection Committee of the Republic of Uzbekistan [reported](https://raqobat.gov.uz/nika-pharm-mchj-astor-alliance-mchj-va-evro-farm-biznes-mchjlar-tomonidan-ortiqcha-olingan-mablaglar-qajtarildi/) on August 3 that three pharmaceutical companies refunded almost 100 million soums for unjustified drug overpricing. That is roughly $8,400 at the [Central Bank of Uzbekistan exchange rate](https://cbu.uz/ru/arkhiv-kursov-valyut/json/USD/2026-08-03/) for August 3 — 11,960.18 soums per dollar.

## Which companies and drugs were inspected

According to the Committee's [publication](https://raqobat.gov.uz/nika-pharm-mchj-astor-alliance-mchj-va-evro-farm-biznes-mchjlar-tomonidan-ortiqcha-olingan-mablaglar-qajtarildi/), violations were found at three companies:

- "NIKA PHARM" LLC — "Gastromeg EZO" capsules 40 mg No. 20;
- "Astor Alliance" LLC — "Dolgit" cream 50 g and "Mezim f" tablets 10,000 IU No. 20;
- "EVRO FARM BIZNES" LLC — "Brovensin" syrup 100 ml.

Together, the three companies received about 100 million soums in unjustified income, the Committee [said](https://t.me/RaqobatGovUz/12532). The publication does not specify the exact overcharge amount for each drug.

## How the violation was detected and what the companies did

The Committee cites the unified information system "Fair Tech," which tracks markups on medicines. It also relies on data from the State Tax Committee on signs of unjustified income at the three companies. Following the inspection, the companies voluntarily corrected the violation and refunded the excess funds received, the Committee [reported](https://raqobat.gov.uz/nika-pharm-mchj-astor-alliance-mchj-va-evro-farm-biznes-mchjlar-tomonidan-ortiqcha-olingan-mablaglar-qajtarildi/). The publication does not specify to whom exactly the money was returned — directly to pharmacy customers or as an adjustment in the companies' financial statements. The Committee provides no information on fines against the three companies.

The Committee noted that the "Fair Tech" system continuously monitors drug markups, and that when violations are found, businesses are advised to correct them voluntarily.
