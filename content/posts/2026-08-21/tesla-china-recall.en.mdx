China's State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) on August 21 ordered Tesla to recall 2,975,910 vehicles across several models, including imported Model X and Model S units, [the agency said in its notice](https://www.samr.gov.cn/zw/zh/art/2026/art_4223bf2706a84189b425d58124dbac7d.html).

The color of the internal emergency mechanical handle is too similar to the interior trim, making it hard for passengers to find and open the door after a crash, [SAMR said](https://www.samr.gov.cn/zw/zh/art/2026/art_4223bf2706a84189b425d58124dbac7d.html). If a severe collision disables the vehicle's low-voltage system, this could hinder evacuation from inside and rescue efforts from outside.

The recall covers 973,156 China-built Model 3 vehicles and 1,956,713 Model Y vehicles. Another 46,041 vehicles are imported — 35,590 Model 3, 8,328 Model X and 2,123 Model S, [according to the regulator](https://www.samr.gov.cn/zw/zh/art/2026/art_4223bf2706a84189b425d58124dbac7d.html).

Tesla will apply free warning stickers to the handles and roll out an over-the-air software update for window controls, so windows automatically roll down after a crash, [the notice said](https://www.samr.gov.cn/zw/zh/art/2026/art_4223bf2706a84189b425d58124dbac7d.html). The recall takes effect September 25, 2026, for China-built cars; imported models are covered starting from the date the document was published.

This is Tesla's largest recall in China's history, [Electrek reports](https://electrek.co/2026/08/21/tesla-recall-3-million-cars-china-door-handles/). The same day, eight other Chinese manufacturers — Xiaomi, Xpeng, Leapmotor, Geely, Chery, Dongfeng, FAW and BAIC — announced recalls over a similar issue, [Nbd.com.cn reports](https://www.nbd.com.cn/articles/2026-08-21/4550416.html).

## Pressure on door design is also mounting in the US

The China recall coincides with tightening requirements for electric vehicle door handles on two continents, [Electrek notes](https://electrek.co/2026/08/21/tesla-recall-3-million-cars-china-door-handles/). In July, the US National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA) rejected a petition to declare Tesla's door handles defective, [the outlet reports](https://electrek.co/2026/08/21/tesla-recall-3-million-cars-china-door-handles/). At the same time, the agency opened a separate rulemaking proceeding that could change the standard, [Electrek writes](https://electrek.co/2026/08/21/tesla-recall-3-million-cars-china-door-handles/).

Several wrongful-death lawsuits have been filed against Tesla, with plaintiffs alleging that door handles prevented passengers from escaping after crashes, [Electrek reports](https://electrek.co/2026/08/21/tesla-recall-3-million-cars-china-door-handles/). In January, a group of members of Congress introduced the SAFE Exit Act, aimed at regulating exactly these types of handles, [the outlet writes](https://electrek.co/2026/08/21/tesla-recall-3-million-cars-china-door-handles/).

In February, Chinese regulators approved a ban on Tesla-style concealed door handles on safety grounds, [Electrek writes](https://electrek.co/2026/08/21/tesla-recall-3-million-cars-china-door-handles/).
