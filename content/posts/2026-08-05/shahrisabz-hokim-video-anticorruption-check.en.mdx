The Anti-Corruption Agency of the Republic of Uzbekistan on August 4 [submitted a formal request](https://anticorruption.uz/ru/article/ijtimoiy-tarmoqlarda-shahrisabz-tumani-hokimi-samad-hasanov-mahalliy-aholining-xonadoniga-kirib-davlat-fuqarolik-xizmatchisining-odob-axloq-qoidalariga-zid-xatti-harakatlar-qilganligi-keng-jamoatchilik-muhokamasiga-sabab-boldi) to the Kashkadarya Region hokimiyat, demanding an internal review of Shahrisabz District hokim Samad Hasanov. The reason was a video of his visit to a local resident's home that circulated on social media.

## Video Sparks Public Reaction, Agency Demands Review

A video of Samad Hasanov's visit to a home in Shahrisabz District circulated on social media in early August and sparked widespread discussion.

The Anti-Corruption Agency [stated](https://anticorruption.uz/ru/article/ijtimoiy-tarmoqlarda-shahrisabz-tumani-hokimi-samad-hasanov-mahalliy-aholining-xonadoniga-kirib-davlat-fuqarolik-xizmatchisining-odob-axloq-qoidalariga-zid-xatti-harakatlar-qilganligi-keng-jamoatchilik-muhokamasiga-sabab-boldi) that officials at any level are required to strictly observe rules of professional ethics when interacting with citizens. Violations of privacy, humiliation of citizens' honor and dignity, and unjustified administrative pressure contradict the law and ethical norms. The agency cited these violations as the grounds for its formal request for an internal review and a legal assessment of the situation.

## Shahrisabz Hokimiyat Attributes Visit to Wrong Address

The Shahrisabz District hokimiyat [said](https://t.me/shahrisabz_tuman_hokimligi/56005) in its Telegram channel that the visit to the home resulted from an error by the mahalla chairman, who mixed up two neighboring houses — according to the hokimiyat, the gates of the houses are located next to each other. The intended destination was the home of an elderly couple with disabilities next door. There, the hokimiyat claims, everything was in exemplary order: the house had been renovated with state support, and the residents receive a pension and financial assistance.

The hokimiyat said there was no intent to humiliate anyone and that the visit was part of ongoing work to improve housing conditions for district residents.

## What Happens Next

The Kashkadarya Region hokimiyat has not yet published the results of the internal review requested by the Anti-Corruption Agency. As of publication, the Commissioner for Human Rights of the Oliy Majlis of the Republic of Uzbekistan (Ombudsman) has not issued an official position on the case. Materials published on the office's website and Telegram channel between August 3 and 5 concern other appeals from Kashkadarya Region and are unrelated to the incident in Shahrisabz.
