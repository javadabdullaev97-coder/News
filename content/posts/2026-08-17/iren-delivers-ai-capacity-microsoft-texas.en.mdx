IREN Limited (NASDAQ: IREN) on August 13 [reported](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/08/13/3344413/0/en/iren-delivers-horizon-1-to-microsoft-and-achieves-nvidia-exemplar-cloud-status-on-gb300-nvl72.html) that it had delivered — and Microsoft had accepted — the first phase of the Horizon 1 artificial intelligence data center at its site in Childress, Texas.

Horizon 1 is a building with direct liquid cooling for chips and 50 MW of IT load capacity, equipped with NVIDIA GB300 NVL72 graphics processing units. Microsoft accepted the facility into operation.

The delivery is part of a cloud GPU services agreement that IREN and Microsoft [signed](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1878848/000114036125040072/ef20058139_ex99-1.htm) on November 3, 2025. The five-year agreement is valued at $9.7 billion, including a 20% upfront payment. To purchase processors and related equipment, IREN separately signed a $5.8 billion contract with Dell Technologies.

IREN's Childress campus is designed for 750 MW of power capacity. The four Horizon buildings — 1, 2, 3 and 4 — will together provide Microsoft with 200 MW of critical IT load. Horizon 2, 3 and 4 are scheduled for delivery later in 2026.

"Horizon 1's delivery demonstrates the strength of our vertically integrated model and our ability to execute complex AI infrastructure projects quickly and at scale," IREN co-founder and co-CEO Daniel Roberts [said](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/08/13/3344413/0/en/iren-delivers-horizon-1-to-microsoft-and-achieves-nvidia-exemplar-cloud-status-on-gb300-nvl72.html).

Separately, the company [announced](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001878848/000114036126032638/ef20080141_ex99-1.htm) that following testing, NVIDIA had awarded its GB300 NVL72 deployment at Horizon 1 Exemplar Cloud status.

## What's next

IREN aims to bring the combined capacity of its AI cloud sites to 480 MW by the end of 2026 and to 1.2 GW in 2027. The next step is delivering the remaining three Horizon buildings in Childress to Microsoft by the end of 2026.
