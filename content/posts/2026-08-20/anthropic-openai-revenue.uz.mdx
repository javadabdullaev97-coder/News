Anthropic 2026-yil ikkinchi choragida daromad bo'yicha birinchi marta OpenAI'ni ortda qoldirdi — $11,6 mlrd, OpenAI'da $6,7 mlrd, deb xabar berdi The Wall Street Journal ikki kompaniya ko'rsatkichlaridan xabardor manbalarga tayanib. Bu ma'lumotni bir nechta nashr keltirmoqda; nashr etilgan paytda rasmiy tasdiq yo'q — ikkala kompaniya ham xususiy bo'lib, choraklik daromadni rasman e'lon qilmaydi.

Anthropic daromadi birinchi chorakka nisbatan ikki baravardan ortiq oshdi, deb yozadi [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/anthropic-passes-openai-on-revenue-for-the-first-time/) va [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/anthropic-beats-openai-in-revenue-for-first-time/); [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/08/18/openai-falls-further-behind-anthropic-with-disappointing-revenue-growth-and-mounting-losses/) esa xuddi shu WSJ hisobotiga tayanib, ehtiyotkorroq baho beradi — o'sish «50 foizdan ortiq» bo'lgan. Yildan yilga o'sish yanada yuqori bo'ldi. [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/anthropic-beats-openai-in-revenue-for-first-time/) ma'lumotlariga ko'ra, Anthropic daromadi 2025-yil ikkinchi choragidagi $787 mlndan 14 barobardan ortiq oshdi.

OpenAI daromadi chorakdan-chorakka 18 foizga oshdi — $5,7 mlrddan $6,7 mlrdgacha, [deb xabar beradi SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/08/18/openai-falls-further-behind-anthropic-with-disappointing-revenue-growth-and-mounting-losses/). Kompaniyaning birjada aksiyalarini birlamchi joylashtirishi — IPO oldidan investorlar kuchliroq o'sishni kutgan edi, [deb ta'kidlaydi The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/anthropic-passes-openai-on-revenue-for-the-first-time/).

OpenAI'ning operatsion zarari birinchi chorakdagi $9,3 mlrddan ikkinchi chorakda $12,3 mlrdgacha oshdi, [deb yozadi SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/08/18/openai-falls-further-behind-anthropic-with-disappointing-revenue-growth-and-mounting-losses/). Anthropic esa, aksincha, o'z tarixida birinchi marta operatsion foyda ko'rsatdi — $559 mln, [deb xabar beradi SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/08/18/openai-falls-further-behind-anthropic-with-disappointing-revenue-growth-and-mounting-losses/); PYMNTS buni «kichik» deb ataydi va musbat tuzatilgan operatsion daromad haqida gapiradi.

## ChatGPT ga qarshi Claude Code

WSJ Anthropic natijalarini Claude Code vositasining muvaffaqiyati bilan bog'laydi — bu ilova dasturchilar so'roviga ko'ra dastur kodini yozadi va tahrirlaydi. Shu bilan birga OpenAI'da ChatGPT o'sishi sekinlashdi, [deb yozadi The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/anthropic-passes-openai-on-revenue-for-the-first-time/) va [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/anthropic-beats-openai-in-revenue-for-first-time/).

Claude modellariga har bir murojaat Anthropic'ga OpenAI modellariga shunga o'xshash murojaatdan ko'ra ko'proq daromad keltiradi — buni Vercel kompaniyasi ma'lumotlari ko'rsatadi, [deb keltiradi The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/anthropic-passes-openai-on-revenue-for-the-first-time/). Joriy oylik ko'rsatkichlar asosida hisoblangan Anthropic'ning yillik daromadi iyulga kelib $65 mlrdgacha oshdi, [deb xabar beradi The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/anthropic-passes-openai-on-revenue-for-the-first-time/) va SiliconANGLE.

OpenAI investorlarga daromad o'sish sur'ati uchinchi chorakda tezlashganini ma'lum qildi — bu iyul oyida yangi sun'iy intellekt (SI) modellari ishga tushirilganidan keyin yuz berdi, [deb yozadi PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/anthropic-beats-openai-in-revenue-for-first-time/).

Biz Anthropic va OpenAI matbuot xizmatlariga so'rov yubordik; javob olingach, materialga qo'shiladi.
