The State Assets Management Agency of the Republic of Uzbekistan on August 5 [sold at an online auction](https://t.me/DAVAKTIVUZ/6193) 237 of 619 listed lots for 54.2 billion soums against a starting price of 53.2 billion soums.

## What was sold

The final deal value exceeded the lots' starting price by 1 billion soums, reaching 54.2 billion soums (≈$4.54 million at the Central Bank's August 5 rate of 11,942.21 soums per dollar) against a starting 53.2 billion soums (≈$4.45 million). A total of 421 legal entities and individuals took part in the bidding, the agency [said](https://t.me/DAVAKTIVUZ/6193) on its Telegram channel.

By category, the agency [gave](https://t.me/DAVAKTIVUZ/6193) the following breakdown: land plots — 16 lots for 43.3 billion soums against a starting 43.2 billion; state enterprise property — 10 lots for 7.3 billion soums with no increase over the starting price; leased state property — 34 lots for 1.2 billion soums against a starting 0.5 billion; customer property — 3 lots for 1.3 billion soums against a starting 1.2 billion; agricultural land — 139 lots for 1 billion soums against a starting 0.9 billion; other assets — 35 lots for 0.1 billion soums against a starting 0.01 billion.

## Fergana and Angren — leases sold for multiples of starting price

Among the lease lots, the widest gap between the starting and final price was for the lease of a secondary school kitchen in the Oltiariq district of Fergana region: the starting annual rent of 6 million soums [rose](https://t.me/DAVAKTIVUZ/6193) to 190 million soums — more than 31-fold. The agency did not disclose the tenant's name.

Almost half as steep, but also a multiple, was the increase on another lot — the lease right to a building kitchen on the balance sheet of Uzbekistan State World Languages University in Angren, Tashkent region: the starting annual rent of 20 million soums [rose](https://t.me/DAVAKTIVUZ/6193) to 287 million soums — more than 14-fold. The agency did not name the winning bidder.

## For comparison

At the previous auction on August 4, the agency [listed](https://leap.uz/posts/2026-08-05/state-assets-auction-aug4-2026) 503 lots, of which 294 sold for 32.6 billion soums against a starting price of 30.3 billion soums — the most notable lot then was a medical center lease in Bukhara that sold for 80 times its starting price, LEAP News reported.

The August 5 auction offered more lots than the previous day's — 619 versus 503 — but the share sold was lower: 38.3% against 58.4%. At the same time, as the day before, the gap between starting and final prices on individual lots again proved to be a multiple — this time in two regions at once.

The State Assets Management Agency regularly holds online auctions through its own electronic platform.
