As of July 1, 2026, Uzbekistan [had 63.3 thousand](https://t.me/uzstataxborot/69156) industrial enterprises in operation — 2.2 thousand more than on March 1, the National Statistics Committee of the Republic of Uzbekistan reported.

## Breakdown by Type of Activity

Food production accounts for the largest number of enterprises — [12.7 thousand, or 20.1% of the total](https://t.me/uzstataxborot/69156). Second place goes to the manufacture of other non-metallic mineral products — 8.9 thousand companies (14%), a category that includes producers of construction materials, glass, cement and ceramics.

Further down the list by number of enterprises: apparel production — 6.4 thousand (10.1%), fabricated metal products — 5.2 thousand (8.2%), furniture — 4.4 thousand (6.9%), textile products — 3.9 thousand (6.1%), rubber and plastic products — 2.9 thousand (4.7%).

These seven categories together account for 70.1% of all industrial enterprises in the country. The National Statistics Committee did not break down the remaining 29.9% by type of activity.

## Growth Over Four Months

According to the [National Statistics Committee](https://stat.uz/uz/matbuot-markazi/qo-mita-yangiliklar/67610-zbekistonda-sanoat-korkhonalari-asosan-ajsi-j-nalishda-faoliyat-yuritmo-da-4), Uzbekistan had 61.1 thousand industrial enterprises as of March 1, 2026 — food production was already in first place then as well (12.2 thousand enterprises), followed by non-metallic mineral products (8.5 thousand) and apparel (6.1 thousand). Over four months, the number of industrial enterprises grew by 2.2 thousand, or 3.6%, while the sector structure changed little.
