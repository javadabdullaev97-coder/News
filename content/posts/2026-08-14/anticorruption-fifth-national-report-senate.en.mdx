The Senate of the Oliy Majlis on August 7 reviewed the fifth national anticorruption report for 2025, presented by Anticorruption Agency Director Akmal Burkhanov, at its 18th plenary session, the Senate's Telegram channel [reported](https://t.me/senatuz/46686).

## What was done for prevention

The agency reported primarily on preventive measures rather than criminal case statistics. In 37 districts and cities of Uzbekistan, "roadmaps" for turning territories into corruption-free zones have been approved, the Anticorruption Agency's press release [said](https://anticorruption.uz/ru/article/Қонунчилик%20палатасида%20Коррупцияга%20қарши%20курашиш%20тўғрисидаги%20бешинчи%20миллий%20маъруза%20кўриб%20чиқилди).

Under the "Digital Market" program, 281 markets and 132 parking lots were digitized. A total of 381 civil servants underwent a corruption resilience assessment.

The report separately noted growth in grant support for nongovernmental organizations working in the anticorruption field: in 2025, grants grew to 3.4 billion soums (~$285,000 at the Central Bank of Uzbekistan's exchange rate for August 7, 2026 — 11,915.64 soums per dollar) and funded 45 projects.

## International agenda

Following the discussion, a resolution was adopted on holding the 12th session of the Conference of the States Parties to the UN Convention against Corruption in Uzbekistan in 2027. The report also contains a general recommendation to strengthen preventive mechanisms in public procurement — without detailing specific amounts or the number of violations.

The session was also confirmed by [Senat.uz](https://senat.uz/ru/post/post-5883) in a publication titled "National Anticorruption Report Discussed" dated August 7.
