UFC [announced the card](https://www.sherdog.com/news/news/UFC-333-gets-VolkanovskiEvloev-title-fight-YanDvalishvili-trilogy-202368) for UFC 333, set for October 24 at Etihad Arena on Yas Island in Abu Dhabi. The card was unveiled on Saturday, August 15.

The main event will be a [title fight](https://www.ufc.com/event/ufc-333) at featherweight: reigning champion Alexander Volkanovski (28-4) will defend his belt against unbeaten challenger Movsar Evloev (20-0). Volkanovski is a two-time featherweight champion who defended the title against Diego Lopes in January 2026. Evloev earned his title shot by beating Lerone Murphy in March 2026 at a UFC Fight Night event in London.

The co-main event will be a bantamweight title fight — the third career meeting between Petr Yan and Merab Dvalishvili. Dvalishvili won their first fight in March 2023 by a lopsided unanimous decision. The rematch [took place](https://www.sherdog.com/news/news/UFC-333-gets-VolkanovskiEvloev-title-fight-YanDvalishvili-trilogy-202368) on December 6, 2025, at UFC 323 in Las Vegas. Yan won by unanimous decision, reclaiming the title and ending Dvalishvili's 14-fight winning streak. The loss stopped Dvalishvili one fight short of four title defenses in a calendar year.

The card also includes three confirmed bouts: Azamat Murzakanov will face Dominick Reyes at light heavyweight, and Lone'er Kavanagh will face Ramazan Temirov at flyweight. Grant Dawson will face Nurullo Aliev at lightweight. Paramount+ and UFC Fight Pass will broadcast the event.
