The National Agency for Perspective Projects (NAPP) [reported](https://t.me/napp_uz/2371) on August 7 that Uzbekistan's insurance market closed the first half of 2026 with premiums up 28.6%, to 8 trillion soums.

## Key Indicators

The number of active insurance policies at the end of the first half reached [13.1 million](https://t.me/napp_uz/2371) — up 14.9% year-on-year.

Insurance premiums totaled [8 trillion soums](https://t.me/napp_uz/2371) (about $671 million at the Central Bank of Uzbekistan's exchange rate for August 7 — 11,915.64 soums per dollar), up 28.6% year-on-year.

Insurers' authorized capital grew 37.2% to reach [4.3 trillion soums](https://t.me/napp_uz/2371) (about $361 million).

Insurance payouts for the half-year grew faster than any other indicator — up 81.3%, to [2.3 trillion soums](https://t.me/napp_uz/2371) (about $193 million).

## What NAPP Has Not Yet Published

The agency did not provide a breakdown by insurance type or individual companies — only five aggregate figures for the market as a whole. NAPP publishes a detailed quarterly report separately on its [website](https://napp.uz/ru/pages/statistics-and-analysis-for-im); the latest one available at the time of publication was the report for Q1 2026.
