Uzbek President Shavkat Mirziyoyev on August 18 [announced a three-year moratorium](https://www.president.uz/ru/lists/view/9510) on small business inspections at a meeting with entrepreneurs in Khiva held in an open-dialogue format.

The moratorium covers any inspections not linked to threats to public health or the interests of other entrepreneurs. Repeat inspections within a year are allowed only with authorization from the Business Ombudsman. A different rule applies to medium and large businesses: voluntary audits are recognized as official. For a first violation, an entrepreneur receives a warning and 10 days to fix the issue — without a fine.

## What changes in licensing

More than [30 types of licenses and permits will be scrapped](https://www.president.uz/ru/lists/view/9510) in the near future, Mirziyoyev said. Issuance timelines for another 35 types will be cut 2-3 times. For 10 types of activity, authorities will switch from a permit-based system to a notification-based one.

One example of simplification is a unified health certificate that will replace three separate documents: veterinary, sanitary and phytosanitary permits. Every new license or permit will be tested for six months in three regions before being introduced.

## Terms for buying state assets

The president announced a package of incentives for businesses [buying state property](https://www.president.uz/ru/lists/view/9510). The down payment at auctions will fall from 30% to 15%. An entrepreneur who pays in full within six months will receive a 25% discount. Those who pay half the amount can pay off the rest in interest-free installments over 7 years.

The auction period will be cut from 30 to 15 days, and from 10 to 5 days for repeat bidding. The starting price of unsold state property will be lowered in stages — by up to 10%. Land for such properties will be offered as a "ready package," complete with a building permit.

A separate benefit applies to bank-owned property — about 2,500 properties worth 9 trillion soums ([≈$761.5 million at the Central Bank's exchange rate on August 19](https://cbu.uz/ru/arkhiv-kursov-valyut/json/USD/2026-08-19/)). It will be sold at a discount, with no down payment or interest, and buyers will be exempt from land and property tax for a year. The advance payment for infrastructure connection will fall from 20% to 5%.
