One week remains before applications close for President AI Award 2026 — in early August, IT Park Uzbekistan [published](https://t.me/itpark_uz/20584) a step-by-step guide on Telegram for submitting the application form on awards.gov.uz. The deadline — August 15 — was moved from the original August 1 back in late July; the new guide does not change the dates, it explains how to fill out the application.

## How to Apply

Applications can only be submitted through the [awards.gov.uz](https://awards.gov.uz/ru/paia) platform. In the [guide](https://t.me/itpark_uz/20584), IT Park listed four mandatory steps: registering on the platform, filling in team and project details, agreeing to the public offer, and final submission of the application. IT Park separately asked teams to check the accuracy and completeness of all data before the final submission.

According to the IT Park guide, the form asks for each team member's full name, PINFL (personal identification number), phone number, email address, role in the project and place of work or study, as well as a project presentation and project logo.

Participation is free. Teams of 3 to 8 people made up of citizens of Uzbekistan or persons permanently residing in the country may apply, provided they already have a working prototype or MVP of an AI product.

## Prize Fund and Categories

The contest's total prize fund is $1,000,000. According to [awards.gov.uz](https://awards.gov.uz/ru/paia), the contest runs across five categories: artificial intelligence in public administration, in industry and entrepreneurship, in healthcare, in education, and in the green economy and agricultural technologies.

After applications close, the jury will select teams to take part in an acceleration program with mentors and industry experts. Following the acceleration program, three winners will be determined in each category: prizes will total $100,000, $60,000 and $40,000 — 15 winners in all.

Funds are not paid out in a single lump sum but in tranches, contingent on meeting key performance indicators; the distribution is handled by IT Park Ventures LLC.

## A Second Contest With a Larger Fund

Alongside President AI Award, IT Park also runs President Tech Award — a contest for technology startups with no restriction to AI. On the instructions of President Shavkat Mirziyoyev, the prize fund for this contest was [increased](https://www.outsource.gov.uz/en/media/the-prize-fund-of-the-president-tech-award-will-be-increased-to-5-million) from $1 million to $5 million: $1 million is allocated as grants, and another $4 million as venture investment for selected teams.

Both contests are part of a single presidential program to support technology entrepreneurship.
