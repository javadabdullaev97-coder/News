The Agency for Assessment of Knowledge and Qualifications, under the Ministry of Higher Education, Science and Innovations, announced final results of the competitive selection — known as the mandate — for bachelor's degree applicants to Uzbekistan's universities for the 2026/2027 academic year on August 15.

## How to Check the Results

[The publication on the agency's official Telegram channel](https://t.me/BaholashUz/11386) went out at 03:00 on August 15. It said results of the selection can be checked four ways:

1. On [my.uzbmb.uz](https://my.uzbmb.uz/) — enter the applicant's passport series and number along with their PINFL (JSHSHIR).
2. On [mandat.uzbmb.uz](https://mandat.uzbmb.uz/) — enter the applicant's ID number.
3. Through the agency's official Telegram bots — @bmba_natija_bot, @bmba_natija_1bot and @bmba_natija_2bot — by sending the ID number from the exam sheet or the test permit.
4. Through the testing results protocol, which the agency sends directly to universities.

## Context

The mandate is the final stage of university admission. The agency assigns applicants to universities and bachelor's degree programs based on entrance test scores and the priorities applicants listed when choosing a university. The agency's publication did not include specific passing scores by program or the number of students admitted.
