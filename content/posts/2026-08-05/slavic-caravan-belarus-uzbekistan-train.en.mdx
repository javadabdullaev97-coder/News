Belarusian Railway dispatched the first "Slavic Caravan" block train to Uzbekistan from the Orsha-Vostochnaya station on July 30, and the train arrived at Chukursay station in Tashkent on August 5.

## Route and first run

The first train [departed](https://www.belarus.by/ru/business/business-news/pervyj-skvoznoj-gruzovoj-poezd-slavjanskij-karavan-otpravilsja-iz-orshi-v-uzbekistan_i_0000217389.html) from the Orsha-Vostochnaya station in Vitebsk region — the point where cargo from all regions of Belarus is consolidated. The train ran without being re-marshalled or making intermediate stops along the Belarus–Russia–Kazakhstan–Uzbekistan route, covering about 4,000 kilometers. According to [BelTA](https://belta.by/society/view/pervyj-skvoznoj-gruzovoj-poezd-slavjanskij-karavan-otpravilsja-iz-orshi-v-uzbekistan-793732-2026), the train included wagons carrying wood-processing products, sugar, potato starch and mixed feed.

The train arrived at [Chukursay](https://t.me/Mintrans_uz/28554) station — a branch of LLC "Tashkent Regional Railway Hub" — at around 9:00 a.m. Tashkent time (7:05 a.m. Minsk time) on August 5, the Ministry of Transport of Uzbekistan reported. The welcoming ceremony was attended by representatives of the Ministry of Transport, JSC "Uzbekiston Temir Yo'llari" and the Embassy of Belarus in Uzbekistan, [reports](https://www.sb.by/articles/v-uzbekistane-vstretili-pervyy-skvoznoy-gruzovoy-poezd-slavyanskiy-karavan-iz-belarusi.html) the state newspaper SB. Belarus Segodnya. According to the outlet, the train was in transit for less than six days.

## Second train and cargo for neighboring countries

The second train [departed](https://www.belarus.by/ru/business/business-news/bzhd-otpravila-vtoroj-poezd-slavjanskij-karavan-v-uzbekistan_i_0000217595.html) from the same Orsha-Vostochnaya station on August 3 and is carrying over 4,000 tons of cargo — wood-processing products and high-value-added food products. Part of the cargo is transiting through Uzbekistan to Kyrgyzstan and Tajikistan.

The Belarusian Railway press center said the "new service demonstrates demand for the project and the route, and opens broad prospects for exporters across all industries" — from food processing to chemicals, construction materials, wood processing and machine-building.

## Logistics and route economics

Belarusian Railway chief Valery Verenich [noted](https://www.belarus.by/ru/business/business-news/pervyj-skvoznoj-gruzovoj-poezd-slavjanskij-karavan-otpravilsja-iz-orshi-v-uzbekistan_i_0000217389.html) that cargo had moved along the Belarus–Uzbekistan route before, "but together with our Uzbek colleagues we designed an entirely different, higher-quality service." According to him, shipping a single wagon used to take up to two months, while the through block train without re-marshalling reaches Uzbekistan in 5–7 days.

Single containers with a capacity of 20 tons can be included in the train — this type of shipping costs at least one and a half times less than road transport. A wagon holds up to 60 tons of cargo, which allows smaller businesses to ship goods in small volumes or in consolidated containers from several shippers, rather than waiting to accumulate a full wagon load. At the initial stage, "Slavic Caravan" trains are planned to run twice a month.

## Context

The project is being implemented, as BelTA notes, "in furtherance of high-level decisions to increase supplies" of Belarusian products to the Uzbek market. Orsha-Vostochnaya was chosen as the consolidation station precisely because cargo from all regions of Belarus is gathered there.

Going forward, the project is expected to add a return load — containers of Uzbek products, including light-industry goods and fruit, for shipment to Belarus. A visit by the chairman of the Vitebsk Regional Executive Committee to Uzbekistan is also expected, to sign a roadmap for cooperation between the region and the Uzbek side.
