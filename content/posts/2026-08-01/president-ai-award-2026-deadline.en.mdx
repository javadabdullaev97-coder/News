IT Park Uzbekistan on July 31 extended the application deadline for the national AI startup contest President AI Award 2026 to August 15 — the previous deadline, August 1, had been set by a resolution of the Cabinet of Ministers of the Republic of Uzbekistan.

## What Changed

IT Park [announced](https://t.me/itpark_uz/20550) the deadline shift on the morning of July 31 on its Telegram channel. There was no other announcement: the new deadline was not published on the it-park.uz website, and the fact of the extension rests on a single message in the messenger app.

The previous deadline was set by [Cabinet of Ministers Resolution No. 288](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/-8253911) of June 5, 2026. The document approved the procedure for holding the "Best Startup in Artificial Intelligence" contest (President AI Award) for candidates for the Prize of the President of the Republic of Uzbekistan. Under the resolution, the contest is announced on July 1 each year, with applications accepted until August 1.

Applications are accepted through the [awards.gov.uz](https://awards.gov.uz/ru/paia) portal. Participation is free.

IT Park listed the application requirements in the same post: a team of 3 to 8 people, a working MVP, a three-minute video pitch, a link to a GitHub repository, a presentation in PDF format and a project logo. All materials must be prepared in Uzbek, Russian and English.

The eligibility requirement for participants is set out in [Resolution No. 288](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/-8253911): applications may be submitted by citizens of Uzbekistan and stateless persons permanently residing in the republic.

## How the Prize Fund Works

The contest's total prize fund is $1,000,000. It runs [across five categories](https://awards.gov.uz/ru/paia): artificial intelligence in public administration, in industry and entrepreneurship, in healthcare and medicine, in education, and in the green economy and agricultural technologies.

There will be [15](https://awards.gov.uz/ru/paia) winners, with awards of $100,000, $60,000 and $40,000. Neither Resolution No. 288 nor awards.gov.uz specifies how the prize places are distributed among the categories.

The awards are not paid out as cash to the team's account. Under [Resolution No. 288](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/-8253911), IT Park Ventures LLC invests the funds in the winning project in exchange for an equity stake. The amount is converted into soums at the Central Bank of Uzbekistan's exchange rate on the payment date and is paid out in two equal tranches — the second is contingent on meeting key performance indicators.

The contest's operating body is the Directorate of the Software Products and Information Technologies Park LLC (IT Park). Overall oversight rests with the organizing committee, which approves the contest's five categories every year by June 1.

Selected teams go through a [five-stage program](https://awards.gov.uz/ru/paia) with one-on-one mentoring support, expert consultations and access to investors — from idea to Demo Day.

## The Second Presidential Contest

IT Park also runs a second presidential contest — [President Tech Award](https://awards.gov.uz/ru/president-tech-awards), with a prize fund of $5,000,000. It is divided into three tracks: incubation ($1,000,000 in grants, 25 winners of $40,000 each), acceleration ($1,500,000 in investment, 15 teams of $100,000 each) and "Best Startup Project" ($2,500,000 in investment, 30 winners across 10 categories). According to the organizers, more than 13,000 people and over 1,800 startups took part across the program's three previous waves, with prizes paid out totaling $3,000,000.

These are separate contests: the $1,000,000 fund applies only to [President AI Award](https://awards.gov.uz/ru/paia), while the $5,000,000 fund applies only to [President Tech Award](https://awards.gov.uz/ru/president-tech-awards). The official launch of the AI Award, according to awards.gov.uz, is [scheduled](https://awards.gov.uz/ru/paia) for 2026.
