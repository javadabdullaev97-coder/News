The National Statistics Committee of the Republic of Uzbekistan said on August 5 that the country's enterprises produced 15.2 trillion soums worth of beverages in January-June 2026 — up 11.1% year-on-year.

## Where beverages are produced

The city of Tashkent accounts for more than 60% of the total volume — [according to the National Statistics Committee](https://stat.uz/ru/press-tsentr/novosti-goskomstata/69670-zbekistonda-yarim-jilda-15-2-trln-s-mlik-ichimliklar-ishlab-chi-arildi-3), that's 9.3 trillion soums (about $782.5 million at the [Central Bank of Uzbekistan](https://cbu.uz/ru/arkhiv-kursov-valyut/json/USD/2026-08-06/) exchange rate for August 6 — 11,886.72 soums per dollar). Tashkent region ranks second with 2.4 trillion soums (about $201.9 million), followed by Khorezm region with 1.1 trillion soums (about $92.5 million). The country's remaining regions, including Karakalpakstan, produce noticeably less — the lowest figure in the agency's list is 13.3 billion soums.

## Up 11.1% year-on-year, but no breakdown by beverage type

The National Statistics Committee does not disclose in this release whether the growth refers to a nominal increase in the value of output or to a physical volume index. The agency also does not publish the absolute production figure for the first half of 2025 — only the percentage increase. It does not provide a breakdown by beverage type (alcoholic, non-alcoholic, juices, water) or data on exports or imports.

The National Statistics Committee [reposted](https://t.me/uzstataxborot/69178) the same figures — 15.2 trillion soums and 11.1% growth — on its Telegram channel.
