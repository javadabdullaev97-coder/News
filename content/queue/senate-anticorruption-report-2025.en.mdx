The Senate of the Oliy Majlis reviewed the [national anti-corruption report](https://senat.uz/ru/plenary-sessions/post-5883) for 2025 at its plenary session on August 8, 2026 — over the year, 7,517 people were criminally prosecuted.

## Key figures from the report

According to data presented at the session, [damage to state interests from corruption crimes](https://senat.uz/ru/plenary-sessions/post-5883) exceeded 2.9 trillion soums in 2025 (about $243 million at the Central Bank of Uzbekistan's exchange rate for August 10 — 11,952.10 soums per dollar).

The report separately highlights violations in public procurement — worth 12.6 trillion soums (about $1.05 billion at the same rate). This is not the same as damage from corruption crimes: it refers to detected violations of procurement procedures, not proven embezzlement of the entire amount.

## Where most violations were found

The largest number of corruption crimes in 2025 occurred in preschool and school education, healthcare, local government bodies (hokimiyats) and commercial banks, according to the [report](https://senat.uz/ru/plenary-sessions/post-5883) reviewed by senators.

The report is prepared by the Anti-Corruption Agency and submitted annually to both chambers of the Oliy Majlis. The Senate [reviewed the previous report, covering 2024, in September 2025](https://anticorruption.uz/uz/article/korrupsiyaga-qarshi-kurashish-togrisidagi-tortinchi-milliy-maruza-senatga-taqdim-qilindi) — at the time, it was the fourth report in this annual series.

## What's next

The Senate usually adopts a resolution following the discussion of the report; its text had not been published on the Senate's website at the time of publication.
