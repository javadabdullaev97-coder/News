The Ministry of Health of Uzbekistan has proposed reimbursing travel costs for patients on scheduled hemodialysis from the state budget — a survey on the initiative will run through August 15, 2026.

## What was proposed

Under the Ministry of Health's proposal, patients would be able to choose the transport that suits them — a taxi, a personal car, or another mode — for trips to and from the hemodialysis procedure, and then receive compensation for the costs incurred [in the established manner](https://t.me/ssvuz/22428). The ministry says this mechanism will let patients save time and money.

The publication does not specify the exact compensation amount, limits on the number of trips, or the procedure for processing the payments.

## What stage the decision is at

This is an initiative, not an adopted decision. The [Ministry of Health's post](https://t.me/ssvuz/22428) states directly that before implementing the proposal, the ministry needs to study the opinions and suggestions of patients themselves — a survey has been organized for this purpose and will run through August 15, 2026. Participation is possible via the [Google Forms form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSf_9yKyzKdL0nT9VAOxaO6zLJY4-lCL-aFQMWlqKiPFXGRSOA/viewform), the link to which was published by the ministry's press service.

As of publication, a draft regulatory act on compensating travel for hemodialysis patients has not been posted on the regulation.gov.uz portal for discussing regulatory legal acts — meaning the mechanism currently exists only as an announced initiative, without an approved document.

## What's next

The Ministry of Health says that it will take patients' opinions and suggestions into account when finalizing the compensation mechanism based on the results of the survey. The ministry's publication does not name dates for completing the development, publishing a draft resolution, or its adoption.
