The foreign ministers of Qatar, Jordan, Indonesia, Pakistan, Turkey, Saudi Arabia, Egypt and the United Arab Emirates on August 16 condemned, in a [joint statement](https://www.mofa.gov.ae/en/mediahub/news/2026/8/16/uae-egypt), Israel's refusal to implement the roadmap for the Gaza peace plan.

In the statement, the ministers [condemned](https://www.mofa.gov.ae/en/mediahub/news/2026/8/16/uae-egypt) Israel's "declared refusal" to follow the roadmap for completing the plan of US President Donald Trump, which was approved by UN Security Council Resolution 2803. They held Israel "directly and fully responsible" for the consequences of any refusal, disruption or failure to comply with the plan.

The ministers also rejected "any attempts to deny or block" Palestinian statehood. They said a just and lasting peace is possible only through the creation of an independent Palestinian state within the 1967 borders with its capital in East Jerusalem. The authors of the statement called on the United States to maintain "active and sustained engagement" to secure Israel's full implementation of the plan.

## Netanyahu Calls the Plan Unacceptable

Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu on August 9, at a cabinet meeting, [said](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/9/israel-rejects-trumps-15-point-plan-for-gaza) Israel rejects Trump's 15-point plan. Netanyahu said Israeli troops would not leave Gaza until Hamas is fully disarmed. He demanded "real, not fictitious disarmament" — including light weapons.

Trump's plan operates within the ceasefire that took effect on October 10, 2025. It [provides](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/7/31/gaza-board-of-peace-announces-hamas-disarmament-agreement-what-we-know) for transferring power in Gaza to a technocratic government and withdrawing Israeli troops in parallel with Hamas's disarmament.

## Kushner Holds Talks With Hamas in Egypt

Jared Kushner, adviser and son-in-law of US President Donald Trump, on August 16 [met](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/16/kushner-meets-hamas-in-egypt-to-push-trumps-gaza-plan) with Hamas political leader Khalil al-Hayya in the city of El Alamein. The talks were attended by Board of Peace envoy Nickolay Mladenov and Board of Peace council member Tony Blair. Egyptian intelligence director Hassan Rashad, Qatari diplomat Ali al-Tawadi and a representative of Turkey also took part.

Hamas confirmed its commitment to the plan's second phase and said it had fully complied with the terms of the ceasefire's first phase. The group accused Israel of violations. According to Gaza authorities, [cited by Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/16/kushner-meets-hamas-in-egypt-to-push-trumps-gaza-plan), more than 1,250 Palestinians have been killed in the strip since October 10, 2025 (as of August 16).

After the meeting, Hamas [called on](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/liveblog/2026/8/17/iran-war-live-tehran-us-mou-set-to-expire-hamass-al-khayya-meets-kushner) the Board of Peace to "compel" Israel to accept the Gaza plan. On August 17, Israel released 35 Palestinian prisoners and [returned](https://www.aljazeera.com/video/newsfeed/2026/8/17/08-17-26-pal-prisoners-release-sv-mp4) them to the Gaza Strip.

## What's Next

Kushner and Mladenov are set to [meet](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/16/kushner-meets-hamas-in-egypt-to-push-trumps-gaza-plan) with Netanyahu on Monday, August 17. The sides will discuss the next steps on Hamas's disarmament and the withdrawal of Israeli troops from Gaza.
