The law allowing children to be given a surname based on their father's first name was signed on August 13, 2026, under number ZRU-1164 — the Ministry of Justice [reported](https://t.me/huquqiyaxborot/20255) on August 14 in its Telegram channel "Huquqiy axborot".

## What changed in the Family Code

Before the amendment, [Article 69 of the Family Code](https://gov.uz/ru/advice/58/document/563) allowed a child's surname to be formed only from the father's or mother's surname. With the parents' agreement and in line with national tradition, the surname could also be formed from the grandfather's first name — on the father's or mother's side. Law ZRU-1164 adds another option: the surname can now be formed directly from the child's father's first name, bypassing the grandfather's name.

The [Ministry of Justice's post](https://t.me/huquqiyaxborot/20255) states that the law takes effect on the day of its official publication — meaning the provision is already in force.

## From Senate approval to signing

The [Senate of the Oliy Majlis approved](https://leap.uz/ru/2026/08/10/senate-father-name-surname-law) the amendment at a plenary session on August 8. Five days passed between approval and the law's signing and numbering.

## What's next

The rule remains an option rather than a requirement: parents can still choose the father's surname, the mother's surname, or — by agreement — a surname based on the grandfather's name. Giving a surname based on the father's first name becomes an additional option when registering newborns at civil registry offices (ZAGS).
