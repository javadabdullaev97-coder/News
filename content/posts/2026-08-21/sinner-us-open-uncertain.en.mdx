Italian Jannik Sinner, the world's No. 1, remains uncertain for the 2026 US Open — a right knee injury kept him from playing in Montreal and Cincinnati, and the New York tournament starts August 30.

Sinner withdrew from the Cincinnati tournament on August 9. "My right knee is bothering me, and even though we're working hard with the medical team, I have to admit — I'm not ready to step on court yet," the tennis player [said](https://www.skysports.com/tennis/news/12040/13571440/jannik-sinner-withdraws-from-cincinnati-open-as-knee-injury-raises-us-open-fitness-concerns). A week earlier he skipped the Montreal tournament, and after his Wimbledon win in July he hadn't played at all.

## Who Else Is in Doubt

As of August 21, 14 players had withdrawn from the 2026 US Open, [reports](https://www.tennis365.com/tennis-news/us-open-withdrawal-list-2026-14-stars-out) Tennis365 — among them Britain's Emma Raducanu and Spain's Alejandro Davidovich Fokina. Besides Sinner, two more stars' status remains unclear: Britain's Jack Draper is recovering from a bone bruise in his arm. Denmark's Holger Rune hasn't played since rupturing his Achilles tendon in October 2025.

The 2026 US Open main draw starts August 30 — Sinner and his team must decide on his participation before that date.
