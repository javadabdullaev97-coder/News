The Central Bank of the Republic of Uzbekistan will raise, starting August 9, 2026, the threshold for cash foreign currency purchases below which a bank does not require an identity document, from $100 to $500.

## Amendments Registered in May, Take Effect in August

The Central Bank of Uzbekistan and the Department for Combating Economic Crimes under the Prosecutor General's Office of the Republic of Uzbekistan [introduced amendments](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/8184199) to the Internal Control Rules on Combating the Legalization of Proceeds from Criminal Activity, the Financing of Terrorism and the Financing of the Proliferation of Weapons of Mass Destruction in Commercial Banks.

Resolution No. 343-B-14 of the Central Bank's board was adopted on April 16, 2026, and Department Resolution No. 16 on April 15. The Ministry of Justice registered the document on May 8, 2026, under No. 2886-12. The document takes effect three months after official publication — on August 9, 2026.

The underlying rules have been in effect since 2017; they were [registered](https://lex.uz/docs/3212192) under No. 2886 on May 23, 2017, and have been amended repeatedly since then.

## Threshold Rises Fivefold

The amendments change subparagraph "b" of clause 22 of the rules: in the third paragraph, the figure "100" is replaced with "500." Until August 9, a bank must identify a customer purchasing cash foreign currency in an amount exceeding $100.

The Central Bank of Uzbekistan [said](https://t.me/centralbankuzbekistan/13369) on July 28 that an identity document will not be required for cash foreign currency purchases equivalent to up to $500. Above that threshold, the customer identification requirement remains in place.

At the [Central Bank's exchange rate](https://cbu.uz/ru/arkhiv-kursov-valyut/json/USD/2026-08-01/) for July 31 — 12,006.39 soums per dollar — $500 amounts to about 6 million soums.

The same subparagraph "b" of clause 22 also changes a second threshold. Instead of 500 times the base calculation value, it now [sets](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/8184199) a fixed amount — 175 million soums, about $14,600 at the July 31 exchange rate.

## What Else Resolution No. 2886-12 Changes

The package of amendments is not limited to the identification threshold. The document also [addresses](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/8184199):

- definitions of beneficial owner and high-risk states;
- the procedure for identification using digital services;
- requirements for accompanying money transfers with information about the parties involved;
- traceability of card transactions and cash withdrawals;
- criteria for classifying transactions as suspicious.

The rules [apply](https://lex.uz/docs/3212192) to all commercial banks in the country.

## Currency Exchange via ATMs and Apps

The Central Bank of Uzbekistan [said](https://t.me/centralbankuzbekistan/13389) on July 31 that currency can be exchanged through automated exchange ATMs operating "24/7" and through commercial banks' mobile apps, without visiting a branch.

The regulator attached to its statement a list of individual banks that exchange currency on weekends.

## What's Next

The $500 threshold will take effect on August 9, 2026. Resolution No. 2886-12 does not provide for a separate transition period.
