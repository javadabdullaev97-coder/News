In the Sangardak mahalla of Sariasiya district, Surxondaryo region, the uprooting and damage of trees on the land of the Uzun forestry was recorded on August 3 — preliminary reports indicate 11 Zarafshan junipers about 100 years old were affected. This was [reported](https://t.me/ecogovuz/49460) by the Telegram channel of the National Committee for Ecology and Climate Change of the Republic of Uzbekistan, citing an [official publication](https://gov.uz/ru/eco/news/view/201748) on the gov.uz portal.

## What Was Established on Site

The work on the forestry's territory was carried out without any permits — despite warnings from environmentalists, [the publication states](https://gov.uz/ru/eco/news/view/201748). A working group has been formed to investigate the circumstances, comprising representatives of the environmental police, the Prosecutor General's Office, the regional administration, and the regional main department of ecology.

The working group is assessing the environmental damage caused and conducting a legal review of whether the work complied with environmental protection legislation. A separate task is to identify those responsible, [the source states](https://gov.uz/ru/eco/news/view/201748). According to the agency, those found responsible will face measures under the law based on the results of the inquiry, and the findings will additionally be made public.

## What's Next

The publication does not specify an official deadline for completing the inquiry. The exact number of damaged trees, the extent of the damage, and those responsible will be known once the interagency group completes its work — the agency has promised to publish the final findings later.
