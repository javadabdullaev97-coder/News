President of Uzbekistan Shavkat Mirziyoyev on August 14 signed [Decree UP-160](https://lex.uz/docs/8406903) on the «Odil sudlov — 2030» («Justice — 2030») strategy, which is meant to build a people-oriented and fair judicial system.

The document introduces [an action program](https://www.president.uz/ru/lists/view/9499) of 57 tasks and 160 measures for 2026–2028, along with 33 target indicators for 2030.

## Interregional Courts and Investigating Judges

Five interregional courts will start operating on July 1, 2027. They will take over case reviews in the areas of courts of general jurisdiction and administrative courts. This is meant to reduce the number of appeals to the capital's courts from the regions, [the decree says](https://www.president.uz/ru/lists/view/9499). The new courts' administrative staff will get 319 positions.

From the same date, investigating judges will gain the right to review the legality and validity of detentions when considering petitions for procedural coercive measures. This complies with the international Prima Facie standard.

## Precedent Registry and Digital Court

A registry will launch on August 1, 2027: the Supreme Court will openly publish and regularly update rulings of its presidium that shape uniform judicial practice.

By the end of 2027, the my.sud.uz portal and its mobile app will be updated, and the search for court rulings will be expanded. At least 50% of court filings will be converted to electronic format, and 100% of hearings will be recorded on audio.

## Court Administration and Access to Justice

An institute of court administration will start operating on January 1, 2028. It will take over courts' organizational tasks not directly related to case review, freeing judges from them. At the same time, a proactive case management standard will be introduced for economic disputes.

By 2030, civil courts will appear in nearly all districts and cities. All courts will adopt a "single window" principle: applications filed with the wrong court will be forwarded by the courts themselves.

## When It Takes Effect

Decree UP-160 has been in force since August 15, 2026. The listed reforms will be introduced in stages: some measures start July 1, 2027, others January 1, 2028. Full rollout of the digital court and the network of civil courts is planned through 2030. Deputy head of the Presidential Administration N.J. Hakimov was appointed responsible for implementing the decree.

## Who Is Affected

The changes will affect citizens and businesses that go to court in civil, administrative and economic cases. Because investigating judges' powers are expanded, the reform will also affect participants in criminal cases at the investigation stage. Judges and court staff are affected too, as a new administrative structure emerges.
