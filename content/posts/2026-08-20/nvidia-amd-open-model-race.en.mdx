Hugging Face reported on August 14 that Chinese labs continue to lead in the size of open-weight models, while in the United States that race is being led not by Meta and Google but by chipmakers Nvidia and AMD.

Open-weight models are those whose parameters are published openly and available for fine-tuning, unlike closed models such as GPT or Gemini. The annual [“State of Open Models”](https://huggingface.co/blog/state-of-open-models-summer-2026) report is compiled by Hugging Face itself, one of the largest catalogs of open models.

According to the report, the upper size limit of Chinese models in 2026 ranged month to month from 754 billion to 2.78 trillion parameters. American models stayed below 130 billion parameters in five of seven months. Labs such as Moonshot, MiniMax, Xiaomi and Z.ai rarely publish models smaller than 70 billion parameters — parameter counts here indicate a model's training scale, not its final quality.

## A Bet on Hardware, Not Architecture

Nvidia and AMD published more new model repositories in 2026 than any other organization in the Hugging Face rankings. Each company has more than 200 repositories, with LiquidAI in third place at roughly 100. A repository is a separate listing of a model or its version on the platform.

Most Nvidia and AMD releases are not original architectures but optimized conversions of other companies' models tailored to their own chips. The report's authors put it simply: “open models are a way to sell chips.”

The exception is the Nemotron line: according to [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/blog/state-of-open-models-summer-2026), Nemotron 3 Ultra has 561 billion parameters and Nemotron 3 Super has 124 billion. Nvidia [debuted](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-debuts-nemotron-3-family-of-open-models) the Nemotron 3 family on December 15, 2025, calling it an open platform for agentic systems. “Open innovation is the foundation of AI progress,” founder and CEO Jensen Huang said at the time.

Google hasn't dropped out of the race: according to Hugging Face, its open models, along with releases from Microsoft, IBM Granite and older OpenAI models, together draw hundreds of millions of downloads every year. Meta's position weakened in 2026, the same data show — Qwen (Alibaba) derivatives now outnumber Meta's entire model output 2.6 times over.
