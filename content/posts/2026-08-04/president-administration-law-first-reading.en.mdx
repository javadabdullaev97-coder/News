The Legislative Chamber of the Oliy Majlis on August 4 passed in first reading a draft constitutional law "On the Administration of the President of the Republic of Uzbekistan," which shifts regulation of the Administration's status from a presidential decree to the level of law.

## What the Bill Sets Out

The document establishes the legal status of the Administration, its main tasks, the procedure for organizing its work and its powers, [according to a post](https://t.me/qonunchilikpalatasi/37129) on the official Telegram channel of the Legislative Chamber.

The bill also spells out the Administration's structure and operating principles. In addition, it defines mechanisms for interacting with state bodies and organizations, as well as guarantees for the activities of its officials and staff, [according to another post on the same channel](https://t.me/qonunchilikpalatasi/37130).

The document separately [regulates](https://t.me/qonunchilikpalatasi/37130) how the Administration interacts with compliance-control and internal anti-corruption control units of state bodies and organizations.

After discussion, deputies [passed the bill](https://t.me/qonunchilikpalatasi/37131) in first reading — the document still needs further work ahead of the second reading.

## Legal Basis — Amendments to the Constitution

The bill was drafted on the basis of Article 109 of the Constitution — as it reads in the version on lex.uz as of [July 25, 2026](https://lex.uz/docs/6445147). It provides that the president forms his own Administration to provide organizational, legal and analytical support for exercising his powers. Its legal status, tasks, procedure for organizing its work and powers are defined by law. The same article of the Constitution establishes that the president forms the Administration and appoints and dismisses its head.

Uzbekistan previously had no separate law on the Presidential Administration. Its structure and operations were last regulated by a [presidential decree](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/4918865) of August 29, 2019, "On Measures to Improve the Efficiency of the Administration of the President of the Republic of Uzbekistan."
