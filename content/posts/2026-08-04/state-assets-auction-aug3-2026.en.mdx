The State Assets Management Agency of the Republic of Uzbekistan on August 3 [sold at an online auction](https://t.me/davaktivuz/6191) 645 of 1,307 listed lots for a total of 103.6 billion soums, against a starting price of 108.2 billion soums.

## What Was Sold

The total value of the deals came to 103.6 billion soums (≈$8.66 million at the Central Bank rate of 11,960.18 soums per dollar on August 3) — 4.6 billion soums below the lots' starting price. A total of 1,668 legal entities and individuals took part in the bidding, [the agency said](https://t.me/davaktivuz/6191) on its Telegram channel.

The lots sold included land plots, agricultural land, leased and state enterprise property, assets sold by court decision, and other assets. The agency listed these categories without breaking down the number of lots in each.

## For Comparison

At the previous auction on July 31, the agency [listed](https://t.me/davaktivuz/6191) 848 lots, of which 461 were sold for a total of 128.8 billion soums, against a starting price of 113.3 billion soums — that auction drew 1,043 participants.

The August 3 auction thus drew more participants and offered more lots than the sale four days earlier, but the total sale amount was lower.

The State Assets Management Agency regularly holds online auctions through its own electronic platform.
