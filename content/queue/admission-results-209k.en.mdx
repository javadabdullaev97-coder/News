The Ministry of Higher Education, Science and Innovation held a [news conference](https://gov.uz/oz/edu/news/view/207479) on August 18 on the results of admissions for the 2026/2027 academic year: based on test results, 389,100 applicants advanced to the selection stage.

This year, 677,900 applicants submitted online applications for bachelor's programs, the ministry [said in a statement](https://gov.uz/oz/edu/news/view/207479). Tests were held in 64 major buildings across the country, with video surveillance and live internet broadcasting.

About 13,000 applicants with national or international certificates, plus another 302 people under established benefits, were exempted from testing.

## How many were recommended for admission — no official confirmation

More than 209,000 applicants were recommended for admission — the figure was cited by First Deputy Minister Sardor Rajabov at the August 18 news conference. This is according to information cited by several outlets; there is no official confirmation as of publication — the final statistics section on the State Testing Center's website (uzbmb.uz) is not loading.

According to the same data, the bachelor's admission quota rose to approximately 202,000 places — several thousand more than a year earlier. Of these, about 36,000 places were allocated for grants. Passing scores for both grant-funded and fee-paying places also rose compared with 2025, outlets reported, citing the same remarks.

Applications for dormitory placement for applicants recommended for admission opened on August 18 and can be filed through public service centers or the my.gov.uz portal, the [Ministry of Higher Education said](https://gov.uz/oz/edu/news/view/207479).

We have sent a request to the Ministry of Higher Education, Science and Innovation asking it to confirm the number of applicants recommended for admission, the quota size and the passing scores; the response will be added to this article.
