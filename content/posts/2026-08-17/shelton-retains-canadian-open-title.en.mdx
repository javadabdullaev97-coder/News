American Ben Shelton [beat](https://www.atptour.com/en/news/shelton-nakashima-montreal-2026-thursday-final) compatriot Brandon Nakashima 6-3, 7-6(4) in the National Bank Open final in Montreal on August 13, winning an ATP Masters 1000 title for the second year in a row. A year ago he won in Toronto; this time it was Montreal.

Shelton did not lose a single set during the tournament and did not face a single break point in the final, Tennis Majors [noted](https://www.tennismajors.com/atp/shelton-defends-montreal-crown-joins-elite-open-era-club-with-gritty-win-over-nakashima-858490.html). He won 83% of points on serve, hit 22 winners against 12 unforced errors; Nakashima had 19 winners and 21 errors.

The win was Shelton's seventh career title and second at Masters 1000 level. His head-to-head record against Nakashima improved to 6-0. Shelton climbed four spots to return to No. 6 in the ATP rankings after the tournament, ATP Tour [said](https://www.atptour.com/en/news/shelton-pif-atp-rankings-14-august-2026). The Montreal champion earned $1,151,420 (about €997,000 at the approximate exchange rate on the match date), ATP Tour [said](https://www.atptour.com/en/news/montreal-2026-champion-prize-money).

## Seventh since Nadal

Shelton became the seventh Open Era player to defend a Canadian Open title, Tennis Majors [wrote](https://www.tennismajors.com/atp/shelton-defends-montreal-crown-joins-elite-open-era-club-with-gritty-win-over-nakashima-858490.html). Before him, only Ivan Lendl, John McEnroe, Andre Agassi, Andy Murray, Novak Djokovic and Rafael Nadal had done it. Nadal was the last to complete the double, winning the tournament in 2018 and 2019.

The Montreal final was the first all-American Masters 1000 final since 2003, when Andy Roddick beat Mardy Fish in Cincinnati, The Guardian [reported](https://www.theguardian.com/sport/2026/aug/13/ben-shelton-brandon-nakashima-canadian-open-final-tennis). Shelton became the first American to win the Canadian Masters 1000 since Roddick's 2003 win.

## What's next

Shelton's next tournament is the Cincinnati Open, an ATP Masters 1000 event running August 13-23 in Cincinnati, ATP Tour [said](https://www.atptour.com/en/news/cincinnati-2026-atp-masters-1000-history-draw-schedule).
