According to Bloomberg, Broadcom held talks on August 20 on debt financing of up to $100 billion — the money is meant to support the growth of Anthropic PBC and other AI companies.

Bloomberg [reported](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-20/broadcom-seeks-more-than-60-billion-in-latest-ai-debt-deal), citing sources, that the debt is intended for growth in Anthropic's computing capacity and unnamed "other companies."

According to [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/08/20/broadcom-reportedly-seeking-up-to-100b-in-debt-financing-for-ai-chip-deal/), those companies may include OpenAI Group PBC — Bloomberg does not name it directly.

The amount consists of two tranches. The junior debt will total about $30 billion, while the senior secured tranche will be $60–70 billion, part of which Broadcom itself may guarantee, [SiliconANGLE writes](https://siliconangle.com/2026/08/20/broadcom-reportedly-seeking-up-to-100b-in-debt-financing-for-ai-chip-deal/).

Broadcom expects to raise part of the funds from Blackstone and Apollo Global Management, its partners in the June 2026 deal, [SiliconANGLE writes](https://siliconangle.com/2026/08/20/broadcom-reportedly-seeking-up-to-100b-in-debt-financing-for-ai-chip-deal/). At the time, the three companies created the AI XPV investment platform and provided Anthropic with $35 billion for data center construction, [SiliconANGLE reports](https://siliconangle.com/2026/08/20/broadcom-reportedly-seeking-up-to-100b-in-debt-financing-for-ai-chip-deal/).
