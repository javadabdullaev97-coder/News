President Shavkat Mirziyoyev on August 17 opened 12 projects worth $176 million and launched construction of 15 more projects worth $7.42 billion in Khorezm region. [The presidential press service reported the news](https://t.me/shmirziyoyev/35096).

The new capacities cover industry, tourism, energy and construction. Their implementation will create about 6,600 jobs, [the presidential press service reported](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9506).

The largest of the new projects is a $6 billion plant producing eco-friendly aviation fuel in Tuprokkala district. Investors include partners from the United Arab Emirates and Chinese company Sinopec, according to the [statement](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9506).

Other projects in the package include $170 million bioethanol production with Slovak partners and the $120 million "Sahro Yulduzi" tourism complex. A waste-processing project generating 71 MW of "green" energy was also announced — an investor from South Korea will invest $100 million. A hotel and retail complex with Japanese partners is valued at $30 million.

## International tourist town project presented in Khiva

Mirziyoyev was [presented with a project](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9507) for an international tourist town in Khiva. The 12.3-hectare complex will house hotels, a museum, restaurants serving national and international cuisine, retail outlets and service businesses.

The architectural concept was developed based on the layout of the ancient city of Vazir, the first capital of the Khiva Khanate since 1511. The project envisions green areas, cascading fountains, internal canals and parking lots with solar panels.

The project will create thousands of jobs, the presidential press service reported. The project's cost and investor were not disclosed in the statement.

## Khiva opens Congress Hall, hotel and Reception House

The president [inspected three new facilities](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9508) in Khiva, built under a decree dated April 30, 2026.

The 1.9-hectare Congress Hall for forums and conferences cost 857 billion soums (≈$72.1 million at the central bank's exchange rate for August 17). Nearby, a three-star hotel with 156 rooms and 310 beds was built for 169 billion soums (≈$14.2 million) — the project created 60 jobs. The 2-hectare "Khiva Ichan-Qala" Reception House cost 260 billion soums (≈$21.9 million).

According to the press service, the three facilities will provide about 2,000 jobs and up to 50,000 tourists annually, while service exports will grow to $4 million.

## Khorezm technical school and Chinese university branch

The president [visited the Khorezm Advanced Vocational Skills Technical School](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9503) in Urgench. On a 5.2-hectare site, two academic buildings, a dormitory, a gym and a cafeteria were built for 72.4 billion soums (≈$6.1 million). In the 2026–2027 academic year, the school enrolled 200 students in 11 fields with elements of dual education under programs from Germany, Switzerland, the United Kingdom, China and South Korea.

A branch of China's Liaoning Normal University [opened in Khiva](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9505). The project was implemented jointly with the Ministry of Higher Education, Science and Innovation and Urgench State University named after Abu Raykhan Beruni. Classes are taught in Uzbek, Chinese and English in fields including "International Education in Chinese," "Tourism Management," "International Business" and "Artificial Intelligence." Enrollment in 2026 will total 160 students, with plans to raise it to 500 a year and 2,000 students in total.

## Urgench airport linked by rail for the first time in the country

Mirziyoyev [inspected a new railway line](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9504) that for the first time in Uzbekistan directly connected Urgench International Airport with Khiva's railway station. The 11-kilometer line cost 225 billion soums (≈$18.9 million), travel time dropped from 80 to 40 minutes, and the new route is designed for 650,000 tourists a year.
