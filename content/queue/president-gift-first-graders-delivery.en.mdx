The Ministry of Preschool and School Education of the Republic of Uzbekistan began delivering "Presidential Gift" school kits to first-graders across the regions on August 5 — sets of 12 items for the 2026/2027 school year.

## How the Delivery Began

The send-off ceremony was [timed](https://t.me/uzedu/44619) to coincide with a press tour for journalists at the Supply and Logistics Service enterprise under the ministry — it was from there that the caravan carrying "Presidential Gift" kits set off for the country's regions. The kit for first-graders consists of 12 types of school supplies.

After the caravan departed, participants of the press tour moved to School No. 202 in Tashkent's Chilanzar district. There, ministry representatives [discussed](https://t.me/uzedu/44619) preparations for the new school year: the process of enrolling children in first grade, updated curricula and textbooks, the weight limit for first-graders' school backpacks, and the work of school-readiness groups. The ministry [published](https://t.me/uzedu/44629) a video of the press tour in a separate post.

## What Remains Unknown

The published statements did not specify the exact list of items in the kit, nor the total number of first-graders and regions covered by the program this year. The funding source and the delivery completion timeline were not disclosed.
