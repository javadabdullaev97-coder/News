The Ministry of Economy and Finance of the Republic of Uzbekistan said on August 5 that the Pension Fund now requests documents itself and transfers payments to the personal accounts of inmates serving sentences in settlement colonies.

## What changed

Previously, assigning a pension, recalculating it, resuming suspended payments, or switching from one type of pension to another required collecting documents on employment record and income. These had to be submitted separately to different agencies.

Now the district (city) branch of the Pension Fund where the settlement colony is located requests the necessary information itself, through interagency data exchange, without additional applications from the inmate or the facility's administration, according to [an explainer from the Ministry of Economy and Finance](https://telegra.ph/Ijtimoiy-adolat-yolidagi-muhim-ozgarish-08-03).

Pensions are now also paid in cashless form — the money is transferred to the inmate's personal account rather than handed out in cash, the ministry notes.

The Ministry of Economy and Finance did not specify in its publication the exact date from which this administrative procedure has been applied in practice or the number of the document that introduced it.

## Who this affects

This concerns people serving custodial sentences specifically in settlement colonies. The procedure for paying pensions to this category is set out in [Article 53-1 of the Law "On State Pension Provision for Citizens"](https://lex.uz/docs/112312): the sums are transferred by district (city) branches of the Pension Fund where the settlement colony is located to the personal accounts of the inmates.

According to the Ministry of Economy and Finance, 2.08 billion soums (about $174,000 at the Central Bank exchange rate as of August 4) was allocated for the social protection of people in settlement colonies in 2025, and another 891.9 million soums (about $74,000) in the first quarter of 2026.

## Legal basis

The ability to count work performed while serving a sentence toward one's employment record has existed in Uzbek law for several years. Item "k" of part one of Article 37 of the Law "On State Pension Provision for Citizens" of September 3, 1993, No. 938-XII was added by [Law of February 15, 2023, No. ZRU-817](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/6384475), "On Amendments and Additions to Certain Legislative Acts of the Republic of Uzbekistan Aimed at Reliably Protecting the Rights and Legitimate Interests of Convicted Persons." The provision reads: the total employment record includes "any work performed by persons sentenced to deprivation of liberty while serving their sentence in penal institutions," provided the institution paid the social tax for that period.

The general procedure for assigning and paying state pensions is governed by the [Regulation approved by Cabinet of Ministers Resolution No. 592 of October 13, 2022](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/6236070).

## What's next

This is the third pension-related post the Ministry of Economy and Finance has published in a week: in late July and early August, the ministry also highlighted the [extension of the period for claiming unclaimed pensions from 12 to 24 months](https://t.me/minecofinuz/16170) and the [crediting of up to 6 years of childcare leave toward work record, up from the previous 3 years](https://t.me/minecofinuz/16178).
