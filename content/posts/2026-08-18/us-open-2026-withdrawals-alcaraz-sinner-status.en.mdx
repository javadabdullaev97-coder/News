Tennis365 reported Monday, August 18, that nine tennis players have now withdrawn from US Open 2026, and the status of defending champion Carlos Alcaraz has come into question for the first time.

On the women's tour, Victoria Mboko (world No. 12) and Hailey Baptiste (No. 32) will miss the tournament — both with serious knee injuries. Emma Raducanu (No. 49) has a stress fracture. They are joined by Ajla Tomljanovic (No. 95), Varvara Gracheva (No. 102), Irina-Camelia Begu (No. 472), Jaqueline Cristian (No. 41) and Laura Siegemund (No. 89); Tennis365 does not specify the nature of their injuries. On the men's tour, Sebastian Korda (No. 50) will miss the tournament after undergoing back surgery for sciatica.

## Alcaraz and Sinner Status Remain in Question

Carlos Alcaraz, the defending US Open champion, has not played since April 2026. A wrist injury sustained at the Barcelona tournament forced the Spaniard to skip Roland Garros and the entire grass-court season, including Wimbledon. [He withdrew on August 4](https://www.tsn.ca/tennis/article/alcaraz-withdraws-from-cincinnati-open-because-of-lingering-wrist-injury/) from the Cincinnati tournament. Tournament director Bob Moran said: "We know that Carlos is doing everything he can to get back to tournaments as soon as possible."

World No. 1 Jannik Sinner [withdrew from Cincinnati on August 9](https://sports.yahoo.com/tennis/article/world-no-1-jannik-sinner-withdraws-from-cincinnati-open-puts-us-open-availability-into-question-165501434.html) due to a knee injury; his participation in the US Open remains in question until he recovers. LEAP News [previously reported](https://leap.uz/ru/2026/08/18/sinner-us-open-participation-doubt) on this in more detail.

Dane Holger Rune, who injured his knee at the Stockholm tournament in October 2025, does not intend to withdraw from the US Open. "I haven't withdrawn from the tournament. I'm working very hard every day, and once I'm ready — I'm ready. I hope that will be the US Open," Rune wrote on social media; the quote is cited by Tennis365.

Alcaraz [remains on the tournament's entry list](https://www.tsn.ca/tennis/article/alcaraz-withdraws-from-cincinnati-open-because-of-lingering-wrist-injury/), TSN confirms; neither he nor Sinner has officially announced withdrawal from the US Open, but both players' participation remains in question until they recover.

The US Open 2026 main draw [starts on August 30](https://www.tennismajors.com/us-open-news/us-open-2026-dates-schedule-full-calendar-858738.html), with the men's final scheduled for September 13.
