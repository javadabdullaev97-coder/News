The Senate of the Oliy Majlis of Uzbekistan reviewed at a plenary session on August 8 a plan to abolish Samarkand district and change the administrative boundaries of the city of Samarkand, as well as the Akdarya, Pastdargom, and Tayloq districts of the region.

## What the Senate Reviewed

The Senate [reviewed a proposal](https://senat.uz/ru/plenary-sessions/post-5884) to incorporate part of the land of Samarkand district into the city of Samarkand and abolish the district as a separate administrative-territorial unit. The remaining land of the district is proposed to be transferred to Tayloq district.

Senators discussed the question of abolishing Samarkand district and changing the boundaries of the Akdarya, Pastdargom, and Tayloq districts together with the city of Samarkand at a [plenary session](https://t.me/senatuz/46689) of the upper house of parliament — the official Telegram channel of the Senate published the corresponding recording the same day.

## How Such Decisions Are Formalized

Changes to the boundaries of districts and cities in Uzbekistan are carried out under the [Law "On the Administrative-Territorial Structure of the Republic of Uzbekistan"](https://lex.uz/docs/4973063) — such decisions are formalized as amendments to the region's administrative-territorial division after review and approval by both chambers of parliament, and take effect after official publication.

The Senate has not yet published the results of its review or any related follow-up documents.
