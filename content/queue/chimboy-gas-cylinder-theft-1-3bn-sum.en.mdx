The Department for Combating Economic Crimes under the Prosecutor General's Office of Uzbekistan reported on August 3 the theft of 1,406 liquefied gas cylinders worth 1.3 billion soums in Chimboy District, Karakalpakstan.

## How the theft was uncovered

According to the [Department's report](https://t.me/Department_uz/12941), the agency's Chimboy District unit conducted the pre-investigation inquiry. It found that materially responsible persons of the supply department at the "Chimboytumangaz" enterprise had misappropriated 1,406 liquefied gas cylinders worth a total of 1.3 billion soums. That is about $108,700 at the [Central Bank of Uzbekistan exchange rate](https://cbu.uz/ru/arkhiv-kursov-valyut/json/USD/2026-08-03/) for August 3 (11,960.18 soums per dollar). The Department opened a criminal case under Article 167 of the Criminal Code ("misappropriation or embezzlement of another's property through theft").

The Department did not name the individuals involved or their positions, nor the period over which the cylinder shortage was identified. According to the investigation, the theft took the form of misappropriation — meaning the cylinders were not physically stolen from outside, but were written off or withheld by employees responsible for accounting for them. The investigation is ongoing; until a court verdict takes legal effect, all persons involved are presumed innocent.

## Third gas cylinder theft case in Karakalpakstan in one day

The Chimboy case is already the third of its kind that the Department has reported in Karakalpakstan on August 3. Earlier the same day, the agency [reported](https://t.me/Department_uz/12933) the misappropriation of 781 cylinders worth 741 million soums (≈$62,000) by employees of "Kegeylitumangaz" in Kegeyli District. The Department also [uncovered](https://t.me/Department_uz/12934) the theft of 1,067 cylinders worth 1 billion soums (≈$83,600) in Takhtakupyr District, at the "Takhtakupyrtumangaz" unit.

The Department opened all three cases under the same Article 167 of the Criminal Code. The total reported damage across the three districts of Karakalpakstan exceeds 3 billion soums (≈$254,000).

## What's next

The Department did not disclose a timeline for completing the investigation into the Chimboy case. For the two previously disclosed gas cylinder theft cases in Karakalpakstan, the agency likewise did not specify whether the case materials had been referred to court.
