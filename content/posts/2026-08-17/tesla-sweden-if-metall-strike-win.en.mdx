Sweden's IF Metall union announced on August 13 that it would end its strike against Tesla — the standoff will officially stop at 00:01 on Wednesday, August 19, after 1,021 days. It is the longest strike in Sweden's modern history, [Electrek notes](https://electrek.co/2026/08/13/tesla-defeats-sweden-if-metall-union-strike/).

According to the union, the reason for ending the strike is that Tesla bought out everyone still on the picket lines, leaving no one left to strike. "This means the conflict no longer has any effect," [IF Metall said in a statement](https://www.ifmetall.se/aktuellt/nyheter/fran-forbundet/2026/augusti/if-metall-avbryter-konfliktatgarder-mot-tesla/).

IF Metall's collective bargaining secretary Simon Petersson said Tesla's position is unprecedented for the Swedish labor market. "Tesla is so opposed to collective agreements that it prefers to buy out employees — union members — rather than give them protected working conditions," [the same statement said](https://www.ifmetall.se/aktuellt/nyheter/fran-forbundet/2026/augusti/if-metall-avbryter-konfliktatgarder-mot-tesla/). IF Metall chairwoman Marie Nilsson called Tesla's former employees "union heroes" who fought for a collective agreement against a company with such strong anti-union sentiment.

## How the Conflict Began

The strike began on October 27, 2023. About 120 mechanics at seven Tesla repair shops in Sweden stopped working at the time, after the company refused to sign a collective agreement (kollektivavtal), [Electrek reports](https://electrek.co/2026/08/13/tesla-defeats-sweden-if-metall-union-strike/). Such an agreement regulates working conditions at most companies in Sweden. Tesla never signed the agreement once during the three years of the conflict, [the outlet notes](https://electrek.co/2026/08/13/tesla-defeats-sweden-if-metall-union-strike/).

According to IF Metall, the strike was supported throughout the conflict by other unions in Sweden and other countries, [the union's press release said](https://www.ifmetall.se/aktuellt/nyheter/fran-forbundet/2026/augusti/if-metall-avbryter-konfliktatgarder-mot-tesla/).

## Response From the International Union Federation

IndustriALL General Secretary Atle Høie said Tesla appears to have no limit to what the company is willing to do. The goal is to keep unions out of its facilities, [IndustriALL said in a statement](https://www.industriall-union.org/if-metall-ends-tesla-strike). "Unfortunately, they managed to buy their way out in Sweden. At least it put more money in the pockets of former IF Metall members. But the lack of protection from having no union and no collective agreement hasn't gone away," [Høie said](https://www.industriall-union.org/if-metall-ends-tesla-strike).

Tesla's position on the end of the strike was not found in verifiable sources at the time of publication.

## What's Next

The end of all the union's actions against Tesla takes effect at 00:01 local time on Wednesday, August 19, [IF Metall says](https://www.ifmetall.se/aktuellt/nyheter/fran-forbundet/2026/augusti/if-metall-avbryter-konfliktatgarder-mot-tesla/).
