A suicide bomber detonated explosives on August 2 at the entrance to a police station in the town of Kabal in Pakistan's Swat Valley, killing at least 14 people, including five police officers, and wounding 22.

## Explosion at the Station Entrance

The attacker detonated the explosives after police tried to stop and search him at the station entrance. This was [reported](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/2/suicide-bomber-kills-14-outside-police-station-in-pakistans-swat) by Swat district police officer Umar Khan. Kabal is located in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa province in the country's northwest, near the border with Afghanistan.

The blast occurred at an intersection near hundreds of rally participants. The number of injured — 22 people — was given by Fida Hussain, deputy inspector general of Swat police. The wounded were taken to a hospital in nearby Saidu Sharif.

Among the dead are nine civilians and five police officers, [Interfax reports](https://www.interfax.ru/world/1107070). Kommersant [also reports](https://www.kommersant.ru/doc/8861028) the same figures — five dead police officers and 22 wounded — adding that, according to its information, the bomber tried to enter the station grounds but was stopped for a search at the gate.

Pakistan's Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi condemned the attack and expressed condolences to the families of the victims.

## Rally Against Rising Violence

By the time of the blast, hundreds of people had gathered at the intersection demanding that authorities take action against rising violence in the region. The explosion occurred during the final speaker's address. Speakers accused authorities of pressuring civilians even as militants operate freely in the nearby mountains.

"We have seen years of bloodshed. We don't want another generation of widows and orphans," one of the rally speakers said, [according to](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/2/suicide-bomber-kills-14-outside-police-station-in-pakistans-swat) Al Jazeera, citing the Pakistani outlet Dawn.

Swat Valley was a stronghold of the Tehrik-i-Taliban Pakistan (TTP) group until militants were driven out by a 2009 military operation, Al Jazeera [notes](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/2/suicide-bomber-kills-14-outside-police-station-in-pakistans-swat). In recent months, militants have started reappearing in the region, prompting protests from local residents, [Interfax notes](https://www.interfax.ru/world/1107070).

## Responsibility and Statistics

No one had claimed responsibility for the attack as of publication time, Kommersant [writes](https://www.kommersant.ru/doc/8861028).

The TTP has carried out attacks in Swat district for years — bombings, targeted killings and assaults on police posts. In late July, the group claimed responsibility for a car bomb attack in Tank district that killed 27 soldiers, Al Jazeera [reports](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/2/suicide-bomber-kills-14-outside-police-station-in-pakistans-swat). No TTP involvement in the Kabal bombing has been claimed.

In July, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa province recorded 154 terrorism-related deaths — more than double the June figure. The data comes from the Pakistan Institute for Conflict and Security Studies, [cited](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/2/suicide-bomber-kills-14-outside-police-station-in-pakistans-swat) by Al Jazeera.
