In January-June 2026, 23,409 citizens of Uzbekistan visited Vietnam for tourism purposes — 7,200 people, or 44.7%, more than in the same period of 2025. The National Statistics Committee of the Republic of Uzbekistan [reported](https://t.me/uzstataxborot/69186) the figures.

## How trip purposes broke down

Of the total trips, most were for leisure travel — 22,030 people. Another 1,212 citizens traveled to Vietnam to visit relatives, 157 people for medical treatment, and 10 people on business trips. The National Statistics Committee [provided](https://stat.uz/ru/press-tsentr/novosti-goskomstata/69682-zbekistonliklarning-v-etnamga-safarlari-44-7-foizga-oshdi-3) this breakdown in a publication on its website.

Tourism trips accounted for about 94% of all trips by Uzbekistan citizens to Vietnam in the first half of the year.
