The National Statistics Committee of the Republic of Uzbekistan said on August 4 that the country produced 235,794 passenger cars in January-June 2026 — 26.8 thousand more than a year earlier.

Domestic sales accelerated over the same period: the Center for Economic Research and Reforms (CERR) recorded an 18% increase in transactions in June compared with May, while demand for electric vehicles jumped 63.5% during the month.

## Cobalt remains the most popular model

According to [National Statistics Committee data on Telegram](https://t.me/uzstataxborot/69166), the breakdown by brand of the 235,794 passenger cars produced in January-June 2026 was as follows:

- Cobalt — 82,951 units;
- Damas — 42,663;
- Tracker — 23,249;
- Onix — 18,715;
- KIA — 15,184;
- BYD — 13,405;
- Haval — 5,061;
- Chery — 4,384;
- Chevrolet Damas Move — 600;
- special-purpose passenger vehicles — 29,582.

The committee did not give a production growth rate in percentage terms in this report — only the absolute difference from January-June 2025, 26.8 thousand cars.

## Primary market sales rose 3.5% over the half-year

A [CERR report](https://cerr.uz/show-news/v-iyune-aktivnost-vrosla-vo-vsex-segmentax-avtornka-ceir) from July 30, which the center also [presented on its Telegram channel](https://t.me/cerruz/2805) on August 3, describes the dynamics of sales of vehicles registered with road traffic safety authorities.

In June 2026, Uzbekistan sold more than 80.2 thousand motor vehicles of all types — 18% more than in May. Activity rose in almost all regions except Jizzakh Region, where sales fell 2.2%. The market grew fastest in Surkhandarya (+33.7%), Khorezm (+32.6%) and Syrdarya (+29%) regions. In Tashkent, vehicle sales rose 9% to 17.9 thousand units.

Of this total, passenger car sales in June amounted to 61.4 thousand units (+14% compared with May):

- on the primary market, 24 thousand new cars were sold (+11%), of which 18.8 thousand were domestically produced (+12%) and 5.1 thousand were imported (+7%);
- on the secondary market, more than 37 thousand used cars were sold (+16%).

Over the entire January-June 2026 period, new passenger car sales rose 3.5% year on year, to 157 thousand units. The secondary market over the same half-year, however, contracted 18% year on year, according to CERR — to approximately 240 thousand transactions, despite the monthly growth in June.

## Electric vehicle demand rose 40% over the half-year

Electric vehicle sales on the primary and secondary markets reached 8.4 thousand units in June — 63.5% more than in May. In the first half of 2026, Uzbekistan sold more than 40 thousand electric vehicles — 40% more than in January-June 2025, [CERR reported](https://cerr.uz/show-news/v-iyune-aktivnost-vrosla-vo-vsex-segmentax-avtornka-ceir).

Demand for electric vehicles rose in almost all regions in June — a 2.9% decline was recorded only in Syrdarya Region. The largest growth was recorded in Jizzakh (+101%), Bukhara (+64.6%) and Fergana (+58.5%) regions. The bulk of electric vehicle sales still comes from Tashkent: the capital accounted for about 63% of the market in June, and sales in the city grew 78% during the month, to 5.3 thousand units.
