OpenAI on August 20 [released](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/changelog) an
Apple Messages plugin for ChatGPT on macOS — it reads, searches and sends
iMessage, SMS and RCS messages through the system Messages app.

The plugin is available on all ChatGPT tiers in the Mac desktop app and
also works in ChatGPT Work and Codex, [according to the company's
changelog](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/changelog). Sending messages is
protected by confirmation: "ChatGPT only sends a message after you approve
its text and recipients," [OpenAI
says](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/changelog).

## Privacy concerns

[Bloomberg
reported](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-20/chatgpt-can-now-control-imessage-potentially-raising-apple-privacy-concerns)
that the new feature could create privacy problems for Apple as the maker
of the iPhone. According to the agency, ChatGPT can now read, write and
send text messages. The plugin also searches conversations and produces
summaries of chats in the Messages app, [Bloomberg
said](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-20/chatgpt-can-now-control-imessage-potentially-raising-apple-privacy-concerns).
Apple itself has not publicly commented on the assessment.

Access to the plugin's messaging permissions can be revoked, [OpenAI
notes](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/changelog) — the process is
described in a separate plugin guide.
