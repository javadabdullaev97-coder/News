Uzbekistan's President Shavkat Mirziyoyev [held](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9491) a phone call with U.S. President Donald Trump on August 7, after which he again invited him to visit Uzbekistan on an official visit.

## What was discussed

At the start of the call, Mirziyoyev once again [congratulated](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9491) Trump on the 250th anniversary of U.S. independence. According to the press service of the President of Uzbekistan, Trump in response noted the "unprecedented level" of Uzbekistan-U.S. relations and the "close, trust-based dialogue" that has developed between the leaders of the two countries.

The sides [confirmed](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9491) the consistent implementation of agreements reached at the highest level and discussed prospects for developing the strategic partnership. The press service noted the implementation of the three-year Program of Economic Cooperation and the first meeting of the U.S.-Uzbekistan Business and Investment Council.

The presidents also [discussed](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9491) expanding ties between Uzbekistan's regions and U.S. states and noted that the visa-free regime introduced for U.S. citizens is helping to boost business contacts. Among the sectors for joint projects, the press service [named](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9491) civil aviation, automotive manufacturing, mining, infrastructure, energy, metallurgy, artificial intelligence and digital technology.

Separately, the sides [emphasized](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9491) the importance of efforts to ensure global security and the peaceful settlement of international and regional conflicts. The statement does not name specific conflicts or countries. The leaders also [reviewed](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9491) the schedule of upcoming bilateral and multilateral events. The statement does not specify which events are meant.

At the end of the call, Mirziyoyev [invited](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9491) Trump to visit Uzbekistan on an official visit "at a convenient time."

## A repeat invitation

This is not the first time the invitation has been extended. A similar phone call between the two presidents took place on [September 5, 2025](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/8465) — at the time, Mirziyoyev also invited Trump to visit Uzbekistan "at a convenient time." No official visit has taken place since then.

The official text on president.uz, as of publication, does not contain specific figures on trade turnover or investment volume between the two countries, nor exact dates for a possible Trump visit to Tashkent. No statement from the American side — the White House — on the call had been issued as of publication.
