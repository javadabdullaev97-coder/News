The Ministry of Health reminded on August 3 about a benefit for children of medics and pharmacists: starting January 1, 2026, the state covers part of university tuition if a parent has at least 15 years of service.

## How Much Is Covered and Who Qualifies

The rule is set out in [Presidential Resolution](https://lex.uz/docs/7821920) No. PQ-336 of November 9, 2025, "On Measures to Support Medical and Pharmaceutical Workers." Children of medical and pharmaceutical workers at state healthcare institutions with at least 15 years of service have 30% of the annual bachelor's tuition fee covered at universities in Uzbekistan — but no more than 15 base calculation values (BCV).

At the current BCV of 412,000 soums, in effect since February 1, 2026, the payment cap is 6,180,000 soums — about $517 at the [Central Bank of Uzbekistan](https://cbu.uz/ru/arkhiv-kursov-valyut/) (CBU) exchange rate for August 3 (11,960.18 soums per dollar). Starting September 1, the BCV will rise to 440,000 soums, raising the compensation cap to 6,600,000 soums (about $552 at the same rate).

The money is paid out of the State Targeted Healthcare Development Fund. The resolution does not cap the number of recipients per year — unlike the mortgage benefit in the same document, which is limited to 500 people.

## What Hasn't Been Explained Yet

Clause 9 of the resolution required the Ministry of Health to develop, within a month of the document's signing, criteria and a procedure for applying the benefit — that is, to determine where to submit an application and which documents are needed. The text of this regulation could not be found in open access.

In its [statement](https://t.me/ssvuz/22394) on August 3, the Ministry of Health did not specify either the application procedure or the list of required documents — the agency limited itself to referring to an explanatory video from its own press service, which promises answers to the questions "who is eligible for the benefit," "how large the subsidy is," and "how to apply."

## Other Support Measures for Medics From the Same Resolution

Resolution No. PQ-336 also introduced a package of other measures for medical and pharmaceutical workers at state institutions:

- a 25% down payment mortgage subsidy for 500 workers a year with at least 15 years of service;
- full payment for publications in high-ranking international scientific journals;
- one-time prizes for winners of a professional contest — 500 million soums to the institution (about $41,800 at the CBU rate for August 3) and 30 million soums to the employee (about $2,508);
- a free sanatorium voucher once a year for those with at least 15 years of service;
- state healthcare institutions switching to a five-day work week starting April 1, 2026.
