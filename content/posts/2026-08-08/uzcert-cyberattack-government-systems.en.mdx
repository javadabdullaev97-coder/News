The UZCERT cyber incident response service at Uzbekistan's Cybersecurity Center [warned](https://t.me/uzcert_live/1357) on August 7 of mass attacks on government agencies and companies via the Remote Desktop Protocol (RDP) — data on some servers has been encrypted.

## What the Initial Analysis Found

According to [UZCERT's post](https://t.me/uzcert_live/1357), attackers are conducting public attacks on RDP services of computer and server systems belonging to Uzbekistan organizations. The initial analysis found infections in information systems at several organizations, and in some cases, encryption of data on servers and workstations. The attackers use foreign IP addresses to manage the malicious C2 infrastructure, the service noted. The investigation is ongoing, and UZCERT has not yet named the exact number of affected organizations.

The same warning was also [published](https://t.me/cyber_csec_uz/2601) on the Telegram channel of the Cybersecurity Center State Unitary Enterprise — a state institution institutionally linked to the UZCERT service (they share the same contacts and address).

## What UZCERT Recommends to Organizations

The service [recommends](https://t.me/uzcert_live/1357) that government agencies and companies:

- disable RDP if it is not needed for operations, or restrict access to it via VPN or a list of trusted IP addresses;
- enable Network Level Authentication (NLA) and keep port 3389 closed;
- change administrator passwords and enable multi-factor authentication;
- check firewall, intrusion detection system, SIEM and EDR/XDR logs for signs of anomalous activity;
- immediately disconnect compromised systems from the network if infection is detected.

UZCERT asks that any detected incidents be reported to incident@uzcert.uz.

## Context: The 2026–2030 Cybersecurity Strategy

The warning comes against the backdrop of a national strategy in this field approved earlier this year. By [decree UP-38](https://lex.uz/ru/doc-passport/8079279) of March 10, 2026, the president approved the Cybersecurity Strategy of the Republic of Uzbekistan for 2026–2030: the document provides for the creation of separate cybersecurity units within the Ministry of Justice, the Ministry of Energy and the State Tax Committee starting April 1, 2026, as well as funding for protective measures from budgetary and extrabudgetary sources.

## What's Next

UZCERT continues to analyze the attacks and asks organizations that have detected signs of infection or data encryption to contact the service directly at incident@uzcert.uz. As of publication, the service had not provided updated figures on the number of affected organizations or the extent of damage.
