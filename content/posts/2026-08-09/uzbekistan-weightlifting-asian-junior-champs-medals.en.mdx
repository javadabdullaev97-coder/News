The Asian Weightlifting Championship for juniors and youth opened in Tashkent on August 7, and by August 9 Uzbekistan's national team had won two bronze medals.

## Who Won the Medals

Soliha Bahodirova brought Uzbekistan's team its first medal of the tournament on August 7 — bronze in the under-45kg category among junior women. She lifted 64kg in the snatch, the National Olympic Committee (NOC) of Uzbekistan [reported](https://olympic.uz/ru/news/olymp-news-312).

On August 8, Azizbek Dadajonov won the second bronze in the under-60kg junior men's category — he lifted 134kg in the clean and jerk to take third place. The Ministry of Sports of Uzbekistan [reported](https://t.me/Minsportuz/90423) the result, and the NOC [confirmed](https://olympic.uz/ru/news/olymp-news-315) it.

## About the Tournament

The Asian Weightlifting Championship for juniors and youth is being held in Tashkent from August 7 to 14 at the Olympic City sports complex, the NOC of Uzbekistan [reported](https://olympic.uz/ru/news/olymp-news-307). More than 300 athletes from 29 Asian countries are competing; on the first day of the tournament, medals were awarded in four weight categories.

On August 6, the day before the championship began, the Executive Committee of the Asian Weightlifting Federation (AWF) held a meeting in Tashkent, the NOC of Uzbekistan [reported](https://olympic.uz/ru/news/olymp-news-306). The meeting was attended by International Weightlifting Federation (IWF) President Mohammed Jalood and AWF President Mohammed Yousef Al Mana. On the Uzbekistan side, Minister of Sports Adham Ikramov, his deputy Shahrullo Mahmudov, and NOC Secretary General Oybek Kasimov took part. Participants discussed preparations for the Asian Games in Japan, the Asian Indoor and Martial Arts Games in Saudi Arabia, and the 2027 Asian Championship in the Republic of Korea.

During the tournament, Kyrgyzstan's Olympic champion and four-time world champion Kanibek Osmonaliev visited the competition venue as a guest. He was greeted by Deputy Minister of Sports Shahrullo Mahmudov, who presented the athlete with a national costume and souvenirs, according to a [post](https://t.me/Minsportuz/90432) on the Ministry of Sports' Telegram channel.

## What's Next

The competition continues through August 14, with medals to be awarded in additional weight categories among juniors and youth in the remaining days. Uzbekistan's final medal count will be known once the tournament concludes.
