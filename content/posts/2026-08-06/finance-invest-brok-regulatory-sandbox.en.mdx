The National Agency for Perspective Projects (NAPP) on August 5 [registered](https://t.me/napp_uz/2369) LLC Finance Invest Brok as a participant in the special legal regime known as the Regulatory Sandbox in the capital market — the company became the regime's 19th participant.

## What the company will do

Within the sandbox, Finance Invest Brok [plans](https://t.me/napp_uz/2369) to issue and place bonds in foreign currency, acting as an investment intermediary. The company is registered in Tashkent's Shaykhantakhur district and has already been [included](https://napp.uz/ru/pages/qimmatli-qog-ozlar-bozori-professional-ishtirokchilarining-reestri) in NAPP's registry of professional securities market participants.

## Who else is in the registry

Before Finance Invest Brok's registration, the capital market's special regime [registry](https://napp.uz/ru/pages/kapital-bozori-sohasidagi-maxsus-rejim-ishtirokchilari-reyestri) listed 18 companies — 12 local and 6 foreign. Local participants include Kapitalbank, Hamkorbank, Ipak Yuli, Asia Alliance Bank, Kapital Securities and Freedom Finance. Foreign organizations registered include Bank of Georgia, OTP Bank, TBC Capital, the Central Depository of Armenia, Auerbach Grayson & Company and Raiffeisen Bank International. With the addition of Finance Invest Brok, the number of participants rose to 19, 13 of them local companies.

## What the Regulatory Sandbox is

NAPP acts as the regulator of Uzbekistan's capital market and [introduced](https://napp.uz/ru/news/kapital-bozori-sohasida-qumdon) the special legal regime known as the Regulatory Sandbox, which allows participants to operate under separate rules. In particular, participants can carry out securities transactions and open accounts at the Central Bank of Uzbekistan in foreign and national currency without obtaining the full set of licenses otherwise required.
