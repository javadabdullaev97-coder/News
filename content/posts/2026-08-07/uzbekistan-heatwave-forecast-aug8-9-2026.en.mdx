Uzhydromet [has forecast](https://gov.uz/ru/hydromet/news/view/203285) a rise in air temperature across Uzbekistan to 37–40 degrees for the August 8–9 weekend, with highs of 41–42 degrees in places in the north, south and desert zone.

## Regional Forecast

According to the Agency of Hydrometeorological Service under the National Committee of the Republic of Uzbekistan for Ecology and Climate Change ([Uzhydromet](https://gov.uz/ru/hydromet/news/view/203285)), daytime temperatures across the republic today, August 7, are holding within 34–37 degrees. On Saturday and Sunday they will rise to 37–40 degrees, reaching 41–42 degrees in places in the north, south and desert zone. No precipitation is expected during this period, and cloud cover will be light and variable — except in mountainous areas, where brief thunderstorms are possible.

[Detailed forecast for August 8](https://t.me/uzgydromet/21899) by region:

- Tashkent (city): night 22–24°, day 36–38°, wind easterly 3–8 m/s.
- Karakalpakstan and Khorezm region: night 20–25°, day 35–40°, wind 7–12 m/s, gusting to 13–18 m/s in places.
- Bukhara and Navoi regions: night 20–25°, day 35–40°, wind 7–12 m/s, strengthening to 13–18 m/s in places.
- Tashkent, Samarkand, Jizzakh and Syrdarya regions: night 20–25°, day 33–38°, wind 7–12 m/s.
- Kashkadarya and Surkhandarya regions: night 20–25°, day 35–40°, wind 9–14 m/s.
- Andijan, Namangan and Fergana regions: night 20–25°, day 33–38°, wind 5–10 m/s.
- Mountainous areas: night 13–18°, day 23–28°, wind 7–12 m/s, gusting to 13–18 m/s in places.

## Wind and Mudflow Risk

Uzhydromet [has warned](https://gov.uz/ru/hydromet/news/view/203285) that on August 8–9 wind in the north and desert zone of the republic may strengthen to 13–18 m/s, with blowing dust in places. In the foothill and mountainous areas of the Tashkent region and the Fergana Valley regions — Andijan, Namangan and Fergana — the risk of mudflows and flash floods remains.

## Weather in Tashkent

For the capital, [Uzhydromet](https://gov.uz/ru/hydromet/news/view/203285) issued a separate forecast: no precipitation is expected, with daytime temperatures around 36 degrees today, rising to 37–39 degrees over the weekend. Nighttime temperatures will hold within 21–24 degrees, with easterly wind at 3–8 m/s.
