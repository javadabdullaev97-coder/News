Uzbekistan's Ministry of Energy [has been running](https://t.me/minenergy_uz/20665) a survey on the performance of hotlines 1154 and 1104 since early August: four days in, 44% of 2,270 surveyed consumers rated operator service as unsatisfactory.

## Which complaints dominate

Over four days, 2,270 consumers from all 14 regions of the country took part in the survey, the ministry [said](https://t.me/minenergy_uz/20678). More than half of the complaints — 54% — concerned power outages, 18% involved gas supply issues, and the remaining 28% covered other topics.

## How respondents rated service quality

Survey participants separately rated the operators' own performance: 33% gave a rating of "excellent" or "good," 24% rated it "satisfactory," and 44% found the service unsatisfactory. According to [the ministry](https://t.me/minenergy_uz/20678), operators fully or partially resolved the complaint, or at least provided information, in 54% of cases. The remaining 42% of complaints went unresolved.

51% of respondents managed to reach an operator in under five minutes, 29% waited more than five minutes, and 49% said they could not get through at all. The ministry noted that some sections of the survey allowed multiple answers, so the shares for different questions do not always add up to 100%.

## What's next

The survey on improving the operation of hotlines 1154 and 1104 [has been running](https://t.me/minenergy_uz/20665) since early August on the ministry's Telegram channel in the form of a poll with preset answer options. The ministry has not yet announced the exact date the survey will end or when final results will be published.
