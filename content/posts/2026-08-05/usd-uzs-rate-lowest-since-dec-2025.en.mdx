The Central Bank of the Republic of Uzbekistan set the official U.S. dollar exchange rate for August 6, 2026, at [11,886.72 soums](https://cbu.uz/ru/arkhiv-kursov-valyut/json/USD/2026-08-06/) — the lowest level since early December 2025 and the second straight day of decline.

## How the rate moved this week

The [Central Bank of Uzbekistan published](https://cbu.uz/ru/arkhiv-kursov-valyut/json/USD/2026-08-06/) the new rate on the evening of August 5 — the regulator announces the rate for the next banking day daily at 17:30. Compared with the rate in effect on August 5 (11,942.21 soums), the dollar weakened by 55.49 soums, or 0.46%.

This is already the second straight decline: [the rate for August 5](https://cbu.uz/ru/arkhiv-kursov-valyut/json/USD/2026-08-05/) was itself 47.25 soums below the previous day's rate of 11,989.46 soums. Before that, on August 4, the rate had instead risen by 29.28 soums after falling 46.21 soums the day before.

The rate [stood at 12,006.39 soums](https://cbu.uz/ru/arkhiv-kursov-valyut/json/USD/2026-07-31/) on Friday, July 31 — the following two days were weekends. Since then, the dollar has weakened against the soum by 119.67 soums, or 1.0%.

## When the rate was last at this level

Official archived data from the Central Bank of Uzbekistan show that the dollar rate had not fallen to the current level since early December 2025. On [December 3, 2025](https://cbu.uz/ru/arkhiv-kursov-valyut/json/USD/2025-12-03/), the dollar was worth 11,880.93 soums — the last time the rate was below today's value. Throughout that entire period — including archive-checked data points in December, January, February, April, May, June and July — the rate stayed above today's level. At certain points it rose above 12,100–12,200 soums per dollar.

The Central Bank of Uzbekistan's exchange rate is calculated as the weighted average of transactions on the currency exchange and the over-the-counter interbank market, and is used by banks, in accounting, and for customs payments.
