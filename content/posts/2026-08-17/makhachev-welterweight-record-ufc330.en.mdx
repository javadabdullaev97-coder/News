Islam Makhachev beat Ian Machado Garry by unanimous decision at UFC 330 in Philadelphia on August 15, extending his win streak to 17 fights — a new UFC record.

All three judges scored the fight for the Russian [49-46, 49-46 and 48-47](https://www.ufc.com/news/ufc-330-islam-makhachev-vs-ian-machado-garry-results). The five-round bout took place at the Xfinity Mobile Arena. Makhachev controlled the Irishman on the ground and dropped him with a head kick in the second round. The champion could not finish his opponent — Garry defended himself on the ground until the final bell, [UFC.com reported](https://www.ufc.com/news/ufc-330-islam-makhachev-vs-ian-machado-garry-results). It was Makhachev's first defense of the welterweight title he won in late 2025.

## Silva's Record Stood for Nearly 13 Years

At UFC 330, Makhachev surpassed Anderson Silva's record of 16 consecutive wins from 2006 to 2013, which had stood for nearly 13 years, [Al Jazeera reported](https://www.aljazeera.com/sports/2026/8/16/ufc-330-makhachev-secures-record-setting-win-against-machado-garry). Makhachev's winning streak began several years ago. Before moving up to welterweight, he defended the lightweight title multiple times. In 2025, Makhachev vacated that belt to avoid holding titles in two weight classes at once.

## Dern Defends Women's Title

In the co-main event, Mackenzie Dern beat Gillian Robertson by unanimous decision — [49-46, 49-46 and 48-47](https://www.sherdog.com/news/news/UFC-330-results-Islam-Makhachev-defeats-Ian-Garry-breaks-UFC-record-202362) — to defend the UFC women's strawweight title. It was Dern's first defense of the belt she won in her previous fight. In the first round, Dern nearly forced her opponent to submit with a chokehold, and Robertson only fought back in the fifth round, maintaining top control until the final bell.
