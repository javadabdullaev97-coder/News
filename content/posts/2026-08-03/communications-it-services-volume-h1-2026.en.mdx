The National Statistics Committee of the Republic of Uzbekistan (Statistics Committee) [reported](https://t.me/uzstataxborot/69163) on August 3 that the volume of communications and IT services in the country grew to 51.9 trillion soums — up 22.1% from a year earlier.

At the exchange rate of the Central Bank of Uzbekistan (CBU) on August 3 — [11,960.18 soums per dollar](https://cbu.uz/ru/arkhiv-kursov-valyut/) — that is about $4.34 billion. Calculated from the growth rate cited by the Statistics Committee, the figure for the first half of 2025 stood at about 42.5 trillion soums.

## What Drove the Growth

The Statistics Committee [presented](https://t.me/uzstataxborot/69163) the breakdown of services by type for the first half of 2026:

- computer programming services — 25.1 trillion soums (about $2.10 billion);
- telecommunications services — 14 trillion soums (about $1.17 billion);
- information sector services — 8.6 trillion soums (about $0.72 billion);
- other communications and IT services — 4.3 trillion soums (about $0.36 billion).

The sum of the individual categories (25.1 + 14 + 8.6 + 4.3 trillion soums = 52 trillion soums) exceeds the Statistics Committee's total by 0.1 trillion soums — the discrepancy is due to rounding in the source data.

## Programming — Nearly Half the Market

According to LEAP News calculations based on Statistics Committee data, computer programming services accounted for about 48% of the total volume of communications and IT services for the half-year. Telecommunications services made up about 27%, information sector services about 17%, and other services about 8%.

The Statistics Committee did not disclose growth rates for individual service categories within the sector — only the overall industry figure of 22.1%.
