The National Statistics Committee of the Republic of Uzbekistan (Statistics Committee) [reported](https://stat.uz/uz/matbuot-markazi/qo-mita-yangiliklar/69625-zbekistonliklar-so-li-ni-sa-lash-khizmatlariga-yarim-jilda-11-trln-s-mdan-zijod-mabla-sarfladi-2) on August 4 that residents of the country spent 11.3 trillion soums on healthcare in January–June 2026 — 16.8% more than a year earlier.

The agency [reposted](https://t.me/uzstataxborot/69177) the data on its Telegram channel. At the Central Bank of Uzbekistan (CBU) exchange rate for [August 4](https://cbu.uz/ru/arkhiv-kursov-valyut/json/USD/2026-08-04/) — 11,989.46 soums per dollar — the amount is about $942.5 million. Based on the growth rate cited by the Statistics Committee, healthcare spending in the first half of 2025 is calculated to have been about 9.7 trillion soums.

## What the Total Consists Of

The Statistics Committee provided a breakdown of spending by type of medical service for January–June 2026:

- other healthcare services — 53.7%;
- inpatient (hospital) services — 31.8%;
- medical practice and dentistry — 14.5%.

The agency did not specify what exactly falls under the category of "other healthcare services."

## Half the Spending Comes From Tashkent and the Region

The Statistics Committee provided a breakdown of spending by region:

- Tashkent — 5.2 trillion soums;
- Tashkent region — 1.1 trillion soums;
- Samarkand region — 830.2 billion soums;
- Fergana region — 675.4 billion soums;
- Namangan region — 550.4 billion soums;
- Andijan region — 507.2 billion soums;
- Bukhara region — 392.3 billion soums;
- Khorezm region — 390.9 billion soums;
- Kashkadarya region — 360.4 billion soums;
- Republic of Karakalpakstan — 335.5 billion soums;
- Surkhandarya region — 262 billion soums;
- Jizzakh region — 251.5 billion soums;
- Syrdarya region — 203.2 billion soums;
- Navoi region — 193.1 billion soums.

Tashkent and the Tashkent region together accounted for 6.3 trillion soums — about 56% of the country's total healthcare spending for the half-year. Residents of the Navoi region spent the least. The sum of regional figures (about 11.25 trillion soums) is slightly lower than the Statistics Committee's total of 11.3 trillion soums — the discrepancy is due to rounding in the source data.

The agency did not provide growth rates for individual regions in this release — only the nationwide figure of 16.8%.
