The hokimiyat of Tashkent's Yunusabad district [said](https://t.me/yunusobodhokimiyati/39028) overnight into August 6 that authorities had dropped plans to build a mahalla house on a parking lot in the Qadrdon mahalla — the site remains a parking area.

The hokimiyat press service's Telegram channel [published](https://t.me/yunusobodhokimiyati/39028) a statement titled "RASMIY MUNOSABAT" ("official position"). It says social media posts and residents' well-founded concerns about the parking lot in the Qadrdon mahalla did not go unnoticed by district leadership.

Yunusabad district hokim A. Saidaliyev personally visited the site, met with residents' representatives and reviewed the situation. Following the visit, the hokimiyat announced that the parking lot would remain unchanged and that residents' interests had been fully taken into account.

The hokimiyat did not specify in its statement what further steps are planned for the site.
