Alibaba Group Holding Ltd. [reported](https://www.alibabagroup.com/en-US/document-2026456290057781248) its financial results for the quarter ended June 30, 2026 on August 20: net profit fell 76% year-on-year. The figure attributable to ordinary shareholders [declined](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1577552/000110465926099220/tm2623667d1_ex99-1.htm) to 10.54 billion yuan ($1.55 billion) from 43.12 billion yuan a year earlier.

In the same quarter, the company's capital expenditure rose 75% year-on-year to 67.7 billion yuan ($9.98 billion), the [report said](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1577552/000110465926099220/tm2623667d1_ex99-1.htm). A year earlier, the figure stood at 38.68 billion yuan. The increase in spending was driven by "fluctuations in procurement cycles and increased computing capacity on processors amid the expected growth in customers' use of AI agents," the [report said](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1577552/000110465926099220/tm2623667d1_ex99-1.htm). The report also noted rising prices across a wide range of chip components.

Alibaba's total revenue for the quarter came to 268.95 billion yuan ($39.64 billion) — up 9% year-on-year. Revenue from the cloud and AI-related computing services segment grew 45% year-on-year to 48.4 billion yuan ($7.14 billion). Revenue from AI-based products reached 12.4 billion yuan ($1.82 billion) — the twelfth consecutive quarter of triple-digit year-on-year growth, according to the report.

The company converts yuan to dollars at a rate of 6.7851 yuan per dollar as of June 30, 2026.
