Uzbekistan's Ambassador Ravshan Usmanov met in Manchester on August 7 with Manchester Airports Group (MAG) Corporate Affairs Director Rhys Whalley — [the two sides discussed](https://dunyo.info/ru/sotrudnichestvo/uzbekistan-i-velikobritaniya-rassmatrivayut-vopros-zapuska-pryamyh) the possibility of launching a direct Tashkent-Manchester flight. Usmanov is Uzbekistan's ambassador to the United Kingdom.

## What Was Agreed

Following the meeting, the two sides [agreed](https://dunyo.info/ru/sotrudnichestvo/uzbekistan-i-velikobritaniya-rassmatrivayut-vopros-zapuska-pryamyh) to work closely together on the organizational aspects of launching the flights. Neither side announced specific launch dates, the name of the carrier, or flight frequency.

The British side noted that Manchester Airport is one of the largest transport and logistics hubs in the northern United Kingdom and Northern Europe. Its annual passenger traffic exceeds 32 million, and its two runways could eventually support growth to 70 million. Rhys Whalley said MAG was ready to support Uzbek air carriers in implementing the project.

Manchester Airports Group operates three UK airports — Manchester, London Stansted and East Midlands. In 2025, 54 million passengers passed through the group's airports. The company's contribution to the UK economy exceeded 8 billion pounds sterling, and it employs more than 7,000 people.

## A Second Attempt to Launch This Route

A direct Tashkent-Manchester flight was already attempted in 2026. On December 15, 2025, Centrum Air [announced](https://www.aeroroutes.com/eng/251215-c6ns26man) it would launch the route on March 30, 2026, with two flights a week on an Airbus A321neo. But during the week of February 7, 2026, the carrier [dropped Manchester](https://aerospaceglobalnews.com/news/centrum-air-backtracks-manchester) from its schedule and booking system without explanation. It kept its other European routes — Frankfurt and Copenhagen starting March 30-31, plus plans for Madrid, Barcelona, Rome and Paris.

The ambassador's meeting with MAG in August 2026 was diplomatic-level talks, not tied to a specific carrier. Neither side named the airline that might operate the route.
