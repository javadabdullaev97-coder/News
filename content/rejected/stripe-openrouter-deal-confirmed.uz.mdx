To'lov xizmati Stripe 19 avgustda sun'iy intellekt (SI) modellari orasida so'rovlarni taqsimlaydigan SI-startapi OpenRouter'ni sotib olganini rasman tasdiqladi — bundan ikki kun oldin LEAP News Bloomberg'ga tayanib [kelishilayotgan bitim haqida yozgan edi](https://leap.uz/uz/2026/08/18/stripe-openrouter-7bn-deal-bloomberg).

Kompaniya bitim haqida bayonot e'lon qildi, [deb xabar beradi SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/08/19/stripe-buys-ai-model-router-openrouter-in-reported-7-5b-deal/). OpenRouter dasturchilarning so'rovlarini yuzlab SI modellari orasida taqsimlaydi va buning evaziga komissiya oladi. Stripe bitimning moliyaviy shartlarini sharhlashdan bosh tortdi, [deb ta'kidlaydi Payments Dive](https://www.paymentsdive.com/news/stripe-openrouter-finally-strike-a-deal/828326/).

Stripe bosh direktori Patrik Kollison bitimni "SI uchun iqtisodiy infratuzilma" yaratish yo'lidagi qadam deb atadi, [deb yozadi SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/08/19/stripe-buys-ai-model-router-openrouter-in-reported-7-5b-deal/). Uning so'zlariga ko'ra, kompaniya so'rovlarni marshrutlash orqali bizneslarga modellarga sarflanadigan xarajatlarni samaraliroq taqsimlashga yordam berishni maqsad qilgan. OpenRouter asoschilari bayonotda platformaning turli SI modellariga nisbatan neytralligini ta'kidladilar, [deb xabar beradi Payments Dive](https://www.paymentsdive.com/news/stripe-openrouter-finally-strike-a-deal/828326/). Ular, shuningdek, xizmatning 2023 yilda asos solinganidan beri har yili o'n baravar o'sib borayotganini qayd etishdi.

## Bitim qancha turishi mumkin

New York Times ma'lumotlariga ko'ra, bitim summasi $7,5 mlrdni tashkil etadi — bu baholashni [Payments Dive NYTga tayanib keltiradi](https://www.paymentsdive.com/news/stripe-openrouter-finally-strike-a-deal/828326/). Shu summadan $1,5 mlrd OpenRouter asoschilari Aleks Atallah va Lui Vishiga tegadi. Axios bitimni $8 mlrddan yuqori deb baholaydi va to'lovning katta qismi Stripe aksiyalari orqali amalga oshirilishini ko'rsatadi — bu haqda [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/08/19/stripe-buys-ai-model-router-openrouter-in-reported-7-5b-deal/) Axios'ga tayanib yozadi. Bloomberg ilgari summani $7 mlrddan ortiq deb baholagan edi — shu raqam bilan [18 avgustda LEAP News materiali chiqqan edi](https://leap.uz/uz/2026/08/18/stripe-openrouter-7bn-deal-bloomberg).

OpenRouter 80 dan ortiq provayderning 400 dan ortiq modeliga kirish imkonini beradi va 10 mln dan ortiq dasturchiga xizmat ko'rsatadi, [deb xabar beradi SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/08/19/stripe-buys-ai-model-router-openrouter-in-reported-7-5b-deal/). Kunlik so'rovlar hajmi 10 trln tokendan oshadi, ularni qayta ishlash uchun xizmat xarajatlarning taxminan 5 foizini ushlab qoladi. Bitimning yopilishi bir necha hafta ichida kutilmoqda, aniq sana e'lon qilinmagan, [deb ta'kidlaydi SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/08/19/stripe-buys-ai-model-router-openrouter-in-reported-7-5b-deal/).
