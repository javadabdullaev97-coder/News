Uzbekistan's State Tax Committee [confirmed](https://t.me/soliqnews/20420) in early August 2026 that part of the VAT previously refunded to catering businesses, tour operators and hotels has been recalculated based on a business sustainability rating, and that companies are required to return the overpayment to the budget.

## What the Tax Committee confirmed

The clarification was prompted by social media reports about the situation in the Samarkand region. The committee [clarified](https://t.me/soliqnews/20420) that the term "VAT cashback" does not exist in current regulations. Legally, it is a "partial refund of value-added tax paid to the budget" for catering businesses, tour operators and hotels.

The mechanism was introduced by the Interim Regulation approved by [Cabinet of Ministers Resolution No. 745 of November 7, 2024](https://lex.uz/uz/docs/-7206097). The procedure is based on [Presidential Resolution PP-39 of January 23, 2024](https://lex.uz/docs/6773342), which introduced a business sustainability rating for entrepreneurship entities starting February 1, 2024. It also relies on [Presidential Resolution PP-193 of May 24, 2024](https://lex.uz/docs/6937347), which linked subsidies, benefits and preferences for businesses to the level of this rating starting May 25, 2024.

As the committee clarified with reference to paragraph 16 of the Interim Regulation, amounts refunded in violation of legal requirements or in excess of the due amount are subject to return to the budget. The Tax Committee said it recalculated refunds based on these requirements and identified entities with excess amounts refunded. The agency notified them in advance and is now taking measures "in the manner established by law" — that is, collecting the difference as a debt.

## How the sustainability rating affects VAT refunds

The sustainability rating is a points-based system for businesses that the tax service has maintained since February 1, 2024. The volume of benefits depends on the assigned category. Entities with the highest "AAA" category are exempt from tax audits (except audits related to criminal cases), and their VAT refunds are processed within one day without a desk audit, [according to Tax Committee materials](https://gov.uz/ru/soliq/news/view/18834). Companies with a lower rating face stricter refund conditions.

The rating is a dynamic figure: points change based on current data about a company's activity rather than being fixed once. Neither the committee nor the resolutions directly explain the recalculation mechanism, but given this dynamic, it can be assumed that if a company's rating was lower at the time of recalculation than at the time of the initial payment, part of the amount could have been deemed excessively refunded.

## Criticism from business

Economist Yuliy Yusupov [wrote on his Telegram channel](https://t.me/yuliy_yusupov/1831) that the initial calculation and payment to companies was made by the tax authority itself — not by the entrepreneurs. Later, the same companies received notifications that they needed to repay the money as tax debt. According to him, the text of Resolution No. 745, which companies relied on when filing applications, does not directly stipulate a requirement linking the amount to the sustainability rating for recipients. At the same time, the rating itself changes daily, and its past values are not stored in the taxpayer's personal account. As a result, a company cannot retroactively verify what its rating was at the time of payment.

Yusupov calls the situation an example of using discretionary mechanisms — benefits and payments decided by an official — instead of transparent rules with no right of retroactive revision. According to him, affected companies are advised to challenge the collection in court.

## What's next

The Tax Committee's official position: amounts deemed excessively refunded are subject to collection under the law. The alternative for companies that disagree is to challenge the collection in court. The agency did not disclose the number or total amount of the sums affected.
