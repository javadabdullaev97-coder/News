Israel carried out two airstrikes on south Lebanon on Saturday, August 15 — according to Lebanon's Ministry of Health, 9 people were killed, including three children, and another 11 were wounded.

## Strikes on Ansar and Deir ez-Zahrani

The first strike hit a house on the outskirts of the village of Ansar. Seven people were killed, including three children, and two more were wounded, [Le Monde reported](https://www.lemonde.fr/en/international/article/2026/08/15/lebanon-nine-killed-by-israeli-airstrikes-in-south-of-country_6756538_4.html), citing Lebanese authorities. The second strike hit the village of Deir ez-Zahrani: two people were killed there and nine wounded, [BBC News reported](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c4gjygx4gjeo).

Rescuers searched for survivors under the rubble of the destroyed house in Ansar — an [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/video/newsfeed/2026/8/15/at-scene-of-deadly-israeli-strike-on-lebanon-home) crew was at the scene and broadcast footage of the destruction.

## Positions of the sides

Lebanese Prime Minister Nawaf Salam said those killed in the strike on Ansar were not "military infrastructure." The women and children among the victims were also not military targets, [The New York Times quoted him as saying](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/08/15/world/middleeast/israel-strikes-lebanon-ansar.html). Salam called on Israel to immediately halt the "extremely dangerous" escalation.

The Israeli military said the strikes were retaliatory and hit Hezbollah infrastructure targets in the Ali al-Taher and Ansar areas, in response to militant actions against Israeli troops in the area, [according to the NYT](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/08/15/world/middleeast/israel-strikes-lebanon-ansar.html).

## The deadliest toll since June

A fragile calm has held between Israel and Hezbollah since June 20, 2026. The August 15 strikes were among the deadliest since it took hold, [Le Monde noted](https://www.lemonde.fr/en/international/article/2026/08/15/lebanon-nine-killed-by-israeli-airstrikes-in-south-of-country_6756538_4.html).
