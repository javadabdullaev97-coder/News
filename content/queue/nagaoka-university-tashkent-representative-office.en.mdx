Nagaoka University of Technology (Japan) [opened a representative office on August 7](https://t.me/eduuz/50282) at Tashkent State Technical University named after Islam Karimov (TSTU) — the ceremony was attended by Japan's ambassador and the leadership of both universities.

## Opening Ceremony

The ceremony at TSTU [was attended](https://t.me/eduuz/50282) by Japan's Extraordinary and Plenipotentiary Ambassador to Uzbekistan Kenji Hirata, the director of the Japan International Cooperation Agency (JICA) office in Uzbekistan Kan Sakai, Nagaoka University of Technology President Shigeharu Kamado, the university's Vice President for International Strategy Yukio Miyashita, and TSTU rector Academician Sadritdin Turabjanov.

The ministry's statement itself is limited to the fact of the office's opening and the list of ceremony participants — the agency has not yet published details about its staff, budget, or a specific list of future programs.

## Expanding Cooperation with Japan

The next day, August 8, Minister of Higher Education, Science and Innovation Kungirotboy Sharipov [met](https://gov.uz/ru/edu/news/view/203749) with Ambassador Hirata and discussed expanding cooperation between the two countries in higher education. According to the ministry, Uzbekistan's universities already cooperate with the universities of Tsukuba, Nagoya, Kyoto and Osaka, and more than 2,300 Uzbekistan citizens are studying at Japanese higher education institutions.

At the [5th Rectors' Forum in Samarkand in 2025](https://gov.uz/ru/edu/news/view/203749), 30 Uzbek and more than 15 Japanese educational institutions established contacts, and more than 10 cooperation agreements were signed.

## What's Planned Next

At the meeting, the two sides also discussed [plans](https://gov.uz/ru/edu/news/view/203749) to accelerate the project to establish a new university jointly with the University of Tsukuba and to develop programs in engineering, IT, medicine, energy and agriculture. They also discussed attracting Japanese professors, expanding academic mobility, and joint summer schools for students. The ministry's statement does not specify a timeline for launching these initiatives.
