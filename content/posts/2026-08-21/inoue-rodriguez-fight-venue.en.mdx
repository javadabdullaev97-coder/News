Japanese outlet [Ronspo reported](https://www.ronspo.com/articles/2026/2026081901/) on August 19 that organizers are considering Fukuoka in February and Tokyo in March 2027 for the fight between Naoya Inoue and Jesse "Bam" Rodriguez.

According to the outlet, the main option is the Mizuho PayPay Dome in Fukuoka in February. If negotiations drag on, the event could be pushed to March and held at the Tokyo Dome. [The tournament is organized](https://www.ronspo.com/articles/2026/2026081901/) by Saudi Arabia's Riyadh Season, a division of the General Entertainment Authority of Saudi Arabia. This would be Riyadh Season's first tournament in Japan. Organizers initially considered the IG Arena in Nagoya but changed the venue, as they want to hold "the biggest tournament in Japan's history," Ronspo notes.

Inoue is the undisputed world champion in the super bantamweight division, holding all four belts. American Jesse Rodriguez is the World Boxing Association (WBA) super champion in the bantamweight division and a three-division world champion. [The contract for the fight has not been signed](https://www.ronspo.com/articles/2026/2026081901/): organizers are waiting for a response from Rodriguez's team.

Earlier, Ohashi Promotions president Hideyuki Ohashi [told](https://news.infoseek.co.jp/article/nikkansports_202608140000667/) Nikkan Sports: "We don't think we should force this." According to him, Rodriguez must first fight a unification bout in the bantamweight division, which had been planned for October but is now being delayed. Until those negotiations are finished, a fight with Inoue looks increasingly unlikely, Ohashi said.

If waiting for Rodriguez takes a long time, Inoue will move up to featherweight and, according to Ohashi, "will no longer return to super bantamweight."
