Nvidia is discussing taking part in a funding round for Mercor, a labeled-data provider, that would value the startup at $20 billion — The Information reported, citing unnamed sources, [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/news/investment-tracker/2026/nvidia-weighs-investment-in-round-valuing-mercor-at-20-billion/) said on Wednesday, August 19.

Mercor supplies the labeled data Nvidia uses to develop its open-source Nemotron family of models. Mercor's revenue from Nvidia orders rose to tens of millions of dollars last quarter, [PYMNTS reported](https://www.pymnts.com/news/investment-tracker/2026/nvidia-weighs-investment-in-round-valuing-mercor-at-20-billion/).

PYMNTS does not disclose the exact size of Nvidia's potential investment or the round's total volume. As of August 20, neither Nvidia nor Mercor has officially confirmed the talks.

Mercor's previous valuation stood at $10 billion. The company reached that figure in October 2025 following a $350 million Series C round, [PYMNTS noted](https://www.pymnts.com/news/investment-tracker/2026/nvidia-weighs-investment-in-round-valuing-mercor-at-20-billion/). On July 9, [TechCrunch reported](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/09/mercor-is-in-talks-for-a-20b-valuation/) that Mercor was in talks for a round valuing it at $20 billion but did not name Nvidia among the participants.
