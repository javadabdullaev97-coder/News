Google 14-avgustda Spirit Airlines kompaniyasining shaxsi aniqlanmagan biznes-ma'lumotlari auksionida g'olib chiqdi — paket uchun sun'iy intellekt modellarini o'qitish maqsadida $10 mln taklif qildi, bu Nyu-York Janubiy okrugi bankrotlik sudining bildirishnomasidan ma'lum bo'ldi.

Paketga xodimlarning taxminan 100 mln xati va Microsoft Teams'dagi qariyb 500 mln yozishmasi kiradi, [deb yozadi Bloomberg Law](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/business-and-practice/google-aims-to-boost-ai-with-purchase-of-spirit-airlines-data). Shuningdek paketga daromad, flot ishi, kadrlar bo'yicha ma'lumotlar, audit va firibgarlik holatlari haqidagi ma'lumotlar kiradi, [Bloomberg Law xabar qiladi](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/business-and-practice/google-aims-to-boost-ai-with-purchase-of-spirit-airlines-data); marketing arxivlarini [Reuters](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/google-buy-spirit-airlines-business-212308890.html) tilga oladi. Alohida bitimga kompaniyaning taxminan 30 mln qator dasturiy kodi hamda dasturiy modellar va algoritmlarni ishlab chiqish bo'yicha ma'lumotlari kiradi, [deb yozadi Bloomberg Law](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/business-and-practice/google-aims-to-boost-ai-with-purchase-of-spirit-airlines-data).

Paketdan yo'lovchilarning shaxsiy ma'lumotlari — 97,5 mln mijoz profili va Free Spirit sodiqlik dasturining taxminan 50,2 mln yozuvi chiqarib tashlangan, [Bloomberg Law xabar qiladi](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/business-and-practice/google-aims-to-boost-ai-with-purchase-of-spirit-airlines-data). Qolgan shaxsiy ma'lumotlarni uchinchi tomon topshirishdan oldin anonimlashtiradi, [deb yozadi Bloomberg Law](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/business-and-practice/google-aims-to-boost-ai-with-purchase-of-spirit-airlines-data). «Biz Spirit Airlines'ning korporativ ma'lumotlar to'plamining bir qismini sotib oldik — bu bizning mahsulotlarimiz va SI modellarimizni takomillashtirish uchun foydali bo'lishi mumkin», — deyilgan [Google bayonotida](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/business-and-practice/google-aims-to-boost-ai-with-purchase-of-spirit-airlines-data).

Savdolarda Google sun'iy intellektni o'qitish uchun ma'lumotlar bo'yicha ixtisoslashgan Mercor kompaniyasidan o'zib ketdi: uning qarshi taklifi $7,5 mln ni tashkil etgan, xuddi shu hujjatlarda aytilgan.

## Keyingi qadamlar

Bitim hali yakunlanmagan. 19-avgustda sudya Shon Leyn Nyu-York Janubiy okrugi bankrotlik sudidagi tinglovda ma'lumotlarni Google'ga sotishni tasdiqlash haqidagi arizani ko'rib chiqishi kerak. Spirit Aviation Holdings kompaniyasining bankrotlik ishi 25-11897-raqami ostida yuritilmoqda. Spirit Airlines 2026-yil mayida yo'lovchi tashishni to'xtatgan va bankrotlik jarayoni doirasida qolgan aktivlarini sotmoqda.
