President Shavkat Mirziyoyev on August 17 [traveled](https://www.president.uz/ru/lists/view/9504) from Urgench to Khiva aboard a new electric train, on a railway line that for the first time in Uzbekistan directly connects an international airport with a city railway station.

The new 11-kilometer branch line connects [Urgench International Airport and Khiva railway station](https://www.president.uz/ru/lists/view/9504). The project cost 225 billion soums (about $18.9 million at the Central Bank of Uzbekistan's exchange rate as of August 17). The line includes one station, a bridge, road crossings and 25 culverts. Travel time between the two cities was cut in half, from 80 to 40 minutes. According to the presidential press service, this is the country's first project directly connecting an airport to the railway network.

A Chinese-made CR200J electric train began operating on the route — six cars with capacity for about 400 passengers and a top speed of 160 km/h. The train has VIP, business and economy class seating. It also offers free Wi-Fi, device charging, climate control and facilities for passengers with disabilities.

In Khiva, the president was also shown 10 trolleybuses that will operate on the same route. The vehicles [were brought from China in July](https://t.me/Mintrans_uz/28717), the presidential press service said. The trolleybuses can travel up to 100 km without connecting to the power grid, and each carries 87 passengers. The cabin features USB and Type-C ports, surveillance cameras, a driver condition monitoring system, lane departure warning and automatic fire suppression.

The new route will be able to carry about 650,000 tourists a year, and the project has created about 30 jobs. "Integrating different modes of transport will help create convenience for residents and tourists, cut travel time and boost the region's tourist appeal," Mirziyoyev said. He instructed officials to ensure safe and high-quality passenger service.

The president first proposed purchasing this type of transport during a visit to Khorezm Region last year.
