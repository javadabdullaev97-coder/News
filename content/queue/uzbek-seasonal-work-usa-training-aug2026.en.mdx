The Migration Agency under the Cabinet of Ministers of Uzbekistan [announced](https://t.me/uzmigration/1306) on August 5 an agreement with another US recruitment company — the parties agreed to prepare and employ Uzbek nationals for seasonal farm work under the H-2A program.

## What is known about the agreement

The agency's post states that the parties agreed to prepare and employ Uzbek citizens for seasonal work in the United States through the H-2A visa program, which is designed for temporary agricultural workers. The name of the partner company [is not disclosed](https://t.me/uzmigration/1306) in the announcement — it is referred to only as "another major US recruitment company" in this field.

The agency has not yet disclosed the number of vacancies, salary levels, or the timeline for the start of candidate selection. The employer's requirements and registration procedure are promised to be published later on the "XORIJDA ISH" Telegram channel — the agency's main channel for job announcements abroad, where the announcement of the agreement itself was also [republished](https://t.me/migratsiyaagentligi/16254).

## Another agreement on seasonal labor migration to the US

This is not the first arrangement between Uzbekistan and the US side on seasonal employment. Earlier in 2026, the Migration Agency had already established a partnership with USAFARMLABOR (USA) for seasonal labor migration in agriculture. On April 1, [the agency warned](https://gov.uz/ru/migration/news/view/149387) citizens that registration for seasonal work in the US would only be conducted after an official announcement of selection based on an employer's request — attempts to register directly through the websites of partner organizations or farms yield no results.

In the same announcement, the agency urged citizens to trust only official announcements and not fall for offers from intermediaries promising expedited employment for a fee.

## What's next

The Migration Agency promised to disclose the specific terms of the new agreement — including candidate requirements, pay, and selection dates — separately. Updates will be published on the official "XORIJDA ISH" Telegram channel and the [xorijdaish.uz](https://xorijdaish.uz) website.
