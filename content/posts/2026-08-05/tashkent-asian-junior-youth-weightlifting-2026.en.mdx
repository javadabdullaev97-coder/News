Uzbekistan's Deputy Minister of Sports Shahrillo Makhmudov and Asian Weightlifting Federation (AWF) General Secretary Mohamed Ahmed Al-Harbi inspected the Olympic Village in Tashkent ahead of the Asian Junior and Youth Championships, which start on [August 7](https://t.me/Minsportuz/89978).

## What Was Inspected Before the Tournament

This was reported in a post by the [Telegram channel of the Ministry of Sports of Uzbekistan](https://t.me/Minsportuz/89978). Makhmudov and Al-Harbi checked the competition halls as well as the coaches' and judges' rooms at the sports complex.

According to the [Asian Weightlifting Federation's technical regulations](https://awf.sport/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/2026-Asian-Youth-Junior-Weightlifting-Championships.pdf), the venue for competitions and training is the Combat Sports Complex at the Olympic Village in Tashkent. The local organizer of the tournament is the Weightlifting Federation of Uzbekistan, acting as the local organizing committee.

In the tournament regulations, Makhmudov is listed as First Vice President of the Weightlifting Federation of Uzbekistan and Deputy Minister of Sports of Uzbekistan. It was in this capacity that he [signed the invitation](https://awf.sport/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/2026-Asian-Youth-Junior-Weightlifting-Championships.pdf) to Asia's national federations. Mohamed Ahmed Al-Harbi is AWF's General Secretary and President of the Saudi Arabian Weightlifting Federation, according to the [Asian federation's website](https://awf.sport/general-secretary/).

## Regulations and Program

The championship is contested in two age groups: youth aged 13–17 (athletes born 2009–2013) and juniors aged 15–20 (born 2006–2011), according to the [AWF regulations](https://awf.sport/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/2026-Asian-Youth-Junior-Weightlifting-Championships.pdf). Medals are awarded in the snatch, clean and jerk, and total in each weight category, with separate prizes for the best athletes and the six strongest teams.

Each national federation may enter up to 16 men and 16 women in the preliminary entry list, and up to 10 athletes per gender (eight main and two reserve) in the final entry. The opening ceremony is scheduled for August 7 and the closing ceremony for August 14. The competition schedule is arranged by day, from the women's 45 kg category on the first day to the men's over-110 kg category on the last.

## What's Next

The regulations set the opening of the training venue for August 4, with the AWF executive committee meeting and technical officials' meeting on August 6. Federations were required to confirm final entries [no later than July 24](https://awf.sport/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/2026-Asian-Youth-Junior-Weightlifting-Championships.pdf), so the list of participating countries was already closed ahead of the tournament.
