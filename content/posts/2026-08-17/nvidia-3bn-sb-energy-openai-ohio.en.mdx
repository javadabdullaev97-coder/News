Nvidia is in talks to invest up to $3 billion in SB Energy, which is building an OpenAI data center in Ohio, [Reuters](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/nvidia-in-talks-to-invest-3-billion-in-sb-energy-as-part-of-openai-data-center-deal-the-information-reports-4861844) reported on August 15, citing The Information.

Reuters explicitly notes that it has not independently verified the information. The source is people familiar with the talks, cited by [The Information](https://www.theinformation.com/articles/nvidia-talks-invest-3-billion-sb-energy-part-openai-data-center-deal).

SB Energy is a unit of SoftBank Group that, together with OpenAI, is developing [a data center project with capacity of up to 10 gigawatts](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/nvidia-in-talks-to-invest-up-to-3bn-in-sb-energy-report/) in Ohio. The investment is being discussed as part of a broader deal: Nvidia is also in talks to provide about $100 billion in credit support for the campus.

## Guarantee Amount Has Already Changed

Talks around the Ohio campus have been underway for months. Reuters notes that the amount of Nvidia's guarantees has changed several times in media reports, with figures diverging.

SB Energy is considering an IPO; no exact date or size has been announced. As of publication, neither Nvidia, SB Energy, nor OpenAI has officially confirmed the investment.
