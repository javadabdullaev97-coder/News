Anthropic's annualized revenue run rate surpassed $65 billion at the end of July 2026 — seven times higher than a year earlier, [Bloomberg reported](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-17/anthropic-revenue-run-rate-surpasses-65-billion-ahead-of-ipo) on August 17.

The figure refers to annualized revenue run rate — the latest period's revenue extrapolated over a full year. According to the agency, the rate grew from about $9 billion at the end of 2025 to $47 billion in May. By the end of July, it topped $65 billion.

In the most recent completed quarter, Anthropic's revenue exceeded $11.5 billion, up from $787 million a year earlier, Bloomberg reports. The company also posted a positive adjusted operating profit for a quarter for the first time.

Anthropic and its rival OpenAI have filed confidential applications for an initial public offering (IPO) — the first-ever sale of the companies' shares on a stock exchange. According to Bloomberg, Anthropic could go public as early as this fall, ahead of OpenAI.

## Pre-IPO Valuation: From $965 Billion to $2 Trillion

On May 28, Anthropic raised $65 billion in a Series H round at a post-money valuation of $965 billion, [Bloomberg reported](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-28/anthropic-raises-at-965-billion-valuation-eclipsing-openai). The company also [announced the round itself](https://www.anthropic.com/news/series-h), citing an annualized revenue run rate of $47 billion at the time.

Anthropic's investors expect the company could be valued at $2 trillion or more in an October offering, [TechCrunch reports](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/17/anthropics-annualized-revenue-surges-to-65b/), citing the Financial Times.

By comparison, OpenAI's annualized revenue run rate stands at about $40 billion — twice what it was at the end of 2025, TechCrunch notes.

Anthropic has not officially confirmed either the offering date or a target valuation.
