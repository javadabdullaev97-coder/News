OpenAI on August 17, 2026, signed a 20-year lease for the PORTS-Pike data center campus in Pike County, Ohio, with SB Energy, a SoftBank unit that will build at least 10 gigawatts of new generation capacity there.

[SB Energy](https://sbenergy.com/nvidia-ai-compute-ports-pike-ohio/), a unit of SoftBank Group, is building, will own and will operate the campus. The company develops energy projects and data centers. The site sits on the former [Portsmouth uranium enrichment plant](https://sbenergy.com/nvidia-ai-compute-ports-pike-ohio/) — a decommissioned gaseous diffusion facility.

SB Energy and SoftBank will build at least 10 gigawatts of new generation capacity — enough to support 8 gigawatts of data center computing power, [the company said in a press release](https://sbenergy.com/nvidia-ai-compute-ports-pike-ohio/). The first phase covers 4.25 gigawatts of computing power — the amount OpenAI leased. Form 8-K is a report on material corporate events that U.S. companies are required to file with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC). According to [Nvidia's Form 8-K filed with the SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000069/nvda-20260817.htm), the company may at its discretion provide credit support for an additional 3.8 gigawatts. Capacity will come online in phases starting in 2028.

## Nvidia's financial guarantees

Nvidia will become the exclusive supplier of computing infrastructure at the PORTS-Pike site, [the company said](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-guarantees-sb-energy-s-ports-pike-technology-campus-in-ohio-to-exclusively-host-nvidia-ai-compute). It will also invest $1.5 billion in SB Energy, alongside existing investors SoftBank Group and OpenAI, [Nvidia said in a press release](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-guarantees-sb-energy-s-ports-pike-technology-campus-in-ohio-to-exclusively-host-nvidia-ai-compute).

Under [Form 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000069/nvda-20260817.htm) filed with the SEC on August 17, Nvidia provided SB Energy with financial guarantees for the initial 4.25-gigawatt capacity commitment. The guarantees are capped at a combined $105 billion.

SB Energy and SoftBank will invest at least $4.2 billion in the regional power grid together with utility AEP Ohio, [according to the release](https://sbenergy.com/nvidia-ai-compute-ports-pike-ohio/). OpenAI will add $40 million to a local community support fund that SB Energy had previously funded with the same amount. The combined fund will total $80 million.

The project will create tens of thousands of jobs in Ohio, SB Energy said, without specifying an exact number. The campus's first capacity is expected to come online in 2028.
