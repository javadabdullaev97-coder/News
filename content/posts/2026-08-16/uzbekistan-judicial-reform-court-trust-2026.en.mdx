President Shavkat Mirziyoyev on August 14, at a meeting with heads of the judicial system, approved the "Justice — 2030" Strategy and signed [Decree UP-160](https://lex.uz/docs/8406903), which took effect on August 15.

An analysis of public complaints and court practice showed that unresolved issues remain in the system, the Constitutional Court [said](https://www.konstsud.uz/ru/news/2026/08/14/rassmotreny-mery-po-dalnejshemu-uglubleniyu-reform-v-sudebno-pravovoj-sisteme) following the meeting. The president noted that the outcome of the reforms should be felt in the main thing: a person should be able to go to court without unnecessary difficulty. That person should have a violated right restored on time and be confident in the fairness of the ruling.

The "Justice — 2030" Strategy sets 57 objectives. The action program for 2026–2028 includes 160 specific measures. By 2030, the plan is to achieve 33 goals, [the decree states](https://lex.uz/docs/8406903).

## What changes in the courts

Starting July 1, 2027, five interregional courts will begin operating — they will take over the powers of the revision instance from regional courts. This will rule out courts reviewing their own rulings and reduce the number of appeals by regional residents to the capital's courts.

By 2030, civil courts will open in stages in almost all districts and cities of the country. Such cases most often concern people's everyday lives. All courts will introduce a "single window" system for accepting applications and digital services. If an application is filed at the wrong court, the court will forward it to the correct jurisdiction itself instead of returning it to the applicant.

For entrepreneurs, a "proactive management of economic cases" standard will be introduced — it is meant to spare businesses from resubmitting documents. To protect the rights of people detained during the investigation stage, a "reasonable suspicion" (Prima Facie) standard will be introduced. An investigating judge will be able to review the legality of detention and whether there are sufficient grounds for prosecution.

Starting in 2028, an institute of judicial administration will begin operating — organizational functions will be taken away from judges. The "Digital Court" concept will be introduced in all courts, the my.sud.uz portal will be modernized, and the share of electronic applications will be raised to at least 50%. Audio recording will be provided for 100% of court hearings, and the number of crime categories tried by jury will grow to at least three, the Supreme Court [said](https://t.me/oliysuduz/51479).

## New law on the Constitutional Court is being drafted

Also, under [Decree UP-253](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/8796) dated December 18, 2025, the president instructed Constitutional Court Chairman Mirza-Ulugbek Abdusalomov to draft a constitutional law on judicial procedure. The deadline was June 1, 2026.

At the August 14 meeting, the parties also discussed proposals for further improving constitutional judicial proceedings, the Constitutional Court [said](https://www.konstsud.uz/ru/news/2026/08/14/rassmotreny-mery-po-dalnejshemu-uglubleniyu-reform-v-sudebno-pravovoj-sisteme). The statement does not specify whether the bill has been submitted to parliament.

## When it takes effect

Decree UP-160 has been in effect since August 15, 2026. The first practical step — creating five interregional courts — is set for July 1, 2027. The action program distributes the remaining measures across 2026–2028.

## Who is affected

The reform affects everyone who goes to court. Residents of remote districts will get civil courts closer to home. Entrepreneurs can expect a simplified procedure for handling economic disputes. Those detained during the investigation stage will face an additional review of the grounds for arrest.
