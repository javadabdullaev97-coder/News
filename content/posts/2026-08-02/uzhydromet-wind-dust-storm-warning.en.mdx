The Hydrometeorological Service Agency under the National Committee of the Republic of Uzbekistan for Ecology and Climate Change (Uzhydromet) warned on August 2 of possible dust storms in four regions and strengthening wind in Tashkent by evening.

## Where and How Strong

Uzhydromet issued the warning at 09:50 Tashkent time. During the day on August 2, wind speeds in parts of the Bukhara, Navoiy, Kashkadarya and Surkhandarya regions [are strengthening to 13–18 m/s](https://t.me/uzgydromet/21849), with dust storms possible in some areas — roughly 47–65 km/h.

The same four regions were listed in a [statement](https://t.me/ecogovuz/49447) by the National Committee of the Republic of Uzbekistan for Ecology and Climate Change. Tashkent was not mentioned in the committee's publication.

## Tashkent: Wind to Strengthen by Evening

In the capital, wind holds at 3–8 m/s during the day but [will strengthen to 10–12 m/s by evening](https://t.me/uzgydromet/21847) — roughly 36–43 km/h. Dust phenomena are possible in some areas. No precipitation is expected in Tashkent, with a daytime temperature of 38–40°C.

Levels of coarse suspended particles in the capital's air exceeded the norm even before the evening wind strengthening. According to Uzhydromet's automatic monitoring stations, at 09:00 on August 2 the PM10 concentration [reached 84 µg/m³, against the national maximum permissible concentration (MPC) of 75 µg/m³](https://t.me/uzgydromet/21848) — an excess of 1.12 times. The PM2.5 reading of 30 µg/m³, against a national MPC of 60 µg/m³, did not exceed the norm.

## Heat Nationwide and the Weekly Forecast

Nationwide on August 2, air temperatures are rising [to 36–41°C](https://t.me/uzgydromet/21847), reaching 42–43°C in places in southern and desert areas. In mountainous areas, temperatures are 29–34°C with wind at 9–14 m/s.

On August 1, Uzhydromet reported that during the week starting August 3, [temperatures are expected to decrease](https://t.me/uzgydromet/21838) across all regions of Uzbekistan.
