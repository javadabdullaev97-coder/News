Mexican boxer Saul "Canelo" Alvarez has been preparing since August 19 for a fight for the world super middleweight title against undefeated champion Christian Mbilli, [according to the World Boxing Council](https://wbcboxing.com/saul-canelo-alvarez-alista-campamento-en-busca-de-reconquistar-el-campeonato-mundial-supermedio-del-wbc-ante-christian-mbilli/) (WBC).

The fight will take place on [October 31 in Riyadh](https://wbcboxing.com/en/canelo-alvarez-vs-christian-mbilli-rescheduled-for-october-31/), Saudi Arabia: the WBC moved the date from the previously announced September 12 because of personal circumstances that delayed the start of Alvarez's training camp.

Mbilli is the WBC's undefeated super middleweight champion: he was elevated from interim to full champion while Alvarez was out of the ring. The October 31 fight will be his [first defense of the title](https://www.skysports.com/boxing/news/12183/13544900/canelo-alvarez-mexican-superstar-will-return-on-september-12-to-fight-christian-mbilli-for-wbc-super-middleweight-championship).

This will be Alvarez's first fight since his [loss](https://www.skysports.com/boxing/news/12183/13544900/canelo-alvarez-mexican-superstar-will-return-on-september-12-to-fight-christian-mbilli-for-wbc-super-middleweight-championship) to American Terence Crawford, which cost the Mexican his status as the undisputed super middleweight champion.
