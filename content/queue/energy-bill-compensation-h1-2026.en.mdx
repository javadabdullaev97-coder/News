In the first half of 2026, 562,918 needy families in Uzbekistan received 92.1 billion soums in electricity and natural gas compensation, the [Telegram channel of the Ministry of Energy](https://t.me/minenergy_uz/20726) reported. Converted, that is approximately $7.6 million at the Central Bank exchange rate as of June 30.

## How much went where

Of the total, 51.4 billion soums went to electricity compensation and 40.7 billion soums to natural gas, [according to the post](https://t.me/minenergy_uz/20726). The compensation covers the difference between the baseline consumption norm and the higher tariff charged for volumes above that norm. The mechanism has been in place since 2024 as part of the energy sector's transition to market tariffs.

## Who qualifies for the payment

The right to compensation is established by [Presidential Resolution PP-134 of April 2, 2025](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/7464353), "On Additional Measures of State Support for the Needy Population in Introducing Market Mechanisms in the Fuel and Energy Sector." Compensation goes to:

- families listed in the Unified Social Protection Registry — receiving child benefits or financial assistance as low-income households;
- families outside the registry additionally classified as needy based on an assessment by the local "mahalla seven" commission (mahalla yettiligi).

The baseline norm above which compensation is calculated is up to 150 kWh of electricity per month and up to 250 cubic meters of gas during the heating season (150 cubic meters in other months). The measure has been in effect since 2025 (the resolution took effect April 5, 2025) through April 1, 2027.

## How to apply

For families already included in the Unified Social Protection Registry, the Ministry of Energy says a simplified compensation procedure applies, based on meter readings. Families outside the registry, and those who need to update their status, must submit an application by the 16th of each month. This can be done through the public services portal ([Resolution No. 848](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/7963113)), a public services center, or a mahalla social worker. Applications are reviewed within the following month, with payment credited to a social or bank card no later than the 27th. This electronic filing procedure was introduced by [Cabinet of Ministers Resolution No. 848 of December 31, 2025](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/7963113). It required the National Social Protection Agency and the Ministry of Digital Technologies to launch online application submission by February 1, 2026.

For clarification on their individual situation, the Ministry of Energy recommends contacting a mahalla social worker, the local "Inson" social services center, or the Social Protection Agency call center at 1140.

## How the support measure has evolved

Initially, during the 2024–2025 heating season, the assistance was one-time and seasonal. [Cabinet of Ministers Resolution No. 204 of April 16, 2024](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/6884060) introduced compensation for the heating season along with one-time financial assistance for low-income families at its start. Resolution PP-134 extended compensation to the entire year starting in 2025 (the resolution took effect April 5, 2025). Resolution No. 848 moved the application process online through my.gov.uz at the end of 2025.
