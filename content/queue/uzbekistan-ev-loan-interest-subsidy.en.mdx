Uzbekistan's Ministry of Economy and Finance on August 7, 2026, [put up for public discussion](https://regulation.adliya.uz/project/10760/33615?contentLang=ru) a draft resolution on state compensation for part of the interest rate on electric vehicle loans — the discussion will run through August 22.

## What the Draft Proposes

The draft [regulation](https://regulation.adliya.uz/project/10760/33615?contentLang=ru) "On the Procedure for Compensating Part of Interest Payments on Loans Issued to the Public for Purchasing Electric Vehicles" applies to individuals. The state will compensate banks from the budget for part of the annual rate above 16% — the threshold that Presidential Decree UP-22 already set as the target rate for electric vehicle loans. LEAP News was unable to verify the exact size of the compensation in percentage points, the range of eligible borrowers, or the duration of the benefit from the draft text on the portal at the time of publication: the page with the regulation would not display. LEAP News will clarify these parameters once the discussion concludes on August 22.

The draft also limits the range of vehicles and loans eligible for the benefit through caps on the car's price and the loan amount, and sets deadlines for transferring the compensation to the bank and crediting it to the borrower — the exact figures did not display on the portal, and LEAP News will publish them separately once a readable version of the draft text becomes available.

## How This Relates to the 16% Rate Decree

The subject of the draft matches an instruction from [Presidential Decree UP-22](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/8050787) of February 16, 2026 — the document approved the state program for implementing the "Uzbekistan — 2030" strategy and, in paragraph 10, instructed authorities to introduce a procedure for public electric vehicle loans at a rate of up to 16% per year. The metadata of the draft published on the portal does not contain a direct link to the decree, but both documents operate with the same rate threshold — 16% per year.

## What's Next

The regulation.adliya.uz portal is accepting proposals on the draft until August 22, 2026, after which the Ministry of Economy and Finance may submit the document to the Cabinet of Ministers for approval. The exact date the resolution takes effect will be known once the discussion concludes.
