Dunyo birinchi raketkasi Aryna Sabalenka 19-avgustda WTA 1000 (Ayollar tennis assotsiatsiyasi) turnirining Sinsinnatidagi to'rtinchi bosqichida chexiyalik Sara Bejlekka 6:7 (7:9), 4:6 hisobida yutqazdi. Bejlek [WTA reytingida](https://www.wtatennis.com/rankings/singles) 35-o'rinni egallaydi.

Birinchi setning tie-breakida Bejlek 5:1 hisobida ilgarilab ketdi, ammo Sabalenka farqni 5:5gacha qisqartirdi. Chexiyalik tennischi uchinchi set-pointda setni 7:6 (9:7) hisobida o'ziniki qildi, [WTA xabar beradi](https://www.wtatennis.com/news/4563121/bejlek-stuns-sabalenka-in-straight-sets-to-reach-cincinnati-quarterfinals). Ikkinchi setda Sabalenka geymlar hisobida 4:1 ilgarilab ketgan edi, biroq Bejlek ketma-ket besh geym yutib, uchrashuvni 6:4 hisobida yakunladi, [Tennis Majors yozadi](https://www.tennismajors.com/wta-tour-news/sabalenkas-shock-exit-against-beljek-leaves-world-no-1-in-limbo-as-rybakina-eyes-the-top-spot-in-cincinnati-858912.html).

«O'zimni ajoyib his qilyapman. So'z topa olmayapman. Men shunchaki ishonishda davom etdim — bugun eng muhimi shu edi», — dedi Bejlek uchrashuvdan so'ng, [WTA xabar beradi](https://www.wtatennis.com/news/4563121/bejlek-stuns-sabalenka-in-straight-sets-to-reach-cincinnati-quarterfinals). 20 yoshli tennischi uchun bu top-10 reytingdagi o'yinchi ustidan qozonilgan birinchi g'alaba va WTA 1000 toifasidagi turnirda birinchi marta chorak finalga chiqishi hisoblanadi.

Xuddi shu bosqichda erkaklar turniridagi birinchi raketka Alexander Zverev ham musobaqadan chetlashdi. Nemis tennischi amerikalik Tommy Paulga 6:4, 6:7, 4:6 hisobida yutqazdi, [Sky Sports xabar beradi](https://www.skysports.com/tennis/news/12040/13574886/cincinnati-open-top-seeds-aryna-sabalenka-and-alexander-zverev-stunned-in-last-16-as-coco-gauff-progresses-to-quarter-finals).

Sabalenkaning mag'lubiyati Elena Rybakinaga reytingda birinchi o'ringa chiqish yo'lini ochadi, [Sky Sports xabar beradi](https://www.skysports.com/tennis/news/12040/13574886/cincinnati-open-top-seeds-aryna-sabalenka-and-alexander-zverev-stunned-in-last-16-as-coco-gauff-progresses-to-quarter-finals). Qozog'istonlik tennischi Sinsinnatida finalga chiqsa, raqibasidan o'zib ketadi. Turnir boshlanishi arafasida Sabalenka [WTA reytingida](https://www.wtatennis.com/rankings/singles) 8&nbsp;670 ochko bilan yetakchilik qilardi, Rybakinada bu ko'rsatkich 8&nbsp;316 ochkoni tashkil etardi.

Chorak finalda Bejlek Madison Keys bilan, Rybakina esa Iga Swiatek bilan bellashadi, [Tennis365 xabar beradi](https://www.tennis365.com/tennis-features/wta-rankings-how-aryna-sabalenkas-cincinnati-exit-affects-no-1-battle-elena-rybakina). Sabalenka — US Open turnirining amaldagi ikki karra chempioni va u yerda 2&nbsp;000 reyting ochkosini himoya qilishi kerak bo'ladi, [Ubitennis yozadi](https://www.ubitennis.net/2026/08/aryna-sabalenka-in-danger-of-losing-no-1-status-after-shock-cincinnati-exit/).

Sabalenka uchun bu ketma-ket uchinchi WTA turniri bo'lib, u yana chorak finalga chiqa olmadi. Sinsinnatigacha u Wimbledonning to'rtinchi bosqichida Naomi Osakaga, Torontodagi 1/8 finalda esa Ekaterina Alexandrovaga yutqazgan edi. Amaldagi dunyo birinchi raketkasi bilan bunday holat 2019-yildan beri sodir bo'lmagan edi, [Sports.ru xabar beradi](https://www.sports.ru/tennis/1117340497-vpervye-s-2019-go-pervaya-raketka-mira-ne-smogla-vyjti-v-1-4-finala-na.html). O'shanda shunga o'xshash seriyani Osakaning o'zi — Dubay, Indian Uells va Mayami turnirlarida — boshdan kechirgan edi.
