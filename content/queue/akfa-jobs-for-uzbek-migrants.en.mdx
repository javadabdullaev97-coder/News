AKFA Holding founder Jakhongir Artykhodjaev on August 18–19 said the company was ready to employ about 10,000 Uzbekistanis working abroad, including those wanting to return from Russia. This is according to accounts carried by several outlets; there was no official confirmation as of publication.

The remarks were made at president Shavkat Mirziyoyev's [open dialogue](https://t.me/shmirziyoyev/35206) with entrepreneurs in Khorezm region — that is how several independent outlets report the initiative, citing the businessman's remarks. The presidential press service's materials on the event have not yet cited Artykhodjaev's words, nor have AKFA Holding's publications.

According to the same outlets, Artykhodjaev proposed creating a separate call center and a digital platform for employing returning migrants. The platform is intended for Uzbekistanis who are returning from Russia and other countries and looking for work at home. He said the president instructed officials to prepare such a platform and involve Uzbekistan's embassies abroad in the effort.

Artykhodjaev also mentioned the possibility of employing Uzbekistanis at Discover Invest projects. These are production sites that AKFA-linked structures are developing in Mexico, Saudi Arabia and Albania, according to the same outlets.

Mirziyoyev held the open dialogue in Khorezm region as part of a series of meetings with entrepreneurs. The same day, the president [presented state awards](https://t.me/shmirziyoyev/35200) to a group of the region's entrepreneurs, the presidential press service said.

LEAP News sent a request to the presidential press service and to AKFA Holding asking them to confirm the details of the initiative; the article will be updated once a response is received.
