President Shavkat Mirziyoyev on August 3 approved a package of proposals to expand access to education for people with disabilities, including extra time on exams for university applicants and teachers starting from the 2027/2028 academic year.

## What Changes for Exams

The measure covers two groups. University applicants with disabilities will get extra time when applying to universities, while teachers with disabilities will get it during certification and the exam for the national certificate in general education subjects. Besides extra time, [they will be provided with](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9483) exam materials prepared in Braille, sign language interpreter services, an assistant, and "other necessary conditions."

The exact percentage by which exam duration will be extended, and the format of the additional materials, [the president's press service](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9483) did not disclose. These parameters, along with the procedure for applying for the benefit during admission, will be set out in documents to be prepared following approval of the proposals.

This is one measure within a broader package: approval of the proposals is not a signed resolution but an instruction to work out and formalize the relevant documents. The head of state, [after reviewing a presentation](https://t.me/shmirziyoyev/35007) of the initiatives, "approved the proposals submitted and gave instructions for the thorough elaboration of the relevant documents."

## Scale of the Problem

Uzbekistan currently has more than 168,000 children with disabilities. Educational and social services cover 133,800 of them, while another 34,600 still need to be brought into the education system, [the president's press service reported](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9483).

To this end, authorities plan to introduce a system of targeted work with each family involving social workers: a child's needs will be assessed comprehensively and at an early stage, after which the appropriate educational environment and necessary services will be determined. The new assessment mechanism will be launched as a pilot on October 1, 2026, in the Khorezm and Navoi regions.

## Other Measures in the Package

Starting from the 2026/2027 academic year, instruction at special schools will be organized based on individual plans drawn up according to a child's needs, functional abilities, and level of curriculum mastery. Blind teachers and primary school teachers at special schools will be provided with personal assistants and aides.

Private schools that open remedial classes for children with disabilities near their place of residence will receive subsidies for each student. Every year, initiatives at no fewer than 50 schools aimed at improving the inclusive environment are planned to be funded on a grant basis.

Starting from 2027, it has been proposed to provide children with text-reading devices, speech synthesizers, Braille displays, and other assistive devices on a voucher basis.

## What's Next

Formally, none of the measures in the package — including the changes for applicants and teachers with disabilities — has yet been formalized as a separate regulatory act: [the president](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9483) approved the proposals and instructed that the relevant documents be prepared. The specific conditions for applying the benefit — from the percentage of extra time to the list of documents required when applying to a university — will become known after these acts are published.
