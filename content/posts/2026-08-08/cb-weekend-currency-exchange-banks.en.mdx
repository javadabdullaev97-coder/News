The Central Bank of Uzbekistan on August 7 published a list of 22 banks that will exchange foreign currency for the public on Saturday and Sunday, August 8-9, at branches, through ATMs and mobile apps.

## Which Banks Are Open This Weekend

The [list](https://telegra.ph/Dam-olish-kunlari-ayrim-banklar-tomonidan-aholiga-qulaylik-yaratish-maqsadida-valyuta-ayirboshlash-va-pul-otkazmalari-shoxobchal-08-07) that the Central Bank of Uzbekistan [attached](https://t.me/centralbankuzbekistan/13408) to its Telegram post includes 22 banks: Agrobank, APEX BANK, NBU, Tenge Bank, Hamkorbank, ANOR BANK, Garant Bank, Hayot Bank, Uzpromstroybank, Trastbank, Ipoteka Bank, InFinBank, Business Development Bank, Ipak Yuli Bank, Xalq Bank, Orient Finans Bank, Saderat Bank, Microcreditbank, Poytaxt Bank, Asakabank, Asia Alliance Bank and Kapitalbank.

Each name in the Central Bank's list is a hyperlink to the bank's own announcement about its weekend hours. The regulator does not provide a single schedule of hours for all 22 banks — each bank sets the hours for its specific branches independently.

## "24/7" ATMs and Mobile Apps

In addition to bank branches, currency can be exchanged through automated currency exchange ATMs that operate "24/7." Commercial banks' mobile apps are also available — currency can be exchanged at any time of day without visiting a branch, the Central Bank of Uzbekistan [said](https://t.me/centralbankuzbekistan/13408). The regulator did not specify any limits on the exchange amount through ATMs or per-transaction limits in its post.

## Second Time in a Row

This is not the first time the Central Bank of Uzbekistan has published such a list: on July 31, the regulator already [reported](https://t.me/centralbankuzbekistan/13389) on the operation of currency exchange ATMs and mobile apps over the weekend — that list covered the previous weekend, August 1-2. The wording about "24/7" ATMs and mobile apps is identical in both posts — for the second time in a row, the Central Bank of Uzbekistan has updated only the list of banks, leaving the rest of the text unchanged.

Separately, starting August 9, an [increase](https://t.me/centralbankuzbekistan/13369) takes effect in the threshold — from $100 to $500 — below which a bank does not require an identity document when purchasing cash currency. This is a separate decision by the Central Bank of Uzbekistan, unrelated to the list of banks working over the weekend.
