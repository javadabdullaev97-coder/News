The death toll from the magnitude 7.7 earthquake off the northern coast of Flores island in Indonesia's East Nusa Tenggara province rose to at least 68 as of Monday evening, August 17. Al Jazeera [reported](https://www.aljazeera.com/gallery/2026/8/17/photos-search-for-indonesia-earthquake-victims-continues?traffic_source=rss) the figure, citing BNPB, Indonesia's National Disaster Management Agency.

More than 200 people were injured, Kommersant [reported](https://www.kommersant.ru/doc/8891437), citing Reuters. The quake struck early on Saturday, August 15. On Monday morning, August 17, [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/crl7686r71yo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss) had reported 53 deaths — by that evening the toll had risen to 68.

Aftershocks and landslides continue to hamper rescuers trying to reach cut-off areas, Al Jazeera [reported](https://www.aljazeera.com/gallery/2026/8/17/photos-search-for-indonesia-earthquake-victims-continues?traffic_source=rss).

## Thousands await aid

The number of people unable to return home rose to nearly 12,800 by Monday morning, BBC [reported](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/crl7686r71yo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss). Survivors are complaining of shortages of food, water and medicine. "My house is now just a pile of rubble. We urgently need food, medicine and clean water," PBS NewsHour [quoted](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/thousands-await-aid-in-indonesia-after-powerful-earthquake-that-killed-at-least-68) Rasyid Jafar, a clothing vendor from Reo village, as saying.

Authorities have sent more than 1,800 military personnel and police officers, ten aircraft and several vessels to the affected areas, PBS NewsHour [reported](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/thousands-await-aid-in-indonesia-after-powerful-earthquake-that-killed-at-least-68). Humanitarian aid has exceeded 100 tons, including nearly 5,400 food packages, PBS reported. Some roads in mountainous areas remain blocked by landslides. "The roads are steep, and many are buried under landslides. It was a life-or-death trip," deputy police chief Marselus Yugo Amboro [told](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/thousands-await-aid-in-indonesia-after-powerful-earthquake-that-killed-at-least-68) PBS NewsHour.

## 14-day state of emergency

The governor of East Nusa Tenggara province declared a 14-day state of emergency on Sunday, August 16, PBS NewsHour [reported](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/thousands-await-aid-in-indonesia-after-powerful-earthquake-that-killed-at-least-68). LEAP News had earlier [reported](https://leap.uz/ru/2026/08/17/world-indonesia-earthquake-toll-53) that the death toll was estimated at 53 as of August 17 — the count has since risen by 15.

The state of emergency, imposed on August 16 for 14 days, expires on August 30 — until then, authorities will continue rescue and recovery operations in the affected regencies.
