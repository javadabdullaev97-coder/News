A magnitude 7.7 earthquake struck off the northern coast of Flores island, Indonesia, on August 15 at 05:58 local time (02:58 Tashkent time), the [U.S. Geological Survey](https://earthquake.usgs.gov/earthquakes/eventpage/us6000tkt2) (USGS) reported.

## Quake parameters

According to the USGS, the epicenter was located at sea about 68 km north-northwest of the city of Ende in East Nusa Tenggara province, at a depth of about 10 km. The epicenter's coordinates are 8.310°S, 121.352°E. [GEOFON](https://geofon.gfz.de/eqinfo/event.php?from=rss&id=gfz2026pwtr), the seismological service of the German Research Centre for Geosciences (GFZ Potsdam), estimated the magnitude at 7.6 with a depth of 25 km and coordinates of 8.33°S, 121.25°E. Indonesia's Meteorology, Climatology and Geophysics Agency ([BMKG](https://www.bmkg.go.id/gempabumi/berpotensi-tsunami/20260815050954)), after refining the parameters, put the magnitude at 7.7 and the depth at 15 km. The agency placed the epicenter 30 km northeast of the town of Mbay in Nagekeo regency. Discrepancies in the first hours after a major earthquake are common practice: different networks of seismic stations calculate parameters using their own models and refine them gradually.

## Tsunami warning

BMKG issued its first tsunami warning two minutes after the quake, [the agency said](https://www.bmkg.go.id/gempabumi/berpotensi-tsunami/20260815050954). BMKG bulletins use Jakarta time (WIB), an hour behind Flores local time (WITA) — times below are converted to Flores local time: the first warning was issued at 06:00, with an update at 06:10. "Siaga" status (expected wave height of 0.5–3 m) was declared, among others, for the northern parts of North Manggarai, North Ngada, Ende and Sikka regencies on Flores. The "Siaga" zone also included Jeneponto and Bantaeng regencies in South Sulawesi. "Waspada" status — a warning for waves under 0.5 m — was declared, among other areas, for a number of coastal districts in West and East Nusa Tenggara. Similar status was given to some areas of South and Southeast Sulawesi. In total, more than two dozen regencies across four provinces were under threat: East and West Nusa Tenggara, South and Southeast Sulawesi. The estimated arrival time for the waves was 06:02 to 07:41 Flores local time.

Indonesia's state news agency [ANTARA News reported](https://www.antaranews.com/berita/5695536/gempa-tujuh-magnitudo-di-ntt-terasa-hingga-ke-lombok) that BMKG's monitoring instruments had already recorded waves: 0.19 meters in Sikka regency, 0.36 meters in Labuan Bajo, and 0.17 meters in Dompu regency (West Nusa Tenggara). BMKG recommended that local authorities in the "Siaga" zone immediately evacuate coastal residents, and that those in the "Waspada" zone stay away from beaches and riverbeds.

## Aftershocks

In the hours following the main shock, the USGS and GEOFON recorded a series of [aftershocks](https://earthquake.usgs.gov/earthquakes/eventpage/us6000tkt2) ranging from magnitude 5.0 to 6.3 in the same Flores area — near Ende, Ruteng and Labuan Bajo.

## What is known about casualties

According to the USGS's [PAGER model](https://earthquake.usgs.gov/earthquakes/eventpage/us6000tkt2), about 1.5 million people were within the zone of strong and very strong shaking (MMI VI–VIII). As of the morning of August 15, [ANTARA News](https://www.antaranews.com/berita/5695536/gempa-tujuh-magnitudo-di-ntt-terasa-hingga-ke-lombok) has not reported any casualties or damage: the earthquake was felt as far as Lombok island in West Nusa Tenggara, hundreds of kilometers from the epicenter. Official data on casualties and damage from Indonesia's National Disaster Management Agency (BNPB) had not been released as of publication.
