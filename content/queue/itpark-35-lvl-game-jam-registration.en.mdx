IT-Park Uzbekistan [has opened registration](https://t.me/itpark_uz/20586) for the "35 LVL Game Jam" — a three-day hackathon for game developers set to take place August 21–23, offline in Tashkent and online across Uzbekistan.

## Format and Organizers

The event [is organized](https://t.me/itpark_uz/20586) by IT-Park Uzbekistan, the School 21 educational campus, and the East Games community. The offline segment will be hosted at the School 21 campus in Tashkent; participation is also possible remotely from anywhere in the country.

The format is a classic game jam: over three days, participants must come up with a game concept and turn it into a playable prototype. The themes around which prototypes must be built [will be announced by organizers](https://t.me/itpark_uz/20586) at the start of the event, not in advance.

## How to Participate

Registration is open on the [event page on Startup Base](https://startupbase.uz/uz/events/35-lvl-game-jam) — IT-Park designates this platform as the official registration point. Requirements for participants' qualifications or experience, the minimum team size, and the application deadline are not specified in [IT-Park's](https://t.me/itpark_uz/20586) announcement. IT-Park also does not mention a prize fund for the event.

Organizers invite registered participants to a separate Telegram chat, where further program details will be posted.
