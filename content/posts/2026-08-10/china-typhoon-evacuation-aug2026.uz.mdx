«Dolphin» tayfuni — bu yilgi Xitoyga urgan eng kuchli tropik siklon — 9-avgust kuni kechqurun Zhejiang viloyatida qirg'oqqa chiqdi va hokimiyatni 1 milliondan ortiq kishini ko'chirishga majbur qildi. Bu haqda [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/9/flight-cancellations-evacuations-as-china-braces-for-typhoon-dolphin) va [Nikkei Asia](https://asia.nikkei.com/economy/natural-disasters/typhoon-dolphin-strikes-eastern-china-forcing-mass-evacuations) xabar beradi.

## Zhejiangda qirg'oqqa chiqish

Tayfun Zhejiang viloyatining sharqidagi Yuhuan shahri yaqinida 9-avgust, yakshanba kuni mahalliy vaqt bilan taxminan soat 17:30da (UTC bo'yicha 09:30da) qirg'oqqa chiqdi — bu haqda [Nikkei Asia xabar beradi](https://asia.nikkei.com/economy/natural-disasters/typhoon-dolphin-strikes-eastern-china-forcing-mass-evacuations), Xitoy meteorologiya idoralariga tayanib.

Tayfun markazidagi shamolning qirg'oqqa chiqish paytidagi maksimal tezligi 151 km/soat (42 m/soniya)ni tashkil etdi — bu Saffir-Simpson shkalasi bo'yicha birinchi toifadagi bo'ronga to'g'ri keladi, [Al Jazeera qayd etadi](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/9/flight-cancellations-evacuations-as-china-braces-for-typhoon-dolphin).

## Ko'chirish va bekor qilingan reyslar

Al Jazeera va Nikkei Asia ma'lumotlariga ko'ra, tayfun qirg'oqqa chiqqan kuni hokimiyat quyidagilarni ko'chirdi:

- Shanxayda — xavf zonalaridan kamida 30 300 kishi;
- Wenzhou shahrida (Zhejiang) — 900 000dan ortiq aholi, qo'shimcha 1 000dan ortiq vaqtinchalik joylashtirish punkti ochildi;
- Taizhou shahrida (Zhejiang) — taxminan 390 000 kishi, ko'chirish 8-avgust, shanba kuni kechqurungacha amalga oshirildi;
- Fujian viloyatida — yuqori xavfli hududlardan 98 900 kishi, hokimiyat tayfunga reagirlash darajasini uchinchi darajaga ko'targanidan keyin — [Al Jazeera xabar beradi](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/9/flight-cancellations-evacuations-as-china-braces-for-typhoon-dolphin), davlat Xinhua agentligiga tayanib.

Umuman olganda, 9-avgust holatiga chop etilgan ma'lumotlarga ko'ra, bo'ron zonasidan 1 milliondan ortiq kishi ko'chirildi.

Shanxaydagi ikki aeroport VariFlight parvoz kuzatuv xizmatining 9-avgustdagi ma'lumotlariga ko'ra taxminan 1 400–1 500 ta reysni bekor qildi. [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/9/flight-cancellations-evacuations-as-china-braces-for-typhoon-dolphin) va [Nikkei Asia](https://asia.nikkei.com/economy/natural-disasters/typhoon-dolphin-strikes-eastern-china-forcing-mass-evacuations) keltirgan raqamlar bir-biridan sal farq qiladi, chunki kun davomida bu ko'rsatkich oshib bordi.

## Yaponiya orqali o'tishi

Xitoyga yetib kelishidan oldin tayfun Yaponiyaning janubidan o'tdi. Okinava prefekturasiga qarashli Kume orolida barqaror shamol tezligi 162 km/soat (100 mil/soat)ga yetdi, shamol zarbalari esa 8-avgust, shanba kuni 216 km/soat (134 mil/soat)gacha kuchaydi, [Al Jazeera yozadi](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/8/typhoon-dolphin-hits-japan-heads-towards-china). 9-avgust holatiga olti kishi zarar ko'rdi, 50 000dan ortiq bino elektr energiyasisiz qoldi — [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/9/flight-cancellations-evacuations-as-china-braces-for-typhoon-dolphin) va [Nikkei Asia](https://asia.nikkei.com/economy/natural-disasters/typhoon-dolphin-strikes-eastern-china-forcing-mass-evacuations) xabar beradi. Aviakompaniyalar Okinavaga va u yerdan, shuningdek Xitoy va Tayvanga reyslarni bekor qildi.

## Bundan keyin

Qirg'oqqa chiqqanidan keyin tayfun meteorologlar bashoratiga ko'ra g'arbga, Xitoyning markaziy va janubi-g'arbiy hududlari tomon harakatlanishda davom etadi, bu esa tog'li hududlarda sel va ko'chki xavfini uzaytiradi. Zhejiangning markaziy va sharqiy qismlarida 10-avgustgacha 250 dan 500 mm gacha yog'ingarchilik kutilmoqda — [Nikkei Asia ta'kidlaydi](https://asia.nikkei.com/economy/natural-disasters/typhoon-dolphin-strikes-eastern-china-forcing-mass-evacuations).
