The World Trade Organization's working party on Uzbekistan's accession held its 13th meeting in Geneva on July 27–28 — the WTO Secretariat confirmed it had received 31 bilateral market access protocols.

## What Was Discussed in Geneva

The most recent of these protocols was signed with India. Such agreements are a standard element of the WTO accession procedure, covering commitments on both goods and services.

Tashkent's delegation at the talks comprised 30 officials. It was led by [Azizbek Urunov](https://www.wto.org/english/news_e/news26_e/acc_27jul26_470_e.htm) — the President of Uzbekistan's representative on WTO affairs, chief negotiator and head of the Food Safety Committee. The delegation also included Deputy Minister of Economy and Finance Akhadbek Khaydarov, Deputy Minister of Agriculture Akmal Kosimov and Deputy Minister of Justice Makhmud Istamov.

Since March 2026, Uzbekistan has [brought 190 legal and regulatory acts into line with WTO norms](https://www.wto.org/english/news_e/news26_e/acc_27jul26_470_e.htm) — mostly in the areas of sanitary and phytosanitary standards and food safety, including the creation of a unified Food Safety Committee.

The working party's chair, Republic of Korea Ambassador Sung-yo Choi, called the block of bilateral agreements "very close to completion" and urged the remaining negotiating partners to close the issue as soon as possible.

At the previous, 12th meeting on March 9, 2026, the Secretariat had received 30 agreements — five more than at the prior meeting, with protocols added for Ecuador, the European Union, Paraguay, Russia and Uruguay. Deputy Prime Minister Jamshid Khodjaev said at the time that the authorities were "making every effort to complete the market access negotiations." The draft working party report contained 62 commitment points by March, compared with 56 at the previous stage.

## How Many Partners Remain

The exact number of countries with which Uzbekistan is negotiating market access does not match across different official statements made in 2026. The [Ministry of Investment, Industry and Trade](https://gov.uz/en/investmiit/news/view/148792) reported on March 19 that negotiations had been completed with 33 of 35 interested countries, with talks with Chinese Taipei (Taiwan) and Kazakhstan expected to be closed by the end of the year. The WTO itself does not state the total number of bilateral negotiating partners in its communiqués — only the number of protocols already received by the Secretariat.

## A Long Road to the Organization

Uzbekistan applied to join the WTO in 1994 and has [been actively negotiating membership terms since 2020](https://www.wto.org/english/news_e/news26_e/acc_27jul26_470_e.htm). The [WTO's 14th Ministerial Conference was held March 26–30, 2026, in Yaoundé](https://www.wto.org/english/thewto_e/minist_e/mc14_e/mc14_e.htm), Cameroon — Uzbekistan had not obtained membership by that point. Four months later, the working party is still discussing unfinished bilateral agreements, with the goal of completing the process by the end of 2026 without tying it to a specific ministerial conference.

## What's Next

The next working party meeting, according to Chair Sung-yo Choi, is planned for late autumn 2026 — after the parties prepare the necessary documentation. Its outcome will determine whether Uzbekistan manages to complete its membership process by the end of the year.
