Bangkok yaqinidagi maktabda bo'lib o'tgan otishma qurbonlari soni shanba kuni, 8 avgustda, olgan jarohatlaridan 12 yoshli o'quvchi qiz vafot etganidan so'ng to'qqiztaga yetdi, [deb xabar beradi](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/8/families-retrieve-bodies-after-deadly-shooting-rampage-outside-bangkok) Al Jazeera. Otishma juma kuni Bangkokdan shimoli-g'arbda joylashgan Nonthaburi provinsiyasida sodir bo'lgan, deb xabar beradi nashr Tailand hokimiyatiga tayanib.

## Nima sodir bo'ldi

Otishma juma kuni, 7 avgustda, mahalliy vaqt bilan soat 10 atrofida Debsirin Nonthaburi maktabida boshlangan, [deb yozadi](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/12-year-old-girl-dies-after-thailand-school-shooting-bringing-death-toll-to-at-least-8) PBS NewsHour. Nashr ma'lumotlariga ko'ra, hujumchi — shu maktabda o'qigan va buvisi bilan bobosi bilan yashagan 14 yoshli o'smir. [PBS NewsHour](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/12-year-old-girl-dies-after-thailand-school-shooting-bringing-death-toll-to-at-least-8) keltirgan ma'lumotlarga ko'ra, o'sha kuni ertalab u avval oila uyida ikkala qariyani o'ldirgan deb taxmin qilinmoqda, so'ngra maktabda o't ochgan va hokimiyat bayonotiga ko'ra, taxminan o'z joniga qasd qilgan.

Hujumchi foydalangan qurol kichik o'lchamli va uning bobosi nomiga qonuniy ro'yxatdan o'tkazilgan deb tasvirlangan, [deb xabar beradi](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/12-year-old-girl-dies-after-thailand-school-shooting-bringing-death-toll-to-at-least-8) PBS NewsHour. Hokimiyat hujumning rasmiy sababini hali e'lon qilmagan.

## Qurbonlar va jarohat olganlar

Shanba kuni, 8 avgustda, halok bo'lganlarning umumiy soni to'qqiztaga yetdi: beshtasi maktab xodimlari, ikkitasi — hujumchining buvisi va bobosi, hujumchining o'zi hamda kasalxonada olgan jarohatlaridan vafot etgan 12 yoshli o'quvchi qiz, [deb xabar beradi](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/8/families-retrieve-bodies-after-deadly-shooting-rampage-outside-bangkok) Al Jazeera. Hokimiyat halok bo'lganlarning ismlarini rasman e'lon qilmagan; voyaga yetmagan qurbon haqidagi ma'lumotlar to'liq ismini ko'rsatmasdan e'lon qilinmoqda.

Shanba kuni holatiga ko'ra, kasalxonalarda 14 nafar jabrlangan davolanmoqda, ulardan yettitasi og'ir ahvolda, [deb xabar beradi](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/12-year-old-girl-dies-after-thailand-school-shooting-bringing-death-toll-to-at-least-8) PBS NewsHour Tailand Sog'liqni saqlash vazirligi ma'lumotlariga tayanib. Mamlakat sud-tibbiy ekspertiza institutining rahbari halok bo'lganlarning ko'pchiligida hayotiy muhim a'zoga — ko'krak yoki boshga — bitta o'lim bilan yakunlangan o'q yarasi qayd etilganini ma'lum qildi.

## Hokimiyat choralari

Debsirin Nonthaburi maktabi 10 dan 14 avgustgacha bo'lgan muddatga o'quv jarayonini to'xtatdi, xodimlarga masofadan ishlash topshirildi, [deyiladi](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/12-year-old-girl-dies-after-thailand-school-shooting-bringing-death-toll-to-at-least-8) PBS NewsHour materialida.
