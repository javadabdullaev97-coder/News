The Senate of the Oliy Majlis, at its plenary session on August 8, 2026, [approved](https://senat.uz/plenary-sessions/post-5876) an amendment to Article 69 of the Family Code that allows a child to be given a surname derived from the father's name.

## What changes in Article 69 of the Family Code

Currently, [a child's surname is determined by the parents' surname](https://gov.uz/ru/advice/58/document/563): if the parents have different surnames, the child is given the father's or mother's surname by their agreement. Separately, the law already allows parents, if they wish, to give the child a surname derived from the grandfather's name — on the paternal or maternal line, in accordance with national traditions.

The law approved by the Senate, "On Amendments and Additions to Certain Legislative Acts of the Republic of Uzbekistan," amends Article 69 of the Family Code and adds another option: a surname derived directly from the father's name, not only from the grandfather's name.

## The Senate's reasoning

The Senate explained the amendment by saying the current procedure did not always suit citizens. It also described the change as expanding the ability to choose a surname in accordance with national and religious traditions.

## What's next

Senate approval is not the final step: the law must still be signed by the president, after which it will receive a number and be officially published. As of August 10, the text of the law had not appeared on the Ministry of Justice's Telegram channel "Huquqiy axborot" (@huquqiyaxborot), which publishes enacted legal acts — LEAP News will return to the topic after the document is signed and published.
