Former world champion Terence Crawford on August 17 once again named Benn as the favorite in the Ryan Garcia-Conor Benn fight for the World Boxing Council (WBC) welterweight title on September 12 in Las Vegas.

"It's going to be a good fight. I'm with Conor Benn!" Crawford said in an interview with Fight Hub TV, cited by [Boxing News](https://boxingnewsonline.net/news/crawford-doubles-down-on-garcia-vs-benn/). Crawford, who retired in December as the No. 1 pound-for-pound boxer in the rankings, attributes his prediction to Benn's advantage in size and punching power.

Garcia and Benn will meet on September 12 at T-Mobile Arena in Las Vegas — it will be the first defense of the WBC title. Garcia won the belt on [February 21](https://www.aljazeera.com/sports/2026/2/22/ryan-garcia-defeats-mario-barrios-to-win-wbc-welterweight-title) by unanimous decision over Mario Barrios — 119-108, 120-107 and 118-109. For Benn, who beat former champion Regis Prograis on points in April, it will be his first career fight for a world title, [Sky Sports reports](https://www.skysports.com/boxing/news/12183/13561973/conor-benn-vs-ryan-garcia-wbc-welterweight-title-fight-announced-in-las-vegas-on-september-12).

## Crawford Hints at a Fight With Garcia That Never Happened

According to Crawford, Garcia earlier tried to lure him out of retirement, promising him a fight if he got past Benn. Crawford himself neither confirmed nor denied this version of his possible return to the ring — the comment was reported by [Boxing News](https://boxingnewsonline.net/news/crawford-doubles-down-on-garcia-vs-benn/) without a direct quote.

## Romero: 'Both of Them Ducked Me'

Rolando Romero is the reigning World Boxing Association (WBA) welterweight champion. On the same day, he said Garcia and Benn were avoiding a fight with him. "Two juice heads," Romero said of them to [Boxing News](https://boxingnewsonline.net/news/champion-says-garcia-and-benn-ducked-him/), declining to predict the outcome of their bout. "I don't care about this fight, they both ducked me," he added. Romero threatened both with a knockout: "I hit hard, I can end your career with one punch."

According to several outlets, Romero beat Garcia on points in May 2025 to win the vacant WBA title. Citing the same reports, he will make the first defense of the belt on August 22 against two-division former champion Teofimo Lopez.
