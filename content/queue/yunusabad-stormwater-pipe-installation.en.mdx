The Tashkent hokimiyat (city administration) [said](https://t.me/poytaxt_uz/51306) on August 7 that a 526 mm diameter, 30-meter pipe is being laid on Yunusota Street in Yunusabad district to drain rain and floodwater.

## What is being done on site

According to the hokimiyat, engineering works are underway on the street: a pipe with a diameter of 526 millimeters and a total length of 30 meters is being laid underground. The goal is to prevent water from pooling during rainfall and ensure its timely drainage.

The hokimiyat did not name a specific completion date or the contractor carrying out the pipe installation.

## Context: city expands stormwater system after May rains

On May 10, 2026, heavy rain flooded 23 addresses in eight districts of Tashkent. Two of them were in Yunusabad district, the hokimiyat [said](https://t.me/poytaxt_uz/48911) at the time. According to its data, 35 millimeters of rain fell in less than two hours — more than half the monthly norm for May.

The hokimiyat attributed the flooding at the time to the fact that the city's current stormwater system is designed to handle up to 20 millimeters of rainfall per day. Among the local causes, it cited clogged drainage elements, unauthorized construction and a blocked arik (irrigation canal) network. The authorities announced a general plan under which the system's capacity should be increased to handle 36 millimeters of rainfall per day.

The hokimiyat did not specify whether the current works on Yunusota Street are directly related to the aftermath of the May rains.
