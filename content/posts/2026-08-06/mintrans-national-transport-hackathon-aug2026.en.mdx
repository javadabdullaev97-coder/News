The Ministry of Transport of Uzbekistan, together with Yandex Uzbekistan, opened the first National Transport Hackathon on August 5 — a three-day competition of digital solutions for the transport sector with a prize fund of [320 million soums](https://t.me/Mintrans_uz/28579).

## Organizers and Format

The hackathon was organized by the Ministry of Transport, its subordinate [Digital Transport Center](https://t.me/Mintrans_uz/28571), and Yandex Uzbekistan. The event was opened by Minister of Transport Ilkhom Makhkamov.

"The knowledge, new ideas and initiatives of our youth are the true driving force behind the development of the transport sector and the country as a whole," Ilkhom Makhkamov [said](https://t.me/Mintrans_uz/28556) at the opening ceremony. According to him, the hackathon is being held as part of the president's policy of digitalizing all spheres of the country's life and unlocking the potential of the younger generation. The minister also announced that the National Transport Hackathon will become an annual event on the ministry's calendar.

First Deputy Minister of Transport Mamanbiy Omarov also attended the opening ceremony. He [said](https://t.me/Mintrans_uz/28564) the ministry is currently implementing more than 40 digitalization projects in the sector, and about 5 million people use data from its information systems every month. All 22 government services provided by the Ministry of Transport have been converted to digital format.

"As the next step, we plan to automate these processes — this is where artificial intelligence will play an important role. That is why we decided to hold such events annually," Mamanbiy Omarov said.

The hackathon [runs for three days](https://t.me/Mintrans_uz/28571): during this time, specialists in transport, data, artificial intelligence and digital products hold in-person meetings with participants. The teams' task is to turn real problems of the transport system into working services, prototypes and analytical solutions.

## Three Tracks

Participants are tasked with developing solutions in three [areas](https://t.me/Mintrans_uz/28571):

- creating services and applications that improve public mobility in air, rail and road transport;
- integrating artificial intelligence technologies into the Ministry of Transport's information systems;
- analyzing large volumes of data in the transport sector and developing solutions for their effective use.

Projects are evaluated against five [criteria](https://t.me/Mintrans_uz/28579), each scored on a five-point scale: the solution's practical applicability, technical quality of implementation, innovativeness, effectiveness of data use, and quality of the project presentation.

## Prize Fund: 320 Million Soums

The hackathon's total prize fund is [320 million soums](https://t.me/Mintrans_uz/28579) (≈$26,800 at the Central Bank of Uzbekistan's exchange rate for August 5 — 11,942.21 soums per dollar).

Winners are determined separately in each of the three tracks:

- 1st place — 50 million soums (≈$4,190);
- 2nd place — 25 million soums (≈$2,090);
- 3rd place — 15 million soums (≈$1,260).

Additionally, five special awards of 10 million soums (≈$840) each are provided — for inclusive mobility, regional mobility, data analysis, AI solution, and best student team.

## What's Next

In its published posts, the Ministry of Transport did not name an exact date for announcing the winners or specify team registration conditions — judging by the wording, selection of participants for the first hackathon was already complete by the time it opened on August 5. The ministry said the event will become annual, so applications for the next National Transport Hackathon are expected to open later.
