President Shavkat Mirziyoyev reviewed the progress of the reconstruction of Urgench International Airport during a visit to Khorezm Region on August 17 — investment in the project has risen to $264 million.

The airport's throughput capacity will increase more than fourfold after the work is completed — from the current 300 to 1,300 passengers per hour, the Ministry of Transport of Uzbekistan [said](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9501). The reconstruction will allow the airport to accommodate wide-body aircraft, including the Boeing 747 and Airbus A350, opening the possibility of direct long-haul flights to Khorezm.

The first phase includes construction of a parking apron for 18 aircraft and a new taxiway. The president also [inspected](https://t.me/shmirziyoyev/35056) the new terminal for official delegations. It will house border and visa control checkpoints, a business lounge, retail outlets, a duty-free zone and a round-the-clock medical station.

The airport's modernization is being implemented under a public-private partnership model with South Korea's Incheon International Airport Corporation. In May 2025, the project's cost was [estimated](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/8096) at $223 million, with the reconstruction and construction expected to be completed in 2025–2027. Over the year, the budget grew by $41 million — to the current $264 million.

Urgench Airport currently handles flights from about ten airlines. The expansion is linked to growing tourist flows to Khiva and other cities in Khorezm Region, which authorities [are positioning](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9501) as venues for international summits.
