The Ministry of Transport of Uzbekistan [reiterated](https://t.me/Mintrans_uz/28616) on August 8 — citing [data](https://t.me/Mintrans_uz/28419) from late July — that authorities have drawn up 79,618 citations for fare evasion on Tashkent buses since the start of 2026.

## How Much Was Collected

Each violator is fined [one-tenth of the base calculation value](https://t.me/Mintrans_uz/28419) — 41,200 soums (≈$3.4 at the Central Bank of Uzbekistan's exchange rate of 12,035.26 soums per dollar on July 30). The basis is Article 144, Part 5 of the Code of the Republic of Uzbekistan on Administrative Liability, which establishes liability for fare evasion on public transport.

The Ministry of Transport [said](https://t.me/Mintrans_uz/28419) that nearly 2 billion soums (≈$166,200) have been collected in total since the start of the year.

## How Passengers Are Checked

The raids are conducted by the Tashkent City Passenger Transportation Monitoring and Control service, [according to the agency's publication](https://t.me/Mintrans_uz/28419).

## Context

The rule requiring passengers on gross-cost-contract buses to pay their fare upon boarding will be tightened starting September 1, 2026, under a separate Cabinet of Ministers resolution — the Ministry of Transport [explained](https://t.me/Mintrans_uz/28602) the relevant rules on August 7. The agency has not yet specified the size of the future fine for violating the new procedure.

## What's Next

The Ministry of Transport did not say whether it plans to expand the scope of the raids before the end of the year. Final 2026 statistics will appear, as previous data did, in the agency's reports.
