The Ministry of Higher Education, Science and Innovation [reminded](https://t.me/eduuz/50019) applicants on August 4 of the last opportunity to submit documents for a 100% state grant at ToshTech Texnika Universiteti for the 2026/2027 academic year.

## What Applicants Need to Know

Entrance exams at ToshTech cover three subjects — mathematics, physics and English, [according to](https://t.me/eduuz/50019) a post on the ministry's channel. Registration is through the [my.uzbmb.uz/service/56](https://my.uzbmb.uz/service/56) service, and the admissions committee's contact phone number is +998 77 288 82 82.

All students enrolled in this intake study on a full state grant, according to the Ministry's post. Per [the official admissions page](https://tashkenttech-edu.uz/ru/undergraduate-admissions), applications are open to Uzbek citizens holding a certificate of general secondary or secondary specialized education. Required documents include a passport, diploma or certificate with transcript, medical certificate (Form 086), photograph and PINFL; IELTS and SAT certificates and international olympiad diplomas are also accepted.

Instruction is full-time and entirely in English in a "lab-based" format, involving foreign faculty, international partnerships and exchange programs with foreign universities, [the Ministry](https://t.me/eduuz/50019) notes.

## Four Majors and a Two-Stage Admissions Process

Applicants can choose one of [four degree programs](https://my.uzbmb.uz/university-about-direction/686): Electrical Engineering, Power Engineering, Materials Science, and Industrial Engineering and Management — all full-time, taught in English. The my.uzbmb.uz portal does not specify how many state-funded seats are allocated to each program.

Admissions at ToshTech proceed in waves. According to [the university's official page](https://tashkenttech-edu.uz/ru/undergraduate-admissions), document submission for the first wave closed on June 25, and entrance exams for the second wave are scheduled for August 2026 — without a specific date given. The Ministry's own post also does not name an exact closing date for the current stage: the phrase "last opportunity" is not accompanied by a calendar date.

## What ToshTech Is

ToshTech Texnika Universiteti was established by Presidential Resolution No. PP-274 of September 8, 2025, [according to](https://tashkenttech-edu.uz/ru) the university's website. Per the site, ToshTech offers both grant-funded and paid education, and students enrolled through the 2029/2030 academic year are entitled to a state stipend.

## How the General Admissions Campaign Works

Admissions to ToshTech are separate from the general university admissions campaign: the university conducts its own subject exams and handles registration through the my.uzbmb.uz/service/56 service rather than the general placement portal. For comparison, [according to the Agency for Assessment of Knowledge and Qualifications (UzBMB)](https://gov.uz/oz/uzbmb/news/view/72477), in the previous intake — for the 2025/2026 academic year — applicants who had already taken entrance exams chose up to five universities and programs on the my.uzbmb.uz portal between July 25 and August 8, 2025. As of publication, the Ministry and UzBMB had not announced comparable dates for the 2026/2027 admissions cycle.
