Iran said on Wednesday, August 5, that it had agreed with Oman on the geographic coordinates of a shipping route through the Strait of Hormuz — a joint statement by the two sides, according to Tehran, is in the final stages of drafting.

## What Iran and Oman agreed

Iran's Ministry of Foreign Affairs (Foreign Ministry) spokesman Esmaeil Baghaei [told reporters](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/aug/05/iran-oman-reach-hormuz-route-coordinates) that the two sides "reached an understanding on the geographic parameters of the route under discussion." The joint statement by Tehran and Muscat [is in the final stage](https://www.scmp.com/news/world/middle-east/article/3363094/iran-and-oman-agree-new-hormuz-route-tehran-says) of review and drafting and covers technical, legal, environmental and security issues.

Baghaei stressed that the agreement will be concluded "if third parties do not obstruct the process" — without naming them directly. He also noted that the agreement itself [does not guarantee security](https://www.scmp.com/news/world/middle-east/article/3363094/iran-and-oman-agree-new-hormuz-route-tehran-says) in the strait.

According to sources familiar with the talks, cited [by](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/aug/05/iran-oman-reach-hormuz-route-coordinates) the outlet, the proposed scheme gives Tehran control over vessels entering the Persian Gulf through Iran's territorial waters. Outbound vessels, according to [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/5/iran-oman-us-close-to-hormuz-deal-what-do-they-all-want), will pass through Omani waters. Sources call this one of the biggest concessions to Iran over the course of the conflict.

## US position and the sticking points

US President Donald Trump and Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent [said](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/05/us-iran-war-trump-hormuz-bessent-iran-deal-close.html) a deal on the strait could be announced "today or tomorrow." Secretary of State Marco Rubio, according to [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/05/us-iran-war-trump-hormuz-bessent-iran-deal-close.html), was more measured: there is progress in the talks, but no final agreement has been reached yet.

Tehran publicly denies holding direct talks with Washington. Iran's Foreign Ministry spokesman [said](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/8/4/us-stocks-near-record-high-oil-falls-as-trump-claims-iran-talks-underway) that Iran is not negotiating with the US and does not plan meetings with American officials. Tehran maintains it is negotiating only with Oman. According to sources, [key details have not yet been agreed](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/aug/05/iran-oman-reach-hormuz-route-coordinates), despite Trump's statements about an imminent deal.

The sides also disagree on the terms themselves. Iran insists on joint management of the strait with Oman, the right to collect passage fees, and control over inbound shipping lanes in its waters. The US is seeking ["full freedom of commercial shipping"](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/5/iran-oman-us-close-to-hormuz-deal-what-do-they-all-want) without Iranian fees or permits and insists on restoring the pre-war passage regime. Oman is acting as mediator, seeking a safe route through its territorial waters.

## Since the war began: blockade and ship traffic

The US and Israeli military campaign against Iran [began on February 28, 2026](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/5/iran-oman-us-close-to-hormuz-deal-what-do-they-all-want); in response, Iran blocked ship traffic through the Strait of Hormuz. Before the war, about 130 vessels passed through the strait daily — now, according to Al Jazeera as of early August, [that number stands at about 8](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/5/iran-oman-us-close-to-hormuz-deal-what-do-they-all-want) a day.

## Market reaction

Oil prices fell throughout the week on expectations of a deal. On August 3, Brent [dropped 5.09%](https://www.thenationalnews.com/business/energy/2026/08/03/oil-prices-slump-on-potential-us-iran-deal-to-open-strait-of-hormuz/) to $83.51 a barrel, and WTI fell 5.67% to $79.87. By August 5, prices [had stabilized](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/05/oil-prices-iran-war-houthis-saudi-tanker.html): Brent traded at $79.43 (+7 cents), WTI at $75.27 (-50 cents).

US stock markets, meanwhile, rose. On August 4, the S&P 500 [gained 1.5%](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/8/4/us-stocks-near-record-high-oil-falls-as-trump-claims-iran-talks-underway), nearing its record high of 7,620.90 points, the Dow Jones rose 1.3% to a record 53,178.41 points, and the Nasdaq Composite gained 2.1%. SimCorp analyst Melissa Brown called the rally unexpected: "This is happening against a fairly negative sentiment backdrop and without a real fundamental reason."

## What this means for Uzbekistan

About 20.9 million barrels of oil and petroleum products pass through the Strait of Hormuz daily — about 20% of global consumption of oil liquids and roughly a quarter of global seaborne oil trade, [estimated](https://www.eia.gov/international/content/analysis/special_topics/World_Oil_Transit_Chokepoints) by the U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA).

Uzbekistan does not receive oil through the strait directly but depends on global prices. In the first half of 2026, the country imported $1.11 billion worth of oil and petroleum products — 8.1% more than a year earlier, [Interfax reported](https://www.interfax.ru/business/1106257). The decline and stabilization of oil prices in early August amid talks over the strait could affect the cost of fuel imports in the second half of the year, if the trend holds.

## What's next

The joint statement by Iran and Oman has not yet been published, and no signing date has been announced. A separate agreement on opening the strait involving the US, which the Trump administration says is close, has not been confirmed by Tehran.

Earlier, on August 2, Iran had already said talks with Oman were in the final stage [(LEAP)](/posts/iran-oman-hormuz-talks-final-stage) — the sides have now moved on to agreeing on specific route coordinates.
