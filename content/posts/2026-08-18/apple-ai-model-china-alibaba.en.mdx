Apple trained a separate large language model for the Chinese market with Alibaba's involvement — Reuters reported this on August 13, citing three sources; The Verge [reported](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/980160/apple-intelligence-china-custom-ai-model-alibaba) the same.

Alibaba supported the training of the model, according to [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/08/14/apples-china-ai-strategy-now-includes-training-its-own-custom-model-per-report/), but neither Apple nor Alibaba commented on it. Sources described the Chinese model to the outlet as a departure from Apple's previous strategy — the company had previously relied on other companies' models to localize Apple Intelligence in China.

Apple's new model is meant to work alongside, not instead of, Alibaba's Qwen model and Baidu's technology. Apple has already cleared this arrangement with Chinese regulators, [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/16/apple-intelligence-approved-for-launch-in-china-with-alibabas-qwen-ai/) writes.

Foreign chatbots such as ChatGPT and Claude are unavailable in mainland China, which is why Apple's approach there differs from other major markets, 9to5Mac notes.

## What Beijing required for Apple Intelligence's launch

The Cyberspace Administration of China — the regulator that oversees content and generative AI services in the country — [registered](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/16/apple-intelligence-approved-for-launch-in-china-with-alibabas-qwen-ai/) the Apple Intelligence service on July 15, TechCrunch reported. The condition was integrating Alibaba's Qwen model into iOS, iPadOS, macOS and visionOS. According to Alibaba, Qwen technology will be built "into the operation of Apple Intelligence" and provide "understanding and generation of text and images."

A Baidu representative confirmed to TechCrunch that the company is also working with Apple on local Apple Intelligence features for China, without disclosing details.

Apple Intelligence is expected to launch in China "within a few months" after an iOS update, 9to5Mac writes, citing Reuters sources — an exact date has not yet been announced. The Apple-Alibaba partnership was first announced in February 2025.
