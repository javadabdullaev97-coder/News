The Institute for Fiscal Analysis under the Ministry of Economy and Finance [proposed](https://ifs.imv.uz/ru/press/news/toshkentda-ekspertlar-va-oav-vakillari-ishtirokida-fiskal-muloqot-bolib-otdi) on July 30 at a Fiscal Dialogue event in Tashkent shifting, over the medium term, to a progressive scale for the personal income tax.

## What was discussed at the Fiscal Dialogue

The Tashkent event brought together representatives of government agencies, experts and journalists. The organizer was the institute itself — officially the [Institute for Reducing the Shadow Economy, Improving Tax and Customs Administration, and Fiscal Analysis](https://ifs.imv.uz/ru/about). It is a state research institution under the Ministry of Economy and Finance, established by [Presidential Resolution PP-364 of November 28, 2025](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/7866326) on the basis of the former Institute for Budget and Tax Research.

The institute [proposed](https://ifs.imv.uz/ru/press/news/toshkentda-ekspertlar-va-oav-vakillari-ishtirokida-fiskal-muloqot-bolib-otdi) shifting, over the medium term, from a flat to a progressive personal income tax (PIT) scale and applying reduced rates to investment income. The institute did not name specific income thresholds or the size of the new rates.

At the same event, the institute put forward two more changes for discussion. The first is to unify the corporate profit tax rate by lowering the increased rate from 20% to 15%. The second is to raise VAT C-efficiency, a measure of tax collection, from 57% to about 63%. According to the institute's estimate, this would bring the budget around 5 trillion soums in additional revenue.

## Tax breaks grew by almost a third

The institute [presented data](https://ifs.imv.uz/ru/press/news/toshkentda-ekspertlar-va-oav-vakillari-ishtirokida-fiskal-muloqot-bolib-otdi) on tax and customs incentives. In 2025, their combined volume reached 178.2 trillion soums — 31.2% more than in 2024. That's about $14.8 billion at the Central Bank of Uzbekistan's exchange rate for July 30 ([12,035.26 soums per dollar](https://cbu.uz/ru/arkhiv-kursov-valyut/)). Most of the incentives were related to VAT — 114.6 trillion soums.

The institute separately mentioned the transfer to the Pension Fund — 23 trillion soums (about $1.91 billion) in 2026. The institute also put forward employment formalization for discussion as a way to reduce the burden on the budget.

## What rate applies now

Uzbekistan currently has a flat personal income tax rate of [12%](https://gov.uz/ru/advice/70/document/1325), regardless of income level. Dividends and interest on deposits are taxed at a reduced rate of 5%.

## What's next

The institute has not named a timeline for turning the proposal into legislation. The website records only the wording about a transition "over the medium term" — without a date and without a draft of amendments to the Tax Code.
