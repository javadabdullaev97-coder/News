In Jalaquduq district of Andijan region, the head of a branch of JSC Andijonsuvtaminot was detained on August 3 on suspicion of taking an $8,500 bribe for issuing technical specifications for a sewage connection.

## What the Investigation Found

The branch head, identified in the reports only by the initials K.T., is suspected of promising a resident of the Tuyalas mahalla in Jalaquduq district, identified as B.A., to issue technical specifications for connecting a sewage line to his house. The money for this — $8,500 — was to be collected on K.T.'s instructions by another person, M.Sh., who was detained red-handed while receiving the payment. This was [reported](https://t.me/uzsuv/45325) by the official Telegram channel of JSC Uzsuvtaminot and [confirmed](https://t.me/Department_uz/12938) by the Department for Combating Economic Crimes under the Prosecutor General's Office of Uzbekistan.

The operation was carried out by the Andijan regional office of the Department together with officers from other law enforcement agencies — neither report specifies which ones.

A criminal case has been opened under Article 168 of the Criminal Code (fraud) and Articles 28 and 211 (giving a bribe). Until a court verdict takes effect, K.T. and M.Sh. are presumed innocent; both agencies identified those involved only by their initials and did not disclose their full names or what happened to B.A.

## How to Report Suspected Corruption

The same day, JSC Uzsuvtaminot's channel [published](https://t.me/uzsuv/45321) a separate appeal to consumers: the company listed typical extortion schemes used by water utility employees — demanding money to recalculate meter readings, offering to "write off" debt for a fee — and specified where to file complaints about such cases.

## What's Next

The investigation continues. Officials have not yet said whether other branch employees are implicated or when the case is expected to go to court.
