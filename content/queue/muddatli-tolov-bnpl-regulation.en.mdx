President Shavkat Mirziyoyev signed Presidential Resolution PP-294 on regulating the installment payment market on August 14 — starting January 1, 2027, operators of installment payment (muddatli to'lov) services must join a special registry kept by the Central Bank of the Republic of Uzbekistan. This is [stated in a post](https://t.me/huquqiyaxborot/20266) on the Telegram channel of the Ministry of Justice, "Huquqiy axborot".

The rules apply to companies that sell goods and services to the public on an installment basis — a segment that has operated in Uzbekistan without separate regulation until now.

## Who must join the registry

The Central Bank keeps the registry of installment operators. Banks and microfinance organizations only need to notify the regulator when they start such activity. Other legal entities register through a personal account on the Central Bank's website — but not all of them. Registration is mandatory for companies whose quarterly installment sales volume exceeds 500 million soums (about $42,000 at the Central Bank's exchange rate as of August 17). Such sales must account for at least half of the company's turnover.

## Terms for buyers

The resolution sets [limits for installment payment agreements](https://t.me/huquqiyaxborot/20267):

- the price of the goods or services cannot exceed 250 Base Calculation Values (BCV). At the current BCV of 412,000 soums, that is about 103 million soums (≈$8,660);
- the installment term is no more than 12 months from the date the agreement is signed;
- the buyer may repay the debt in full or in part at any time without penalties, late fees or additional commissions;
- all additional payments — commissions, late fees, penalties — combined cannot exceed half the annual installment amount and must be listed in the agreement separately from the price of the goods.

Operators are also required to [carry out digital identification and verification of customers](https://t.me/huquqiyaxborot/20268) and report transaction data to credit bureaus. An exception applies to small purchases of up to 3 BCV, about 1.2 million soums (≈$104): these are not subject to mandatory reporting to bureaus. Operators must also comply with prudential standards and debt-load limits to be set by the Central Bank.

## When it takes effect and who it affects

All provisions of Presidential Resolution PP-294 take effect on January 1, 2027. They apply to banks, microfinance organizations and other companies that sell goods and services to the public on an installment basis and meet the established turnover threshold. This concerns large players in the market, not one-off private transactions.
