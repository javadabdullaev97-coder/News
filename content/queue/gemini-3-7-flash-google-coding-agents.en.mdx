Google [launched Gemini 3.7 Flash](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/introducing-gemini-3-7-flash/) on August 13, 2026 — a fast, low-cost artificial intelligence model for coding and autonomous agents — three weeks after [Gemini 3.6 Flash](https://siliconangle.com/2026/08/13/google-launches-gemini-3-7-flash-coding-ai-agent-projects/).

Google describes the new model as its "most intelligent working model for coding and agents" to date, [the company said on its blog](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/introducing-gemini-3-7-flash/). The model debugs code, carries development tasks through to a finished result and automates workflows — from web development to business operations.

Gemini 3.7 Flash is available in Google AI Studio, through the Gemini API and in Android Studio. The model also runs on the Google Antigravity agent platform and in the Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform. Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers will get it in Gemini Spark — a service available in more than 160 countries.

By the company's own benchmarks, the new model outperformed its predecessor on several tests:

- FrontierCode 1.1: 43.6% versus 34.4% for Gemini 3.6 Flash
- DeepSWE v1.1: 65.3% versus 49.0%
- GDP.pdf: 34.0% versus 22.0%
- AutomationBench: 30.4% versus 17.0%
- WebDev Arena Elo: 1,588 points versus 1,538

[SiliconANGLE, citing the benchmarks](https://siliconangle.com/2026/08/13/google-launches-gemini-3-7-flash-coding-ai-agent-projects/), notes that on the GDP.pdf test the model outperformed Claude Sonnet 5 by 6 percentage points. The gap with GPT-5.6 Terra is 9.3 points.

The introductory API price is $0.75 per 1 million input tokens and $3.75 per 1 million output tokens, [Google said](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/introducing-gemini-3-7-flash/). [VentureBeat calls this a temporary 50% discount](https://venturebeat.com/technology/googles-gemini-3-7-flash-targets-coding-and-agents-with-a-50-introductory-price-cut) off the standard rate.

## What's Next

The introductory prices are in effect through December 31, 2026. Starting January 1, 2027, the rate will double — to $1.50 per 1 million input tokens and $7.50 per 1 million output tokens.
