The Tashkent branch of Hududgaz Poytaxt, part of Hududgazta'minot JSC, suspended natural gas supply from 10:00 to 18:00 on August 7 in the Nurli Yo'l mahalla of Tashkent's Bektemir district, leaving about 270 households without fuel.

## What Was Cut and Where

Consumers in the Nurli Yo'l mahalla on Navbahor and Ziyolilar streets [were left](https://t.me/HtoshGaz/9885) without gas for eight hours — 270 private households and two wholesale consumers. The organization cited repair work as the reason for the outage, without disclosing its nature or the facility where it was carried out.

The organization did not separately report the time gas supply would resume after 18:00. The text of the announcement indicates that gas was expected to return immediately after the eight-hour work window ended.

## How the Outage Was Announced

The organization [published](https://t.me/HtoshGaz/9885) the announcement on its Telegram channel at 13:10 — more than three hours after the outage itself began at 10:00. The editorial team found no posts warning about the work in the channel over the preceding days.

In the text of the announcement, the organization apologized for the temporary inconvenience and asked residents to be understanding about the work.
