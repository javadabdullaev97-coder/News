IT Park Uzbekistan and outsourcing service operator Sonect have signed a memorandum on cooperation in IT outsourcing and BPO during the Global Business Services Forum 2026 (GBSF) in Tashkent.

## GBSF-2026 forum draws participants from 45 countries

The forum was held July 24-25 at the IT Park venue in Tashkent. It brought together more than [450 executives from international companies, investors, government representatives and industry experts from more than 45 countries](https://gov.uz/en/news/view/197345). The organizer was [IT Park Uzbekistan](https://www.outsource.gov.uz/en/events/global-business-services-forum-gbsf-2026). The forum's program included plenary sessions, industry case studies and meetings with representatives of government and private organizations.

Minister of Digital Technologies Sherzod Shermatov spoke at the forum's opening. According to [him](https://gov.uz/ru/news/view/197345), "thanks to large-scale reforms in recent years, the country has built a modern and competitive ecosystem for the development of information technologies."

## Sonect opens offices in three cities

Sonect is a new name for the company that previously operated in Russia under the Teleperformance Russia brand and is part of the global Teleperformance group. On [July 25](https://www.it-park.uz/en/itpark/news/sonect-has-announced-its-entry-into-the-uzbek-market-following-a-series-of-negotiations-with-it-park-uzbekistan), the company announced its entry into the Uzbek market following a series of negotiations with IT Park.

Sonect plans to open an operations center with teams in Tashkent, Samarkand and Fergana. The company intends to hire Russian-speaking specialists to serve international clients — user support, technical support, content moderation and back-office services. The parent group Teleperformance has [more than 490,000 employees and generated €10.3 billion in revenue in 2024](https://www.it-park.uz/en/itpark/news/sonect-has-announced-its-entry-into-the-uzbek-market-following-a-series-of-negotiations-with-it-park-uzbekistan).

## IT Park cites growing interest from international business

On August 7, [IT Park published a video interview on Telegram](https://t.me/itpark_uz/20585) with Sonect CEO Ekaterina Osina. In it, she talks about the company's experience working in Uzbekistan — qualified personnel and the local innovation environment. Osina also notes the support IT Park provides to international businesses entering the market.

The memorandum with Sonect fits into the broader growth of IT services exports: according to [IT Park data](https://t.me/itpark_uz/20583), in the first half of 2026 its residents exported services to 102 countries, compared with 8 in 2017.
