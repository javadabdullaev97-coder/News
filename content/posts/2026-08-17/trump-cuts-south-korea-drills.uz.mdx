AQSH prezidenti Donald Tramp mudofaa vaziri Pit Xegsetga, Janubiy Koreya bilan 17–27 avgust kunlari o'tkazilayotgan "Ulchi Freedom Shield" qo'shma harbiy mashqlarini "sezilarli darajada qisqartirish"ni buyurdi [(Al Jazeera)](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/17/trump-scales-down-war-games-with-south-korea-why-it-matters).

Tramp mashqlarni bekor qilish uchun endi kech bo'lib qolganini, ammo ularning ko'lamini keskin qisqartirishga buyruq berganini aytdi [(CBS News)](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/trump-scale-back-south-korea-military-exercises/). Mashqlarda dastlab taxminan 18 000 janubiy koreyalik va 28 500 amerikalik harbiy xizmatchi ishtirok etishi rejalashtirilgan edi — 1976-yildan boshlangan dastur 11 kun davom etishi kerak edi [(Al Jazeera)](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/17/trump-scales-down-war-games-with-south-korea-why-it-matters).

## Sabablar: Eron va Kim Chen In bilan munosabatlar

AQSH prezidenti qarorni Shimoliy Koreya rahbari Kim Chen In bilan bo'lgan "juda yaxshi munosabatlari" bilan izohladi. Uning so'zlariga ko'ra, mashqlar KXDRga nisbatan "butunlay noo'rin va dushmanona signal beradi" — mamlakat esa uning davrida "tahdid solmagan va hurmat bilan" munosabatda bo'lgan [(CBS News)](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/trump-scale-back-south-korea-military-exercises/).

Tramp, shuningdek, ilgari Janubiy Koreya prezidentiga Eronni yadrosizlantirish bo'yicha AQSH sa'y-harakatlariga qo'shilishni taklif qilgani va rad javobi olganini aytdi. "Yaqinda men Janubiy Koreya prezidentidan Eron Islom Respublikasini yadrosizlantirishda bizga qo'shilishni xohlaydimi, deb so'radim, ular esa: 'Yo'q, rahmat!' — deb javob berishdi" [(Al Jazeera)](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/17/trump-scales-down-war-games-with-south-korea-why-it-matters). Shu bilan birga, CBS News ma'lumotlariga ko'ra, Trampning o'zi bu sababni qaror bilan "bir jihatdan bog'liq bo'lmagan" deb atagan. U alohida mashqlar narxini yana bir dalil sifatida tilga oldi [(CBS News)](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/trump-scale-back-south-korea-military-exercises/).

## Reaktsiya: Pxenyan oldindan tahdid solgan edi

Shimoliy Koreya Trampning e'lonidan oldin, juma kuni "qat'iy choralar" ko'rishga tahdid solgan va mashqlarni "tajovuzkor urushga repetitsiya" deb atagan edi [(CBS News)](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/trump-scale-back-south-korea-military-exercises/). Tramp shunga o'xshash qarorni birinchi prezidentlik davrida, 2018-yilda ham qabul qilgan edi: o'shanda u mashqlarni to'liq to'xtatish haqida gapirib, ularni "provokatsion" deb atagan edi [(CBS News)](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/trump-scale-back-south-korea-military-exercises/).

Qisqartirilgan formatdagi "Ulchi Freedom Shield" mashqlari 27-avgustgacha davom etadi.
