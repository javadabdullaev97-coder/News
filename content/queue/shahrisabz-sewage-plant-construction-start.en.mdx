On August 5, Shahrisabz [laid a capsule](https://t.me/uzsuv/45370) into the foundation of a wastewater treatment plant with a capacity of 39,000 cubic meters per day — the ceremony was led by A. Suvankulov, chairman of the board of O'zsuvta'minot JSC. Also attending was Murotjon Azimov, hokim of Kashkadarya region.

## What Is Being Built

The new facility is designed to treat [39,000 cubic meters of wastewater per day](https://t.me/uzsuv/45150) using modern technology. Once operational, centralized sewerage will cover 152,000 residents of the city of Shahrisabz and 25,000 residents of Shahrisabz district — about 177,000 people in total, [according to a post](https://t.me/uzsuv/45150) by O'zsuvta'minot.

Construction is part of the project "Reconstruction and Construction of Wastewater Disposal Systems in the City of Shahrisabz and the Center of Kitab District," which is [funded](https://t.me/uzsuv/45150) by the French Development Agency (AFD).

## Funding and Link to the 2022 Resolution

The Shahrisabz–Kitab project is part of a broader program approved by [Presidential Resolution PP-171 of March 18, 2022](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/-5914826). Under this document, AFD is providing a €80.3 million loan for the Shahrisabz and Kitab project, with the total cost of this part of the program at €91 million. The remaining €10.7 million comes from Uzbekistan. Another AFD loan of €24.7 million goes to a separate wastewater disposal systems project in Karmana district, Navoi region.

In total, AFD is providing €105 million in loans for the program plus a €9 million grant from the European Union. These figures match data on the [official AFD page](https://www.afd.fr/en/carte-des-projets/developing-sanitation-systems-secondary-cities-regions-navoi-and-kashkadarya-uzbekistan) for the project developing sanitation systems in Navoi and Kashkadarya regions. The project started in 2021 and covers three towns — Karmana, Kitab and Shahrisabz. Resolution PP-171 sets a 2022–2027 timeframe for implementing the program; the agency has not yet named a separate completion date for the Shahrisabz facility specifically.

## What Has Already Been Done Under the Program

Under the same program, AFD earlier announced a tender (bidding closed April 17, 2026) for construction of a wastewater treatment plant in Kitab with a capacity of [12,000 cubic meters per day](https://www.developmentaid.org/tenders/view/1612804/construction-of-wastewater-treatment-plant-in-kitab-city-design-build-operate) under a design-build-operate scheme; the tender was published February 23, 2026. No separate tender for construction of the facility in Shahrisabz was found as of publication — apparently, that part of the contract has already moved to the construction stage.
