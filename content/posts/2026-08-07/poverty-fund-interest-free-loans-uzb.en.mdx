The Ministry of Employment and Poverty Reduction on August 7 reminded entrepreneurs of an interest-free loan of up to 4.12 million soums for each home-based worker hired from a poor family — the funds come from the State Targeted Poverty Reduction Fund. This was reported in a [post](https://t.me/mehnatvazirligi/20202) on the ministry's official Telegram channel.

## Who Can Get the Loan and How Much

Business entities that hire unemployed citizens or members of poor families seeking self-employment as home-based workers are eligible for the loan. The terms are set out in [Presidential Resolution PP-64 of February 14, 2025](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/7387013), "On Additional Measures to Improve the System for Allocating Financial Assistance to Promote Entrepreneurship and Ensure Employment of the Population."

The loan amount is up to 10 times the base calculation value (BCV) for each home-based worker hired. With the current BCV of [412,000 soums](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/7553336) (in effect since August 1, 2025), that comes to up to 4.12 million soums (≈$346 at the Central Bank of Uzbekistan exchange rate on August 7, 2026) per worker.

A mandatory condition is that the entrepreneur must hire at least 10 home-based workers. The total loan amount per entrepreneur cannot exceed 200 times the BCV — up to 82.4 million soums (≈$6,917). The funds are issued for a term of up to 3 years and are transferred non-cash to the entrepreneur's account, not in cash.

## Where to Apply

Under Resolution PP-64, applications are submitted electronically through the Unified Portal of Interactive Public Services (my.gov.uz) or through public service centers. Loans are issued only to entrepreneurs with no outstanding tax or fee debts. Recipients are required to register with the tax authorities as a business entity within one month of receiving the assistance.

For consultations on the program, the [Ministry of Employment](https://t.me/mehnatvazirligi/20202) provided the phone number +998 71 238 69 96 and the Telegram bot @KQDMJ_chatbot.

## Other Loans from the Same Fund

Resolution PP-64 also provides for other types of assistance from the State Targeted Poverty Reduction Fund. Members of low-income families who want to set up self-employment on their own can receive an interest-free loan to purchase equipment — up to 50 times the BCV (about 20.6 million soums, ≈$1,729 at the Central Bank of Uzbekistan exchange rate on August 7, 2026) for a term of 6 months to 3 years. Dehkan farms (small household farms) from poor families can receive up to 200 times the BCV (about 82.4 million soums, ≈$6,917 — see above) for a term of up to 5 years — for well drilling, pumping equipment, and irrigation systems.
