Polshalik tennischi Iga Swiatek 13-avgust kuni Torontoda o'tkazilgan WTA 1000 toifasidagi "Canadian Open" turniri finalida qozog'istonlik Elena Rybakinani 6:2, 6:3 hisobida mag'lub etdi.

G'alaba Swiatekning taxminan 11 oy davom etgan titulsiz davriga [chek qo'ydi](https://www.theguardian.com/sport/2026/aug/14/iga-swiatek-takes-swing-at-critics-after-ending-title-drought-in-cincinnati). Polshalik tennischi uchun bu — 2026 mavsumidagi birinchi trofey.

Yarim finalda Swiatek ukrainalik Elina Svitolinani [mag'lub etdi](https://www.ubitennis.net/2026/08/iga-swiatek-battles-into-first-final-of-2026-faces-no-1-chasing-rybakina-in-toronto/) — 6:3, 1:6, 6:3, muvaffaqiyatsiz ikkinchi to'plamdan so'ng o'zini qo'lga oldi. Rybakina esa amerikalik Coco Gauffni 5:7, 6:2, 6:2 hisobida mag'lub etib finalga chiqdi.

Final oldidan tashkilotchilar g'alaba Rybakinani reyting jadvalida birinchi raketka Aryna Sabalenkadan atigi to'rt ochko orqada qoldirishini ta'kidlagan edi, [deb xabar bergan edi](https://www.ubitennis.net/2026/08/iga-swiatek-battles-into-first-final-of-2026-faces-no-1-chasing-rybakina-in-toronto/) UbiTennis. Finaldagi mag'lubiyat WTA reytingidagi avvalgi taqsimotni saqlab qoldi.

«Bu titulni adolatsiz tanqid va nafratga duch kelayotgan barcha odamlarga bag'ishlamoqchiman. Kurashda davom eting, o'sing, o'zingizga e'tibor qarating — siz uchun nima yaxshi ekanini o'zingiz bilasiz», — [dedi](https://www.theguardian.com/sport/2026/aug/14/iga-swiatek-takes-swing-at-critics-after-ending-title-drought-in-cincinnati) Swiatek mukofotlash marosimida.

Guardian ma'lumotlariga ko'ra, sportchining so'zlari o'ziga qaratilgan tanqidga — asosan Polsha OAV va muxlislari tomonidan, uning notekis o'tayotgan mavsumi fonida — javob bo'lgan. Torontogacha Swiatek Katta Shlem turnirlarida chorak finaldan nariga o'ta olmagan va murabbiyini almashtirgan edi.

Finalga tayyorgarlik ko'rishda unga ilgari Rafael Nadal bilan ishlagan murabbiy Francisco Roig yordam bergan — bu haqda matchdan oldin UbiTennis [yozgan edi](https://www.ubitennis.net/2026/08/iga-swiatek-battles-into-first-final-of-2026-faces-no-1-chasing-rybakina-in-toronto/).
