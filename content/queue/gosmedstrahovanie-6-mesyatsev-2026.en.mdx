Uzbekistan's State Medical Insurance Fund allocated 745.7 billion soums for treatment of beneficiary patients and free medicine in January–June 2026, the [Ministry of Health](https://t.me/ssvuz/22409) reported. At the Central Bank's exchange rate as of August 4, that is approximately $62.2 million.

## Beneficiary treatment: 591.2 billion soums for 44,633 patients

In the first half of the year, [44,633 patients were treated](https://t.me/ssvuz/22409) at 165 medical institutions nationwide under the program supporting beneficiary categories of citizens. The Fund paid 591.2 billion soums (≈$49.3 million) for high-tech operations for these patients.

The largest amounts went to Tashkent region — 80.1 billion soums, Tashkent city — 75.5 billion soums, and Samarkand region — 63.7 billion soums. Among the patients treated, 58.8% were adults and 41.2% were children. Patients most often sought treatment for eye and heart conditions.

## Reimbursement: 1.7 million patients received free medicine

Under the reimbursement program — which compensates pharmacies for the cost of medicine for patients with chronic diseases — [1,716,578 patients received services](https://t.me/ssvuz/22396) in the same period, and 3,713,563 electronic prescriptions were issued. The Fund paid contracted pharmacies 154.5 billion soums (≈$12.9 million) for free medicine.

The largest amounts under this program went to Tashkent city — 31.0 billion soums, Bukhara region — 23.8 billion soums, and Khorezm region — 22.6 billion soums. Monthly payments rose from 16.7 billion to 28.5 billion soums between January and June.
