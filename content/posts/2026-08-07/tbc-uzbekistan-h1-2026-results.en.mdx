TBC Uzbekistan said on August 6 that payment volume processed through the bank grew 45% year-on-year in the second quarter of 2026, to 38.8 trillion soums (about $3.26 billion). The same day, parent company TBC Bank Group PLC [published](https://www.investegate.co.uk/announcement/rns/tbc-bank-group--tbcg/2q-and-1h-2026-results-report/9707288) consolidated IFRS results showing that the Uzbek subsidiary's net profit fell 23.4% in the first half of the year, while its share of non-performing loans (NPL) rose to 10.6%.

## Payments and Ecosystem Grow

Payment volume through TBC Uzbekistan's services totaled 38.8 trillion soums in the second quarter (about $3.26 billion at the Central Bank of Uzbekistan's exchange rate for August 6 — 11,886.72 soums per dollar) — up 45% from a year earlier, the bank [said](https://tbcbank.uz/ru/news/articles/news-article-50/) in its press release. The company estimates its share of Uzbekistan's payments market at more than 20%. The number of active merchants accepting payments through TBC reached 23,600 — up 13% from the first quarter.

The number of TBC Salom debit cards in circulation exceeded 1.2 million — up 129% year-on-year. The number of TBC Osmon credit cards reached 212,000 — up 200%. Monthly active users (MAU) of the ecosystem totaled 5.8 million in the second quarter. Net fee and commission income for the quarter rose 5% year-on-year and 15% quarter-on-quarter, to 179.2 billion soums.

## Profit Falls for Second Straight Quarter

According to [TBC Bank Group PLC's](https://www.investegate.co.uk/announcement/rns/tbc-bank-group--tbcg/2q-and-1h-2026-results-report/9707288) consolidated results, the Uzbek segment's net profit totaled 20.6 million lari in the second quarter of 2026 (about $7.7 million at the average lari-to-dollar rate for the first half used in the report — 2.6883) — down 36.3% from a year earlier. For the first half of the year, segment profit totaled 41.3 million lari (about $15.4 million) — a 23.4% year-on-year decline. Return on equity (ROE) for the Uzbek business fell to 10.6% for the half-year, against 16.9% a year earlier. These figures come from TBC Bank Group PLC's consolidated IFRS results. They may differ from TBC Uzbekistan's reporting under national accounting standards, which the bank files separately with the regulator in Uzbekistan, due to differing methodologies for profit recognition and provisioning.

The share of non-performing loans (NPL) in the gross loan portfolio rose from 6.7% at the end of March to 10.6% at the end of June 2026 — more than 2.5 times the 4.1% recorded a year earlier. Cost of risk for the half-year rose by 0.7 percentage points, to 10.9%. In the report, the company attributes the rise in cost of risk to the bank "continuing to provision for older loan vintages, as well as adapting to changes in the automated debt collection system."

## Loan Portfolio and Deposits

TBC Uzbekistan's gross loan portfolio stood at 2,305.8 million lari (about $872 million) as of June 30, 2026, according to consolidated results — down 6.4% from a year earlier. Quarter-on-quarter, the portfolio grew 0.4%. This figure was converted using the period-end lari exchange rate of 2.6453, which differs from the average rate of 2.6883 used above to convert profit figures. Growth came from business loans, credit cards, buy-now-pay-later (BNPL) financing and POS lending, which offset a decline in unsecured cash consumer loans. According to the bank's press release, the aggregate loan portfolio totaled 10.481 trillion soums at the end of the first half (about $881.7 million at the Central Bank of Uzbekistan's exchange rate for August 6), of which 19% is business loans, 13% is POS loans and installment financing, and 10% is credit cards.

Customer deposits, according to consolidated results, grew 8.2% year-on-year, to 1,450.7 million lari, though they fell 7.1% quarter-on-quarter. According to the bank, the aggregate deposit portfolio exceeded 6.594 trillion soums (about $554.7 million at the Central Bank of Uzbekistan's exchange rate for August 6), of which 5% is held in TBC Salom card accounts.

## What's Next

On July 24, TBC Bank Group [completed](https://www.investegate.co.uk/announcement/rns/tbc-bank-group--tbcg/acquisition-of-majority-stake-in-olx-uzbekistan/9687231) a deal to acquire a controlling stake (50% plus one share) in online classifieds service OLX Uzbekistan through a joint venture with Titan Investments, which holds the remaining stake. OLX Uzbekistan has more than 5 million monthly users and over 2 million active listings. The group said it intends to offer financial and payment services through the platform while keeping it as an independent classifieds marketplace. The deal's value was not disclosed.

TBC Bank Group CEO Vakhtang Butskhrikidze said in comments on the results that the group's loan portfolio "has stabilized and is starting to return to growth," while elevated cost of risk reflects ongoing provisioning for older loan vintages.
