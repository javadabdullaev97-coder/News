The Tashkent hokimiyat (city administration) said on August 5 in its Telegram channel Poytaxt.uz that 291 of 419 gas distribution points (GRP) in the capital's Yunusabad district have already been switched to automatic remote control.

## Why gas pressure drops

The post on the hokimiyat's channel says gas pressure fluctuations for consumers are caused not only by the volume of gas supplied but also by the outdated control system of the distribution points themselves. The modernization is meant to address specifically the second cause — the condition of the GRP network through which gas reaches homes in the district.

Of the 419 medium-pressure gas distribution points in Yunusabad district, 291 have already been converted to "smart" GRPs — about 70% of the total, according to hokimiyat data.

## How a "smart" GRP works

According to the post, an automated GRP remotely and automatically regulates gas pressure and detects malfunctions without human involvement. As a result, gas supply disruptions should be detected faster, and pressure fluctuations for end consumers should decrease.

The Tashkent hokimiyat did not name in the post either the specific organization responsible for the modernization or a timeline for completing work on the district's remaining 128 points.

## What's next

The post says that once automated GRP control is rolled out across all of Tashkent, natural gas losses in the city's networks should drop to 2%. The hokimiyat estimates this will save thousands of cubic meters of gas. The hokimiyat has not yet provided data on which other districts of the capital are already undergoing or planning similar modernization.
