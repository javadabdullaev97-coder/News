Swedish startup Lovable, which develops a service that generates applications from text descriptions, [raised $400 million](https://lovable.dev/blog/series-c) in a Series C round on August 12 — the company's valuation doubled to $13.3 billion.

The round was led by Menlo Ventures, with [Scaleup Europe Fund](https://eqtgroup.com/news/eqt-selected-to-lead-the-scaleup-europe-fund-2026-05-18) as co-lead investor — the fund is managed by investment firm EQT on behalf of the European Commission. Balderton Capital, Carmignac, Kaszek Ventures, Tencent and World Innovation Lab also joined the round. Existing investors — Accel, DST Global, Salesforce Ventures and HubSpot Ventures — increased their stakes, tech.eu [reported](https://tech.eu/2026/08/12/lovables-valuation-doubles-to-13bn-as-raises-400m/).

## Who joined the round

Scaleup Europe Fund is a €5 billion fund. [EQT has managed it since May 2026](https://eqtgroup.com/news/eqt-selected-to-lead-the-scaleup-europe-fund-2026-05-18); the fund is financed by the European Commission together with private investors, including Novo Holdings, Santander and Allianz. The fund was created to support European technology companies at the growth stage — from AI and quantum computing to biotech and space.

Lovable closed its previous round in December 2025 — $330 million at a $6.6 billion valuation, AiBusiness [reported](https://aibusiness.com/generative-ai/euro-vibe-coding-standout-lovable-valued-at-13-3b). The valuation doubled in eight months.

## Lovable's growth over six months

Founders Anton Osika and Fabian Hedin launched Lovable in November 2024. Since launch, users have created more than 60 million projects, and apps built on the platform receive over 900 million visits a month, [the company's blog said](https://lovable.dev/blog/series-c). Lovable names Nvidia, Adidas and Deutsche Telekom among its clients.

The company [plans](https://lovable.dev/blog/series-c) to grow headcount by 50% in 2026, to 450 people, prioritizing hiring in machine learning, infrastructure and security. Lovable is expanding its presence in Boston, San Francisco, New York and London while keeping its headquarters in Stockholm, tech.eu [reported](https://tech.eu/2026/08/12/lovables-valuation-doubles-to-13bn-as-raises-400m/).
