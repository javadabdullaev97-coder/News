The Ministry of Higher Education, Science and Innovations of Uzbekistan [said](https://t.me/eduuz/50219) on August 7 that applicants can fix rejected applications for transfer between universities through August 10, 2026, inclusive.

## Why Applications Were Rejected

This concerns citizens who submitted applications to transfer to state universities within the established deadlines and under the established procedure. Following review, some applications [were rejected](https://t.me/eduuz/50219) due to deficiencies in the applications — the ministry does not detail the specific reasons for rejection in its post.

To give applicants an additional opportunity, the ministry [opened](https://t.me/eduuz/50219) a repeat editing window for students with rejected applications — from now through August 10, 2026. After August 10, fixing applications will not be allowed, and applications with unresolved deficiencies will not be reviewed, the ministry said in its post.

## Separate From the Extension for Private University Admissions

This is not the same as the [extension for accepting new transfer and reinstatement applications at non-state universities](https://leap.uz/en/2026/08/06/private-university-transfer-deadline-aug10), which the ministry announced on August 6. That measure concerned submitting new applications through the `transfer.edu.uz` and `my.uzbmb.uz` platforms. The current decision concerns a different action: fixing already submitted but rejected applications for transfer to state universities.

Both deadlines coincide — August 10, 2026. Applications for transfer between universities within the country, including state universities, are processed through the same platform — [transfer.edu.uz](https://t.me/eduuz/50135), which the ministry referenced in its August 6 post.
