IT Park Uzbekistan on August 8 [reminded](https://t.me/itpark_uz/20591) followers on Telegram that applications for President Tech Award 2026 close on August 10 for incubation and acceleration, and on August 31 for Best Startup Project.

## How to Apply

Applications are accepted only through the [awards.gov.uz](https://awards.gov.uz/ru/pta) platform. In the [message](https://t.me/itpark_uz/20591), IT Park described four steps: registering on the platform, filling in team and project details, agreeing to the public offer, and final submission of the application. This is the same scheme used in the parallel President AI Award contest, whose deadline expires August 15.

President Tech Award is a contest for technology startups with no industry restriction to artificial intelligence, unlike President AI Award.

## Three Tracks and a $5 Million Prize Fund

The contest consists of three tracks with different deadlines and amounts. The incubation program is for early-stage startups, and its fund, [according to](https://awards.gov.uz/ru/pta) awards.gov.uz, is $1 million. The acceleration program is for growth-stage teams seeking access to investors and mentors, and its fund is $1.5 million. The Best Startup Project contest involves venture investment across 10 directions and has the largest fund — $2.5 million. Together, the three tracks make up the contest's total prize fund of $5 million.

On the instructions of President Shavkat Mirziyoyev, the total President Tech Award fund was [increased](https://www.outsource.gov.uz/en/media/the-prize-fund-of-the-president-tech-award-will-be-increased-to-5-million) from $1 million to $5 million. Under the new rules, $1 million is allocated as grants, and the remaining $4 million as venture investment for selected teams. In 2025, the contest received 6,000 applications.

## A Second IT Park Contest in the Same Weeks

Alongside Tech Award, IT Park is running [President AI Award](https://leap.uz/en/2026/08/07/president-ai-award-deadline-week-left) — a contest focused specifically on artificial intelligence with a total prize fund of $1 million. Applications for it are open until August 15. Both contests are part of a single presidential program to support technology entrepreneurship and are administered through the unified awards.gov.uz platform.
