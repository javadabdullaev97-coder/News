US Ambassador to Israel Mike Huckabee on August 13 called the settlers' siege of three Palestinian homes in the West Bank village of Kusra, south of Nablus, a "horrifying act of terror." Israeli authorities, at the request of the US embassy, sent additional troops to the village to remove the settlers and restore order, [Al Jazeera reports](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/13/us-diplomat-calls-israeli-settler-siege-of-west-bank-homes-act-of-terror).

## Siege Began on Sunday

According to [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/13/us-diplomat-calls-israeli-settler-siege-of-west-bank-homes-act-of-terror), settlers have surrounded three homes in Kusra since Sunday, August 9: they blocked access roads to the houses, cut off water and electricity, and prevented residents from leaving to buy food. One of the besieged homes is inhabited by the family of a US citizen of Palestinian descent — he does not hide his dual citizenship, the outlet notes.

Huckabee commented on the situation involving this family separately: "Actions by those who carried out this horrific act of terror meant to intimidate and harass this family are disgusting," [the ambassador said](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/13/us-diplomat-calls-israeli-settler-siege-of-west-bank-homes-act-of-terror).

## Israel's Response and Troops in the Village

[According to the BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cj4kppdk2qwo), Kusra Mayor Abdel Azim Wadi said the village had been declared a closed military zone, and Israeli troops used some of the vacated Palestinian homes as temporary positions. An Israeli government spokesperson called the settlers' actions "reprehensible" and "unacceptable" and promised an investigation and arrests of those responsible.

On Wednesday, August 12, the Israel Defense Forces deployed an additional infantry unit to Kusra — according to its own statement, to strengthen order and operational control in the area. Overnight into Thursday, August 13, the military also said it had dismantled two illegal settler outposts on the outskirts of Kusra and neighboring Jalud, confiscated equipment, and detained one Israeli, [Al Jazeera reports](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/13/us-diplomat-calls-israeli-settler-siege-of-west-bank-homes-act-of-terror).

## Washington's Position

Huckabee, known as a consistent supporter of Israeli settlements in the West Bank, publicly condemned the settlers' actions — a rare move for a diplomat with such a reputation. He said Israeli forces arrived in Kusra at the request of the US embassy, [Al Jazeera writes](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/13/us-diplomat-calls-israeli-settler-siege-of-west-bank-homes-act-of-terror).
