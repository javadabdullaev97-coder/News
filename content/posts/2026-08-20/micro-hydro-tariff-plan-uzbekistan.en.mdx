President Shavkat Mirziyoyev on August 19 reviewed a presentation on measures to further develop the network of small and micro hydropower plants (HPPs) and instructed relevant agencies to speed up project implementation, the presidential press service [said](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9513).

Under the current program involving the private sector, Uzbekistan plans to build around 3,000 small and micro hydropower plants with a combined capacity of 164 MW. As of August 19, 64 facilities with a capacity of 41.6 MW had been commissioned — roughly a quarter of the plan — while initiators had been found for only 932 projects.

For comparison: in January–July 2026, all hydropower plants in the country generated 5.4 billion kWh of electricity out of a total of 54.3 billion kWh, the Telegram channel of the Ministry of Energy of Uzbekistan [reported](https://t.me/minenergy_uz/20782).

## What measures were proposed to investors

To speed up the program, the presentation proposed several changes to the rules for building plants and purchasing electricity from them. There is no final document with an effective date yet — the president instructed officials to refine and accelerate the rollout of the measures.

- The presentation proposed [setting a minimum tariff floor](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9513) at auctions for electricity purchases and differentiating it by plant capacity. This is meant to solve the problem of projects that win auctions at a "zero" price but are never built, and to give investors clear, stable conditions.
- Design and construction of micro hydropower plants with capacity up to 100 kW were proposed to move to a simplified procedure.
- Projects up to 50 kW were proposed to be exempted from urban planning documentation review and state architectural and construction oversight.
- Investors would be allowed to change the structure of canals, rivers and channels to create additional water head, while small and micro hydropower plants at water management facilities built for their own needs could be built without an auction.
- A digital platform was also proposed that would cover the entire project cycle — from site selection to launching and operating the plant.

## Plans through 2030

By 2030, the capacity of small and micro hydropower plants is planned to reach 204.8 MW. This is expected to provide an annual output of 620 million kWh of "green" electricity — enough for 350,000 households. According to estimates, the program will allow the country to save 187 million cubic meters of natural gas annually and prevent 311,000 tons of carbon dioxide emissions each year.

The president instructed relevant agencies to speed up project implementation, attract more private investment, use financial resources efficiently, localize equipment production and train qualified personnel.
