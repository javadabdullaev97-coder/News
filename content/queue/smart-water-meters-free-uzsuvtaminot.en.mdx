Uzsuvtaminot JSC on August 9 raised the question, in the [Specialist's Tribune](https://t.me/uzsuv/45491) column of its Telegram channel, of whether new customers are charged for installing "smart" cold water meters — under current rules, the service is free for households.

## What Comes Free

The rule is set out in [Presidential Resolution PP-257](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/6033210) of May 24, 2022. Since January 1, 2023, Uzsuvtaminot JSC and regional water utilities have replaced water meters with expired calibration periods for households with new ones at their own expense — meaning customers pay nothing.

The resolution separately covers the case of new customers: a drinking water facility is commissioned already complete — with meters installed and water connected to the building. Residents only need to sign a contract, either through a public service center or directly with Uzsuvtaminot JSC, and have the meter sealed. The meter itself is already installed as part of network construction, so there is no need to buy one separately.

Not only meter replacement is free, but the installation procedure itself: the public service "[installation, removal and sealing of a cold water meter](https://my.gov.uz/ru/service/224)" on the my.gov.uz portal is listed with the status "Service cost: Free" — applications can be filed either at a public service center or directly with Uzsuvtaminot JSC.

The requirement that "smart" meters with remote data transmission become the standard for new facilities was set by another presidential resolution — [PP-343](https://lex.uz/docs/6648585) of October 24, 2023. Since April 1, 2024, digital water metering solutions have been mandatory in all new water supply and sewerage projects. That means the meter a new customer receives today under the government program is already "smart" by default.

## Businesses Pay for Their Own Meters

The rule applies only to households. For legal entities and individual entrepreneurs, the same PP-257 sets the opposite order. Since January 1, 2023, they have been required to install modern meters with remote data transmission at their own expense. The state only allows the cost of the meter to be deducted from profit tax or turnover tax (for legal entities) or personal income tax (for entrepreneurs). The deduction is capped at four times the base calculation value per meter.

Since January 1, 2024, legal entities that have not installed a modern meter are billed for water and sewerage at rates applied to customers without a meter. That means a higher consumption norm rather than payment based on actual usage.

## If Payment Is Demanded

For an individual signing a contract with Uzsuvtaminot JSC for the first time, the service of installing, removing and sealing a meter is listed as free in the [my.gov.uz registry](https://my.gov.uz/ru/service/224). The public service makes no distinction between connection scenarios — a new facility or an existing network. The law establishes payment only for legal entities and entrepreneurs, not for households. Clause 4 of [PP-257](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/6033210) explicitly exempts two cases from payment: replacing an expired meter (subclause "a") and new turnkey facilities (subclause "b"). In other cases, a resident is entitled to cite the service's free status in the my.gov.uz registry and file an application directly through a public service center.
