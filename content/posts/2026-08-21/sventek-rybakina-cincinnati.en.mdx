Iga Swiatek advanced to the semifinals of the WTA 1000 Cincinnati Open — her quarterfinal opponent Elena Rybakina retired from the match on August 20 while trailing Swiatek 4-1, [WTA reports](https://www.wtatennis.com/news/4563969/swiatek-reaches-cincinnati-semifinals-as-rybakina-succumbs-to-left-ankle-injury).

Rybakina, the tournament's No. 2 seed, withdrew because of a left ankle injury — she stopped the match while serving at 1-4, 40-15, [according to WTA](https://www.wtatennis.com/news/4563969/swiatek-reaches-cincinnati-semifinals-as-rybakina-succumbs-to-left-ankle-injury). The match lasted 31 minutes, [according to the tournament's live scoresheet](https://www.wtatennis.com/tournaments/1017/cincinnati/2026/scores/LS007).

The retirement cost Rybakina a chance to reach No. 1 in the WTA rankings for the first time in her career. A win in Cincinnati would have put the Kazakhstani player on top, but Aryna Sabalenka will keep the position heading into the US Open, [WTA reports](https://www.wtatennis.com/news/4563969/swiatek-reaches-cincinnati-semifinals-as-rybakina-succumbs-to-left-ankle-injury).

Swiatek, the No. 7 seed, [according to WTA](https://www.wtatennis.com/news/4563969/swiatek-reaches-cincinnati-semifinals-as-rybakina-succumbs-to-left-ankle-injury) will face American Jessica Pegula in the semifinals.
