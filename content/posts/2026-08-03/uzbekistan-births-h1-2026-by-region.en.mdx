The National Statistics Committee of the Republic of Uzbekistan (Natsstatkom) [reported](https://t.me/uzstataxborot/69161) preliminary birth data for January–June 2026 on August 3: the country registered 373,389 live births, with Samarkand region recording the most.

## Where births were highest

Most newborns in the first half of the year were registered in [Samarkand region](https://t.me/uzstataxborot/69161) — 46,436. Next come:

- Fergana region — 41,773
- Kashkadarya region — 41,261
- Surkhandarya region — 35,461
- Andijan region — 32,949
- Namangan region — 31,441
- Tashkent region — 29,024
- the city of Tashkent — 27,446
- Bukhara region — 17,535
- Khorezm region — 17,112
- Jizzakh region — 16,949
- the Republic of Karakalpakstan — 15,894
- Navoi region — 10,724
- Syrdarya region — 9,384

The fewest live births were registered in Syrdarya and Navoi regions. The sum of figures for all 14 regions matches the national total of 373,389.

## Second consecutive year of decline

Natsstatkom did not provide a comparison with January–June 2025 in this report. An earlier [release](https://stat.uz/img/demografiya-press-reliz-rus-_p71889.pdf) from the committee on the demographic situation for January–March 2025 shows that the number of live births in the republic was already declining at that point: 198.1 thousand live births were registered in the first quarter of 2025 — 7.6 thousand fewer than in the first quarter of 2024. The birth rate for January–March 2025 stood at 21.4 per mille.

## What's next

Natsstatkom's full first-half demographic report — with data on mortality, marriages, divorces and migration by region — had not yet come out at the time of publication. The committee has published such reports with a delay before: for example, the one for January–March 2025 came out about a month after the preliminary Telegram post. Until a similar report for 2026 is released, first-half figures cannot be compared with last year using official data.
