Bloomberg ma'lumotlariga ko'ra, Broadcom kompaniyasi 20 avgustda 100 milliard dollargacha bo'lgan qarz moliyalashtiruvi bo'yicha muzokaralar olib bordi — mablag' Anthropic PBC va boshqa sun'iy intellekt (SI) kompaniyalarining o'sishini qo'llab-quvvatlashi kerak.

Bloomberg [xabar qilishicha](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-20/broadcom-seeks-more-than-60-billion-in-latest-ai-debt-deal), manbalarga ko'ra, qarz mablag'i Anthropicning hisoblash quvvatlarini oshirish va nomi aytilmagan «boshqa kompaniyalar» uchun mo'ljallangan.

[SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/08/20/broadcom-reportedly-seeking-up-to-100b-in-debt-financing-for-ai-chip-deal/) ma'lumotlariga ko'ra, bu kompaniyalar orasida OpenAI Group PBC ham bo'lishi mumkin — Bloomberg uni bevosita tilga olmagan.

Mablag' ikki transhdan tashkil topadi. Kichik (subordinatsiyalangan) qarz taxminan 30 milliard dollarni, ta'minlangan katta transh esa 60–70 milliard dollarni tashkil etadi, uning bir qismini Broadcomning o'zi kafolatlashi mumkin, [deb yozadi SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/08/20/broadcom-reportedly-seeking-up-to-100b-in-debt-financing-for-ai-chip-deal/).

Broadcom mablag'ning bir qismini 2026-yil iyun oyidagi bitim hamkorlari — Blackstone va Apollo Global Management'dan jalb qilishni rejalashtirmoqda, [deb yozadi SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/08/20/broadcom-reportedly-seeking-up-to-100b-in-debt-financing-for-ai-chip-deal/). O'shanda uchta kompaniya AI XPV investitsiya platformasini tashkil etib, Anthropicga data-markazlar qurilishi uchun 35 milliard dollar ajratgan edi, [deb xabar beradi SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/08/20/broadcom-reportedly-seeking-up-to-100b-in-debt-financing-for-ai-chip-deal/).
