Captain Boburmirzo Pardayev, a group commander in the Republican Special Rescue Battalion of Uzbekistan's Ministry of Emergency Situations, died on the morning of August 15 while fighting a fire in the Quyi Darxon mahalla (neighborhood community) in Tashkent's Uchtepa district.

A report of a fire in one of the houses on the territory of the Quyi Darxon mahalla [reached the duty unit through the «112» emergency system at 03:41](https://t.me/MCHSUzbek/46585). Rescuers from Tashkent's Main Directorate for Emergency Situations and personnel of the Ministry's Republican Special Rescue Battalion responded to the scene.

From the first seconds, rescuers fought the blaze while evacuating residents from the burning house. Captain Pardayev, deputy commander of the unit and a group commander of the battalion, [personally led people to safety](https://t.me/MCHSUzbek/46585), but was killed himself while fighting the fire.

The full name of the deceased is Pardayev Boburmirzo Komiljon ugli; his rank was captain. [The leadership and personnel of the Ministry of Emergency Situations expressed condolences](https://t.me/MCHSUzbek/46585) to the family and loved ones of the fallen rescuer.

The ministry has not yet reported on the cause of the fire or the number of residents who were evacuated. LEAP News is following developments.
