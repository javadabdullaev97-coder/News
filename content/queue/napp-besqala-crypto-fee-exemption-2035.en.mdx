The National Agency for Prospective Projects (NAPP) exempted residents of the Besqala Mining Valley mining zone in Karakalpakstan from monthly crypto asset turnover fees until January 1, 2035, on July 27.

## What Changed

The exemption was introduced by a joint resolution of NAPP, the Ministry of Economy and Finance and the State Tax Committee. The Ministry of Justice [registered](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/6212771) it on July 27 under No. 3388-2 — as an amendment to the existing Regulation on Setting Fee Rates for Crypto Asset Turnover Activities. NAPP [announced](https://t.me/napp_uz/2354) the amendment on its Telegram channel in late July.

This isn't a new tariff for the whole market, but a targeted exemption: the break applies only to companies registered as residents of the Besqala Mining Valley zone. Fee rates for the rest of Uzbekistan's crypto market participants remain unchanged.

## Where the Exemption Came From

The president [established](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/8146982) the special Besqala Mining Valley mining zone in the Republic of Karakalpakstan by Resolution No. PP-143 of April 17, 2026. The document already provided for exempting residents' mining income from taxes and fees until January 1, 2035. Instead, residents pay the zone's directorate 1% of their income monthly. NAPP serves as the directorate's working body and issues residents permits for mining activity.

The July 27 amendment enshrines this exemption directly in the regulation that governs monthly crypto asset turnover fees. Until then, the document formally did not account for the exemption announced in April.

## What Market Participants Pay Without the Exemption

Outside the Besqala zone, legal entities pay fees monthly starting from the date they receive a license or permit document. The fee is split unevenly between the state and NAPP — 80% goes to Uzbekistan's state budget, 20% to NAPP's accounts, [according to](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/6212771) the regulation. At the current base calculation value of 412,000 soums, [set](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/7553336) by presidential decree effective August 1, 2025, the monthly rates are as follows:

- crypto exchange — 740 BCV, or 304.88 million soums (≈$25,500 at the Central Bank's rate [for August 5](https://cbu.uz/ru/arkhiv-kursov-valyut/json/USD/2026-08-05/));
- crypto shop — 185 BCV, or 76.22 million soums (≈$6,380);
- mining pool — 100 BCV, or 41.2 million soums (≈$3,450);
- miner — 10 BCV, or 4.12 million soums (≈$345);
- crypto depository — 5 BCV, or 2.06 million soums (≈$173).

These rates remain unchanged for licensees outside the Besqala Mining Valley. NAPP, the Ministry of Finance and the State Tax Committee approved the fee regulation itself in August 2022, but the current fee rates for crypto exchanges and crypto shops were set by a later amendment — reg. No. 3388-1 of March 5, 2024, which took effect June 20, 2024.
