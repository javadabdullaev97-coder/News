Uzbekistan's Ministry of Higher Education, Science and Innovations [announced](https://t.me/eduuz/50500) the final results of the competitive selection — the mandate — for bachelor's programs for the 2026/2027 academic year on the morning of August 15: there are four ways to check them.

## How to Check Results

The ministry listed four ways to find out selection results:

- on the [my.uzbmb.uz](https://my.uzbmb.uz) portal — using passport series and number along with the identification number (ID);
- on the [mandat.uzbmb.uz](https://mandat.uzbmb.uz) website — using the applicant's ID number;
- through one of three Telegram bots listed in the ministry's [post](https://t.me/eduuz/50500) — `@bmba_natija_bot`, `@bmba_natija_1bot` and `@bmba_natija_2bot`;
- at the university itself or at the Knowledge Assessment Center (Bilimni baholash markazi) — using the official results protocol.

## What Is Known About This Year's Selection

The mandate is the final stage of the admission campaign: it is formed after applicants take entrance tests and indicate their priority universities and fields of study. In its [post](https://t.me/eduuz/50500), the ministry did not provide the total number of universities, fields of study, or applicants who passed selection this year — that data has not yet been published.

## What's Next

The ministry did not specify in its announcement the deadlines for submitting documents, enrollment, or filing appeals over selection results. LEAP News will clarify this information as official explanations are published and update the article.
