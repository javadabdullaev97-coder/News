Power supply in Tashkent's Almazar and Shaykhantahur districts was cut for 32 minutes on August 5 after construction equipment operated by a construction company damaged a power line, the Ministry of Energy of the Republic of Uzbekistan said.

## What Happened

The incident occurred at 15:17 on the 110-kilovolt "L-Navoiy-2" power line in Tashkent, according to a [statement](https://t.me/minenergy_uz/20682) from the Ministry of Energy. The damage to the network caused a brief power outage in parts of the Almazar and Shaykhantahur districts.

According to the ministry, the accident was caused by the driver of construction equipment belonging to a construction company, who failed to follow safety requirements while working near the power line.

Power engineers quickly restored the electricity supply through backup power sources at 15:49, the Ministry of Energy [said](https://t.me/minenergy_uz/20682). The outage lasted 32 minutes.

## Ministry's Response

The Ministry of Energy's post includes an appeal to residents: if anyone witnesses construction equipment operating near power grids without safety precautions, they should not remain indifferent and should report it.

The ministry did not disclose the name of the construction company or the identity of the equipment driver.
