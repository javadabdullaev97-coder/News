The People's Democratic Party (PDPU) on August 7 [issued a statement](https://xdp.uz/partiya-yangiliklari/12156) opposing the practice of placing "Shameful House" and "Shameful Mahalla" labels on homes and mahallas, calling it a violation of citizens' constitutional rights.

## The video linked to Chinaz District

The apparent trigger for the public debate was a video address linked to Sherzod Salikhov, hokim of Chinaz District in Tashkent Region — it [circulated](https://t.me/chinozpressa/39166) on the hokimiyat's official Telegram channel, but its content and exact publication date could not be verified directly.

## What the PDPU said

The party's statement, published on the xdp.uz website as "RASMIY BAYONOT" (official statement), does not name Chinaz District directly, but uses the same terminology — "Uyatli xonadon" and "Sharmandali mahalla" — as the video address linked to hokim Salikhov. The PDPU says that in recent days, in certain regions, representatives of hokimiyats, responsible organizations, and mahalla committees have been entering citizens' homes and making demands for improvements. If these demands are not met, they threaten to put up such labels on the home or mahalla — the party notes that this has triggered widespread discussion and objections from residents.

According to the party's wording, such actions violate citizens' constitutional rights to honor, dignity, privacy, and the inviolability of the home. Humiliating a person's honor and dignity, as well as attempts to apply administrative measures not provided for by law, the statement says, contradict the principles of democratic and lawful governance.

At the same time, the PDPU is not opposed to improvement work itself: the party calls caring for courtyards, streets, and mahallas a "good initiative" and the social responsibility of every citizen. But, in the party's view, this should be achieved through personal example, social cooperation, respect for the law and human dignity, and practical assistance — not coercion. The PDPU separately notes that how well-maintained mahallas and homes are depends directly on how local hokimiyats and relevant organizations themselves fulfill their duties. This includes road repairs, drinking water supply, electricity and gas supply, utility infrastructure, and timely garbage collection.

The party called on officials to completely abandon "unlawful methods" of dealing with the public, including the humiliation of a person's dignity, honor, and reputation. The PDPU insists on the principles of the rule of law, respect for human dignity, social equality, and social partnership.
