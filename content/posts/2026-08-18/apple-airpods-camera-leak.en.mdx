A video showing camera-equipped AirPods was found in the macOS Tahoe 26.7 Release Candidate test build released on August 17, [Engadget reports](https://www.engadget.com/2238891/apple-appears-to-have-leaked-its-camera-equipped-airpods/), citing a discovery by MacRumors. Apple has made no official announcement of the product.

The clip was [found](https://9to5mac.com/2026/08/17/airpods-with-camera-get-their-clearest-leak-yet/) inside the test (release candidate) version of macOS that Apple distributes to developers before a final release. In the video, a man wearing AirPods asks Siri to remember a book he's looking at. Siri's voice explains the Visual Intelligence feature: "With Visual Intelligence, your world becomes savable. See something you like? Just ask me to save it for later." The same footage was [posted](https://www.engadget.com/2238891/apple-appears-to-have-leaked-its-camera-equipped-airpods/) on X by user @aaronp613.

In the build's code, the model appears under the internal name B790, Engadget writes. According to the outlet, this could be a separate model from another camera-equipped AirPods version code-named B798, which Bloomberg reported on earlier. That model isn't expected before 2027. If B790 is ready by this fall, the clip in the beta build makes an announcement at Apple's next presentation more likely.

## A History of Camera AirPods Rumors

Engadget notes that Bloomberg columnist Mark Gurman was the first to report on the development of camera-equipped AirPods, back in May. According to Bloomberg's May report, the project was already in an advanced testing stage at that time. The video found in the beta build is the first visual confirmation of how the earbuds' camera is meant to work with Siri and Visual Intelligence.

Both Engadget and 9to5Mac agree: if the B790 model is ready by fall, it will be presented at Apple's [iPhone 18 event](https://9to5mac.com/2026/08/17/airpods-with-camera-get-their-clearest-leak-yet/), expected in September. Apple has not yet named an official date for the event.
