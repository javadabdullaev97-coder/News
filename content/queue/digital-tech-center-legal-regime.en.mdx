President Shavkat Mirziyoyev signed a constitutional law on the International Center for Digital Technologies on August 19 — companies registered on its territory will receive a special legal regime until 2100.

The special regime is meant to provide a convenient, stable and reliable legal environment primarily for export-oriented entrepreneurs in the digital technology sector. This is [stated in a post](https://t.me/mitcuz/24230) on the Telegram channel of the Ministry of Digital Technologies. The ministry said resident companies of the center will receive additional tax benefits and broader opportunities under international law, and called on entrepreneurs to invite foreign partners to Uzbekistan.

The ministry calls the structure being created Enterprise Uzbekistan, though its [law listing](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/-8420999) on the lex.uz portal records the name without this designation — "On the International Center for Digital Technologies." In a [separate post](https://t.me/mitcuz/24231), the ministry describes the law as a document creating a new legal environment for international companies and investors. According to the ministry, it is aimed at developing digital technologies, attracting foreign investment and expanding the export of digital services.

## When it takes effect

According to the [law's listing](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/-8420999) on lex.uz, the effective date is listed as January 21, 2027 — about five months after signing. The full text of the law is posted on the portal as a scanned PDF file. A readable text version was not available as of August 21.

## Who it affects

The special legal regime applies to entrepreneurs registered with the International Center for Digital Technologies. According to the ministry, these are primarily companies that export digital products and services, as well as foreign investors the ministry is inviting to register in the territory.
