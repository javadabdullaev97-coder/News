Iga Swiatek WTA 1000 toifasidagi Sinsinnati turnirida yarim finalga chiqdi — chorak final raqibi Elena Rybakina 20 avgust kuni Swiatek foydasiga 4:1 hisobida o'yinni tark etdi, [WTA xabar qiladi](https://www.wtatennis.com/news/4563969/swiatek-reaches-cincinnati-semifinals-as-rybakina-succumbs-to-left-ankle-injury).

Turnirda ikkinchi raqam ostida saralangan Rybakina chap to'piq bo'g'imi jarohati sababli o'yinni tark etdi — u 1:4 va 40:15 hisobida servis berayotganda uchrashuvni to'xtatgan, [WTA materialida aytiladi](https://www.wtatennis.com/news/4563969/swiatek-reaches-cincinnati-semifinals-as-rybakina-succumbs-to-left-ankle-injury). Uchrashuv 31 daqiqa davom etgan, [turnirning live-protokoliga ko'ra](https://www.wtatennis.com/tournaments/1017/cincinnati/2026/scores/LS007).

Voz kechish Rybakinani karerasida birinchi marta WTA reytingining bosh o'rniga chiqish imkoniyatidan mahrum qildi. Sinsinnatidagi g'alaba qozoqistonlik tennischini birinchi o'ringa olib chiqishi mumkin edi, biroq US Open boshlanishi oldidan bu o'rinni Aryna Sabalenka saqlab qoladi, [WTA xabar qiladi](https://www.wtatennis.com/news/4563969/swiatek-reaches-cincinnati-semifinals-as-rybakina-succumbs-to-left-ankle-injury).

Ettinchi raqam ostida saralangan Swiatek [WTA ma'lumotlariga ko'ra](https://www.wtatennis.com/news/4563969/swiatek-reaches-cincinnati-semifinals-as-rybakina-succumbs-to-left-ankle-injury) yarim finalda amerikalik Jessika Pegula bilan uchrashadi.
