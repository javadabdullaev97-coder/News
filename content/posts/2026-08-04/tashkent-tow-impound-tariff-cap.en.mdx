The Tashkent Traffic Management Center (TsODD) introduced maximum rates on August 4 for towing and storing vehicles at impound lots — 20% of the base calculation value (BCV) per day and per kilometer.

## How Much Towing and Storage Now Cost

According to [a TsODD statement on Telegram](https://t.me/codd_tashkent/291), three maximum rates have applied since August 4 for towing vehicles to impound lots and storing them there:

- storing a vehicle at an impound lot — no more than 20% of the BCV per day;
- towing by specialized vehicle — no more than 20% of the BCV per kilometer;
- the total cost of towing a vehicle to an impound lot cannot exceed 4 BCV.

The agency explains the decision as necessary to make calculations understandable for drivers and to eliminate inflated charges at impound lots.

At the current BCV of [412,000 soums](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/7553336), this means:

- storage — 82,400 soums (≈$6.9 at the Central Bank's exchange rate) per day;
- towing — 82,400 soums (≈$6.9) per kilometer;
- the cap on towing cost — 1,648,000 soums (≈$137.5).

The exchange rate for August 4 was [11,989.46 soums per dollar](https://cbu.uz/ru/arkhiv-kursov-valyut/).

## How the BCV Is Calculated and When It Will Change

The base calculation value is a standard used in Uzbekistan to calculate fines, state duties, licensing fees and other mandatory payments. The current amount — 412,000 soums — was set by [Presidential Decree No. UP-91 of June 2, 2025](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/7553336) and has been in effect since August 1, 2025.

Starting September 1, 2026, the BCV will rise to [440,000 soums](https://www.lex.uz/ru/docs/8283680) under Decree No. UP-115 of June 23, 2026. If TsODD does not change its calculation formula, the maximum towing and storage rates in Tashkent will rise proportionally. Storage and towing will increase to 88,000 soums per day and per kilometer, and the towing cap will reach 1,760,000 soums.
