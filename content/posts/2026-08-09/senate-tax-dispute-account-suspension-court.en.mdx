The Senate of the Oliy Majlis of Uzbekistan [approved a law](https://uza.uz/uz/posts/tadbirkorlar-huquqlari-va-qonuniy-manfaatlarini-ishonchli-himoya-qilish-kuchaytiriladi_892529) on August 8 at its 18th plenary session that reduces the administrative burden on business entities — the document changes the procedure for freezing business bank accounts in tax disputes.

## What changes

The law amends the Economic Procedural Code and the Tax Code. The new provision separates two scenarios for suspending operations on an entrepreneur's bank accounts, [according to UzA](https://uza.uz/uz/posts/tadbirkorlar-huquqlari-va-qonuniy-manfaatlarini-ishonchli-himoya-qilish-kuchaytiriladi_892529):

- if the taxpayer acknowledges the violation and does not object to the tax authority's decision, the suspension of account operations is carried out based on the tax authority's own decision — without going to court;
- if the entrepreneur disagrees with the decision and files an objection, the question of freezing the account is referred to a court.

To go to court, the tax authority must submit evidence of the violation, proof that the taxpayer was notified, and documents on the objection filed, according to the UzA report. The law also introduces an expedited mechanism for lifting restrictions on an account — if the violation has been remedied or a court has denied the suspension of operations.

The report on the bill was presented to the Senate by Erkin Gadoev, chairman of the Committee on Budget and Economic Issues.

## Senate leadership's position

Senate Chairwoman Tanzila Narbaeva noted the need to inform entrepreneurs about the changes and instructed the State Tax Committee, the Ministry of Justice and the Chamber of Commerce and Industry to conduct outreach, [UzA reports](https://uza.uz/uz/posts/tadbirkorlar-huquqlari-va-qonuniy-manfaatlarini-ishonchli-himoya-qilish-kuchaytiriladi_892529).

According to the law's authors, the changes are expected to reduce business costs, ease the burden on courts and strengthen the protection of entrepreneurs' rights.

## What's next

The law has been approved by the Senate and is being sent to the president for signature. Sources have not yet specified the effective date, the law's number, or exact deadlines for filing objections and going to court — these details will become available once the full text is published on lex.uz.
