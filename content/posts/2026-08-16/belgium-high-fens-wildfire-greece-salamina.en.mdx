A wildfire in the High Fens nature reserve in eastern Belgium has become the largest in the country's history and approached the German border on August 16 — about 600 people have been evacuated.

The fire has burned more than 27 square kilometers of the reserve's heather moorlands and peat bogs, [Al Jazeera reports](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/16/belgiums-largest-wildfire-on-record-spreads-towards-german-border). That exceeds the 2011 fire — Belgium's previous record of 14 square kilometers in the same area.

The fire broke out on Friday, August 14. By Saturday, authorities had ordered the evacuation of residents in the village of Bütgenbach and the municipality of Waimes, who were housed in a sports hall in Malmedy, [Euronews reports](http://ru.euronews.com/my-europe/2026/08/16/fires-europe-belguim-germany-france).

Belgium activated the European Union's civil protection mechanism. More than 250 firefighters and rescuers from Belgium, Germany, Luxembourg and the Netherlands have been deployed to fight the fire, [Al Jazeera reports](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/16/belgiums-largest-wildfire-on-record-spreads-towards-german-border).

"The High Fens are burning, and Belgium is not alone in facing this," said EU Commissioner for Crisis Management Hadja Lahbib, [Associated Press reports](https://www.news4jax.com/news/world/2026/08/16/firefighters-battle-a-massive-belgian-wildfire-that-forced-hundreds-to-evacuate/).

Two water bomber planes and three helicopters arrived in Belgium from Sweden, the Netherlands and Czechia, [Euronews reports](http://ru.euronews.com/my-europe/2026/08/16/fires-europe-belguim-germany-france). Sweden sent two tanker planes, the Netherlands two helicopters and Czechia one, [Associated Press specifies](https://www.news4jax.com/news/world/2026/08/16/firefighters-battle-a-massive-belgian-wildfire-that-forced-hundreds-to-evacuate/).

The fire came within about 3 kilometers of the German town of Monschau. Overnight, authorities recommended residents of three outlying districts temporarily leave their homes because of smoke, [Al Jazeera reports](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/16/belgiums-largest-wildfire-on-record-spreads-towards-german-border). About 2,000 residents of the village of Gay, evacuated earlier, returned home on Saturday evening, [Euronews reports](http://ru.euronews.com/my-europe/2026/08/16/fires-europe-belguim-germany-france).

Belgian Interior Minister Bernard Kenten said firefighters had stopped the fire from advancing toward the German border and protected Monschau, [Associated Press reports](https://www.news4jax.com/news/world/2026/08/16/firefighters-battle-a-massive-belgian-wildfire-that-forced-hundreds-to-evacuate/).

## Wildfire on Salamina: Two People Killed

On the Greek island of Salamina, west of Athens, a wildfire killed two people on August 16 — their bodies were found in the village of Peristeria, [Associated Press reports](https://www.news4jax.com/news/world/2026/08/16/firefighters-battle-a-massive-belgian-wildfire-that-forced-hundreds-to-evacuate/). The two victims appear to have been a married couple; five more people with burns were taken to a medical station, [Reuters reports](https://whtc.com/2026/08/16/two-die-as-wildfires-sweep-greek-island-of-salamina/).

Firefighters, planes and helicopters have been deployed to fight the Salamina fire. According to differing figures from [AP](https://www.news4jax.com/news/world/2026/08/16/firefighters-battle-a-massive-belgian-wildfire-that-forced-hundreds-to-evacuate/) and [Reuters](https://whtc.com/2026/08/16/two-die-as-wildfires-sweep-greek-island-of-salamina/), their number ranged from 192 to 260 (as of Sunday evening, August 16).

A second fire broke out in the Selinia area, from where coast guard and fire boats evacuated beachgoers, [Associated Press reports](https://www.news4jax.com/news/world/2026/08/16/firefighters-battle-a-massive-belgian-wildfire-that-forced-hundreds-to-evacuate/). Greek fire service spokesman Brigadier General Vasilios Vathrakogiannis said strong winds carried the fire into residential areas within seven minutes, [Associated Press reports](https://www.news4jax.com/news/world/2026/08/16/firefighters-battle-a-massive-belgian-wildfire-that-forced-hundreds-to-evacuate/).

The fires coincided with the peak of Greece's summer tourist season. Sunday fell on the weekend following the Orthodox feast of the Dormition of the Virgin Mary on August 15 — the busiest weekend of the summer, [Reuters reports](https://whtc.com/2026/08/16/two-die-as-wildfires-sweep-greek-island-of-salamina/).

The fires in Belgium and Greece are part of a broader wave across Europe. In France, a fire is burning in the Landes region (more than 1,700 hectares, 550 firefighters); in Spain's Huesca province, up to 16,600 hectares have burned, [Euronews reports](http://ru.euronews.com/my-europe/2026/08/16/fires-europe-belguim-germany-france).
