Uzbekistan's Cabinet of Ministers, under Resolution No. 440 of August 13, introduced free and discounted fares on flights, trains and buses from October 1 for war veterans and people affected by the accident at the Chernobyl nuclear power plant.

## Who qualifies

The resolution [applies](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/8400823) to four categories of citizens: disabled veterans of the 1941-1945 war, participants in that war, and people granted equivalent status. The fourth category covers citizens who suffered radiation sickness as a result of the Chernobyl accident and took part in cleanup efforts. The document's wording is broader than a reference to "liquidators" alone: the benefit covers both those who suffered radiation sickness and those directly affected by the disaster's consequences.

The benefit applies to three types of transport: flights (economy-class tickets), railways (economy, platzkart/reserved-seat and kupe/compartment carriages), and intercity buses.

## What's free and what's discounted

The document provides for these categories to travel either free of charge or at a 50% discount on the ticket price, depending on the benefit category. Discounted tickets will be issued by Uzbekistan Airways and other Uzbekistan resident airlines, as well as authorized divisions of Uzbekiston Temir Yo'llari (the national railway operator). Discounted tickets can also be obtained at bus terminals and bus stations, [the resolution says](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/8400823).

## How to apply

Producing an ID card or biometric passport is enough to obtain a discounted ticket. The new procedure does not require the separate certificates or paper vouchers previously needed to confirm benefit status. Eligibility for a benefit category will be verified automatically through a unified registry of benefit recipients, [the document states](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/8400823).

## What's next

Starting January 1, 2027, purchasing discounted air, rail and bus tickets will become available online through the "Baraka" mobile app for the social card. Resolution No. 440 took effect on the day of its official publication, August 13, 2026; the transport benefit itself takes effect from October 1, 2026.
