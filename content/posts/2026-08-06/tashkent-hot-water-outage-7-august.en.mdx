Heating operator Veolia Energy Tashkent will temporarily shut off hot water in the service zone of Heat Plant No. 4 (TS-4) in Tashkent on Friday, August 7 — twice: from 05:00 to 08:00 and from 22:00 to 24:00.

## Why the Water Will Be Shut Off

The cause is operational switching in the 110 kV electrical grid at the Beshkurgan substation, [the company said](https://t.me/veoliaenergytashkent_official/1436) in its Telegram channel on August 6. Once the switching is complete, hot water supply will resume as normal.

## Which Areas Will Be Affected

The restrictions will affect the TS-4 zone — covering sections along Amir Temur and Mustaqillik avenues, the old city districts of Sebzar and Karakamysh, and blocks 1–19 of Yunusabad district. The company published [a full list of streets and buildings](https://telegra.ph/Manzillar-Adresa-08-06) subject to the shutoff separately.
