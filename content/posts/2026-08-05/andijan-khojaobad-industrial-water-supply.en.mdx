Uzsuvtaminot JSC said in a publication this week that more than 30 manufacturing enterprises in the industrial zone of Khojaobad district, Andijan region, now have uninterrupted, quality drinking water supply.

## What Uzsuvtaminot Says

A [post](https://t.me/uzsuv/45338) on the company's national Telegram channel says more than 30 manufacturing enterprises operating in the Khojaobad district industrial zone are receiving water without interruptions. The company did not name a specific infrastructure facility that enabled the water supply in the post.

## What the Regional Division Says About Water Supply in the District

The company's regional division — [Andijon suv ta'minoti](https://t.me/uzsuvandijon/20633) — separately reported on water supply in the same district. The sources do not establish a direct link between this publication and the national channel's post about the 30-plus enterprises. Both reports concern Khojaobad district, but different facilities — the industrial zone as a whole and specifically the "Yangi hayot" free economic zone. According to the regional channel, Khojaobad district is an industrial territory where hundreds of business entities currently operate. To ensure stable operation of production facilities in the free economic zone at the "Yangi hayot" mahalla (Uzbek for "New Life"), a 3-kilometer water pipeline is being laid. In parallel, two new wells are being drilled and three water storage tanks with a capacity of 500 cubic meters each are being built.

The regional channel describes this work as ongoing, not completed — the phrase "olib borilmoqda" means "currently underway." The company did not give an exact start or completion date for the work, or the amount invested in the project. It is unclear whether the "Yangi hayot" free economic zone is among the same 30-plus enterprises mentioned by the national channel or represents a separate stage of the district's infrastructure development — the sources do not clarify this.
