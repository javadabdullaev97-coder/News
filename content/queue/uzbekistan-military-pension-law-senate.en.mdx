The Senate of the Oliy Majlis of Uzbekistan [approved](https://uza.uz/uz/posts/ozbekiston-respublikasi-oliy-mazhlisi-senatining-on-sakkizinchi-yalpi-mazhlisi-togrisida-ahborot_892434) a law on state pension provision for military personnel at its eighteenth plenary session on August 7 — the [maximum pension](https://uza.uz/uz/posts/harbiy-sohada-30-yildan-ortiq-xizmat-qilgan-shaxslar-uchun-pensiya-hisoblash-miqdori-oshiriladi_892341) for over 30 years of service will rise from 75% to 100%. The document's full title is the law "On State Pension Provision for Military Personnel (Officers) and Members of Their Families."

## What Changes in Pension Provision

The law establishes, for the first time at the level of a separate legal act, the circle of persons entitled to state pension provision, [according to a UzA report](https://uza.uz/uz/posts/harbiy-sohada-30-yildan-ortiq-xizmat-qilgan-shaxslar-uchun-pensiya-hisoblash-miqdori-oshiriladi_892341). The document also defines the conditions for granting seniority pensions, disability pensions, and survivor pensions — along with the procedure for calculating, recalculating, and paying them. Until now, this area was regulated mainly by acts adopted back in the Soviet period, together with a number of international agreements.

The main change is an increase in the maximum pension for those who have served more than 30 calendar years: from 75% to 100% of the calculated amount. The higher rate applies not only to those who retire in the future, but also to current pensioners with more than 30 years of service. It also applies to their family members.

The law also [strengthens guarantees](https://uza.uz/uz/posts/harbiy-sohada-30-yildan-ortiq-xizmat-qilgan-shaxslar-uchun-pensiya-hisoblash-miqdori-oshiriladi_892341) for granting survivor pensions to families of fallen servicemen. This concerns those who died in the line of duty or died from wounds or illness sustained during service.

## Who Presented the Bill

The bill was presented by Kutbiddin Burkhanov, chairman of the Senate Committee on Defense and Security. He noted that the law continues reforms in social support for military personnel and law enforcement officers. The document serves the [comprehensive streamlining](https://uza.uz/uz/posts/harbiy-sohada-30-yildan-ortiq-xizmat-qilgan-shaxslar-uchun-pensiya-hisoblash-miqdori-oshiriladi_892341) of their state pension provision on the basis of national rather than Soviet-era legislation.

## Part of a Package of Six Laws

The law on military pensions is one of six laws [approved by the Senate](https://uza.uz/uz/posts/ozbekiston-respublikasi-oliy-mazhlisi-senatining-on-sakkizinchi-yalpi-mazhlisi-togrisida-ahborot_892434) on the first day of its eighteenth plenary session on August 7. On the same day, senators approved laws on market surveillance, on general product safety, amendments to the law on state pension provision for citizens, amendments to the law "On Advertising" (concerning credit advertising), and a constitutional law on the Presidential Administration.

Work on the military pension reform began earlier: back in April 2026, the Ministry of Defense [reported](https://gov.uz/ru/mudofaa/news/view/150410) that the bill set the same 100% pension for those with 30 years of service.

## What's Next

The law is being sent to the president of Uzbekistan for signature. At the time of publication, the full text had not yet been posted on lex.uz — it should appear after signing and official publication. The exact date the law takes effect has not yet been announced.
