The my.gov.uz portal reported in its Telegram channel on July 30 that final entrance exam results for applicants to academic lyceums at Uzbekistan's universities for the 2026/2027 academic year are now available in their personal accounts.

## How to check the result

Results can be checked through the personal account on the my.gov.uz portal or in the MyGov app, [the publication says](https://t.me/MyGovUz/3956):

1. Log in to "Personal Account" on my.gov.uz or in the MyGov app.
2. Open the "My Applications" section.
3. Find the academic lyceum application — status and result are shown on its card.

## How the list is compiled and where to appeal

The final list of applicants — the admission list — is compiled based on entrance test scores and the priority the applicant indicated in the application: up to three academic lyceums in order of preference. If the score is sufficient for the first-priority lyceum, admission goes there; if not, the second choice is considered, then the third, [according to the admission regulation](https://regulation.gov.uz/oz/d/78599). Where scores are tied at the last passing spot, all applicants with that score are admitted.

Applicants who disagree with their test result may file an appeal with the Knowledge Assessment Agency — in writing or electronically — within three business days after the results are published. Appeals regarding test results are reviewed within two weeks, [the regulation states](https://regulation.gov.uz/oz/d/78599).

## Who runs admissions

The academic lyceums mentioned in the announcement operate under universities. Admission to them is overseen by the Ministry of Higher Education, Science and Innovation — through the "Academic Lyceum Admission" service on the my.edu.uz portal — not by the school education authority. As of August 6, the service's page shows the status "admission period closed," [according to my.edu.uz data](https://my.edu.uz/ru/services/15).
