The death toll from an earthquake in western Colombia surpassed 280 as of August 15 — five days after a magnitude 7.4 tremor, international news agencies reported, citing the country's authorities.

The earthquake struck on the morning of August 10. Its epicenter was in the Choco department, near the town of San Jose del Palmar, 400 km west of Bogota, [Al Jazeera reported](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/11/colombia-quake-frantic-search-for-survivors-as-over-2700-reported-missing). Cali, Pereira, Manizales and Quibdo were hit hardest, according to CBS News. The port city of Buenaventura also suffered serious damage, Al Jazeera noted.

As of August 15, more than 400 people remain missing, according to [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/video/newsfeed/2026/8/15/08-15-2026-colombia-quake-reported-mp4). Days earlier, [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/colombia-earthquake-death-toll-rises-frantic-search-for-survivors/) cited two estimates of the number of missing: about 500 according to official figures from Colombian authorities, and more than 4,100 according to independent civilian databases. The figures diverge because search-and-rescue operations are still underway, making an exact count impossible for now.

## Buenaventura: Residents Blame Authorities for Slow Aid

In the port city of Buenaventura, the earthquake destroyed about 540 homes and damaged another 3,416, [Al Jazeera reported](https://www.aljazeera.com/features/longform/2026/8/15/buenaventura-residents-criticise-state-neglect-after-colombias-earthquake). Resident Alfonso Grueso told the outlet that after the mayor's initial visit, virtually no aid arrived from authorities. Resident Betsy Tenorio said help came for only one hour.

## What Is Known About the Scale of the Disaster

Nationwide, the earthquake affected more than 25,800 families, [CBS News reported](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/colombia-earthquake-death-toll-rises-frantic-search-for-survivors/), citing authorities. Colombian President Abelardo de la Espriella said the government's main efforts are focused on searching for the missing.

In Cali, clearing the rubble is not yet complete, and relatives of the missing continue to demand information from authorities, [Al Jazeera noted](https://www.aljazeera.com/video/newsfeed/2026/8/15/08-15-2026-colombia-quake-reported-mp4).
