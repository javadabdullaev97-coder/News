Singer Shakira announced on August 18 a $1.25 million fundraising campaign to rebuild schools in Colombia's western Chocó department, the region hardest hit by the August 10 earthquake, [AP News](https://www.wral.com/news/ap/24d2e-shakira-makes-surprise-visit-to-earthquake-damaged-colombian-schools-and-says-she-will-help-rebuild/) reported.

The singer traveled personally to Quibdó, the capital of Chocó. The funds will come from the FIFA Global Education Fund and promoter Live Nation — $500,000 from each. The Development Bank of Latin America and the Caribbean (CAF) will contribute another $250,000, AP reported. The money will go toward rebuilding schools and getting children back into classrooms.

"What worries me most is that schools don't get rebuilt once the emergency is over," Shakira said.

The project has also been joined by a foundation run by Howard Buffett, son of American investor Warren Buffett — it will work with Shakira's own charitable foundation, Pies Descalzos, AP noted.

A magnitude 7.4 earthquake struck Colombia on August 10, centered in the Chocó department, Al Jazeera [reported](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/10/7-4-magnitude-earthquake-hits-colombia-killing-at-least-20). By August 17, the death toll had risen to 289, Al Jazeera [wrote](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/17/as-rubble-clears-colombia-begins-focusing-on-recovery-after-the-earthquake) — LEAP News [previously reported](https://leap.uz/ru/2026/08/18/colombia-earthquake-toll-289-damage) on the damage and the authorities' response in more detail.

## What's Next

This Sunday, August 23, Shakira will perform alongside Maluma and Sebastián Yatra at a charity concert in Bogotá, AP reported.
