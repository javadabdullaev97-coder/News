Yemen's Houthi movement attacked Saudi Aramco's oil refinery in Jizan in southwestern Saudi Arabia with a drone on Sunday morning, August 9 — a fire broke out at the facility and was later extinguished, [reported](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/9/saudi-arabia-says-fire-extinguished-at-aramco-facility-in-jizan) Al Jazeera.

## What happened

Saudi Aramco's industrial safety teams extinguished the fire at one of the refinery's facilities in Jizan, Saudi Arabia's Ministry of Energy [said](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/9/saudi-arabia-says-fire-extinguished-at-aramco-facility-in-jizan). There were no casualties, Saudi authorities [confirmed](https://www.vedomosti.ru/politics/news/2026/08/09/1219800-husiti-atakovali). The ministry did not officially name the cause of the fire, stating that "competent authorities are completing the necessary procedures to resolve the incident."

The refinery in Jizan, on the Red Sea coast near the border with Yemen, processes 400,000 barrels of oil per day, [notes](https://www.kommersant.ru/doc/8875761) Kommersant.

## Houthis claim responsibility

Houthi armed forces spokesman Yahya Sarea said the movement "managed to strike the Aramco refinery in Jizan with a drone, and the strike was precise," [reports](http://ru.euronews.com/my-europe/2026/08/09/yemens-houthis-claim-an-attack-on-a-saudi-arabian-refinery) Euronews. According to him, the attack was a response to Saudi drones violating the airspace of Yemen's Saada and Hajjah provinces, [reports](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/9/saudi-arabia-says-fire-extinguished-at-aramco-facility-in-jizan) Al Jazeera.

## Not the first strike on this refinery

In late July, shelling forced Aramco to halt operations at the Jizan refinery — at the time, a missile launch at the province occurred three days before the plant's shutdown, [recalls](https://www.kommersant.ru/doc/8875761) Kommersant. Vedomosti [points to](https://www.vedomosti.ru/politics/news/2026/08/09/1219800-husiti-atakovali) a series of Houthi strikes in recent weeks: on oil transport facilities on July 27, on a tanker on August 5, on a troop camp on August 7.

The attack on the Jizan refinery comes amid renewed military confrontation between the Houthis and government forces of Yemen and Saudi Arabia — on August 6-7, the Houthis [struck](https://leap.uz/en/2026/08/07/houthi-yemen-saudi-escalation-aug2026) troops in Marib and Hadramawt and shelled the Saudi province of Najran.
