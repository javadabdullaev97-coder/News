From September 1, 2026, passengers on city buses operating under gross contracts must pay the fare or confirm a benefit when boarding — this is set out in [Cabinet of Ministers Resolution No. 276](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/8244019) of June 3.

## What changes

The resolution introduces mandatory fare payment right at the entrance for regular city bus routes organized under the gross-contract system. This can be done in three ways, [the document says](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/8244019):

- tap a transport card on the validator;
- pay contactlessly with a bank card or smartphone (NFC, QR code, biometrics and other contactless technologies);
- confirm eligibility for discounted or free travel with a social or transport card.

If a passenger has not paid the fare or confirmed a benefit by any of these methods when boarding, they are considered a fare dodger and subject to administrative liability under the established procedure. The resolution does not specify the amount of the penalty.

Under the gross-contract system, the carrier receives a fixed payment from the city rather than living off fare revenue on the route. This model applies to regular municipal bus routes in Tashkent and regional centers of Uzbekistan, so the new requirement will primarily affect the capital's network — Tashkent's entire city bus fleet has already switched to this model.

The same document, in a separate clause, zeroes out the recycling fee on imports of new buses and electric buses in category M3 (60 or more passengers) meeting Euro-5 standard or higher — the benefit will remain in effect until December 31, 2027, [according to the resolution](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/8244019).

## When it took effect

Resolution No. 276 was signed on June 3, 2026, and took effect the next day, June 4. The mandatory boarding-payment requirement takes effect later than the rest of the document's provisions, on a delayed date — September 1, 2026. The Ministry of Justice's "Huquqiy axborot" Telegram channel [published](https://t.me/huquqiyaxborot/19930) a brief summary of the resolution on June 3.

The Ministry of Transport briefly [mentioned](https://t.me/Mintrans_uz/28602) the upcoming tightening on August 7 — against the backdrop of [its own statistics](https://leap.uz/en/2026/08/08/tashkent-bus-fare-evaders-80k-fined-aug2026) showing it had fined nearly 80,000 fare dodgers on Tashkent buses since the start of the year — but without details on payment methods or the validator requirement set out in Resolution No. 276 itself.

## What's next

By September 1, carriers under gross contracts must equip their buses with validators supporting card, smartphone and social-card payments for beneficiaries. The resolution does not contain a separate timeline for equipping the fleet or a list of routes where the system must be operational on day one — these details may appear later in bylaws or clarifications from relevant agencies.
