Google will stop making Pixel smartphones, watches and earbuds in
China starting in 2027, moving production to Vietnam and India.
[Nikkei Asia](https://asia.nikkei.com/spotlight/supply-chain/exclusive-google-plans-to-stop-making-pixel-products-in-china-in-2027)
reported this on August 18, citing suppliers.

According to Nikkei Asia, cited by several independent outlets
referencing the same report, Google has notified suppliers of its
plans for
[2026](https://asia.nikkei.com/spotlight/supply-chain/exclusive-google-plans-to-stop-making-pixel-products-in-china-in-2027).
The company expects to increase Pixel shipments by 8-10%, to about
13 million phones, up from 12 million shipped in 2025.

According to information reported by Nikkei Asia and repeated by
outlets citing it, Google does not sell Pixel in the Chinese market.
That, according to the same data, makes the exit easier compared
with competitors that have retail sales in China. According to the
same sources, if the plan is carried out,
[Google will become the second major global smartphone maker after
Samsung Electronics](https://asia.nikkei.com/spotlight/supply-chain/exclusive-google-plans-to-stop-making-pixel-products-in-china-in-2027)
to fully move assembly outside China.

## Vietnam Already Assembles High-End Pixel Phones

Google's confidence in the timeline is backed by this year's
experience. As early as January 13, 2026, Nikkei Asia
[reported](https://asia.nikkei.com/spotlight/supply-chain/google-to-develop-and-make-high-end-phones-in-vietnam-this-year)
that the company was beginning to develop and make flagship
smartphones in Vietnam "from scratch." Previously, the country only
assembled already-finished models. Apple is following a similar
path, according to the agency: the company is building a similar
full production cycle for the iPhone in India, moving its supply
chain away from China.
