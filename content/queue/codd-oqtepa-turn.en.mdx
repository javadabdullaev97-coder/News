The Tashkent Center for Traffic Organization (TsODD) said the left-turn scheme at the Oqtepa square intersection has changed: drivers now turn left directly on a traffic light signal, without detouring via Muqimiy Street.

Previously, drivers heading from Lutfiy Street toward Nurafshon and Seoul streets had to turn onto Muqimiy Street and make a U-turn there. [TsODD said](https://t.me/codd_tashkent/302) drivers now perform this maneuver directly at the intersection — on the traffic light signal, without entering the adjacent street.

TsODD said the new left-turn scheme was introduced to simplify traffic flow and cut unnecessary mileage for drivers. It is meant to prevent congestion at the intersection.
