The US Senate on Friday, August 7, [approved](https://www.interfax.ru/world/1108149) a bill on new sanctions against Russia by a vote of 86 to 11, which also introduces tariffs on imports from Russia.

## What the Bill Includes

The bill, named the "Lindsey O. Graham Sanctioning Russia and Iran Act of 2026," [passed the Senate](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-07/us-senate-passes-russia-sanctions-bill-championed-by-graham) with bipartisan support and combines two previously separate packages: sanctions against Russia, and an extension of sanctions against Iran through 2031 with secondary measures against third-country companies doing business with Tehran, Kommersant [writes](https://www.kommersant.ru/doc/8875144).

The Russia-related part of the law gives the president the authority to [impose a tariff](https://www.kommersant.ru/doc/8875144) of up to 500% on all imports from Russia to the US. Separately, the law allows tariffs of up to 100% on goods from five countries — the largest buyers of Russian oil and gas — among which China and India are [named](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/7/31/how-us-senate-russia-sanctions-could-spell-100-tariffs-for-india-china). The exact size of the tariff will be determined by the US Trade Representative, [Interfax](https://www.interfax.ru/world/1108149) notes. Countries that get [less than 15%](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/russia-sanctions-bill-iran-senate-lindsey-graham/) of their consumed natural gas from Russia and take "meaningful" steps to cut purchases are exempt from the tariff.

The sanctions cover Russian President Vladimir Putin, [more than 20](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/7/31/how-us-senate-russia-sanctions-could-spell-100-tariffs-for-india-china) senior Russian officials and companies linked to the defense sector, as well as Russian oligarchs and financial institutions. A separate section of the law targets the "shadow fleet" of tankers [used](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/7/31/how-us-senate-russia-sanctions-could-spell-100-tariffs-for-india-china) to circumvent existing export restrictions on Russian oil.

## What's Next

The bill now moves to the House of Representatives, which has been on its summer recess since early August — [consideration](https://www.interfax.ru/world/1108149) will not begin before September. Kommersant [writes](https://www.kommersant.ru/doc/8875144) that the House will take up the bill in September. According to Interfax, some House Democrats are skeptical of the bill, viewing it as a tool that gives the White House the power to impose major tariffs on its own — this could delay consideration and lead to amendments. According to Democratic Senator Richard Blumenthal, the bill [was supported](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/russia-sanctions-bill-iran-senate-lindsey-graham/) by Trump; House Speaker Mike Johnson said he was pleased with the agreement reached with the White House.

## Named After Graham

The bill is named after Republican Senator Lindsey Graham of South Carolina, who, [according to](https://ru.euronews.com/2026/08/07/us-senate-approves-russia-sanction) Euronews's Russian-language service, had been pushing for sanctions against Russia for more than a year. Shortly before his death, he announced that he had agreed on a revised version of the bill with the White House, the outlet notes. The senator [died](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/russia-sanctions-bill-iran-senate-lindsey-graham/) in July 2026 shortly after returning from a trip to Kyiv. His Senate seat was taken by his sister, Darlene Graham.
