The Israeli military struck south Lebanon overnight into Saturday, August 15, killing at least 11 people. Lebanon's Ministry of Health and the National News Agency (NNA) reported the deaths. It is the deadliest strike since the ceasefire between Israel and Hezbollah took effect on June 20, [Al Jazeera notes](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/15/israeli-strike-kills-seven-in-south-lebanon-as-attacks-intensify).

Seven people, including three children and women, were killed in a strike on a house on the outskirts of the village of Ansar in Nabatieh district, with two more wounded there. Four more people were killed and at least 17 wounded in a strike on nearby Deir Zahrani, [PBS NewsHour reports, citing the Associated Press](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/israeli-airstrikes-on-southern-lebanon-kill-11-people-health-ministry-says). A total of 19 people were hurt in both strikes. According to Al Jazeera, one of the strikes hit a valley between Ansar and Zrariyeh. It is the deepest strike inside Lebanese territory in the two months since the ceasefire.

The Israeli military said the strikes targeted "Hezbollah infrastructure" near the Ali Taher ridge and in Ansar, in response to a militant attack on Israeli troops. The office of Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu said Hezbollah attacked Israeli soldiers inside Lebanon and seriously wounded three of them. The military then struck the Hezbollah headquarters that had ordered the attack. Israel later said the group had "deliberately placed civilians" in the building, [PBS NewsHour reported](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/israeli-airstrikes-on-southern-lebanon-kill-11-people-health-ministry-says).

## Lebanon and Hezbollah's response

Lebanese Prime Minister Nawaf Salam said the seven people killed in the strike on Ansar were not military infrastructure, and that the women and children killed were not military targets. He said control over weapons on Lebanese territory is solely the responsibility of the Lebanese state, [PBS NewsHour reports](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/israeli-airstrikes-on-southern-lebanon-kill-11-people-health-ministry-says).

Hezbollah called on Lebanon to halt "this aggression" instead of continuing US-mediated talks. The group said the American side is "a partner of the Israeli enemy in its crimes," [PBS NewsHour reports](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/israeli-airstrikes-on-southern-lebanon-kill-11-people-health-ministry-says).

The ceasefire agreement between Israel and Lebanon took effect on June 20, 2026. Since then, seven rounds of US-mediated talks have taken place, the latest in Rome in early August, [PBS NewsHour notes](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/israeli-airstrikes-on-southern-lebanon-kill-11-people-health-ministry-says).
