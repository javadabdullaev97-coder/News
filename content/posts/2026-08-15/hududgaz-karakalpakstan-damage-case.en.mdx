The Economic Crimes Department under the Prosecutor General's Office [said](https://t.me/Department_uz/12993) on August 15 that, according to the investigation, the Hududgaz Karakalpakstan branch concealed the causes of discrepancies in gas volumes. The damage to the state and society totaled 251.5 billion soums (about $21.1 million at the Central Bank's exchange rate on August 15).

The department's regional office in Karakalpakstan carried out the pre-investigation check. According to its findings, branch employees responsible for receiving and supplying gas did not investigate the reasons for the gap between the volume of gas received and the volume delivered to consumers. They wrote off the discrepancy as ordinary losses.

A criminal case has been opened under Article 167 of the Criminal Code (embezzlement or misappropriation of property) and Article 205 (abuse of power or official authority). The investigation is ongoing, and those responsible have not yet been named.

This is not the first criminal case in the gas supply sector in 2026. Earlier this year, the same department [reported](https://t.me/Department_uz/12928) the embezzlement of 19.9 billion soums during the servicing of electronic gas meters in Tashkent. Converted at the current Central Bank exchange rate, rather than the rate on the date of that case, that is about $1.7 million. According to the investigation, 273 meters understated gas consumption readings — that case was also opened under Article 167.
