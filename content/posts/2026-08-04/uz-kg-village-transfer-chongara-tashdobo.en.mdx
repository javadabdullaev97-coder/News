A joint Kyrgyz-Uzbek working group began the organizational and technical transfer of the villages of Chon-Gara and Tash-Dobo, formerly part of Uzbekistan's Fergana Region, on August 3 — the office of the Kyrgyz president's representative in Batken Region [reported](https://www.interfax.ru/world/1107299).

## What is happening in the villages

The joint working commission [held](https://www.interfax.ru/world/1107299) a session in Kadamjai district, Batken Region, on July 24. The sides discussed the civil status of residents in the territories being transferred, along with resettlement, property and business-activity issues.

Starting August 3, the joint working group [began](https://www.interfax.ru/world/1107299) work directly in the villages: specialists are registering residents and explaining the procedure for further paperwork. According to preliminary data as of August 3, about 2,500 residents are affected, most of them ethnic Kyrgyz. Once registration is complete, the process of granting them Kyrgyz Republic citizenship will begin.

## Terms of the exchange

Chon-Gara and Tash-Dobo previously belonged to Uzbekistan's Fergana Region. In exchange for these villages, Kyrgyzstan [is handing over](https://www.interfax.ru/world/1107299) to Uzbekistan a border plot of equal area.

Kommersant [reported](https://www.kommersant.ru/doc/8763096) on the transfer of these villages and the citizenship process for their residents as early as June 23, citing Askat Alagozov, press secretary to the Kyrgyz president. The events of August 3 continue this process rather than start it anew.

Separately, as part of the same package of border demarcation agreements, the two sides in late June exchanged plots totaling 236 hectares for the construction of a road between the villages of Say and Tayan — Askat Alagozov, press secretary to the Kyrgyz president, [announced](https://www.kommersant.ru/doc/8763096) this on Facebook, Kommersant reported. According to him, the new road will cut the route for Batken Region residents from the previous 255 km to about 55 km.

## A broader package of border agreements

On July 30 in Cholpon-Ata, Presidents Shavkat Mirziyoyev of Uzbekistan and Sadyr Japarov of Kyrgyzstan [signed](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9472) a package of bilateral documents, including a treaty on sections of the Uzbek-Kyrgyz state border — part of the ceremony marking the alliance treaty between the two countries, LEAP News [reported](https://leap.uz/ru/2026/08/01/uz-kg-alliance-treaty-cholpon-ata).

The presidents [ratified](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/5745) the basic treaty on delimitation of the state border between the two countries back in November 2022. At the time, the sides agreed on 35 border sections totaling 302.29 km and provided for the transfer of land plots in several border zones. Uzbekistan received about 4,977 hectares, while Kyrgyzstan received 1,019 hectares of pastureland plus another 12,849 hectares in the "Govasay" section as compensation. Issues around shared use of water resources at the Chashma spring in Sokh district were addressed in separate agreements.

## What's next

No exact date has been set for completing the citizenship procedure.
