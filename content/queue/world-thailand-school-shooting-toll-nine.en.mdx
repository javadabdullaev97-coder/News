The death toll from a school shooting near Bangkok rose to nine after a 12-year-old student died from her injuries on Saturday, August 8, Al Jazeera [reports](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/8/families-retrieve-bodies-after-deadly-shooting-rampage-outside-bangkok). The shooting took place on Friday in Nonthaburi province, northwest of Bangkok, the outlet reports, citing Thai authorities.

## What Happened

The shooting began on Friday, August 7, at around 10 a.m. local time at Debsirin Nonthaburi School, PBS NewsHour [reports](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/12-year-old-girl-dies-after-thailand-school-shooting-bringing-death-toll-to-at-least-8). According to the outlet, the attacker was a 14-year-old student at the school who lived with his grandparents. According to information cited by [PBS NewsHour](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/12-year-old-girl-dies-after-thailand-school-shooting-bringing-death-toll-to-at-least-8), earlier that same morning he allegedly killed both grandparents at the family's home, then opened fire at the school and, according to authorities, is believed to have taken his own life.

The weapon used by the attacker was described as small and legally registered to his grandfather, PBS NewsHour [reports](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/12-year-old-girl-dies-after-thailand-school-shooting-bringing-death-toll-to-at-least-8). Authorities have not yet named an official motive for the attack.

## Victims and Injured

By Saturday, August 8, the overall death toll reached nine: five school staff members, the attacker's grandparents, the attacker himself, and a 12-year-old student who died in the hospital from her injuries, Al Jazeera [reports](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/8/families-retrieve-bodies-after-deadly-shooting-rampage-outside-bangkok). Authorities have not officially released the names of those killed; information about the minor victim is published without her full name.

As of Saturday, 14 injured people remained hospitalized, seven of them in critical condition, PBS NewsHour [reports](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/12-year-old-girl-dies-after-thailand-school-shooting-bringing-death-toll-to-at-least-8), citing data from Thailand's Ministry of Public Health. The head of the country's forensic institute said most of those killed had a single fatal gunshot wound to a vital area — the chest or the head.

## Authorities' Response

Debsirin Nonthaburi School suspended in-person classes from August 10 to 14, and staff have been instructed to work remotely, according to [PBS NewsHour](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/12-year-old-girl-dies-after-thailand-school-shooting-bringing-death-toll-to-at-least-8).
