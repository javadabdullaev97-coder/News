The Central Bank of the Republic of Uzbekistan set the dollar rate at [11,857.35 soums](https://cbu.uz/ru/arkhiv-kursov-valyut/json/all/2026-08-18/) on August 18, 2026 — the lowest level since August 2023.

The dollar weakened by 33.83 soums over the day: on August 17, the official rate stood at [11,891.18 soums](https://cbu.uz/ru/arkhiv-kursov-valyut/json/all/2026-08-17/). The dollar last fell below today's level on August 10, 2023, when the rate stood at [11,667 soums](https://cbu.uz/ru/arkhiv-kursov-valyut/json/all/2023-08-10/). The very next day, [August 11, 2023](https://cbu.uz/ru/arkhiv-kursov-valyut/json/all/2023-08-11/), the rate exceeded 12,000 soums and has not dropped to the current level since.

The euro weakened by 7.11 soums on August 18, to 13,746.23 soums. The Russian ruble lost 2.51 soums and fell below 140 soums for the first time since February 2025, to 139.32 soums. In late January 2025, the ruble rate was also below 140 soums — [132.13 soums on January 31](https://cbu.uz/ru/arkhiv-kursov-valyut/json/all/2025-01-31/) — but then rose back above that level. It stayed there until August 18, 2026.
