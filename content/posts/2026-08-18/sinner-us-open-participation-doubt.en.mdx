World No. 1 Jannik Sinner will play at the 2026 US Open only if his right knee fully recovers — no decision had been made as of August 17, [Sports.ru reports, citing Italy's La Gazzetta dello Sport](https://www.sports.ru/tennis/1117337822-sinner-sygraet-na-us-open-tolko-v-sluchae-polnogo-vosstanovleniya-gazz.html).

Sinner withdrew from the Cincinnati Open on August 9 because of problems with his right knee. "My knee is bothering me, and despite working hard with the medical team, I have to admit: I'm not ready to compete yet," the tennis player said at the time. [Al Jazeera reports](https://www.aljazeera.com/sports/2026/8/9/jannik-sinner-withdraws-from-cincinnati-open-with-knee-injury). Sinner's last official match before that came in mid-July, when he won Wimbledon, [Tennis365 notes](https://www.tennis365.com/tennis-news/jannik-sinner-injury-out-us-open-rumours-swirling).

After withdrawing from the tournament, Sinner returned to Turin and began recovering under medical supervision. According to [Sports.ru](https://www.sports.ru/tennis/1117337822-sinner-sygraet-na-us-open-tolko-v-sluchae-polnogo-vosstanovleniya-gazz.html), the tennis player trained at the Circolo della Stampa Sporting club and then underwent an examination at the J Medical center. To get clearance for the tournament, he must pass a fitness test — playing several sets at high intensity, [Tennis365 reports](https://www.tennis365.com/tennis-news/jannik-sinner-injury-out-us-open-rumours-swirling).

## A Decision Within Days

A final decision on traveling to New York is expected within three to four days after August 17, [Tennis365 writes](https://www.tennis365.com/tennis-news/jannik-sinner-injury-out-us-open-rumours-swirling). Former tennis player Greg Rusedski said on a podcast: "If he's not in New York at the start of the week before the tournament, I don't see how he plays." [Tennis365 reports](https://www.tennis365.com/tennis-news/jannik-sinner-injury-out-us-open-rumours-swirling). As of August 17, neither Sinner's team nor the ATP had issued an official statement on the outcome of the fitness test.

## What's Next

According to Rusedski, if Sinner doesn't arrive in New York at the start of the week before the tournament begins, he won't have a chance to play. The matter will be decided by the outcome of the fitness test that Sinner's team plans to hold in the coming days.
