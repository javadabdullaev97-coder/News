MVP x PFL chief executive John Martin said the league is in a period of exclusive negotiations with lightweight champion Usman Nurmagomedov, [Sports.ru reported](https://www.sports.ru/boxing/1117340477-generalnyj-direktor-mvp-x-pfl-u-nas-est-period-eksklyuzivnyx-peregovor.html) on August 20.

MVP x PFL is a merger of the Professional Fighters League (PFL), a mixed martial arts promotion, and Most Valuable Promotions (MVP), a boxing company. MVP's co-owner is boxer Jake Paul. "We have a period of exclusive negotiations with Usman Nurmagomedov. We're preparing an offer and plan to sit down at the negotiating table with him," [Martin said](https://www.sports.ru/boxing/1117340477-generalnyj-direktor-mvp-x-pfl-u-nas-est-period-eksklyuzivnyx-peregovor.html) on The Ariel Helwani Show.

The Ultimate Fighting Championship (UFC), another mixed martial arts league, cannot currently offer Nurmagomedov a contract. "UFC hasn't made him any offer. They don't have the right to," the chief executive said. He did not specify the exact length of the window.

According to [Sherdog](https://www.sherdog.com/news/news/Daniel-Cormier-tells-Usman-Nurmagomedov-its-time-for-legacy-202396), former UFC champion and analyst Daniel Cormier called on Nurmagomedov on camera backstage at UFC 330 to sign with the UFC. The outlet quotes Cormier's phrase as "time for legacy."

Nurmagomedov remains undefeated and, according to Sports.ru, knocked out Archie Colgan on August 1 in his final fight under his PFL contract. The win closed out his contract with the promotion and opened a period of negotiations over a new deal.
