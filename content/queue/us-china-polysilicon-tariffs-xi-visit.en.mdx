The Trump administration is preparing a tariff of at least 15% and minimum import prices on polysilicon and products made from it, [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-05/trump-readies-tariffs-and-price-floors-to-bolster-us-polysilicon) reported on August 5 — the decision could come as early as August 6. The announcement is expected ahead of Chinese President Xi Jinping's visit to Washington.

## What Washington is proposing

According to Bloomberg, the plan involves a tariff of at least 15% on polysilicon and products made from it, as well as [minimum import prices](https://www.kommersant.ru/doc/8863559) on silicon wafers, solar modules and photovoltaic cells. These materials are used in the production of semiconductors and solar panels. In parallel, authorities are planning a temporary compensation program for American producers that will be hurt by a sharp spike in raw material prices.

The parameters of the order, according to [Vedomosti](https://www.vedomosti.ru/politics/news/2026/08/06/1219154-tramp-gotovitsya), are still being coordinated and could change before signing — a final decision has not yet been made. Analysts at Roth Capital Partners, cited by Vedomosti, suggest that with minimum prices factored in, the actual burden on importers could reach 25–35%.

## Why Washington wants this

The goal of the measure is to reduce American industry's dependence on foreign, primarily Chinese, supplies of polysilicon and to build up domestic production of raw materials for chips and solar panels. Experts at the Consumer Technology Association, cited by Bloomberg, point to the difficulty of tracking the origin of silicon in finished devices in practice — this could complicate the administration of the new tariffs.

## China has already responded with restrictions

A day earlier, on August 5, China's Ministry of Commerce [imposed sanctions on six American organizations and tightened export controls on drones](https://ru.euronews.com/business/2026/08/05/china-imposes-export-controls-on-us-companies-ahead-of-xis-visit-to-washington) — Beijing described the measures as a response to Washington's actions on export controls.

## Xi Jinping's visit

According to [Euronews](https://ru.euronews.com/business/2026/08/05/china-imposes-export-controls-on-us-companies-ahead-of-xis-visit-to-washington), Beijing introduced the new restrictions ahead of Chinese President Xi Jinping's expected visit to Washington in September — his first since meeting Trump in Beijing in May of this year. Observers described that meeting as a sign of warming in bilateral relations. The exact date of the visit has not yet been officially announced.
