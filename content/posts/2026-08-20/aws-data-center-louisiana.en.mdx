Amazon's cloud division AWS announced on August 18 a third data center campus in Shreveport, Louisiana, with a $6 billion investment, bringing the company's investment in the state's data centers to $18 billion.

This is AWS's third campus in the Shreveport–Bossier City metro area in northwest Louisiana, [the company said in a press release](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/company-news/amazon-data-center-louisiana-new-jobs). AWS announced the first two campuses in Caddo and Bossier parishes in February 2026, with $12 billion in investment. The new campus brought total investment in the state's data centers to $18 billion.

Together, the three campuses will create up to 750 permanent jobs at the data centers themselves and up to 2,500 jobs in related fields, AWS said. Construction will employ up to 2,250 people, according to estimates from STACK Infrastructure, which is building the campuses. Amazon also plans to direct up to $400 million toward upgrading water supply and wastewater systems for all three campuses.

Louisiana Governor Jeff Landry said investments of this scale put the state "at the center of operations that the whole country relies on." Shreveport Mayor Tom Arceneaux said the project would bring "more jobs and more opportunities for local businesses."

Susan Bourgeois, Secretary of Louisiana Economic Development, said the "true measure of a project like this is how widely its benefits are felt." AWS Director of Data Center Supply Keith Klein said "Amazon pays its own way, and the communities where we operate are better off because we're there."

DataCenterDynamics [previously reported](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/aws-pledges-6bn-investment-in-data-center-campus-in-shreveport-louisiana/) on AWS's plans to expand its Louisiana campuses earlier in 2026, as part of Amazon's push to scale up its cloud and AI infrastructure in the United States.
