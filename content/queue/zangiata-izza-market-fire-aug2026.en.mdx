A fire in shops in the Tokzor mahalla of Zangiata district, Tashkent region, was reported to the duty unit of the [Ministry of Emergency Situations of the Republic of Uzbekistan](https://t.me/MCHSUzbek/46356) on August 6 at 12:29, with the blaze covering about 400 square meters.

## How the Fire Was Extinguished

Rescue crews from the Ministry of Emergency Situations left for the scene immediately after the alert and began extinguishing the fire, [the ministry's channel reported](https://t.me/MCHSUzbek/46356). In its first message, the ministry stated that no data on casualties had been received by that hour and that the situation was fully under control.

The fire was fully extinguished at 14:09 — less than two hours after it was reported. According to preliminary data from the Ministry of Emergency Situations, about 400 square meters of shop space were damaged by the fire. In an updated message, the ministry confirmed the incident resulted in no injuries.

## What the Ministry Did Not Specify

The ministry did not name the cause of the fire — the message does not specify it. Nor does it give the exact number of shops damaged: the Ministry of Emergency Situations refers only to "shops" in the Tokzor mahalla, without naming a specific market or complex or specifying how many outlets were affected. A comment from the Tashkent region hokimiyat could not be found as of publication.

## What Comes Next

The Ministry of Emergency Situations did not say whether an investigation into the cause of the fire is planned, nor did it give a timeframe for one. The ministry also did not specify whether operations at the affected shops would be restricted.
