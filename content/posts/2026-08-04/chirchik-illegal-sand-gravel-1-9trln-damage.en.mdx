The Department for Combating Economic Crimes under the Prosecutor General's Office reported on August 4 illegal sand and gravel extraction from the bed of the Chirchik River in Tashkent and the region — the damage amounted to 1.9 trillion soums.

## What the Investigation Found

According to the [Department's statement](https://t.me/Department_uz/12947), 101,200 m³ of sand-and-gravel mixture were extracted from the riverbed without a permit. The raid was carried out jointly by the Department's Tashkent city office and the National Committee for Ecology and Climate Change. According to the Department, the extraction was carried out by the responsible officials of "B.I." LLC themselves. A criminal case has been opened under Article 169 of the Criminal Code (theft) and Article 192-11 (abuse of authority by officials of a non-state organization).

At the [CBU exchange rate](https://cbu.uz/ru/arkhiv-kursov-valyut/json/USD/2026-08-04/) for August 4 — 11,989.46 soums per dollar — 1.9 trillion soums amounts to about $158.5 million.

The Department did not disclose the names or positions of those involved, the exact period during which the extraction took place, or the specific stretch of riverbed in Tashkent or the region. The investigation continues.

## Moratorium on Riverbed Extraction

Since May 1, 2024, Uzbekistan has had an indefinite moratorium on extracting non-metallic materials — sand, gravel, sand-and-gravel mixture — from the beds, coastal strips and water-protection zones of seven rivers: the Chirchik, Syrdarya, Sangzor, Zarafshan, Naryn, Kashkadarya and Surkhandarya. The moratorium was introduced by [Presidential Decree No. PF-14 of January 17, 2024](https://lex.uz/docs/6764439). The same decree increased fines for illegal extraction during the moratorium tenfold, with the proceeds directed to the Ecology Fund for restoration work.

## What's Next

The case is at the preliminary investigation stage. Until a court verdict takes effect, the individuals mentioned in the Department's statement are presumed innocent. The agency did not specify a timeline for completing the investigation or referring the case to court.
