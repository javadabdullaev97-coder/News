The unified public services portal my.gov.uz announced on August 3 that registration for the Hajj queue can now be completed entirely online — without visiting a mahalla citizens' assembly, [the portal said in a post](https://t.me/MyGovUz/3965).

## How the electronic queue works

Previously, the first step for those wishing to perform the Hajj was to appear in person at the mahalla. Now the application is filled out electronically on my.gov.uz, and part of the data — including information about the mahram, the accompanying male relative required for a woman — is generated automatically by the system through integration with other government databases, [the portal's post said](https://t.me/MyGovUz/3965). The entire procedure can be completed from home, and the place in the queue is tracked electronically.

According to the [description of the "Hajj Queue Registration" service](https://my.gov.uz/ru/service/1272) on my.gov.uz, no additional documents are required to submit the application, and the request is processed automatically within three minutes. The service is provided by the Committee on Religious Affairs of the Republic of Uzbekistan.

## How much it costs

A fee equal to 25% of the base calculation value (BCV) is charged for each person added to the queue, [my.gov.uz said](https://t.me/MyGovUz/3965). The fee amounts to 103,000 soums (≈$8.6 at the Central Bank's exchange rate for August 3, 2026 — 11,960.18 soums per dollar) — the same figure is listed on [the service page on the portal](https://my.gov.uz/ru/service/1272), which corresponds to a BCV of 412,000 soums.

## Why registration became mandatory

Electronic registration in the queue for Hajj and Umrah appeared after the Cabinet of Ministers of the Republic of Uzbekistan changed the procedure for organizing these trips [by Resolution No. 27 of January 24, 2026](https://www.lex.uz/ru/docs/8015422). The document took effect on January 26, 2026.

As of February 1, 2026, registration on the Unified Hajj and Umrah Portal became mandatory for Hajj trips, and as of May 1, 2026 — for Umrah: a trip cannot be organized without entering the pilgrim's data into the portal, [the Fergana news agency reported](https://fergana.agency/news/145049/), citing the document that had taken effect.

The my.gov.uz service the portal announced is part of the same reform: it gives citizens another way to submit a Hajj application without going to either the mahalla or the specialized portal directly.
