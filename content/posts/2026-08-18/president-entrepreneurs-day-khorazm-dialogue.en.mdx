President Shavkat Mirziyoyev on August 18 held the sixth open dialogue with entrepreneurs in Khorezm, timed to Entrepreneurs' Day — a state holiday in Uzbekistan — and gathered about 500 businesspeople in person.

Another roughly 25,000 businesspeople joined by video link from district television studios for the first time, the Telegram channel of the National Agency for Perspective Projects [reported](https://t.me/napp_uz/2398).

Mirziyoyev congratulated the country's 1.2-million-strong business community, which contributes to strengthening the economy, on Entrepreneurs' Day, according to a [post](https://t.me/napp_uz/2398) by NAPP.

Over five years of open dialogues, Uzbekistan has adopted 84 laws and 861 decrees and resolutions that removed barriers for business, the [Ministry of Economy and Finance](https://t.me/minecofinuz/16300) said.

The credit portfolio for business grew almost threefold to 450 trillion soums, the ministry said. That is about $37.95 billion at the Central Bank of Uzbekistan's exchange rate on August 18. Entrepreneurs used tax and customs benefits worth 650 trillion soums (≈$54.82 billion), according to the same statement.

Some businesspeople independently raised $1 billion from foreign banks for their projects, according to a [statement](https://t.me/DAVAKTIVUZ/6207) by the State Assets Management Agency.

Mirziyoyev instructed officials to keep reducing administrative pressure on business and expand opportunities for entrepreneurs in the regions, the [NAPP Telegram channel](https://t.me/napp_uz/2398) reported.

## Fixed capital investment triples

Enterprises' investment in fixed capital tripled over five years to 363 trillion soums (≈$30.61 billion), the [State Assets Management Agency](https://t.me/DAVAKTIVUZ/6208) said. The president noted that business is shifting from launching new enterprises to expanding capital, according to the agency's statement.

The number of districts with industrial output exceeding $100 million grew from 66 to 116, the agency said. Districts with services turnover above 1 trillion soums rose to 152 from a previous 41. 3,500 enterprises now work with leading international marketplaces.

## Khorezm attracts $4 billion in investment

Khorezm region has attracted $4 billion in foreign investment in recent years and put 10,000 new production facilities into operation. The Telegram channel of the press service of National Electric Grids of Uzbekistan JSC, reposting a statement from the presidential press service, [reported](https://t.me/uzmetaxborotxizmati/59545) this.

The region created the Hazorasp free economic zone and 31 industrial zones, and built power plants with a combined capacity of 275 megawatts in Tuproqqal'a and Yangiariq.

Small business generates 72% of Khorezm region's economy and provides 81% of its exports — the region ranks first in the country by this indicator, according to the statement.
