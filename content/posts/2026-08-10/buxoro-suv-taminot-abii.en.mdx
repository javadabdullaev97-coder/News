Komilbek Yusupov, acting deputy chairman of the management board of O'zsuvta'minot JSC, inspected the modernization of water supply and sewerage systems financed by the Asian Infrastructure Investment Bank (AIIB) in Bukhara region on August 9, the company's [Telegram channel](https://t.me/uzsuv/45525) reported.

## What is being upgraded

The current phase of the project involves building and reconstructing two water treatment facilities and upgrading a water intake facility. It also envisages laying 47 kilometers of trunk water supply networks, according to a [post](https://t.me/uzsuv/45525) on O'zsuvta'minot's channel. During the visit, Yusupov instructed officials in charge to prepare quality project documentation and organize the work within the set deadlines.

## Who will gain access to water and sewerage

Once the work is completed, 1.788 million people in 195 mahallas of Bukhara region will be connected to centralized drinking water supply, and 507,000 people in 42 mahallas will get sewerage services, according to O'zsuvta'minot's statement. The channel did not give a specific completion date or cost for this phase.

## AIIB financing

The modernization of Bukhara region's water supply and sewerage systems is being carried out in two phases with the participation of the Asian Infrastructure Investment Bank. In April 2020, AIIB's board of directors [approved](https://www.aiib.org/en/projects/details/2020/approved/Uzbekistan-Bukhara-Region-Water-Supply-and-Sewerage-Project.html) the first loan of $385 million — at the time, it was [AIIB's first financing of the water sector in Uzbekistan and Central Asia as a whole](https://www.aiib.org/en/news-events/news/2020/AIIB-Commits-USD385M-for-Improving-Water-Access-in-Uzbekistan.html). The project was designed for 1.15 million residents of the region.

On April 13, 2022, the bank [approved](https://www.aiib.org/en/projects/details/2022/approved/Uzbekistan-Bukhara-Region-Water-Supply-and-Sewerage-Phase-II.html) a second loan — $248.4 million — to extend water supply and sewerage coverage to the region's remaining districts. Across both phases, AIIB has allocated about $633 million for the program. The program's goal is to raise the region's [water supply coverage from 54% and sewerage coverage from 34% to 100%](https://www.aiib.org/en/news-events/news/2020/AIIB-Commits-USD385M-for-Improving-Water-Access-in-Uzbekistan.html), the bank noted when approving the first loan. O'zsuvta'minot's statement on Yusupov's visit does not specify which of the two loans the current phase of work — two water treatment facilities and 47 kilometers of networks — falls under.

## What's next

O'zsuvta'minot did not name an exact completion date for the current phase of work — Yusupov instructed officials in charge to prepare project documentation and organize the work "within the set deadlines." The bank tracks the project's implementation through regular monitoring reports; AIIB representatives' last field visit to sites in Bukhara region took place in 2025–2026.
