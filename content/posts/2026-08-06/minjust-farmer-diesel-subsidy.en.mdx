The Ministry of Justice of the Republic of Uzbekistan on July 29 [registered](https://t.me/huquqiyaxborot/20202) a temporary regulation on subsidizing part of the cost of diesel fuel for cotton and wheat producers. The compensation will be up to 2 million soums (≈$168 at the Central Bank exchange rate as of August 6) per ton.

## Terms of the Subsidy

The document underwent state registration under number 3912, the Telegram channel "Huquqiy Axborot," run by the Ministry of Justice, [reported](https://t.me/huquqiyaxborot/20202). The subsidy applies to diesel fuel purchased by cotton and wheat producers through exchange trading between April 1 and July 1, 2026.

If the price per ton of fuel exceeds 13 million soums (≈$1,094 at the Central Bank exchange rate as of August 6), the state compensates the difference — but no more than 2 million soums per ton. The farmer must use the fuel for agrotechnical work provided for in the agrotechnological map between April and June, and must enter their fields and sown crops into the "Agroportal" system. No separate application is required: according to [the channel](https://t.me/huquqiyaxborot/20202), the subsidy is calculated automatically based on Agroportal data.

## Legal Basis

The diesel fuel subsidy procedure was adopted on the basis of [Presidential Decree No. PF-82](https://lex.uz/uz/docs/-8187068) dated May 8, 2026, "On Additional Measures for Financial Support of Cotton and Wheat Producers," which took effect on May 12. In addition to the diesel compensation, the decree provides for other support measures — in particular, compensation of part of the interest on preferential loans. For cotton producers, this is 2.5 percentage points for early repayment or 5 percentage points for repayment by the end of the year; for wheat producers, 4 percentage points for repayment by August 1.

The temporary regulation registered by the Ministry of Justice on July 29 details specifically the diesel subsidy mechanism — who receives the payment, how, and within what timeframe the purchasing season falls.
