Authorities in the Canadian province of British Columbia declared a state of emergency on Saturday, August 8, after fast-moving wildfires forced the evacuation of more than 20,000 people.

## Summerland and Peachland Evacuated Overnight

The Bald Range wildfire was discovered Friday evening and began rapidly approaching the community of Summerland, [PBS NewsHour reports](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/wildfires-force-20000-to-flee-as-british-columbia-declares-state-of-emergency). All roughly 12,000 residents of Summerland were ordered to leave their homes overnight. Approximately 8,000 people were then evacuated in neighboring Peachland and its surrounding area, [CBS News writes](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/bald-range-wildfire-canada-british-columbia/). The fire also affected the Faulder area and land near Okanagan Lake.

Some residents were cut off by the advancing fire; more than 50 people were evacuated by air using 18 aircraft, [CBS News reports](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/bald-range-wildfire-canada-british-columbia/).

## Bald Range Fire Growing Rapidly

Estimates of the Bald Range fire's size vary between sources: [PBS NewsHour reported](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/wildfires-force-20000-to-flee-as-british-columbia-declares-state-of-emergency) approximately 50 square kilometers, while CBS News put the figure at more than 100 square kilometers (over 39 square miles). Both reports describe growth over several hours after the fire was first detected Friday evening. [According to](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/8/british-columbia-issues-evacuation-orders-ahead-of-fast-moving-wildfires) Al Jazeera, the fire advanced roughly 15 kilometers toward Summerland at an unusually fast pace for a blaze of this kind.

The flames became so intense that they created their own weather system, [according to](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/bald-range-wildfire-canada-british-columbia/) a CBS News report citing provincial authorities. British Columbia Premier David Eby called the situation a "significant escalation of fire activity."

## Province Declares State of Emergency

Eby [declared](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/bald-range-wildfire-canada-british-columbia/) a provincial state of emergency Saturday morning. The status gives authorities additional powers — including the right to restrict movement in the area and provide temporary housing for evacuees, explained provincial Emergency Management Minister Kelly Greene, whose comments were cited by CBS News.

More than 100 wildfires are burning simultaneously across British Columbia, with about half of them uncontrolled, [Al Jazeera reports](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/8/british-columbia-issues-evacuation-orders-ahead-of-fast-moving-wildfires). The outlet cites hot weather, strong winds and prolonged drought as causes.

## No Deaths Confirmed Yet

The Royal Canadian Mounted Police is investigating a report of a possible death near Summerland but cannot confirm it yet due to hazardous conditions on the ground, [CBS News reports](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/bald-range-wildfire-canada-british-columbia/). No deaths had been officially confirmed as of publication. Provincial authorities have not yet released the number of homes destroyed.
