Large enterprises in Uzbekistan produced 10.1 million tons of cement in January-June 2026 — 0.2 million tons more than a year earlier, the National Statistics Committee of the Republic of Uzbekistan (National Statistics Committee) [said](https://stat.uz/ru/press-tsentr/novosti-goskomstata/69674-tsement-ishlab-chi-arish-azhmi-10-mln-tonnadan-oshdi-3) on August 6.

## What the report shows

The agency reported data only for large enterprises — excluding small businesses and the informal sector. The figure was published simultaneously in the [National Statistics Committee's Telegram channel](https://t.me/uzstataxborot/69184) and on the [stat.uz website](https://stat.uz/ru/press-tsentr/novosti-goskomstata/69674-tsement-ishlab-chi-arish-azhmi-10-mln-tonnadan-oshdi-3) — both publications cite the same figure, 10.1 million tons. The agency did not disclose a regional breakdown of production, the names of individual plants, or data on cement exports or imports for the reporting period.

## Growth slows

The National Statistics Committee provided cement production figures for January-June of the past three years:

- 2024 — 7.7 million tons;
- 2025 — 9.9 million tons;
- 2026 — 10.1 million tons.

From 2024 to 2025, output grew by almost 29% (2.2 million tons). Over the following year, growth slowed to about 2% — an increase of roughly 0.2 million tons. The agency did not give reasons for the slowdown.

## Why it matters for Uzbekistan

The construction sector remains one of the main consumers of cement. At a [meeting on February 3, 2026](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/8900), President Shavkat Mirziyoyev set a target to build housing for 140,000 apartments in 2026 and to raise the pace of housing construction to 280,000 apartments a year by 2040. The same meeting noted that the growing scale of construction had "given a powerful boost to the development of the building materials industry."

Cement also remains an export commodity. According to earlier National Statistics Committee data, Uzbekistan [increased cement exports](https://stat.uz/ru/press-tsentr/novosti-goskomstata/65102-tsement-eksporti-3-8-barobarga-oshgan-ir-iz-respublikasi-asosij-kharidor-3) 3.8-fold in January-September 2025 — to 1.1 million tons worth $44.4 million. Kyrgyzstan was the main buyer at 488,700 tons, followed by Kazakhstan (316,800 tons) and Afghanistan (272,700 tons). The National Statistics Committee has not yet published cement export data for the first half of 2026.
