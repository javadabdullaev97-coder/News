The Munich Higher Regional Court on August 6, 2026, sentenced 25-year-old Afghan national Farhad N. to life in prison for ramming a car into a trade union demonstration on February 13, 2025, killing two people.

## Sentence: life in prison without early release

The court found Farhad N. guilty of two counts of murder and 23 counts of attempted murder, [Kommersant](https://www.kommersant.ru/doc/8864061) and [Reuters](https://www.internazionale.it/ultime-notizie-reuters/2026/08/06/afghan-national-handed-life-sentence-for-deadly-car-attack-on-munich-union-rally) reported. The court established the "particular severity of guilt" of the defendant — a formulation under German law that effectively rules out parole after the standard 15 years for a life sentence, [AP](https://abcnews.com/International/wireStory/german-court-sentences-suspect-deadly-munich-car-ramming-135418059) reported.

According to the prosecution, Farhad N. acted for religious motives and intended to attack randomly chosen people in Germany "in response to the situation in Islamic countries" — that is how AP described his motive. At the same time, as [Kommersant](https://www.kommersant.ru/doc/8864061) notes, the defendant was not established to be a member of any known terrorist organization.

## How the attack unfolded

On February 13, 2025, Farhad N. drove a Mini Cooper into participants of a Ver.di trade union demonstration in central Munich. About 1,500 people took part in the rally, whose participants were demanding higher wages and longer vacations — kindergarten, hospital and public utility workers, [Kommersant](https://www.kommersant.ru/doc/8864061) reported.

A woman and her two-year-old daughter were killed. According to AP, 44 people were injured; Kommersant had reported more than 40 injured. When detained, the defendant shouted "Allah is great!", [Kommersant](https://www.kommersant.ru/doc/8864061) reported.

## Course of the proceedings

The case was heard by the Munich Higher Regional Court, the instance that in Germany handles trials for particularly serious crimes. The verdict was delivered about a year and a half after the attack; [Reuters](https://www.internazionale.it/ultime-notizie-reuters/2026/08/06/afghan-national-handed-life-sentence-for-deadly-car-attack-on-munich-union-rally) notes that the defendant admitted to the act of ramming the car itself.
