President Shavkat Mirziyoyev signed Resolution PQ-292 on August 4, introducing the 'oilabay' system from September 1, 2026 — a dedicated social worker assigned to every family of a child with special educational needs.

## What Changes From September 1

Under the new rules, comprehensive assessment groups at 'Inson' centers will determine special educational needs for children aged 3 to 18 and refer them to state educational institutions; their work will launch on a pilot basis from October 1, 2026, in the Navoiy and Khorezm regions, [the resolution says](https://t.me/huquqiyaxborot/20218).

The 'oilabay' ('family approach') system launches in parallel: a social worker is assigned to each family. The worker visits the family, helps it access educational and social services, and monitors whether the child participates in the programs it is entitled to, [the document's text shows](https://t.me/huquqiyaxborot/20218).

The resolution also introduces individual learning plans for schools starting with the 2026/2027 academic year and provides subsidies to private schools that open remedial classes, [the document shows](https://t.me/huquqiyaxborot/20218).

## University Admission Benefits and Equipment

The resolution also changes university admission terms for applicants with disabilities. Starting with the 2027/2028 academic year, they will receive an additional 50% of time to take entrance exams, and exam materials will be available in Braille, audio and electronic formats, [the resolution's second clause states](https://t.me/huquqiyaxborot/20219).

From May 1, 2027, children enrolled in the voucher-based special education system will begin receiving modern assistive equipment, [the Ministry of Justice said](https://t.me/huquqiyaxborot/20219).

## How Many Children Are Affected

A day before signing the resolution, on August 3, the president [reviewed proposals](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9483) to expand the coverage of children with special educational needs by education and social services. According to figures presented at the meeting, the country has more than 168,000 children with disabilities, of whom 133,800 are already covered by educational and social services, while another 34,600 children still need to be brought into the system.

The same pilot launch of comprehensive assessment groups in the Khorezm and Navoiy regions from October 1, 2026, is also enshrined in the resolution itself; the president was separately briefed on it at the August 3 meeting. Other measures discussed at the meeting included introducing personal assistants for visually impaired teachers, [according to a statement on the president's website](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9483). Whether this item will be included in the text of Resolution PQ-292 or subsequent acts does not follow from the published materials — the resolution itself addresses only assistive devices for students.

Education for children with special needs has been regulated in Uzbekistan since 2020, when [Resolution PP-4860](https://lex.uz/docs/5044745) was adopted, approving the concept and roadmap for developing inclusive education.

## What's Next

The 'oilabay' system launches nationwide on September 1, 2026. Comprehensive assessment groups at 'Inson' centers will begin working on a pilot basis from October 1, 2026, in the Khorezm and Navoiy regions. Admission benefits for university applicants take effect from the 2027/2028 academic year, and voucher-based assistive equipment provision starts May 1, 2027.
