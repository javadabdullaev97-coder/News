President Shavkat Mirziyoyev on August 5 signed Resolution No. PQ-293 on subsidies for pedigree farms for young cattle — payments will run from September 1, 2026, to January 1, 2030.

## How much a calf is worth

According to [the Ministry of Justice](https://t.me/huquqiyaxborot/20227), the Agrarian Sector Payments Agency will pay subsidies from the state budget to pedigree farms for offspring from identified pedigree cows:

- 1.5 million soums for the first calf;
- 2 million soums for the second calf;
- 1 million soums for keeping a calf alive to 12 months;
- another 1 million soums for keeping it alive from 12 to 18 months.

At the Central Bank's rate for August 5 (1 dollar = 11,942.21 soums), that is roughly $126, $167, $84 and $84 for each of the four payments. The last two amounts match because both payments equal 1 million soums.

## Livestock import and quarantine

The resolution also establishes a service enterprise under the Agency for the Development of Livestock and Pasture Farming. It will [handle](https://t.me/huquqiyaxborot/20227) the import of cattle and small ruminants from abroad, the organization of quarantine sites inside the country, and a 12-month package of services for farms — feeding, veterinary care, artificial insemination, breeding consultations and zootechnical services.

## Livestock breeders' association — still just a proposal

[LEAP News reported](https://leap.uz/posts/2026-08-05/uzbekistan-livestock-poultry-895m-2028) on August 5 that at a [presidential presentation](https://t.me/portal_gov_uz/42783) of the $895 million livestock and poultry development plan for 2026–2028, officials proposed creating a Livestock Breeders' Association — a body to represent the sector's interests and develop cooperation and breeding work. The text of Resolution No. PQ-293 does not mention creating an association: the document regulates only the subsidies for young cattle and the work of the new livestock import and quarantine service.

Of that same plan, $463 million is earmarked for livestock development — funds meant to deliver an additional 190,000 tonnes of meat by 2028. Resolution No. PQ-293 is part of the mechanism meant to secure that growth: the subsidy is tied to keeping offspring from pedigree animals alive, not just to purchasing them.
