Uzbekistan's Ministry of Economy and Finance reported on August 4 on the results of its infrastructure development program for the first half of 2026 — schools, kindergartens, hospitals and utility networks were built.

## Education and healthcare

In January-June 2026, the program [created 6,722 new school places and 1,940 kindergarten places](https://t.me/minecofinuz/16185). This was announced at an August 4 briefing by Ravshana Umarova, press secretary and adviser to the minister of economy and finance on information policy.

In healthcare, [outpatient clinics for 465 visits and hospitals with 696 beds were opened](https://t.me/minecofinuz/16185), according to a post on the ministry's Telegram channel.

## Utility networks and irrigation

In parallel, [61.5 km of water supply and sewerage networks and 65.9 km of heating networks were laid](https://t.me/minecofinuz/16185). The irrigation system gained [435.8 km of canals and 80.1 km of collectors](https://t.me/minecofinuz/16185) — all the work was carried out under the same program.

## About the program

Uzbekistan's Social and Production Infrastructure Development Program for 2026-2028 was approved by [Presidential Resolution No. PP-393 of December 27, 2025](https://www.lex.uz/ru/docs/7955944). The document requires at least [40% of the annual capital investment volume](https://www.lex.uz/ru/docs/7955944) to be disbursed for each project in the first half of the year — the threshold is higher, 50%, for carryover projects.

## What's next

Under the terms of the resolution, [educational facilities must be commissioned by September 1, and heating facilities by October 1](https://www.lex.uz/ru/docs/7955944). [Healthcare, water supply and road facilities must be put into operation by December 1, 2026](https://www.lex.uz/ru/docs/7955944).
