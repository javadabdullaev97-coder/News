Toshkent shahar suv ta'minoti JSC will halt drinking water supply in 22 Tashkent mahallas from 10:00 on August 3 to 10:00 on August 4 due to work at a water distribution facility in Mirzo Ulugbek district.

## Who will be affected by the shutoff

The company [said](https://t.me/uzsuvtoshshahar/5396) on August 2 that the work would take place in certain districts of the capital. The water distribution facility itself is located in Mirzo Ulugbek district, but the operator did not specify which districts would be left without water.

The following [will be left](https://t.me/uzsuvtoshshahar/5397) without centralized water supply:

- 22 mahallas;
- 884 apartment buildings;
- 7,006 private houses;
- 242 government and social facilities;
- 33,017 residents.

The operator's messages only mention the number of facilities — the company did not publish the names of the mahallas or streets.

## Operator asks residents to stock up on water

The company [is asking residents](https://t.me/uzsuvtoshshahar/5397) to stock up on drinking water in advance. Water [will be delivered by special transport](https://t.me/uzsuvtoshshahar/5397) to all affected mahallas as well as to government institutions and social facilities. The company did not publish a delivery schedule or distribution points.

The operator is asking residents to report delivery disruptions through the Telegram bot [@poytaxtuz_bot](https://t.me/poytaxtuz_bot), listed in the signature of its messages. No phone numbers are given in these messages.

## Why the supply will be halted for 24 hours

Within 24 hours, specialists [will replace five main valves](https://t.me/uzsuvtoshshahar/5396): two with a diameter of 1,200 mm and three with a diameter of 800 mm. The work is split into three eight-hour stages — draining water from the network, replacing the valves, and refilling the pipes. The 10:00 start time was chosen so residents could use the morning peak consumption period first.

The wording in the operator's messages differs. In the Russian-language announcement, the work [is described as scheduled preventive maintenance](https://t.me/uzsuvtoshshahar/5396). In the Uzbek-language message about the Mirzo Ulugbek district facility, it says [urgent preventive work has become necessary](https://t.me/uzsuvtoshshahar/5397).

Water supply is expected to resume [by 10:00 on August 4](https://t.me/uzsuvtoshshahar/5397). The company has not announced any extension of the work or change to the timeline.
