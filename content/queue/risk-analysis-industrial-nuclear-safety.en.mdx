The Ministry of Justice on August 1 [registered](https://t.me/huquqiyaxborot/20225) regulation No. 3915 introducing the electronic "Risk Analysis" system for oversight in the fields of industrial, radiation and nuclear safety, as well as amusement ride safety.

## What changes for business

The document introduces automated risk assessment of violations for business entities operating at industrial, radiation and nuclear safety facilities, as well as those operating amusement rides. The system assigns each company one of three risk levels — high, medium or low, [according to the publication](https://t.me/huquqiyaxborot/20225) by the Ministry of Justice.

Only facilities with high and medium risk levels will be subject to inspections. Low-risk enterprises are exempt from inspections. The system generates its assessment based on data from state information systems, statistics, complaints and media publications — without involvement of the enterprise itself. At the same time, requesting additional documents from entrepreneurs as part of the risk analysis is prohibited, and the assigned risk level alone cannot serve as grounds for sanctions.

The exact frequency of inspections for high- and medium-risk facilities, and the effective date of the act, are not specified in the Ministry of Justice's publication.

## Who falls under oversight

Oversight in this area has been carried out since 2024 by the Committee for Industrial, Radiation and Nuclear Safety under the Cabinet of Ministers. The agency was created to implement unified state policy toward hazardous production facilities, sources of ionizing radiation and the peaceful use of atomic energy.

The expansion of its powers and the transition to remote oversight mechanisms are provided for by [presidential decree No. UP-224](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/7281206) of December 24, 2024. The document's full title is "On Additional Measures to Improve State Oversight in the Field of Industrial, Radiation and Nuclear Safety."

The same decree set tasks for 2025 to introduce remote oversight mechanisms and to launch the "Unified Integrated Ecosystem" information system, integrating it with the information systems of other agencies.

The risk-analysis regulation registered on August 1 operates in the same area of oversight. Instead of scheduled inspections of all enterprises in sequence, the oversight body focuses on facilities that the system identifies as higher risk.
