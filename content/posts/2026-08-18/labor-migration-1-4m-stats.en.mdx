Around 1.4 million Uzbek citizens worked abroad in nearly 40 countries in the first half of 2026, according to Migration Agency data cited by the Central Bank in a [review](https://cbu.uz/uz/press_center/reviews/4342919/) published August 12.

The Migration Agency under the Cabinet of Ministers was established on the basis of the former Agency for External Labor Migration.

Russia hosts the most migrants — 834,200 people, or about 60% of the total. Kazakhstan employs 84,400 Uzbek citizens, Turkey 72,100, and South Korea 46,400. European countries have 258,000 labor migrants, and another 97,700 are in other countries, according to [data from the review](https://cbu.uz/upload/iblock/02b/n4p9nyn7idoap0d08g8rre91i096qec2/sharh.pdf).

Among labor migrants, 398,900 people, or 28.6%, are women. Young people make up 28.9%, or 402,100 people. Since the start of 2026, the agency has organized employment abroad in high-paying positions for 124,500 citizens.

## CIS Share Declines

The geography of labor migration is changing, the review notes. According to the review, the share of CIS countries in its structure fell 29%, the share of the European region rose 42%, and East Asia's share rose 26%.

The review notes that one of the main economic reasons behind choosing a destination is the gap in wage levels between countries. By 2030, the labor shortage in Europe could reach 7.5–10.5 million people. The review attributes this estimate to the Institute for Employment Research at Germany's Federal Employment Agency and the European Commission.

## New Destinations for Migrants

The agency has signed 48 agreements with 23 countries on organized labor recruitment. Of these, 18 agreements with 14 states were signed in 2025–2026. The agency cooperates with more than 325 major employers and recruitment companies from 36 countries.

The agency's prospective partners include Germany, the United Kingdom, Italy, Spain, Sweden, France, Bulgaria, Romania, Slovakia, Slovenia, Serbia, the United States and Japan. The review lists the United States, Italy, Sweden, France and Slovenia as new destinations. Employers in these countries are particularly interested in workers in services, healthcare, construction and logistics. The review also mentions agriculture, industry, welding and metalworking, woodworking, the oil and gas sector, and mining.

The Central Bank [announced the publication of the review](https://t.me/centralbankuzbekistan/13421) on its Telegram channel. At the same time, remittances to Uzbekistan rose 13% in January–June to reach $9.3 billion. The volume of transfers via mobile P2P services grew 32%, with their share of total transfers reaching 51.7%, according to the same document.
