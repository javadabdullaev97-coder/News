Indoneziyaning Sharqiy Nusa-Tenggara viloyatida, Flores orolining shimoliy qirg'og'ida yuz bergan 7,7 magnitudali zilzilada halok bo'lganlar soni dushanba, 17-avgust kuni kechqurungi holatga ko'ra kamida 68 nafarga yetdi. Bu haqda Al Jazeera BNPB — Indoneziyaning tabiiy ofatlar oqibatlarini bartaraf etish milliy agentligiga ishora qilib [xabar beradi](https://www.aljazeera.com/gallery/2026/8/17/photos-search-for-indonesia-earthquake-victims-continues?traffic_source=rss).

200 dan ortiq kishi jarohat oldi, [xabar beradi](https://www.kommersant.ru/doc/8891437) Kommersant Reutersga ishora qilib. Zilzila shanba, 15-avgust kuni ertalab sodir bo'lgan edi. Dushanba, 17-avgust kuni ertalab [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/crl7686r71yo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss) 53 kishi halok bo'lgani haqida xabar bergan edi — o'sha kunning kechqurungiga kelib raqam 68 taga yetdi.

Ortidan kelayotgan zilzilalar va ko'chkilar qutqaruvchilarning kesilib qolgan hududlarga yetib borishiga hamon xalaqit bermoqda, [xabar beradi](https://www.aljazeera.com/gallery/2026/8/17/photos-search-for-indonesia-earthquake-victims-continues?traffic_source=rss) Al Jazeera.

## Minglab odam yordam kutmoqda

Uyiga qaytolmayotganlar soni dushanba kuni tongga kelib deyarli 12 800 nafarga yetdi, [xabar beradi](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/crl7686r71yo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss) BBC. Jabrlanganlar oziq-ovqat, suv va dori-darmon yetishmasligidan shikoyat qilmoqda. «Uyim endi faqat vayronalar uyumi. Bizga tezda oziq-ovqat, dori va toza suv kerak», — [keltiradi](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/thousands-await-aid-in-indonesia-after-powerful-earthquake-that-killed-at-least-68) PBS NewsHour Reo qishlog'idan bo'lgan kiyim-kechak sotuvchisi Rashid Jafarning so'zlarini.

Hokimiyat jabrlangan hududlarga 1 800 dan ortiq harbiy va politsiya xodimini, o'nta samolyot va bir necha kemani yubordi, [xabar beradi](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/thousands-await-aid-in-indonesia-after-powerful-earthquake-that-killed-at-least-68) PBS NewsHour. Insonparvarlik yordami 100 tonnadan oshdi, jumladan deyarli 5 400 ta oziq-ovqat toʻplami, deb xabar beradi PBS. Tog'li hududlardagi yo'llarning bir qismi ko'chkilar bilan berkilib qolgan. «Yo'llar tik, ko'plari ko'chki bilan ko'milgan. Bu o'lim bilan chegaradosh sayohat edi», — [dedi](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/thousands-await-aid-in-indonesia-after-powerful-earthquake-that-killed-at-least-68) PBS NewsHourga politsiya boshlig'i o'rinbosari Marselus Yugo Amboro.

## 14 kunlik favqulodda holat

Sharqiy Nusa-Tenggara viloyati gubernatori yakshanba, 16-avgust kuni 14 kunlik favqulodda holat rejimini e'lon qildi, [xabar beradi](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/thousands-await-aid-in-indonesia-after-powerful-earthquake-that-killed-at-least-68) PBS NewsHour. Ilgari LEAP News [xabar bergan edi](https://leap.uz/ru/2026/08/17/world-indonesia-earthquake-toll-53) — 17-avgust holatiga ko'ra halok bo'lganlar soni 53 kishi deb baholangan edi, o'shandan beri raqam yana 15 taga oshdi.

16-avgustda joriy etilgan 14 kunlik favqulodda holat rejimi 30-avgustda tugaydi — shu sanagacha hokimiyat jabrlangan okruglarda qutqaruv va tiklash ishlarini davom ettiradi.
