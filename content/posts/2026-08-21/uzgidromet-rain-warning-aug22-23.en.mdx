Uzhydromet issued an urgent hydrometeorological warning on August 21: heavy rain is expected on August 22-23 in the foothill and mountain areas of seven regions of Uzbekistan, [the agency said in a post on its Telegram channel](https://t.me/uzgydromet/22049).

The rain could trigger mudslides and floods, the agency warned. The regions at risk are Kashkadarya, Surkhandarya, Jizzakh, Tashkent, Namangan, Fergana and Andijan.

In Kashkadarya region, the warning covers the districts of Yakkabog', Dehqonobod, Chiroqchi, Kitob, Shahrisabz, Qamashi and G'uzor. In Surkhandarya region — the districts of Sariosiyo, Uzun, Oltinsoy, Denov, Boysun, Sherobod, Sho'rchi, Qumqo'rg'on and Muzrabot. In Jizzakh region — the districts of Zomin, Baxmal, G'allaorol, Sharof Rashidov, Forish and Yangiobod.

In Tashkent region, the areas at risk are the districts of Ohangaron, Bo'stonliq, Parkent, Piskent, O'rtachirchiq and Yuqorichirchiq, as well as the cities of Angren and Olmaliq. In Namangan region — the districts of Pop, Kosonsoy, Chortoq, Chust, Namangan and Yangiqo'rg'on. In Fergana region — the districts of So'x and Fergana, as well as the village of Shohimardon and the city of Beshariq. In Andijan region — the districts of Andijon, Asaka, Jalaquduq, Qo'rg'ontepa, Paxtaobod, Izboskan, Xo'jaobod and Marhamat, as well as the city of Xonobod.

Uzhydromet is urging residents of foothill and mountain areas, visitors staying there, and drivers traveling on mountain roads to take precautions on August 22 and 23.
