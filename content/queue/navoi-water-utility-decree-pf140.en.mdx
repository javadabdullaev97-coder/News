Navoiy suv ta'minoti JSC held a meeting in Navoiy region on August 4, 2026, on the implementation of Presidential Decree No. PF-140 of July 24, 2026, the official O'zsuvta'minot Telegram channel [reported](https://t.me/uzsuv/45339).

The document's full title is "On additional measures to improve drinking water supply and sewerage systems."

## What was discussed at the meeting

At the meeting, Navoiy suv ta'minoti reviewed [industry development, service quality, process digitalization and executive discipline](https://t.me/uzsuv/45339). Staff members were assigned to carry out the tasks set by the decree. The publication does not name those responsible or give specific figures for Navoiy region — deadlines, volumes of work, or funding amounts.

## What the decree provides

Decree No. PF-140 was [signed](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/8358785) on July 24, 2026, and took effect on July 28. The document sets target indicators for the development of drinking water supply and sewerage by region for 2026-2027: coverage of the population with centralized water supply must rise to 84.8%, and sewerage service coverage to 23% nationwide and 76% in cities.

Starting September 1, 2026, a unified information system for monitoring investment projects at Uzsuvta'minot JSC will launch. It will record data on all water supply and sewerage facilities transferred to the company. Starting January 1, 2027, authority to approve water supply and sewerage tariffs will shift to the Interagency Tariff Commission under the Cabinet of Ministers.

## No regional figures yet

The decree contains 12 annexes with planned indicators for water supply and sewerage network facilities. A breakdown for Navoiy region — specific amounts, deadlines and coverage of the region's residents — has not yet appeared in materials published by Navoiy suv ta'minoti JSC or the Navoiy region hokimiyat.
