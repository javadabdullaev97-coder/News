The Central Bank of Uzbekistan and the Department for Combating Economic Crimes under the Prosecutor General's Office have raised the threshold in banks' internal control rules from $100 to $500; the amendment took effect August 9.

The Ministry of Justice registered the resolution on May 8, 2026, under number 2886-12. The document [was published on lex.uz](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/8184199): it amends commercial banks' internal control rules on combating money laundering, terrorism financing, and financing the proliferation of weapons of mass destruction.

## What Changes

In paragraph 22 of these rules, the resolution replaces the figure "100" with "500" — a fivefold increase in the threshold. The amendment is being read as raising the amount up to which a bank does not require a client's passport for a cash foreign currency transaction. That does not literally follow from the published text: the previous wording of paragraph 22 is not given in the registry, and the document itself does not specify the type of transaction.

The same amendment also changes a second threshold in paragraph 22: "500 times the base calculation value (BRV)" has been replaced with 175 million soums. Which type of transaction this change applies to also does not follow from the text available on lex.uz.

## Who It Concerns and From When

The internal control rules are mandatory for commercial banks. The resolution takes effect "three months after the date of official publication," according to the text on lex.uz. Counting from the registration date gives August 9, 2026; the Tashkent region hokimiyat cited the same date.

The Central Bank has not published an official statement about the amendments: no such material appears on cbu.uz or on its Telegram channel. The only available text of the document is the act's publication in the lex.uz registry.
