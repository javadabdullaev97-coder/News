A working group from the Ministry of Employment and Poverty Reduction [inspected](https://t.me/mehnatvazirligi/20189) construction of a leather-and-shoe center worth 350 billion soums (≈$29.2 million at the Central Bank's July 31 exchange rate) in Kushtepa district, Fergana region, on July 31.

## What is being built in Kushtepa district

The group was led by Minister of Employment and Poverty Reduction Botir Zakhidov. According to [the ministry](https://t.me/mehnatvazirligi/20189), a modern leather-and-shoe microproduction center is being built in Kushtepa district. The project is meant to create new jobs, develop entrepreneurship and boost the district's export potential. The ministry did not disclose the exact number of jobs to be created or the center's launch date.

The trip was part of the "Dolzarb qirq kunlik" program — an on-site review of project implementation in Fergana region.

## Export raspberry plantations

The working group also [inspected](https://t.me/mehnatvazirligi/20189) the raspberry plantations set up in the Obod and Sanoat mahallas of Fergana district. According to the ministry, 20 mahallas in the district specialize in growing raspberries for export. Following the inspection, the ministry instructed that work on the projects continue — the publication did not disclose specific instructions.

The ministry named both projects — the shoe cluster and the raspberry plantations — as factors meant to ensure employment and boost incomes for the region's population.
