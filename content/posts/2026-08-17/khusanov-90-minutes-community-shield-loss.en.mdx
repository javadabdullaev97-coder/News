Manchester City's Uzbekistani defender Abdukodir Khusanov played all 90 minutes of the Community Shield final against Arsenal on August 16, 2026 — his team lost 0-3.

The match at Principality Stadium in Cardiff drew 58,056 spectators, according to [Sky Sports](https://www.skysports.com/football/arsenal-vs-manchester-city/report/556659).

The hosts took the lead just 24 seconds into the match. Riccardo Calafiori scored the fastest goal in Community Shield history — the previous record had stood since 1968, [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/sports/2026/8/16/arsenal-cruise-to-3-0-win-over-manchester-city-in-community-shield) writes.

Kai Havertz doubled the lead in the 28th minute. In that episode, Khusanov failed to track Christos Dzolis, who set up Havertz for a header, [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/sports/2026/8/16/arsenal-cruise-to-3-0-win-over-manchester-city-in-community-shield) writes. Martin Ødegaard set the final score in the 48th minute.

Arsenal's attacking standout was club newcomer Christos Dzolis, who provided both assists in his debut match. According to [Sky Sports](https://www.skysports.com/football/arsenal-vs-manchester-city/report/556659), the only other player to achieve that in a club debut was Willian, in September 2020.

The match was the first official game for Manchester City's new manager, Enzo Maresca. He replaced Pep Guardiola in the role during the off-season, [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/sports/2026/8/16/arsenal-cruise-to-3-0-win-over-manchester-city-in-community-shield) reports.

## How Khusanov Played

Khusanov started the match at right back, [Sports Illustrated](https://www.si.com/soccer/arsenal-vs-man-city-player-ratings-champions-lay-down-ominous-marker-win-community-shield) notes. The outlet rated his performance 6.9 out of 10. The defender lost his position in both first-half goal-scoring episodes — on the goals by Calafiori and Havertz. Khusanov was not among City's substitutes, according to [Sky Sports](https://www.skysports.com/football/arsenal-vs-manchester-city/report/556659) — he played the entire match.

According to SI, manager Maresca will need to decide whether the right-back role suits the pacy Uzbekistani over the long term.

Khusanov joined Manchester City from France's Lens in January 2025 for €40 million (≈$41.5 million), [Fox Sports](https://www.foxsports.com/articles/soccer/man-city-signs-20yearold-khusanov-for-415-million-to-kick-off-expected-january-spending-spree) reported.

## Manager's Reaction

After the match, Maresca linked the loss to the early goal conceded. "A lot in the game depends on the first goal you concede. After that, the reaction was good," the manager said, his words cited by [Sky Sports](https://www.skysports.com/football/arsenal-vs-manchester-city/report/556659).
