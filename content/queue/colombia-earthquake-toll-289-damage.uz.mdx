Kolumbiya prezidenti Abelardo de la Espriella 17 avgustda 10 avgustdagi zilzila natijasida yetkazilgan bevosita zararni 30 trln peso, taxminan $9,6 mlrd, deb baholadi — u bu haqda Bogotadagi matbuot anjumanida ma'lum qildi, [Reuters xabar beradi](https://www.freemalaysiatoday.com/category/world/2026/08/18/colombia-s-president-pegs-early-quake-losses-at-us-9-6bil).

7,4 magnitudali zilzila 10 avgustda mamlakat g'arbidagi Choko departamentida sodir bo'ldi. 17 avgust holatiga ko'ra halok bo'lganlar soni 289 nafarga yetdi, 4 187 kishi jabrlandi, [Al Jazeera xabar beradi](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/17/as-rubble-clears-colombia-begins-focusing-on-recovery-after-the-earthquake), rasmiy hisobotga tayangan holda. Bir kun oldin hokimiyat xuddi shu 289 halok bo'lgan va 143 darak-daraksiz yo'qolgan haqida ma'lumot bergan, yarador bo'lganlar soni esa 3 937 kishi deb baholangan, [PBS NewsHour qayd etgan](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/colombian-president-asks-trump-to-suspend-tariffs-to-help-earthquake-recovery). Ilgari LEAP News [15 avgust holatiga ko'ra halok bo'lganlar soni 280 nafardan oshgani haqida](https://leap.uz/uz/2026/08/15/colombia-earthquake-death-toll-280) xabar bergan edi.

Prezident e'lon qilgan 30 trln pesodan 24,5 trlni binolarga, 5,5 trlni esa infratuzilmaga to'g'ri keldi, [Reuters ma'lum qiladi](https://www.freemalaysiatoday.com/category/world/2026/08/18/colombia-s-president-pegs-early-quake-losses-at-us-9-6bil). «Bu dastlabki raqam bo'lib, yangi ma'lumotlar va har bir mintaqadagi zarar bahosi kelib tushishi bilan aniqlashtiriladi», — dedi de la Espriella. Bir hafta oldin zararni moliya vaziri kamida $6,4 mlrd deb baholagan edi, [Al Jazeera yozgan edi](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/17/as-rubble-clears-colombia-begins-focusing-on-recovery-after-the-earthquake); o'shandan beri baho deyarli bir yarim baravar oshdi. 14 700 dan ortiq uy vayron bo'ldi, yana 81 500 dan ortig'i shikastlandi, [16 avgustda PBS NewsHour xabar bergan edi](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/colombian-president-asks-trump-to-suspend-tariffs-to-help-earthquake-recovery), Kolumbiya hokimiyatiga tayangan holda.

## Prezident Trampdan tariflarni to'xtatishni so'radi

De la Espriella shanba kuni, 15 avgustda, AQSh prezidenti Donald Trampga kolumbiyalik tovarlarga qo'yilgan yuqori tariflarni vaqtincha to'xtatish so'rovi bilan murojaat qildi. Maqsad — zilziladan zarar ko'rgan bizneslarga yordam berish, [PBS NewsHour xabar beradi](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/colombian-president-asks-trump-to-suspend-tariffs-to-help-earthquake-recovery). Tramp ma'muriyatidan darhol javob kelmadi. AQSh Kolumbiyaga oziq-ovqat, vaqtinchalik turar-joy va dori-darmon uchun allaqachon $26,5 mln ajratgan, deb qayd etadi nashr.

## Uy-joy vaziri ta'tili janjalga sabab bo'ldi

Uy-joy vaziri Xayme Andres Beltran dam olish kunlarini oilasi bilan Karib dengizidagi Santa-Marta kurortida o'tkazdi — plyajdagi suratlar ijtimoiy tarmoqlarga tarqaldi, [South China Morning Post yozadi](https://www.scmp.com/news/world/americas/article/3364351/colombian-ministers-caribbean-holiday-amid-earthquake-crisis-stokes-fury). Chap oppozitsiya davom etayotgan inqiroz fonida sayohatni noo'rin deb atab, vazirga nisbatan ishonchsizlik votumi boshlash niyatini bildirdi.
