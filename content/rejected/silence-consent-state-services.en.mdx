President Shavkat Mirziyoyev announced at the sixth open dialogue with entrepreneurs in the Khorezm region on August 18 that half of state services for businesses will shift to the "silence means consent" principle.

If an entrepreneur's application is not reviewed within the set deadline, it is automatically considered approved in their favor. Several official state Telegram channels [circulated](https://t.me/eduuz/50612) this wording, citing the president's remarks. They included the channels of the Ministry of Higher Education, Science and Innovation and the Supreme Court.

The business ombudsman will separately review every denial of a business license, [according to a statement](https://t.me/oliysuduz/51533). Liability for unjustified denials will be strengthened — the president did not specify sanctions or how they would be applied during the dialogue.

The president also announced a relaxation of licensing rules. More than 30 types of activity will be removed from the list of licensed activities, and permit-issuance deadlines for 35 types will be cut by a factor of 2–3. Another 10 types of permits will shift to a notification-based procedure: entrepreneurs will not need to wait for approval and will only need to [notify](https://t.me/eduuz/50612) the relevant agency before starting operations.

The Khorezm dialogue is the sixth held since 2021. A republican headquarters made up of representatives from more than 50 agencies worked on preparations for the dialogue starting May 15. It [received](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9493) about 6,000 requests from entrepreneurs and had resolved more than 4,200 of them favorably by mid-August.
