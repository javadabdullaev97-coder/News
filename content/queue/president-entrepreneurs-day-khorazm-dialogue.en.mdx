President Shavkat Mirziyoyev held the sixth open dialogue with entrepreneurs in Khorazm on August 18, timed to Entrepreneurs' Day — a state holiday in Uzbekistan — and gathered about 500 businesspeople in person.

Another roughly 25,000 businesspeople connected via video link from district TV studios for the first time, the National Agency of Perspective Projects (NAPP) Telegram channel [reported](https://t.me/napp_uz/2398).

Mirziyoyev congratulated the country's 1.2-million-strong business community, which contributes to strengthening the economy, on Entrepreneurs' Day, according to a NAPP [post](https://t.me/napp_uz/2398).

Over five years of open dialogues, Uzbekistan adopted 84 laws and 861 decrees and resolutions that removed barriers for business, the [Ministry of Economy and Finance](https://t.me/minecofinuz/16300) said.

The credit portfolio for businesses grew almost threefold to 450 trillion soums, the ministry said. That is about $37.95 billion at the Central Bank of Uzbekistan's exchange rate for August 18. Entrepreneurs used tax and customs benefits worth 650 trillion soums (≈$54.82 billion), according to the same statement.

Some entrepreneurs independently raised $1 billion from foreign banks for their projects, according to a State Assets Management Agency [statement](https://t.me/DAVAKTIVUZ/6207).

Mirziyoyev instructed officials to keep reducing administrative pressure on business and expand opportunities for entrepreneurs in the regions, the [NAPP Telegram channel](https://t.me/napp_uz/2398) reported.

## Fixed capital investment triples

Enterprises' investment in fixed capital tripled over five years to 363 trillion soums (≈$30.61 billion), the [State Assets Management Agency](https://t.me/DAVAKTIVUZ/6208) said. The president noted business's shift from launching new enterprises to expanding capital, according to the agency's statement.

The number of districts (tumans) with industrial output exceeding $100 million grew from 66 to 116, the agency said. Districts with services turnover above 1 trillion soums rose to 152 from 41 previously. Some 3,500 enterprises work with leading international marketplaces.

## Khorazm attracts $4 billion in investment

Khorazm region has attracted $4 billion in foreign investment and commissioned 10,000 new production facilities in recent years, the Telegram channel of the press service of JSC Oʻzbekiston Milliy Elektr Tarmoqlari [reported](https://t.me/uzmetaxborotxizmati/59545), reposting a statement from the president's press service.

The region set up the Khazorasp free economic zone and 31 industrial zones, while power plants with a combined capacity of 275 megawatts were built in Tuprakkala and Yangiaryk.

Small business accounts for 72% of Khorazm region's economy and provides 81% of its exports — the highest share in the country by this metric, according to the statement.
