American AI startup Groq closed a $350 million Series A round at a $3.5 billion valuation on August 17, 2026, [the company said in a statement](https://groq.com/newsroom/groq-closes-usd350-million-series-a-building-the-world-s-leading-ai-inference-cloud).

Investment firm Disruptive led the round; Nvidia's participation was announced as planned — the deal itself had not closed at the time of the announcement. The funds will go toward expanding Groq's data center network: the company aims to grow capacity from 54 to more than 200 megawatts in 2027.

The new valuation is nearly half what Groq received in September 2025. At the time, the company raised [$750 million at a $6.9 billion valuation](https://techcrunch.com/2025/12/24/nvidia-acquires-ai-chip-challenger-groq-for-20b-report-says/), TechCrunch reported.

## Company Disputes the "Down Round" Label

[A Groq representative told TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/17/groq-raises-350m-to-fuel-its-pivot-from-ai-chips-to-neocloud/) that the company does not consider the new round a down round. According to the representative, the $3.5 billion figure values a different, "post-licensing" version of Groq that emerged after the December deal with Nvidia.

## The December Deal With Nvidia

On December 24, 2025, Groq [announced](https://groq.com/newsroom/groq-and-nvidia-enter-non-exclusive-inference-technology-licensing-agreement-to-accelerate-ai-inference-at-global-scale) a non-exclusive licensing agreement with Nvidia for its inference technology — the processing of queries by already-trained AI models. Groq founder Jonathan Ross and company president Sunny Madra moved to Nvidia along with part of the team. Simon Edwards became Groq's new CEO, and the company itself continued operating independently.

CNBC valued the deal at [$20 billion](https://techcrunch.com/2025/12/24/nvidia-acquires-ai-chip-challenger-groq-for-20b-report-says/) — Groq itself has not confirmed that figure.

The August round was Groq's second in two months: in June, the company raised [$650 million](https://groq.com/newsroom/groq-raises-usd650m-to-scale-its-ai-inference-cloud-business) to develop its cloud infrastructure for inference.
