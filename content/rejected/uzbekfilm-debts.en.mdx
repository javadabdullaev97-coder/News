First Deputy Culture Minister and director of the Cinematography Agency Jahongir Akhmedov [reported](https://t.me/uzbekkinopress/30519) in a Telegram channel that film studio Uzbekfilm has accumulated €22.9 million in loan obligations and more than 1 billion soums in tax debt. The tax debt converts to roughly $84,400 at the [Central Bank](https://cbu.uz/ru/) exchange rate as of August 21.

According to him, the studio's assets, including land plots, have been pledged as collateral for the loans. Because of the tax debt, 12 vehicles on Uzbekfilm's balance sheet could be put up for auction within 12 days. Akhmedov also reported debts owed to the studio's employees — he did not specify the exact amount of wage arrears.

Akhmedov said Uzbekfilm lacks a full-fledged business model: the studio depends on state orders and has not shifted to market-based operations. By his estimate, to break even the studio needs to complete at least 60 billion soums worth of work per year — about $5.1 million at the Central Bank exchange rate as of August 21. Akhmedov said the film studio needs not just to be preserved but reformed — by attracting private investment and establishing international cooperation.
