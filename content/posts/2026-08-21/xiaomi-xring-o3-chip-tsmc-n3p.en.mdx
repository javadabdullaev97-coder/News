Xiaomi is preparing a new generation of its in-house Xring smartphone processor — on August 18, the company's chairman and founder Lei Jun confirmed the chip's development in a post on his Weibo page, [DigiTimes reports](https://www.digitimes.com/news/a20260821VL206/xiaomi-xring-launch-silicon-testing-tsmc.html).

According to [DigiTimes](https://www.digitimes.com/news/a20260821VL206/xiaomi-xring-launch-silicon-testing-tsmc.html), the new chip will be designated Xring O3. It will be manufactured on TSMC N3P — an enhanced 3-nanometer process from Taiwan's TSMC, one of the world's largest chipmakers. In China, Xiaomi's processor line is called Xuanjie.

Supply chain sources cited by DigiTimes say the chip has returned from the fab and passed initial power-on testing — a functionality check performed right after manufacturing. It then moved to device-level testing. Similar information was published on August 19 by the Chinese outlet [ithome.com](https://www.ithome.com/0/991/685.htm), citing industry sources. According to them, the chip returned from the fab in late 2025 and was powered on for the first time that same day.

Xiaomi has not yet named an exact date for the new chip's unveiling or the device in which it will debut. According to the Chinese outlet [ithome.com](https://m.ithome.com/html/991336.htm), Xiaomi President Lu Weibing said shipments of the previous generation — Xring O1 — have exceeded one million units across three models.
