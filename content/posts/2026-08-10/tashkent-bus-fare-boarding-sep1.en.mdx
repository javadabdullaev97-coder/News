Starting September 1, 2026, passengers on city buses operating under gross contracts must pay the fare or confirm a discount at boarding, under [Cabinet of Ministers Resolution No. 276](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/8244019) signed June 3.

## What changes

The resolution introduces mandatory fare payment at the moment of boarding for regular city bus routes organized under the gross-contract system. This can be done in three ways, [according to the document](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/8244019):

- tapping a transit card on the validator;
- paying contactlessly with a bank card or smartphone (NFC, QR code, biometrics or other contactless technology);
- confirming eligibility for a discounted or free ride with a social or transit card.

If a passenger does not pay the fare or confirm a discount by any of these methods at boarding, they are considered a fare evader and subject to administrative liability under established procedure. The resolution does not specify the amount of the penalty.

Under the gross-contract system, the carrier receives a fixed payment from the city rather than relying on fare revenue from the route. This model applies to regular municipal bus routes in Tashkent and regional centers of Uzbekistan, so the new requirement will primarily affect the capital's network — Tashkent's entire city bus fleet has already been switched to this model.

The same document, in a separate provision, zeroes out the recycling fee on imports of new buses and electric buses of category M3 (60 or more passengers) meeting Euro-5 or higher standards — the benefit will remain in effect through December 31, 2027, [according to the resolution](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/8244019).

## When it takes effect

Resolution No. 276 was signed on June 3, 2026, and took effect the next day, June 4. The mandatory boarding-payment requirement takes effect later than the document's other provisions, on a deferred date — September 1, 2026. The Ministry of Justice's Telegram channel "Huquqiy axborot" [published](https://t.me/huquqiyaxborot/19930) a brief summary of the resolution on June 3.

The Ministry of Transport briefly [mentioned](https://t.me/Mintrans_uz/28602) the upcoming tightening on August 7 — against the backdrop of its own [statistics](https://leap.uz/en/2026/08/08/tashkent-bus-fare-evaders-80k-fined-aug2026) showing it had fined nearly 80,000 fare evaders on Tashkent buses since the start of the year — but without details on payment methods or the validator requirement that Resolution No. 276 itself specifies.

## What's next

By September 1, carriers on gross contracts must equip their buses with validators supporting card, smartphone, and social-card payments for eligible riders. The resolution contains no separate timeline for fleet equipping or list of routes where the system must work on day one — these details may appear later in subordinate regulations or clarifications from relevant agencies.
