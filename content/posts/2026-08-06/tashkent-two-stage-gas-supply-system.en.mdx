The Tashkent hokimiyat announced on August 6 that the capital is switching to a two-stage gas supply system: pressure regulators have already been installed in [5,649 homes](https://t.me/poytaxt_uz/51275) against a target of 5,484 sites.

## What's changing: a regulator in every home

The essence of the switch is that a [regulator is being installed](https://t.me/poytaxt_uz/51275) (a gas pressure regulator) in every Tashkent household. This is a standard technical device: it keeps gas pressure within set limits regardless of the load on the network, making supply more stable. The regulator supplements the existing system of district-level gas distribution stations (GRP) — regulation now takes place in two stages: first at the GRP, then directly at the consumer's premises.

The hokimiyat [did not name](https://tashkent.uz/uz/news/7380) the specific districts where the work is underway and did not provide the names of the officials or units responsible for overseeing the project.

## Plan exceeded by 3%

According to [data](https://tashkent.uz/uz/news/7380) from the hokimiyat, the plan called for installing regulators at 5,484 sites. In practice, the work has already been completed at 5,649 sites — 165 more than planned, or about 3%. The hokimiyat did not clarify whether this refers to exceeding an annual plan or an interim result of a broader program.

The hokimiyat [links](https://t.me/poytaxt_uz/51275) the modernization to preparations for the autumn-winter season: according to its data, it should keep gas pressure within normal limits during the cold season and reduce the risk of supply disruptions.

## What's next

The hokimiyat did not specify a completion date for the program at the city's remaining sites and did not report how many homes in Tashkent are to be equipped with regulators in total. The publication contains no official data on the project's cost or the contractors carrying out the installation.
