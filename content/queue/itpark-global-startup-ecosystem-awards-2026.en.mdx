The Global Startup Ecosystem Awards ceremony will be held in Uzbekistan for the first time on September 23, as part of ICT Week Uzbekistan 2026 — the international research platform StartupBlink will serve as a partner, [the IT Park Uzbekistan Telegram channel said](https://t.me/itpark_uz/20594).

## What the Award Is

The ceremony will honor cities, countries and regions that showed high growth, leadership and innovation potential according to the Global Startup Ecosystem Index — StartupBlink's annual ranking, [the publication says](https://t.me/itpark_uz/20594). Besides honoring the world's leading startup ecosystems, the program includes a panel discussion with international experts and ecosystem representatives, as well as networking with government agencies, investors and startup communities. In addition, negotiations on new partnerships between countries are planned.

Registration for the event is open at [ictweek.it-park.uz](https://ictweek.it-park.uz/).

## ICT Week Uzbekistan 2026

The ceremony will take place as part of ICT Week Uzbekistan 2026, an annual technology exhibition that this year will be held September 22-25 in Tashkent. The event is held with the support of Uzbekistan's Ministry of Digital Technologies, [according to gov.uz](https://gov.uz/en/digital/news/view/130680).

## StartupBlink and Uzbekistan's Standing

StartupBlink is an international research and consulting firm that publishes the Global Startup Ecosystem Index every year — a ranking of startup ecosystems in more than 100 countries and 1,500 cities. In the 2026 edition of the index, released in May, Uzbekistan climbed to 79th place in the global country ranking, up 19 positions from its 2025 level. StartupBlink estimated the country's ecosystem growth year-on-year at 227.3%. Tashkent climbed 121 positions in the global city ranking and entered the top 300 for the first time, [the country's profile on the StartupBlink website shows](https://www.startupblink.com/startup-ecosystem/uzbekistan).

Organizers of the annual StartupBlink Ecosystem Awards ceremony have held it in different countries and in an online format in various years. The September event in Tashkent will be the first time it is held in Uzbekistan, [according to the ceremony's page on the StartupBlink website](https://lp.startupblink.com/ecosystemawards/).
