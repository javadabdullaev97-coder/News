President Shavkat Mirziyoyev on August 4 [reviewed](https://t.me/shmirziyoyev/35014) proposals to reorganize the Cadastre Agency under the Ministry of Economy and Finance and approved shifting its services to a "one application — one payment" principle.

## What Will Change in the Agency's Work

To spare citizens from having to visit different service windows, the agency has been [instructed](https://t.me/uz_kadastr/11462) to launch the "Kadastr maslahat burchagi" (consultation desk) and "Tezkor kadastr" (express cadastre) services.

Proposals to reorganize the agency's entire operations around the "one application — one payment" principle were also reviewed. Under the new procedure, when a property right arises, an application will be generated automatically and the service provided proactively, without a separate request from the citizen. All cadastre-related services will be combined into a single application — expected to cut unnecessary requests by 200,000 a year.

According to the agency's estimates, the reform will save time and money for 1.2 million applicants a year.

Property rights registration will be moved fully online. As a result, about 500,000 applications a year will be registered within seconds — down from the current 10–15 days.

The published materials do not specify an exact timeline for the transition to the new system or name specific officials responsible.

## Reduced Penalties

At the same presentation, proposals to streamline administrative liability were [approved](https://t.me/uz_kadastr/11463). Citizens who voluntarily remove unauthorized construction will face lighter penalties. For a first failure to file a property rights registration application within the set deadline, a warning will be issued instead of a fine.

Officials in charge have been instructed to simplify services, cut unnecessary steps and payments, delegate part of the decision-making authority to lower levels, and improve working conditions for agency staff.

## Context

The Cadastre Agency has operated under the Ministry of Economy and Finance since October 2022. At the time, [Presidential Resolution](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/6246733) No. PP-405 approved the agency's projected performance targets for 2023–2026 and introduced a vertical management system for its territorial offices. The same document, starting in 2026, made full state registration of rights to state-owned real estate a condition for receiving budget funding.
