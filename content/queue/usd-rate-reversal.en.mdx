The Central Bank of Uzbekistan on August 24 set the U.S. dollar rate at [11,853.55 soums](https://cbu.uz/ru/arkhiv-kursov-valyut/json/USD/2026-08-24/) per dollar — 58.67 soums above the three-year low of 11,794.88 soums reached on August 20. The rate reversed after the decline LEAP News reported earlier.

The decline did not stop at the August 19 level of 11,820.40 soums. The rate fell further: on August 20, the Central Bank [set](https://cbu.uz/ru/arkhiv-kursov-valyut/json/USD/2026-08-20/) the rate at 11,794.88 soums — the low point before the reversal. The next trading day, August 21, the rate [rose](https://cbu.uz/ru/arkhiv-kursov-valyut/json/USD/2026-08-21/) to 11,847.16 soums — up 52.28 soums in a single day. The bank does not revise the rate over the weekend, August 22-23, since the exchange is closed. On Monday, August 24, the rate climbed another 6.39 soums to 11,853.55 soums. The rate has now risen for a second consecutive trading day.

## Rate Remains Below Mid-August Level

Despite the rise over the past two trading days, the rate on August 24 remains below its level before the decline began. On August 13, before the rate started falling, the Central Bank [set](https://cbu.uz/ru/arkhiv-kursov-valyut/json/USD/2026-08-13/) the rate at 11,948.58 soums — 95.03 soums, or about 0.8%, above the current value.
