The Central Bank of the Republic of Uzbekistan said on Wednesday, August 5, that 34,341 deals for real estate and vehicles were processed through escrow accounts across the country in July 2026.

## Breakdown by deal type

Of the total number of deals, 18,306 were for vehicles and 16,035 for real estate, [according to a post](https://t.me/centralbankuzbekistan/13398) on the Central Bank's Telegram channel. Another 1,096 deals remained under review at the end of the month.

## Tashkent and three regions lead in deal volume

Tashkent recorded the highest number of deals in July, with 9,674. The Fergana (3,401 deals), Samarkand (2,810) and Andijan (2,567) regions also showed high activity, the Central Bank of Uzbekistan [said](https://t.me/centralbankuzbekistan/13398). These four regions accounted for more than half of all deals for the month.

## Why deals go through escrow accounts

Mandatory cashless payments for the purchase and sale of real estate and vehicles were introduced by [presidential decree No. UP-246](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/7897677) of December 10, 2025, "On additional measures to promote cashless payments and reduce the share of the shadow economy." The document required payments for goods and services exceeding 25 million soums to be made in cashless form. For deals involving real estate and vehicles, this requirement applies with no threshold on the amount — regardless of the deal's value. The rule for these two categories took effect on April 1, 2026.

The buyer's funds pass through a bank account that the Central Bank of Uzbekistan calls an escrow account, while the transfer to the seller happens through data exchange between the information systems of banks and notary bodies, as described in the decree.
