The Tashkent City Trunk Electric Networks branch (a division of National Electric Networks of Uzbekistan JSC) [said](https://t.me/Toshkent_shaharMET_Axborot/2982) it has completed the modernization of the Botanicheskaya substation in Tashkent, which has been operating since 1970.

## What Was Replaced at the Substation

The Botanicheskaya substation, rated at 110/35/6 kV, [ran](https://t.me/poytaxt_uz/51276) on transformers with a capacity of 40 MVA and 63 MVA — 103 MVA combined. They were replaced with two new 80 MVA transformers each, bringing the substation's total capacity to 160 MVA — an increase of about 55%.

Along with the transformers, the branch upgraded electrical equipment and relay protection and automation devices. The goal, according to the [operator](https://t.me/Toshkent_shaharMET_Axborot/2982), is to improve the stability of power supply, reduce the risk of outages during peak loads, and improve power quality for residents and businesses in the district. The same text on the modernization's parameters and goals was first [published](https://t.me/poytaxt_uz/51276) by the Tashkent hokimiyat press service — the branch's post is a repost of that message.

## Part of a Nationwide Program

The substation's modernization comes amid decisions made at the presidential level. On July 27, Shavkat Mirziyoyev [held](https://president.uz/oz/lists/view/9456) a video conference on priority tasks for the fuel and energy sector, where it was said that about 4,000 accidents occurred in the country's power grids in June and July, and that power losses in the grids stand at 17.2%.

The meeting cited figures on investment in the sector: over the past decade, $23 billion in foreign investment has been attracted to the country's energy sector, power plants with a combined capacity of 9.5 gigawatts have been brought online, and 30 trillion soums (≈$2.5 billion at the Central Bank's August 7 rate of 11,915.64 soums per dollar) has been allocated to modernizing electric and gas networks.

The same branch Telegram channel also [reported](https://t.me/Toshkent_shaharMET_Axborot/2983) on the fulfillment of presidential directives for the energy sector.

## What's Next

The branch is simultaneously modernizing the Astronomicheskaya substation, rated at 110/35/6 kV — according to the [branch's channel](https://t.me/Toshkent_shaharMET_Axborot/2974), once the work is complete its capacity will rise from 32 MVA to 50 MVA. An exact completion date for the work at Astronomicheskaya has not yet been announced.
