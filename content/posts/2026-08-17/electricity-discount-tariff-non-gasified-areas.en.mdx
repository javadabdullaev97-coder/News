On July 27, following a videoconference on the fuel and energy sector, President Shavkat Mirziyoyev instructed the Ministry of Energy to calculate the infrastructure and additional electricity volume needed to improve heating system stability and strengthen power infrastructure in 1,916 non-gasified mahallas.

## What the president ordered

According to [materials from the presidential administration](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9456), the country's power grids suffered about 4,000 accidents in June–July, and electricity losses reached 17.2% — equivalent to 4.8 billion kWh over six months. Over the past decade, the energy sector has attracted $23 billion in investment.

Following the meeting, the former minister of energy and the head of Hududiy Elektr Tarmoqlari JSC were dismissed. Those responsible were instructed to calculate the infrastructure and additional electricity volume needed to improve heating system stability and strengthen power infrastructure in 1,916 mahallas where, as of July 27, natural gas delivery is difficult or impossible. The same order provides for subsidies to buy liquefied gas for families included in the Unified Social Protection Registry.

## Khodjayev takes over as minister, pilot launched in Yangiyul

Sherzod Khodjayev held the post of minister of energy as of July 31, according to a [Ministry of Energy post](https://t.me/minenergy_uz/20647) about his visit to Yangiyul district.

Yangiyul district became a separate pilot area where, according to the [Ministry of Energy](https://t.me/minenergy_uz/20647), the minister held an open dialogue with 27 entrepreneurs on July 31 and urged them to demand that Elektr Tarmoqlari fulfill its obligations under contracts rather than settle issues informally. By October 1, authorities are to study the electricity and gas needs of residents and businesses in 51 mahallas in the district and use that data to build an energy consumption balance — a model officials plan to later extend nationwide.
