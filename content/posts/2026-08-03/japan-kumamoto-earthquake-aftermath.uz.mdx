Yaponiyaning Kyushu orolining janubida 28-iyul kuni 7,1 balli zilzila yuz berdi — Kumamoto prefekturasi hokimiyati 31-iyul kuni 34 kishining halok bo'lganini tasdiqladi, 9 mingdan ortiq aholi hali ham evakuatsiya markazlarida qolmoqda.

## 28-iyulda nima yuz berdi

Zilzila [qayd etilgan](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/7/28/japan-kumamoto-earthquake-what-happened-damage-victims-latest-updates) mahalliy vaqt bilan taxminan soat 16:30da Kyushu orolining janubidagi Kumamoto prefekturasida. Yaponiya meteorologiya agentligi (JMA) ma'lumotlariga ko'ra, uning magnitudasi 7,1 ni tashkil etdi; AQSh Geologik xizmati keyinchalik zilzila kuchini 6,8 deb baholadi. Zilziladan so'ng darhol hokimiyat sunami haqida ogohlantirish e'lon qildi, keyinroq bu ogohlantirish bekor qilindi.

Zilzila Kashima shahridagi Aeon Mall savdo markazida portlashga sabab bo'ldi — binoning ikkinchi qavati qulab tushdi. Yatsushiro shahridagi Nippon Paper Industries zavodida mo'ri qulab tushdi, zilziladan zarar ko'rgan hududlarda turar-joy binolari vayron bo'ldi, [PBS NewsHour xabar beradi](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/quake-survivors-in-southwestern-japan-sleep-in-cars-in-sweltering-heat-as-death-toll-rises-to-34).

Yaponiya Bosh vaziri Sanae Takaichi zilziladan bir kun keyin [bunday dedi](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/7/28/japan-kumamoto-earthquake-what-happened-damage-victims-latest-updates): «Hozir ham qutqarilishni kutayotgan odamlar bor, va bu vaqt bilan poyga».

## Qurbonlar va vayronagarchilik

Kumamoto prefekturasi hokimiyati juma kuni, 31-iyulda, [34 kishining halok bo'lganini tasdiqladi](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/show/news-wrap-death-toll-from-japan-earthquake-rises-to-34); yana bir o'lim holati nashr etilgan vaqtda zilzila bilan bog'liqligi tekshirilmoqda. Kamida 96 kishi jarohat oldi, ulardan oltitasi og'ir ahvolda, [PBS NewsHour ma'lumot beradi](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/quake-survivors-in-southwestern-japan-sleep-in-cars-in-sweltering-heat-as-death-toll-rises-to-34).

Eng ko'p qurbon ikki obyekt bilan bog'liq. Yatsushirodagi Nippon Paper Industries zavodida qulagan mo'ri ostidan 11 kishi qutqarildi, to'qqiz kishi halok bo'ldi. Kashimadagi Aeon Mall savdo markazidagi portlashda yetti kishining halok bo'lgani tasdiqlandi — hokimiyat yo'qolganlar sonini oshkor qilmayapti. Zilzila 1 500 dan ortiq binoga zarar yetkazdi yoki ularni vayron qildi, ulardan 179 tasi butunlay vayron bo'ldi.

Hafta oxiriga kelib mintaqadagi taxminan 80 ming uy suv ta'minotisiz qolgan edi, [PBS NewsHour ma'lumot beradi](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/quake-survivors-in-southwestern-japan-sleep-in-cars-in-sweltering-heat-as-death-toll-rises-to-34); zarar ko'rgan hududlarda elektr ta'minoti juma kuni kechqurunga kelib to'liq tiklandi.

## Evakuatsiya markazlaridagi hayot

9 mingdan ortiq kishi [evakuatsiya markazlarida tunlamoqda](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/quake-survivors-in-southwestern-japan-sleep-in-cars-in-sweltering-heat-as-death-toll-rises-to-34), u yerda hokimiyat konditsionerlar uchun qo'shimcha quvvat manbalari o'rnatmoqda. Zarar ko'rganlarning bir qismi shaxsiy fazoni saqlash uchun mashinalarda tunashni afzal ko'radi, ammo konditsioner uchun yoqilg'i yetishmasligiga duch kelmoqda. Kunduzgi issiqlik 36 darajagacha yetadi — hokimiyat favqulodda harorat ogohlantirishini e'lon qildi.

Zarar ko'rganlardan biri, Haruto Kubo, zilziladan beri mashinada yashamoqda: kunduzi ovqat izlab yuradi, kechasi mashinada tunaydi, [PBS NewsHour hikoya qiladi](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/show/news-wrap-death-toll-from-japan-earthquake-rises-to-34).

## Keyin nima bo'ladi

[Yaponiya meteorologiya agentligi ogohlantirdi](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/7/28/japan-kumamoto-earthquake-what-happened-damage-victims-latest-updates) — asosiy zilziladan keyingi hafta davomida kuchli qo'shimcha zilzilalar (aftershok) yuz berishi mumkin, eng katta xavf esa keyingi ikki-uch kunda saqlanadi. Portlash yuz bergan Kashimadagi Aeon Mall savdo markazi zilziladan atigi bir oy oldin ochilgan edi. Bino 2016-yilgi Kumamoto zilzilalaridan keyin qayta tiklangan edi, [Al Jazeera ta'kidlaydi](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/7/28/japan-kumamoto-earthquake-what-happened-damage-victims-latest-updates).
