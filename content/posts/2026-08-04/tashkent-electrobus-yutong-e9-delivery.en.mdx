Ideal Avto Servis received the symbolic keys to 10 new Yutong E9 electric buses for Tashkent's public transport system at a ceremony on August 4.

## Handover Ceremony

The ceremony [was held](https://t.me/codd_tashkent/292) by the Tashkent Traffic Management Center (TsODD) for Ideal Avto Servis LLC, a company that handles passenger transport in the capital. Mirakbar Ikramov, head of the Main Directorate for Transport Development and Road Transport Infrastructure of Tashkent city, [took part](https://t.me/Mintrans_uz/28536) in the event. Neither TsODD nor the Ministry of Transport of the Republic of Uzbekistan (Mintrans) specified in their statements which routes the new electric buses will serve or how much their purchase cost.

The Yutong E9 electric buses are environmentally friendly — they emit no harmful substances into the atmosphere — and are equipped with full passenger amenities, which, according to the agencies, should raise service quality to a new level.

## Fleet Renewal Program

Tashkent is implementing a broader bus fleet renewal program. In February 2025, the transport minister [discussed](https://gov.uz/oz/news/view/36121) with the vice president of Yutong Bus the status of a project to purchase 200 electric buses for Tashkent, as well as a prospective project to acquire another 1,000 buses for the city.

Since June 3, 2026, Uzbekistan has applied a zero recycling fee rate through the end of 2027 on imports of new buses, electric buses and trolleybuses with a capacity of 60 or more passengers that meet the Euro-5 environmental standard or higher — this incentive [was established](https://lex.uz/uz/docs/-8244019) by Cabinet of Ministers Resolution No. 276 and is aimed at lowering the cost of purchasing modern urban transport.
