Uzhydromet's automatic monitoring stations recorded PM10 particulate levels in Tashkent at 103 мкг/м³ at 9:00 a.m. on August 21 — 1.37 times above the maximum permissible concentration of 75 мкг/м³ ([Uzhydromet](https://t.me/uzgydromet/22041)). The agency's full name is the Hydrometeorological Service Agency under the National Committee for Ecology and Climate Change of the Republic of Uzbekistan.

Levels of finer PM2,5 particles stood at 29 мкг/м³. The maximum permissible concentration for this indicator is 60 мкг/м³, meaning no violation ([Uzhydromet](https://t.me/uzgydromet/22041)).

The morning reading on August 21 was the highest in the past 24 hours. On the evening of August 20, automatic stations recorded PM10 at 82 мкг/м³ — 1.09 times above the limit ([Uzhydromet](https://t.me/uzgydromet/22038)).

For August 21, Uzhydromet forecast temperatures of 36–38 degrees Celsius in Tashkent, gusty wind of up to 10–12 m/s, and possible dust storms in some regions of the republic ([Uzhydromet](https://t.me/uzgydromet/22040)).
