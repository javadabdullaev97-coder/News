Uzsuvtaminot JSC has reminded customers that water supply and sewerage services must be paid 100% in advance by the 10th of the month: the next payment deadline is August 10, 2026, [the company said](https://t.me/uzsuv/45453) in a post on its Telegram channel.

## The requirement has been in force since 2018 and applies to all customers

The 100% prepayment procedure is not new this month — it is a rule introduced by [Presidential Decree UP-5241](https://lex.uz/docs/3413658) of November 16, 2017. According to the document, starting January 1, 2018, water supply and sewerage services are provided to "customers of all groups" on the basis of full prepayment for the coming month. That means the requirement applies to individuals, legal entities and budget-funded organizations alike, with no exceptions.

For nonpayment, the decree provides for measures "up to full disconnection from water supply and sewerage systems." Reconnection is paid: for Group I customers (individuals), the fee equals twice the base calculation value; for Groups II and III (legal entities and budget-funded organizations), it is ten times that value.

Since 2019, services have been provided by a single entity — [Uzsuvtaminot JSC](https://lex.uz/docs/4611203), established under Presidential Decree UP-5883 of November 26, 2019, on the basis of state-owned water utility enterprises previously subordinate to the Ministry of Housing and Communal Services.

## How the prepayment amount is calculated

According to [the company's post](https://t.me/uzsuv/45453), where meters are installed, the advance payment amount is calculated based on the average consumption volume over the past three months. The final adjustment based on actual meter readings takes place once the customer submits readings for the past month.

## Late payment penalty: 0.1% for individuals, 0.4% for legal entities per day

If the prepayment is not received by the 10th, according to the company, [a penalty is charged](https://t.me/uzsuv/45453) on the outstanding amount: 0.1% per day for individuals and 0.4% per day for legal entities. The maximum penalty is capped at 50% of the overdue payment amount.

## How and where to pay

Services can be paid through the personal account portal at [cabinet.uzsuv.uz](https://cabinet.uzsuv.uz). After entering an account number, a confirmation code is sent by phone, granting access to the account with a payment history and meter reading submission. The company accepts inquiries and complaints through the Telegram bot [@suvmuammobot](https://t.me/suvmuammobot) and via a single call center, [listed in the channel description](https://t.me/uzsuv) — 1255.
