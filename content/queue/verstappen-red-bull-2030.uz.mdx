Maks Verstappen 20-avgustda Red Bull Racing bilan shartnomasini 2030-yil mavsumi oxirigacha uzaytirdi — bu haqda [jamoa xabar berdi](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/verstappen-signs-contract-extension-with-red-bull-until-the-end-of-2030.4gUd8JEkYnKmD6bNMS5syw), uning uy gonkasi hisoblanadigan Zandvortdagi Niderlandiya Gran-prisidan bir kun oldin.

Avvalgi shartnoma 2028-yil oxirigacha amal qilardi — yangi shartnoma hamkorlikni yana ikki mavsumga uzaytiradi.

Red Bull Racing rahbari Loran Mekies Verstappenning jamoada qolishini [«ajoyib yangilik»](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/verstappen-signs-contract-extension-with-red-bull-until-the-end-of-2030.4gUd8JEkYnKmD6bNMS5syw) deb atadi. Pilotning jamoaga va Red Bullning avtosport tarixidagi o'rniga ta'siri «o'zgartiruvchi bo'ldi», deyilgan jamoa bayonotida.

[Sky Sports](https://www.skysports.com/f1/news/12433/13574907/max-verstappen-red-bull-driver-signs-new-contract-extension-until-2030-formula-1-season-ahead-of-dutch-grand-prix) ma'lumotlariga ko'ra, shartnomada Verstappenga 2027-yildan boshlab boshqa jamoaga o'tish imkonini beruvchi band bo'lgan. Bu huquq, agar u yozgi tanaffusga — mavsum o'rtasida poyga bosqichlari orasida bo'ladigan tanaffusga — qadar chempionat ochkolari yig'indisi bo'yicha eng yaxshi ikki pilot orasiga kirmasa, ishga tushishi kerak edi. Verstappen bu huquqdan foydalanmadi. Nashr ma'lumotiga ko'ra, shu yilning boshida uning menejerlari McLaren bilan 2027-yil mavsumiga o'tish bo'yicha muzokaralar olib borgan.

Verstappen bu qarorni butun «Formula-1» karerasi o'tgan jamoaga bog'langanligi bilan izohladi. «Jamoa men uchun ikkinchi oila kabi, va men bu yerda o'zimni uyimdagidek his qilaman», — [dedi u](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/verstappen-signs-contract-extension-with-red-bull-until-the-end-of-2030.4gUd8JEkYnKmD6bNMS5syw).

Alohida sharhida pilot shunday qo'shimcha qildi: «Menga boshqa rangda chiqishning hojati yo'q» — bu bilan Red Bull mashinalarining o'ziga xos ko'k rangini nazarda tutgan. Uning so'zlariga ko'ra, jamoa egalaridan biri bo'lgan Ditrix Mateshits hayotligida ham u aynan shu jamoada karerasini yakunlashni xohlashini aytgan edi. «Men hech qachon shunchaki o'zgarish uchun jamoa almashtirish istagini yoki zaruratini his qilmaganman», — [dedi Verstappen](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/i-dont-need-to-drive-in-another-colour-verstappen-explains-why-he-chose-to-stay-with-family-at-red-bull.4z0k66NtGxU2V8WlKEEg7N).

Red Bulldagi karerasi davomida Verstappen 71 poygada g'alaba qozongan va to'rt marta chempionlik unvonini qo'lga kiritgan, [deb ta'kidlaydi Formula1.com](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/verstappen-signs-contract-extension-with-red-bull-until-the-end-of-2030.4gUd8JEkYnKmD6bNMS5syw).
