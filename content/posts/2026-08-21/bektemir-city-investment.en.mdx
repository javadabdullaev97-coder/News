Uzbekistan's Cabinet of Ministers, in Resolution No. 83 of February 28, 2026, added the Bektemir City business complex in Tashkent's Bektemir district to a list of 31 comprehensive development projects for the capital. The 58-hectare site is planned to receive $1.2 billion in investment, according to the [resolution](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/8069188).

The complex will be built within the boundaries of the Zabardast mahalla in Bektemir district. Under the resolution, the site will include a residential complex as well as services and entertainment facilities. Land plots for development will be divided into lots and put up for electronic auctions in stages.

The project's implementation on the ground is overseen by the Tashkent City Hokimiyat: the corresponding decision was published on the e-qaror.gov.uz government decisions portal under registration number 419-14-0-Q/26. The Cabinet named Deputy Prime Minister Abdulla Ramatov, Minister of Construction and Housing and Communal Services Shavkat Khidoyatov, and Tashkent City Hokim Shavkat Umurzakov as responsible for implementing the resolution.

## Another Project in Bektemir District

Under the same resolution, the Cabinet added a second, larger project in the same district to the list. Housing will be built in the Nurafshon and Mirishkor microdistricts on 150 hectares, with $3 billion in investment.

In total, the list includes 31 comprehensive development projects in Tashkent covering 2,311.4 hectares. The total volume of announced investment exceeds $16.28 billion.
