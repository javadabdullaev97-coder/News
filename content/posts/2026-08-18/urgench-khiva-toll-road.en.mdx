President Shavkat Mirziyoyev on August 17 announced the launch of the Urgench-Khiva toll road, Uzbekistan's first toll highway. The [president's press service reported](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9506) this following his visit to Khorezm region.

Travel time between the cities will drop from 45 to 20 minutes, the statement said. The road connects Urgench's international airport with the historic center of Khiva. The highway was among a package of 12 projects worth $176 million commissioned in Khorezm region that day. At the same time, construction began there on 15 more facilities worth $7.424 billion.

The four-lane highway stretches 35 kilometers, and construction cost $120 million, [Interfax reported](https://www.interfax.ru/world/1105064), citing the president's remarks. The road includes 15 bridges, one tunnel, five underground crossings and 101 culverts, with capacity exceeding 20,000 vehicles per day. Construction began in February 2026.

Travel-time projections have changed since construction began. In July, [Interfax reported](https://www.interfax.ru/world/1105064) expectations of a reduction from 60 to 17 minutes — on the day of the launch, the president's press service cited different figures: from 45 to 20 minutes. Before the highway opened, the trip between Urgench and Khiva took about 45 minutes, according to the president's press service.

## How much will the toll cost

The toll rate for the new highway had not been announced at the time of launch. Neither the president's press service nor the relevant road committee had published rates as of this report's publication.
