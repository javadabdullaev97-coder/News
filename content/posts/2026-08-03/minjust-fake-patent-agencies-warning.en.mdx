The Ministry of Justice of the Republic of Uzbekistan [warned](https://t.me/adliyangiliklari/29936) entrepreneurs on July 30 about scammers who call on behalf of nonexistent "patent agencies," promise guaranteed trademark registration, and threaten those who refuse by claiming the trademark will be transferred to someone else.

## How the Scheme Works

According to [the Ministry of Justice](https://t.me/adliyangiliklari/29936), certain individuals pose as employees of a "patent agency," "patent center," or "patent office" and call citizens and entrepreneurs. For a fee, they promise to guarantee trademark registration. If an entrepreneur refuses, in some cases they are threatened: the trademark will allegedly be registered to someone else, or its use will be restricted.

Organizations bearing such names are not authorized state bodies, the warning says.

## Who Actually Registers Trademarks

The only authorized state body for issuing patents and certificates for intellectual property is the Ministry of Justice itself. This is established by [Administrative Regulation No. 480](https://lex.uz/uz/docs/-6613366) of September 19, 2023: applications are accepted by public service centers, the Unified Interactive State Services Portal, the ministry's dedicated information system, and by mail. Submission through the portal is not available to non-resident applicants.

A formal examination of the application takes 30 days, while examination of the mark itself takes 6 to 7 months from the filing date. The regulation does not specify the amount of patent fees, referring instead to a separate law on state duties; the certificate is issued only after the fee has been paid in full.

Trademark registration is voluntary, the Ministry of Justice emphasized: no one has the right to compel it.

An applicant can submit registration documents independently through the `im.adliya.uz` portal or through a patent attorney. The Ministry of Justice maintains the state register of patent attorneys: a candidate takes a qualification exam that is recorded on video and broadcast on the ministry's website, and recertification takes place at least once every five years — this is established by [Cabinet of Ministers Resolution No. 298](https://lex.uz/docs/245254) of July 1, 2003. The right to register a trademark itself and its 10-year term of validity are defined by [Law No. 267-II](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/-24925) of August 30, 2001.

## What the Ministry of Justice Advises

The ministry urged people not to trust suspicious calls and unfounded promises. If signs of fraud are detected, it advised contacting law enforcement.
