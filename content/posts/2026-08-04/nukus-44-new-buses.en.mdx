Uzbekistan's Ministry of Transport [reported](https://t.me/Mintrans_uz/28543) on August 4, 2026, that over the past two months 44 new buses were delivered to Nukus for city routes — 32 from Fayz Avangard LLC and 12 from Mangit lestorg.

## Who supplied the buses

Two companies handle passenger transport in Nukus. Fayz Avangard LLC delivered 32 buses, Mangit lestorg LLC — 12. Both sources — the Ministry of Transport and the [Jokargy Kenes of Karakalpakstan](https://t.me/joqargikenes/32278) — cite the same figures: 44 buses in two months.

The new vehicles are being distributed across Nukus's intracity routes under the gross-cost contract system, the Ministry of Transport said. The make and model of the buses, as well as the cost of the delivery, are not specified in the statements from the Ministry of Transport or the Jokargy Kenes.

## Context

Both sources describe the renewal of Nukus's bus fleet as part of a broader program to develop public transport in the country's regions. How many buses currently operate on Nukus routes in total, and whether further deliveries are planned, is not specified in the published statements.
