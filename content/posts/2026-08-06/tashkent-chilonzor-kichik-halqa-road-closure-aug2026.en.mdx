The Center for Traffic Organization of Tashkent (CODD) [said](https://t.me/codd_tashkent/296) it will temporarily close a section of the Little Ring Road (Kichik halqa) in Chilanzar District to traffic from 11 p.m. on August 6 to 8 a.m. on August 10.

## Which Section Is Closing and Why

The restriction covers the side of the Little Ring Road between its intersection with Bunyodkor Avenue and its intersection with Arnasay Street, CODD [said in its post](https://t.me/codd_tashkent/296). Major repairs are underway on this section — crews are laying new asphalt concrete pavement.

The work affects only one side of the road: that side is being closed for the duration of the repairs, while the other side remains open to traffic.

## How the Detour Is Organized

According to CODD, vehicle traffic will be redirected for the duration of the repairs to the opposite (operating) side of the Little Ring Road under a temporary traffic scheme. CODD has arranged a separate route for ambulances.

The center [asked](https://t.me/codd_tashkent/296) drivers to plan their routes in advance, reduce speed in the work zone and follow temporary road signs.

## What's Next

CODD says it will restore traffic on the repaired side of the road by 8 a.m. on August 10 — the restriction will last 81 hours, or three days and nine hours.
