Uzbek authorities on August 17 were discussing raising the fine for fare evasion on buses, trams and trolleybuses from 41,200 to 220,000–440,000 soums. According to several media outlets citing Muhammadjon Qahhorov, director of Tashkent's passenger transport monitoring agency, there was no official confirmation from the Ministry of Transport as of publication.

According to the same source — Qahhorov's interview with Uzbekistan 24 TV — the fine for a first offense could rise to 220,000 soums, and to 440,000 soums for a repeat offense. Fare evasion on a city bus, trolleybus or tram is currently punishable by a fine of 41,200 soums [under Part 5 of Article 144](https://lex.uz/docs/97661) of the Code on Administrative Liability.

## Where the new figures come from

In Uzbekistan, fines are calculated not in fixed sums but as a share of the base calculation value (BCV) — a universal figure used to calculate fines, duties and benefits. The current 41,200 soums is exactly 10% of the BCV, which has stood at 412,000 soums since August 2025.

Starting September 1, 2026, the BCV will rise to 440,000 soums under [Presidential Decree UP-115](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/8283680) of June 23, 2026. The decree is not directly linked to the fine increase under discussion.

If the proposal to raise the fine is adopted, the rate will rise from 10% to 50% of the BCV for a first offense — 220,000 soums under the new value. For a repeat offense, the rate will rise to 100% of the BCV — 440,000 soums.

## What has already been decided and what remains a proposal

The legal basis for the discussion is [Cabinet of Ministers Resolution No. 276](https://lex.uz/docs/8244019) of June 3, 2026. It instructed the Ministry of Transport, together with the Ministry of Internal Affairs, to draft within three months a regulatory act toughening liability for fare evasion.

The same resolution has already established, in a separate clause, that from September 1, 2026, passengers must pay for their fare when boarding a bus. They must tap a transport card on the validator, pay by bank card, or confirm a benefit with a social card. Failure to do so means the passenger is considered a fare evader. This part of the decision requires no further approval and takes effect on the specified date.

Raising the fine itself, however, remains only a proposal. According to information cited by several media outlets referencing Muhammadjon Qahhorov, the bill has already been submitted to the Legislative Chamber of the Oliy Majlis, but parliament has not yet made a decision.

The deadline set by the Cabinet of Ministers for drafting the bill expires around September 3, 2026 — three months after Resolution No. 276 was signed.
