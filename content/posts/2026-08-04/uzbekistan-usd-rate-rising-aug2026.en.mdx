The Central Bank of the Republic of Uzbekistan set the official U.S. dollar rate for August 4 at [11,989.46 soums](https://cbu.uz/ru/arkhiv-kursov-valyut/json/USD/2026-08-04/) — up 29.28 soums (0.24%) from the rate in effect on August 3.

## Rise ends a three-day decline

The dollar rate had been [declining](https://cbu.uz/ru/arkhiv-kursov-valyut/json/USD/2026-08-03/) for three consecutive trading days — from a peak of 12,050.34 soums on July 29 to 11,960.18 soums on August 3. Over those three days, the soum strengthened against the dollar by more than 90 soums. The rate for August 4 is the first increase after this run of declines.

By date, the official USD/UZS rate looked as follows:

- July 29 — 12,050.34 soums (local peak)
- July 30 — 12,035.26 soums
- July 31 — 12,006.39 soums
- August 3 — 11,960.18 soums
- August 4 — 11,989.46 soums

## Over a two-week span, the rate has barely moved

Compared not with the most recent local low but over a longer period, the picture looks different. Two weeks ago, on [July 21](https://cbu.uz/ru/arkhiv-kursov-valyut/json/USD/2026-07-21/), the official rate was 11,960.09 soums per dollar — almost identical to the August 3 level. The rate for August 4 is only 29.37 soums, or 0.25%, above the level from two weeks earlier.

A week ago, on July 28, the rate was 11,988.92 soums — practically the same level as now, on August 4 (11,989.46 soums). Over the week, the official dollar rate changed by less than 1 soum.

The Central Bank did not publish separate explanations for the exchange rate fluctuations over the past week.
