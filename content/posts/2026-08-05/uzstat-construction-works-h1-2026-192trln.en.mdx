Uzbekistan's National Statistics Committee [said](https://t.me/uzstataxborot/69169) that construction work worth 192 trillion soums — about $16 billion — was completed in the country in January-June 2026.

## More than half of all investment

Construction work accounted for more than half of all fixed capital investment for the half-year. Total fixed capital investment in January-June 2026 amounted to [338.9 trillion soums](https://stat.uz/oz/matbuot-markazi-2/qo-mita-yangiliklar-2/69425-zbekiston-respublikasida-asosij-kapitalga-investitsiyalar-yanvar-iyun-2026-jil-1) (about $28.3 billion). Based on these two figures from the National Statistics Committee, LEAP News calculates that construction and installation work accounted for 56.7% of that amount.

The Central Bank's exchange rate on August 4, 2026, was 11,989.46 soums per US dollar, according to [the exchange rate archive](https://cbu.uz/ru/arkhiv-kursov-valyut/?date=04.08.2026).

## GDP grew 8.5% in H1

The published construction data comes against a backdrop of overall economic growth. Uzbekistan's gross domestic product [grew 8.5%](https://stat.uz/oz/matbuot-markazi-2/qo-mita-yangiliklar-2/69574-zbekiston-yalpi-ichki-ma-suloti-azhmi-8-5-foizga-sdi) in real terms in January-June 2026 compared with the first half of 2025, the National Statistics Committee said.

The National Statistics Committee itself did not provide a breakdown of construction work volume by region or facility type in the published infographic — the post is limited to the overall half-year figure.
