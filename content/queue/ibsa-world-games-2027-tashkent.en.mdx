Tashkent will host the IBSA World Games — a multi-sport international competition for athletes with visual impairments — from May 26 to June 6, 2027, the Ministry of Sport of Uzbekistan [said](https://t.me/Minsportuz/89940).

## What the Competition Is

The organizer is the International Blind Sports Federation (IBSA): under its auspices, blind and visually impaired athletes, coaches, classifiers and volunteers from around the world compete. According to the [Ministry of Sport](https://t.me/Minsportuz/89940), Tashkent will become the first city in Central Asia to host the games.

The federation made the decision to select Tashkent well before the exact dates were announced now: at an IBSA board meeting on November 15, 2024, Tashkent was [named](https://ibsasport.org/presidents-blog-ibsa-board-meeting-of-november-15-2024/) the preferred host city for the IBSA World Games 2027.

## The Previous Edition — a Benchmark for Scale

The most recent IBSA World Games took place in 2023 in Birmingham, United Kingdom — with more than 1,000 athletes from over 70 countries taking part, [according to](https://ibsasport.org/ibsa-world-games/ibsa-world-games-2023/) IBSA. The program included nine disciplines: judo, goalball, blind football, chess, powerlifting, bowling, shooting, showdown, and a general (athletics) program.

There is no official data yet on the number of participating countries or sports for the 2027 Games in Tashkent — the organizers have not published this information.

## What's Next

The Ministry of Sport and IBSA have not yet disclosed the exact sports program, venues within Tashkent, or the expected number of participants. LEAP News will follow official announcements as they are published.
