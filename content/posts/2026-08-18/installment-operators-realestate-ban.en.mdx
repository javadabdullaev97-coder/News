The president signed Resolution PP-294, "On Measures to Improve the Provision of Installment Payment Services to the Population," on August 14, changing the rules for installment operators in Uzbekistan. From [January 1, 2027](https://t.me/huquqiyaxborot/20275), such operators' activities will be regulated separately, and housing will be excluded from the list of goods and services that can be sold through an intermediary operator.

## What is an installment operator

Operators are legal entities that provide installment services to the public, [according to a publication](https://t.me/huquqiyaxborot/20275) on the "Huquqiy axborot" channel of the Ministry of Justice of the Republic of Uzbekistan (hereinafter, the Ministry of Justice). The right to conduct such activity arises only after inclusion in a registry maintained by the Central Bank of the Republic of Uzbekistan (the Central Bank), according to the [Central Bank of Uzbekistan's Telegram channel](https://t.me/centralbankuzbekistan/13427). According to the Ministry of Justice, banks and microfinance organizations are not classified as operators — they are subject to their own regulatory rules.

## Why housing cannot be sold through an intermediary

Under subparagraph "d" of paragraph 3 of the resolution, real estate and other property with restricted turnover cannot be the object of an installment service provided by an intermediary operator. The Ministry of Justice's publication puts it directly: [an intermediary operator can no longer sell you a home "on installment,"](https://t.me/huquqiyaxborot/20275) meaning a broker will no longer be able to sell housing to a buyer on installment.

The rule does not ban installment plans for apartments as such — the restriction applies specifically to the intermediary scheme through an operator. The document's text indicates that a developer or owner can still sell housing to a buyer on installment directly.

## Limits on amount, term and fees

The resolution also sets limits for the installment service itself offered by operators. The price of goods or services must not exceed 250 base calculation units, and the contract term must not exceed 12 months. The official value of the base calculation unit for 2026 varies across open sources — from 375,000 to 412,000 soums per unit. For this reason, we do not give an exact limit in soums or dollars. The combined amount of fees and markups on top of the principal debt must not exceed half of the annual installment amount, and early repayment will be allowed without penalties.

## When it takes effect

The resolution took effect as an act on [August 17, 2026](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/8407553), according to the document card on lex.uz. Requirements for operators, including the ban on intermediary sales of real estate, take effect separately — starting January 1, 2027.

## Who is affected

The rule applies to companies that currently sell goods and services to the public on installment — from appliances and furniture to intermediary schemes involving housing. Those planning to buy an apartment on installment through an intermediary will, from 2027, need to find a direct deal with the seller or use bank and mortgage instruments.
