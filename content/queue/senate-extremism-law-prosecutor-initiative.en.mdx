The Senate of the Oliy Majlis approved a law on countering extremism, submitted at the initiative of the Prosecutor General of the Republic of Uzbekistan — the Telegram channel of the Prosecutor General's Office [reported](https://t.me/uzbprokuratura/87880) on August 8.

## What the Law Changes

Uzbekistan's current legislation provides for liability only for religious extremism — other forms of it were not subject to sanctions. Judging by the text that the Legislative Chamber of the Oliy Majlis [reviewed](https://uza.uz/ru/posts/ekstremizmga-qarshi-kurashishning-huquqiy-asoslari-yanada-mustahkamlanadi_841173) in its first reading back on April 7, the law expands the definition of extremism and extends liability to its national, racial and political forms.

Amendments are being made to the Criminal Code and the Code of Administrative Liability, as well as to the laws on freedom of information, informatization, prevention of offenses, state youth policy, education, employment, publishing activities and psychological assistance to the population, and to the law "On Countering Extremism" itself. Liability is being tightened for the preparation, storage, distribution and public display of materials that threaten public safety and order.

At the same time, the document introduces incentive and protection measures for citizens who report facts of extremist activity, and establishes restrictions for persons previously convicted of extremism-related crimes.

## How the Law Passed Through the Chambers

On June 5, the Senate Committee on Defense and Security Affairs [discussed](https://uza.uz/ru/posts/senat-zhamoat-tartibiga-tahdid-soluvchi-materiallar-tarqalishining-oldi-olinadi_866755) the bill, noting the need to strengthen public safety measures and reinforce interaction between government agencies and society in countering the spread of dangerous materials. After the committee discussion, the Senate approved the law on August 8.

## What's Next

After Senate approval, the law is sent to President Shavkat Mirziyoyev for signature. The exact date of signing and the assignment of an official number to the law have not yet been announced — the Prosecutor General's Office and the Senate have not disclosed this information. After signing, the document will be published in the National Database of Legislation (lex.uz), where the exact wording of the new provisions, the amount of sanctions and the date the law takes effect will become known.
