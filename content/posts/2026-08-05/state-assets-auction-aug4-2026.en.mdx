The State Assets Management Agency of the Republic of Uzbekistan [sold at an online auction](https://t.me/DAVAKTIVUZ/6192) 294 of 503 listed lots on August 4 for a total of 32.6 billion soums against a starting price of 30.3 billion soums.

## What Was Sold

The final transaction total exceeded the lots' starting value by 2.3 billion soums, reaching 32.6 billion soums (about $2.73 million at the Central Bank's August 3 rate of 11,960.18 soums per dollar). The August 4 rate was not published: cbu.uz was unavailable at the time of verification. A total of 517 legal entities and individuals took part in the bidding, the agency [said](https://t.me/DAVAKTIVUZ/6192) on its Telegram channel.

## Bukhara Clinic Lease Up 80-Fold

The widest gap between starting and final price came in the lot for a branch of a medical center in Bukhara Region: the lease rights to the building, with a starting annual rent of 2 million soums, [sold](https://t.me/DAVAKTIVUZ/6192) for 161 million soums — more than 80 times the starting price. The agency did not name the specific medical center or the winning bidder.

## For Comparison

At the previous auction on August 3, the agency [listed](https://leap.uz/posts/2026-08-04/state-assets-auction-aug3-2026) 1,307 lots, of which 645 sold for a total of 103.6 billion soums against a starting price of 108.2 billion soums — the final total came in below the starting price that time, with 1,668 people taking part in the auction, LEAP News reported.

The August 4 auction drew fewer lots and participants than the previous day's sale, but for the first time since August 3 the final sale total exceeded the starting value — largely due to a sharp price increase on individual lots, including the Bukhara lease.

The State Assets Management Agency regularly holds online auctions through its own electronic platform.
