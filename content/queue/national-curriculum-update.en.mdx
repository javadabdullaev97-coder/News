Uzbekistan's Ministry of Preschool and School Education presented an updated national school curriculum on August 18, the product of several years of work by 147 Uzbek and 30 foreign specialists.

The Republican Education Center and several foreign organizations developed the curriculum together with the ministry, [according to a post](https://t.me/uzedu/44963) on the agency's official Telegram channel.

Specialists held more than 260 working meetings and consultations. According to the ministry, national working groups for all school subjects systematically collaborated with international experts for the first time.

Representatives of the ministry, the Presidential Administration and partner organizations spoke at the presentation.

The ministry did not specify which academic year the curriculum will take effect. Which grades and subjects it will cover was also not addressed at the presentation.
