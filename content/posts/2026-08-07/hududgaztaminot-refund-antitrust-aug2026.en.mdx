The Competition Development and Consumer Protection Committee announced on August 6 that farm enterprise Burgutqal'a Saodati in Karakalpakstan had been refunded over 96 million soums that its branch, Hududgaz Karakalpakstan, had unjustifiably withheld for gas.

The branch belongs to JSC Hududgaztaminot. Converted into dollars, the overpayment amounted to about $8,060 at the [Central Bank's exchange rate](https://cbu.uz) for August 7 — 11,915.64 soums per dollar.

## What the Review Found

The Committee's review was launched after a complaint from the farm itself. The commission found that when calculating gas consumption, Hududgaz Karakalpakstan had used readings from a secondary meter at the gas distribution station instead of the main metering device. The discrepancy between the readings amounted to 42,149 cubic meters — the exact volume by which the branch overcharged the farm, [the Committee's publication says](https://raqobat.gov.uz/raqobat-qomitasi-aralashuvi-bilan-tadbirkordan-gaz-uchun-asossiz-undirilgan-tolov-qajtarilishi-taminlandi/). The Committee called the use of the secondary meter's data unjustified — the branch had no technical or metrological grounds for disregarding the main device's readings.

## What the Committee Did

The Committee's special commission opened proceedings on the complaint and issued Hududgaztaminot a binding order to recalculate the charges and voluntarily refund the overpayment. The branch credited 96 million soums to Burgutqal'a Saodati's account through its own accounting system, [the Committee's Telegram channel reported](https://t.me/RaqobatGovUz/12542).
