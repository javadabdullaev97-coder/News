The Tashkent Center for Road Traffic Organization (CODD) has installed over 70 new signal-controlled pedestrian crossings in the capital since March 2026, the center [said](https://t.me/codd_tashkent/276) in a Telegram post on August 6.

## What Changed at the Crossings

Each new crossing was preceded by an analysis: CODD specialists [studied](https://t.me/codd_tashkent/276) vehicle and pedestrian traffic volumes at locations across the city. They identified potentially hazardous spots and developed engineering solutions for them.

At stretches where pedestrians previously had to pick their own moment to cross through the flow of traffic, CODD [introduced](https://t.me/codd_tashkent/276) separate traffic-light phases. The signal for pedestrians switches on independently of the signal for vehicles, with no overlap between the two flows.

The center did not provide a list of addresses or a breakdown by district — it said only that the crossings had appeared "in various districts" of Tashkent.

## Who Is Responsible

CODD [operates under the Tashkent Hokimiyat](https://tashkent.uz/ru/news/7306) and is responsible for organizing traffic in the capital. The post about the crossings was published under the heading "City for People" (#ShaharInsonUchun).

## What's Next

CODD has not yet disclosed the specific addresses of the new crossings, their total number by district, or the timeline for the next phase. In its post, the center noted only that the creation of safe pedestrian infrastructure in the city is ongoing.
