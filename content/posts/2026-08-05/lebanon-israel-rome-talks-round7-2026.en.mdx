Lebanon and Israel on August 4 began a U.S.-mediated seventh round of direct talks in Rome on implementing a June agreement on the withdrawal of Israeli troops from southern Lebanon and the disarmament of Hezbollah.

## Where the Talks Are Taking Place and Who Is Involved

The talks are being held at the U.S. embassy in Rome and are scheduled to run for three days — from Tuesday, August 4, through Thursday, [Euronews reports](http://ru.euronews.com/my-europe/2026/08/04/new-talks-between-lebanon-and-israel-in-rome-hezbollah-opposed-again). This is the seventh round of direct U.S.-mediated talks between Lebanon and Israel since the process began in April, [Al Jazeera notes](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/4/seventh-round-of-israel-lebanon-talks-begins-whats-on-the-agenda).

Lebanon's political delegation is represented by former Lebanese ambassador to the United States Simon Karam and current ambassador Nada Mouawad, while the military side is represented by Brigadier General George Rizkallah. The delegation also includes adviser Antoine Sfeir and land surveying expert Najib Massihi, [Al Jazeera writes](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/4/seventh-round-of-israel-lebanon-talks-begins-whats-on-the-agenda). Senior Israeli political and military officials are taking part on Israel's side; the outlet does not name them.

U.S. Ambassador to Lebanon Michel Issa warned that pushing the process forward too quickly risks endangering the very civilians the agreement was meant to protect. "There is a real difference between drafting an agreement on paper and implementing it responsibly," [Al Jazeera quotes him as saying](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/4/seventh-round-of-israel-lebanon-talks-begins-whats-on-the-agenda).

## What's on the Agenda

The talks are based on a trilateral framework agreement signed on June 26 in Washington. It provides for a phased withdrawal of Israeli troops from southern Lebanon, the deployment of the Lebanese army there, and the disarmament of "non-governmental armed groups" — a direct reference to Hezbollah, [Al Jazeera's report states](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/4/seventh-round-of-israel-lebanon-talks-begins-whats-on-the-agenda). The withdrawal is proceeding through so-called "pilot zones" — areas Israel is leaving first as a test of the mechanism.

In this round, Lebanon intends to propose adding a new area south of the Litani River to the pilot zones — either Bint Jbeil or Khiam, [Al Jazeera writes](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/4/seventh-round-of-israel-lebanon-talks-begins-whats-on-the-agenda); the country's military delegation will present maps and documents on the deployment of the Lebanese army in Zawtar al-Gharbiya. According to [Interfax](https://www.interfax.ru/world/1107401), the Lebanese side is also pressing for the inclusion of the town of Bint Jubail, Khiam, Zawtar al-Sharqiya and part of the Nabatieh district. The round's agenda also includes border issues and the reconstruction of devastated areas in southern Lebanon.

## Positions of the Parties

Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu has previously said the country will maintain a buffer zone along the border until Hezbollah disarms, [Al Jazeera quotes him as saying](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/4/seventh-round-of-israel-lebanon-talks-begins-whats-on-the-agenda).

Lebanese Prime Minister Nawaf Salam accused Hezbollah of dragging the country into "absurd military adventures," [Euronews reports](http://ru.euronews.com/my-europe/2026/08/04/new-talks-between-lebanon-and-israel-in-rome-hezbollah-opposed-again).

Hezbollah opposes the talks. The group's secretary-general, Naim Qassem, said direct negotiations would bring Lebanon "only shame, humiliation, disappointment and constant concessions," [Al Jazeera reports](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/4/seventh-round-of-israel-lebanon-talks-begins-whats-on-the-agenda). The Shiite Amal movement, allied with Hezbollah, has also voiced opposition to the talks, Euronews notes.

## Context

The current round is not the first in Rome: the sixth round of talks was held there on July 14-15, also at the U.S. embassy, [Al Jazeera notes](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/7/14/lebanon-israel-hold-talks-in-rome-on-implementing-framework-deal). According to the outlet, Israel controls part of the border territory in southern Lebanon and maintains a buffer zone roughly 10 kilometers wide along the border, citing the need to protect against Hezbollah attacks.

As of publication, neither side had commented on the outcome of the round, which is due to conclude on Thursday, August 6.
