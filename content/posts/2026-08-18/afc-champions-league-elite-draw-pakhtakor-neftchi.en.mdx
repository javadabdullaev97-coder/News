Tashkent's Pakhtakor and Fergana's Neftchi learned their opponents for the league stage of the Asian Football Confederation's (AFC) Champions League Elite on August 18, as the draw was held in Kuala Lumpur.

The tournament expanded to 32 clubs for the first time this season. It is the first expansion of the field since the competition was renamed under its current format in the 2024/25 season, [according to the AFC](https://www.the-afc.com/en/club/afc_champions_league_elite.html/news/expanded-aclelite-set-league-stage-draw).

Pakhtakor will host Al Nassr (Riyadh), Esteghlal (Tehran), Tractor (Tabriz) and Al-Qadsiah (Al Khobar) at home. Away, the club will play Al-Ahli (Jeddah), Al-Quwa Al-Jawiya (Baghdad), Al-Hilal (Riyadh) and Al-Ittihad (Jeddah). Pakhtakor [published the list of opponents](https://t.me/Pakhtakor_FC_ru/27451) itself, saying the club landed in the draw's fourth pot.

Neftchi will host Al-Hilal, Al-Ahli, Al-Quwa Al-Jawiya and Al-Ittihad in Fergana. On the road, the team will face Al Nassr, Esteghlal, Tractor and Al-Qadsiah — the club [published the data](https://t.me/pfcneftchi_uz/77524) on its Telegram channel. Pakhtakor and Neftchi will not play each other in the league stage, as neither club appears on the other's list of opponents.

Each club will play eight league-stage matches — four at home and four away.

## Ronaldo: Only the Match Is Confirmed, Not the Player

One of Pakhtakor's home opponents is Al Nassr, the Saudi club for which Cristiano Ronaldo plays. The Tashkent match is the only fixture in the stage involving Ronaldo's club.

Neither the AFC, nor Al Nassr, nor Pakhtakor have yet confirmed Ronaldo's personal participation in the match. The exact date of the game has not been set, and the squad the Saudi club brings to Tashkent will be determined closer to the match.

The AFC will announce the full league-stage calendar with dates and venues later, Pakhtakor's Telegram channel reported.
