The joint trade union committee of Uzbekneftegaz JSC [published a report](https://teletype.in/@uzbekneftegaz_ung_uz/JSRi-rfIPn2) on August 8 on an inspection of 10 production facilities in the Kungrad and Muynak districts of Karakalpakstan: 165 shift workers were living in the Uchsoy dormitory, which has a capacity of 70.

## What the Inspection Found

The commission inspected a condensate loading rack, the Arslon, Surgil and Severny Berdak fields, the Vostochny Berdak and Urga compressor stations, the Uchsoy workers' dormitory and the settlement under construction next to it — 10 facilities in all on the Ustyurt plateau, [the trade union committee's report says](https://teletype.in/@uzbekneftegaz_ung_uz/JSRi-rfIPn2). Uzbekneftegaz JSC's official Telegram channel [briefly reported](https://t.me/ao_uzbekneftegaz/13510) on the inspection itself.

According to the paperwork, the facilities had 90 dormitories with 424 beds. The commission actually counted 115 dormitories with 430 beds — a discrepancy in both the number of buildings and their designated use. The Uchsoy dormitory, designed to house 70 workers, had 165 people living in it at the time of the inspection — more than double its capacity, [the report states](https://teletype.in/@uzbekneftegaz_ung_uz/JSRi-rfIPn2).

## Living Conditions and Safety

The trade union committee noted that the trailer-dormitories have no fencing, which the commission deemed a risk to both property and human life: [the report cites a case](https://teletype.in/@uzbekneftegaz_ung_uz/JSRi-rfIPn2) in which a fox got into the trailer camp's grounds. Some facilities have no fire extinguishers at all, and some of those that do have failed inspection; firefighting equipment points are located 150–200 meters from the residential modules.

The commission also recorded faulty air conditioners, unsanitary conditions in showers and washrooms, and heating problems — some locations still use outdated stoves, while diesel generators are stored outdoors without any covering.

## How Shift Workers Are Fed

Catering at the facilities is outsourced, and according to the trade union committee, the system operates with violations. The main cause is that food is delivered from the Bukhara region: the long journey degrades its quality by the time it is cooked. Some of the kitchen staff did not have valid medical certificates. The commission also found unauthorized substitutions in recipes — margarine is used in the kitchen instead of butter, [the report says](https://teletype.in/@uzbekneftegaz_ung_uz/JSRi-rfIPn2).

## What Has Been Decided

Based on the inspection results, the trade union committee determined that five dormitories require major repairs, and three more need to be rebuilt from scratch. Construction of a 70-bed dormitory at the Surgil field had already begun but was halted midway; construction of another dormitory at the condensate loading rack facility also remains unfinished, [the report says](https://teletype.in/@uzbekneftegaz_ung_uz/JSRi-rfIPn2).

The report links improvements in workers' conditions to the policy pursued by Abdugani Sanginov, chairman of the management board of Uzbekneftegaz JSC; the document does not name a specific person responsible for addressing the deficiencies listed. The publication's author is Khabibulla Azizov, deputy chairman of the company's joint trade union committee.
