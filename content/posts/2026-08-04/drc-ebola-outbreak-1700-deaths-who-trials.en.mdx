The death toll from the Ebola outbreak in the Democratic Republic of Congo surpassed 1,700 by August 4, WHO and international news agencies reported — the largest Ebola outbreak in the country's history.

## Outbreak Concentrated in Ituri Province

The DR Congo's Ministry of Health [declared the outbreak](https://www.who.int/emergencies/disease-outbreak-news/item/2026-DON602) on May 15, 2026 — the country's 17th Ebola outbreak since 1976. As early as May 17, [WHO declared it](https://www.who.int/emergencies/disease-outbreak-news/item/2026-DON614) a public health emergency of international concern.

As of July 30, according to data WHO [published](https://www.who.int/emergencies/disease-outbreak-news/item/2026-DON614) on August 1, 3,626 confirmed cases had been recorded — 3,605 in DR Congo, 20 in Uganda and one in France — along with 1,589 deaths, a case-fatality rate of 44%. Almost all infections were in Ituri province, which accounted for 88% of cases and 82.6% of deaths. The outbreak has spread across five provinces and 49 health zones.

According to data for August 4 cited by [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/4/dr-congo-ebola-outbreak-kills-more-than-1700-as-who-accelerates-trials), citing WHO, and by [Kommersant](https://www.kommersant.ru/doc/8862174), citing a WHO spokesperson, the death toll surpassed 1,700, and the number of confirmed infections reached 3,800. Ituri province's share of total infections rose to nearly 90%, [Al Jazeera reports](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/4/dr-congo-ebola-outbreak-kills-more-than-1700-as-who-accelerates-trials).

Among health workers, WHO [recorded](https://www.who.int/emergencies/disease-outbreak-news/item/2026-DON614) 151 infections and 44 deaths — a case-fatality rate of 29% in this group. The outbreak has surpassed the 2018–2020 epidemic in eastern DR Congo, which sickened 3,317 people, and has become the largest in the country's history, [WHO notes](https://www.who.int/emergencies/disease-outbreak-news/item/2026-DON614).

## WHO Accelerates Vaccine Trials

The Bundibugyo virus is a rare strain of Ebolavirus for which there is still no approved vaccine or specific treatment, WHO [notes](https://www.who.int/emergencies/disease-outbreak-news/item/2026-DON614). Even so, clinical trials of experimental vaccines and treatments are [moving faster](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/4/dr-congo-ebola-outbreak-kills-more-than-1700-as-who-accelerates-trials) than in past Ebola outbreaks, said Vasee Moorthy, acting head of WHO's R&D Blueprint research program. "If you compare this outbreak with previous Ebola outbreaks, we've managed to get trials up and running faster," Moorthy [said](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/4/dr-congo-ebola-outbreak-kills-more-than-1700-as-who-accelerates-trials), adding that preclinical data looks "encouraging."

By comparison, the West Africa Ebola epidemic of 2014–2016 took eight months to reach a death toll of a thousand. The current outbreak in DR Congo is [considered](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/4/dr-congo-ebola-outbreak-kills-more-than-1700-as-who-accelerates-trials) the fastest-growing on record and the second-largest after the West Africa epidemic.

## Response Hampered by Resource Shortage

WHO spokesperson Tarik Jašarević [said](https://www.kommersant.ru/doc/8862174) the scale of the epidemic exceeds the capacity of current response measures: there are not enough safe-burial teams or field staff. "We need additional support, not just financially but in terms of other resources, to scale up the response across all areas," Jašarević [said](https://www.kommersant.ru/doc/8862174).

## What's Next

WHO Director-General Tedros Adhanom Ghebreyesus [plans to visit](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/4/dr-congo-ebola-outbreak-kills-more-than-1700-as-who-accelerates-trials) Kinshasa and Bunia, the administrative center of Ituri province. Africa CDC Director-General Jean Kaseya has also arrived in the region, visiting Bunia on Tuesday. Response teams are tracking more than 17,000 contacts of infected people, checking on about 80% of them daily, [Al Jazeera reports](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/4/dr-congo-ebola-outbreak-kills-more-than-1700-as-who-accelerates-trials).
