The National Agency of Prospective Projects (NAPP) on August 21 [reported](https://t.me/napp_uz/2411) the completion of the pilot issuance of mortgage bonds: five banks placed bonds worth 200.88 billion soums (≈16.96 million US dollars at the Central Bank's exchange rate on August 21).

The pilot was carried out under Presidential Decree No. UP-26 of February 21, 2025, "On additional measures for the further development of housing and mortgage markets" ([lex.uz](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/7395940)). According to NAPP, the decree envisaged placing mortgage bonds worth 200 billion soums among five banks on an experimental basis. The bonds were backed by a pool of mortgage loans from these banks.

Five banks took part in the pilot — Uzmilliybank, Asakabank, Uzpromstroybank (O'zsanoatqurilishbank), Xalq Bank and Agrobank, [NAPP reported](https://t.me/napp_uz/2411). The last two of the five deals took place on August 13 and August 21. The first was worth 40.15 billion soums with the participation of Uzpromstroybank, the second — 39.63 billion soums with the participation of Asakabank. The deals were concluded on the Republican Stock Exchange "Toshkent"; the five closed deals placed a combined 200.88 billion soums.

The bonds were issued by a specialized company, UMRC SPV LLC — a subsidiary of the Mortgage Refinancing Company of Uzbekistan JSC (UMRC).

## How this scheme works

Mortgage bonds are a securitization instrument: a bank assembles a pool of already-issued mortgage loans and transfers it as collateral to a specialized company. The company issues bonds backed by this pool and sells them to investors on the exchange. The proceeds from the sale return to the bank as additional financing. There is no need to wait until borrowers fully repay their previous loans — the funds can be directed immediately toward new mortgage loans.
