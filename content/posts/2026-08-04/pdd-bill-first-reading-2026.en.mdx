The Legislative Chamber of the Oliy Majlis [adopted](https://t.me/qonunchilikpalatasi/37149) in first reading on August 4 a bill toughening liability for serious traffic violations — the document amends four legislative acts.

## What changes in the codes

The bill [amends](https://t.me/qonunchilikpalatasi/37145) the Criminal Code, the Criminal Procedure Code and the Administrative Code, as well as the law "On Road Traffic."

Criminal liability is proposed for letting a minor drive a vehicle. Liability would apply only if this [resulted](https://t.me/qonunchilikpalatasi/37148) in the victim suffering moderate or serious bodily harm, death, or other grave consequences.

Separate administrative liability is introduced for several other types of violations. These include organizing or taking part in illegal races, leaving a truck parked on the shoulder at night in a position dangerous to other road users, and exceeding the speed limit by more than 80 km/h. These same provisions were discussed by Legislative Chamber factions on August 3, LEAP News [reported](https://leap.uz/en/2026/08/03/pdd-bill-speeding-80kmh-vehicle-seizure).

## New sanction — temporary vehicle seizure

The key new measure is the temporary seizure of a motor vehicle for a period of six months to one year. As [stated](https://t.me/qonunchilikpalatasi/37148) in the publication on the Legislative Chamber's channel, the measure will apply to drivers without a driving license or those deprived of one. This covers those who committed road hooliganism, organized illegal races, drove while intoxicated, or refused a medical examination.

## Fine discount temporarily unavailable for repeat offenders

For drivers who repeatedly commit certain serious traffic violations, the bill [proposes](https://t.me/qonunchilikpalatasi/37148) to temporarily suspend the current procedure for paying an administrative fine at a discount. The publication from the Legislative Chamber does not specify exactly how long the discount would be suspended.

Deputies [emphasized](https://t.me/qonunchilikpalatasi/37149) that the bill's main goal is not tougher punishment as such, but the prevention of dangerous violations on the roads.

## What's next

Adoption in first reading is the next procedural step after discussion at the level of Legislative Chamber factions on August 3. At that time, deputies recommended studying the experience of foreign countries and ensuring the uninterrupted operation of traffic lights and warning signs ahead of speed control devices, LEAP News [reported](https://leap.uz/en/2026/08/03/pdd-bill-speeding-80kmh-vehicle-seizure). The date for the bill's second reading has not yet been announced.
