Uzbekistan's Ministry of Internal Affairs on August 7 put a draft new instruction on administrative cases involving traffic violations up for public discussion on the [regulation.adliya.uz](https://regulation.adliya.uz/project/7787/34193) portal, with proposals accepted through August 17.

## What is known about the draft

The document's full title is "On approving the instruction on the procedure for processing and reviewing administrative cases related to violations of traffic rules." The [draft](https://regulation.adliya.uz/project/7787/34193) was developed by the Ministry of Internal Affairs and, according to the draft's card on the portal, has been coordinated with the Prosecutor General's Office and the Supreme Court of Uzbekistan.

The document consists of two annexes: the text of the instruction itself and a list of regulatory acts that lose force upon its adoption. The `regulation.adliya.uz` portal is the Ministry of Justice's official platform for public discussion of draft departmental acts. In the first week of discussion, the draft received 19 proposals and more than 700 views (as of August 14).

The new draft's title broadens the scope of the current document. The existing 2011 instruction regulates only the "review" of administrative cases, while the draft's title adds the word "processing." This means the document is meant to cover an earlier stage — the procedure by which an inspector draws up the case itself.

## Current procedure

The processing and review of such cases is currently regulated by an instruction [approved by a Ministry of Internal Affairs order and registered by the Ministry of Justice on July 5, 2011, under No. 2240](https://lex.uz/docs/-1832522). It was already amended [on October 13, 2022](https://lex.uz/docs/-6238403). The new draft put up for discussion on August 7 apparently is meant to replace this procedure. Which specific 2011 provisions are being repealed and exactly what changes in the procedure — the draft does not disclose this in the readable part of its portal card.

## What's next

Proposals from citizens and organizations are being accepted on the `regulation.adliya.uz` portal through August 17. Once the public discussion concludes, the draft must go through coordination and registration with the Ministry of Justice before the instruction takes effect. The Ministry of Internal Affairs has not yet named an exact effective date.
