Dolphin tayfuni 9 avgust kuni kechqurun Zhejiang viloyatidagi Yuhuan shahri yaqinida, barqaror shamol tezligi soatiga 151 km bo'lgan holda quruqlikka chiqdi — bu 2026-yilda Xitoyga urgan eng kuchli bo'ron.

## Yo'nalish: Okinavadan Zhejiangacha

Materik Xitoyga chiqishidan oldin tayfun dam olish kunlari Yaponiyaning janubiy Okinava prefekturasi orqali o'tdi, [The Guardian yozadi](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/aug/09/typhoon-dolphin-makes-landfall-in-china-as-its-strongest-storm-this-year). 9 avgust kuni tushdan keyin Xitoy milliy meteorologiya markazi Zhejiang va Shanxay uchun eng yuqori — «qizil» — xavf darajasini e'lon qildi, [South China Morning Post xabar beradi](https://www.scmp.com/news/china/article/3363428/red-alert-shanghai-cancels-60-flights-typhoon-dolphin-closes). Ikkala hudud ham siklonning «xavfli yarim doirasi»da — eng kuchli shamol va yomg'ir tushadigan zonada — qoldi. Tayfun Yuhuan yaqinida mahalliy vaqt bilan taxminan soat 17:30 da (Grinvich bo'yicha 09:30 da) barqaror shamol tezligi soatiga 151 km bo'lgan holda quruqlikka chiqdi. Bu AQSh shkalasi bo'yicha birinchi toifadagi uraganga teng, [The Guardian aniqlashtiradi](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/aug/09/typhoon-dolphin-makes-landfall-in-china-as-its-strongest-storm-this-year).

## 1 mln dan ortiq evakuatsiya qilindi, minglab reys bekor qilindi

E'lon qilingan ma'lumotlarga ko'ra, Sharqiy Xitoyning qirg'oqbo'yi hududlaridan 1 mlndan ortiq kishi evakuatsiya qilindi — bu raqamni [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/aug/10/typhoon-dolphin-china-shanghai-flooding-evacuations) 10 avgust ertalabki holatga ko'ra keltiradi. [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/9/flight-cancellations-evacuations-as-china-braces-for-typhoon-dolphin) hududlar bo'yicha taqsimotni beradi: Wenzhoudan 900 000dan ortiq aholi, Taizhoudan taxminan 390 000, Shanxaydan kamida 30 300 kishi, Fujian viloyatidan esa taxminan 98 900 kishi ko'chirildi.

Aviaqatnov bo'ronning quruqlikka chiqishidan oldinoq to'xtab qoldi: 9 avgust kuni kechqurun tayfun quruqlikka chiqqan paytga kelib Shanxay reyslarning taxminan 80 foizini bekor qildi [(SCMP)](https://www.scmp.com/news/china/article/3363428/red-alert-shanghai-cancels-60-flights-typhoon-dolphin-closes) — bu taxminan 1 400 reysga to'g'ri keldi [(Al Jazeera)](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/9/flight-cancellations-evacuations-as-china-braces-for-typhoon-dolphin). Dushanba, 10 avgust kuni Dolphin Shanxaydan shimolga siljigach, parvozlar bilan bog'liq vaziyat yaxshilana boshladi. Pudun va Xunqiao aeroportlarida jami 943 ta reys — jadvalning taxminan 40 foizi — bekor qilindi, [deb yozadi SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/news/china/politics/article/3363486/40-flights-grounded-shanghai-long-lived-typhoon-dolphin-moves-north).

## Tayfun fonida Gonkongda rekord issiqlik

Tayfun Gonkongda yomg'ir emas, balki g'ayrioddiy issiqlikni keltirib chiqardi: siklon chekkasidagi pastga yo'nalgan havo oqimlari ochiq osmonni ta'minladi. Gonkong observatoriyasi 9 avgust kuni kunduzi 36,9°S haroratni qayd etdi. Bu shaharda 1884-yildan, ya'ni kuzatuvlar boshlanganidan buyon, 142 yil ichidagi eng issiq kun, [deb xabar beradi SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/news/hong-kong/health-environment/article/3363429/hong-kong-breaks-record-hottest-day-typhoon-dolphin-brings-extreme-heat). Sheungshui hududidagi avtomatik meteostansiyada termometr ustuni 39,8°S gacha ko'tarildi — bu yerda uskuna o'rnatilgan 1980-yillardan buyongi maksimum. [The Guardian ham](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/aug/10/hong-kong-heat-record-hottest-day-ever-typhoon-dolphin) shahardagi mutlaq issiqlik rekordini tasdiqlaydi. Gonkong observatoriyasi issiq havo hafta o'rtasigacha saqlanib qolishini ogohlantirdi.

## Wenlingda bolakay yo'qolgan

[SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/news/china/article/3363450/chinese-boy-9-missing-after-being-swept-away-wave-typhoon-dolphin-approached-coast) ma'lumotlariga ko'ra, juma kuni, 7 avgustda, Wenling shahar okrugidagi (Zhejiang viloyati) qirg'oqbo'yi Shitang qishlog'ida to'lqin 9 yoshli bolani dengizga oqizib ketgan. U materikni qirg'oqbo'yi orol bilan bog'laydigan to'g'onda to'rt kishilik oila (ikki kattalar va ikki bola) bilan bo'lgan — bu allaqachon e'lon qilingan bo'ron ogohlantirishiga qaramay sodir bo'lgan. Yakshanba, 9 avgust kuni kechgacha bo'lgan ma'lumotlarga ko'ra, qidiruv ishlari davom etayotgan edi. Yuqori suv toshqinlari, tez oqimlar va girdoblar qutqaruv xizmatlarining ishini murakkablashtirgan — o'sha paytda qidiruv natijalari haqida xabar berilmagan.

## Keyin nima bo'ladi

[SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/news/china/politics/article/3363486/40-flights-grounded-shanghai-long-lived-typhoon-dolphin-moves-north) ma'lumotlariga ko'ra, dushanba, 10 avgust ertalabiga kelib Dolphin kuchsizlanib borayotgan, ammo «uzoq umr ko'rayotgan» bo'ron maqomini saqlab qolgan holda materik ichkarisiga, shimolga siljishda davom etmoqda. Zhejiang va qo'shni viloyatlarda toshqin va ko'chki xavfi hali ham dolzarbligicha qolmoqda, Shanxayda aviaqatnov esa bir zumda emas, balki asta-sekin tiklanmoqda.
