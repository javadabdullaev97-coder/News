On August 5, the Tashkent hokimiyat (city administration) published two separate posts: one about 654 kilometers of power grids repaired over the first half of the year, the other about the modernization of 291 out of 419 gas distribution stations in Yunusabad.

## Power Grids: 654 km of Capital Repairs in Six Months

In the first six months of 2026, power engineers [carried out capital repairs](https://t.me/poytaxt_uz/51266) on 654 kilometers of overhead power lines and 527 transformer substations in Tashkent. Another 787 kilometers of lines and 1,084 transformer substations underwent routine repairs.

According to [the hokimiyat](https://t.me/poytaxt_uz/51266), this work is part of preparations for the autumn-winter season. Worn wires and pylons fail more often under load in cold weather, and the upgraded lines and transformers are expected to reduce the number of emergency power outages in winter.

## Gas: "Smart" Gas Distribution Stations in Yunusabad

A separate line of work is replacing standard medium-pressure gas distribution stations (GDS) with "smart" ones. According to [the hokimiyat](https://t.me/poytaxt_uz/51267), 291 of the 419 GDS in Yunusabad district have already been modernized — about 69%.

The new GDS remotely regulate gas pressure and automatically detect malfunctions without a technician having to visit the site. According to the hokimiyat's assessment, this should stabilize network pressure during the cold season and even out gas supply in apartment buildings where residents previously complained of fluctuations. According to [the hokimiyat](https://t.me/poytaxt_uz/51267), a reduction in natural gas losses to 2% is expected once the system is rolled out across all of Tashkent, not just Yunusabad district.

## What This Means for Residents

For residents, this means two things. Upgraded power grids and transformers reduce the risk of emergency power outages during cold snaps and rising network load — a typical winter problem on worn-out sections. "Smart" GDS should eliminate the pressure fluctuations typical of old systems in apartments on upper floors. A dispatcher sees sensor readings remotely and doesn't have to wait for a manual inspection round — this should speed up the response to leaks and malfunctions.

## What's Next

The hokimiyat did not specify a deadline for completing the modernization of the remaining 128 GDS in Yunusabad district. Citywide rollout of "smart" GDS is mentioned in the August 5 post as a condition for reducing gas losses to 2%, but without specific timelines for other districts. The power grid report covers only the first half of the year — the hokimiyat has not yet published data for July and subsequent months of seasonal preparation.
