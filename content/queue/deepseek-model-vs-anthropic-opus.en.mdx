DeepSeek on August 21 unveiled DeepSeek-V4-Flash-Vision-Exp, an experimental AI model with image recognition, [Bloomberg reports](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-21/deepseek-unveils-test-model-to-rival-anthropic-s-opus-4-8). The company said that on agentic tasks with visual input, the new model approaches Opus 4.8, developed by Anthropic.

The new model is an experimental version of the text model DeepSeek-V4-Flash. It adds support for visual prompts: the model can analyze images and screenshots, not just text.

According to the [DeepSeek update log](https://api-docs.deepseek.com/updates/) from August 21, the new model shows the same results on text tasks as the base DeepSeek-V4-Flash. This applies to agentic work, reasoning and knowledge tasks. On benchmarks requiring image recognition, according to DeepSeek, the model showed a notable improvement over V4-Flash. Its scores on agentic tasks with visual input approached the level of Opus 4.8.

The company cited its own test results: 83.9 points on Terminal-Bench 2.1, 57.7 on NL2Repo, 59.3 on DeepSWE, 63.6 on DSBench-Hard and 64.3 on Chartography. There has been no independent verification of these figures by third-party organizations as of publication.

The model is available through the DeepSeek API under the name `deepseek-v4-flash-vision-exp`. The company designated it as experimental — meaning its capabilities and scores may change before the final release.
