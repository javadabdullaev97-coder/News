Google won an auction for Spirit Airlines' de-identified business data on August 14, offering $10 million for the package to train AI models, according to a notice from the U.S. Bankruptcy Court for the Southern District of New York.

The package includes about 100 million employee emails and roughly 500 million Microsoft Teams conversations, [Bloomberg Law writes](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/business-and-practice/google-aims-to-boost-ai-with-purchase-of-spirit-airlines-data). It also includes revenue data, fleet operations data, personnel records, and information on audits and fraud cases, [Bloomberg Law reports](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/business-and-practice/google-aims-to-boost-ai-with-purchase-of-spirit-airlines-data); marketing archives are mentioned by [Reuters](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/google-buy-spirit-airlines-business-212308890.html). Separately, the deal includes about 30 million lines of code and data on the company's development of software models and algorithms, [Bloomberg Law writes](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/business-and-practice/google-aims-to-boost-ai-with-purchase-of-spirit-airlines-data).

Passenger personal data is excluded from the package — profiles of 97.5 million customers and about 50.2 million records from the Free Spirit loyalty program, [Bloomberg Law reports](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/business-and-practice/google-aims-to-boost-ai-with-purchase-of-spirit-airlines-data). A third party will de-identify the remaining personal data before it is transferred, [Bloomberg Law writes](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/business-and-practice/google-aims-to-boost-ai-with-purchase-of-spirit-airlines-data). "We acquired a portion of Spirit Airlines' corporate data set — it may be useful for improving our products and AI models," [Google said in a statement](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/business-and-practice/google-aims-to-boost-ai-with-purchase-of-spirit-airlines-data).

Google outbid Mercor, a company specializing in data for AI training, in the auction: its counteroffer was $7.5 million, according to the same documents.

## What's Next

The deal has not closed yet. On August 19, Judge Sean Lane is set to consider a motion to approve the sale of data to Google at a hearing in the U.S. Bankruptcy Court for the Southern District of New York. The bankruptcy case of Spirit Aviation Holdings is being heard under case number 25-11897. Spirit Airlines stopped passenger flights in May 2026 and is selling off its remaining assets as part of the bankruptcy process.
