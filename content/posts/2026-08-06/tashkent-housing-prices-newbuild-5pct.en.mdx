[The National Statistics Committee of the Republic of Uzbekistan](https://stat.uz/img/news/press-reliz-rppi-2026-lotin_p64634.pdf) reported on July 15, 2026, that new-build apartment prices in Tashkent rose 5% year-over-year, and 7.1% nationwide, under a new hedonic regression method.

## New Calculation Methodology

Starting from the second quarter of 2026, the National Statistics Committee [calculates the housing price index](https://stat.uz/img/news/press-reliz-rppi-2026-lotin_p64634.pdf) using the hedonic regression method. The methodology accounts not only for an apartment's floor area, but also its location, floor level and other parameters — this makes it possible to separate changes in a property's price from changes in the structure of market supply. The base year for calculations is 2024.

The data source is real estate listings on the OLX.uz platform. Where listings are insufficient, the agency additionally uses data from the Telegram channels of real estate agencies and private realtors. The weights used to calculate the index are based on the number of transactions according to the Ministry of Justice of the Republic of Uzbekistan, multiplied by the average housing price in the region.

Under the new methodology, single-family houses have been included in the index calculation for the first time — previously the statistics were limited to apartments.

## Prices in Tashkent and Nationwide

In Tashkent, [prices for apartments on the primary market](https://stat.uz/img/news/press-reliz-rppi-2026-lotin_p64634.pdf) — that is, new builds — rose 5% year-over-year and 1% compared to the first quarter of 2026. On the capital's secondary market, prices rose 3.2% year-over-year and 1.5% quarter-over-quarter. Overall, apartments in Tashkent became 3.9% more expensive over the year.

Nationwide, [apartments on the primary market](https://api.siat.stat.uz/media/uploads/sdmx/sdmx_data_4726.pdf) rose 7.1% year-over-year, and 4.2% on the secondary market. Price growth in regions outside Tashkent was higher than in the capital: apartments overall became 7% more expensive over the year, new builds rose 8.7%, and secondary housing rose 5.1%.

Prices for single-family houses nationwide rose 2% year-over-year. In Tashkent, prices on the secondary market for private houses fell 0.7% quarter-over-quarter, while in other regions they rose 2.8%.

## Overall Dynamics Since the 2024 Base Year

According to the [National Statistics Committee's composite index](https://api.siat.stat.uz/media/uploads/sdmx/sdmx_data_4724.pdf), where 2024 equals 100%, residential property prices nationwide reached 106.8% by the second quarter of 2026. For apartments, the figure was 107.1%, and for single-family houses, 106.1%.

The agency notes that for comparability, the indices for periods starting from 2024 have been retrospectively recalculated using the new methodology. This means all figures cited already reflect the hedonic regression method, not the old calculation approach.
