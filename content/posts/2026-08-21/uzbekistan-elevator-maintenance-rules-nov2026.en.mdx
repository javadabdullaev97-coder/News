The Ministry of Construction and Housing and Communal Services of
Uzbekistan registered [Order No. 3824-1](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/-8406077)
on August 14, 2026, mandating monthly elevator maintenance in apartment
buildings — the document takes effect on November 16, 2026.

## What Changes

The order amends the [Rules for the Maintenance and Operation of
Apartment Buildings](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/8165716) — the base Act No.
3824 of April 29, 2026, that governs the work of management
organizations. New clauses 97¹–97³ require the management body to
"ensure accident-free and safe operation of elevators throughout their
entire service life," as stated in the
[order](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/-8406077).

Elevators must undergo maintenance monthly and a periodic technical
inspection once a year.

## Dispatch Communication and Emergency Systems

The document requires installing dispatch control with two-way
communication between the elevator cab and the operator, as well as
providing communication equipment in the machine room. The order also
requires keeping the elevator's rescue equipment and video surveillance
system in working condition. Light and sound signaling devices
indicating the status of shaft doors are specified separately, [the text
of the order states](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/-8406077).

## Who Is Affected

The new requirements apply to organizations that manage apartment
buildings — the order specifically requires them to ensure monthly
maintenance, an annual technical inspection, and the functioning of the
dispatch and emergency systems.

## When It Takes Effect

The order was adopted on July 10, 2026, under number 01/2-28, registered
with the Ministry of Justice on August 14, 2026, under number 3824-1,
and takes effect on November 16, 2026.
