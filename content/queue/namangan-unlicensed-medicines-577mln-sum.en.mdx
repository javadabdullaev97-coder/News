The Department for Combating Economic Crimes under the Prosecutor General's Office of Uzbekistan [said](https://t.me/Department_uz/12969) on August 8 that it had halted the circulation of medicines without quality documents worth 577.4 million soums in Namangan region.

## What Was Found and Where

According to the [agency](https://t.me/Department_uz/12969), officers of the Department's division for Yangi-Namangan district, together with other law enforcement agencies, carried out an operation at a residential building in the Boburshoh mahalla (neighborhood community). There, according to the agency, citizen A.T. and other individuals were storing 13,184 packages of 56 kinds of medicines with a total value of 577.4 million soums (≈$48,500 at the Central Bank exchange rate on August 8) for sale — without documents confirming their quality. The drugs were seized as physical evidence.

The Department did not name the specific types or manufacturers of the seized medicines.

## Criminal Case Under Article 186-3

A criminal case has been opened over the incident under Article 186-3 of the Criminal Code of Uzbekistan, which provides for liability for the circulation of substandard or unregistered medicines. According to investigators, investigative actions are underway; the Department has not yet reported any detentions or charges against the individuals involved.

The agency has not disclosed the names of those involved, other than the initials of citizen A.T.
