The Ministry of Preschool and School Education of the Republic of Uzbekistan and the my.gov.uz portal opened the second stage of first-grade admission at 10:00 a.m. on August 1 — accepting applications outside the microdistrict (the assigned neighborhood school zone) under an additional quota.

## How to Apply

Parents submit applications through the [my.gov.uz portal](https://t.me/MyGovUz/3961): they need to select the first-grade admission service, check the "admission outside the microdistrict" option, indicate the desired school district and specific school, fill in the child's details, and submit the application. The service is available at the address of permanent or temporary registration, [the portal said](https://t.me/MyGovUz/3960).

If temporary registration is not displayed in the system, the portal recommends updating the data through the id.egov.uz service and resubmitting the application, [the instructions state](https://t.me/MyGovUz/3961). Once the application is approved, the system assigns a date and time to appear at the school with documents.

The ministry has also published video instructions on how to apply — [in Uzbek](https://t.me/uzedu/44562) and [in Russian](https://t.me/uzedu/44563).

## How the Additional Quota Works

The second stage opens access to schools outside the microdistrict of permanent residence. Applications can be submitted "if the selected school has available places," [the official my.maktab.uz service channel clarified](https://t.me/mymaktab_uz/235).

For parents who need help applying, the ministry has set up support desks at schools — [they operate](https://t.me/uzedu/44561) Monday through Friday, from 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.

## Where Electronic Admission Is Available

Admission outside the microdistrict via my.gov.uz is available in all regions where the electronic first-grade admission system has already been introduced: [in Karakalpakstan](https://t.me/uzedu/44561), and the Andijan, Bukhara, Fergana, Jizzakh, Namangan, Navoi, Kashkadarya, Samarkand, Syrdarya, Surkhandarya and Khorezm regions, as well as in Tashkent and the Tashkent region.

## What's Next

The ministry and the portal have not yet stated an exact end date for admission outside the microdistrict in their announcements.
