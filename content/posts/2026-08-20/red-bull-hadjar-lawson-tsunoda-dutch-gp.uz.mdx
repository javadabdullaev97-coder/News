Red Bull 19-avgust kuni [tasdiqladi](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/red-bull-confirm-hadjar-to-miss-dutch-grand-prix-as-lawson-steps-up-and-tsunoda-returns.3D3k7oc9dUwwEBZC7upjR1): pilot Isack Hadjar bilagidan jarohat olgani sababli Formula-1ning Niderlandiya Gran-prisida qatnasha olmaydi — bu hafta oxirida uning RB22'dagi o'rnini Racing Bulls'dan qaytgan Liam Lawson egallaydi.

«Hadjar yozgi tanaffus davomida bilagidan jarohat oldi, bu esa unga 2026-yilgi Niderlandiya Gran-prisida ishtirok etish imkonini bermaydi. Biz Lawsonni qaytaryapmiz — u ushbu hafta oxirida RB22'da uning o'rnini egallaydi», — [deyilgan Red Bull Racing bayonotida](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/red-bull-confirm-hadjar-to-miss-dutch-grand-prix-as-lawson-steps-up-and-tsunoda-returns.3D3k7oc9dUwwEBZC7upjR1).

Lawson 2026-yilgi mavsumda Red Bull'ning zaxira jamoasi Racing Bulls tarkibida chiqqan. 2025-yil boshida u Red Bull Racing asosiy tarkibida ikkita bosqichda ishtirok etgan, shundan so'ng qaytadan Racing Bulls'ga tushirilgan, [deb xabar beradi Formula1.com](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/red-bull-confirm-hadjar-to-miss-dutch-grand-prix-as-lawson-steps-up-and-tsunoda-returns.3D3k7oc9dUwwEBZC7upjR1).

Lawson bo'shatgan Racing Bulls'dagi o'rinni Yuki Tsunoda egallaydi. U zaxira jamoadagi hamkasbi — mavsum yangi kelgan Arvid Lindblad bilan yana birga chiqadi, [deb xabar beradi Formula1.com](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/red-bull-confirm-hadjar-to-miss-dutch-grand-prix-as-lawson-steps-up-and-tsunoda-returns.3D3k7oc9dUwwEBZC7upjR1).

Bu bosqichda Lawson bilan Red Bull Racing'da jamoadosh bo'lib Max Verstappen qoladi — u uchun Niderlandiya Gran-prisi [uy poygasi](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/red-bull-confirm-hadjar-to-miss-dutch-grand-prix-as-lawson-steps-up-and-tsunoda-returns.3D3k7oc9dUwwEBZC7upjR1) hisoblanadi.

Zandvoort'dagi bosqich Formula-1 taqvimining ushbu davrida oxirgi marta o'tkaziladi, [deb xabar beradi GrandPrix.com](https://www.grandprix.com/news/formula-1-to-leave-zandvoort-after-2026-dutch-gp.html) trassa tashkilotchilariga tayanib. 2026-yilga bir martalik shartnoma uzaytirilishi 2024-yil dekabrida kelishilgan edi, yangi bitim esa imzolanmadi.

Trassa egasi Robert van Overdijk qarorni [moliyaviy yuk](https://www.grandprix.com/news/formula-1-to-leave-zandvoort-after-2026-dutch-gp.html) bilan izohlagan: tashkilotchilar chempionat bosqichini o'tkazish huquqi uchun ko'proq to'lashga tayyor bo'lmagan. Zandvoort'dagi hafta oxiri an'anaviy ravishda sprint formatida — shanba kuni qo'shimcha qisqa poyga bilan — o'tkaziladi.
