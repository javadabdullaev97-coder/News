The death toll from the earthquake near Indonesia's Flores island has risen to at least 38 as of the evening of August 15, Indonesia's National Disaster Management Agency (BNPB) [reported](https://asia.nikkei.com/economy/natural-disasters/at-least-38-dead-in-eastern-indonesia-earthquake-of-magnitude-7.7).

Earlier the same day, LEAP News [reported](https://leap.uz/en/2026/08/15/flores-earthquake-death-toll-20) that rescuers near the city of Maumere had found 20 dead — that figure reflected an early stage of the operation, when communications with Nagekeo district, the area closest to the epicenter, had not yet been restored.

## BNPB's new estimate

BNPB chief Suharyanto [said](https://asia.nikkei.com/economy/natural-disasters/at-least-38-dead-in-eastern-indonesia-earthquake-of-magnitude-7.7) at a briefing in Jakarta that the death toll had reached 38. According to [Kommersant](https://www.kommersant.ru/doc/8890499), about 23 people were injured — two in serious condition, another 11 with minor injuries. More than 2,000 residents have been evacuated from affected areas, Nikkei Asia [reported](https://asia.nikkei.com/economy/natural-disasters/at-least-38-dead-in-eastern-indonesia-earthquake-of-magnitude-7.7), citing BNPB leadership.

[According to](https://www.aljazeera.com/video/newsfeed/2026/8/15/aftermath-of-deadly-indonesia-earthquake) Al Jazeera, the worst damage hit East Nusa Tenggara province (Nusa Tenggara Timur), and part of the province remains difficult for rescuers to reach.

## Infrastructure damage

The earthquake damaged [about 50 homes](https://www.kommersant.ru/doc/8890499), as well as schools, hospitals and government buildings, Kommersant writes, citing BNPB. A waiting hall collapsed at the port in Maumere. Landslides blocked a section of the Trans-Flores highway — a roughly 700-kilometer road connecting the island's districts. The airport in Komodo was partially damaged but remains operational.

According to Nikkei Asia, a total of [five airports](https://asia.nikkei.com/economy/natural-disasters/at-least-38-dead-in-eastern-indonesia-earthquake-of-magnitude-7.7) were affected in the earthquake zone. Authorities [lifted](https://asia.nikkei.com/economy/natural-disasters/at-least-38-dead-in-eastern-indonesia-earthquake-of-magnitude-7.7) the tsunami warning about 2.5 hours after the quake — recorded waves did not exceed 1 meter.

## Aftershocks continue

The earthquake's epicenter was located about 700 kilometers east of Bali; the quake struck around 6 a.m. local time on Saturday, August 15. Since the main shock, seismologists have [recorded](https://asia.nikkei.com/economy/natural-disasters/at-least-38-dead-in-eastern-indonesia-earthquake-of-magnitude-7.7) 235 aftershocks, including four above magnitude 5.0. BNPB estimates aftershocks could continue for another two weeks or so.

Indonesian authorities do not rule out further revisions to the death toll — rescuers have yet to reach the areas farthest from the epicenter.
