The Ministry of Preschool and School Education of Uzbekistan [held](https://t.me/uzedu/44749) testing on August 8 to select teachers for 13 new general education schools across the republic — more than 6,000 candidates competed for the vacancies.

## Where the New Schools Are Opening

The testing covered candidates from the Republic of Karakalpakstan, the Navoi, Syrdarya, Jizzakh, Bukhara and Samarkand regions, and the city of Tashkent, [the ministry said in its post](https://t.me/uzedu/44749). The ministry did not specify how many schools are located in each region or how many teaching positions need to be filled.

## How the Selection Was Conducted

The tests were held as part of an open competition on the [pedagog.uzedu.uz](https://pedagog.uzedu.uz) electronic platform, which the ministry uses to recruit teaching staff. The August 8 post [states](https://t.me/uzedu/44749) that more than 6,000 candidates submitted applications. The ministry did not disclose the test format, the list of subjects, or the criteria for selecting winners.

## What Remains Unknown

The ministry has not announced the opening date for the 13 new schools or the results of the testing — how many candidates passed the selection and received appointments. The ministry may publish this data separately once the competitive procedures are complete.
