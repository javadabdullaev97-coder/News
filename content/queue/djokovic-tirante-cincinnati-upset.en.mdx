Novak Djokovic lost 2-6, 6-4, 6-4 to Argentina's Thiago Agustín Tirante in the second round of the ATP Masters 1000 tournament in Cincinnati on August 15.

At 72% humidity, the Serb comfortably won the first set. He then began struggling with the heat — the match required a medical timeout, [Ubitennis reports](https://www.ubitennis.net/2026/08/thiago-agustin-tirante-stuns-struggling-djokovic-in-cincinnati/). Tirante capitalized on his opponent's decline and closed out both remaining sets with late breaks.

After the match, 39-year-old Djokovic attributed the collapse to a longstanding health issue. "It's just a condition I have, it's been bothering me for the past couple of years. A lot of issues, especially when it's humid and hot," he [said](https://www.ubitennis.net/2026/08/novak-djokovic-casts-doubt-on-cincinnati-return-after-surprise-defeat/) at a press conference, without specifying the diagnosis.

Djokovic acknowledged he may have played his last match in Cincinnati. "I certainly hope [to return], but it seems unlikely. But we'll see what the future brings," he said, answering a question about his plans for the tournament.

For Tirante, whose defeat of the tennis legend brought him the biggest win of his career, the match marked a breakthrough of the season. "I think it's the best win of my career. I played well on court and handled the nerves of playing against a legend like Novak," the [Argentine said](https://www.ubitennis.net/2026/08/thiago-agustin-tirante-stuns-struggling-djokovic-in-cincinnati/) after the match. He will face Spain's Martín Landaluce in the third round.

The Cincinnati tournament is being played without two other top players. World No. 1 Jannik Sinner withdrew due to a knee injury, and World No. 2 Carlos Alcaraz withdrew due to a wrist injury, the [ATP Tour reported](https://www.atptour.com/en/news/sinner-cincinnati-2026-withdrawal) on August 10.
