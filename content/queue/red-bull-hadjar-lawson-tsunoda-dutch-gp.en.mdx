Red Bull [confirmed](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/red-bull-confirm-hadjar-to-miss-dutch-grand-prix-as-lawson-steps-up-and-tsunoda-returns.3D3k7oc9dUwwEBZC7upjR1) on August 19 that driver Isack Hadjar will miss the Formula 1 Dutch Grand Prix because of a wrist injury — his seat in the RB22 for this weekend will be taken by Liam Lawson, who returns from Racing Bulls.

"Hadjar picked up a wrist injury during the summer break that will rule him out of the 2026 Dutch Grand Prix. We're bringing Lawson back — he'll replace him in the RB22 this weekend," [Red Bull Racing said in a statement](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/red-bull-confirm-hadjar-to-miss-dutch-grand-prix-as-lawson-steps-up-and-tsunoda-returns.3D3k7oc9dUwwEBZC7upjR1).

Lawson raced for Racing Bulls, Red Bull's junior team, in the 2026 season. In early 2025 he already drove two rounds for the main Red Bull Racing lineup before being demoted back to Racing Bulls, [Formula1.com reports](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/red-bull-confirm-hadjar-to-miss-dutch-grand-prix-as-lawson-steps-up-and-tsunoda-returns.3D3k7oc9dUwwEBZC7upjR1).

The Racing Bulls seat vacated by Lawson will go to Yuki Tsunoda. He returns to partner junior-team rookie Arvid Lindblad, [Formula1.com reports](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/red-bull-confirm-hadjar-to-miss-dutch-grand-prix-as-lawson-steps-up-and-tsunoda-returns.3D3k7oc9dUwwEBZC7upjR1).

Max Verstappen remains Lawson's teammate at Red Bull Racing for this round — the Dutch Grand Prix is a [home race](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/red-bull-confirm-hadjar-to-miss-dutch-grand-prix-as-lawson-steps-up-and-tsunoda-returns.3D3k7oc9dUwwEBZC7upjR1) for him.

The Zandvoort round will take place for the last time in this cycle of the Formula 1 calendar, [GrandPrix.com reports](https://www.grandprix.com/news/formula-1-to-leave-zandvoort-after-2026-dutch-gp.html), citing the circuit's organizers. A one-off extension of the contract through 2026 was agreed back in December 2024, and a new deal was never signed.

Circuit owner Robert van Overdijk cited [financial pressure](https://www.grandprix.com/news/formula-1-to-leave-zandvoort-after-2026-dutch-gp.html) as the reason for the decision: organizers were not prepared to pay more for the right to host the championship round. The Zandvoort weekend traditionally runs in sprint format, with an additional short race on Saturday.
