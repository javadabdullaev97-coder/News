President Shavkat Mirziyoyev on August 18 announced at an [open dialogue with entrepreneurs](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9511) in Khiva an average halving of business fines and a three-year moratorium on small business inspections.

The event — the president's [sixth open dialogue](https://leap.uz/en/2026/08/18/president-entrepreneurs-day-khorazm-dialogue) with business — drew about 25,000 entrepreneurs from all regions of the country. Some joined from district studios, according to a [statement](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9511) on the president's website.

## Fines to Be Halved on Average

"The size of all types of fines will be reduced by half on average," Mirziyoyev [said](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9511). The president did not give a date for the measure to take effect.

A voluntary preventive audit is being introduced for medium and large businesses. If an entrepreneur undergoes it and fixes the violations found, no fine will apply, the president [said](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9511).

## Three-Year Moratorium on Small Business Inspections

Small businesses will be exempt from inspections for three years, except in cases "involving harm to people's health or the interests of other entrepreneurs," the [remarks](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9511) say. An exact start date for the moratorium has not been announced.

## Business Support Law May Offer a Discount for Prompt Payment of Fines

Separately from the announcements at the open dialogue, Mirziyoyev on August 17 signed [Law ZRU-1168](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/8409757) on guarantees of freedom of entrepreneurial activity and business support. The document takes effect on November 18, 2026.

According to reports cited by several outlets; no official confirmation had emerged as of publication — one of the law's provisions may reduce a fine for prompt payment after it is imposed. The exact wording of the article is unconfirmed: the law was published on Lex.uz as a scan without a text layer.

## When It Takes Effect and Who It Affects

Law ZRU-1168 takes effect on November 18, 2026. Starting in 2027, two new principles take effect — a presumption in favor of the entrepreneur and a warning for a first violation.

"When resolving disputes between entrepreneurs and the state, the principles of a 'presumption in favor of the entrepreneur' and a 'warning for a first violation' will be introduced," the president [said](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9511).

The remarks did not give dates for when the fine reduction or the inspection moratorium take effect. They will appear once these measures are formalized in separate legal acts.
