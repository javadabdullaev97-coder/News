The Traffic Organization Center of Tashkent (TOC) restricted all vehicle traffic from 11 p.m. on July 31 to 10 p.m. on August 3 on sections of Akkurgan and Parkent streets adjoining Mustaqillik Avenue.

## Where traffic is restricted

The restriction [applies](https://t.me/codd_tashkent/288) to two sections. The first is Akkurgan Street, from its intersection with Mustaqillik Avenue to Osiyo Street. The second is Parkent Street, from its intersection with Mustaqillik Avenue to Parkent-2 Street.

The reason is the completion of asphalt paving in the second phase of the Mustaqillik Avenue reconstruction. TOC said the restriction was needed to finish the work safely and to a high standard, and [recommended](https://t.me/codd_tashkent/288) that drivers plan their routes in advance.

TOC's announcement included a [detour map](https://t.me/codd_tashkent/288) for vehicles. It did not mention any changes to public transit routes.

## Second phase of the avenue's reconstruction

This is a continuation of work that has been underway on Mustaqillik Avenue since July 16. From 1 a.m. on July 16 to 10 p.m. on July 31, TOC [closed](https://t.me/codd_tashkent/262) the avenue section from its intersection with Parkent Street to the intersection with Mirzo Ulugbek Street. At the same time, starting at 2 a.m. on July 16, the center reopened an already-repaired section — from the intersection with Mirzo Ulugbek Street to Akkurgan Street.

TOC operates [under the Tashkent hokimiyat](https://tashkent.uz/ru/news/7306) and is responsible for traffic organization in the capital. In late July, the city's first "Green Wave" section [launched](https://tashkent.uz/ru/news/7362): center specialists synchronized traffic lights at 13 intersections on Buyuk Ipak Yuli Street.

## What's next

The restriction on Akkurgan and Parkent streets remains in effect until 10 p.m. on August 3. TOC has not yet announced a timeline for the next phase of work on Mustaqillik Avenue.
