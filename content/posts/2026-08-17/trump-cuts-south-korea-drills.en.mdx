US President Donald Trump ordered Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth to "substantially reduce" the joint Ulchi Freedom Shield military exercise with South Korea, which runs from August 17 to 27 [(Al Jazeera)](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/17/trump-scales-down-war-games-with-south-korea-why-it-matters).

Trump said it was too late to cancel the drills but ordered a sharp cut to their scale [(CBS News)](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/trump-scale-back-south-korea-military-exercises/). The exercise had originally involved about 18,000 South Korean troops and 28,500 US troops — the program, which began in 1976, was set to run for 11 days [(Al Jazeera)](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/17/trump-scales-down-war-games-with-south-korea-why-it-matters).

## Reasons: Iran and the relationship with Kim Jong Un

The US president attributed the decision to his "very good relationship" with North Korean leader Kim Jong Un. He said the exercise "sends a signal that's totally inappropriate and hostile" toward North Korea, which he said had behaved "unthreatening and respectful" under his watch [(CBS News)](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/trump-scale-back-south-korea-military-exercises/).

Trump also said he had earlier asked South Korea's president to join US efforts to denuclearize Iran and was turned down. "I recently asked the President of South Korea if she wanted to join us in denuclearizing the Islamic Republic of Iran, and they said, 'No thanks!'" [(Al Jazeera)](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/17/trump-scales-down-war-games-with-south-korea-why-it-matters). CBS News reported that Trump himself called that reason "somewhat unrelated" to the decision on the drills. He separately cited the cost of the exercise as another factor [(CBS News)](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/trump-scale-back-south-korea-military-exercises/).

## Reaction: Pyongyang had already threatened action

North Korea threatened unspecified "stern steps" and called the exercise "a rehearsal for an aggressive war" on Friday, before Trump's announcement [(CBS News)](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/trump-scale-back-south-korea-military-exercises/). Trump made a similar decision during his first term in 2018, when he spoke of halting the drills entirely, calling them "provocative" [(CBS News)](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/trump-scale-back-south-korea-military-exercises/).

The scaled-down Ulchi Freedom Shield exercise will continue through August 27.
