Murotjon Azimov, hokim of Kashkadarya region, allocated 3.78 billion soums (≈$317,000) to 856 students from low-income families to cover contract-based tuition — [the decision was announced](https://t.me/qashvilhokimligi/51945) on the regional hokimiyat's Telegram channel on August 14.

## Who will receive the aid

According to [the hokimiyat](https://t.me/qashvilhokimligi/51945), the money will go to children from low-income families who were admitted to universities but could not pay for their contract-based tuition on their own. The publication does not provide an exact list of the fields of study or universities where the recipients were enrolled.

The publication describes the decision as a personal order by hokim Azimov — the hokimiyat does not mention any separate resolution by the Cabinet of Ministers or presidential decree specifically regulating this payment.

## How much money and how it was distributed

The total amount is 3,780,600,000 soums, or an average of about 4.4 million soums (≈$370) per student. Converted at the Central Bank's exchange rate for August 14 (1 dollar = 11,937.89 soums), the total comes to roughly $317,000.

The hokimiyat links the decision to the upcoming 35th anniversary of Uzbekistan's independence, marked on September 1. The publication states that "ahead of the 35th anniversary of independence," similar initiatives in the region are continuing consistently.

## What the decision does not specify

The hokimiyat's publication does not give the number of the resolution or order. There is no list of universities or specific amounts per student. The mechanism for selecting the 856 recipients is also not described — it is not stated whether an application process was held or whether the list was compiled from social protection system data (the "Temir daftar" registry / local social protection agencies).

The message also contains no information indicating that applications from other needy students are still being accepted. Based on the wording of the post, this appears to be a decision that has already been made for a predetermined list of recipients.

This article is based on a single source — the regional hokimiyat's official Telegram channel; there was no independent confirmation from other agencies at the time of publication.
