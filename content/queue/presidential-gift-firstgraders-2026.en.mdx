[LEAP News reported](https://leap.uz/en/2026/08/05/president-gift-first-graders-delivery) on August 5 that the Ministry of Education had begun [delivering](https://gov.uz/ru/uzedu/news/view/202382) "Presidential Gifts" to first-graders — the complete makeup of the package, exact coverage figures and the delivery completion timeline through August 25 are now known.

## What's included

The "Presidential Gift" package [consists of 12 items](https://gov.uz/ru/uzedu/news/view/202382): a school backpack, 12 notebooks of 12 sheets each, a pen, two regular pencils, a set of 12 colored pencils, a set of colored paper, plasticine, a drawing album, two erasers, a set of 12 watercolor paints, glue and a pencil case.

On August 5, the ministry [organized a press tour](https://gov.uz/ru/uzedu/news/view/202382) covering the shipment of "Presidential Gifts" to the regions — the shipment itself was organized by "Supply and Logistics Service," an enterprise under the ministry. Journalists were shown preparations for the new school year using secondary school No. 202 in Tashkent's Chilanzar district as an example, where 175 first-graders are expected to be enrolled this year.

## Timeline and coverage

According to the plan, [more than 795,000 children](https://gov.uz/ru/uzedu/news/view/202382) will be enrolled in first grade in the 2026/2027 school year. As of August 5, about 680,000 future students had been enrolled — 85% of the planned figure.

All the kits [will arrive in the regions by August 15](https://gov.uz/ru/uzedu/news/view/202382), and by August 25 they will be fully distributed to schools — accounting for the actual number of enrolled first-graders. Gifts will go to students not only at state schools, but also at private educational institutions, as well as schools under the jurisdiction of other ministries and agencies.

The ministry [compiled the lists of first-graders](https://gov.uz/ru/uzedu/news/view/202382) via the electronic platform adm.maktab.uz, which also includes students from non-state schools.

## When the gifts will be presented

"Presidential Gifts" [will be presented to first-graders](https://gov.uz/ru/uzedu/news/view/202382) in a formal ceremony following events marking the First Bell Day.

In total, more than 6.7 million children [will study](https://gov.uz/ru/uzedu/news/view/202382) in 10,225 schools nationwide in the 2026/2027 school year, taught by more than 550,000 teachers.
