Uzbekistan's president signed the law "On Realtor Activity" (Law No. ZRU-1163) on August 7 — the document introduces mandatory certification for realtors and places regulation of the industry under the [State Assets Management Agency](https://t.me/huquqiyaxborot/20233).

## What Changes for Realtors

To work as a realtor, a person must now [complete a training program, pass a qualification exam and obtain a barcoded certificate](https://t.me/huquqiyaxborot/20233) — issued by a professional public association of realtors. A realtor can start working only after being entered into the unified registry.

The law defines four types of realtor services:

- intermediation in real estate transactions;
- organizing the sale of real estate properties and rights to them;
- trust management of real estate properties;
- providing market information, consulting and advertising services.

## Who Will Regulate the Industry

The law designates the [State Assets Management Agency](https://t.me/huquqiyaxborot/20233) as the authorized body for realtor activity. Under the previous version of the law, the body responsible for this area was not fixed directly in the text — it [was determined by the Cabinet of Ministers of the Republic of Uzbekistan through a separate decision](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/1714042).

## How the Industry Was Regulated Before

Until August 7, realtor activity in Uzbekistan was regulated by the [law of December 22, 2010 (28 articles, 6 chapters)](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/1714042). Under that law, only an individual entrepreneur holding a qualification certificate could work as a realtor. A realtor organization was required to have at least two certified employees, including its head, and a civil liability insurance policy. Certificates were issued, suspended and revoked by the authorized state body — the qualification exam was conducted jointly with professional associations, which had to have at least 50 members.

The previous law contained no requirements for a unified registry of realtors or a barcoded certificate — these are new elements introduced by Law No. ZRU-1163.
