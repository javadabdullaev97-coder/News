The General Prosecutor's Office announced on August 18 the launch of the "Illegal Construction" Telegram bot (@noqonuniy_qurilish_bot), through which Tashkent residents can report unauthorized construction to help preserve the city's architectural character.

This is [reported](https://t.me/toshkentprokuraturasi/132989) on the Tashkent City Prosecutor's Office Telegram channel. The agency urged residents to report cases of illegal construction through the bot "without delay."

The project is also aimed at ensuring compliance with urban planning norms and organizing public oversight of unauthorized construction, the post said.

On August 19, the Tashkent hokimiyat [also reported](https://t.me/poytaxt_uz/51542) on the bot via its Poytaxt.uz Telegram channel. According to the hokimiyat, the bot can be used to report illegal construction near apartment buildings. It also accepts complaints about unauthorized construction at sports facilities and playgrounds, in green areas and other public spaces.

"Remember, every plot of land is an asset belonging to each of us and to our people," the Tashkent City Prosecutor's Office said in its statement.
