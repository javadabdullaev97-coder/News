Run rate — last month's revenue multiplied by 12 — at Anthropic, the developer of the Claude AI assistant, surpassed $65 billion by the end of July 2026, [Bloomberg reported](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-17/anthropic-revenue-run-rate-surpasses-65-billion-ahead-of-ipo), citing people familiar with the matter.

That is more than seven times higher than at the end of 2025. At the time, Anthropic's run rate stood at about $9 billion, [the company itself reported](https://www.anthropic.com/news/google-broadcom-partnership-compute) in April. According to Bloomberg, the July figures were disclosed as part of a regular update for investors. Anthropic had not officially confirmed the figures as of publication.

## How Anthropic's Revenue Grew

According to the company's own data, run rate grew multiple times over throughout 2026. On February 12, when Anthropic [raised $30 billion](https://www.anthropic.com/news/anthropic-raises-30-billion-series-g-funding-380-billion-post-money-valuation) in a Series G round at a $380 billion valuation, run rate stood at $14 billion. On April 6, [announcing an expanded partnership](https://www.anthropic.com/news/google-broadcom-partnership-compute) with Google and Broadcom for TPU chip supplies, the company said run rate had surpassed $30 billion — more than three times higher than at the end of 2025.

On May 28, Anthropic [closed a Series H round](https://www.anthropic.com/news/series-h) at $65 billion, valuing the company at $965 billion. At the time, run rate stood at $47 billion. The size of that round — $65 billion — matches the run rate figure Bloomberg reported in August, but the two are different metrics. The May figure referred to the amount of capital raised, not revenue.

## Quarterly Revenue and an IPO This Fall

Separately from run rate, Bloomberg [estimated](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-14/anthropic-revenue-ahead-of-ipo-surges-over-14-fold-in-second-quarter) Anthropic's actual revenue for the second quarter of 2026. It exceeded $11.5 billion, up from $787 million in the same period of 2025 — a year-over-year increase of at least 14-fold.

On June 1, Anthropic [confidentially filed](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-01/anthropic-files-confidentially-for-ipo-as-claude-demand-surges) paperwork for an initial public offering (IPO) with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission. According to Bloomberg, Goldman Sachs, JPMorgan Chase and Morgan Stanley are underwriting the offering. The listing could take place as early as this fall, if market conditions allow.
