Solar panel owners in Uzbekistan received 330.822 billion soums in subsidies from the state budget in January-June — 5.2 times more than a year earlier, the State Tax Committee reported on July 31.

## H1 Payments Exceed All of Last Year

68,600 individuals [received](https://t.me/soliqnews/20297) the payments. At the [Central Bank of Uzbekistan](https://cbu.uz/ru/arkhiv-kursov-valyut/json/USD/2026-07-31/)'s exchange rate for July 31 — 12,006.39 soums per dollar — that is roughly $27.6 million.

In H1 2025, 28,500 people under the same program [received](https://t.me/soliqnews/15893) 63.498 billion soums (≈$5.3 million). The amount grew 5.2-fold, and the number of recipients 2.4-fold. Figures for past periods here and below are converted to dollars at the same rate — for July 31, 2026 — for comparability.

The H1 2026 result also exceeded the total for all of 2025. That year, 45,381 people [received](https://gov.uz/ru/minenergy/news/view/126838) subsidies worth 209.201 billion soums (≈$17.4 million), the Ministry of Energy of the Republic of Uzbekistan (Ministry of Energy) reported.

The subsidy rate is fixed at 1,000 soums per kilowatt-hour. Based on that rate, LEAP News calculates that households fed about 330.8 million kWh into the grid in January-June. The ratio is confirmed by an earlier State Tax Committee report: for April-May 2026, 60,193 people [received](https://t.me/soliqnews/19921) 156.125 billion soums (≈$13 million) for 156.1 million kWh.

For comparison, from the start of 2026 through July 2, Uzbekistan's solar and wind plants [generated](https://t.me/minenergy_uz/20355) 6 billion kWh — 3.8 billion kWh from solar and 2.2 billion kWh from wind, the Ministry of Energy reported. The periods don't match: the estimated volume of household generation covers January-June.

## Khorezm, Bukhara and Karakalpakstan Led in April-May Payments

According to State Tax Committee data for April-May 2026, the largest subsidies were [assessed](https://t.me/soliqnews/19921) in Khorezm region — 29.1 billion soums, Bukhara region — 27.1 billion soums, and the Republic of Karakalpakstan — 19.1 billion soums.

The same three regions led for all of 2025 as well: Khorezm — 55.4 billion soums, Karakalpakstan — 29.1 billion soums, Bukhara — 25.7 billion soums, [according to data](https://gov.uz/ru/minenergy/news/view/126838) from the Ministry of Energy.

## How the "Quyoshli xonadon" Program Works

The program has been in effect since April 1, 2023. Paragraph 15 of [Presidential Resolution PP-57](https://lex.uz/docs/-6385716) of February 16, 2023, sets the subsidy from the state budget at 1,000 soums per kilowatt-hour. It is paid for electricity that an individual generated with their own solar panels in excess of their own consumption and fed into the unified power grid. The current version of the resolution caps the total capacity of an installation at 50 kW.

The procedure for selling surplus electricity is set by [Cabinet of Ministers Resolution No. 247](https://lex.uz/docs/-6498473) of June 14, 2023. Under it, subsidy funds for "Quyoshli xonadon" participants are allocated by the 25th of the month following the billing month, while registration and payment go through the "Cashback and Benefits" service in the "Soliq" mobile app.
