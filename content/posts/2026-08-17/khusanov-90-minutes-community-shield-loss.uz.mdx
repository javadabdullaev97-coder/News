«Manchester City»ning o'zbekistonlik himoyachisi Abdukodir Xusanov 2026-yil 16-avgust kuni «Arsenal»ga qarshi Angliya super kubogi finalida barcha 90 daqiqa maydonda bo'ldi — uning jamoasi 0:3 hisobida yutqazdi.

Kardiffdagi Principality Stadium'da o'tkazilgan bu o'yinni 58 056 tomoshabin kuzatdi, deb xabar beradi [Sky Sports](https://www.skysports.com/football/arsenal-vs-manchester-city/report/556659).

Mezbonlar o'yinning 24-soniyasidayoq hisobni ochdi. Riccardo Calafiori Angliya super kubogi tarixidagi eng tezkor golni urdi — oldingi rekord 1968-yildan buyon saqlanib kelgan edi, deb yozadi [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/sports/2026/8/16/arsenal-cruise-to-3-0-win-over-manchester-city-in-community-shield).

28-daqiqada Kai Havertz hisobni ikkilantirdi. Bu epizodda Xusanov Christos Dzolisni nazoratsiz qoldirdi, u esa Havertzga bosh bilan zarba berish imkonini yaratdi, deb yozadi [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/sports/2026/8/16/arsenal-cruise-to-3-0-win-over-manchester-city-in-community-shield). Yakuniy hisobni 48-daqiqada Martin Ødegaard belgiladi.

«Arsenal»ning hujumdagi yetakchisi jamoaning yangi futbolchisi Christos Dzolis bo'ldi — u debyut o'yinida ikkala golga ham gol uzatma (assist) berdi. [Sky Sports](https://www.skysports.com/football/arsenal-vs-manchester-city/report/556659) ma'lumotlariga ko'ra, klub debyutida bunday natijaga ilgari faqat Willian 2020-yil sentyabrda erishgan edi.

Bu o'yin «Manchester City»ning yangi bosh murabbiyi Enzo Maresca uchun birinchi rasmiy o'yin bo'ldi. U mavsumlararo davrda bu lavozimda Pep Guardiolani almashtirgan edi, deb xabar beradi [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/sports/2026/8/16/arsenal-cruise-to-3-0-win-over-manchester-city-in-community-shield).

## Xusanov qanday o'ynadi

Xusanov o'yinni o'ng himoyachi mavqeida boshladi, deb ta'kidlaydi [Sports Illustrated](https://www.si.com/soccer/arsenal-vs-man-city-player-ratings-champions-lay-down-ominous-marker-win-community-shield). Nashr uning o'yinini 10 balldan 6,9 ball bilan baholadi. Himoyachi birinchi taymning ikkala golli epizodida ham — Calafiori va Havertz golida — pozitsiyasini yo'qotdi. «Manchester City»ning almashtirilgan futbolchilari orasida Xusanov yo'q edi, deb ta'kidlaydi [Sky Sports](https://www.skysports.com/football/arsenal-vs-manchester-city/report/556659) — himoyachi butun o'yinni maydonda o'tkazdi.

SI fikricha, murabbiy Mareska tezkor o'zbekistonlik futbolchiga uzoq muddatda o'ng himoyachi roli mos keladimi yoki yo'qmi, degan savolni hal qilishi kerak bo'ladi.

Xusanov «Manchester City»ga fransuz «Lans» klubidan 2025-yil yanvar oyida 40 million yevroga (≈41,5 million dollar) o'tgan edi, deb xabar bergan edi [Fox Sports](https://www.foxsports.com/articles/soccer/man-city-signs-20yearold-khusanov-for-415-million-to-kick-off-expected-january-spending-spree).

## Murabbiyning reaktsiyasi

O'yindan so'ng Mareska mag'lubiyatni tez o'tkazib yuborilgan gol bilan bog'ladi. «O'yinda ko'p narsa birinchi o'tkazib yuborilgan golga bog'liq. Undan keyingi reaksiya yaxshi bo'ldi», — dedi murabbiy, uning so'zlarini [Sky Sports](https://www.skysports.com/football/arsenal-vs-manchester-city/report/556659) keltiradi.
