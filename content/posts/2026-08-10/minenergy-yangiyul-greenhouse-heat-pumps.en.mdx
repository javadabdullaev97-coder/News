Uzbekistan's Minister of Energy Sherzod Khodjayev on August 9 inspected a 4-hectare greenhouse complex in Bunyodkor mahalla (neighborhood community) in Yangiyul district that is heated by heat pumps instead of gas and coal.

## What the minister was shown

The complex in Bunyodkor mahalla in Yangiyul district was [built](https://t.me/minenergy_uz/20703) by Yangiyo'l – Agro Star LLC using Chinese technology. Special heat pumps running solely on electricity heat the 4-hectare greenhouses — heating with gas and coal has been abandoned entirely.

The greenhouses are also fitted with a special heat-retaining cover: it stays closed in winter to trap heat and opens in summer. According to the Ministry of Energy, this helps maintain the required temperature year-round and allows three harvests a year.

## Solar panels and energy balance

The complex installed solar panels with a capacity of 100 kW. Expected output is about 252,000 kWh of electricity a year, of which about 24,000 kWh is planned to be sold as surplus energy.

The main construction work at the site has been completed: a 400 kVA transformer has been installed, and 7 wells have been drilled for water supply.

The company's website [says](https://yangiyol-agrostar.uz/yangiliklar/2) it specializes in growing and exporting tomatoes (including cherry tomatoes), bell peppers and herbs, and increased its export volume by 40% in the first quarter of 2025 — shipping to Russia and Kazakhstan. Neither the ministry nor the company disclosed the amount invested in the greenhouse complex.

## Context: the "mahallabay" system in Yangiyul

The minister's visit [took place](https://t.me/minenergy_uz/20703) as part of work on instructions given by the president at a video conference: Yangiyul district is piloting the "mahallabay" system of work, under which the district is improving electricity and gas supply and pushing for more economical use of energy resources.
