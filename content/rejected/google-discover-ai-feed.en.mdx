On August 20, Google announced the upcoming launch in the Google app of a new feature for text-based customization of the Discover feed — the recommendation feed of articles and videos on the app's home screen.

The feature will arrive "in the coming days," [Google said on its official blog](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/search/personalize-search-discover-news/). Users open the three-dot menu in the Discover feed and describe in words what kind of content they want to see more or less of.

Google gives example queries: "kitchen remodel ideas, only eco-friendly" and "weekend trip videos, but no tents" (LEAP News's translation). The feed [adjusts right after the query and remembers the preferences](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/search/personalize-search-discover-news/) for future recommendations.

The customization works like a dialogue: the system can ask clarifying questions to better understand the request, [9to5Google reports](https://9to5google.com/2026/08/20/google-discover-customize/). Google began testing the feature back in late 2025.

In the same post, Google [announced](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/search/personalize-search-discover-news/) a "preferred source" button for websites in Search and AI Overviews: more than 600,000 sites have already added it. Google also [announced](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/search/personalize-search-discover-news/) audio edition customization in the Google News app on Android.
