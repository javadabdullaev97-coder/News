Promoter Eddie Hearn on Thursday, August 20, sent Saudi company Sela a list of terms for the Fury-Joshua fight in New York. [According to BoxingScene](https://www.boxingscene.com/articles/eddie-hearn-to-list-demands-for-anthony-joshua-tyson-fury-to-go-ahead-in-new-york), the fight could take place at Madison Square Garden on November 20.

Earlier that same morning, Hearn had said the opposite: his company Matchroom would "absolutely walk away" from the fight if it were held outside the United Kingdom. He cited Joshua's contractual obligation to fight on home soil — [he said](https://www.sports.ru/boxing/1117340461-eddi-xirn-esli-poedinok-fyuri-i-dzhoshua-organizuyut-ne-v-velikobritan.html).

According to BoxingScene, broadcast partner Netflix and the Saudi side of the negotiations are pushing for the move to New York. Joshua had previously insisted on the contract clause requiring the fight to be held in Britain. The venue dispute has been escalating all week: LEAP News earlier reported that [Warren warned of a risk the fight could collapse](https://leap.uz/en/2026/08/17/fury-joshua-cancel-risk). It also reported that [the negotiations entered a critical week](https://leap.uz/en/2026/08/18/fury-joshua-talks-critical-week-nyc) amid discussion of New York. Later, [Hearn said he might sue Zuffa Boxing head Dana White](https://leap.uz/en/2026/08/20/fury-joshua-venue-dispute-escalation) over the venue dispute.

## Fury's Camp Fears the Fight Could Collapse

At the same time, Fury's manager Spencer Brown warned that Joshua could withdraw from the fight altogether. According to him, the public is "getting bored" of endless speculation about the venue, [BoxingScene reports](https://www.boxingscene.com/articles/people-are-getting-bored-of-anthony-joshua-tyson-fury-speculation-says-furys-manager).

As of publication, neither Matchroom nor Sela had officially confirmed the fight's venue or date.
