Swedish logistics company Einride announced on August 18 that it had ordered 500 Tesla Semi electric trucks — the largest single delivery of Tesla electric trucks to date, [the company said in a press release](https://www.einride.tech/press/einride-partners-with-tesla-for-the-world%E2%80%99s-largest-tesla-semi-deployment).

Deliveries will begin in September 2026 and run over 24 months, with the trucks rolled out in phases. The vehicles will enter service in California, Texas, New Jersey, Illinois and Georgia, hauling freight for Amazon and other Einride customers.

The order triples Einride's active electric truck fleet — Electrek estimates the company currently operates about 250 trucks, a number that will rise to about 750 after delivery. The deal is fully financed through third-party financing arrangements; the company did not disclose details or the lender.

Einride estimates the deal's potential at roughly $800 million in annual recurring revenue over the long term, if the business plans agreed with shippers are converted into active hauling. The company's fleet management platform, Saga AI, has already processed more than 19 million electric miles driven.

Einride CEO Ruzbeh Charli said the order confirms the company's ability to operate at the scale its customers require. He said the partnership with Tesla shows how quickly the technology is moving from concept to real-world operation.

Dan Priestley, Tesla's Semi program director, said electric trucks lower the cost per mile through fuel savings. He added that lower maintenance costs and greater uptime compared with diesel trucks are also important.

The previous record for the largest Tesla Semi order belonged to WattEV, which [ordered 370 trucks](https://electrek.co/2026/08/18/einride-500-tesla-semi-deployment-largest-order/) in May 2026 for port operations in California.
