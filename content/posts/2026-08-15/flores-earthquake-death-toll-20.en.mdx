The death toll from a magnitude 7.7 earthquake off the Indonesian island of Flores has risen to at least 20 as of the evening of August 15, [Al Jazeera reported](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/15/at-least-two-killed-as-magnitude-7-7-quake-hits-indonesia), citing local authorities.

LEAP News [reported earlier](https://leap.uz/en/2026/08/15/flores-indonesia-earthquake-tsunami) that the quake struck off the eastern coast of Flores early Saturday morning, August 15, and that authorities expected the death toll to rise.

## New Data on Deaths and Injuries

Rescuers around the port city of Maumere found 20 dead, six injured and two more people still trapped under rubble. City rescue agency chief Fathur Rahman [said](https://www.kommersant.ru/doc/8890422) so, as quoted by Reuters. The earlier estimate of five deaths — two in Sikka Regency and three in Manggarai Regency — reflected an early stage of the rescue operation.

Nagekeo, Sikka and West Manggarai regencies sustained the most damage, [Kommersant reported](https://www.kommersant.ru/doc/8890422), citing Reuters. About 2,000 residents were evacuated from Nagekeo Regency, the area closest to the epicenter, where communications remain down, complicating rescue efforts and the count of victims.

## Damage and Tsunami

The earthquake damaged homes, warehouses and administrative buildings in the affected regencies, [Kommersant reported](https://www.kommersant.ru/doc/8890422). [Kommersant also reported](https://www.kommersant.ru/doc/8890422) that tsunami waves under 1 meter high were recorded in several coastal areas after the earthquake. A tsunami warning issued by authorities immediately after the quake was later lifted, [Al Jazeera reported](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/15/at-least-two-killed-as-magnitude-7-7-quake-hits-indonesia).

Indonesian officials have not ruled out further revisions to the death toll — as of publication, communications with Nagekeo Regency, the area closest to the epicenter, had not been fully restored.
