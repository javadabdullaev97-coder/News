President Shavkat Mirziyoyev announced on August 18 at the sixth open dialogue with entrepreneurs that the advance payment for electricity and gas will be cut from 100% to 15%, [the Telegram channel](https://t.me/Press_Secretary_Uz/7716) of the presidential press secretary said.

The benefit applies to companies connected to the ASKUE and ASKUG systems — automated metering of electricity and gas consumption — that have no history of payment violations. The [Telegram channel of the Ministry of Energy cited the same figure](https://t.me/minenergy_uz/20779).

Full advance payment for energy resources was tying up businesses' working capital, Mirziyoyev noted. "Overall, we are radically changing the management system in the energy sector. A new system will appear in all districts that will operate based on entrepreneurs' current needs and future plans," the president said.

Preparations for the dialogue have been overseen since May by a republican headquarters that includes representatives of more than 50 ministries and agencies. Entrepreneurs submitted around 6,000 appeals, more than 4,200 of which have already been resolved positively, according to a [report](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9493) on president.uz dated August 13.

## Loan collateral requirements eased

For business loans up to 10 billion soums (≈$843,000 at the Central Bank's exchange rate on August 18), a "counter-guarantee" system will come into effect. Companies without full collateral coverage will be able to obtain loans.

The state will take on 30% of the collateral, the "Business Guarantee Company" another 30%, and banks 15%. Entrepreneurs themselves will need to cover only 25% of the collateral, [the Telegram channel of the presidential press secretary said](https://t.me/Press_Secretary_Uz/7714). Collateral property will be valued at its real price rather than an understated one.

## Advance payment for state asset purchases to be halved

The initial payment for purchasing state assets will drop from 30% to 15%. Entrepreneurs who pay the full amount within six months will receive a 25% discount.

If a business pays half the asset's value, the remainder can be paid off over seven years interest-free. The starting price of state property that remains unsold after three months will be gradually reduced — to as low as 10% of the initial value — and the auction period will be shortened by half.
