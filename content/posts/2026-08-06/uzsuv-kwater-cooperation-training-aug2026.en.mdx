Uzsuvtaminot JSC and South Korea's K-Water on August 5 [announced](https://t.me/uzsuv/45386) a new stage of cooperation on training personnel for Uzbekistan's water supply and sanitation system at the company's Scientific and Training Center.

## What changed

The partnership links K-Water (Republic of Korea) and the Scientific and Training Center, a private educational institution under Uzsuvtaminot JSC. Under the partnership, new curricula [have been developed](https://t.me/uzsuv/45386), based on international experience in managing drinking water supply and sanitation systems. They are planned to be introduced into the training process in stages.

Uzsuvtaminot's post does not specify the exact start date of the training, the number of trainees, or the program's financial parameters.

## Training areas

The new programs [cover](https://t.me/uzsuv/45386) nine areas:

- quality control and management of drinking water;
- detecting and reducing water losses in networks;
- network operation, technical condition assessment, and rehabilitation using modern technologies;
- efficient management of drinking water and wastewater treatment facilities;
- laboratory analysis and organization of laboratory operations;
- operation of pumps, valves, and hydromechanical equipment;
- automated control and real-time monitoring through SCADA systems;
- digitalization of water supply infrastructure based on GIS technologies;
- efficient use of electrical equipment and energy conservation.

According to Uzsuvtaminot's wording, the goal is to train specialists of international standard with modern knowledge and practical skills, as well as to digitalize the water supply system.

## Context

The company's own wording — that "cooperation is entering a new stage" — indicates that engagement with K-Water is not the first for Uzsuvtaminot. The Korean side has previously taken part in training Uzbek specialists in water-related fields. Uzsuvtaminot's post does not disclose details of earlier stages of cooperation, such as the dates of previous agreements or the number of employees who have completed internships.
