A magnitude 7.7 earthquake struck off the eastern coast of Indonesia's Flores island early Saturday, August 15, killing at least five people, with authorities expecting the death toll to rise.

## What is known about the earthquake

The quake struck at 5:58 a.m. local time (21:58 GMT Friday), with an epicenter located about 68 kilometers north-northwest of the city of Ende in East Nusa Tenggara province, at a depth of 10 kilometers, [the BBC reports](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c5y0zz63gero). The magnitude 7.7 reading was determined by Indonesia's Meteorology, Climatology and Geophysics Agency (BMKG).

Three aftershocks — measuring 5.9, 5.6 and 6.1 — struck within half an hour of the main quake, [the BBC reports](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c5y0zz63gero).

## Tsunami warning

BMKG issued a tsunami warning for the coasts of East and West Nusa Tenggara, South and Southeast Sulawesi provinces, urging residents to move away from the shore and rivers to higher ground. The warning was [later lifted](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/15/at-least-two-killed-as-magnitude-7-7-quake-hits-indonesia), Al Jazeera reports.

## Casualties and damage

According to the BBC, at least five people died — two in Sikka regency and three in Manggarai regency — after being crushed by structures that collapsed in the earthquake while they slept. The outlet notes the death toll could rise (figures as of the morning of August 15).

The earthquake damaged buildings, with the Maumere district hardest hit, while power outages hampered search-and-rescue operations, [the BBC writes](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c5y0zz63gero). Al Jazeera also [reports](https://www.aljazeera.com/video/newsfeed/2026/8/15/on-the-ground-in-indonesia-following-7-7-magnitude-earthquake) landslides and blocked roads in the disaster zone.
