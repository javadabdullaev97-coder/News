The National Statistics Committee of the Republic of Uzbekistan [reported](https://t.me/uzstataxborot/69171) that fixed capital investment in January-June 2026 rose 17.5% year-on-year compared with the first half of 2025, reaching 338.9 trillion soums.

## Investment Breakdown

At the Central Bank exchange rate for July 31, 2026, this is approximately $28.2 billion (about 12,006 soums per dollar). The bulk — 235.6 trillion soums (≈$19.6 billion at the Central Bank exchange rate for July 31, 2026), or about 70% of the total volume — came from [foreign investment and loans](https://t.me/uzstataxborot/69171). The remaining sources of financing, according to the National Statistics Committee, were:

- enterprises' own funds — 38.6 trillion soums;
- foreign loans guaranteed by the Republic of Uzbekistan — 23.4 trillion soums;
- funds of the population — 19 trillion soums;
- the republican budget — 16 trillion soums;
- commercial bank loans and other borrowed funds — 4.8 trillion soums;
- the Reconstruction and Development Fund — 1.4 trillion soums.

The sum of the seven items is 338.8 trillion soums; the 0.1 trillion soum discrepancy with the total figure is due to rounding in the original source.

## Dynamics

The National Statistics Committee provides comparison only with the first half of 2025. The agency does not include a sectoral breakdown of investment (by economic sector) or a regional breakdown by province in this report.
