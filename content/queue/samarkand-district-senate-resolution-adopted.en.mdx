The Senate of the Oliy Majlis of Uzbekistan adopted a resolution abolishing Samarkand District and expanding the borders of Samarkand city following a session on August 8, the upper house's official website reported.

## What the Senate decided

According to [Senate data](https://senat.uz/ru/plenary-sessions/post-5884), the Senate adopted the resolution after discussing the issue at a plenary session. Samarkand city will absorb 17,226.5 hectares of land previously belonging to Samarkand, Oqdaryo, Pastdargom and Tayloq districts of the region. The remaining territory of Samarkand District — 28,295 hectares — will be transferred to Tayloq District, and Samarkand District itself will be abolished as a separate administrative-territorial unit.

On August 9, LEAP News [reported](https://leap.uz/en/2026/08/09/samarkand-city-expands-district-abolished) that the Senate had considered the issue at a plenary session on August 8. According to the upper house's official website, the resolution was adopted at that same session.

## How the land will be distributed

Of the 17,226.5 hectares being added to the city, 6,263 hectares will retain their status as agricultural land — they will not be reclassified, according to the Senate's text. The remaining territory is designated for residential and non-residential development, small industrial zones and technology parks.

After the border expansion, Samarkand's population is expected to grow to 880,000 — bringing the city closer to millionaire-city status, according to the Senate's publication.

## Why the borders are changing

The Senate explains the decision by the need "to expand the territory of Samarkand city, increase its investment and tourist appeal, and diversify economic sectors," according to the text on the upper house's website. The publication also states that the issue was preliminarily discussed and approved at meetings of mahalla citizens' assemblies, as well as at meetings of the city, district and regional councils of Samarkand region, before being brought to the Senate's plenary session.

Changes to district and city borders in Uzbekistan are carried out under the [Law "On the Administrative-Territorial Structure of the Republic of Uzbekistan"](https://lex.uz/docs/4973063). A separate document — a law or resolution formalizing the Senate's decision and published on lex.uz — had not been found as of this article's publication.
