The Ministry of Transport of Uzbekistan announced that from September 1, 2026, passengers on Tashkent city buses operating under gross-cost contracts must pay their fare immediately upon entering the vehicle.

At the same time, the ministry will step up checks on fare evaders. The Transport Oversight Inspection and the State Institution for Monitoring and Control of Urban Passenger Transport will now check payment directly inside buses. That's according to [a post by the Ministry of Transport](https://t.me/Mintrans_uz/28827) on Telegram.

Passengers caught riding without a ticket face a fine of 0.1 of the base calculation value — currently [41,200 soums](https://t.me/Mintrans_uz/28827), the ministry said.

The new requirement applies to all Tashkent city buses operating under the gross-cost contract system. Under this scheme, the carrier is paid by the city for completed trips rather than directly by passengers.

The Ministry of Transport cited "current legislation" but did not name the specific law or resolution introducing the new requirement.
