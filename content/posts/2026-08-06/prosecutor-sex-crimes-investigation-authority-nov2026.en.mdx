Starting November 1, 2026, the investigation of crimes against sexual freedom in Uzbekistan will pass exclusively to the Prosecutor's Office — the Prosecutor General's Office [said](https://t.me/uzbprokuratura/87718), citing a presidential decree of March 3.

## Which cases move to the Prosecutor's Office

The provision is set out in [Presidential Decree No. UP-33 of March 3, 2026](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/8070196), "On Additional Organizational and Legal Measures to Strengthen the Protection of the Rights of Women and Children and Prevent Cases of Harassment and Violence Against Them." Under paragraph 14 of the decree, starting November 1 only prosecutorial authorities will conduct pre-investigation inquiries and preliminary investigations under the following articles of the Criminal Code:

- 118 — rape;
- 119 — forced satisfaction of sexual desire in an unnatural form;
- 121 — coercion of a person into sexual relations;
- 128 — sexual relations with a person under the age of sixteen;
- 128¹ — entering into sexual relations with a person aged sixteen to eighteen through the provision of material assets or a property or other benefit;
- 129 — lewd acts against a person under the age of sixteen.

The same paragraph of the decree establishes that materials on an administrative offense involving sexual harassment of a minor are referred to court only after a prosecutor rules that the act shows no signs of a crime.

## Part of a broader package of measures

The transfer of authority to the Prosecutor's Office is one provision of Decree No. UP-33, which [the president signed on March 3, 2026](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/8982) and which took effect the following day. The document also introduces other measures to protect women and children from violence:

- starting April 1, 2026, women with a protection order have, with their consent, the mobile app "my.ihma.uz" installed on their phone with an emergency call function;
- the Ministry of Internal Affairs has been instructed to draft a regulation requiring mandatory outpatient psychiatric examination when there are grounds to suspect a mental disorder;
- people convicted of crimes against minors under Articles 118 and 119 of the Criminal Code are excluded from the category of prisoners eligible for transfer to settlement colonies;
- starting September 2026, a training program for investigators and judges on working with women and child victims is being introduced.

## What comes next

Starting January 1, 2027, cases of harassment and violence against women and children should, as a rule, be investigated and heard by investigators and judges who have undergone special training — as provided for in paragraph 16 of the decree.
