Deputy Prosecutor General of Uzbekistan S. Samadov inspected the state of electricity supply in the Tuya-Bo'g'iz mahalla of Yangiyul district, Tashkent region, and found a debt of 582.1 million soums for electricity, the Prosecutor General's Office [said](https://t.me/uzbprokuratura/87841) on August 8.

## What was found in the mahalla

According to the inspection, 1,166 subscribers — 1,152 individuals and 14 legal entities — had accumulated the debt. At the [Central Bank exchange rate](https://cbu.uz/ru/arkhiv-kursov-valyut/json/USD/2026-08-08/) for August 7 (11,915.64 soums per dollar), the debt amounts to about $49,000.

Prosecutors also recorded:
- 23 electricity meters not connecting to the metering system;
- 101 subscribers with declared consumption of up to 50 kWh per month, for whom tasks were also assigned to responsible officials.

The district has been placed in the "yellow" electricity supply category — the source does not disclose the exact categorization criteria but links it to subscribers' accumulated debt.

Tashkent region prosecutor A. Mirzayev took part in the inspection on behalf of the region. Following the inspection, the prosecutor's office assigned tasks to prosecutorial staff and responsible organizations to eliminate the violations; the source does not give specific deadlines for their completion.

## On-site reception of residents

The Tashkent Region Prosecutor's Office has organized daily on-site receptions for mahalla residents at their place of residence — complaints are accepted there directly, without a visit to the district office. The format and duration of the receptions are not specified in the statement.

The Prosecutor General's Office did not name the agency or company responsible for debt collection and meter maintenance in the mahalla — the publication contains only a general reference to "responsible organizations."
