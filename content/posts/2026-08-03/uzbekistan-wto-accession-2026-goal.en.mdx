The World Trade Organization (WTO) Working Party on the Accession of Uzbekistan held its 13th meeting on July 27–28 — the parties [reaffirmed](https://www.wto.org/english/news_e/news26_e/acc_27jul26_470_e.htm) the plan to complete the country's accession to the organization within 2026.

## What was discussed at the 13th meeting

Uzbekistan's delegation was led by Azizbek Urunov, the president's representative for WTO affairs and chief negotiator. The delegation also included Deputy Minister of Economy and Finance Akhadbek Khaydarov, Deputy Minister of Agriculture Akmal Kosimov and Deputy Minister of Justice Makhmud Istamov, the WTO [said](https://www.wto.org/english/news_e/news26_e/acc_27jul26_470_e.htm).

According to Urunov, "Uzbekistan's accession process has reached a more advanced stage": member countries are submitting fewer written questions, meaning Tashkent is gradually closing out their concerns. He stressed that keeping up the pace matters given the goal of completing accession in 2026.

As of July 27, the WTO secretariat had [registered](https://www.wto.org/english/news_e/news26_e/acc_27jul26_470_e.htm) 31 bilateral protocols on market access for goods and services — the latest signed with India. Each such protocol sets out the terms on which a partner country's goods and services will enter the Uzbek market.

## What remains unresolved

WTO members still have questions on [currency regulation](https://www.wto.org/english/news_e/news26_e/acc_27jul26_470_e.htm), import procedures, trade facilitation, investment policy, competition policy and technical barriers to trade.

The Working Party's chair, Republic of Korea Ambassador Sung-yo Choi, hopes to hold the next meeting in late autumn — once all the necessary documentation has been circulated.

## What happened at the previous meeting

The Working Party's 12th meeting took place on March 9, 2026. The delegation was then led by Deputy Prime Minister Jamshid Khodjaev, who said Uzbekistan was ready to [successfully complete](https://www.wto.org/english/news_e/news26_e/acc_09mar26_316_e.htm) the accession process "this year." The working party report from that meeting contained 62 draft commitments by Uzbekistan, up from 56 in the previous version of the document.

By March, Uzbekistan had signed market-access protocols with 30 partners, including Ecuador, the European Union, Paraguay, Russia and Uruguay. Those five agreements were concluded between the Working Party's 11th and 12th meetings.

## What's next

Uzbekistan applied to join the WTO in 1994. Substantive negotiations on membership terms have been underway since 2020. The next, 14th meeting of the Working Party is expected in late autumn 2026 — an exact date has not yet been set.
