President Shavkat Mirziyoyev said on August 18 at an open dialogue with entrepreneurs in the Khorezm region that farmers in Uzbekistan received more than [400 billion soums](https://t.me/Press_Secretary_Uz/7711). This is the result of the zero VAT rate on agricultural products. The amount is equivalent to about $33.9 million at the exchange rate published on the official website of the [Central Bank of Uzbekistan](https://cbu.uz/ru/) as of August 20, 2026 — 11,794.88 soums per dollar.

«Due to the introduction of the „zero" rate on agricultural products, farmers were left with 400 billion soums,» — [the broadcast of the speech](https://t.me/Press_Secretary_Uz/7711) on the channel of the president's press secretary states.

At the same meeting, Mirziyoyev said cafes and restaurants received 100 billion soums (about $8.5 million) in VAT refunds under the cashback system.

The exact date the zero VAT rate on agricultural products was introduced, the list of products it covers, and the period over which the 400 billion soums accumulated were not specified in the speech. The dialogue in the Khorezm region was the sixth open-format meeting between the president and entrepreneurs.
