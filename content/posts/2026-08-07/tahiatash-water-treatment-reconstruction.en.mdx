Joint-stock company Uzsuvtaminot is rebuilding the Tahiatash water treatment station in the district of the same name in Karakalpakstan, with a design capacity of 20,000 cubic meters of water a day, the company said in a post on its Telegram channel that carries no publication date.

## Progress: 55% and two new reservoirs

Construction and installation work at the station is 55% complete, Uzsuvtaminot [said](https://t.me/uzsuv/45428) on its Telegram channel. The facility is located in the Aydinjol mahalla of Tahiatash district. Two clean-water reservoirs with a capacity of 2,000 cubic meters each are being built on site.

Uzsuvtaminot acting deputy board chairman Komilbek Yusupov reviewed the progress of construction; the date of the visit is not given in the publication. According to the company, he discussed with contractors the pace of the project, the quality of work completed and questions around bringing the station into operation. The company has not yet named an exact launch date for the facility.

## Water supply in Karakalpakstan: from 75.1% to 83.5%

Centralized water supply in Karakalpakstan currently covers 75.1% of the population, Uzsuvtaminot [said](https://t.me/uzsuv/45432) on its Telegram channel, citing the company's regional division in the republic.

The president [approved](https://lex.uz/docs/8066619), by Resolution PP-70 of February 21, 2026, a program for the socioeconomic development of Karakalpakstan for 2026 and subsequent years. The document sets a target of raising centralized water supply coverage in the republic's population centers to 83.5% by 2030. The same document provides for building 89.8 kilometers of drinking water supply networks and two water distribution facilities for residents of two citizens' assemblies of mahallas in Tahiatash district.

The resolution earmarks 2.2 trillion soums (approximately $184.6 million at the Central Bank of Uzbekistan's exchange rate on August 7) for water management programs in Karakalpakstan in 2026, to be drawn from the state budget, local budgets, and funds from international financial institutions and donors.

## What's next

Uzsuvtaminot has not disclosed a timeline for completing the reconstruction of the Tahiatash station or a planned date for bringing it into operation. The company continues to publish progress reports on water supply facilities in Karakalpakstan on its Telegram channel.
