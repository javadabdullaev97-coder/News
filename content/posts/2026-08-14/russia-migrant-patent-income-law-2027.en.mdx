Russian President Vladimir Putin signed [Law No. 241-FZ](http://publication.pravo.gov.ru/document/0001202607260001?index=1) on July 26, 2026. Starting January 1, 2027, it allows Russia's Ministry of Internal Affairs (MVD) to deny a work patent or revoke one already issued if a migrant's income is insufficient.

The grounds are the same in both cases: the MVD has no data on the foreigner's income, or the income turns out to be below the established level. The law amends the Federal Law "On the Legal Status of Foreign Citizens in the Russian Federation" and directly affects labor migrants from Uzbekistan who obtain work patents in Russia.

## What changes from January 1, 2027

A migrant working under a patent is required to provide for themselves and any dependent family members present with them in Russia with income no lower than an established minimum. The minimum is calculated using a formula: the regional per capita subsistence minimum multiplied by a regional coefficient and by the number of dependents in Russia.

The sources contain no official clarification on whether the new check applies to a patent's initial issuance. It is known that the ministry will base its decision on the patent's fate on income data for periods starting in 2027 — this primarily concerns patent renewal and documents already in effect.

## How the MVD will obtain income data

Russia's Federal Tax Service will transmit data on payments accrued to foreigners to the MVD, but as of August 14, 2026, the procedure and timing of such transfers have not been officially published. The data will be based on employers' insurance contribution reporting, as well as income statements, copies of tax declarations and documents confirming personal income tax payment, if the migrant submits them personally.

## What happens to patents already issued

A lack of income data or income below the established minimum becomes direct grounds for denying or refusing to renew a patent or work permit, as well as for revoking an already issued patent, temporary residence permit (RVP) or residence permit (VNZh). The rule also applies to citizens of Central Asia, including Uzbekistan.

If a patent is not renewed or is revoked, the foreigner must leave Russia along with any minor children within 15 days, unless other legal grounds for staying in the country exist.

## Additional personal income tax payment

The same day, Putin signed another law — on the advance personal income tax payment. From January 1, 2027, it expands the circle of foreigners required to pay a fixed advance personal income tax payment. The base rate for those working under a patent, at organizations or for individual entrepreneurs [remains](https://ria.ru/20260726/putin-2107081224.html) at 1,200 rubles per month (accounting for the regional coefficient). For those working for individuals without a patent or work permit, a rate of 1,700 rubles per month is introduced. The payment increases by 50% for each dependent on Russian territory.

## What a labor migrant needs to know and do

- **Income must be official.** Income statements, tax declarations and confirmation of personal income tax payment are taken into account — "gray" cash earnings will not pass this check, because the Federal Tax Service only sees what an employer has reported or what the migrant has declared personally.
- **The first patent may not be affected by the check** — the MVD will not yet have an income history for someone who has not previously held a patent, but there is no official clarification on this point.
- **The number of dependents in Russia matters.** The more family members a migrant supports in the country, the higher the required income threshold — and conversely, the threshold for a single person is lower than for a family.
- **The procedure and timing for transferring income data from the Federal Tax Service to the MVD have not yet been officially published** (as of August 14, 2026), so discrepancies in employer reporting are worth tracking in advance rather than waiting for patent renewal.
- **The deadline to leave after a patent is revoked is short** — 15 days for the migrant and any minor children.

## What's next

The law's core provisions take effect January 1, 2027; some provisions take effect from October 1, 2026 and later, and certain ones 90 days after official publication. The exact regional coefficients and the procedure for submitting income-confirming documents are to be established by subordinate regulations that, as of August 14, 2026, have not been published.
