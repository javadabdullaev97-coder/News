President Shavkat Mirziyoyev reviewed Uzbekistan's livestock and poultry farming development plan for 2026-2028 on August 5, which envisions [$895 million in investment](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9486) and an additional 290,000 tons of meat production.

## Where the Money Will Go

According to [the presidential portal](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9486), $463 million is planned for livestock farming development — expected to yield an additional 190,000 tons of meat by 2028. The growth is projected to come from restoring idle livestock enterprises, supporting specialized districts, building cattle sheds on household farms, and implementing new investment projects.

Another $432 million is earmarked for poultry farming. These investments are expected to yield an additional 100,000 tons of poultry meat, expand compound feed production capacity, and introduce modern slaughter systems.

The meat market shortage that prompted the plan was assessed [in the presentation](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9486) as follows: the country's annual demand stands at 2.15 million tons, current production at 1.6 million tons, leaving a gap of about 550,000 tons.

## Livestock Imports

To build up its herd, Uzbekistan [plans to import](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9486):

- 150,000 sheep from Mongolia;
- 100,000 head of pedigree cattle from China and European countries;
- 2,000 head of cattle from Belarus.

## Livestock Breeders Association and Digital Platform

The presentation [proposed creating](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9486) a livestock breeders association — a body meant to protect the interests of industry participants, develop cooperation, conduct breeding work, and promote scientific research.

The president was also presented with the Smart Fermer digital platform. Through it, farmers will be able to call veterinarians, register livestock, apply for subsidies, and receive AI-based recommendations.

## Context: The Agency Already Exists

In May 2026, Mirziyoyev [signed Resolution No. PP-179](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/8214086) establishing the Agency for Livestock and Pasture Development under the Ministry of Agriculture. The document transfers to the agency the projects, rights, obligations, and property of the former Committee for Veterinary Medicine and Livestock Development. The same document established a Livestock Sector Support Fund without legal-entity status under the agency. The agency is responsible for implementing investment, credit, and grant projects of international financial institutions in the sector. The investment and livestock import plan announced on August 5 relies on this structure.
