Germany's Interior Minister Alexander Dobrindt said on August 9 that the country has become a daily target of hybrid war — following a series of drone incidents at military and civilian sites.

## Explosive-laden drone at Leipzig/Halle airport

On the night of August 5, an employee of Leipzig/Halle airport in Schkeuditz spotted a flying object near the runway. In a zone with heightened security, next to a Ukrainian An-124 cargo plane, [a drone was found](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/5/germany-investigates-explosive-device-attached-to-drone-at-leipzig-airport) fitted with an explosive device — [as LEAP News reported at the time](https://leap.uz/en/2026/08/06/leipzig-airport-drone-explosive-ukraine). The device did not detonate: according to investigators, its detonator had become disconnected, [Euronews reports](https://www.euronews.com/my-europe/2026/08/06/explosive-drone-why-leipzighalle-airport-keeps-becoming-a-target-for-sabotage).

Leipzig/Halle airport is one of Europe's largest cargo hubs, handling more than 2,500 tonnes of freight a day. It is home to NATO's SALIS strategic airlift program, under which defense ministries of nine NATO countries operate An-124 aircraft, [Euronews notes](https://www.euronews.com/my-europe/2026/08/06/explosive-drone-why-leipzighalle-airport-keeps-becoming-a-target-for-sabotage). In 2023, the airport recorded 21 cases of unauthorized drones appearing on its grounds. In July 2024, four packages containing incendiary devices were sent through the courier services DHL and DPD. One of them ignited at a sorting center before the flight had even departed.

Since the first incident, the situation has escalated: over the past week drones were also spotted over a Bundeswehr military site and over Bulgarian territory.

## US intelligence links the drone to Russia, German authorities remain cautious

On August 8, US intelligence [assessed](https://www.dw.com/ru/разведка-сша-связала-взрывной-дрон-в-аэропорту-германии-с-россией/a-78290446) the Leipzig drone as likely linked to Russia. CBS News [reported](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/drones-germany-bundeswehr-military-base/) the same, citing sources in the US intelligence community: American officials assess the Leipzig drone as having "Russian origin." The drones spotted over Meckenheim two days later coincided in timing and, according to CBS News, heightened concern among Western intelligence agencies. The source did not offer a separate assessment of the origin of those drones.

German authorities have not officially named the drone's origin. "The German government has not publicly accused any foreign power of this week's drone overflights," [Al Jazeera writes](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/8/drones-spotted-in-bulgaria-and-germany-raise-concerns-of-russian-escalation). At the same time, according to the outlet, individual German politicians have publicly pointed to Russia, which has been waging war against Ukraine since 2022.

Russia's embassy in Berlin [called](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/8/drones-spotted-in-bulgaria-and-germany-raise-concerns-of-russian-escalation) the Leipzig incident a "fabricated provocation" and another example of accusations against Moscow made without evidence. NATO and the European Union have previously held Russia responsible for drones appearing over alliance sites. NATO's Secretary-General has previously said that "Moscow's reckless behavior poses a danger to all of us," Al Jazeera notes.

## Drones over a Bundeswehr military base with Patriot systems

On Thursday, August 7, around 22:00, the security system at a Bundeswehr military site in Meckenheim (North Rhine-Westphalia) detected two drones, [CBS News reports](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/drones-germany-bundeswehr-military-base/). Germany's Joint Forces Command confirmed the drones' appearance, military police were deployed to the site, and Euskirchen district police opened an investigation.

The Meckenheim site houses an underground storage facility for equipment and spare parts and is staffed by specialists who service American Patriot air defense missile systems, [Al Jazeera notes](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/8/drones-spotted-in-bulgaria-and-germany-raise-concerns-of-russian-escalation). Who operated the drones has not been established.

On August 8, a similar incident occurred in Bulgaria. A drone carrying a significant amount of explosives crashed into a sunflower field about a kilometer from a compressor station on the Trans-Balkan gas pipeline, near the border with Romania, [Al Jazeera writes](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/8/drones-spotted-in-bulgaria-and-germany-raise-concerns-of-russian-escalation). According to Bulgaria's Ministry of Defense, it was likely a Maya-type decoy drone — a model used by the Ukrainian army. The Ukrainian side said its armed forces had not deliberately directed any weapons toward Bulgaria.

## "We are not at war, but every day we are a target of hybrid war"

On August 9, Dobrindt [told](https://www.dw.com/ru/глава-мвд-фрг-германия-ежедневно-становится-мишенью-гибридной-войны/a-78291649) the newspaper Bild am Sonntag that Germany "is not at war" but "becomes a target of hybrid war every day." Foreign powers, he said, use espionage, sabotage, cyberattacks and covert operations to destabilize Germany or inflict direct damage on it — and this is a "constant reality."

The minister did not name a specific country as the source of the threat.
