Uzbekistan's team competed August 2–8 in its debut appearance at the International Olympiad in Artificial Intelligence (IOAI 2026) in Astana, winning one silver and two bronze medals, the Ministry of Preschool and School Education [said](https://t.me/uzedu/44740).

## Who won medals

Bilolbek Yuldashkhodjayev, a student at the Presidential School in Namangan, won the silver medal. Muhammadziyo Saytjonov, also a student at the Presidential School in Namangan, and Umrzok Ochilov, a student at the Presidential School in Samarkand, won bronze medals, [the ministry said](https://t.me/uzedu/44740) in a statement. All three competed for Uzbekistan's national team — its first participation in IOAI.

## Where and how many participants

IOAI 2026 is the third edition of the olympiad, [organized](https://ioai-official.org/republic-of-kazakhstan/) by the Competitive Programming Federation of Kazakhstan (CPFED). The competition ran August 2–8 in Astana. According to the uzedu Telegram channel, about 500 schoolchildren from 103 countries took part. The [ioai2026.kz](https://ioai2026.kz/) website does not publish final statistics on the number of countries and participants.

## Not to be confused with IOI in Tashkent

IOAI (International Olympiad in Artificial Intelligence) is not the same as IOI (International Olympiad in Informatics). The two are separate international competitions for schoolchildren covering different subject areas — IOAI took place in Astana a week before Uzbekistan [hosts IOI](https://leap.uz/en/2026/08/06/uzbekistan-ioi-2026-host) for the first time in Tashkent, August 9–16.
