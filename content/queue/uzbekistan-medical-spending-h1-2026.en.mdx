The [National Statistics Committee of the Republic of Uzbekistan](https://t.me/uzstataxborot/69177) reported that the volume of healthcare services in Uzbekistan totaled 11.3 trillion soums in January-June 2026 — up 16.8% from the first half of 2025. Converted at the [exchange rate of the Central Bank of the Republic of Uzbekistan](https://cbu.uz/ru/arkhiv-kursov-valyut/) (CBU) for August 6, that is about $950.6 million.

## Structure of Healthcare Services

Of the total volume of healthcare services, 53.7% came from other medical services, 31.8% from inpatient treatment facility services, and 14.5% from medical practice and dentistry, [the National Statistics Committee's publication said](https://t.me/uzstataxborot/69177). The agency does not publish a separate breakdown by paid and free services within this indicator.

A year earlier, in January-June 2025, the volume of comparable services was about 9.7 trillion soums — an estimate derived from the 16.8% growth rate cited by the [National Statistics Committee](https://stat.uz/ru/press-tsentr/novosti-goskomstata/69415-sotsial-no-ekonomicheskoe-polozhenie-respubliki-uzbekistan-za-yanvar-iyun-2026-goda).

## Tashkent Accounts for Nearly Half the Total

By region, the [National Statistics Committee](https://t.me/uzstataxborot/69177) broke down the volume of healthcare services as follows:

- Tashkent — 5.2 trillion soums (about 46% of the national total);
- Tashkent region — 1.1 trillion soums;
- Samarkand region — 830.2 billion soums;
- Fergana region — 675.4 billion soums;
- Namangan region — 550.4 billion soums;
- Andijan region — 507.2 billion soums;
- Bukhara region — 392.3 billion soums;
- Khorezm region — 390.9 billion soums;
- Kashkadarya region — 360.4 billion soums;
- Republic of Karakalpakstan — 335.5 billion soums;
- Surkhandarya region — 262 billion soums;
- Jizzakh region — 251.5 billion soums;
- Syrdarya region — 203.2 billion soums;
- Navoi region — 193.1 billion soums.

The lowest volume of healthcare services in soum terms was recorded in Navoi, Syrdarya and Jizzakh regions — in each, the indicator did not exceed 260 billion soums for the half-year.
