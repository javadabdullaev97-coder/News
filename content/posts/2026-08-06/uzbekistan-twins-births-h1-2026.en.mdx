The National Statistics Committee of the Republic of Uzbekistan [said](https://t.me/uzstataxborot/69170) on August 5 that more than 8,000 children were born in multiple births — twins, triplets or more — in Uzbekistan in January–June 2026.

## How births broke down by number of children

Of the 373,389 live births registered in the country during the first half of the year, [365,161](https://t.me/uzstataxborot/69170) were single births. Another 7,996 children were born as twins — about 3,998 births with two children at once. In 232 cases, three or more children were born.

The three figures — single births, twins, and triplets or more — together add up to the national total of [373,389](https://t.me/uzstataxborot/69161), which the National Statistics Committee published on August 3 in a separate report broken down by region.

## Comparison with the first quarter

The agency had reported similar statistics before. According to [data](https://stat.uz/uz/matbuot-markazi/qo-mita-yangiliklar/68209-3-ojda-zbekistonda-4-mingdan-orti-egizak-cha-alo-tu-ildi-2) from the National Statistics Committee dated May 8, 191,086 live births were registered in the country in January–March 2026, of which 186,723 were single births. Another 4,231 children were born as twins (2,115 births), and 132 as part of triplets or more.

The share of births with two or more children at once out of the total number of live births stood at about 2.2% in the first half of the year. In the first quarter, the figure was close — about 2.3%.

## What's next

The National Statistics Committee's full demographic release for the first half of 2026 — with data on mortality, marriages and migration — had not been published as of press time.
