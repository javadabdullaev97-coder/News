The Tashkent hokimiyat has begun work to turn the area near the Islam Karimov memorial complex into an open public park spanning 37 hectares — the city's press service [said](https://t.me/poytaxt_uz/51269) on August 5 in its Telegram channel Poytaxt.uz.

## What's Planned

According to the hokimiyat, the [redevelopment of the area](https://t.me/poytaxt_uz/51269) follows three directions: openness and convenience for visitors, ecological development of green zones, and modern infrastructure. The hokimiyat has not yet named specific completion dates or the project's budget.

The Scientific and Educational Memorial Complex of the First President of the Republic of Uzbekistan Islam Karimov has operated since 2017 — it was [established](https://islomkarimov.uz/ru/page/kompleks-haqida) by a presidential resolution of April 25 that year. The complex includes a museum, a research and education center, a library and a conference hall.

## Mirziyoyeva's July Inspection

The decision to develop the park zone followed an inspection of the city's parks that the head of the Presidential Administration, Saida Mirziyoyeva, conducted in July. [Mirziyoyeva's Telegram channel](https://t.me/SShMirziyoyeva/4129) says Tashkent critically lacks green zones and that creating new parks has become one of the city's priorities. The post lists a number of city sites slated for redevelopment.

In the Poytaxt.uz post about the start of the work, the hokimiyat directly [names](https://t.me/poytaxt_uz/51269) the July inspection as the reason for the decision.

## What's Next

The Tashkent hokimiyat has not published a work schedule and has not specified when the area near the memorial complex will become accessible to visitors. LEAP News will monitor the hokimiyat's official publications and will provide details once the city names specific dates.
