Uzbekistan's Cabinet of Ministers adopted [Resolution No. 435](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/8383792) on additional state support measures for livestock and fish farming on August 6 — the document introduces preferential loans at 10% annual interest for building complexes and purchasing pedigree livestock.

## How Much Can Be Borrowed and at What Rate

The resolution sets two limits. For building livestock and fish farming complexes and purchasing livestock — up to 5 billion soums (≈$420,600 at the Central Bank's exchange rate on August 6). For building facilities that produce semen from pedigree bulls and for fish farming — up to 20 billion soums (≈$1.68 million).

The borrower rate is 10% annually, of which 4 percentage points is the bank's margin. The loan term is up to 10 years, including a four-year grace period. After the grace period, the debt is repaid in equal monthly installments. If the borrower misses a payment, the outstanding portion of the loan is converted into a standard commercial loan funded from the bank's own resources.

## Where the Money Comes From

The resources come from two sources. The Fund for State Support of Agriculture annually places 1 trillion soums (≈$84.1 million) in commercial banks for 10 years at 6% annually. The state budget covers the difference between this rate and the market rate. The Reconstruction and Development Fund allocates the equivalent of $50 million in national currency to Xalq Bank and Mikrokreditbank. The terms are the same: 10 years, including a four-year grace period, at 6% annually.

## When It Takes Effect

The resolution took effect on August 7, the day after it was signed.

## Who It Applies To

The loans are available to business entities that build livestock and fish farming complexes, purchase pedigree livestock, or build facilities producing semen from pedigree bulls and fish stocking material. The document contains no requirements for a minimum share of domestic production or other sector-specific restrictions.
