The Central Bank of Uzbekistan on August 18 set the official U.S. dollar exchange rate for August 19 at [11,820.40 soums](https://cbu.uz/ru/arkhiv-kursov-valyut/json/USD/2026-08-19/) per dollar — the rate has fallen for a fourth consecutive business day.

The decline began on [August 14](https://cbu.uz/ru/arkhiv-kursov-valyut/json/USD/2026-08-14/), when the rate dropped to 11,937.89 soums — 10.69 soums below the August 13 level (11,948.58 soums). The decline continued: the rate stood at 11,891.18 soums on [August 17](https://cbu.uz/ru/arkhiv-kursov-valyut/json/USD/2026-08-17/) and 11,857.35 soums on [August 18](https://cbu.uz/ru/arkhiv-kursov-valyut/json/USD/2026-08-18/). Over four business days, the dollar weakened against the soum by 128.18 soums, or about 1.1%.

The dollar exchange rate in Uzbekistan last fell below 11,820 soums in the first half of August 2023. On [August 1](https://cbu.uz/ru/arkhiv-kursov-valyut/json/USD/2023-08-01/), 2023, the dollar traded at 11,618.15 soums, and by [August 18](https://cbu.uz/ru/arkhiv-kursov-valyut/json/USD/2023-08-18/) that year it had risen to 12,066.19 soums. The Central Bank has not recorded a rate this low since then, according to the archive of official exchange rates.
