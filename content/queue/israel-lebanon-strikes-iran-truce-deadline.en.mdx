Israel on Sunday, August 16, struck southern Lebanon for a second day near Beaufort Castle in Nabatieh, where hundreds of families are fleeing, as the US-Iran truce is set to expire Monday.

LEAP News [reported](https://leap.uz/ru/2026/08/16/israel-strikes-lebanon-ansar-11-killed) that overnight into Saturday, August 15, airstrikes on the village of Ansar and neighboring Deir al-Zahrani in Nabatieh district killed at least 11 people. It was the deadliest day since the truce took effect on June 20.

On Sunday, Israel resumed strikes on Nabatieh — around Beaufort Castle, a UNESCO-listed site. Hundreds of families [fled north](https://www.aljazeera.com/video/newsfeed/2026/8/16/israel-strikes-south-lebanon-again-as-families-flee-north), clogging roads.

## Lebanon Calls Strikes a 'Massacre,' Hezbollah Vows Response

Lebanese President Joseph Aoun called the strikes [a 'clear signal' to the negotiating track](http://ru.euronews.com/2026/08/15/israel-lebanon-attacks-president). Prime Minister Nawaf Salam described the escalation as "extremely severe," while parliament speaker Nabih Berri called what happened [a 'massacre'](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/16/why-has-israel-escalated-attacks-in-southern-lebanon-despite-ceasefire).

Hezbollah warned Israel of a "proportionate response" to the strikes, [Al Jazeera reports](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/16/why-has-israel-escalated-attacks-in-southern-lebanon-despite-ceasefire). The group for the first time linked the escalation to the interests of Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu ahead of the October 27 election — according to Hezbollah, the strikes serve his electoral goals.

## US-Iran Truce Set to Expire Monday

The Lebanon escalation coincided with the expiration of the US-Iran truce. It was signed in June and expires Monday, August 17 — talks [made no progress](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-16/israel-strikes-lebanon-as-end-of-us-iran-ceasefire-looms) heading into the weekend, Bloomberg reports.

Iran is separately continuing talks with Oman on reopening shipping through the Strait of Hormuz. Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi said reopening the strait depends on Washington's compliance with the June memorandum, [Al Jazeera reports](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/liveblog/2026/8/16/iran-war-live-talks-on-hormuz-strait-continue-israel-kills-11-in-lebanon).

The June framework agreement tied the Lebanon ceasefire to Hezbollah's disarmament and the withdrawal of Israeli troops once the dismantling of the group's military infrastructure was verified. Since the escalation began on March 2, 2026, the death toll in Lebanon [has surpassed 4,300](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/16/why-has-israel-escalated-attacks-in-southern-lebanon-despite-ceasefire).
