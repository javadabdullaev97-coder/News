Payments company Stripe has agreed to buy AI startup OpenRouter for more than $7 billion, [Bloomberg reported](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-16/stripe-nears-deal-to-buy-ai-firm-openrouter-for-over-7-billion) on August 16, citing sources familiar with the negotiations.

Neither side has officially confirmed the deal. A Stripe spokesperson told [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/stripe-is-reportedly-acquiring-ai-startup-openrouter-for-more-than-7-billion/) that the company does not comment on rumors and speculation; OpenRouter also declined to comment.

OpenRouter is a service that gives developers unified access to more than 400 AI models from different companies, letting them switch between models by price, speed or task. The startup was founded in 2023 and is led by [Alex Atallah](https://the-decoder.com/stripe-is-reportedly-acquiring-ai-startup-openrouter-for-more-than-7-billion/). According to Bloomberg, OpenRouter has about 8 million users.

## Valuation Quintupled in Three Months

In May 2026, OpenRouter raised $113 million at a $1.3 billion valuation, [SiliconANGLE reported](https://siliconangle.com/2026/08/16/stripe-reportedly-finalizes-deal-buy-ai-model-router-openrouter-7b/). The round was led by CapitalG, Alphabet's venture arm, with participation from Andreessen Horowitz and Menlo Ventures. A year earlier, the company's valuation was below $650 million. The Stripe deal, if completed, would value the company at nearly five times its May valuation.

According to the same data, weekly volume through the platform reached 25 trillion tokens in May. Annual revenue in March stood at about $50 million, up from $19 million at the end of 2025. OpenRouter charges roughly a 5% fee on customers' spending on model calls.

Stripe has processed OpenRouter's payments since January 2026, SiliconANGLE noted. Together with OpenAI, the company co-authored the Agentic Commerce Protocol, a standard for automated payments between AI agents.
