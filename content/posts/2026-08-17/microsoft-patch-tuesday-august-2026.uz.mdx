Microsoft 11 avgustda avgust oyidagi Patch Tuesday chiqarilishida Windows va boshqa qo'llab-quvvatlanadigan dasturiy ta'minotdagi kamida 398 ta zaiflikni bartaraf etdi — kamchiliklardan birini tajovuzkorlar allaqachon ishlatmoqda.

Kompaniyaning o'z byulleteni — [Security Update Guide](https://api.msrc.microsoft.com/cvrf/2026-Aug) — avgust relizi uchun 422 ta CVE yozuvini ko'rsatadi. [Krebs on Security](https://krebsonsecurity.com/2026/08/microsoft-plugs-nearly-400-security-holes/) va [Tenable](https://www.tenable.com/blog/microsofts-august-2026-patch-tuesday-addresses-398-cves-cve-2026-68820) torroq raqamni — 398 tani — keltiradi, ehtimol, bu o'tgan oylarning takroriy e'lonlarisiz. [Qualys](https://blog.qualys.com/vulnerabilities-threat-research/patch-tuesday/2026/08/11/microsoft-patch-tuesday-august-2026-security-update-review) umumiy son bo'yicha o'z raqamini — 421 ni — keltiradi. Manbalar 42 tadan ([Krebs](https://krebsonsecurity.com/2026/08/microsoft-plugs-nearly-400-security-holes/), [Tenable](https://www.tenable.com/blog/microsofts-august-2026-patch-tuesday-addresses-398-cves-cve-2026-68820)) 62 tagacha zaiflikni ([The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/microsoft-patches-398-flaws-including.html), [Qualys](https://blog.qualys.com/vulnerabilities-threat-research/patch-tuesday/2026/08/11/microsoft-patch-tuesday-august-2026-security-update-review)) kritik deb hisoblaydi.

Allaqachon ishlatilayotgan zaiflik — Windowsning tarmoq ulanishlarini boshqaruvchi Ancillary Function Driver for WinSock (afd.sys) drayveridagi [CVE-2026-68820](https://msrc.microsoft.com/update-guide/en-US/advisory/CVE-2026-68820). CVSS bo'yicha 7,0 ball olgan use-after-free xatosi lokal tarzda imtiyozlarni SYSTEM darajasiga ko'tarish imkonini beradi. [NVD](https://nvd.nist.gov/vuln/detail/CVE-2026-68820) ma'lumotlariga ko'ra, zaiflik AQSh CISA agentligining faol ishlatilayotgan kamchiliklar katalogiga kiritilgan. Idora uni 25 avgustgacha bartaraf etishni tavsiya qildi, deb xabar beradi [Qualys](https://blog.qualys.com/vulnerabilities-threat-research/patch-tuesday/2026/08/11/microsoft-patch-tuesday-august-2026-security-update-review).

Yana ikkita zaiflik yamoq chiqishidan oldin ochiq e'lon qilingan, biroq ularning ishlatilgani tasdiqlanmagan. Windows foydalanuvchi profillari xizmatidagi CVE-2026-62832 7,8 ball oldi — Microsoft uning ishlatilishini ehtimoldan yiroq emas deb hisoblaydi. Windows konteynerlarini izolyatsiya qilish drayveridagi CVE-2026-72971 5,5 ball to'pladi — bu holatda kompaniya ishlatilish ehtimolini past deb baholaydi.

## Foydalanuvchilar nima qilishi kerak

Asosiy tavsiya o'zgarmagan — avgust yangilanishlarini Windows Update markazi orqali imkon qadar tezroq o'rnatish kerak, ayniqsa internetga ulangan tizimlarda. Kritik tuzatishlar orasida Windows DNS-serveri va Microsoft QUIC protokolidagi CVSS bo'yicha 9,8 ball olgan zaifliklar bor. Ikkalasi ham autentifikatsiyasiz masofadan turib kod bajarish imkonini beradi, deb xabar beradi [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/microsoft-patches-398-flaws-including.html). Lokal SharePoint fermalari administratorlari ikkala tuzatish — iyul va avgustniki — o'rnatilganini tekshirishi lozim, aks holda zaiflik saqlanib qoladi.

Avgust relizi 2026-yil uchun rekord bo'lgan iyul relizidan kichikroq — o'shanda Microsoft 570 dan ortiq zaiflikni bartaraf etgan edi — biroq iyunnikidan, taxminan 200 tadan, ikki barobar ko'p, deb xabar beradi [Krebs on Security](https://krebsonsecurity.com/2026/08/microsoft-plugs-nearly-400-security-holes/).
