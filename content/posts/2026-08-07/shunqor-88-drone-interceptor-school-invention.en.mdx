The Ministry of Higher Education, Science and Innovation of the Republic of Uzbekistan announced on August 7 the creation of the "Shunqor-88" drone interceptor, which, according to the ministry, detects and neutralizes dangerous drones at a distance of 8–10 kilometers.

## Drone specifications and its creator

The developer is Bilolkhon Ismoilov, who presented "Shunqor-88" together with his team at the national review-contest "Kelajak muhandislari" ("Engineers of the Future"), [according to a post](https://t.me/eduuz/50179) by the Ministry of Higher Education, Science and Innovation on Telegram. According to the post, the drone reaches speeds of up to 450 kilometers per hour.

The ministry estimates the production cost of "Shunqor-88" at $20,000–50,000. For comparison, [the same post](https://t.me/eduuz/50179) cites the cost of traditional missile-based air defense systems — $2–5 million per unit. The ministry does not specify which systems it is referring to or what methodology the development team used to calculate the production cost.

Ismoilov's educational institution and age, the region where the drone was developed, and the method of interception — physical capture, electronic jamming, or another method — are not specified in the post. The ministry also did not report on testing dates, patenting, or plans for implementing the development.

## The "Kelajak muhandislari" contest

"Kelajak muhandislari" is a national review-contest and international engineering festival for young inventors organized by the Ministry of Higher Education, Science and Innovation, [according to the contest's official website](https://km.edu.uz/ru). Participants can include schoolchildren aged 6–18, university and college students aged 17–25, and specialists up to age 40. The festival brings together up to 2,000 participants across 9 tracks and 39 contests. The ministry did not specify in which age category Ismoilov competed.
