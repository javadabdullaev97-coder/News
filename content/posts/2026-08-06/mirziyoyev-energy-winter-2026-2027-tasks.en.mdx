President Shavkat Mirziyoyev was [briefed](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9487) on August 6 on the progress of tasks set for the energy sector — including preparations of electricity and gas supply for the autumn-winter season.

## What was discussed

The report is based on a video conference the president [held](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9487) on July 27. It covered sector management, the stability of local electricity and gas supply, the number of accidents, and energy losses. As a result, five tasks were set: establish supply at the district and mahalla level, form regional energy balances, and conduct an inventory of networks and equipment. Separately, officials were instructed to prepare for the autumn-winter period and introduce digital technologies and artificial intelligence.

At the presentation on August 6, the new leadership of the Ministry of Energy, the Regional Electric Networks joint-stock company and Uzenergoinspection [reported](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9487) on the progress of these instructions.

## Yangiyul pilot to expand to 13 districts

A special task force to improve electricity and gas supply is already operating in Yangiyul district. It holds open dialogues with entrepreneurs and residents there, reviewing complaints about outages, capacity shortages, low gas pressure, obtaining technical specifications, and debts, according to [the report](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9487) on the president's website.

Based on this experience, a similar system is planned for another 13 districts across the country, and later extended to the remaining regions. The report does not specify the names of these districts or the timeline for the rollout.

Separately, oversight of the issuance of technical specifications for connecting businesses to electricity and gas networks is being tightened, with an eye toward transparency and preventing corruption.

## Who is responsible for what

The president divided the tasks among three bodies:

- **The Ministry of Energy** is forming a unified sector management system, coordinating industry enterprises, and is responsible for the timely implementation of investment projects and systematic preparation for the autumn-winter period.
- **The Regional Electric Networks JSC** is to improve the stability of local electricity supply, gradually modernize infrastructure, reduce emergency outages, and improve service quality.
- **Uzenergoinspection** is tightening oversight of the rational use of energy resources and compliance with technical requirements, working to cut losses, and accelerating energy audits of major consumers.

As part of winter preparations, "problem" networks and equipment are also being upgraded, and an electronic system is being developed to relay instructions to industry staff and monitor their implementation. Special attention is being paid to mahallas with limited access to gas supply and to attracting the private sector into liquefied gas deliveries.

## What's next

Mirziyoyev instructed those responsible to implement all measures "based on a targeted approach and precise calculations." He also instructed officials to ensure stable electricity and gas supply for the population and sectors of the economy, according to [the report](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9487). Neither the presidential press service nor the Ministry of Energy specified a date for the next progress review.
