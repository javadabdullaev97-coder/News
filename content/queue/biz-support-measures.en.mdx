President Shavkat Mirziyoyev on August 18 announced new business support measures — from loan counter-guarantees to a financial center in Tashkent — at the sixth open dialogue with entrepreneurs in Khorezm.

## Counter-Guarantee: Collateral for Only 25% of the Loan

A counter-guarantee system is being introduced for small-business loans of up to 10 billion soums (≈$843,000 at the Central Bank's exchange rate for August 18). An entrepreneur needs to provide collateral for only 25% of the loan amount. The rest of the collateral is split between the state (30%), the Business Guarantee Company (30%) and banks (15%), the Telegram channel of the National Agency for Perspective Projects (NAPP) [said](https://t.me/napp_uz/2399).

The AI-based Virtual Tax Consultant portal also launched there — it helps entrepreneurs cut costs and reduce reporting errors. In 2027, similar AI assistants will appear in construction, labor relations, foreign trade and government services.

## Three Ecosystems: From Start to Scale-Up

The president presented three business support ecosystems for different stages of development, [according to a post](https://t.me/joqargikenes/32371) on the Telegram channel of the Jokargy Kenes of Karakalpakstan, which reprinted a statement from the president's press service.

Business Start (Biznes-start) is designed for beginners. Under it, entrepreneurs use artificial intelligence to learn how to prepare projects, loan documents and reports. Beginners receive ready-made business plans and collateral-free loans of up to 200 million soums (≈$16,900). The program received 100 billion soums (≈$8.43 million) in funding.

Business Lift (Biznes-lift) targets companies that have already set up production, while Business Rise (Biznes-yuksalish) is for those ready to scale up. A single credit services portal now serves all stages, offering online loans of up to 5 billion soums (≈$421,700) per application.

## TIFC and Enterprise Uzbekistan Expected to Attract Up to $25 Billion

The Tashkent International Financial Center (TIFC) and the Enterprise Uzbekistan international digital technology center are set to launch in 2027. Together they are expected to attract $20-25 billion in capital, new knowledge and technology, [according to a post](https://t.me/joqargikenes/32370) on the Karakalpakstan channel.

## Logistics: Poti and Anaklia Ports, Truck Incentives

In 2027, a logistics center with an annual capacity of 500,000 tons will begin operating at the Georgian port of Poti, and practical work will start on the port of Anaklia, the Jokargy Kenes [said](https://t.me/joqargikenes/32370). New transport and logistics centers will also appear inside the country in Olot, Termez, Yangiyul, Akhangaran and Khanabad.

The customs duty and recycling fee on imported trucks have been abolished. A zero VAT rate was introduced for providing railcars for international transport, [according to the same post](https://t.me/joqargikenes/32372).

## Land Now Comes With a Building Permit

Khokims now sell land plots for business at auction together with a building permit and a ready-made project. Previously, collecting these documents took anywhere from six months to a year. The advance payment on the infrastructure fee was cut from 20% to 5%, the State Assets Management Agency [said](https://t.me/davaktivuz/6209).

Banks will put up for sale 2,500 real estate properties on their balance sheets worth a combined 9 trillion soums (≈$759 million). The terms include a discount, no down payment, no interest, and no property tax in the first year, according to the same agency post.
