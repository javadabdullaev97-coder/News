The National Statistics Committee of the Republic of Uzbekistan said on July 30 that transport services totaled 103.9 trillion soums in January–June 2026, while the headline of the same release cites 104.2 trillion.

## Release Headline and Body Diverge by 300 Billion Soums

The Statistics Committee published the data on July 30 and republished it on its Telegram channel on [August 1](https://t.me/uzstataxborot/69159). The [publication's headline](https://stat.uz/uz/matbuot-markazi/qo-mita-yangiliklar/69539-6-ojda-transport-khizmatlari-azhmi-104-2-trln-s-mga-etdi-2) states 104.2 trillion soums (≈$8.68 billion), while the body of the same release states 103.9 trillion soums (≈$8.65 billion). The discrepancy is 300 billion soums (≈$25 million).

Both figures appear in another release from the agency. In the [market services summary](https://stat.uz/uz/matbuot-markazi/qo-mita-yangiliklar/69509-zbekistonda-bozor-khizmatlari-azhmi-664-9-trln-s-mga-etdi-2) from July 29, transport services for January–June 2026 are listed as 103.9 trillion soums, while 104.2 trillion soums is the volume of financial services. The Statistics Committee has not published any clarification on which of the two figures in the transport release is correct.

## Road Transport Accounts for Over Half the Volume

Structure of transport services for January–June 2026, according to the Statistics Committee:

- road transport — 52.5%
- auxiliary transport activities — 15.1%
- air transport — 14.4%
- rail transport — 9.5%
- pipeline transport — 8.5%

The agency does not cite growth rates versus January–June 2025 in this release.

## Transport Within the Structure of Market Services

Total market services in Uzbekistan reached 664.9 trillion soums (≈$55.4 billion) in the first half of 2026, up 16.9% from the same period in 2025.

Above transport in absolute volume:

- trade services — 170 trillion soums (≈$14.2 billion)
- accommodation and food services — 115.2 trillion soums (≈$9.6 billion)
- financial services — 104.2 trillion soums (≈$8.68 billion)

Below transport are communications and IT services (51.9 trillion soums) and education services (23.8 trillion soums).

Transport is separated from financial services by the same 300 billion soums that diverge within the transport release itself. In this summary, the Statistics Committee gives the breakdown by service type only in absolute values, without percentage shares.

The conversion to US dollars uses the official exchange rate of the [Central Bank of Uzbekistan](https://cbu.uz/ru/arkhiv-kursov-valyut/json/USD/2026-08-01/) for August 1, 2026: 12,006.39 soums per dollar.
