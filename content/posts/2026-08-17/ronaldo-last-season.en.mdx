Cristiano Ronaldo, 41, said in an interview with Vogue magazine published on Sunday, August 16, that the current season will probably be his last in professional football.

"This is probably my last year in football, and I want to leave an impressive legacy," said the 41-year-old forward for Portugal's national team and Saudi club Al-Nassr, [The Guardian reported](https://www.theguardian.com/football/2026/aug/16/cristiano-ronaldo-says-this-year-is-probably-his-last-as-a-footballer), citing the Vogue interview.

Speaking about life after football, Ronaldo said his future is "completely planned." "I have so many things to do that it's hard to name one. Football will leave a big void, and I'll have to fill the time in different ways. I want to rest more, travel, play padel — and keep enjoying what we've earned over 25 years with a lot of sacrifices," [Marca quoted](https://www.marca.com/en/football/2026/08/16/cristiano-ronaldo-s-last-dance-soccer-legend-announces-imminent-retirement.html) the footballer as saying.

Ronaldo has played for Al-Nassr, a Saudi Pro League club, since January 2023. Over his club and national team career he has scored more than 950 goals, [The Guardian noted](https://www.theguardian.com/football/2026/aug/16/cristiano-ronaldo-says-this-year-is-probably-his-last-as-a-footballer). The forward had previously spoken vaguely about ending his career — "soon" or "in a year or two" — and the statement about the current season was the first specific one.

## His future with Portugal's national team remains undecided

The 2026 World Cup was Ronaldo's sixth in his career and, he said, his last. In a group-stage match on June 23, Portugal's national team beat tournament debutant Uzbekistan 5-0. Ronaldo scored twice, becoming the first footballer in history to score at six different World Cups, [Al Jazeera reported](https://www.aljazeera.com/sports/2026/6/23/ronaldo-scores-twice-as-portugal-thrash-uzbekistan-5-0-at-world-cup-2026).

Portugal lost to Spain and was eliminated from the tournament in the round of 16 in Dallas on July 6. After the defeat, Ronaldo said he would not rush a decision about his future with the national team, [Al Jazeera reported](https://www.aljazeera.com/sports/2026/7/6/all-to-know-about-ronaldos-world-cup-career-as-it-ends-in-spain-loss).

Ronaldo has not yet made an official announcement about retiring from football.
