The Central Bank of Uzbekistan [said](https://t.me/centralbankuzbekistan/13386) on July 30 that reports circulating on social media about taxation of interest income on deposits were based on an idea from a research study, not an official proposal.

## What the Central Bank Said

The agency [published a statement](https://t.me/centralbankuzbekistan/13386) on its Telegram channel on the evening of July 30 — in response to reports circulating on social media about a possible tax on bank deposit interest. The statement said the idea was put forward by the Institute for Reducing the Shadow Economy and Fiscal Analysis under the Ministry of Economy and Finance as part of a research and analytical study. Research institutions regularly study international experience and economic processes, so it is natural for them to put forward various analytical proposals.

The Central Bank clarified that the idea was voiced as part of a presentation of the research findings, not as an official proposal. The regulator added that when examining such initiatives, it pays particular attention to a comprehensive assessment of their impact on the cost of financial resources, banks' resource base and financial stability.

## Where the Proposal Came From

This is an initiative that the institute itself [presented](https://ifs.imv.uz/ru/press/news/rasmiy-bayonot-bank-omonatlari-foizlarini-soliqqa-tortish-taklifi-xususida), also on July 30, at the "Fiscal Dialogue" event. It was an idea for professional discussion among experts, without the status of a draft law or an official decision. The institute proposed taxing interest income on individuals' deposits at a rate comparable to the dividend tax rate. That rate currently stands at [5%](https://gov.uz/ru/advice/70/document/1325), according to data from the Government of Uzbekistan portal.

Interest on individuals' deposits in Uzbekistan is currently not taxed, and the Central Bank's statement does not change that. Neither the institute nor the regulator has said anything about drafting a law or a timeline for a possible introduction of such a tax.
