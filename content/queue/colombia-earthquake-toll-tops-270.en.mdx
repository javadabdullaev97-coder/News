A magnitude 7.4 earthquake destroyed dozens of towns in western Colombia on Monday, August 10 — as of August 14, the death toll had passed 270, according to Al Jazeera.

## Epicenter and scale of the quake

The quake struck at around 7:34 a.m. local time in Chocó department, 5 km from the city of San José del Palmar, at a depth of about 110 km, the U.S. Geological Survey (USGS) [reports](https://earthquake.usgs.gov/earthquakes/eventpage/us6000tjl2/executive). According to [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/13/search-efforts-in-colombia-enter-final-phase-as-quake-toll-reaches-265), it was Colombia's strongest earthquake since the start of the century.

## Death toll rising every day

According to [Al Jazeera's August 13 report](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/13/search-efforts-in-colombia-enter-final-phase-as-quake-toll-reaches-265), the confirmed death toll had reached 265, with more than 3,500 people injured. [PBS NewsHour reported](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/show/news-wrap-earthquake-death-toll-in-colombia-tops-260) that nearly 500 people were missing at the time — Colombian authorities acknowledge the figure is likely underestimated. A day later, on August 14, [Al Jazeera reported](https://www.aljazeera.com/video/newsfeed/2026/8/14/colombias-rescuers-search-rubble-as-hope-fades-on-finding-survivors) that the death toll had passed 274, with the final count still being refined as rubble is cleared. More than 150 aftershocks have [been recorded](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/show/news-wrap-earthquake-death-toll-in-colombia-tops-260) since the main shock.

The highest death tolls were in the cities of Pereira (83) and Cali (74), [as of August 13](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/13/search-efforts-in-colombia-enter-final-phase-as-quake-toll-reaches-265). More than 9,550 homes were destroyed.

## Children's hospital partially collapses in Cali

Part of a children's hospital building in Cali partially collapsed — [surveillance cameras captured](https://www.aljazeera.com/video/newsfeed/2026/8/13/13-08-clip-colombia-hospital-co) the moment the structure gave way during the August 10 earthquake, Al Jazeera reports.

## Rescuers move into final phase

The critical 72-hour window, when the chances of finding survivors under the rubble are highest, has passed. Cali Mayor Alejandro Éder [said](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/13/search-efforts-in-colombia-enter-final-phase-as-quake-toll-reaches-265) that this "doesn't mean there won't be survivors after [72 hours], but finding them will become harder." The search-and-rescue operation has officially entered its final phase.

## Dispute over the Mexican rescue team

Colombia allowed rescue teams from the United States (fire departments from Los Angeles County and Fairfax County), El Salvador, Ecuador and Israel to work in the country. Peru and Brazil also offered assistance, [Al Jazeera reports](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/13/mexico-says-colombia-rejected-its-earthquake-rescue-team). Mexico's military rescue brigade was not allowed into the country. Mexican President Claudia Sheinbaum said Colombian authorities demanded the Mexican team undergo re-certification, even though the brigade already holds international certification and has experience working in 98 countries. "Colombia requested certification. They have it, but now they're asking for another one," [Sheinbaum said](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/13/mexico-says-colombia-rejected-its-earthquake-rescue-team). Colombia's Foreign Minister Omar Bula responded that the country "does not exclude any country" that has offered help and is working "without ideological or political considerations."

## What's next

Colombian President Abelardo de la Espriella [declared](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/13/search-efforts-in-colombia-enter-final-phase-as-quake-toll-reaches-265) a state of economic emergency and pledged to create a fund to rebuild hospitals, schools, housing and infrastructure in the affected areas.
