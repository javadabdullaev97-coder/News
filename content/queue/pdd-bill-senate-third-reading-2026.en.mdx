The Legislative Chamber of the Oliy Majlis on August 6 discussed in second reading, adopted in third reading, and sent to the Senate of the Oliy Majlis a bill toughening liability for serious traffic violations.

## What was discussed in second reading

Deputies on August 6 [discussed](https://t.me/qonunchilikpalatasi/37187) article by article in second reading a bill to improve road safety. The document [amends](https://t.me/qonunchilikpalatasi/37190) the Criminal Code, the Criminal Procedure Code, the Administrative Code, and the law "On Road Traffic." These are the same four acts as in the version approved in first reading on August 4, LEAP News [reported](https://leap.uz/posts/2026-08-04/pdd-bill-first-reading-2026).

Administrative liability is proposed to be [toughened](https://t.me/qonunchilikpalatasi/37190) for letting a minor drive a vehicle, illegally organizing or taking part in vehicle races, and leaving trucks parked on the shoulder at night in a position dangerous to traffic safety. Separately, tougher penalties will apply to violations of the procedure for deregistering vehicles and exceeding the speed limit by more than 80 km/h.

## Drunk driving and cancellation of fine discount

The document also [provides](https://t.me/qonunchilikpalatasi/37191) for tougher liability for driving a vehicle while intoxicated or evading a sobriety test. For drivers who repeatedly commit certain serious traffic violations, the bill [proposes](https://t.me/qonunchilikpalatasi/37191) temporarily suspending the procedure for paying an administrative fine at a discount. The Legislative Chamber discussed this provision already at first reading, LEAP News [reported](https://leap.uz/posts/2026-08-04/pdd-bill-first-reading-2026).

## Vehicle seizure — a provision from first reading

On August 4, the Legislative Chamber [approved](https://leap.uz/posts/2026-08-04/pdd-bill-first-reading-2026) in first reading the temporary seizure of a motor vehicle for a period of six months to one year for drivers without a driving license or those deprived of one. The provision covers road hooliganism, organizing illegal races, driving while intoxicated, or refusing a medical examination. In its reports on the second and third readings on August 6, the Legislative Chamber did not separately mention this provision.

## What's next

After third reading, the [bill](https://t.me/qonunchilikpalatasi/37192) was sent to the Senate of the Oliy Majlis. The date of its consideration in the upper chamber has not yet been announced. The Legislative Chamber's plenary session on August 6 [concluded](https://t.me/qonunchilikpalatasi/37193) its work.
