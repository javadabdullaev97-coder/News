Walmart va Sam's Club tarmog'i 2026-yil 24-avgustdan AQShdagi ba'zi do'konlarida smartfon orqali kontaktsiz to'lov — Tap to Pay texnologiyasini qabul qila boshlaydi, [deb ma'lum qildi kompaniya](https://corporate.walmart.com/news/2026/08/21/more-ways-to-pay-tap-to-pay-is-coming-to-walmart-and-sams-club). Qo'llab-quvvatlanadigan xizmatlar orasida Apple Pay va Google Pay ham bo'ladi, deb [MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/21/walmart-to-begin-accepting-apple-pay-in-us/) va [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/08/21/walmart-finally-launching-apple-pay-support-next-week/) xabar beradi. Tap to pay — xaridor karta yoki naqd pul o'rniga terminalga smartfon yoki soatni tutadigan texnologiya.

[Walmart](https://corporate.walmart.com/news/2026/08/21/more-ways-to-pay-tap-to-pay-is-coming-to-walmart-and-sams-club) ma'lumotlariga ko'ra, yangi to'lov usuli allaqachon mavjud variantlarni to'ldiradi, jumladan kompaniyaning o'z xizmati Walmart Pay'ni — tarmoq uni yangi variantlar bilan bir qatorda qabul qilishda davom etadi. «Biz xaridorlar va klub a'zolari qanday to'lashni tanlay olishini xohlaymiz...» — deyiladi Walmart bayonotida, uni [MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/21/walmart-to-begin-accepting-apple-pay-in-us/) keltiradi.

Walmart o'n yildan ortiq vaqt davomida Apple Pay va Google Pay'ni qabul qilmagan, [deb yozadi](https://9to5mac.com/2026/08/21/walmart-finally-launching-apple-pay-support-next-week/) [9to5Mac va MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/21/walmart-to-begin-accepting-apple-pay-in-us/). Chakana savdo tarmog'i o'zining Walmart Pay xizmatiga ustunlik bergan. [MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/21/walmart-to-begin-accepting-apple-pay-in-us/) bahosiga ko'ra, kompaniya ikkala xizmatni ham qo'llab-quvvatlamagan AQShning yirik tarmoqlaridan biri bo'lib qolgan.

Google tomonidan ishga tushirishni [Google Pay'ning rasmiy blogi](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/platforms/google-pay/tap-to-pay-google-pay-walmart/) tasdiqladi. «Keyingi haftadan boshlab xaridorlar AQShdagi bir qator Walmart va Sam's Club do'konlarida Google Pay orqali bir marta teginish bilan to'lashlari mumkin bo'ladi», — deyilgan nashrda. Topilgan manbalarda Apple'ning hamkorlik haqida to'g'ridan-to'g'ri bayonoti yo'q.

## Keyin nima bo'ladi

Tap to Pay 2026-yil oxiriga kelib AQShdagi butun tarmoqqa yetib boradi — xizmat Walmart va Sam's Clubning barcha do'konlarida ishlay boshlaydi, [deb ma'lum qiladi](https://corporate.walmart.com/news/2026/08/21/more-ways-to-pay-tap-to-pay-is-coming-to-walmart-and-sams-club) kompaniya. [MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/21/walmart-to-begin-accepting-apple-pay-in-us/) va [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/08/21/walmart-finally-launching-apple-pay-support-next-week/) ma'lumotlariga ko'ra, Apple Pay ham qo'llab-quvvatlanadigan xizmatlar qatoriga kiradi. Google Pay'ning qo'llab-quvvatlanishini [Google blogi](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/platforms/google-pay/tap-to-pay-google-pay-walmart/) tasdiqlaydi. Tarmoqning yoqilg'i quyish shoxobchalarida smartfon orqali kontaktsiz to'lov 2027-yil o'rtalariga kelib ulanadi — bu muddatlarni ham [Walmart](https://corporate.walmart.com/news/2026/08/21/more-ways-to-pay-tap-to-pay-is-coming-to-walmart-and-sams-club), ham [Google](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/platforms/google-pay/tap-to-pay-google-pay-walmart/) tasdiqlaydi.
