The National Statistics Committee of Uzbekistan [reported](https://t.me/uzstataxborot/69185) on August 3 that in 2025, the country's botanical gardens received 1.17 million visitors — 3.8 times more than in 2024.

## Three gardens for the whole country

As of January 1, 2026, three botanical gardens operate in Uzbekistan — in the Republic of Karakalpakstan, the Bukhara region, and Tashkent. In 2025, their combined area [grew](https://stat.uz/ru/press-tsentr/novosti-goskomstata/69609-zbekistonda-botanika-bo-lariga-tashriflar-soni-3-8-barobarga-oshdi-3) by 6 hectares to reach 113.3 hectares.

## What's next

Presidential Decree [UP-47](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/8101214) of March 25, 2026, launched the National Project "Yashil makon" (Green Space). It calls for building additional botanical gardens and dendrological parks on at least 100 hectares across Karakalpakstan, the regions, and Tashkent. The decree obliges the Cabinet of Ministers of the Republic of Uzbekistan to complete the task by November 1, 2028. It also sets a target of raising the country's greenery coverage from 14.2% to 30% by 2030.
