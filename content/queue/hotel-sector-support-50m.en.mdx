Uzbekistan's President Shavkat Mirziyoyev on August 18 announced [$50 million in loans for hotels](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9510) at an open dialogue with entrepreneurs in Khorezm region.

The funds will go toward building, renovating and equipping hotels. Entrepreneurs will be able to get such loans [at 16% annual interest in soums for up to ten years](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9510), according to a transcript of the remarks.

The Cabinet of Ministers of the Republic of Uzbekistan will extend by another two years [the period for granting subsidies for the construction of three-, four- and five-star hotels](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9510).

In addition, the tax break for hotels on land tax and property tax has been extended through 2030 — the rate [has been cut tenfold](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9510).

Separately, the president announced another measure for the tourism industry: services that tour operators provide to foreign tourists [will be treated as exports and exempted from VAT](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9510).
