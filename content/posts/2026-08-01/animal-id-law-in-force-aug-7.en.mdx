The Law "On Identification, Registration and Tracking of Animals" takes effect in Uzbekistan on August 7, 2026, making registration mandatory for livestock, horses, camels, dogs and cats.

## What the Law Establishes

President Shavkat Mirziyoyev signed [Law No. ZRU-1079](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/7676785) on August 6, 2025. The Legislative Chamber of the Oliy Majlis adopted it on February 13, and the Senate approved it on May 28, 2025. The document's page on lex.uz lists August 7, 2026, as the effective date; as of August 1, 2026, the act was still marked as not yet in force.

Registration covers cattle, sheep, goats, pigs, horses, camels, dogs and cats owned by individuals and legal entities.

Identification is carried out using tools the law describes as "visual tags, electronic microchips and tags with embedded microchips." Each identification tool carries the animal's identification number. Data on identified and registered animals is entered into an electronic information system.

The authorized body is the Veterinary and Livestock Development Committee under the Ministry of Agriculture of the Republic of Uzbekistan. A dedicated Center for Animal Identification, Registration and Monitoring under the committee was [established](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/6583139) earlier, by Presidential Resolution No. PP-285 of August 24, 2023.

## Registration Deadlines

The law [sets](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/7676785) a separate deadline for each species:

- cattle, sheep, goats and camels — within 14 days of birth;
- pigs — within 1 month;
- dogs and cats — within 3 months;
- horses — within 4 months;
- animals imported into the country — no later than 21 days after import.

If the deadline is missed, registration must be completed within a month after it expires.

## Who Pays

Under [Resolution No. PP-285](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/6583139), the animal identification, registration and monitoring system is being "introduced free of charge" in districts specializing in livestock breeding. Their list is given in Annex 1 to the resolution. The rollout is split into two stages: 25 such districts in 2024–2025, and the remaining districts of the republic in 2026–2027. Under the same document, the system covers "animals, including pets."

$33 million has been [allocated](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/6583139) to roll out the animal identification, registration and monitoring system. The funds come under Resolution No. PP-211 of July 5, 2023, on implementing the "Livestock Sector Development (Phase 2)" project with the participation of the International Development Association and the International Bank for Reconstruction and Development.

When adopting the law, the Oliy Majlis [noted](https://parl.uz/oz/articles/economics/hayvonlarni_identifikatsiya_qilish_ro_yxatga_olish_va_kuzatish_yuzasidan_qonun/) that it provides for animal identification and registration free of charge to owners. At the same time, the law's article on funding names "the animal owners' own funds, charitable donations, and funds from the State Budget," among other sources not prohibited by legislation.

## Liability and What's Next

A separate article of the law is devoted to liability for violating animal identification, registration and tracking legislation.

The document's page on lex.uz lists two versions of the law: one in force from August 7, 2026, and another taking effect on January 1, 2028.
