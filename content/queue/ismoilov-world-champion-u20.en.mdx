Khorezm wrestler Kuvonchbek Ismoilov won gold at the U20 world Greco-Roman wrestling championships in Bratislava, Slovakia, the [Ministry of Sports of Uzbekistan](https://t.me/minsportuz/90889) reported on August 18. In the final, he defeated Russia's Salim Kazmakhov 9-7. The opponent's name and spelling are based on data from the National Olympic Committee of Uzbekistan and United World Wrestling (UWW).

Ismoilov competed in the under-63kg weight category. The [National Olympic Committee of Uzbekistan](https://t.me/OlympicUz/46326) independently confirmed the win, also citing the final score of 9-7.

After the final, United World Wrestling President Nenad Lalović presented Ismoilov with the championship belt — the Ministry of Sports [reported](https://t.me/minsportuz/90890) separately.

The U20 world championships combine freestyle, women's and Greco-Roman wrestling. The tournament in Bratislava runs from August 16 to 23, according to [materials](https://uww.org/article/u20-world-championships-2026-entries) from United World Wrestling.
