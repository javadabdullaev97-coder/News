After President Shavkat Mirziyoyev's video conference on July 27, which brought a change of leadership at energy companies, Regional Electric Networks JSC (RES) began repairing "red-category" transformers in Davlatabad district and Syrdarya region.

## What counts as "red category"

RES divides transformers, power grids and other infrastructure by wear and risk of failure. The "red" category covers the most problematic equipment: worn-out gear that fails most often and drives complaints from residents and businesses about power outages.

According to [RES data](https://t.me/hetmuloqot/26447), in Davlatabad district the company is already upgrading power supply to mahallas in this exact category — the work is running alongside a broader program to improve the district's grids. A similar modernization [is underway](https://t.me/hetmuloqot/26416) in Syrdarya region: plans there call for installing 560 reinforced-concrete poles, laying 21.5 kilometers of power lines and building 10 new transformer substations, involving more than 200 specialists and 50 pieces of equipment.

## New leadership after the July 27 meeting

The "red-category" repair program is part of the energy reform launched after President Shavkat Mirziyoyev's video conference on July 27. At the meeting, [priority tasks were set](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9456) for managing the sector and increasing accountability for power and gas supply outages; the same meeting brought a change of leadership at key energy companies, including RES — a new chairman of the management board took over the company.

RES [reported](https://t.me/hetmuloqot/26417) to the president on measures to implement the tasks set at the meeting in early August. Following that, the company's regional units launched inspections of transformer substations, power grids and meters across the country's regions.

## What remains unknown

RES has not disclosed the total number of "red-category" transformers nationwide or the share already repaired — the company has so far released this data only for individual districts.
