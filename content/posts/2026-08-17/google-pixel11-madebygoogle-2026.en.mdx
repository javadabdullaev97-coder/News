Google unveiled the Pixel 11 smartphone lineup, the Pixel Watch 5 smartwatch and the Pixel Tag item tracker on August 12, the company [said](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/devices/pixel/made-by-google-2026/) on its official blog, The Keyword.

The Pixel 11 lineup includes four models: the [Pixel 11](https://blog.google/intl/en-in/products/hardware/the-pixel-11-series-your-most-personal-pixel-yet/) at $899 and the Pixel 11 Pro at $1,099. Google also launched the Pixel 11 Pro XL at $1,299 and the foldable Pixel 11 Pro Fold at $1,899. All models feature the Tensor G6 chip. The main camera on the Pixel 11 and Pixel 11 Pro Fold got an updated 48-megapixel sensor, with light sensitivity up 56% compared with the previous generation. The Pixel 11 Pro and Pro XL feature a new 50-megapixel main sensor and a separate 48-megapixel telephoto module with a 30% sensitivity gain.

The Pixel Watch 5 starts at [$399](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/devices/pixel/pixel-watch-5/) for the 41mm version and $429 for the 45mm version. The watch gained a breathing-stop detection feature with automatic emergency calling and an updated GPS module.

Pixel Tag, Google's first item tracker built on the Find Hub network, [costs](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/devices/pixel/google-pixel-tag/) $29 per unit or $99 for a four-pack. The case is made of stainless steel, rated IP67, and the replaceable battery lasts up to a year. The tracker goes on sale November 11.

Preorders for the Pixel 11 and Pixel Watch 5 opened August 12, with sales starting August 20.
