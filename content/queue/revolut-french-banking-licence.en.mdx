British neobank Revolut received a full banking license in France from regulator ACPR and the European Central Bank on August 10, according to reports from several outlets; there has been no official confirmation as of publication.

The license was granted to the company's French subsidiary, Revolut Bank S.A. The decision followed a joint review by the [Autorité de contrôle prudentiel et de résolution](https://www.regafi.fr/) (ACPR, France's banking supervisor) and the European Central Bank. It was formally approved by the ECB's Governing Council. Until now, Revolut had operated in France under a Lithuanian banking license obtained in 2018. The company provided services through EU passporting, without the status of a full-fledged French bank.

## What Changes for Customers in France

According to outlets citing a statement from [Revolut](https://www.revolut.com/news/revolut_receives_french_banking_licence/), deposits of French customers are now protected by the national guarantee scheme Fonds de garantie des dépôts et de résolution (FGDR). Protection has increased to 100,000 euros per depositor. The company also gained the right to offer mortgage loans and regulated savings products, including the Livret A and Livret de développement durable et solidaire (LDDS) accounts. Revolut had not previously offered these instruments in France.

Revolut's customer base in France numbers roughly 7-8 million users. The transfer of accounts to the new Revolut Bank S.A. structure will be gradual. According to reports, each customer will receive notice two months before their account is transferred, with the process itself starting in the coming months.

The Lithuanian entity Revolut Bank UAB will continue serving the rest of the European Economic Area — both structures remain under ECB supervision.

## What's Next

Revolut Bank S.A. will roll out full banking services in stages to other Western European countries — following France, Germany, Ireland, Italy, Spain and Portugal are next in line. The company also confirmed it will open a Western Europe headquarters in Paris in 2027. Revolut said it plans to invest more than 1 billion euros and hire more than 600 employees in the region.
