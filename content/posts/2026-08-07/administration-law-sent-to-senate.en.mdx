The Legislative Chamber of the Oliy Majlis of Uzbekistan adopted the draft constitutional law "On the Administration of the President of the Republic of Uzbekistan" in its third reading on August 6 and sent the document to the Senate for consideration.

The chamber adopted the bill in its first reading on August 4 — [LEAP News reported](https://leap.uz/en/2026/08/04/president-administration-law-first-reading) that the document elevates the status of the Presidential Administration from a decree to a standalone law.

## What the Bill Establishes

The document [establishes](https://t.me/qonunchilikpalatasi/37175) the legal status of the Presidential Administration within the system of government, its main objectives, the organization of its work and its powers.

According to the [text of the bill](https://t.me/qonunchilikpalatasi/37176), the Administration is formed by the president of Uzbekistan and led by a head, whom the president appoints and dismisses. The bill also defines the Administration's structure, the principles of its work, the procedure for interacting with other government bodies and organizations, and guarantees for officials, managers and rank-and-file staff.

The bill separately [sets out](https://t.me/qonunchilikpalatasi/37180) mechanisms for the Administration's interaction with compliance units and internal anti-corruption control units of government bodies.

## Discussion and International Experience

In drafting the document, lawmakers [studied](https://t.me/qonunchilikpalatasi/37180) the legislation of the United States, Finland, the Czech Republic, Slovakia, Romania, Latvia, Taiwan, Kazakhstan and Turkmenistan, where the legal status of the presidential administration (chancellery, office) is likewise defined by separate laws.

The bill was discussed in the factions of all political parties and in the chamber's relevant committee with the participation of legal scholars and industry experts. Proposals received were taken into account in preparing the bill for its second reading.

After discussion, lawmakers [adopted](https://t.me/qonunchilikpalatasi/37181) the bill in its third reading and sent it to the Senate of the Oliy Majlis. To take effect, the bill must be approved by the Senate, after which it is signed by the president.
