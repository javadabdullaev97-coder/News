The Prosecutor General's Office [said](https://t.me/uzbprokuratura/87790) on August 7 that it has begun preparing investigators for the transfer of sexual offense cases under its jurisdiction — a provision of Presidential Decree No. UP-33 that takes effect on November 1.

## Seminar in Andijan and a New Manual

The Prosecutor's Office of Andijan Region held a training seminar for investigators, judges, forensic psychologists, researchers and experienced prosecutors. Participants [discussed](https://t.me/uzbprokuratura/87790) the specifics of investigating sexual offenses, according to the publication on the Prosecutor General's Office channel. The seminar was led by A. Imomnazarov, head of the Prosecutor General's Office department overseeing the activities of investigative units within the prosecutor's office.

The Prosecutor General's Office has developed a training manual on the specifics of investigating sexual offenses and will provide it to all investigators, the publication says. The exact date of the seminar and the number of participants are not specified in the publication.

## What Is Already Known About the Transfer of Authority

Starting November 1, 2026, pre-investigation checks and preliminary investigations into rape and other sexual offenses, including crimes against minors, will be handled exclusively by the prosecutor's office. This provision is set out in [clause 14 of Presidential Decree No. UP-33 of March 3, 2026](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/8070196), "On Additional Organizational and Legal Measures to Strengthen the Protection of the Rights of Women and Children and Prevent Harassment and Violence Against Them." LEAP News covered the decree in detail on [August 6](https://leap.uz/en/2026/08/06/prosecutor-sex-crimes-investigation-authority-nov2026).

The decree, [signed by the president on March 3](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/8982) and taking effect the next day, is described in the Prosecutor General's Office publication as an "entirely new system" for protecting women and children from harassment and violence. Besides transferring authority to the prosecutor's office, the document introduces an app with an emergency call function for women with protective orders and mandatory psychiatric examination by decision of the Ministry of Internal Affairs (MIA). It also provides for special training of investigators and judges starting in September 2026 — details on all these measures are in LEAP News' August 6 report.

## What's Next

Starting November 1, 2026, the prosecutor's office gains exclusive authority to investigate sexual offenses. Starting January 1, 2027, cases of harassment and violence against women and children should generally be handled by investigators and judges who have completed special training — this is set out in clause 16 of Decree No. UP-33. The seminar in Andijan Region and the manual prepared for it are part of this training, which, according to the Prosecutor General's Office, will continue until the provision takes effect.
