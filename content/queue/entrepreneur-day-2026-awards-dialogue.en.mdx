President Shavkat Mirziyoyev on August 13, ahead of Entrepreneur's Day, [signed a decree](https://www.president.uz/ru/lists/view/9494) awarding a group of business representatives and a [directive](https://www.president.uz/ru/lists/view/9495) presenting the 'Faol tadbirkor' badge to more than 100 entrepreneurs.

The same day, the president [was briefed](https://www.president.uz/ru/lists/view/9493) on preparations for the sixth open dialogue with business circles.

## State awards and honorary titles

The [presidential decree](https://www.president.uz/ru/lists/view/9494) honors representatives of business, industry, construction and agriculture with state awards. The decree cites "significant results in developing the country and boosting its export potential" and initiative shown in entrepreneurial activity.

Odiljon Boltaev, director for development and investment at "Azia metall prof" LLC, received the honorary title "Honored Entrepreneur of the Republic of Uzbekistan." Bakhtiyor Khamraev, director of "Gidro stanko servis" LLC, was awarded the title "Honored Industrial Worker." The title "Honored Builder" went to Mukhsinjon Isakov, general director of a construction company, and Yorkin Babakhanov, founder of "Central Asian construction" LLC. Bakhtiyor Akhmedov, head of a farm, received the title "Honored Agricultural Worker."

Besides the honorary titles, the decree distributes among entrepreneurs the orders "Fidokorona Khizmatlari Uchun" (For Devoted Service), "Mehnat Shuhrati" (Labor Glory), "Do'stlik" (Friendship), "Mardlik" (Courage) and "Salomatlik" (Health), 2nd degree, and the medals "Sog'lom Turmush" (Healthy Lifestyle), "Jasorat" (Bravery), "Kelajak Bunyodkori" (Builder of the Future) and "Shuhrat" (Glory) — according to the presidential website, the Order of Do'stlik went to about 15 people and the Shuhrat medal to about 20.

## 'Faol tadbirkor' badge

By a separate [directive](https://www.president.uz/ru/lists/view/9495), the president awarded the "Faol tadbirkor" ("Active Entrepreneur") badge to more than 100 business representatives from all regions of the country. The badge recognizes contributions to developing the national economy, improving the business climate, introducing innovative technologies, creating jobs and active participation in social life. It was established by a [Cabinet of Ministers resolution](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/6163353) in August 2022 and is awarded annually around August 20, Entrepreneur's Day.

The recipients represent textile manufacturing, agriculture, tourism, technology, education and services.

## Preparations for sixth open dialogue with business

The president [was briefed](https://www.president.uz/ru/lists/view/9493) on preparations for the upcoming sixth open dialogue with entrepreneurs. Since May 15, a republican headquarters made up of representatives from more than 50 ministries and agencies has been operating — it receives business inquiries around the clock through a call center and official bots.

About 6,000 inquiries have come in through these channels. More than 4,200 of them have already been resolved positively, and nearly 1,800 are still under review.

The president instructed officials to conduct a detailed analysis of the inquiries and the results of a survey of entrepreneurs by region and industry, and to prepare proposals for improving enterprise efficiency and introducing innovations and energy-saving technologies. He also ordered officials to address lending and infrastructure issues, improve tax administration and strengthen legal protection for business. For each issue, officials were instructed to define a specific solution, an implementation mechanism, those responsible and deadlines.

The presidential press service's statement did not specify a date for the open dialogue itself.
