The Tashkent City Prosecutor's Office said on August 4 it had opened a criminal case against officials of the developer of the New Port residential complex in Yakkasaray district — the residents' house was demolished before their forced eviction was completed.

## What the Investigation Found

According to the prosecutor's office, officials of "Mega Logistics Servise" LLC [unlawfully demolished](https://t.me/poytaxt_uz/51254) a residential building on A. Qahhor Street in the Damarik mahalla of Yakkasaray district. The house was torn down even though enforcement proceedings for the residents' forced eviction [had not been completed](https://t.me/toshkentprokuraturasi/132693).

On the site of the demolished house, the company [built](https://t.me/poytaxt_uz/51254) five multi-story blocks of the New Port residential complex without permit documentation: one 20-story building, two 19-story buildings, one 12-story building and one 2-story building.

## Residents' Complaints and Unpaid Compensation

The inspection was prompted by a social media post from local resident R. Payanov and other residents, who [said](https://t.me/toshkentprokuraturasi/132693) they had not been paid full compensation for the demolished house. The prosecutor's office confirmed that compensation payments to residents were not made in full. The agency did not disclose the exact compensation amount or the number of affected families.

The prosecutor's office's statements did not specify which articles of the Criminal Code the case was opened under. According to investigators, the case involves the unauthorized demolition of housing and illegal construction. The prosecutor's office also did not clarify the procedural status of the company's officials — whether they are charged as defendants or suspects. The case has [been placed](https://t.me/poytaxt_uz/51254) under special oversight, with final decisions to be made once the investigation concludes.

## Prosecutor General's Office Announces Tighter Construction Oversight

The same day, the Prosecutor General's Office [published](https://t.me/toshkentprokuraturasi/132695) a separate announcement about stepping up oversight measures in Tashkent's construction sector, without naming any specific projects, including New Port.

The New Port case is not the first trigger for inspections of unauthorized construction in the city in recent days. On July 31, Saida Mirziyoyeva, head of the Presidential Administration of the Republic of Uzbekistan, [instructed](https://t.me/SShMirziyoyeva/4148) the Prosecutor General's Office to carry out an inventory of construction on courtyards and public spaces across Tashkent — after visiting the Lolazor mahalla, where trees and a children's playground were slated for demolition to make way for a business center. New Port in Yakkasaray is a separate, standalone case: it does not fall under the courtyard inventory, as it involves a demolished residential house and commercial residential construction built on its site.

## What's Next

The Tashkent Prosecutor's Office did not give a timeline for completing the investigation and did not say whether officials of "Mega Logistics Servise" have been formally charged. The agency has not yet provided information on whether the New Port blocks built without permits will be demolished.
