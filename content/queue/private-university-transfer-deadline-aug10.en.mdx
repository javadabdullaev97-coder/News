The Ministry of Higher Education, Science and Innovation of Uzbekistan extended, on August 6, the
deadline for transfer and reinstatement applications at non-state universities — to August 10,
2026 inclusive. This is [stated in a post](https://t.me/eduuz/50135) on the ministry's Telegram
channel.

## Where and how to apply

According to the text of the post, the extended deadline covers three types of applications:
transfer of students to non-state universities (including on other grounds), transfer within a
single non-state university, and reinstatement for study.

Transfer and reinstatement between state and non-state universities within the country is
processed through the `transfer.edu.uz` platform. Transfer from foreign universities is processed
through the `my.uzbmb.uz` platform. Both platforms are listed [in the ministry's
message](https://t.me/eduuz/50135) as the way to submit documents.

## The deadline is firm

Applications for the 2026/2027 academic year are accepted through August 10, 2026 inclusive.
The ministry will not consider applications submitted later — this is stated explicitly in the
text of the post.

The ministry's message does not specify the date from which the previous deadline had been in
effect.
