Aleksandr Zverev 18-avgust kuni fransuz Terence Atmaneni 7:6(4), 7:6(6) hisobida mag'lub etib, Sinsinnatidagi ATP Masters 1000 turnirining 1/8 finaliga chiqdi, [xabar qildi](https://www.aljazeera.com/sports/2026/8/18/cincinnati-open-williams-sisters-fall-in-doubles-while-zverev-advances) Al Jazeera. Bu nemis tennischisining mavsumdagi 46-g'alabasi.

Zverev — turnirning birinchi raketkasi. U Sinsinnatiga notekis yoz fonida kelmoqda. 7-iyun kuni nemis tennischisi [birinchi Grand Slem unvonini qo'lga kiritdi](https://www.rolandgarros.com/en-us/article/2026-edition-zverev-cobolli-final-report) — Roland Garrosda besh setlik finalda italiyalik Flavio Kobollini 6:1, 4:6, 6:4, 6:7(5), 6:1 hisobida mag'lub etdi. 12-iyul kuni u Uimbldon finaliga chiqdi, biroq Jannik Sinnerga [yutqazdi](https://www.atptour.com/en/news/sinner-zverev-wimbledon-2026-final-sunday). 5-avgust kuni esa Monrealda, National Bank Open turnirida, Zverev ikkinchi bosqichda gollandiyalik Tallon Griekspoorga kutilmaganda [yutqazdi](https://www.atptour.com/en/news/griekspoor-zverev-montreal-2026-wednesday) — 6:7(3), 6:2, 6:4.

Sinsinnatida turnirning birinchi raketkasi Kemeron Norri ustidan 3:6, 6:3, 6:3 hisobida g'alaba qozonish bilan boshladi, so'ngra Terence Atmaneni mag'lub etdi. «U juda tajovuzkor o'ynadi, forhendlari kortning har tomoniga uchib borardi, shu sababli o'zimga ham tajovuzkor o'ynash qiyin bo'ldi», — dedi Zverev uchrashuvdan so'ng. Atmane 2025-yilgi Sinsinnati yarim finalchisi edi.

## Swiatek unvonini himoya qilmoqda

Iga Swiatek 17-avgust kuni Mariya Sakkarini 4:6, 6:1, 6:1 hisobida mag'lub etib, 1/8 finalga chiqdi, [xabar qiladi](https://www.wtatennis.com/news/4561453/from-a-set-down-swiatek-wins-12-of-last-14-games-vs-sakkari-in-cincinnati) WTA Tennis. Ettinchi raqamli saralangan polshalik birinchi setni yutqazgan bo'lsa-da, keyingi 14 geymning 12 tasini o'z foydasiga hal qildi.

U chempionlik unvonini himoya qilmoqda: 2025-yilda Swiatek finalda Jasmin Paoliniga qarshi 7:5, 6:4 hisobida g'alaba qozonib, Sinsinnatida birinchi marta [chempion bo'lgan edi](https://cincinnatiopen.com/news/its-right-back-to-work-for-defending-champ-swiatek/). O'shanda u ochiq era davrida bu turnir chempioni bo'lgan birinchi polshalik ayol bo'ldi. Bu mavsumda u Torontoda National Bank Open'da g'alaba qozongandan so'ng darhol Sinsinnatiga keldi — finalda Elena Rybakinani 6:2, 6:3 hisobida mag'lub etgan edi. Bu uning 2026-yildagi birinchi WTA 1000 darajasidagi unvoni. Torontodan turnir o'tkazilayotgan Meysonga parvoz bir sutkadan kam vaqt oldi. «Biz shunchaki yig'ilib, aeroportga bordik — mana, men shu yerdaman», — dedi Swiatek.

Keyingi bosqichda Swiatek fransuz tennischisi Dian Parri bilan uchrashadi.

Sinsinnati — ATP Masters 1000 va WTA 1000 turnirlari birgalikda o'tkaziladigan musobaqa bo'lib, [11-23-avgust kunlari](https://cincinnatiopen.com/news/cincinnati-open-releases-2026-schedule/) Ogayo shtatining Meyson shahrida hardda bo'lib o'tmoqda. Ikkala turnir finali 23-avgust, yakshanba kuni o'ynaladi.
