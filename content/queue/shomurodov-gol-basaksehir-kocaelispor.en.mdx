Istanbul Basaksehir [beat](https://www.trtspor.com.tr/haber/futbol/basaksehir-3-puani-2-golle-aldi-32653535) Kocaelispor 2-0 in the opening round of the 2026/27 Süper Lig season — Uzbekistan national team forward Eldor Shomurodov scored one of the goals in the 72nd minute on August 16.

Defender Christopher Operi opened the scoring in the 14th minute. He [scored](https://www.hurriyet.com.tr/sporarena/basaksehir-2-0-kocaelispor-super-lig-43274931) after a through pass behind the defense from teammate Abbosbek Fayzullaev, the second Uzbekistani in Basaksehir's squad. In the 27th minute, a goal by Emin Bayram was disallowed after a VAR review: the referee [ruled](https://www.dha.com.tr/spor/istanbul-basaksehir-kocaelispor-2-0-2928277) there had been a foul on a visiting defender before the goal.

Shomurodov set the final score in the 72nd minute, converting a cross from the flank with a close-range shot. The match at Fatih Terim Stadium ended in a home win. Kocaelispor started the season without points.

Shomurodov joined Basaksehir from Roma on loan with an [option to buy](https://www.asroma.com/en/news/73493/eldor-shomurodov-joins-istanbul-basaksehir) the contract in summer 2025. Fayzullaev [signed](https://www.sporx.com/futbol/superlig/istanbulbb/abbosbek-fayzullaev-basaksehir-de-SXHBQ1126436SXQ) a five-year contract with the club on July 30, 2025, moving from CSKA Moscow. Last season, Shomurodov [shared](https://www.mynet.com/super-lig-in-gol-krali-belli-oldu-onuachu-ve-shomurodov-zirvede-502221-myspor) the Süper Lig's top scorer title with Nigerian forward Paul Onuachu — both finished with 22 goals for the season.
