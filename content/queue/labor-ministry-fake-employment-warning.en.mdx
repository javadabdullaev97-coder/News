Uzbekistan's Ministry of Employment and Poverty Reduction warned on August 5 that if an employer registers a worker as employed in the Unified National Labor System without their consent, it risks the loss of benefits and a distorted pension work record.

## What this looks like in practice

In a [post on its Telegram channel](https://t.me/mehnatvazirligi/20209), the ministry describes a specific case: a person is registered as an employee at an LLC where they have never worked, without their knowledge. On the surface this may look harmless — a person might assume they are getting work record "for free" and that money is going into the pension fund.

The ministry calls this a misconception and lists the real consequences of such a record:

- problems registering as unemployed;
- effects on receiving social benefits and allowances;
- discrepancies in tax and income data;
- confusion over work record and pension contributions.

The ministry separately stresses that an "employed" record in the system does not by itself mean that pension fund contributions are being paid for the person or that real work record is being credited to them.

## Where to turn if data was entered without consent

In an [earlier clarification](https://t.me/mehnatvazirligi/20187), the ministry answers a reader's question after their employment record turned out to be unlawfully registered at an organization where they never worked. There are several channels to reach out to:

- the ministry's hotline — (71) 200-06-00;
- the call center of the Unified National Labor System — (71) 200-70-30;
- the Telegram bot `@mehnat_daftari_yordamchi_bot`;
- the State Labor Inspectorate.

The legal basis for removing a fake record is [Cabinet of Ministers Resolution No. 971](https://lex.uz/docs/4630025) of December 5, 2019, "On organizational measures to ensure the implementation and operation of the interagency hardware-and-software complex 'Unified National Labor System.'" According to the ministry's post, data on unlawfully registered employment is deleted from the system based on the finding of a state labor inspector.

Individuals can check their own employment data in their personal account on the [my.mehnat.uz](https://my.mehnat.uz) portal.
