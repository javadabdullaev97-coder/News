The National Agency for Perspective Projects (NAPP) on August 20 [reported](https://napp.uz/uz/news/elektron-tijorat-operatorlari-faoliyatini-xabardor-qilish-tartibida-amalga-oshirish-qoidalari-tasdiqlandi) that it had approved rules requiring e-commerce operators in Uzbekistan to notify the agency when they start operating. The requirement takes effect September 23–24, 2026: the date differs between the Russian- and Uzbek-language versions of NAPP's announcement, and the agency has not explained the discrepancy.

The rules were approved by [NAPP director's order No. 24 of August 5, 2026](https://t.me/napp_uz/2406). The notification requirement itself for e-commerce operators has been in effect since July 1, 2025. It was introduced by [Presidential Resolution PP-885](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/7282643) of December 26, 2024, which required such activity to be conducted only through a resident legal entity of Uzbekistan. The new NAPP order describes the specific mechanism for filing a notification, which had been missing for more than a year.

## Who It Affects

The rules apply to legal entities that start or stop operating as e-commerce operators. The requirement to set up a local company had already applied to them since July 2025 under PP-885; the new document regulates the procedure itself for notifying NAPP about it.

## How to Submit the Notification

The notification is submitted through the agency's information system using the OneID unified identification system, according to [a post on NAPP's Telegram channel](https://t.me/napp_uz/2406). It must include the legal entity's name, address, bank details and information about its head.

The operator must inform NAPP of any change in this information within 10 working days.

## When It Takes Effect

According to NAPP's Russian-language publication, the regulation takes effect on September 24, 2026. The Uzbek-language version of the same message states September 23 — a one-day discrepancy that the agency has not explained.
