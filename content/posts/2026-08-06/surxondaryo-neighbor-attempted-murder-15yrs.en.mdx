The Supreme Court of Uzbekistan's Telegram channel said on August 5 that a court in Surkhandarya region sentenced a man to 15 years in prison for the attempted murder of his neighbor, without disclosing further details of the case.

## What the Supreme Court said

The [post](https://t.me/oliysuduz/51348) on the official Telegram channel of the Supreme Court of Uzbekistan says the man was found guilty of attempting to murder his neighbor and was sentenced to 15 years in prison. The exact publication date of the post is not displayed — only a "11:06" timestamp is visible; the date "August 5" was determined by the post's position in the channel's feed at the time of verification. The post does not name the convicted man or the victim, the exact district or locality in Surkhandarya region, or the circumstances and motive of the attempted murder.

## What remains undisclosed

The Supreme Court's channel does not specify which court instance issued the sentence — a district, inter-district, or regional criminal court — or under which article of the Criminal Code the act was classified. It also does not report whether the sentence has taken legal effect or can be appealed.
