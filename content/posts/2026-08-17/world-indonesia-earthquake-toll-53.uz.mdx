Indoneziyaning Flores orolida yuz bergan zilziladan halok bo'lganlar soni 16-avgust kechqurungi va 17-avgust tongi holatiga ko'ra kamida 53 kishiga yetdi, bu haqda Indoneziya Milliy ofatlarni boshqarish agentligi (BNPB) [ma'lum qildi](https://www.antaranews.com/berita/5697401/bnpb-umumkan-total-korban-meninggal-dunia-gempa-ntt-53-jiwa).

LEAP News avvalroq [xabar bergan edi](https://leap.uz/ru/2026/08/15/flores-earthquake-toll-38) — 15-avgust kuni kechqurun BNPB qurbonlar sonini 38 kishi deb baholagan edi, o'shandan buyon bu son yana 15 kishiga oshdi.

Eng ko'p qurbon Manggarai okrugida qayd etildi — 25 kishi, yana 20 kishi Sharqiy Manggaraida halok bo'ldi, [deb ma'lum qiladi](https://www.antaranews.com/berita/5697401/bnpb-umumkan-total-korban-meninggal-dunia-gempa-ntt-53-jiwa) BNPB. Sikka, Ende, G'arbiy Manggarai va Nagekeo okruglarida yana sakkiz kishi halok bo'ldi. 135 kishi jarohat oldi, [deb xabar beradi](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/rescuers-in-indonesia-recover-more-bodies-in-earthquake-aftermath-raising-death-toll-to-53) AP agentligi. 7,7 magnitudali zilzila shanba, 15-avgust kuni erta tongda Floresning shimoliy sohilida, Sharqiy Nusa-Tenggara provinsiyasida yuz berdi.

## Mustaqillik kuniga yordam

Indoneziya hukumati zarar ko'rgan hududlarga yuborilgan gumanitar yordam umumiy hajmini 52 tonnaga yetkazdi, [deb ma'lum qildi](https://en.antaranews.com/news/427375/govt-sends-52-tons-of-aid-to-quake-hit-areas-of-e-nusa-tenggara) Vazirlar mahkamasi kotibi Teddi Indra Vijaya. So'nggi partiya 25 tonnani tashkil etdi va unga 5 tonna tayyor oziq-ovqat, 4 tonna ichimlik suvi va 16 tonna don mahsulotlari kirdi. Yuk Labuan-Bajo, Maumere va yana olti okrugga [yetib keldi](https://en.antaranews.com/news/427375/govt-sends-52-tons-of-aid-to-quake-hit-areas-of-e-nusa-tenggara). Zarar ko'rgan hududlar uchun dori-darmon va chodirlar alohida partiya sifatida yuborilgan bo'lib, ushbu partiya og'irligiga kirmaydi, [deb xabar beradi](https://en.antaranews.com/news/427375/govt-sends-52-tons-of-aid-to-quake-hit-areas-of-e-nusa-tenggara) Antara News. Alohida, Indoneziya harbiy-havo kuchlari Kupangdagi El-Tari aviabazasidan ikkita Hercules va bitta Boeing samolyotida 13 tonna yuk tashidi, [deb xabar beradi](https://en.antaranews.com/news/427349/ntt-quake-indonesia-air-force-airlifts-13-tons-of-emergency-aid) Antara News.

Gumanitar operatsiya Indoneziyaning 81 yillik mustaqillik bayrami — 17-avgust nishonlanishi bilan bir vaqtga to'g'ri keldi. Jakartada tantanalar davom etayotgan bir paytda zarar ko'rgan hududlar aholisi yordam yetkazishning kechikishidan shikoyat qildi, [deb xabar beradi](https://www.euronews.com/2026/08/17/thousands-in-indonesia-await-aid-after-quake-kills-53-overshadowing-independence-day) Euronews.

## Aftershoklar davom etmoqda

Asosiy zilziladan buyon seysmologlar 995 ta aftershokni qayd etdi, ulardan 46 tasini aholi his qildi, [deb xabar beradi](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/rescuers-in-indonesia-recover-more-bodies-in-earthquake-aftermath-raising-death-toll-to-53) AP. Dushanba, 17-avgust kuni Flores yaqinida 5,9 magnitudali qayta zilzila yuz berdi, [deb xabar beradi](https://www.scmp.com/news/asia/southeast-asia/article/3364225/magnitude-59-aftershock-strikes-indonesias-flores-quake-death-toll-rises-53) SCMP.

Qutqaruvchilar hozircha Manggarai, Sharqiy Manggarai va Nagekeoning bir qator qiyin yetib boriladigan hududlariga yetib bora olmayapti — ko'chkilar yo'llarni to'sib qo'ygan. U yerda qidiruv ishlari davom etmoqda, darak-nishonsiz yo'qolganlar haqida hozircha xabar yo'q, [deb xabar beradi](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/rescuers-in-indonesia-recover-more-bodies-in-earthquake-aftermath-raising-death-toll-to-53) AP. 17-avgust tongiga kelib evakuatsiya qilinganlar soni deyarli 13 mingga yetdi, deb xabar beradi Euronews. Xuddi shunday 7,7 magnitudali zilzila Floresga 1992-yilda ham tushgan edi — o'shanda u va undan kelib chiqqan tsunami taxminan 2&nbsp;500 kishining hayotiga zomin bo'lgan edi.
