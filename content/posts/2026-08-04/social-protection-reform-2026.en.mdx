President Shavkat Mirziyoyev on August 3 [reviewed a presentation of proposals](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9483) for reforming the social protection system — covering children with disabilities, homeless people, human trafficking victims and discounted travel.

## Discounted Travel to Move to a Mobile App

Starting January 1, 2027, discounted air, rail and intercity bus tickets will be available online through the "Baraka" social card mobile app, [according to a report on the president's website](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9483). Paper coupons will be abolished: to receive a discount, it will be enough to present an ID card or biometric passport, with eligibility checked through the "Unified National Social Protection" information system.

The new procedure applies to citizens in 11 categories, who are entitled to either free travel or a 50% discount on air, rail and intercity transport. The process for using benefits will be simplified separately for people with disabilities and veterans of the 1941–1945 war, people with equivalent status, and participants in the cleanup of the Chernobyl nuclear disaster. More than 48,400 citizens currently qualify for these benefits, and they used discounted travel 1.7 million times in 2025.

## Rehabilitation Centers for Homeless People to Become Reintegration Centers

Uzbekistan operates 11 rehabilitation centers with a total of 726 beds — 1,100 people were placed in them in the first half of 2026, [according to the presentation](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9483). These institutions will be transformed into temporary accommodation and social reintegration centers.

Homeless people will be able to apply on their own, be referred there through the work of the mahalla (neighborhood community) "seven" group, or be brought in with the help of a social worker or prevention inspector. An individual plan will be developed for each person, covering document restoration, medical and social assistance, vocational training and employment. They are also planned to receive vocational training vouchers, be included among socially vulnerable population categories, and have jobs reserved for them. Employment assistance units will open at the centers, and vacant premises will be leased to entrepreneurs on preferential terms.

## Human Trafficking Victims to Be Identified at the Mahalla Level

Starting September 1, 2026, a system for assessing the situation of human trafficking victims will begin operating through "Inson" centers at the mahalla and district level. Such people will then be referred to specialized services, [according to the president's website](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9483).

The Republican Center for the Rehabilitation and Adaptation of Women will be renamed the Republican Center for the Rehabilitation of Women and Children, with its territorial branches renamed accordingly. Centers in Karakalpakstan and Kashkadarya, Samarkand and Fergana regions will begin admitting women who are trafficking victims along with their minor children — 40 additional beds will be created there.

It was also proposed to introduce separate criminal liability for child trafficking and to restrict people convicted of human trafficking from working in labor and external migration. Regular monitoring of the services provided was also proposed.

## Children With Disabilities to Move to Individual Education Plans

Uzbekistan is home to more than 168,000 children with disabilities, of whom 133,800 are covered by educational and social services. Another 34,600 still need to be brought into the education system, [according to the presentation](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9483).

To achieve this, a system of targeted work with each family will be introduced with the involvement of social workers. A child's needs will be assessed comprehensively and at an early stage, after which a suitable educational environment and services will be selected. The new assessment mechanism will launch as a pilot on October 1, 2026, in Khorezm and Navoi regions.

Starting with the 2026/2027 school year, instruction at special schools will be organized on the basis of individual plans that take into account each child's needs and functional abilities. Blind teachers and primary classes at special schools will be given personal assistants and aides. Private schools that open special education classes near where children live will receive subsidies for each student. Annual initiatives by at least 50 schools to improve inclusive environments will be funded on a grant basis.

Starting in 2027, it has been proposed to provide children with text-reading devices, speech synthesizers, Braille displays and other assistive tools on a voucher basis. Starting with the 2027/2028 school year, people with disabilities will be given extra time, Braille materials, sign language interpretation and assistant services during entrance and certification exams.

## What's Next

The president approved the proposals presented and instructed officials to carefully develop the relevant documents, ensure quality delivery of the new services at the local level, and maintain strict oversight of implementation, [according to the report](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9483). For now, these remain approved proposals rather than a signed decree: no timeline for adopting the final regulatory acts was given.

Over the past three years, the number of types of social services in the country has grown from 23 to 140. On average, 4.6 million such services are provided annually, about 2 million of them at the mahalla level.
