World No. 1 Aryna Sabalenka lost to Czech player Sara Bejlek in the fourth round of the WTA 1000 (Women's Tennis Association) tournament in Cincinnati on August 19 — 6:7 (7:9), 4:6. Bejlek is ranked 35th in the [WTA rankings](https://www.wtatennis.com/rankings/singles).

Bejlek led 5:1 in the first-set tiebreak, but Sabalenka narrowed the gap to 5:5. The Czech converted her third set point to take the set 7:6 (9:7), [the WTA reports](https://www.wtatennis.com/news/4563121/bejlek-stuns-sabalenka-in-straight-sets-to-reach-cincinnati-quarterfinals). In the second set, Sabalenka led 4:1 in games, but Bejlek won five straight games to close out the match 6:4, [Tennis Majors reports](https://www.tennismajors.com/wta-tour-news/sabalenkas-shock-exit-against-beljek-leaves-world-no-1-in-limbo-as-rybakina-eyes-the-top-spot-in-cincinnati-858912.html).

"I feel incredible. I can't find the words. I just kept believing — that was the main thing today," Bejlek said after the match, [the WTA reports](https://www.wtatennis.com/news/4563121/bejlek-stuns-sabalenka-in-straight-sets-to-reach-cincinnati-quarterfinals). For the 20-year-old, it was her first win over a top-10 player and her first quarterfinal appearance at a WTA 1000 event.

The men's top seed, Alexander Zverev, was also eliminated in the same round. The German lost to American Tommy Paul — 6:4, 6:7, 4:6, [Sky Sports reports](https://www.skysports.com/tennis/news/12040/13574886/cincinnati-open-top-seeds-aryna-sabalenka-and-alexander-zverev-stunned-in-last-16-as-coco-gauff-progresses-to-quarter-finals).

Sabalenka's defeat opens a path for Elena Rybakina to reach world No. 1, [Sky Sports reports](https://www.skysports.com/tennis/news/12040/13574886/cincinnati-open-top-seeds-aryna-sabalenka-and-alexander-zverev-stunned-in-last-16-as-coco-gauff-progresses-to-quarter-finals). The Kazakhstani player will overtake her rival if she reaches the final in Cincinnati. Going into the tournament, Sabalenka led the [WTA rankings](https://www.wtatennis.com/rankings/singles) with 8,670 points, while Rybakina had 8,316.

In the quarterfinals, Bejlek will face Madison Keys, while Rybakina takes on Iga Swiatek, [Tennis365 reports](https://www.tennis365.com/tennis-features/wta-rankings-how-aryna-sabalenkas-cincinnati-exit-affects-no-1-battle-elena-rybakina). Sabalenka is the reigning two-time US Open champion and will need to defend 2,000 ranking points there, [Ubitennis writes](https://www.ubitennis.net/2026/08/aryna-sabalenka-in-danger-of-losing-no-1-status-after-shock-cincinnati-exit/).

This is the third straight WTA tournament in which Sabalenka has failed to reach the quarterfinals. Before Cincinnati, she lost to Naomi Osaka in the fourth round at Wimbledon and to Ekaterina Alexandrova in the round of 16 in Toronto. Such a streak for a reigning world No. 1 hasn't happened since 2019, [Sports.ru reports](https://www.sports.ru/tennis/1117340497-vpervye-s-2019-go-pervaya-raketka-mira-ne-smogla-vyjti-v-1-4-finala-na.html). Back then, a similar run was experienced by Osaka herself — at tournaments in Dubai, Indian Wells and Miami.
