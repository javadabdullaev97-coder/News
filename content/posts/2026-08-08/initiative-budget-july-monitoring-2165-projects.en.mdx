The Ministry of Economy and Finance of Uzbekistan [checked](https://t.me/minecofinuz/16198) the progress of works at 2,165 sites under construction within the Initiative Budget program in July 2026 and reported that another 257 sites had been put into operation.

## What the monitoring found

Ministry specialists conducted on-site mobile inspections at 2,165 sites where, as of the end of July, construction and repair work under Initiative Budget ("Tashabbusli byudjet") projects was still ongoing, the Ministry of Economy and Finance's channel [said in a post](https://t.me/minecofinuz/16198). The ministry separately clarified that, among winning projects from past seasons, 257 sites had fully completed work and were put into operation during July.

The results of each inspection — the status of the site at the time of the visit and photographs — are available on the page of the specific project on the [openbudget.uz](https://openbudget.uz/mobile-monitoring/list/53) portal, in the "Mobile Monitoring" section.

## What has already been built

In a separate [post](https://t.me/minecofinuz/16216), the Ministry of Economy and Finance gave examples of sites where work is underway or has been completed in the regions:

- school No. 15 in Urgench (Khorezm region) received 97 types of educational equipment worth 585 million soums (about $49,100 at the Central Bank's exchange rate for August 7 — 11,915.64 soums per dollar);
- in the Dehkanabad district of Kashkadarya region, 2.89 km of road between the Beshkuton mahalla and the district center was paved;
- in Chust (Namangan region), a gym opened in the Bogormos mahalla;
- in the Ellikkala district of Karakalpakstan, Mustaqillik and Beshtom streets were paved;
- in the Shofirkon district of Bukhara region, paving of 21.4 km of internal roads is planned; work is currently underway on a 3.2 km section in the Pattakhon mahalla;
- in the Mirzachul district of Jizzakh region, an infrastructure project is being implemented at school No. 18 — a winning project from the second season of 2025, whose construction is continuing into 2026.

## How the Initiative Budget works

The Initiative Budget is a participatory budgeting program: residents propose projects to solve local problems (road repairs, schools, street lighting, sports and social facilities), vote for them on the openbudget.uz portal, and selected projects are funded from the budget. The program's legal basis is the [presidential resolution](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/6429855) "On measures to further improve the practice of initiative budgeting and support active mahallas" (PP-117 of April 10, 2023), which developed the mechanism launched by the presidential resolution "On measures to ensure openness of budget data and active citizen participation in the budget process" ([PP-3917](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/3879207) of August 22, 2018). The Ministry of Economy and Finance operates the program.

The July monitoring covers sites from seasons already selected — a separate process from the selection of applications for the second season of 2026: the intake of proposals for it closed on August 3 with a result of 23,551 applications, and from August 4 to 18 the submitted projects are going through the [selection stage](https://leap.uz/en/2026/08/04/tashabbusli-byudjet-2026-otbor-nachalsya).

## What's next

Construction and repair work on winning projects from past seasons continues, and the ministry says it will keep conducting on-site monitoring, publishing statuses and photos on openbudget.uz. At the time of publication, the Ministry of Economy and Finance had not named a date for announcing the winners of the second season 2026 selection, which will be followed by voting.
