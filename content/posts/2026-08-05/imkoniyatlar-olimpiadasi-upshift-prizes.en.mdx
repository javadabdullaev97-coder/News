The Ministry of Justice of Uzbekistan announced on August 5 that winners of the "Imkoniyatlar olimpiadasi" (Abilympics) contest for people with disabilities and the youth contest UPSHIFT will receive cash prizes of 15 to 30 base calculation values.

## What the resolution provides

According to the [Ministry of Justice's publication](https://t.me/huquqiyaxborot/20220), the document establishes that the two contests will be held on an annual basis.

"Imkoniyatlar olimpiadasi" (Abilympics) is a professional skills contest for people with disabilities held jointly with non-governmental non-profit organizations. In each contest category, the winner and runners-up will receive a one-time cash prize of 15 to 30 times the base calculation value (BCV).

"O'sish va rivojlanish" (UPSHIFT) is a contest for youth social initiatives. Its winners will receive a grant of the same size — 15 to 30 BCVs — as well as consulting and project support from volunteer mentors to help ensure their projects' sustainable development.

## The BCV amount has not yet been confirmed

The resolution does not specify the current base calculation value. Under [Presidential Decree PF-115-son](https://www.lex.uz/en/docs/8283656) of June 23, 2026, the BCV will rise to 440,000 soums starting September 1, 2026. The exact amount of the 15–30 BCV prizes and grants will depend on which value is applied at the time of payment.

## What the source does not specify

The Ministry of Justice's Telegram channel did not provide the resolution's number or full date, and did not specify from what year the payments will take effect or which agency will administer the contests. As of publication, the text of the document had not been found publicly available on lex.uz.
