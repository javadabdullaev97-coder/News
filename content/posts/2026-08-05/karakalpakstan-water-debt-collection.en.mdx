The Bureau of Forced Execution of Karakalpakstan has recovered 1.1 billion soums in water debt from debtors under 516 writs of execution since the start of 2026, the Prosecutor General's Office of Uzbekistan [reported on August 5](https://t.me/uzbprokuratura/87651).

## How much was recovered and under which cases

According to the Prosecutor General's Office, the Bureau recovered the funds based on court rulings in favor of the region's water utilities. Debtors under the 516 writs of execution included both individuals and legal entities. Converted at the exchange rate of the Central Bank of the Republic of Uzbekistan for August 5, 2026 (11,942.21 soums per dollar), the recovered amount is about $92,100.

## What awaits the remaining debtors

Under the remaining 124 unexecuted writs, the Bureau applied enforcement measures. In 45 cases, it garnished debtors' wages, and in 79 cases it restricted their right to leave Uzbekistan. [The Prosecutor General's Office clarified](https://t.me/uzbprokuratura/87651) that measures to collect water debts in the region are ongoing.
