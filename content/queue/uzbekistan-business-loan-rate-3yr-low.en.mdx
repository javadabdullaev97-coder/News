The interest rate on soum business loans in Uzbekistan fell to 21.8% per annum in June 2026 — the lowest level since October 2023, according to the [Central Bank of Uzbekistan](https://cbu.uz/ru/statistics/dks/4248563/).

## Loans cheaper for second consecutive month

The weighted average interest rate on all soum loans — for individuals and legal entities combined — stood at 21.6% per annum in June. That is 0.4 percentage points lower than in May (22%) and 1.4 percentage points lower than a year earlier, in June 2025, when the rate stood at 23%.

Business loans grew cheaper over the month, falling from 22% in May to 21.8% per annum — the lowest level in almost three years.

For loans in foreign currency, preliminary data show the average rate in June was around 10.3% per annum — almost unchanged over the month, according to the [Central Bank of Uzbekistan](https://cbu.uz/ru/statistics/dks/4248571/).

## Amid diverging trends in deposits

The decline in lending rates coincides with diverging trends in deposits. As [LEAP News reported on August 5](https://leap.uz/posts/2026-08-05/cb-sum-deposit-yield-rise), the average yield on soum deposits rose to 17.6% per annum in June from 17.1% in May — but this was driven mainly by business deposits and short-term deposits. Household deposit rates, meanwhile, continued to decline, falling to 19.2% per annum — the lowest level in recent months.

As a result, the gap in June between the cost of borrowing for business (21.8%) and the yield on corporate deposits (15.3%, according to the central bank) exceeded 6.5 percentage points.
