World Boxing Council (WBC) President Mauricio Sulaiman said on August 19 that the organization will approve the fight between Tyson Fury and Anthony Joshua as the final eliminator for the championship title. [Sports.ru reported](https://www.sports.ru/boxing/1117339067-sulejman-o-poedinke-fyuri-i-dzhoshua-wbc-odnoznachno-odobrit-etot-boj-.html) the statement.

"The WBC would absolutely approve that as a final eliminator... Tyson Fury is the No. 1 contender of the division," Sulaiman was quoted as saying by [MiddleEasy](https://middleeasy.com/boxing/anthony-joshua-tyson-fury-wbc-eliminator-agit-kabayel).

The organization's president called Fury "one of the greatest ambassadors of the WBC in history" and separately highlighted Joshua's standing: "Anthony Joshua, he was the first ranked by the WBC when he started boxing... Maybe this will be the time to bring Anthony Joshua to the WBC," [MiddleEasy reported](https://middleeasy.com/boxing/anthony-joshua-tyson-fury-wbc-eliminator-agit-kabayel).

If the WBC officially confirms the fight as an eliminator, the winner will face reigning WBC heavyweight champion Agit Kabayel for the title, [MiddleEasy reported](https://middleeasy.com/boxing/anthony-joshua-tyson-fury-wbc-eliminator-agit-kabayel).

Sulaiman's position doesn't answer the question of where and when the fight will take place — the bout itself has not yet been officially confirmed by either side. LEAP News [reported](https://leap.uz/en/2026/08/20/fury-joshua-venue-dispute-escalation) on August 20 that Joshua's promoter Eddie Hearn raised the possibility of a lawsuit against Zuffa Boxing head Dana White. The dispute concerns negotiations to move the fight to New York. Joshua and Hearn themselves insist on a venue in the United Kingdom, while talks over Madison Square Garden continue.
