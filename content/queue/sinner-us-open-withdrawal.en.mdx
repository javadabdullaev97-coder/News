Italy's Jannik Sinner, the world No. 1, officially withdrew from the 2026 US Open on August 21 — a right knee injury kept him from competing in New York, Sky Sports [reports](https://www.skysports.com/tennis/news/12040/13575443/jannik-sinner-world-no-1-withdraws-from-us-open-with-knee-injury-and-misses-out-on-final-grand-slam-of-the-season).

"We worked a lot with the team and the medical staff, but we made the difficult decision — I won't be able to play the US Open this year," Sinner's statement [reads](https://www.tennis365.com/tennis-news/jannik-sinner-speaks-us-open-2026-withdrawal). Tennis365 puts the statement at 106 words. "Over the last two days we trained in Monte Carlo and realized I need more time to recover my right knee," the player said. He added that he was "very sad and disappointed," since New York holds a special place in his heart.

Earlier the same day, LEAP News [reported](https://leap.uz/en/2026/08/21/sinner-us-open-uncertain) that Sinner's status ahead of the tournament remained unclear. The player had not taken the court since his Wimbledon title in July and skipped the Montreal and Cincinnati tournaments because of the same injury, Tennis Majors [notes](https://www.tennismajors.com/us-open-news/breaking-jannik-sinner-withdraws-from-2026-us-open-due-to-knee-injury-858970.html).

Sinner's withdrawal is the 15th among players pulling out of the 2026 US Open, and the fifth among men, Tennis365 [counts](https://www.tennis365.com/tennis-news/jannik-sinner-speaks-us-open-2026-withdrawal). Sinner is the defending 2024 US Open champion; after treatment, he plans to return during the Asian tournament swing in Beijing and Shanghai, Tennis Majors [reports](https://www.tennismajors.com/us-open-news/breaking-jannik-sinner-withdraws-from-2026-us-open-due-to-knee-injury-858970.html).

## Who else has withdrawn from the tournament

Besides Sinner, the main draw of the 2026 US Open will also be missing Spain's Alejandro Davidovich Fokina (world No. 27), and Americans Ethan Quinn (No. 58) and Sebastian Korda (No. 64). Canada's Gabriel Diallo (No. 105) and American Emilio Nava (No. 108) will also skip the tournament, Tennis365 [reports](https://www.tennis365.com/tennis-news/jannik-sinner-speaks-us-open-2026-withdrawal). The main draw begins August 30.
