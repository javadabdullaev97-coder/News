OpenAI 20 avgustda ChatGPT uchun macOS'da Apple Messages plaginini [taqdim
etdi](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/changelog) — u tizimning «Messages»
ilovasi orqali iMessage, SMS va RCS xabarlarini o'qiydi, qidiradi va yuboradi.

Plagin Mac uchun desktop ilovasidagi ChatGPT'ning barcha tariflarida mavjud,
shuningdek ChatGPT Work va Codex'da ham ishlaydi, — [kompaniya
changelog'ida aytilishicha](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/changelog). Xabar
yuborish tasdiqlash bilan himoyalangan: «ChatGPT xabarni faqat siz uning
matni va qabul qiluvchilarini tasdiqlagandan so'ng yuboradi», — [deb
ta'kidlaydi OpenAI](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/changelog).

## Maxfiylik bo'yicha tashvishlar

[Bloomberg xabar
berishicha](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-20/chatgpt-can-now-control-imessage-potentially-raising-apple-privacy-concerns),
yangi funksiya iPhone ishlab chiqaruvchisi Apple uchun maxfiylik
muammolarini yaratishi mumkin. Agentlik ma'lumotlariga ko'ra, ChatGPT endi
matnli xabarlarni o'qiy, yoza va yubora oladi. Shuningdek, plagin
yozishmalar bo'yicha qidiruv o'tkazadi va «Messages» ilovasidagi suhbatlar
bo'yicha xulosalar oladi, — [deb xabar beradi
Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-20/chatgpt-can-now-control-imessage-potentially-raising-apple-privacy-concerns).
Apple kompaniyasining o'zi bu bahoni ommaviy sharhlamagan.

Plaginning yozishmalarga kirish huquqini bekor qilish mumkin, — [OpenAI
ta'kidlaydi](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/changelog) — tartib plagin
bo'yicha alohida qo'llanmada tavsiflangan.
