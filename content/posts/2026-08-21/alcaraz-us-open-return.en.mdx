Carlos Alcaraz [announced on August 20](https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/carlos-alcaraz-announces-return-injury-175508404.html) that he will play the 2026 US Open in New York from August 30 to September 13 — he hasn't competed since April because of an injury to his right wrist.

The Spaniard went without tournaments for more than [four months](https://www.sports.ru/tennis/1117340894-alkaras-sygraet-na-us-open-ispanecz-provedet-pervyj-turnir-s-aprelya.html) — he suffered the injury on April 14 at a tournament in Barcelona. He missed the French Open, Wimbledon and the Cincinnati tournament.

Alcaraz [ranks second](https://www.skysports.com/tennis/news/12040/13575157/carlos-alcaraz-fabrizio-romano-breaks-news-us-open-champion-is-to-make-comeback-from-wrist-injury-at-flushing-meadows) in the ATP world rankings and is defending the US Open title he won in 2025.

The tennis player confirmed the news himself — he posted on Instagram together with journalist Fabrizio Romano with the words [“Here we go”](https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/carlos-alcaraz-announces-return-injury-175508404.html).

As of August 20, [11 players had withdrawn](https://www.tennis365.com/tennis-news/us-open-withdrawal-list-2026-11-stars-out) from the tournament. They include Emma Raducanu, who has a stress fracture in her shin, and Sebastian Korda, who underwent back surgery. Jannik Sinner's participation has not yet been confirmed: the Italian skipped the Cincinnati tournament because of a knee injury but remains on the US Open entry list.
