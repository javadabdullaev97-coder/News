IT Park Uzbekistan reported on August 6 that by the end of the first half of 2026 the number of exporting residents had reached [1,050 companies](https://t.me/itpark_uz/20583), which supply services to 102 countries worldwide.

## Geography grew 12-fold since 2017

In 2017, when IT Park had just started operating, residents exported services to only 8 countries, the organization [said](https://www.it-park.uz/ru/itpark/news/geografiya-eksporta-rezidentov-it-park-rasshirilas-do-102-stran) in its publication. By mid-2026, the coverage had grown more than 12-fold — to 102 countries, with 8 new countries added to the list of export destinations this year alone.

Of the 1,050 exporting residents, 315 companies operate through IT Park's regional offices outside Tashkent, the organization [noted](https://t.me/itpark_uz/20583). The publication does not provide export volume in monetary terms.

## Where the services go

IT Park [provided](https://www.it-park.uz/ru/itpark/news/geografiya-eksporta-rezidentov-it-park-rasshirilas-do-102-stran) a breakdown of exports by region:

- North America — 54%
- CIS countries and other states — 22%
- European Union and the United Kingdom — 16%
- MENA region (Middle East and North Africa) — 8%

## Residents with foreign capital

Separately, IT Park [reported](https://t.me/itpark_uz/20583) 1,095 residents with foreign capital, of which 349 companies are registered at the organization's regional offices.

## Context

IT Park's growing export geography comes amid a regular weeding-out of residents for reporting violations: on August 5, the [organization stripped](https://leap.uz/en/2026/08/05/itpark-70-companies-lose-resident-status) 70 companies of resident status for failing to submit their second-quarter 2026 report. Both processes — the growth in the number of exporters and the removal of violators — are running in parallel and are not related to each other.
