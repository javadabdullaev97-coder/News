The National Statistics Committee under the President of the Republic of Uzbekistan reported on July 29 that the country's exports fell 8.8% to $15.9 billion in January–June 2026 — the decline was driven by the precious metals commodity group.

## Sales Concentrated in a Single Month

According to [monthly data](https://api.siat.stat.uz/media/uploads/sdmx/sdmx_data_3080.xlsx) from the National Statistics Committee, exports under HS group 71 — pearls, precious and semi-precious stones, precious metals — totaled $2,219.0 million in January–June 2026. The same period in 2025 saw $6,791.8 million. The decline is 3.1-fold, or $4,572.9 million.

Within the half-year, shipments were distributed unevenly. Month-by-month dynamics for 2026:

- January — $27.8 million
- February — $71.9 million
- March — $133.3 million
- April — $1,669.3 million
- May — $143.6 million
- June — $173.1 million

April accounted for 75.2% of the group's half-year volume. In May and June, shipments stayed in the $140–175 million range — an order of magnitude below April's level.

The same data show the decline began back in 2025. In October 2025, exports under the group totaled $51.7 million, in November — $21.1 million, in December — $59.7 million. Before that, in September 2025, the figure was $1,524.7 million.

Group 71 includes not only gold but also pearls, stones, silver and jewelry. There is no separate line for gold in the National Statistics Committee's public data: the breakdown only goes down to the 2-digit HS code.

## Central Bank: No Gold Exports in the First Quarter

The Central Bank of the Republic of Uzbekistan issued a [balance of payments review](https://cbu.uz/en/press_center/releases/4083080/) for Q1 2026; in the [publications catalog](https://cbu.uz/ru/publications/balance-of-payments/) it is dated June 30. The review states that the decline in exports compared to the same period last year is mainly explained by the absence of gold exports in the reporting quarter.

The Central Bank estimated Q1 exports at $5.6 billion, with a current account deficit of nearly $5.8 billion. At the same time, exports of goods excluding gold rose 29%, and exports of services rose 18%.

International reserves grew by about $2.7 billion over the quarter, while the foreign currency portion declined by $3.1 billion. The Central Bank attributed the growth to world gold prices.

The review for Q2 2026 had not been published as of August 1.

## Exports Excluding Gold Rose 28.7%

According to [National Statistics Committee data](https://stat.uz/ru/press-tsentr/novosti-goskomstata/69502-zbekistonning-tash-i-savdo-ajlanmasi-yarim-jilda-41-mlrd-dollarga-etdi-4), Uzbekistan's foreign trade turnover totaled $41 billion in January–June 2026 — up 7.4% from a year earlier. Imports rose 21% to $25.1 billion.

Exports of goods excluding gold totaled $8.2 billion, up 28.7% compared to the same period in 2025.

Uzbekistan traded with 195 countries. The largest shares of turnover went to China (23.1%), Russia (17.1%), Kazakhstan (6.8%), Turkey (3.4%) and Afghanistan (2.6%).
