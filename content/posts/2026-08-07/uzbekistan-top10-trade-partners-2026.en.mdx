The National Statistics Committee of the Republic of Uzbekistan has published the top 10 largest trade partners of Uzbekistan for January–June 2026: China kept first place with trade turnover of [$9.5 billion](https://t.me/uzstataxborot/69179), followed by Russia in second place with $7 billion.

## Who made the top 10

According to the [National Statistics Committee](https://stat.uz/ru/press-tsentr/novosti-goskomstata/69678-top-10-zbekistonning-eng-jirik-savdo-amkor-davlatlari-5), Uzbekistan traded with 195 countries worldwide in the first half of 2026. The ten largest partners by trade turnover:

1. China — $9.5 billion
2. Russia — $7 billion
3. Kazakhstan — $2.8 billion
4. Turkey — $1.4 billion
5. Afghanistan — $1.1 billion
6. Republic of Korea — $940 million
7. France — $890.9 million
8. UAE — $807.7 million
9. Kyrgyzstan — $624.6 million
10. Germany — $623.2 million

The top three positions — China, Russia and Kazakhstan — account for more than half of the top 10's trade turnover. The gap between the leader and second place is $2.5 billion, and between second and third place — $4.2 billion.

The National Statistics Committee did not include each country's share of total trade turnover or year-on-year changes for each partner in the publication.
