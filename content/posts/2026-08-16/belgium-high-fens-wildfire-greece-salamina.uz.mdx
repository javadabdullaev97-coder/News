Belgiyaning sharqidagi Yuqori Fenn (High Fens) qo'riqxonasidagi yong'in mamlakat tarixidagi eng yirigiga aylandi va 16-avgustda Germaniya chegarasiga yaqinlashdi — taxminan 600 kishi evakuatsiya qilindi.

Olov qo'riqxonaning vereska bosgan tekisliklari va torf botqoqliklarining 27 kvadrat kilometrdan ortiq maydonini qamrab oldi, [deb xabar beradi Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/16/belgiums-largest-wildfire-on-record-spreads-towards-german-border). Bu 2011-yilgi yong'in maydonidan — o'sha hududdagi Belgiyaning avvalgi 14 kvadrat kilometrlik rekordidan — kattaroq.

Olov juma kuni, 14-avgustda paydo bo'lgan. Shanba kuniga kelib hokimiyat Bütgenbach posyolkasi va Waimes jamoasi aholisini evakuatsiya qilishni e'lon qildi — ular Malmedidagi sport zalida joylashtirildi, [deb xabar beradi Euronews](http://ru.euronews.com/my-europe/2026/08/16/fires-europe-belguim-germany-france).

Belgiya Yevropa Ittifoqining fuqarolik muhofazasi mexanizmini ishga tushirdi. Yong'inni o'chirishga Belgiya, Germaniya, Lyuksemburg va Niderlandiyadan 250 dan ortiq o't o'chiruvchi va qutqaruvchi jalb qilindi, [deb xabar beradi Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/16/belgiums-largest-wildfire-on-record-spreads-towards-german-border).

«Yuqori Fenn yonmoqda, Belgiya bunga yolg'iz qarshi turmaydi», — dedi YI inqirozlarni boshqarish bo'yicha komissari Hadja Lahbib, [deb xabar beradi Associated Press](https://www.news4jax.com/news/world/2026/08/16/firefighters-battle-a-massive-belgian-wildfire-that-forced-hundreds-to-evacuate/).

Shvetsiya, Niderlandiya va Chexiyadan Belgiyaga ikkita suv tashuvchi samolyot va uchta vertolyot yetib keldi, [deb xabar beradi Euronews](http://ru.euronews.com/my-europe/2026/08/16/fires-europe-belguim-germany-france). Shvetsiya ikkita sisterna-samolyot, Niderlandiya ikkita vertolyot, Chexiya bitta vertolyot yubordi, [deb aniqlashtiradi Associated Press](https://www.news4jax.com/news/world/2026/08/16/firefighters-battle-a-massive-belgian-wildfire-that-forced-hundreds-to-evacuate/).

Yong'in nemis shahri Monschauga taxminan 3 kilometr masofagacha yaqinlashdi. Kechasi hokimiyat tutun tufayli uchta chekka hudud aholisiga vaqtincha uylarini tark etishni tavsiya qildi, [deb xabar beradi Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/16/belgiums-largest-wildfire-on-record-spreads-towards-german-border). Ilgari evakuatsiya qilingan Gay qishlog'ining taxminan 2 ming aholisi shanba kuni kechqurun uylariga qaytdi, [deb xabar beradi Euronews](http://ru.euronews.com/my-europe/2026/08/16/fires-europe-belguim-germany-france).

Belgiya ichki ishlar vaziri Bernard Kenten o't o'chiruvchilar olovni nemis chegarasi tomon siljishiga yo'l qo'ymaganini va Monschauni himoya qilganini ma'lum qildi, [deb xabar beradi Associated Press](https://www.news4jax.com/news/world/2026/08/16/firefighters-battle-a-massive-belgian-wildfire-that-forced-hundreds-to-evacuate/).

## Salamina orolidagi yong'in: ikki kishi halok bo'ldi

Afinaning g'arbidagi Gretsiya Salamina orolida 16-avgust kuni yong'in ikki kishining hayotiga zomin bo'ldi — ularning jasadlari Peristeria qishlog'ida topildi, [deb xabar beradi Associated Press](https://www.news4jax.com/news/world/2026/08/16/firefighters-battle-a-massive-belgian-wildfire-that-forced-hundreds-to-evacuate/). Ikki halok bo'lgan, ko'rinishidan, er-xotin bo'lgan, yana beshta kuygan kishi tibbiyot punktiga yetkazildi, [deb xabar beradi Reuters](https://whtc.com/2026/08/16/two-die-as-wildfires-sweep-greek-island-of-salamina/).

Salaminadagi yong'inni o'chirishga o't o'chiruvchilar, samolyotlar va vertolyotlar jalb qilindi. [AP](https://www.news4jax.com/news/world/2026/08/16/firefighters-battle-a-massive-belgian-wildfire-that-forced-hundreds-to-evacuate/) va [Reuters](https://whtc.com/2026/08/16/two-die-as-wildfires-sweep-greek-island-of-salamina/)ning turli ma'lumotlariga ko'ra, ularning soni 192 dan 260 tagacha bo'lgan (16-avgust, yakshanba kuni kechqurungi holat).

Selinia hududida ikkinchi yong'in o'chog'i alangalandi — u yerdan qirg'oq qo'riqlash va o't o'chirish qayiqlari plyajlardan dam oluvchilarni evakuatsiya qildi, [deb xabar beradi Associated Press](https://www.news4jax.com/news/world/2026/08/16/firefighters-battle-a-massive-belgian-wildfire-that-forced-hundreds-to-evacuate/). Gretsiya o't o'chirish xizmati vakili brigada generali Vasilios Vathrakogiannis kuchli shamolda olov yetti daqiqada turar-joy massivlariga o'tib ketganini ma'lum qildi, [deb xabar beradi Associated Press](https://www.news4jax.com/news/world/2026/08/16/firefighters-battle-a-massive-belgian-wildfire-that-forced-hundreds-to-evacuate/).

Yong'inlar Gretsiyada yozgi turistik mavsumning avji bilan ustma-ust tushdi. Yakshanba kuni 15-avgustdagi pravoslav Muqaddas Bibi Maryam bayramidan keyingi dam olish kunlariga — yozning eng band hafta oxiriga — to'g'ri keldi, [deb xabar beradi Reuters](https://whtc.com/2026/08/16/two-die-as-wildfires-sweep-greek-island-of-salamina/).

Belgiya va Gretsiyadagi yong'inlar Yevropa bo'ylab kengroq to'lqinning bir qismi. Frantsiyada Landes mintaqasi yonmoqda (1700 gektardan ortiq, 550 o't o'chiruvchi), Ispaniyaning Huesca provinsiyasida 16 600 gektargacha maydon kuydi, [deb xabar beradi Euronews](http://ru.euronews.com/my-europe/2026/08/16/fires-europe-belguim-germany-france).
