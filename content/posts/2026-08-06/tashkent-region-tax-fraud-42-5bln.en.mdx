The Department for Combating Economic Crimes under the Prosecutor General's Office of Uzbekistan reported on August 6 that it had uncovered a scheme by six companies in Tashkent Region to evade 42.5 billion soums in taxes. Separately, the inspection found that employees of the regional tax department, while auditing the same companies, had failed to assess them for another 5.2 billion soums in taxes.

## How the scheme worked

According to the [Department's statement](https://t.me/Department_uz/12961), the pre-investigation review was carried out jointly with the State Tax Committee (hereinafter, the Tax Committee). It found that officials of six limited liability companies and other individuals connected to them illegally reclaimed 32.6 billion soums in value-added tax from the budget. That is roughly $2.74 million at the [Central Bank of Uzbekistan's exchange rate](https://cbu.uz/ru/arkhiv-kursov-valyut/json/USD/2026-08-06/) for August 6 of 11,886.72 soums per dollar. At the same time, they sold 195.9 billion soums (roughly $16.48 million) worth of goods for cash without depositing the proceeds in a bank, violating a number of trade rules, according to the inspection. Investigators say these actions amounted to deliberate evasion of 42.5 billion soums in taxes — about $3.58 million.

## Role of tax inspectors

According to the same statement, officials of the Tashkent Region tax department were conducting audits of the same companies. The auditors drew up reports that failed to assess the companies for 5.2 billion soums (roughly $437,000) in taxes. The Department classified this as causing significant damage to the state and society.

## Criminal case opened, investigation continues

A criminal case has been opened over the matter under the relevant articles of the Criminal Code, and the investigation continues. The Department did not name the individuals involved or state their positions. Nor did it clarify whether this is a single case covering both the company representatives and the tax inspectors, or separate proceedings. Under the law, all those involved are presumed innocent until a court verdict takes legal effect.

## Context: other Department cases in July–August

The Tashkent Region case is not the first economic case the Department has reported in recent days. On August 5, the agency [said](https://t.me/Department_uz/12949) it had uncovered a scheme in Surkhandarya Region to embezzle a 25 billion soum (roughly $2.10 million at the Central Bank's exchange rate for August 6) bank loan. The scheme involved embezzlement through re-pledging property using forged documents. And on August 3, the Department [reported](https://t.me/Department_uz/12937) opening a case over inflated volumes of thermal energy in Fergana Region. This allowed a heat-supply company to receive 12.9 billion soums (roughly $1.09 million at the Central Bank's exchange rate for August 6) in excess subsidies from the budget.
