Uzbekistan Foreign Minister Bakhtiyor Saidov met on August 4 in New Delhi with U.S. President's Special Representative for South and Central Asia, Ambassador Sergio Gor — the two sides discussed the C5+1 format.

## What was agreed

Saidov and Gor [noted](https://t.me/Bakhtiyor_Saidov/4334) "the steady development of strategic partnership relations between Uzbekistan and the United States across all areas," according to a post on the minister's Telegram channel. The two sides exchanged views in detail on further intensifying dialogue at the highest and high levels, as well as on priority areas of bilateral cooperation.

Separately, Saidov and Gor discussed cooperation within the C5+1 format — a dialogue platform between the United States and the five Central Asian countries — preparations for upcoming joint events, and current issues on the regional and international agenda. Following the meeting, the sides agreed to continue an "open and trusting dialogue" between Uzbekistan and the United States and to expand practical cooperation, the post [said](https://t.me/Bakhtiyor_Saidov/4334).

The meeting was also [confirmed](https://t.me/portal_gov_uz/42781) by Uzbekistan's government portal gov.uz, which published a report on its Telegram channel about Saidov's talks with the U.S. special representative in New Delhi.

## Context of the visit

The meeting with Gor took place as part of Saidov's visit to India. On August 4, the minister took part in the Uzbekistan–India Business Forum, at which the sides agreed to increase trade turnover from $1.3 billion to $2 billion, gov.uz [reported](https://gov.uz/ru/news/view/201784).
