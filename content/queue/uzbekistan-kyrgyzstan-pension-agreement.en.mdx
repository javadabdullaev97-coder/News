Uzbekistan and Kyrgyzstan on July 30 signed an agreement on state social insurance and pension provision for citizens who worked in both countries, the Extra-Budgetary Pension Fund under Uzbekistan's Ministry of Economy and Finance [said](https://t.me/PensionFundRUz/8025).

## What the agreement covers

The document was signed by the government of the Republic of Uzbekistan and the Cabinet of Ministers of the Kyrgyz Republic at a high-level meeting in Cholpon-Ata, [according to a post](https://telegra.ph/Na-vstreche-na-vysshem-urovne-podpisano-Soglashenie-mezhdu-Uzbekistanom-i-Kyrgyzstanom-o-pensionnom-obespechenii-07-30) on the Uzbekistan Pension Fund's channel. Once the agreement takes effect, the pension for work record accrued on the territory of a specific country will be assigned and paid by that country alone, under its own national legislation.

If a pensioner relocates to the territory of the other party, they continue receiving the pension already assigned. The burial benefit is determined by the legislation of the state where the pensioner permanently resided at the time of death.

Work record accrued before the agreement takes effect will be credited by the parties under each country's national legislation. Such record is also credited under the [CIS Agreement of March 13, 1992](https://t.me/PensionFundRUz/8025) on guarantees of the rights of citizens of CIS member states in the field of pension provision.

The Social Fund of the Kyrgyz Republic [notes](https://sf.gov.kg/ru/kyrgyzstan-i-uzbekistan-podpisali-soglashenie-o-gosudarstvennom-soczialnom-strahovanii-i-pensionnom-obespechenii/) that pension assignments will take into account insurance record accrued in both countries. The document also rules out double payment of insurance contributions for workers temporarily posted from one country to the other. The agreement will take effect after ratification by both parties — no specific date has been named yet.

## Part of a broader package

The pension agreement is one of 12 bilateral documents signed at the exchange ceremony in Cholpon-Ata, [the press service](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9472) of the president of Uzbekistan said. The main outcome of talks between presidents Shavkat Mirziyoyev and Sadyr Japarov was the signing of the Treaty on Allied Relations. According to the Uzbek side's wording, the document raised bilateral relations to the level of an alliance.

The package also included a treaty on the Uzbek-Kyrgyz section of the state border, an agreement on mutual recognition of higher education credits, and cooperation plans for tourism, culture and the agro-industrial complex for 2026-2027.

## Who is affected

The agreement is addressed to citizens of Uzbekistan and Kyrgyzstan who worked on the territory of both countries at different times. Before the document was signed, their pension rights were covered under the general 1992 CIS Agreement.
