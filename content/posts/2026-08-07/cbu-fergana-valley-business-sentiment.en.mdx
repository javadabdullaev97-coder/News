The Central Bank of the Republic of Uzbekistan [published](https://cbu.uz/uz/press_center/reviews/4046038/) a business sentiment review for the first quarter of 2026 on June 24: the share of Fergana Valley entrepreneurs reporting improved business conditions rose to 65%, up from 57% a year earlier. In early August, the Central Bank separately highlighted this data in its [Telegram channel](https://t.me/centralbankuzbekistan/13387).

## What the Fergana Valley Survey Found

The survey covered entrepreneurs in all three regions of the Fergana Valley — Andijan, Namangan and Fergana. According to [Central Bank data](https://cbu.uz/uz/press_center/reviews/4046038/), respondents attributed the improved conditions primarily to better-quality banking services and expanded access to credit resources. They also cited simplified licensing procedures, increased competition and reduced administrative interference.

Entrepreneurs rated the level of monopolization, tax administration, gas and electricity supply, and customs tariffs less positively than other factors. But even on these indicators, the share of positive assessments rose by 10-15 percentage points year on year.

## Business Activity Indicators

The share of Fergana Valley enterprises reporting growth in orders, production and demand for labor increased by 3-5 percentage points year on year. Growth in orders was reported by 56% of surveyed companies — mainly in catering, agriculture, manufacturing and crafts. An increase in energy consumption was reported by 57% of respondents.

Demand for additional labor grew in utilities, manufacturing, catering, agriculture and construction. At the same time, the share of entrepreneurs reporting growth in loan repayments fell by 11 percentage points year on year — most noticeably in catering, crafts, education, finance and construction.

The share of enterprises operating at high capacity remained almost unchanged at 51%, the same as a year earlier. Among the factors behind underutilization, entrepreneurs cited disruptions in gas and electricity supply, difficulties with tax administration, a shortage of working capital and rising prices for motor fuel.

## Future Expectations and Nationwide Context

For the next quarter, 68% of surveyed companies in the Fergana Valley expect order volumes to grow. Over a three-year horizon, 73% of the region's entrepreneurs believe in an improvement in the country's economy and business climate — up from 56% a year earlier.

Nationwide, the share of businesses reporting improved conditions in the first quarter of 2026 was 64%, up from 56% a year earlier, [the Central Bank review says](https://cbu.uz/uz/press_center/reviews/4046038/). The survey, conducted in April 2026 via social media, covered 2,487 entrepreneurs from all regions of the country. Among the six consolidated regional groups into which the Central Bank breaks down its results, the highest figure was recorded in the Kashkadarya and Surkhandarya regions, at 70%. The Fergana Valley ranked second with 65%.
