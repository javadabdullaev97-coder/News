Deputy Minister of Sports of Uzbekistan Avaz Karimov, during a working trip to Fergana Region on August 8, inspected the construction of new facilities at the rowing base of the Fergana Training Center for Olympic and Paralympic Sports.

## What Is Being Built

At the kayaking and canoeing base, workers are [building](https://t.me/Minsportuz/90416) 12 dormitories, a canteen, a gym and a boathouse — a covered structure for storing rowing boats. Karimov reviewed the progress of the construction and repair work and [instructed](https://t.me/Minsportuz/90416) those responsible to ensure proper organization of the construction process and compliance with the established deadlines. The Ministry of Sports did not specify a completion date, project cost or contractor in its post.

## About the Center

The institution on whose grounds the construction is taking place is [registered](https://orginfo.uz/en/organization/1faadc3c040b/) as the Fergana Training Center for Olympic and Paralympic Sports, according to Orginfo.uz, an unofficial commercial register of legal entities. According to the same data, the center is located in Fergana at 55 A. Navoi Street and has operated since November 20, 2003.

## What's Next

The Ministry of Sports has not disclosed a timeline for completing construction of the dormitories, canteen, gym and boathouse. Supervisors have been instructed to monitor the quality of work and adherence to schedule — the ministry has yet to provide new dates.
