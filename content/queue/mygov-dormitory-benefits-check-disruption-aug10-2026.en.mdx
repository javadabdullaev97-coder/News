The Ministry of Higher Education, Science and Innovation of the Republic of Uzbekistan and the my.gov.uz portal warned on August 6 of disruptions in determining benefit categories when applying for university dormitories — due to technical work in the system of the National Agency for Social Protection until August 10.

## What exactly might not work

[The Ministry of Higher Education](https://t.me/eduuz/50137) reported on August 6 that unscheduled preventive maintenance is underway on the servers of the "Yagona milliy ijtimoiy himoya" ("Unified National Social Protection System") information system of the National Agency for Social Protection. As a result, until August 10 there may be temporary disruptions specifically in determining benefit categories when submitting online applications for placement in student dormitories through the my.gov.uz portal.

A similar warning was [published](https://t.me/MyGovUz/3970) by the my.gov.uz portal itself — it clarified that the disruptions could affect any electronic services that rely on data from the social protection system, not only dormitory placement.

Both messages describe the disruptions as a possibility rather than a full halt in processing: applications through my.gov.uz continue to be accepted, but the system that determines a student's eligibility for a subsidized place — for example, for children raised in "Mehribonlik" homes, orphans or students with disabilities — may temporarily malfunction.

## Applications have been open since August 1 and are not tied to this date

The Ministry of Higher Education [opened applications](https://leap.uz/en/2026/08/01/student-dorm-applications-mygovuz) for dormitory places on August 1. Under the regulations approved by Cabinet of Ministers Resolution No. 376 of August 14, 2023, applications are accepted from August 1 until the end of the academic year. August 10 is not a deadline for submitting applications, but the date until which technical work has been announced in an adjacent system that only affects the determination of an applicant's benefit category.

## What students should do

Neither the ministry's nor the portal's messages contain direct instructions for applicants and students — both were limited to notifying about possible disruptions. If there are problems submitting an application through my.gov.uz, applicants can contact the [portal's contact center](https://my.gov.uz/ru/service/870) at 1006 and 1242, or by phone at +998 (71) 230-64-64 — these are listed on the service page "Submitting an application for placement in a student dormitory." As of publication, there had been no official announcement of the completion of the maintenance work.
