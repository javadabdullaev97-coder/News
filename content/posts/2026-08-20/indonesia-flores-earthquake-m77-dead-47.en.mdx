A magnitude 7.7 earthquake struck off the northern coast of Indonesia's Flores island on Saturday, August 15 — as of the evening of August 15, at least 47 people were killed, according to BBC and Deutsche Welle (DW).

According to the [US Geological Survey](https://earthquake.usgs.gov/earthquakes/eventpage/us6000tkt2) (USGS), the epicenter was located 68 km north-northwest of the city of Ende at a depth of 10 km. The quake was recorded at 04:58 local time — 21:58 GMT on Friday, August 14.

The USGS assigned the event a yellow alert level and estimated that around 2 million people were exposed to strong and damaging shaking. The agency also warned of a high risk of landslides near the epicenter.

## Destruction and tsunami warning

The quake [destroyed hundreds of buildings](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c3v00w5ylw9o) on the island, BBC reports. According to [DW](https://www.dw.com/ru/землетрясение-в-индонезии-десятки-погибших-и-обрушения-зданий/a-78379562), several buildings collapsed and landslides occurred. Authorities issued a tsunami warning along the coast but later lifted it, DW reports.

## Rescue operation

Rescuers are searching for survivors under the rubble, and an operational assessment of the damage is underway, [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c3v00w5ylw9o) reports. The USGS recorded aftershocks near the epicenter, including a [magnitude 4.5 quake](https://earthquake.usgs.gov/earthquakes/eventpage/us6000tl3k) 63 km north of the city of Ruteng. The death toll may change as debris is cleared.

On the evening of August 15, the death toll was estimated at 38 — [LEAP News reported on this](https://leap.uz/en/2026/08/15/flores-earthquake-toll-38), citing BNPB.

A similarly powerful magnitude 7.8 earthquake struck the same area in 1992, killing about 2,500 people, the [USGS](https://earthquake.usgs.gov/earthquakes/eventpage/us6000tkt2) notes.
