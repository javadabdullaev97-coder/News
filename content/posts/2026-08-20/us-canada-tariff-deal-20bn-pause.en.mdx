US President Donald Trump on August 19 paused for three days 50% tariffs on $20.2 billion of Canadian exports, announcing a trade deal with Canada just ahead of the deadline. [PBS NewsHour](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/trump-says-us-and-canada-reached-last-minute-deal-to-delay-50-us-tariffs-on-canadian-imports) and [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/8/19/us-canada-reach-trade-deal-to-avert-steep-tariffs-trump-says) reported this.

The tariffs were set to take effect [overnight on August 20](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/trump-says-us-and-canada-reached-last-minute-deal-to-delay-50-us-tariffs-on-canadian-imports), but Trump announced the pause less than two hours before the deadline. "I have paused the 50% Tariffs against Canada...for a three day period, based on the fact that Canada and the U.S.A...have a DEAL!" [Trump said](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/trump-says-us-and-canada-reached-last-minute-deal-to-delay-50-us-tariffs-on-canadian-imports). According to Al Jazeera, [the tariffs are suspended until August 22](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/8/19/us-canada-reach-trade-deal-to-avert-steep-tariffs-trump-says).

Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney [confirmed](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/trump-says-us-and-canada-reached-last-minute-deal-to-delay-50-us-tariffs-on-canadian-imports) "substantial progress" in the talks but noted that "important work remains." The two sides did not disclose the full terms of the deal.

About 5% of all Canadian exports to the US were under threat from the tariffs — roughly [$20 billion](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/trump-says-us-and-canada-reached-last-minute-deal-to-delay-50-us-tariffs-on-canadian-imports) worth of goods, ranging from hockey sticks to wooden spatulas, according to PBS NewsHour. Al Jazeera [cites a different list](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/8/19/us-canada-reach-trade-deal-to-avert-steep-tariffs-trump-says) — electronics, industrial equipment, furniture, dairy products and wine worth $20.2 billion.

According to PBS NewsHour, Canada [committed to eliminating](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/trump-says-us-and-canada-reached-last-minute-deal-to-delay-50-us-tariffs-on-canadian-imports) measures the US side considers discriminatory toward American alcohol, dairy products and automobiles.

Trump also said the Keystone XL pipeline, shut down under the Joe Biden administration, "may be awoken from the grave!" The president did not clarify whether this means the project's fate is part of the deal's terms.

According to Al Jazeera, the pause runs until August 22, by which time the two sides are expected to agree on the remaining details of the deal.
