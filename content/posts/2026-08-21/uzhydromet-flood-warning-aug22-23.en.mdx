Uzhydromet warned on August 21 of mudslide and flood risk on August 22–23 in the foothill and mountainous districts of seven regions — Kashkadarya, Surkhandarya, Jizzakh, Tashkent, Namangan, Fergana and Andijan.

The emergency bulletin was [published](https://t.me/uzgydromet/22043) at 06:30 on the agency's official Telegram channel. It said that rain expected on August 22–23 could bring mudslide and flood events to the foothill and mountainous districts of the republic.

Uzhydromet urged residents of foothill and mountainous districts, vacationers and drivers heading into the mountains to take precautions. The agency also warned that rainwater could accumulate in some districts of the republic and flood the affected areas.

According to Uzhydromet, the warning covers the foothill and mountainous areas of the following regions:

- Kashkadarya region — Yakkabog', Dehqonobod, Chiroqchi, Kitob, Shahrisabz, Qamashi, G'uzor;
- Surkhandarya region — Sariosiyo, Uzun, Oltinsoy, Denov, Boysun, Sherobod, Sho'rchi, Qumqo'rg'on, Muzrabot;
- Jizzakh region — Zomin, Baxmal, G'allaorol, Sharof Rashidov, Forish, Yangiobod;
- Tashkent region — Ohangaron, Bo'stonliq, Parkent, Piskent, O'rtachirchiq, Yuqorichirchiq, and the cities of Angren and Almalyk;
- Namangan region — Pop, Kosonsoy, Chortoq, Chust, Yangiqo'rg'on and the city of Namangan;
- Fergana region — Sokh, Shakhimardan, Beshariq and the city of Fergana;
- Andijan region — Andijon, Asaka, Jalaquduq, Qo'rg'ontepa, Paxtaobod, Izboskan, Xo'jaobod, Marhamat and the city of Khanabad.

The warning applies both days — August 22 and 23. During this period, Uzhydromet recommends avoiding mountain gorges and riverbeds in the listed districts, and advises drivers against unnecessary trips into the mountains.
