US President Donald Trump on August 17 demanded that Iran "raise the white flag of surrender," on the day a 60-day memorandum on ship passage through the Strait of Hormuz formally expired.

Trump made the remarks in a phone interview with Fox News, [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/17/trump-demands-iran-put-up-the-white-flag-of-surrender-as-mou-expires) reports. According to him, Washington is in no hurry to reach a settlement and maintains a direct communication channel with officers of the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps. Negotiations have effectively stalled. The US is rejecting Iran's demands for reparations and control over the strait, relying instead on economic pressure through a port blockade, [PBS NewsHour](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/the-60-day-deadline-for-a-peace-deal-with-iran-is-expiring-heres-where-things-stand) reports. The same day, Trump repeated his threat to bomb Oman if it interferes with the blockade — [LEAP News reported](https://leap.uz/en/2026/08/17/iran-oman-hormuz-escalation) on this August 17.

The memorandum, signed on June 17, called for a ceasefire, the lifting of the naval blockade and the removal of sanctions, [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/17/us-iran-memorandum-of-understanding-expires-how-and-why-it-fell-apart) reports. Iran was to receive $300 billion for reconstruction in exchange for free passage of ships through the strait. The deal fell apart because of ambiguity in three articles, the outlet notes. The clause on the strait allowed for different shipping routes from Iran and Oman. The ceasefire article did not mention Israel, and the terms for financing reconstruction were never spelled out.

## Iran Signals Readiness for Ground War

Brigadier General Yadollah Javani is the political chief of the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps. On Sunday evening he told state television that the armed forces are ready to defend the country and that their actions "could take on an offensive character," [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/17/iran-signals-readiness-for-us-ground-operations-as-mou-expires) reports. Javani said opponents should expect "strategic surprises."

Commander of the Ground Forces Amir Hatami announced a $30,000 reward for killing or capturing an American service member in the event of a ground operation on Iranian territory. The reward is doubled for women. Mohsen Rezaei, secretary of the Supreme National Security Council, said seizing the Strait of Hormuz would require at least 100,000 troops. He suggested the US had already considered such a scenario but abandoned it because the plans were "immature."

The same day, Trump linked the reduction of joint drills with South Korea to Seoul's refusal to help on Iran. On Truth Social, he wrote that he had asked South Korea's president to join in the "denuclearization" of Iran and received the answer "no, thank you," [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/newsletters/2026-08-17/trump-reduces-us-south-korea-drills-citing-lack-of-help-on-iran) reports. The Pentagon will scale back its participation in the Ulchi Freedom Shield exercises, which run from August 17 to 27.

Al Jazeera's video feed labels the current phase of the confrontation ["War on Iran: Phase II, Day 37"](https://www.aljazeera.com/video/newsfeed/2026/8/17/war-on-iran-phase-ii-day-37). About five months have passed since the conflict began in late February.
