Typhoon Dolphin made landfall near the city of Yuhuan in Zhejiang province on the evening of August 9 with sustained winds of 151 km/h — the strongest storm to hit China in 2026.

## The Path: From Okinawa to Zhejiang

Before making landfall on mainland China, the typhoon passed over Japan's southern Okinawa prefecture over the weekend, [the Guardian writes](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/aug/09/typhoon-dolphin-makes-landfall-in-china-as-its-strongest-storm-this-year). On the afternoon of August 9, China's national meteorological center issued its highest, "red," alert for Zhejiang and Shanghai, [the South China Morning Post reports](https://www.scmp.com/news/china/article/3363428/red-alert-shanghai-cancels-60-flights-typhoon-dolphin-closes). Both regions fell within the cyclone's "dangerous semicircle" — the zone with the strongest winds and heaviest rain. The typhoon made landfall near Yuhuan at around 5:30 p.m. local time (09:30 GMT) with sustained winds of 151 km/h. That is equivalent to a Category 1 hurricane on the U.S. scale, [the Guardian notes](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/aug/09/typhoon-dolphin-makes-landfall-in-china-as-its-strongest-storm-this-year).

## More Than 1 Million Evacuated, Thousands of Flights Cancelled

According to published figures, more than 1 million people were evacuated from coastal areas of eastern China — a figure cited by [the Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/aug/10/typhoon-dolphin-china-shanghai-flooding-evacuations) as of the morning of August 10. [Al Jazeera provides](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/9/flight-cancellations-evacuations-as-china-braces-for-typhoon-dolphin) a regional breakdown: more than 900,000 residents were evacuated from Wenzhou, about 390,000 from Taizhou, at least 30,300 from Shanghai, and about 98,900 from Fujian province.

Air travel ground to a halt even before the storm made landfall: by the time the typhoon came ashore on the evening of August 9, Shanghai had cancelled about 80% of flights [(SCMP)](https://www.scmp.com/news/china/article/3363428/red-alert-shanghai-cancels-60-flights-typhoon-dolphin-closes) — roughly 1,400 flights [(Al Jazeera)](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/9/flight-cancellations-evacuations-as-china-braces-for-typhoon-dolphin). On Monday, August 10, as Dolphin moved north of Shanghai, the flight situation began to improve. Pudong and Hongqiao airports together cancelled 943 flights — about 40% of the schedule, [SCMP reports](https://www.scmp.com/news/china/politics/article/3363486/40-flights-grounded-shanghai-long-lived-typhoon-dolphin-moves-north).

## Record Heat in Hong Kong Amid the Typhoon

The typhoon triggered not rain but an extreme heatwave in Hong Kong: downward air currents on the cyclone's periphery brought clear skies. The Hong Kong Observatory recorded a temperature of 36.9°C on the afternoon of August 9. That is the hottest day in the city since record-keeping began in 1884 — 142 years, [SCMP reports](https://www.scmp.com/news/hong-kong/health-environment/article/3363429/hong-kong-breaks-record-hottest-day-typhoon-dolphin-brings-extreme-heat). At an automatic weather station in the Sheung Shui area, the thermometer climbed to 39.8°C — a record since the equipment was installed there in the 1980s. [The Guardian also confirms](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/aug/10/hong-kong-heat-record-hottest-day-ever-typhoon-dolphin) the city's all-time heat record. The Hong Kong Observatory warned that the hot weather would persist through midweek.

## Missing Child in Wenling

According to [SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/news/china/article/3363450/chinese-boy-9-missing-after-being-swept-away-wave-typhoon-dolphin-approached-coast), on Friday, August 7, a wave swept a 9-year-old boy into the sea in the coastal village of Shitang in Wenling city district, Zhejiang province. He was with a family of four (two adults and two children) on a causeway connecting the mainland to a coastal island, despite a storm warning already in effect. As of Sunday evening, August 9, the search was continuing. High tides, fast currents and whirlpools complicated the work of rescue services — no results of the search had been reported at that time.

## What's Next

According to [SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/news/china/politics/article/3363486/40-flights-grounded-shanghai-long-lived-typhoon-dolphin-moves-north), by Monday morning, August 10, Dolphin continued moving north inland, weakening but retaining its status as a "long-lived" storm. The threat of flooding and landslides in Zhejiang and neighboring provinces remains, and air travel in Shanghai is recovering gradually rather than all at once.
