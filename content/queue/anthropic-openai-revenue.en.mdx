Anthropic overtook OpenAI in revenue for the first time in the second quarter of 2026 — $11.6 billion versus $6.7 billion, The Wall Street Journal reported, citing sources familiar with both companies' figures. Several outlets cite the same account; there is no official confirmation as of publication — both companies are private and do not officially disclose quarterly revenue.

Anthropic's revenue more than doubled compared with the first quarter, [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/anthropic-passes-openai-on-revenue-for-the-first-time/) and [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/anthropic-beats-openai-in-revenue-for-first-time/) write; [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/08/18/openai-falls-further-behind-anthropic-with-disappointing-revenue-growth-and-mounting-losses/), citing the same WSJ report, gives a more cautious estimate — growth of "more than 50%." Year over year, growth was even higher. According to [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/anthropic-beats-openai-in-revenue-for-first-time/), Anthropic's revenue rose more than 14-fold — from $787 million in the second quarter of 2025.

OpenAI's revenue grew 18% quarter over quarter — from $5.7 billion to $6.7 billion, [SiliconANGLE reports](https://siliconangle.com/2026/08/18/openai-falls-further-behind-anthropic-with-disappointing-revenue-growth-and-mounting-losses/). Investors had expected stronger growth ahead of the company's planned IPO — the initial public offering of its shares on an exchange, [The Decoder notes](https://the-decoder.com/anthropic-passes-openai-on-revenue-for-the-first-time/).

OpenAI's operating loss grew from $9.3 billion in the first quarter to $12.3 billion in the second, [SiliconANGLE writes](https://siliconangle.com/2026/08/18/openai-falls-further-behind-anthropic-with-disappointing-revenue-growth-and-mounting-losses/). Anthropic, by contrast, posted an operating profit for the first time in its history — $559 million, [SiliconANGLE reports](https://siliconangle.com/2026/08/18/openai-falls-further-behind-anthropic-with-disappointing-revenue-growth-and-mounting-losses/); PYMNTS calls it "small" and describes a positive adjusted operating income.

## ChatGPT vs. Claude Code

The WSJ links Anthropic's results to the success of Claude Code, a tool that writes and edits program code on developers' requests. At the same time, ChatGPT's growth slowed at OpenAI, [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/anthropic-passes-openai-on-revenue-for-the-first-time/) and [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/anthropic-beats-openai-in-revenue-for-first-time/) write.

Every request to Claude models brings Anthropic more revenue than a comparable request to OpenAI's models — that's according to data from Vercel, [cited by The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/anthropic-passes-openai-on-revenue-for-the-first-time/). Anthropic's annualized revenue, based on current monthly figures, rose to $65 billion by July, [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/anthropic-passes-openai-on-revenue-for-the-first-time/) and SiliconANGLE report.

OpenAI told investors that revenue growth accelerated in the third quarter — after the company launched new AI models in July, [PYMNTS writes](https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/anthropic-beats-openai-in-revenue-for-first-time/).
