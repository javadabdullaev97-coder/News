President Shavkat Mirziyoyev [was presented with a report](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9482) on August 3 on the performance of joint-stock company Uzbekneftegaz for the first half of 2026 and priority tasks for the rest of the year.

## Gas Output Exceeds Plan

In the first half of the year, Uzbekneftegaz [extracted](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9482) 11.5 billion cubic meters of natural gas. Output of oil, diesel fuel, jet fuel and liquefied gas exceeded projected figures — the report does not provide specific numbers for these four categories for the first half.

## Second-Half Plan: Rising Demand for Jet Fuel

By the end of the year, the company plans [to produce](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9482) 1.1 million tons of gasoline, 983,000 tons of diesel fuel and 310,000 tons of jet fuel. The plans also include 606,000 tons of liquefied gas and about 1.18 million tons of synthetic products.

An increase in the number of flights on routes through Central Asia amid escalating geopolitical tensions is driving up demand for aviation fuel. In July–December 2026, demand for jet fuel [will total](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9482) 375,000 tons — 27% more than in the same period last year.

## $38.8 Million for Exploration

$38.8 million [will be allocated](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9482) to modernize joint-stock company Uzbekgeofizika — for equipment upgrades and the purchase of a field complex for 3D seismic surveys.

The company also plans to establish a seismic data processing center together with foreign partners, including the American company Schlumberger. The area of annual 3D seismic surveys is set to grow from 1,800 to 5,000 square kilometers, and survey depth from 4,500 to 8,000 meters.

## Presidential Instructions

Mirziyoyev [instructed](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9482) that the population and economic sectors be provided with an uninterrupted supply of petroleum products. He also set tasks for the technological modernization of industry enterprises and improving energy efficiency — without specifying deadlines or target indicators in the published report.
