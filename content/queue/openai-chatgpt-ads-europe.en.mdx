OpenAI will start showing ads in ChatGPT to free-tier and paid Go subscription users in 31 European countries starting August 24, Agence France-Presse (AFP) reported, citing the company.

The ads will not affect ChatGPT's premium subscriptions. Go is a paid tier that costs 8 euros ($9.3) a month in Europe.

According to AFP, ads in ChatGPT are always labeled as advertising and visually separated from the chatbot's responses. The ads do not affect the content of responses, OpenAI said. The company also said user conversations remain closed to advertisers and that customer data is not sold.

"This expansion gives marketers across Europe a new way to reach people at the moment they're researching options, comparing, and making decisions." That is what OpenAI's statement, cited by AFP, says.

Advertising first appeared in ChatGPT in the United States in February 2026. The list of 31 countries where ads will roll out on August 24 is limited to Europe — Uzbekistan, like other Central Asian countries, is not on the list.
