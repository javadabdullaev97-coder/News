According to AppleInsider, Apple laid off at least 60 employees from its Vision Products Group and VR development team on August 20 — the company itself has not confirmed this.

AppleInsider was the first to publish the information, citing an unnamed source familiar with the situation. In an updated version of the article, the outlet wrote that an entire team working on VR development had been cut. [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/08/20/apple-reportedly-lays-off-60-vision-employees-amid-shifting-priorities/) also reported on AppleInsider's publication, citing it as well, and noted that Apple "has not confirmed or commented on" the information.

## Apple shifts priorities to AI glasses

According to [AppleInsider](https://appleinsider.com/articles/26/08/20/layoffs-in-apples-vision-products-group-prove-slow-progress-in-spatial-computing), the decision reflects the direction set by the company's new operations leadership under John Ternus. Apple is deprioritizing closed VR headsets in favor of glasses with built-in AI. 9to5Mac expects the first model of such glasses — with cameras and Siri instead of an AR display — to be unveiled at WWDC in 2027.

Vision Products Group was already reorganized about a year ago: some employees were redistributed among Apple's larger hardware and software divisions at the time, 9to5Mac writes. The Vision Pro headset itself received an updated M5 chip in October 2025, and in June 2026 its price rose to $3,699. Apple's leadership continues to call spatial computing a company priority and notes that the field is at an "early stage."
