Uzbekistan's Competition Development and Consumer Rights Protection Committee this week [warned](https://t.me/RaqobatGovUz/12608) sellers at "school markets" against unjustified overpricing of textbooks, uniforms and stationery ahead of the start of the school year.

The committee reminded sellers that they are required to give buyers full and accurate information about a product's quality and price. The agency [published](https://t.me/RaqobatGovUz/12600) a similar appeal to businesses in text format around the same days.

If they find unjustified price increases, consumers can contact the committee's single trust hotline — [(+998 71) 207-48-00, short number 1159](https://raqobat.gov.uz/ru/contacts/). The call center operates on weekdays from 9 a.m. to 6 p.m.

The committee's publications did not specify concrete enforcement measures — fine amounts or timelines for additional inspections.
