The Central Bank of the Republic of Uzbekistan set the official US dollar exchange rate for August 7, 2026, at 11,915.64 soums on August 6 — up 28.92 soums (0.24%) from the previous day's rate.

## Euro and Pound Rise Along with Dollar

The euro rate for August 7 [stood at](https://cbu.uz/ru/arkhiv-kursov-valyut/json/all/2026-08-07/) 13,749.46 soums — up 32.19 soums (0.23%) over the day. The British pound rose to 16,034.88 soums, gaining 27.03 soums (0.17%).

A day earlier, on August 6, all three currencies [had weakened](https://cbu.uz/ru/arkhiv-kursov-valyut/json/all/2026-08-06/). The dollar lost 55.49 soums — falling to 11,886.72 soums, the euro lost 25.83 soums, to 13,717.27 soums, and the pound lost 43.67 soums, to 16,007.85 soums.

## Weekly Dollar Rate: Friday's Gain Did Not Offset the Decline

According to official Central Bank data, the dollar moved in both directions throughout the past work week:

- August 3 (Monday) — [11,960.18 soums](https://cbu.uz/ru/arkhiv-kursov-valyut/json/USD/2026-08-03/), −46.21 soums from Friday, July 31
- August 4 (Tuesday) — [11,989.46 soums](https://cbu.uz/ru/arkhiv-kursov-valyut/json/USD/2026-08-04/), +29.28 soums
- August 5 (Wednesday) — [11,942.21 soums](https://cbu.uz/ru/arkhiv-kursov-valyut/json/USD/2026-08-05/), −47.25 soums
- August 6 (Thursday) — [11,886.72 soums](https://cbu.uz/ru/arkhiv-kursov-valyut/json/all/2026-08-06/), −55.49 soums
- August 7 (Friday) — [11,915.64 soums](https://cbu.uz/ru/arkhiv-kursov-valyut/json/all/2026-08-07/), +28.92 soums

Despite Friday's gain, the August 7 rate remains 44.54 soums (0.37%) below the level set on Monday, August 3.

## Monthly Trend: Dollar Weaker Than in Early July

In early July, the Central Bank of Uzbekistan [set](https://cbu.uz/ru/arkhiv-kursov-valyut/json/USD/2026-07-01/) the dollar rate at 12,009.27 soums. By August 1, the dollar had weakened to 12,006.39 soums — a rate that [was in effect](https://cbu.uz/ru/arkhiv-kursov-valyut/json/USD/2026-08-01/) from Friday, July 31. Over the period from July 1 to August 7, the dollar depreciated against the soum by 93.63 soums, or 0.78%.
