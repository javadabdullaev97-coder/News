Novak Djokovic lost 6:2, 4:6, 4:6 to Thiago Agustin Tirante in the second round of the Cincinnati Masters 1000 on August 15. According to Sky Sports and Tennis365, his opponent ranks 50th in the world, while Tennis Majors puts him at 65th. After the match, Djokovic said he has been dealing with a chronic health condition for several years that worsens in heat and humidity.

"It's just a health condition that's been bothering me for the last couple of years. A lot of issues, especially when it's humid and hot," [Djokovic said](https://www.skysports.com/tennis/news/12110/13573671/novak-djokovic-reveals-ongoing-health-condition-after-defeat-in-cincinnati) at the post-match press conference. He did not name a specific diagnosis.

During the second set, the Serb went down on the court on his hands and knees and needed a medical timeout. A doctor and a physiotherapist came onto the court with cold towels, [Tennis Majors reports](https://www.tennismajors.com/atp/tirante-stuns-ailing-djokovic-for-the-biggest-win-of-his-career-in-cincinnati-858592.html). The match lasted more than two and a half hours and was Djokovic's first since his defeat to Jannik Sinner in the Wimbledon semifinal.

Former French tennis player Camille Pin, who commentated the match live on Eurosport, called Djokovic's condition more worrying than the match result itself. "It's quite unusual that this happened not in the third set after 2 hours 40 minutes of play. From the very start Novak struggled to breathe, and that's a fairly negative sign," [Pin said](https://www.welovetennis.fr/atp/atp-cincinnati/camille-pin-sur-novak-djokovic-il-y-a-quelque-chose-de-plus-inquietant-que-sa-defaite).

## Less than two weeks until the US Open

The main draw of the 2026 US Open starts on August 30, with the men's singles final set for September 13, [Tennis Majors reports](https://www.tennismajors.com/us-open-news/us-open-2026-dates-schedule-full-calendar-858738.html). According to Tennis365, Djokovic will play mixed doubles with Aryna Sabalenka before the singles tournament begins.

The 24-time Grand Slam champion is seeking his first major title since the 2023 US Open. The win in Cincinnati was, according to Tennis Majors, the biggest of Tirante's career.
