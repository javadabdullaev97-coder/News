OpenAI launched ChatGPT for Teens on August 18, 2026 — a separate version of the chatbot for users aged 13 to 17 with stricter content restrictions and new parental control tools.

Teen mode turns on automatically. If a user enters an age under 18 during sign-up, the account immediately gets restrictions, [the Associated Press reported](https://www.clickondetroit.com/business/2026/08/18/openai-launches-chatgpt-for-teens-promising-a-more-age-appropriate-chatbot/). The company also uses an age assurance system — an algorithm that estimates age from the nature of a conversation rather than from documents. Restrictions apply if the system predicts a user is under 18. That prediction carries more weight than the age listed in a profile, [Engadget reported](https://www.engadget.com/2238773/chatgpt-stricter-teen-mode-starts-rolling-out-today/).

The chatbot cuts off conversations about self-harm, suicidal topics and explicit sexual content. It is also barred from calling itself the user's "friend," [Engadget reported](https://www.engadget.com/2238773/chatgpt-stricter-teen-mode-starts-rolling-out-today/).

A separate Study Mode switches the bot to a Socratic method: instead of giving a ready-made answer, it pushes the teen to find the solution on their own, [the AP reported](https://www.clickondetroit.com/business/2026/08/18/openai-launches-chatgpt-for-teens-promising-a-more-age-appropriate-chatbot/). To manage workload, the app has Study Hours — hours when Study Mode is on by default — and Quiet Hours — hours when the chatbot is unavailable. During long sessions, the system reminds teens to take a break, [Engadget reported](https://www.engadget.com/2238773/chatgpt-stricter-teen-mode-starts-rolling-out-today/).

## Parental Controls

Parental control features activate only if both the teen and the parent agree to link their accounts, [the AP reported](https://www.clickondetroit.com/business/2026/08/18/openai-launches-chatgpt-for-teens-promising-a-more-age-appropriate-chatbot/).

After that, a parent can set "quiet hours" and receive notifications about higher-risk situations. For example, separate alerts arrive for conversations about eating disorders, [the AP reported](https://www.clickondetroit.com/business/2026/08/18/openai-launches-chatgpt-for-teens-promising-a-more-age-appropriate-chatbot/). If the system detects signs of acute distress in a teen, the parent receives a message by text, in-app notification or email within an hour, [Engadget reported](https://www.engadget.com/2238773/chatgpt-stricter-teen-mode-starts-rolling-out-today/).

## Context

The launch of the separate version comes amid lawsuits against OpenAI: plaintiffs claim conversations with ChatGPT harmed the mental health of minor users, [the AP reported](https://www.clickondetroit.com/business/2026/08/18/openai-launches-chatgpt-for-teens-promising-a-more-age-appropriate-chatbot/).

OpenAI Vice President of Global Policy Anne O'Leary said the company wants to "treat teens like teens." She said that means not treating them as adults or young children, [the AP reported](https://www.clickondetroit.com/business/2026/08/18/openai-launches-chatgpt-for-teens-promising-a-more-age-appropriate-chatbot/). OpenAI's head of child development, Allison Mishkin, explained that the team looked for "hypothetical signals" — the kind that could lead a teen to form an emotional attachment to the model, [the AP reported](https://www.clickondetroit.com/business/2026/08/18/openai-launches-chatgpt-for-teens-promising-a-more-age-appropriate-chatbot/).

According to a 2025 study by Common Sense Media, more than 70% of teens use AI chatbots as companions, not just for schoolwork, [the AP reported](https://www.clickondetroit.com/business/2026/08/18/openai-launches-chatgpt-for-teens-promising-a-more-age-appropriate-chatbot/).
