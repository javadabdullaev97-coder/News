The National Statistics Committee of Uzbekistan said that as of July 1, 2026, the country had 32 business entities per 1,000 residents.

## Regions: Tashkent Nearly Doubles the National Average

The data [were published in the agency's Telegram channel](https://t.me/uzstataxborot/69158), broken down by all 14 regions. In descending order:

- Tashkent city — 50
- Khorezm Region — 46
- Syrdarya Region — 41
- Navoi Region — 38
- Jizzakh Region — 35
- Bukhara Region — 35
- Tashkent Region — 33
- Andijan Region — 32
- Republic of Karakalpakstan — 30
- Fergana Region — 27
- Samarkand Region — 27
- Kashkadarya Region — 26
- Namangan Region — 26
- Surkhandarya Region — 24

The gap between the leader and the lowest-ranked region is more than twofold: Tashkent has 50 business entities per 1,000 residents, compared with 24 in Surkhandarya Region.

## What Counts as a Business Entity

The July 1, 2026 statement itself does not disclose the methodology. The closest official metric is published quarterly by the agency in the "Kichik tadbirkorlik subyektlari" ("Small Business Entities") section of its [analytical bulletin](https://stat.uz/files/619/choraklik-natijalar-2026-yanvar-mart-uz/4312/11-.pdf?preview=1). As of April 1, 2026, this category included: small enterprises and micro-firms, including farms and dekhkan households — 507,138 units; individual entrepreneurs (YaTT) — 324,090; dekhkan households as a separate category — 353,472; craftsmen — 22,670; sellers of imported goods — 971. In total — 1,208,341 active small business entities.

The National Statistics Committee did not directly confirm that the "32 entities per 1,000 residents" figure for July 1, 2026, was calculated using the same small-business methodology — this link still needs to be verified through a separate request to the agency. For this reason, the figure cannot yet be compared directly with the quarterly small-business data.

Uzbekistan's permanent population as of January 1, 2026, stood at [38,236,704 people](https://stat.uz/ru/press-tsentr/novosti-goskomstata/66703-zbekistonning-doimij-a-olisi-musta-illik-jillarida-17-6-millionga-k-pajdi-3); exact population figures for July 1, 2026, have not been found in verified sources.
