US Ambassador to Israel Mike Huckabee on August 13 called the settler siege of three Palestinian homes in the village of Qusra, south of Nablus in the West Bank, a "horrific act of terror." Israeli authorities, at the request of the US embassy, sent additional troops to the village to remove the settlers and restore order, [Al Jazeera reports](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/13/us-diplomat-calls-israeli-settler-siege-of-west-bank-homes-act-of-terror).

## The Siege Began on Sunday

According to [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/13/us-diplomat-calls-israeli-settler-siege-of-west-bank-homes-act-of-terror), settlers have surrounded three homes in Qusra since Sunday, August 9: they blocked access to the houses, cut off water and electricity, and prevented residents from leaving to buy food. One of the besieged homes belongs to the family of a US citizen of Palestinian descent, who does not hide his dual citizenship, the outlet notes.

Huckabee commented on this family's situation separately: "Actions by those who carried out this horrific act of terror meant to intimidate and harass this family are disgusting," [the ambassador said](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/13/us-diplomat-calls-israeli-settler-siege-of-west-bank-homes-act-of-terror).

## Israel's Response and Troops in the Village

[According to the BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cj4kppdk2qwo), Qusra mayor Abdel Azim Wadi said the village had been declared a closed military zone, and that Israeli troops used some of the vacated Palestinian homes as temporary positions. An Israeli government spokesperson called the settlers' actions "reprehensible" and "unacceptable" and promised an investigation and arrests of those responsible.

On Wednesday, August 12, the Israel Defense Forces deployed an additional infantry unit to Qusra — according to its own statement, to strengthen order and operational control in the area. Overnight into Thursday, August 13, the military also said it had dismantled two illegal settler outposts on the outskirts of Qusra and neighboring Jalud, confiscated equipment, and detained one Israeli, [Al Jazeera reports](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/13/us-diplomat-calls-israeli-settler-siege-of-west-bank-homes-act-of-terror).

## Washington's Position

Huckabee, known as a consistent supporter of Israeli settlements in the West Bank, publicly condemned the settlers' actions — a rare step for a diplomat with that reputation. He said Israeli forces arrived in Qusra at the request of the US embassy, [Al Jazeera writes](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/13/us-diplomat-calls-israeli-settler-siege-of-west-bank-homes-act-of-terror).
