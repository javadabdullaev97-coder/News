Weightlifter Diyorbek Odilov won the Central Asian Open Weightlifting Championship in the under-65kg category at a tournament being held in Tashkent during the Asian Championship on August 7–14, the Ministry of Sports of Uzbekistan [reported](https://t.me/Minsportuz/90436).

## Odilov's Result

Odilov lifted 127kg in the snatch and 153kg in the clean and jerk, for a 280kg total. Based on his results in both lifts and in the combined total, he received two gold medals and one silver, earning him the title of Central Asian Open Weightlifting Champion in the under-65kg category, [according to a post](https://t.me/Minsportuz/90436) on the Ministry of Sports' Telegram channel. The ministry marked the athlete's victory with a ceremony raising the national flag and playing the national anthem of Uzbekistan.

The tournament is being held in Tashkent alongside the Asian Weightlifting Championship for juniors and youth, where Uzbekistan's national team earlier won two bronze medals, [LEAP News reported](/article/uzbekistan-weightlifting-asian-junior-champs-medals).

## About the Tournament

The Asian Weightlifting Championship for juniors and youth is being held in Tashkent from August 7 to 14, 2026, [according to the calendar](https://awf.sport/events/asian-youth-junior-championships/) of the Asian Weightlifting Federation (AWF).
