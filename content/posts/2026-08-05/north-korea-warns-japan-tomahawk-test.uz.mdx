Shimoliy Koreya rahbari Kim Jong Unning singlisi Kim Yo Jong chorshanba kuni, 5 avgustda, Yaponiya Tomahawk qanotli raketasini sinovdan o'tkazganiga javoban Pxenyan qo'shimcha harbiy choralarni ko'rib chiqishini aytdi.

## Kim Yo Jong nima dedi

Shimoliy Koreyaning davlat KCNA agentligi tarqatgan bayonotda Kim Yo Jong Yaponiya qurolli kuchlarini mudofaa doktrinasidan chekinishda ayblagan. Uning so'zlariga ko'ra, Yaponiya armiyasi oldindan zarba berish qobiliyatiga ega harbiy kuchga aylanmoqda, — [deb yozadi Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/5/north-koreas-kim-yo-jong-issues-military-threat-over-japan-missile-test). Pxenyan Yaponiyani o'z xavfsizligi jiddiyroq tahdid ostida qolganini his qildiradigan «qo'shimcha harbiy variantlar»ni qabul qilish niyatida.

«Biz uni [Yaponiyani] bundan afsuslantirishimiz kerak», — dedi Kim Yo Jong. Al Jazeera bahosiga ko'ra, bu uning so'nggi oylarda Tokioga qaratilgan eng keskin iboralaridan biri, [deb yozadi nashr](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/5/north-koreas-kim-yo-jong-issues-military-threat-over-japan-missile-test).

Kim Yo Jong shuningdek, Tokioni «harbiy jinoyatchi davlat» ambitsiyalarini tiklashda ayblab, buni Yaponiya armiyasining hujum imkoniyatlarini kengaytirishi bilan bog'ladi, [deb xabar beradi Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-05/north-korea-warns-of-more-military-steps-over-japan-s-rearmament). Alohida u AQShni Yaponiyaning harbiy qudratini oshirishga ko'maklashayotganlikda aybladi. Uning so'zlariga ko'ra, Vashington Tomahawk raketalarini yetkazib bermoqda, kemalarni ulardan foydalanish uchun moslashtirmoqda va sinovlarni qo'llab-quvvatlamoqda, — [deb yozadi Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/5/north-koreas-kim-yo-jong-issues-military-threat-over-japan-missile-test).

## Tomahawk raketasi sinovi

Yaponiya 29 iyulda AQShning Tomahawk qanotli raketasini sinovdan o'tkazdi va uni Tinch okeanida «Chokai» (Chokai) esminetsidan uchirdi, — [deb xabar beradi Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/video/newsfeed/2026/8/5/japan-tests-us-tomahawk-missile-as-it-moves-away-from-post-wwii-status). Nashr bu sinovni Tokioning urushdan keyingi pasifistik maqomdan asta-sekin chekinish yo'lidagi navbatdagi qadami deb ataydi.

Kim Yo Jong shuningdek, Yaponiyaning boshqa raketa sinovlariga va may oyida Filippinda AQSh bilan o'tkazilgan qo'shma harbiy mashqlarda ishtirokiga ishora qildi. U buni Tokioning harbiy kuchayishining qo'shimcha belgilari deb atadi, [deb yozadi Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/5/north-koreas-kim-yo-jong-issues-military-threat-over-japan-missile-test).

Pxenyan hozircha qanday «qo'shimcha harbiy variantlar»ni ko'rib chiqayotganini oshkor qilgani yo'q, [deb ta'kidlaydi Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-05/north-korea-warns-of-more-military-steps-over-japan-s-rearmament).
