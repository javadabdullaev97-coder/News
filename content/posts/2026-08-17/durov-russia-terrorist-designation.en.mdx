Russia's Federal Financial Monitoring Service (Rosfinmonitoring) on July 30 added Telegram founder Pavel Durov to its list of individuals linked to terrorism and extremism, [RIA Novosti reported](https://ria.ru/20260730/durov-2107936977.html), citing the agency's website.

A day earlier, on July 29, the Public Relations Center of Russia's Federal Security Service (FSB) charged Durov in absentia with facilitating terrorist activity, [Kommersant reported](https://www.kommersant.ru/doc/8847075). The agency also put him on an international wanted list, [Kommersant wrote](https://www.kommersant.ru/doc/8847075). According to the FSB, Telegram's administration did not remove channels and bots that Ukrainian intelligence services used to plan sabotage and terrorist attacks in Russia, [Interfax reported](https://www.interfax.ru/russia/1106589).

Inclusion on the list means Durov's accounts and property in Russia are frozen, [RIA Novosti clarified](https://ria.ru/20260730/durov-2108002731.html). Banks are also barred from servicing his transactions, except for tax payments, wages and damage compensation. He is also barred from communicating with media, posting information online, holding public events and participating in elections. The agency separately stressed that the restrictions apply to Durov personally, not to Telegram as a legal entity.

## Durov's Response

"Russia declared me a 'terrorist' for refusing to comply with its demands for mass surveillance and censorship on Telegram. Under Russian law, I am now barred from 'publishing information online,'" [Durov said](https://t.me/durov/535) on his personal channel the day he was added to the list. He added that Russian officials "clearly got confused about who can disconnect whom from the internet."

## Citizenship and Extradition Prospects

If Durov's arrest or extradition happens, Moscow's Lefortovo District Court will handle the case — it issues a two-month pretrial detention warrant, [Kommersant wrote](https://www.kommersant.ru/doc/8847075). Durov holds four citizenships: Russia, Saint Kitts and Nevis, the UAE and France, the last of which he obtained in 2021.

Telegram has been restricted in Russia since August 2025. As of February 10, 2026, the communications regulator said the service still did not comply with legal requirements, [Interfax noted](https://www.interfax.ru/russia/1106589).
