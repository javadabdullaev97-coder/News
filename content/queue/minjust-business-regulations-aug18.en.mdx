The Ministry of Justice of the Republic of Uzbekistan (Ministry of Justice) [published](https://t.me/huquqiyaxborot/20270) the text of law ZRU-1168 on August 17, which for the first time introduces the concept of social entrepreneurship into legislation and defines tax breaks, subsidies and other forms of state support.

The law defines social entrepreneurship as the activity of business entities aimed at solving social and environmental problems or mitigating their consequences, [according to a post](https://t.me/huquqiyaxborot/20270) on the Ministry of Justice's Telegram channel "Huquqiy Axborot."

## Forms of Support

The state will support such businesses in six ways, [according to the Ministry of Justice's post](https://t.me/huquqiyaxborot/20270):

- tax breaks and preferences;
- subsidizing expenses for social projects;
- allocating government social contracts;
- financial support;
- staff training and professional development;
- consulting and information assistance.

The law also introduces criteria for responsible business conduct. Companies that respect human rights and prevent harmful effects on the environment and employee health will be eligible for support, [according to the post](https://t.me/huquqiyaxborot/20270).

## Part of a Broader Reform

The same law ZRU-1168 makes other changes to business legislation. It elevates to the level of law a ban on penalizing businesses for requirements not entered in the unified register of mandatory requirements, which the Ministry of Justice operates, [the same channel reported](https://t.me/huquqiyaxborot/20269).

## Who It Affects

The rule applies to business entities whose activity is aimed at solving social or environmental problems. State agencies that allocate subsidies and social contracts gain a legal basis for targeted support of such enterprises.

## When It Takes Effect

The law takes effect three months after official publication, [according to the Ministry of Justice's post](https://t.me/huquqiyaxborot/20270). The exact date of official publication had not been announced at the time of publication.
