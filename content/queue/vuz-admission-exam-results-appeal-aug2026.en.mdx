The Agency for Assessment of Knowledge and Qualifications said on August 15 that applicants who disagree with their mandate results can file an appeal from August 15 through 24 through their personal account on my.uzbmb.uz.

The agency [announced](https://t.me/BaholashUz/11386) the mandate results — the competitive selection of bachelor's degree applicants for the 2026/2027 academic year — the same day, August 15; [LEAP News reported on it](https://leap.uz/en/2026/08/15/uzbekistan-university-admission-results-2026).

The agency [said](https://t.me/BaholashUz/11387) review of appeals will begin on August 19: applicants will be notified of the date, time and location of the hearing. Appeals are accepted only from applicants who were not disqualified from the exam.

The Agency for Assessment of Knowledge and Qualifications was established in 2022 on the basis of the State Testing Center, which was created in 1994. The agency is responsible for selecting applicants through testing, organizing exams and publishing results.
