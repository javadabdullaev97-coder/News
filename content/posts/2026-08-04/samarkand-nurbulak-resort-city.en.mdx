The Tourism Committee of the Republic of Uzbekistan announced on August 3 that it had prepared a draft presidential resolution on creating the sanatorium-resort city "Nurbulak" on 470 hectares in the Nurabad district of Samarkand region.

## What the Project Proposes

The future city's main natural resource will be therapeutic thermal radon springs, [according to a statement](https://gov.uz/ru/uzbektourism/news/view/201376) from the Tourism Committee. Within a month of the project's approval, "Nurbulak" is planned to be granted the status of a regional urban settlement, with corresponding administrative-territorial changes.

The hokimiyat (regional administration) of Samarkand region will be instructed to develop the city's master plan within six months. Design and appraisal costs will be covered by the Fund for the Development of Tourism Infrastructure in Mountainous and Remote Areas, with the expenses later reimbursed from revenue generated by the sale of land plots.

## Incentives for Investors

The project offers [entrepreneurs](https://gov.uz/ru/uzbektourism/news/view/201376) building sanatorium and resort facilities a package of incentives through January 1, 2035. It includes a full exemption from customs duties on imported equipment, technology and raw materials, and a 50% discount on land tax.

From October 1, 2026, medical institutions providing services to foreign citizens through tour operators will be able to receive a cashback of 50% of the value-added tax paid. Investors are promised preferential loans through commercial banks from the Fund for Reconstruction and Development of the Republic of Uzbekistan for building sanatorium and resort facilities. The rate will not exceed the key rate of the Central Bank of Uzbekistan (CBU), which stands at about 14% per year, [according to the CBU](https://cbu.uz/ru/monetary-policy/refinancing-rate/).

## Target Indicators

The project aims to bring the number of sanatorium and resort facilities in "Nurbulak" to 50, with a total capacity of 7,400 beds, double the tourist flow, and grow medical tourism service exports 2.3 times. The average length of tourists' stays is planned to increase from 5 to 8 days.

The document also envisages adopting a national medical tourism brand, "Avicenna" — a unified digital platform in five languages (Uzbek, English, Russian, Chinese and Arabic). The platform will feature information on medical institutions, their certifications and tourist facilities, as well as an electronic registry of organizations after verification. The brand is planned to be promoted through participation in international exhibitions, B2B meetings and consultation events in priority countries.

## What's Next

The document remains a draft resolution for now — the Tourism Committee has not named a date for its signing by the president. The first official step after approval will be granting "Nurbulak" the status of an urban settlement within a month; the second will be the development of the master plan by the Samarkand region hokimiyat within six months.
