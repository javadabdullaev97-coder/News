The Republican Stock Exchange "Toshkent" completed a third placement of "UMRC SPV" mortgage bonds worth 40.4 billion soums (≈3.38 million dollars at the Central Bank's rate on August 3) on August 3, the [National Agency of Prospective Projects](https://t.me/napp_uz/2363) (NAPP) reported.

## Deal details

The bonds were placed by UMRC SPV LLC — a special purpose company under UMRC JSC (Mortgage Refinancing Company of Uzbekistan), acting as mortgage agent. The placement was carried out through closed subscription, with "Milliy bank" as the deal's participant (according to NAPP's publication), [the statement said](https://t.me/napp_uz/2363).

With the third deal included, the total volume of "UMRC SPV" mortgage bonds placed since the program launched has reached 121.1 billion soums (≈10.13 million dollars at the Central Bank's rate on August 3), [NAPP reports](https://t.me/napp_uz/2363).

The project is being implemented under [Presidential Decree No. UP-26 of February 21, 2025](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/7395940) "On additional measures for the further development of housing and mortgage markets."

## Three deals in a month and a half

This is the third exchange deal involving "UMRC SPV" mortgage bonds since the program's launch.

The first deal took place on July 8, 2026: bonds worth 40.56 billion soums [were placed through closed subscription with the participation of JSCB "Xalq banki"](https://uzse.uz/boards/4300). This was the first placement in Uzbekistan's history of mortgage bonds backed by a pool of mortgage loans from commercial banks.

The second deal took place on July 31, 2026: [bonds worth 40.14 billion soums](https://t.me/napp_uz/2361) were placed with the participation of Agrobank JSC.

According to the "Toshkent" exchange, the first issue of "UMRC SPV" mortgage bonds has a [face value of 100 soums per unit](https://t.me/napp_uz/2148). The issue is backed by mortgage collateral and a guarantee from the Mortgage Refinancing Company of Uzbekistan, and is registered by NAPP under the "regulatory sandbox" special legal regime for the mortgage bond market. According to the exchange, the pilot project is designed for a total volume of 200 billion soums (≈16.72 million dollars at the Central Bank's rate on August 3).

The funds raised are used to finance and refinance mortgage loans issued by commercial banks to the public.

## What's next

NAPP has not named the timing of the next "UMRC SPV" bond placements. The pilot project envisages further deals until the stated volume of 200 billion soums is exhausted.
