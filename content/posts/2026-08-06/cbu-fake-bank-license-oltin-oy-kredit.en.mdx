The Central Bank of Uzbekistan [reported](https://t.me/centralbankuzbekistan/13401) on August 6 that a fake license, purportedly issued in its name to a bank called "Oltin Oy Kredit," is circulating online — no such organization is in the Central Bank's registry.

## What the regulator found

According to [Central Bank data](https://t.me/centralbankuzbekistan/13401), the fake document names "Oltin Oy Kredit," a joint-stock commercial bank. The regulator clarified that no organization by this name exists, and the Central Bank never issued it a banking license.

The Central Bank urged citizens and organizations to be vigilant against this kind of fraud.

## How to verify a bank's license

The Central Bank recommends checking the list of organizations with a valid license on the regulator's [official website](https://cbu.uz/ru/credit-organizations/banks/head-offices/) — in the register of commercial bank head offices.

## Not the Central Bank's first warning about fakes

In May 2025, the Central Bank already [warned](https://cbu.uz/uz/press_center/news/2416025/) of fraudsters who, posing as its "security service," messaged citizens on Telegram and offered to arrange loans through fake banking apps in order to steal money from their accounts. The "Oltin Oy Kredit" case is a different scheme: this time, it was not correspondence that was faked, but the license document itself.
