On August 4, Uzhydromet warned of strengthening wind up to 13–18 m/s and dust storms in most regions of Uzbekistan — PM10 particle levels in Tashkent's air exceeded the norm by 1.21 times by 09:00.

## Wind and Dust Storms by Region

The Hydrometeorological Service Agency under the National Committee of the Republic of Uzbekistan for Ecology and Climate Change (Uzhydromet) issued a [warning](https://t.me/uzgydromet/21864) at 09:30 Tashkent time. During the day on August 4, wind speeds are strengthening to 13–18 m/s — roughly 47–65 km/h — in parts of Karakalpakstan and the Bukhara, Navoiy, Tashkent, Samarkand, Jizzakh, Syrdarya, Kashkadarya, Surkhandarya and Fergana regions, as well as in mountainous areas. Dust storms are possible in some areas.

In Tashkent itself, wind will strengthen to 12–15 m/s (about 43–54 km/h); dust phenomena are also possible in some parts of the city.

This is the second wind-strengthening warning in three days: on [August 2](https://t.me/uzgydromet/21849), Uzhydromet expected wind to strengthen to 10–12 m/s in Tashkent and 13–18 m/s in four regions in the south and west of the country. By August 4, the warning zone had expanded to cover almost the entire territory of Uzbekistan, and wind speed in the capital rose to 12–15 m/s.

## Air Quality in Tashkent

According to Uzhydromet's automatic monitoring stations, at 09:00 on August 4 the PM10 concentration in Tashkent's air [reached 91 µg/m³](https://t.me/uzgydromet/21863), against the national maximum permissible concentration (MPC) of 75 µg/m³ — an excess of 1.21 times. PM2.5 remained within the norm at 25 µg/m³, against an MPC of 60 µg/m³.

For comparison: on August 2, at the same time of day, PM10 in Tashkent exceeded the norm by 1.12 times.

## Mudflow Risk Through August 7

On August 3 at 16:50, Uzhydromet issued a separate [emergency warning](https://t.me/uzgydromet/21858): due to rain expected August 4–7, mudflow and flood phenomena are possible in the foothill and mountainous areas of the Tashkent, Namangan, Fergana and Andijan regions. The list of high-risk districts includes, in particular, the Akhangaran, Bostanlyk and Parkent districts of the Tashkent region, the Pop and Chust districts of the Namangan region, the Fergana and Beshariq districts of the Fergana region, and the Andijan and Asaka districts of the Andijan region. The agency recommended that residents, vacationers and drivers heading into mountainous areas take precautions.

The mudflow warning is in effect all week, including August 4, alongside the warning about strengthening wind and dust storms.

## What's Next

According to Uzhydromet's [forecast](https://t.me/uzgydromet/21865) for the period from 20:00 on August 4 to 20:00 on August 5, wind in Tashkent will weaken to 3–8 m/s with gusts up to 10–12 m/s; dust phenomena are also possible in some areas. No precipitation is expected in the capital; nighttime temperatures will be 21–23°C, daytime 33–35°C.
