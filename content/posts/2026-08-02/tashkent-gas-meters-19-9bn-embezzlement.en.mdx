The Department for Combating Economic Crimes under the Prosecutor General's Office of the Republic of Uzbekistan said on July 31 that a criminal case had been opened over the embezzlement of 19.9 billion soums in budget funds during the servicing of gas meters in Tashkent.

## What the Department Said

According to the [Department's statement](https://t.me/Department_uz/12928), a pre-investigation review found that officials of JSC Hududgazta'minot and LLC Texnopark, along with other individuals, acted in prior collusion and embezzled or misappropriated 19 billion soums in budget funds "allegedly for the technical maintenance of electronic gas meters" (translated from Uzbek). At the [Central Bank of Uzbekistan's exchange rate](https://cbu.uz/ru/arkhiv-kursov-valyut/json/USD/2026-07-31/) of 12,006.39 soums per dollar on July 31, that is about $1.58 million.

Separately, the Department found that readings on 273 gas meters made by Texnopark and installed for consumers by Hududgazta'minot had been understated. The agency estimated the damage to state and public interests from this at 861.1 million soums (≈$71,700).

The Department put the total figure of 19.9 billion soums (≈$1.66 million) in the headline of its statement but did not explain how it was calculated. The 19 billion and 861.1 million soums it cited add up to 19.86 billion, which rounds to 19.9 billion; the Department itself does not provide this breakdown.

The criminal case was opened over what occurred, under the relevant articles of the Criminal Code, and the investigation is ongoing. The Department did not name the suspects or their positions, the period the identified violations relate to, or the number of meters involved in the first episode.

## What the Meter Replacement Program Covers

The mass replacement of gas metering devices with electronic ones has been underway since 2022. Cabinet of Ministers of the Republic of Uzbekistan [Resolution No. 430 of August 5, 2022](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/6142840) called for equipping 3.1 million rural households with modern electronic meters.

For Tashkent, the same document does not refer to individual meters at consumers' premises but to meters on shared gas pipelines at the entrances of apartment buildings — 11,500 buildings in the Navoi and Bukhara regions and Tashkent. Another part of the program covers metering devices on distribution gas networks.

The resolution defines funding sources as follows: Hududgazta'minot's own funds, loans from domestic and international financial institutions, and other sources not prohibited by law. The company pays for the SIM cards through which the meters transmit readings itself.

Budget funds are not named in this list, even though the Department specifically refers to the embezzlement of budget money. The Department's statement does not indicate on what basis these funds were spent.

## Metering Devices at the President's Meeting

On July 27, President Shavkat Mirziyoyev [said](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9456) at a meeting on the fuel and energy sector that hundreds of thousands of electricity and gas metering devices were not transmitting data to the centralized system.

Following that meeting, the Minister of Energy and the head of Regional Electric Networks were dismissed. The heads of National Electric Networks of Uzbekistan, Thermal Power Plants, Uztransgaz and Hududgazta'minot were given a probationary period through the end of the year.

## What's Next

The investigation is ongoing, and the Department has not given a timeline for its completion. Until a court verdict takes effect, the individuals referred to in the Department's statement are presumed innocent.
