Anthony Joshua's promoter Eddie Hearn said on August 19 that he is ready to sue Zuffa Boxing head Dana White over venue talks for the fight with Tyson Fury, [MMA Fighting reports](https://www.mmafighting.com/boxing/505831/eddie-hearn-says-he-should-be-suing-dana-white-over-fury-joshua-claims-white-responds).

According to Hearn, White is "posing as someone with the right to stage the fight" in New York, [MMA Fighting reports](https://www.mmafighting.com/boxing/505831/eddie-hearn-says-he-should-be-suing-dana-white-over-fury-joshua-claims-white-responds). Hearn claims White is using Joshua's name in venue negotiations without proper authority.

White rejected Hearn's claims. "I stopped listening to Eddie Hearn three months ago. Is there anyone who talks more nonsense than this guy?" he said, [Sherdog reports](https://www.sherdog.com/news/news/Dana-White-has-stopped-listening-to-Eddie-Hearn-amid-FuryJoshua-talks-202424). Asked about the threat of a lawsuit, White responded with a question of his own — who is actually promoting the fight, [BoxingScene reports](https://www.boxingscene.com/articles/eddie-hearn-vs-dana-white-still-overshadows-anthony-joshua-vs-tyson-fury).

## What's Behind the Venue Dispute

The dispute intensified amid reports that the sides had reportedly agreed to hold the fight on November 20 at Madison Square Garden in New York, [BoxingScene reports](https://www.boxingscene.com/articles/eddie-hearn-vs-dana-white-still-overshadows-anthony-joshua-vs-tyson-fury). Hearn and Joshua are insisting the fight be held in the United Kingdom.

According to BoxingScene, Netflix and Saudi financier Turki Alalshikh are pushing for the move to the U.S. Both sides are counting on peak television audiences during American primetime. Hearn and Joshua say the change of venue and date requires renegotiating the terms of the existing agreement.

On August 17, White [said](https://leap.uz/en/2026/08/17/dana-white-joshua-fury-tv-venue-deal) he had already closed the television and venue deals for the fight. The sides are now publicly disputing where and when the fight will take place, and there is no official confirmation of the date or venue as of publication.
