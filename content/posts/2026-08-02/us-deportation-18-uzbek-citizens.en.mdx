On the evening of August 1, a charter flight from the United States carrying 18 Uzbek citizens deported for violating US immigration law arrived at Tashkent International Airport, the Dunyo news agency under the Ministry of Foreign Affairs reported.

## What the official side reported

The return was the result of "coordinated cooperation between the competent authorities of Uzbekistan and the United States," the agency [said in its report](https://dunyo.info/ru/reportazh-ia-dunyo/v-tashkent-pribyl-ocherednoy-charternyy-reys-iz-ssha-s-deportirovannymi): the two sides coordinated the identification of the citizens, the processing of documents, and safe transportation.

The report does not name the airline, flight number, departure airport, or route. The composition of the group — age, gender, region of origin — is also not disclosed. As of August 2, no mention of this flight was found on the websites or Telegram channels of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, the Ministry of Internal Affairs, or the Migration Agency under the Cabinet of Ministers.

## Flights from the US in 2026

Dunyo has reported on charter flights from the US earlier in 2026:

- May 1 — [26 people](https://dunyo.info/ru/reportazh-ia-dunyo/v-tashkent-pribyl-charternyy-reys-iz-ssha-s-deportirovannymi-grazhdanami);
- June 11 — [24 people](https://dunyo.info/ru/reportazh-ia-dunyo/tashkent-pribyl-ocherednoy-charternyy-reys-iz-ssha-s-deportirovannymi), the plane landed at 4 a.m.;
- August 1 — 18 people.

That adds up to 68 people. Neither side publishes a running annual total, and the list above is limited to Dunyo's reports — flights the agency did not report on are not included.

The United States is not the only origin. On the night of May 13, a [charter flight from Sweden arrived in Tashkent](https://dunyo.info/ru/reportazh-ia-dunyo/v-tashkent-pribyl-charternyy-bort-iz-shvecii-s-deportirovannymi) carrying 20 Uzbek citizens expelled for the same reason — violation of the host country's immigration law.

## Grounds and procedure

Reports on flights from the US cite a single ground — violation of US immigration law. They contain no breakdown by category (overstaying a legal period of residence, denial of asylum, visa violations, an immigration court ruling), so the structure of the deportations cannot be judged from open data.

Who pays for the flight is also not disclosed: the reports speak only of the joint organization of the flights. [Decree No. UP-162 of October 17, 2024](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/7170461) established the Migration Agency under the Cabinet of Ministers and the Migration Fund in Uzbekistan. The fund is named as a source of financing for migration-related measures, but the published reports do not link its funds to specific deportation flights.

The procedure for relatives of the returnees — where to apply, how to confirm that a person is on the flight list — is not described in the agencies' published reports. There is no public registry of deportees in open government sources.

## What's next

No schedule for future flights has been announced. For all the flights mentioned above, the agency reported only after arrival — on the day the plane landed.
