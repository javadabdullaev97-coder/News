The Ministry of Economy and Finance of the Republic of Uzbekistan said on July 30 that the period for claiming unclaimed pensions has been extended from 12 to 24 months — the rule has been in effect since January 1, 2024.

## What Changed

The rule is set out in [Law ZRU-922 of March 18, 2024](https://lex.uz/docs/6842776). The document's full title is "On Amendments and Additions to Certain Legislative Acts of the Republic of Uzbekistan Aimed at Further Strengthening the Social Protection of Citizens." It replaced the words "twelve months" with "twenty-four months" in part one of Article 59 of the law [On State Pension Provision for Citizens](https://lex.uz/docs/112312). This article is titled "Payment of Pension for Past Periods." It governs the receipt of amounts already assigned and accrued to a pensioner but not received on time for various reasons.

Law ZRU-922 took effect on the day of its official publication — March 19, 2024. But according to its own text, the new rule applies to relations arising from January 1, 2024.

## Who Is Affected

This concerns pensioners who were unable to claim payments already assigned to them on time — for example, due to a prolonged absence from Uzbekistan, [the Ministry of Economy and Finance explains](https://telegra.ph/Chet-elda-bolsangiz-ham-pensiyangiz-saqlanib-qoladi-07-30). Previously, such money could be claimed within 12 months of accrual; now the period is 24 months.

The ministry believes the extended period gives pensioners more time to claim money already assigned to them and strengthens the protection of their rights, according to [a post by the agency](https://t.me/minecofinuz/16170).

## How It Works in Other Countries

In the United Kingdom and Ireland, pension payments can be backdated, but the period is limited to 12 months — 52 weeks, [the Ministry of Economy and Finance notes](https://telegra.ph/Pension-Rights-Remain-Protected-Even-If-You-Live-Abroad-07-30). Similar mechanisms for paying out previously unclaimed pensions operate in Russia, Belarus, Kazakhstan, Ukraine and Azerbaijan.
