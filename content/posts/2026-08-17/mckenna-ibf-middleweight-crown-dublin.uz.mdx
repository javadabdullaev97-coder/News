Irlandiyalik Aaron Makkenna 2026-yil 8-avgustda Xalqaro boks federatsiyasi (IBF) versiyasi bo'yicha o'rta vazn toifasida jahon chempioni bo'ldi — hakamlarning ittifoqli qarori bilan u Dublinda mag'lubiyatsiz italiyalik Etinosa Olihani [mag'lub etdi](https://www.boxingscene.com/articles/aaron-mckenna-new-ibf-middleweight-champion-after-outpointing-etinosa-oliha).

Bo'sh unvon uchun jang Zuffa Boxing 10 turniri doirasida 3Arena arenasida bo'lib o'tdi. Makkenna uchun bu vatanida o'tkazgan birinchi professional jangi va jahon chempioni unvoni uchun ilk urinishi edi. Hakamlar 12 raund yakunida g'alabani ittifoqli ravishda irlandiyalikka [berishdi](https://www.worldboxingnews.com/mckenna-vs-oliha-live-scorecard/) — 118:110, 117:111 va 116:112.

Jangdan oldin ikkala boksyor ham mag'lubiyatsiz holda ringga chiqqan edi. Oliha professional martabasida birinchi mag'lubiyatga [uchradi](https://www.skysports.com/boxing/news/12183/13571057/aaron-mckenna-wins-dream-homecoming-bout-against-etinosa-oliha-to-become-ibf-middleweight-world-champion-in-dublin).

«Yangi jahon chempioni. Men bu haqda yillar davomida gapirib keldim... Otam men va akam uchun butun umrini bag'ishladi... Nihoyat, bu kamarni qo'limda ushlab turibman», — jangdan so'ng Makkenna shunday [dedi](https://www.skysports.com/boxing/news/12183/13571057/aaron-mckenna-wins-dream-homecoming-bout-against-etinosa-oliha-to-become-ibf-middleweight-world-champion-in-dublin).

## Nega unvon bo'sh edi

IBF 2026-yil mart oyida o'rta vazn unvonini bo'shatib, qozog'istonlik Janibek Alimxonulini antidoping qoidalarini buzgani uchun kamardan [mahrum qildi](https://www.worldboxingnews.com/ibf-vacates-middleweight-janibek/). 2025-yil 15-noyabrda olingan namunada boksyorda meldoniy topildi — natijani qayta namuna ham tasdiqladi. AQSh Boks komissiyalari assotsiatsiyasi Alimxonulini 2025-yil 2-dekabrdan chetlashtirdi. U IBF unvonini majburiy tartibda 2026-yil 4-iyulgacha himoya qilib ulgurmas edi. Jahon boks tashkiloti (WBO) boshqacha qaror qabul qilib, Alimxonuli kamarini o'zida saqlab qoldi va jazoni bir yillik diskvalifikatsiya bilan cheklab qo'ydi.

## Keyin nima bo'ladi

Makkenna 2026-yil 8-dekabrgacha unvonni ixtiyoriy tartibda himoya qilishi shart — aks holda IBF unga majburiy da'vogar Amari Jons bilan jang tayinlaydi, deb [xabar beradi](https://sports.yahoo.com/boxing/live/aaron-mckenna-vs-etinosa-oliha-live-results-round-by-round-updates-ring-walks-start-time-for-zuffa-boxing-10-060000870.html) Yahoo Sports.
