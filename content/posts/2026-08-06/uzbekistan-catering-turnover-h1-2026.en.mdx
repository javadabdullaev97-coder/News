The National Statistics Committee of the Republic of Uzbekistan said on August 6 that turnover at catering businesses in Uzbekistan reached [126.8 trillion soums](https://t.me/uzstataxborot/69176) in January–June 2026 — up 12.1% year-on-year.

## Number of Businesses

As of July 1, 2026, [29,204 commercial businesses](https://t.me/uzstataxborot/69176) operated in Uzbekistan's catering sector — cafes, restaurants, and other food outlets. The National Statistics Committee did not provide a breakdown by region or type of establishment in the publication.

The 126.8 trillion soum turnover is equivalent to roughly [$10.7 billion](https://cbu.uz/ru/arkhiv-kursov-valyut/?date=06.08.2026), based on the Central Bank of Uzbekistan's exchange rate on August 6 of 11,886.72 soums per dollar.

## What the Statistics Committee Did Not Say

The National Statistics Committee's post does not say how many of the businesses are small businesses or individual entrepreneurs. It also does not compare the absolute number of establishments with the figure from a year earlier. The agency limited itself to three figures: the number of businesses as of the reporting date, first-half turnover, and the year-on-year growth rate.
