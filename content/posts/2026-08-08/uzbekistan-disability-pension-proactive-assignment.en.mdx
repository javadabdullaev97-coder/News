The Senate of Uzbekistan approved pension law amendments on August 8, and per the bill's content since its first reading on April 14, they introduce proactive, application-free pension assignment for disabled people of groups I and II.

## What the Senate approved

The Senate of the Oliy Majlis's 18th plenary session began on [August 7](https://uza.uz/uz/posts/senatning-on-sakkizinchi-yalpi-mazhlisi-oz-ishini-boshladi_892277) and continued on August 8. Among the [documents approved that day](https://uza.uz/uz/posts/senat-yalpi-mazhlisi-oz-ishini-davom-ettirmoqda_892500) were amendments to the Law "On State Pension Provision for Citizens," aimed at improving the pension assignment procedure. At the same session, senators approved laws on state pension provision for military servicemen and their family members, on market surveillance, and on advertising.

The bill on improving the pension services system passed its first reading in the Legislative Chamber of the Oliy Majlis on [April 14, 2026](https://t.me/qonunchilikpalatasi/34893). Senate approval is not the final step: the law will take effect only after the president signs it and it is officially published.

## How the proactive procedure already works

Per the bill's content, the amendments are meant to enshrine in law a mechanism that has been in practical use since July 1, 2025. Presidential resolution [PP-194 of May 27, 2025](https://www.lex.uz/docs/7546356) ordered the introduction of proactive services for disability pension assignment, and [Cabinet of Ministers Resolution No. 398 of June 30, 2025](https://lex.uz/docs/7605437) established the procedure for its operation.

When the National Agency for Social Protection under the President records that a citizen has a group I or II disability, the information is automatically forwarded to the Pension Fund through the "Electronic Government" platform — without a personal visit and without an application. The Fund requests data on work record, income, place of residence, and family composition from the Ministry of Justice, tax authorities, the Ministry of Internal Affairs, the Ministry of Employment, and Xalq Bank, and assigns the pension on that basis. Notification of the assignment — or of a denial — is sent by SMS to the phone number registered in the citizen's name; if no such number exists, notification is sent by mail at the budget's expense.

For pension recipients who have accumulated at least one year of work record since the pension was assigned, the payment amount is automatically recalculated once every two years — also without applying to the Pension Fund.

## How much the pension will be

As of July 1, 2026, the minimum disability pension is [1,083,000 soums](https://www.lex.uz/uz/docs/8283656) per month (about $91 at the Central Bank of Uzbekistan's exchange rate on August 8 — 11,915.64 soums per dollar). The amount was set by Presidential Decree UP-115 of June 23, 2026, on raising wages, pensions, scholarships, and benefits.

## What's next

The law still needs the president's signature and official publication on lex.uz — only then will the amendments take effect as part of the Law "On State Pension Provision for Citizens." At the time this article was published, the version of the law in force on lex.uz still reflected an earlier amendment — dated May 19, 2025.
