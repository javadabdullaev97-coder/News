Uzbekistan's Ministry of Economy and Finance on August 7, 2026, [put up for public discussion](https://regulation.adliya.uz/project/10760/33615?contentLang=ru) a draft resolution on state compensation for part of the interest rate on electric vehicle loans — the discussion will run through August 22.

## What the Draft Proposes

The draft [regulation](https://regulation.adliya.uz/project/10760/33615?contentLang=ru) "On the Procedure for Compensating Part of Interest Payments on Loans Issued to the Public for Purchasing Electric Vehicles" applies to individuals. The state will compensate the bank from the budget for part of the annual rate above 16% — the threshold that Presidential Decree UP-22 already set as the target rate for electric vehicle loans — but no more than 8 percentage points, during the first two years of the loan.

The compensation applies to soum-denominated loans for purchasing an electric vehicle on the primary market. The vehicle's price must not exceed 1,000 times the base calculation value (BCV) — under the rate in effect as of August 2026, that is 412 million soums (≈$34,600 at the Central Bank's exchange rate of 11,915.64 soums per dollar on August 7). The loan amount eligible for compensation is capped at 300 million soums (≈$25,200). The draft leaves borrower eligibility criteria to the banks' discretion.

The funding source is additional revenue in the 2026 budget and the parameters of the 2027–2030 budgets.

## How This Relates to the 16% Rate Decree

The subject of the draft matches an instruction from [Presidential Decree UP-22](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/8050787) of February 16, 2026 — the document approved the state program for implementing the "Uzbekistan — 2030" strategy and, in paragraph 10, instructed authorities to introduce a procedure for public electric vehicle loans at a rate of up to 16% per year. The draft's metadata published on the portal contains no direct reference to the decree, but both documents operate with the same rate threshold — 16% per year.

## Price Threshold Will Rise in September

Under the rates currently in effect, 1,000 BCV equal 412 million soums, but starting September 1, 2026, the BCV will rise to 440,000 soums under [Presidential Decree UP-115 of June 23, 2026](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/8283680) on raising wages, pensions, scholarships and benefits. If the compensation resolution is adopted after that date, the price threshold for an electric vehicle to qualify for the program will automatically rise to 440 million soums (≈$36,900 at the exchange rate on August 7).

## Critics: The Measure Benefits Wealthy Buyers

Economist Otabek Bakirov [wrote on his Telegram channel](https://t.me/the_bakiroo/13036) that the draft is "a very bad, harmful and antisocial proposal." In his view, compensating interest on loans for electric vehicles worth hundreds of millions of soums effectively redistributes budget funds in favor of wealthy buyers at the expense of other citizens' taxes.

Instead of subsidizing interest, Bakirov proposed lowering or abolishing the recycling fee on electric vehicles — according to him, the fee had already been raised before and pushed up electric vehicle prices.

## What's Next

The regulation.adliya.uz portal is accepting proposals on the draft until August 22, 2026, after which the Ministry of Economy and Finance may submit the document to the Cabinet of Ministers for approval. The Cabinet of Ministers has not yet approved the draft, and the exact date the resolution takes effect will be known once the discussion concludes.
