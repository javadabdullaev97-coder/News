Irish boxer Aaron McKenna became the International Boxing Federation (IBF) middleweight world champion on August 8, 2026, [beating](https://www.boxingscene.com/articles/aaron-mckenna-new-ibf-middleweight-champion-after-outpointing-etinosa-oliha) unbeaten Italian Etinosa Oliha by unanimous decision in Dublin.

The bout for the vacant title took place at the 3Arena as part of the Zuffa Boxing 10 card. It was McKenna's first professional fight in his home country and his first attempt at a world title. Judges [awarded](https://www.worldboxingnews.com/mckenna-vs-oliha-live-scorecard/) the Irishman a unanimous decision after 12 rounds — 118:110, 117:111 and 116:112.

Both boxers had entered the ring unbeaten before the fight. Oliha [suffered](https://www.skysports.com/boxing/news/12183/13571057/aaron-mckenna-wins-dream-homecoming-bout-against-etinosa-oliha-to-become-ibf-middleweight-world-champion-in-dublin) the first defeat of his professional career.

"New world champion. I've been saying this for years... My father sacrificed his whole life for me and my brother... Finally, I'm holding this belt in my hands," McKenna [said](https://www.skysports.com/boxing/news/12183/13571057/aaron-mckenna-wins-dream-homecoming-bout-against-etinosa-oliha-to-become-ibf-middleweight-world-champion-in-dublin) after the fight.

## Why the title was vacant

The IBF vacated the middleweight title in March 2026, [stripping](https://www.worldboxingnews.com/ibf-vacates-middleweight-janibek/) Kazakhstan's Janibek Alimkhanuly of the belt for violating anti-doping rules. A sample taken on November 15, 2025, tested positive for meldonium — a result confirmed by a second sample. The Association of Boxing Commissions suspended Alimkhanuly effective December 2, 2025. He would not have been able to make his mandatory IBF title defense by July 4, 2026. The World Boxing Organization (WBO) made a different decision, keeping the belt with Alimkhanuly and limiting his punishment to a one-year suspension.

## What's next

McKenna must make a voluntary defense of the title by December 8, 2026, or the IBF will order a fight against mandatory challenger Amari Jones, Yahoo Sports [reported](https://sports.yahoo.com/boxing/live/aaron-mckenna-vs-etinosa-oliha-live-results-round-by-round-updates-ring-walks-start-time-for-zuffa-boxing-10-060000870.html).
