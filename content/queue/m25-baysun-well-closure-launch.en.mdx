Uzbekneftegaz on August 15 announced the start of practical work to seal an emergency well at the "25 Years of Independence" field in the Boysun district of Surkhandarya region — almost two years after the gas blowout began.

## What Happened on August 15

Uzbekneftegaz leadership, representatives of the Boysun district hokimiyat and specialists from the Chinese corporation CNPC met with district residents and presented [the plan for sealing the emergency well](https://t.me/neftgaz_press_sekretar/310). Practical work to eliminate the accident officially began the same day.

## The Accident Has Lasted Almost Two Years

A technological accident at well No. 604 of the M-25 field occurred on September 1, 2024: during drilling operations the technological process was disrupted and gas containing hydrogen sulfide began escaping from the well, [the Ministry of Energy reported](https://gov.uz/uz/minenergy/news/view/22006). By September 15, 2024, the blowout had been brought under control, but on September 17, 2024, the same well suffered a repeat high-pressure gas blowout. As a result, 4 people died and 11 were hospitalized, ten of whom were discharged that same evening.

The Ministry of Energy noted that conditions at the field complicate the work: hydrogen sulfide content in the extracted gas reaches 7%, pressure reaches up to 65 megapascals, and temperature up to 150 degrees Celsius.

## Who Uzbekneftegaz Is Working With

The Chinese drilling company CCDC — a subsidiary of CNPC — has been brought in to eliminate the accident. On July 24, Uzbekneftegaz leadership [held a working meeting with CCDC representatives](https://teletype.in/@uzbekneftegaz_ung_ru/ixGhIjpEWlq), discussing the delivery of drilling rigs, high-tech equipment for work under elevated pressure, the involvement of specialists and the finalization of design solutions for the well.

## What's Next

According to Uzbekneftegaz, drilling and subsequent work to seal the well will take several more months; the company has not yet disclosed the exact completion timeline, the cost of the work, or the volume of accumulated gas released.
