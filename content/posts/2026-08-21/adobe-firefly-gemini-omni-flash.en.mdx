Adobe on August 20 [made available](https://blog.adobe.com/en/publish/2026/08/20/adobe-firefly-expands-its-creative-ai-studio-generate-music-speech-and-sound-effects-in-one-place) AI tools for generating music, speech and sound effects in its Firefly service, and added the Google Gemini Omni Flash model to it.

Firefly is Adobe's web service for generating images and video using artificial intelligence; three new tools now add sound work to the same interface, [Adobe said in the post](https://blog.adobe.com/en/publish/2026/08/20/adobe-firefly-expands-its-creative-ai-studio-generate-music-speech-and-sound-effects-in-one-place).

Each tool relies on a separate Adobe model. Generate Music runs on the Firefly Music Model and creates instrumental tracks matched to a video's length and mood. Generate Sound Effects uses the Firefly Audio Model and generates sounds from a text description of an action. Generate Speech, built on the Firefly Speech Model, turns script text into a voiceover with a choice of voice, pace and emotional tone. The option includes voices from Adobe partner ElevenLabs, a voice synthesis service, [Adobe said](https://blog.adobe.com/en/publish/2026/08/20/adobe-firefly-expands-its-creative-ai-studio-generate-music-speech-and-sound-effects-in-one-place). Output from all three tools is licensed for commercial use, [Adobe said in the post](https://blog.adobe.com/en/publish/2026/08/20/adobe-firefly-expands-its-creative-ai-studio-generate-music-speech-and-sound-effects-in-one-place).

Firefly also integrated [Gemini Omni Flash](https://deepmind.google/models/model-cards/gemini-omni-flash/), a multimodal Google DeepMind model that accepts text, images, video and audio as input. A prompt — a text command for the neural network — can now be supplemented with video, audio and images. A user describes a rough idea and edits a video scene over several steps of dialogue, [Adobe said](https://blog.adobe.com/en/publish/2026/08/20/adobe-firefly-expands-its-creative-ai-studio-generate-music-speech-and-sound-effects-in-one-place).

Google embeds a digital SynthID watermark in the model's output to verify content origin. The developer currently restricts the feature that replaces real people's voices in video, [according to the model card](https://deepmind.google/models/model-cards/gemini-omni-flash/). The model card was published on May 19, 2026 — Gemini Omni Flash was added to Firefly three months later.
