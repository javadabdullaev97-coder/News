Alibaba Group reported results on August 20 for the quarter ended June 30, 2026: revenue from its cloud division rose 45% year-over-year, while capital expenditure on AI infrastructure grew 75%.

The company's total revenue grew 9% year-over-year, reaching 268.95 billion yuan ($39.64 billion), Alibaba Group said in an [investor report](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001577552/000110465926099220/tm2623667d1_ex99-1.htm). Net profit attributable to shareholders fell nearly 75% to 10.5 billion yuan ($1.6 billion). A year earlier the figure stood at 43.1 billion yuan ($6.4 billion), [Associated Press reported](https://www.clickondetroit.com/business/2026/08/20/alibaba-quarterly-profit-drops-75-as-ai-investment-spending-grows/).

Revenue from the AI Cloud and Compute Services division grew 45% year-over-year. The division covers cloud computing and AI-based services. The figure reached 48.44 billion yuan ($7.14 billion), [the report said](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001577552/000110465926099220/tm2623667d1_ex99-1.htm). Revenue from AI-related products reached 12.38 billion yuan ($1.82 billion). It has grown at triple-digit rates for twelve consecutive quarters, the company said.

Capital expenditure — spending on servers, chips and data centers — grew 75% year-over-year. The figure reached 67.68 billion yuan (about $10 billion). A year earlier, capital spending stood at 38.68 billion yuan. Alibaba attributed the growth to three factors: increased computing capacity amid demand for AI agents, fluctuations in the procurement cycle, and higher component prices, [the report said](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001577552/000110465926099220/tm2623667d1_ex99-1.htm).

Alibaba is one of China's largest companies. Last year the company said it planned to invest at least 380 billion yuan (about $56 billion) over three years. The funds will go toward cloud computing and AI infrastructure, [Associated Press noted](https://www.clickondetroit.com/business/2026/08/20/alibaba-quarterly-profit-drops-75-as-ai-investment-spending-grows/).

Alibaba CEO Eddie Wu said revenue growth from cloud and AI services would "continue to accelerate in the coming quarters alongside further profitability gains." This will happen as the company expands its computing capacity, [Associated Press reported](https://www.clickondetroit.com/business/2026/08/20/alibaba-quarterly-profit-drops-75-as-ai-investment-spending-grows/).
