The National Statistics Committee of the Republic of Uzbekistan has published the [preliminary results](https://stat.uz/ru/press-tsentr/novosti-goskomstata/69022-predvaritel-nye-rezul-taty-perepisi-naseleniya-i-sel-skogo-khozyajstva-provedennoj-v-respublike-uzbekistan-v-2026-godu) of the 2026 Population and Agriculture Census: as of January 15, [39,047,321 people](https://t.me/census_2026/1287) were living in the country.

## How the Census Was Conducted

The census was conducted [from January 15 to February 28, 2026](https://gov.uz/ru/pages/2026) in two stages. From January 15 to 31, residents could register themselves through the census.stat.uz portal using the OneID system, and from February 4 to 28, mahalla workers went door to door with tablets. It was based on [presidential decree No. UP-173 of September 19, 2025](https://gov.uz/ru/pages/2026), "On Conducting the Population and Agriculture Census in the Republic of Uzbekistan." The questionnaire included 71 questions — 54 on population and 17 on agriculture.

The census Telegram channel states that this is the first [population and agriculture census conducted in the country](https://t.me/census_2026/1299) in the history of independent Uzbekistan.

## Population Size and Structure

The new figure is [810,617 people, or 2.1%, higher](https://t.me/census_2026/1280) than the previous estimate from current statistical records — 38,236,704 people as of January 1, 2026.

According to the census, Uzbekistan has [19,766,166 men (50.6%) and 19,281,155 women (49.4%)](https://t.me/census_2026/1294). The urban population is [21,276,729 people (54.5%), and the rural population is 17,770,592 people (45.5%)](https://t.me/census_2026/1294).

Age structure: [12.5 million people (32%) are below working age, 21.7 million (55.7%) are of working age, and 4.8 million (12.3%) are above working age](https://t.me/census_2026/1293).

Uzbeks make up [89.4% of the country's population](https://t.me/census_2026/1272), and [91.3% of residents](https://t.me/census_2026/1272) named Uzbek as their native language.

## Housing Stock and Infrastructure

The census identified [78,200 additional housing units](https://t.me/census_2026/1288) that had not previously been recorded in current statistics. In total, the country has [6.3 million real estate units and 193,518 km of internal streets](https://t.me/census_2026/1288).

## Agriculture

A separate section of the census covered agriculture — the second most significant part of the questionnaire after the demographic section. As of January 15, 2026, households of citizens and legal entities across the country held [12.2 million cattle, 20.3 million sheep, 2.8 million goats, 251,000 horses, 120.9 million poultry, and 917,000 bee colonies](https://t.me/census_2026/1296).

The National Statistics Committee has not yet published data on cultivated areas or the number of farms and dehqon (household) farms — LEAP News will monitor the release of this part of the report.
