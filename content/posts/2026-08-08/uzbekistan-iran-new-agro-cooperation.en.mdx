Uzbekistan's Minister of Agriculture Ibrohim Abdurakhmonov discussed new areas of cooperation in agriculture with Iranian Ambassador Mohammad Ali Iskandari on August 7 — from mechanization to joint investment projects.

## What was agreed

The meeting between the minister and the Extraordinary and Plenipotentiary Ambassador of the Islamic Republic of Iran to Uzbekistan [took place](https://www.gov.uz/oz/news/view/203424) on August 7. The parties outlined several areas of practical cooperation:

- mechanization of agriculture and the introduction of modern machinery and technology;
- development of agricultural science and innovation;
- expansion of exports of mineral fertilizers and legumes from Uzbekistan to Iran;
- joint investment projects in seed production and pedigree livestock breeding;
- study of advanced Iranian experience in agriculture, and training and internships for specialists.

[The parties confirmed](https://www.gov.uz/oz/news/view/203424) their readiness to consistently continue practical cooperation in these areas, noting the importance of strengthening economic ties and ensuring food security. The government portal does not publish specific investment amounts, supply volumes or project implementation timelines.

## What's next

The publication [does not name](https://www.gov.uz/oz/news/view/203424) any next steps — working groups, dates for signing agreements, or the relevant agencies that will develop the listed areas. Details may appear in further statements from Uzbekistan's Ministry of Agriculture or the Iranian embassy.
