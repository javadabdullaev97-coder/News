The State Tax Committee said on August 1 that 112.8 billion soums in June cashback had been confirmed for payment to more than 5.5 million consumers, while more than 96,000 citizens were added to the registry of suspicious buyers.

## How Much Was Confirmed for Payment

June cashback totaled [112.8 billion soums](https://t.me/soliqnews/20326), about $9.4 million at the [Central Bank's exchange rate](https://cbu.uz/ru/arkhiv-kursov-valyut/json/USD/2026-07-31/) for July 31 (12,006.39 soums per dollar). The money is owed to more than 5.5 million consumers who registered fiscal receipts in the tax authorities' mobile app.

Confirmation does not come immediately or to everyone at once. The State Tax Committee [explained](https://t.me/soliqnews/20326) that the remaining amounts are confirmed automatically as sellers submit tax reports, resolve discrepancies in them, and pay off tax arrears.

Consumers get back 1% of a purchase's value. The cap is purchases worth no more than 24,720,000 soums a month, meaning a maximum of [247,200 soums](https://t.me/soliqnews/19428) in cashback.

Cashback [is not accrued](https://t.me/soliqnews/20326) for:

- receipts older than 10 days;
- car, plane, and train tickets;
- communication services;
- utility services.

## What Gets People Added to the Suspicious Buyers Registry

The registry is a list of people the tax authorities consider participants in schemes involving other people's receipts. It covers those who register receipts for purchases made by someone else — receipts obtained from intermediaries or from mailings in social media. Such a person receives cashback for goods they did not buy themselves. Following June, [more than 96,000 citizens](https://t.me/soliqnews/20326) were added to the registry; following May, the State Tax Committee [reported](https://t.me/soliqnews/19801) on June 23 more than 123,000.

The consequence is direct: under [clause 7 of Cabinet of Ministers Resolution No. 255](https://lex.uz/docs/-6008267) dated May 12, 2022, receipts of people entered in the registry are not registered in the tax authorities' mobile app. The cashback mechanism stops working for such a person — the app no longer accepts their receipts.

On May 25, 2026, the State Tax Committee [warned](https://t.me/soliqnews/19336) that registering other people's receipts distributed through social media or intermediaries is unlawful and carries liability.

## What to Do if Someone Else Registered Your Receipt

In the same May 25 clarification, the agency [described the procedure](https://t.me/soliqnews/19336) for a legitimate buyer whose receipt was appropriated by someone else. Such a buyer is entitled to submit documents to the tax authorities confirming that they made the purchase and received the QR receipt. If the confirmation is accepted, the cashback is returned to them.

If these circumstances cannot be confirmed with documents, the question of returning the cashback is resolved in court.
