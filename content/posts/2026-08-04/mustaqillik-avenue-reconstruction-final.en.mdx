The Center for Road Traffic Organization (CODD) of Tashkent and the press service of the Tashkent hokimiyat announced on August 3 that the reconstruction of Mustaqillik Avenue on a 1.7-kilometer stretch has entered its final stage.

## Work completed on the site

Builders [milled](https://t.me/codd_tashkent/290) 46,078 square meters of old asphalt pavement and reinforced the road base. A layer of coarse-grained asphalt concrete 9 centimeters thick was laid on the same area, and 57,968 square meters of basalt geogrid were placed between the asphalt layers — it increases the pavement's strength and extends its service life. The final layer was a wear-resistant PSHMA-20 top layer 5 centimeters thick, also covering 46,078 square meters.

For public transport passengers on the avenue, [six modern bus stops were installed](https://t.me/codd_tashkent/290). New traffic lights were installed at intersections and the traffic control system was updated.

The drainage system was also renewed: 1,800 meters of drainage channels were replaced, another 1,600 meters of channels and 111 running meters of metal pipes were laid where there had been no stormwater drainage before, plus 500 meters of existing metal pipes were renewed. A total of 3,400 meters of curbs were installed on the roadway and shoulders, and sidewalks and crosswalks were put in order. Separately, 1,700 meters of irrigation system pipes were laid and green areas along the street were landscaped.

## Timeline of the works

The reconstruction has been underway since July 2, when CODD [closed](https://t.me/codd_tashkent/253) the section of the avenue from Mirzo Ulugbek Street to Akkurgan Street to traffic — work there continued until July 13. The next step, from July 16 to 31, saw the center close the adjacent stretch — from Parkent Street to Mirzo Ulugbek Street — while simultaneously [opening](https://t.me/codd_tashkent/262) the already repaired section from Mirzo Ulugbek to Akkurgan.

From 23:00 on July 31 to 22:00 on August 3, CODD additionally [restricted](https://t.me/codd_tashkent/288) traffic on the adjoining Akkurgan and Parkent streets — asphalt laying for the second stage of the works was being completed there.

CODD operates [under the Tashkent hokimiyat](https://tashkent.uz/ru/news/7306) and is responsible for organizing road traffic in the capital.

## What's next

CODD and the hokimiyat have not yet named the exact date for the full completion of Mustaqillik Avenue's reconstruction — both statements describe the current stage as final, but without final delivery deadlines for the project.
