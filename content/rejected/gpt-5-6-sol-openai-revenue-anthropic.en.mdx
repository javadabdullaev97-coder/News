OpenAI's revenue grew 35% since the start of the third quarter of 2026 after the launch of the GPT-5.6 Sol model in early July, the company told employees at an internal meeting on July 29, [CNBC reports](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/29/openai-cfo-sarah-friar-tells-employees-arr-in-july-topped-all-of-q2.html).

This is annualized revenue (ARR) — a metric that converts the current sales run rate into an annual figure. OpenAI calculates and discloses it this way itself: the company remains private and does not publish financial statements. According to Chief Financial Officer Sarah Friar, revenue from enterprise customers is growing even faster — [more than 50%](https://the-decoder.com/gpt-5-6-sol-drives-openais-revenue-surge-as-it-regains-ground-on-anthropic/) since the start of the quarter, The Decoder writes. LEAP News could not open the CNBC page describing this meeting directly to verify the exact wording — The Decoder independently reproduces the 35% and 50%+ growth figures.

Friar and board chair Bret Taylor attribute the growth to three products — the GPT-5.6 Sol model, the enterprise AI agent ChatGPT Work, and the Codex coding tool. CNBC reported this following the July 29 meeting; its page is not accessible for direct verification, and other sources for this article do not reproduce this detail.

## What Ramp's data shows

Ramp tracks spending on corporate cards and accounts for more than 70,000 companies in the US and publishes a monthly index of business AI spending. In its [August 12 release](https://ramp.com/data/ai-index-august-2026), Ramp notes that Anthropic still leads OpenAI by share of client companies — 43.5% versus 39.7%. Anthropic's share is also growing faster — up 1.1 percentage points over the month versus 0.23 for OpenAI.

More recent Ramp data points to a shift in growth rates, but not in market share. Ramp economist Ara Kharazian told [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/20/openai-is-gaining-on-anthropic-with-business-users-new-data-indicates/) that OpenAI is growing faster than Anthropic in business API spending in the third quarter of 2026. Ramp did not disclose exact dollar figures. According to The Decoder, OpenAI's quarter-over-quarter API spending growth was 82% versus 76% for Anthropic. The source of this figure is a post by Kharazian on X, which LEAP News could not open and verify directly. TechCrunch, whose article is closer to the original source and includes a direct quote from Kharazian, does not cite these figures at all — only a qualitative assessment of the growth.

"GPT-5.6 Sol is genuinely good and is increasingly becoming developers' choice," Kharazian told TechCrunch. "Anthropic's Fable 5 model has disappointed both in adoption pace and real-world use — because of pricing and regulators' data storage requirements."

In total revenue, Anthropic still leads OpenAI. According to Wall Street Journal and Bloomberg data cited by The Decoder, Anthropic's quarterly revenue was $11.6 billion versus $6.7 billion for OpenAI. Anthropic's annualized revenue reached $65 billion. LEAP News did not verify these figures directly with WSJ or Bloomberg.

## What's next

OpenAI is considering a public share offering in 2027 but is not ruling out an earlier IPO if the current growth pace continues. Friar told employees this on August 19, [CNBC reports](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/19/open-ai-ipo-timing-2027-friar.html). The CNBC page is not accessible for direct verification of the quote, and there is no independent confirmation of the IPO date in other sources for this article.
