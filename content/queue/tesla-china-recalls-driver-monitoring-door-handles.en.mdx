Tesla filed a plan on August 21 to recall about 2.7 million Model 3 and Model Y vehicles in China, according to several outlets; there is no official confirmation as of publication.

The cause was that with the steering assistant engaged, the system only monitored torque on the steering wheel and did not adequately check whether the driver was looking at the road, [NBD reports](https://www.nbd.com.cn/articles/2026-08-21/4550354.html). According to the outlet, the recall covers 2,740,642 China-built vehicles.

Tesla plans to fix the issue through an over-the-air (OTA) update: torque monitoring will be supplemented with driver gaze tracking via the in-cabin camera, [writes 快科技 (mydrivers.com)](https://news.mydrivers.com/1/1145/1145518.htm). According to the same outlet, the recall covers vehicles built between March 4, 2019, and December 7, 2025.

The same day, Tesla filed a plan for another recall with China's State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR). The reason is concealed door handles that are hard to find after a crash; details were [previously reported by LEAP News](https://leap.uz/en/2026/08/21/tesla-china-recall).
