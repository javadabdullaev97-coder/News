Matnli tavsiflar asosida ilova yaratuvchi xizmat ishlab chiquvchi shvetsiyalik Lovable startapi 12 avgustda Series C raundida [$400 mln jalb qildi](https://lovable.dev/blog/series-c) — kompaniya qiymati ikki baravar oshib, $13,3 mlrdga yetdi.

Raundning yetakchi investori Menlo Ventures jamg'armasi bo'ldi, hamyetakchi investor sifatida [Scaleup Europe Fund](https://eqtgroup.com/news/eqt-selected-to-lead-the-scaleup-europe-fund-2026-05-18) qatnashdi — uni Yevrokomissiya topshirig'i bilan EQT investitsiya kompaniyasi boshqaradi. Raundga Balderton Capital, Carmignac, Kaszek Ventures, Tencent va World Innovation Lab ham qo'shildi. Mavjud investorlar — Accel, DST Global, Salesforce Ventures va HubSpot Ventures — o'z ulushlarini oshirdi, [xabar qildi](https://tech.eu/2026/08/12/lovables-valuation-doubles-to-13bn-as-raises-400m/) tech.eu.

## Raundda kimlar ishtirok etdi

Scaleup Europe Fund — hajmi 5 mlrd yevro bo'lgan jamg'arma. [2026-yil mayidan buyon uni EQT boshqaradi](https://eqtgroup.com/news/eqt-selected-to-lead-the-scaleup-europe-fund-2026-05-18), jamg'armani Yevrokomissiya xususiy investorlar — jumladan Novo Holdings, Santander va Allianz — bilan birgalikda moliyalashtiradi. Jamg'arma o'sish bosqichidagi yevropalik texnologik kompaniyalarni — sun'iy intellekt va kvant hisoblashdan tortib biotexnologiya va kosmosgacha — qo'llab-quvvatlash uchun tashkil etilgan.

Lovable oldingi raundni 2025-yil dekabrida yakunlagan — $330 mln, $6,6 mlrd qiymatda, [deb ko'rsatadi](https://aibusiness.com/generative-ai/euro-vibe-coding-standout-lovable-valued-at-13-3b) AiBusiness. Sakkiz oy ichida qiymat ikki baravar oshdi.

## Lovable yarim yil ichida qanday o'sdi

Lovable xizmatini 2024-yil noyabrida asoschilar Anton Osika va Fabian Xedin ishga tushirgan. Ishga tushirilgandan beri foydalanuvchilar 60 mln dan ortiq loyiha yaratdi, platformada yaratilgan ilovalarga oyiga 900 mln martadan ortiq tashrif buyuriladi, [deb yozilgan kompaniya blogida](https://lovable.dev/blog/series-c). Lovable mijozlari orasida Nvidia, Adidas va Deutsche Telekomni nomlaydi.

Kompaniya 2026-yilda xodimlar sonini 50 foizga — 450 nafargacha oshirishni [rejalashtirmoqda](https://lovable.dev/blog/series-c), ustuvorlik mashinali o'rganish, infratuzilma va xavfsizlik sohalarida yollashga beriladi. Lovable Bostonda, San-Fransiskoda, Nyu-Yorkda va Londonda faoliyatini kengaytirmoqda, shtab-kvartirasi esa Stokgolmda saqlanib qolmoqda, [deb xabar beradi](https://tech.eu/2026/08/12/lovables-valuation-doubles-to-13bn-as-raises-400m/) tech.eu.
