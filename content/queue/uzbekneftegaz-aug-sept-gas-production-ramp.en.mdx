Joint-stock company Uzbekneftegaz has declared August and September months of ramping up natural gas production — according to [a statement](https://teletype.in/@uzbekneftegaz_ung_uz/jr7k2Ifm-aU) from the company's press service following an August 7 working meeting.

## What Was Decided at the Meeting

The August 7 meeting was chaired by Uzbekneftegaz Chairman Abdugani Sanginov. Participants [discussed](https://teletype.in/@uzbekneftegaz_ung_uz/jr7k2Ifm-aU) increasing gas production volumes, efficient field operation, modernization of production infrastructure and workers' living and working conditions. The 10-day action plan for ramping up production set for August was analyzed by day, well and field.

The company says reforms launched since the start of the year led to August and September being designated as months of ramping up gas production. During August, 27 wells are planned to be brought into operation.

## Technical Instructions: Power Supply and Well Repairs

Management instructed staff to improve the reliability of power supply and power facilities, upgrade transformer equipment, replace insulators and ensure efficient operation of gas-piston power stations. A separate task is to speed up major and routine well repairs and clearly delineate responsibility for this process; to improve well repair technology, the company intends to cooperate with research institutes in China.

To ensure drilling work in the regions is completed on time, staff were instructed to speed up deliveries of imported equipment, prepare roads and infrastructure in advance, and bring in additional equipment.

## Preparing for 2027 After Shortcomings in Investment Program Calculations

The meeting [recorded](https://teletype.in/@uzbekneftegaz_ung_uz/jr7k2Ifm-aU) systemic shortcomings in the calculations for the 2026 targeted and investment programs — in light of this, the company has been instructed to begin preparing high-quality programs for 2027 already now. The agenda includes streamlining projects, speeding up 3D seismic surveys, forming a geological exploration program for 2027, timely organization of work on new blocks, and preventing delays at construction sites.

## Working Conditions in the Ustyurt Region Deemed Unsatisfactory

Meeting participants [assessed](https://teletype.in/@uzbekneftegaz_ung_uz/jr7k2Ifm-aU) working conditions at production facilities in the Ustyurt region as unsatisfactory. The company has been instructed to fully renovate dormitories for workers, provide them with protective clothing and bring living conditions up to modern standards.

## Context: National Gas Output Is Falling

The announcement of the production ramp-up months follows Uzbekneftegaz's report on financial results for the first half of 2026: the company reported 110% year-on-year growth in net profit, while the National Statistics Committee of the Republic of Uzbekistan [recorded](https://stat.uz/img/press-relizlar/press-reliz-sanoat-07-2026-rus_p46063.pdf) a 16.4% drop in national natural gas output — to 18.3 billion cubic meters, down from 21.9 billion a year earlier. At the same time, Chairman Sanginov instructed staff to develop a plan to curb the decline in output at wells of the Mubarek Oil and Gas Production Department, [LEAP News reported](https://leap.uz/en/2026/08/05/uzbekneftegaz-profit-110pct-mubarek-plan).

## What's Next

The company did not name a target figure for the overall production increase in August and September, nor did it specify a deadline for approving the plan to curb the output decline at Mubarek that was announced in late July. The specific dates in the published materials are: bringing 27 wells into operation in August, completing dormitory repairs at Ustyurt by August 25, and finishing dormitory repairs at Kokdumalak, Pomuk and Alan by year-end.
