Overnight on August 5, Ecopolice detained two men who were spearfishing in a canal in the Tarakkiyot mahalla (neighborhood) of Syrdarya district, the National Committee of the Republic of Uzbekistan for Ecology and Climate Change (the Ecology Committee) [reported](https://gov.uz/ru/eco/news/view/202432).

## How the poachers were discovered

The raid was carried out jointly by officers of the Syrdarya Water Basin Control Department of the State Inspectorate for Environmental Control (Ecopolice) and the Syrdarya Region Ecology Department, according to a [post](https://t.me/ecogovuz/49467) on the agency's Telegram channel. The two men, who were fishing after dark, were spotted from the air using a drone.

A rubber boat, a fishing spear, a pump and a pair of oars were confiscated from the detainees — all the items were handed over for safekeeping to the regional ecology department, the agency [specified](https://gov.uz/ru/eco/news/view/202432). The poachers' catch consisted of eight snakehead fish, one grass carp and one crucian carp.

## What the offenders face

Reports were filed against the offenders under Part 4 of Article 90 of the Code of the Republic of Uzbekistan on Administrative Liability. This article establishes liability for hunting and fishing without a permit, in prohibited areas, during prohibited periods, or using prohibited fishing gear. According to the Ecology Committee, the case materials were sent to the Syrdarya District Criminal Court for further review.

The agency did not explain why a case under an administrative article was referred to a court that handles criminal cases — this point requires clarification from the Ecology Committee itself.
