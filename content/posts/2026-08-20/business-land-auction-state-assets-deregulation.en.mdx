President Shavkat Mirziyoyev announced on August 18 at the 6th Open Dialogue with Entrepreneurs in the Khorezm region that land sold at auctions will now come with a ready-made construction permit — instead of the current six to 12 months of waiting.

The [Telegram channel of the president's press secretary](https://t.me/Press_Secretary_Uz/7715) said local governments (hokimiyats) will put land plots up for auction as a "ready package" — together with an architectural planning assignment, a project and a construction permit. The advance payment for the infrastructure fee when buying land will be cut from 20% to 5%.

## State Assets and Bank Property

The down payment for buying state assets will be cut from 30% to 15%. An entrepreneur who pays for an asset in full within six months will get a 25% discount. Those who pay at least half the price will be allowed to pay the remainder interest-free over seven years. If an asset finds no buyer within three months, the starting price will be cut by 10% every following three months — all the terms are [listed in the same post](https://t.me/Press_Secretary_Uz/7715).

Banks will be allowed to sell businesses non-core property on their balance sheets — about 2,500 properties worth 9 trillion soums (about $759 million at the Central Bank's exchange rate on August 18). These assets will be sold at a discount, with no down payment and no interest on installments. The buyer will be exempt from property and land tax for a year.

## Five Bonded Industrial Zones

Five "bonded industrial zones" will be created in the free economic zones of Navoi, Jizzakh, Namangan, Urgut and Khazarasp to produce 50 goods prioritized for localization. Buildings with ready-made infrastructure for these zones will get $50 million in funding. Entrepreneurs will not pay taxes or customs duties on raw materials and components imported into the zones. At a localization level of 30-40% and above, they will get additional benefits on the domestic market.

## Licenses and Courts

Licenses and permits will be abolished for more than 30 types of activity. For another 35 types, issuance time will be cut two to three times, and 10 permits will move to a notification-based system, according to the [president's press secretary channel](https://t.me/Press_Secretary_Uz/7723). Veterinary, sanitary and phytosanitary permits for exporting products will be replaced with a single "health certificate," which will be piloted for six months in three regions.

Fines for businesses will be cut roughly in half on average — currently 51 government agencies have the right to fine entrepreneurs, [Mirziyoyev said](https://t.me/Press_Secretary_Uz/7722). Starting next year, disputes between businesses and the state will introduce a "presumption of the entrepreneur's innocence": sanctions will not apply until guilt is proven in court. For a first violation that causes no harm to life, health or property, entrepreneurs will get 10 days to fix it instead of facing punishment. The state will cover the court fee from the second judicial instance in disputes with regulatory bodies. The expert council under the Chamber of Commerce and Industry will get the right to review disputes in customs, construction, sanitation, quarantine and veterinary matters.
