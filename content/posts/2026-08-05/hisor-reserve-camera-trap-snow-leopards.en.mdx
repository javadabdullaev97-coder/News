Camera traps in the Tankhozdarya area of the Hisor State Nature Reserve in Kashkadarya Region photographed a group of snow leopards and a Siberian ibex, the National Committee of the Republic of Uzbekistan for Ecology and Climate Change [said](https://t.me/ecogovuz/49462) on August 4.

Cameras installed in the Tankhozdarya area captured snow leopards moving as a group, as well as a Siberian ibex, [the committee said](https://t.me/ecogovuz/49462) in a post on its Telegram channel. The exact number of animals in the group and the date of the footage were not disclosed.

The Hisor State Nature Reserve is located in Kashkadarya Region, in the mountains on the southwestern spurs of the Gissar Range, and covers [80,986.1 hectares](https://gov.uz/oz/eco/news/view/22695) of territory. The snow leopard is among the species found here that are listed in the Red Book of Uzbekistan.
