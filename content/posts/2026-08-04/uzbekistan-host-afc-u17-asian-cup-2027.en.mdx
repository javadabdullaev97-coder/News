The Executive Committee of the Asian Football Confederation (AFC) on July 30 in Kuala Lumpur transferred hosting rights for the 2027 AFC U-17 Asian Cup to Uzbekistan, previously assigned to Saudi Arabia.

## What the AFC Executive Committee Decided

According to [The Peninsula Qatar](https://thepeninsulaqatar.com/article/30/07/2026/japan-confirmed-as-host-nation-of-asia-u-23-football-cup-2028), the AFC Executive Committee at the same meeting transferred hosting rights for the 2027 U-17 Asian Cup to Uzbekistan, while awarding the 2029 edition to Saudi Arabia. The original article puts it this way: "the AFC transferred the hosting rights for the 2027 AFC U-17 Asian Cup to Uzbekistan, while awarding the 2029 edition to Saudi Arabia."

The decision on the 2027 U-17 Cup was made at the same meeting where the AFC Executive Committee confirmed Japan as host of the 2028 U-23 Asian Cup — the Olympic qualifier for the 2028 Los Angeles Games. Japan's bid was chosen over competing bids from Jordan, Kyrgyzstan, Qatar, and Uzbekistan itself; the UAE had earlier withdrawn its bid. The AFC's [media releases page](https://www.the-afc.com/en/about_afc/media/media_releases.html) (a general listing, not a standalone article) covering the July 30 meeting focuses mainly on the U-23 tournament. There is no mention in the publicly available part of the transfer of 2027 U-17 hosting rights to Uzbekistan — this detail is documented through The Peninsula Qatar, which quoted the AFC's decision.

## UFA Confirms the Tournament

The Uzbekistan Football Association (UFA) [announced](https://t.me/FAUzbekistan/88934) on its official Telegram channel that the 2027 U-17 Asian Cup will be held in Uzbekistan. The post went out on the same day as the AFC Executive Committee meeting in Kuala Lumpur — July 30.

## Saudi Arabia Loses One Tournament, Gains Another

Saudi Arabia had originally been awarded hosting rights for three consecutive editions of the U-17 Asian Cup — 2026, 2027, and 2028. The decision was [made](https://www.the-afc.com/en/about_afc/committees.html/news/saudi-arabia-recommended-as-host-of-afc-u17-asian-cup™-from-2026-to-2028) by the AFC Competitions Committee in Jeddah on May 3, 2025, beating out bids from China and Iraq.

Saudi Arabia has already hosted the 2026 tournament — in the [May 22 final](https://www.the-afc.com/en/national/afc_u17_asian_cup.html/news/japan-survive-china-pr-fightback-to-clinch-title), Japan's national team beat China 3-2. Now the 2027 edition has been transferred to Uzbekistan, and Saudi Arabia, according to The Peninsula Qatar, received the 2029 edition in return.

## Uzbekistan Is the Tournament's Defending Champion

Uzbekistan's national team holds the current U-17 Asian Cup title. In April 2025, the team [won](https://www.the-afc.com/en/national/afc_u17_asian_cup.html/news/final-preview-uzbekistan-v-saudi-arabia) the final against tournament hosts Saudi Arabia in the city of Taif.

## What's Next

The AFC has not yet published exact dates or venues for the 2027 final tournament in Uzbekistan, nor details of the qualifying format. Several outlets cite the period May 12–29, 2027, but LEAP News cannot confirm this date directly from an official release or the UFA channel — the editorial team will clarify the calendar and qualification rules once the AFC publishes them.
