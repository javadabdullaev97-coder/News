Israel struck southern Lebanon on August 14-16 in attacks authorities called the deadliest since the June ceasefire began — at least 11 people were killed, Lebanon's Ministry of Health said on the evening of August 16.

The highest death tolls were recorded in the villages of Ansar and Deir Zahrani in Nabatieh district. Seven people were killed in Ansar, including three children, with two wounded. Four people were killed in Deir Zahrani, with 17 wounded — the figures, cited from Lebanon's Ministry of Health, come from [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/15/israeli-strike-kills-seven-in-south-lebanon-as-attacks-intensify).

The Israeli military said the strikes targeted Hezbollah infrastructure in response to an attack near the Ali al-Taher ridge.

Lebanese Prime Minister Nawaf Salam rejected Israel's justification for the strikes and called the escalation "extremely dangerous." Parliament Speaker Nabih Berri called the incident a "massacre" and a "war of extermination." President Joseph Aoun condemned the actions as a violation of the ceasefire framework agreement, [Al Jazeera reports](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/15/israeli-strike-kills-seven-in-south-lebanon-as-attacks-intensify).

## Memorandum With Iran Expires as Washington Prepares Sanctions

The escalation in Lebanon coincided with the expiration of another document. The memorandum of understanding between the US and Iran was signed by Donald Trump and Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian on June 17 in Islamabad, with Pakistan mediating. It set a 60-day window for negotiating a final deal — a deadline that expires on August 17, [Al Jazeera writes](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/16/us-iran-mou-is-set-to-expire-what-to-know).

Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi said Tehran "has not yet decided whether to resume talks with the US." According to him, the document meant "the end of the war, not a truce," so in his view there is nothing to extend — Washington itself violated the terms. Qatar and Pakistan continue to mediate the talks.

At the same time, Washington is preparing a new package of sanctions against Iran. US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said measures "unlike any before" would be applied, without disclosing details, [Al Jazeera reports](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/16/us-iran-mou-is-set-to-expire-what-to-know). Since July, the US Treasury has repeatedly imposed targeted sanctions through the Office of Foreign Assets Control (OFAC) on networks that help Iran evade restrictions. The latest came on August 7, against currency exchangers and shell companies, [according to the OFAC registry](https://ofac.treasury.gov/recent-actions/20260807).

According to Bloomberg, the aircraft carrier USS Abraham Lincoln has remained in the region for months because of the war with Iran. The outlet also writes about plans to replace it with another carrier strike group, [Bloomberg reports](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-16/us-commander-says-mental-health-a-priority-aboard-uss-lincoln).

Talks between Israel and Lebanon on disarming Hezbollah and withdrawing Israeli troops are proceeding separately, in Rome, based on a deal agreed in June in Washington. The status of the Ali al-Taher ridge, where the attack on Israeli soldiers occurred, was the focus of the dispute between the sides in the latest round of talks, [Al Jazeera reports](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/5/lebanon-and-israel-still-split-over-disarming-hezbollah-at-rome-talks).
