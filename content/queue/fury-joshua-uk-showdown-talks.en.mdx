Promoter Eddie Hearn and boxer Anthony Joshua [insist](https://www.boxingscene.com/anthony-joshua-eddie-hearn-remain-firm-tyson-fury-showdown-taking-place-uk--190123): the fight with Tyson Fury must take place in the UK — those are the contract terms, Hearn said earlier.

In comments published in late July, Hearn said: "The only way this fight doesn't happen in England is if Joshua and I myself agree to that. We have a legally binding contract that took a very long time to negotiate."

The Saudi side wants to move the venue. Turki Al-Sheikh, chairman of Saudi Arabia's General Entertainment Authority, is considering alternative venues, including New York's Madison Square Garden — he wants a wider global audience.

Both sides are targeting November 2026, but the exact venue has not yet been agreed.

## Joshua's Trainer Expects Knockout of Fury

Joshua is currently training with Oleksandr Usyk's team, led by Sergey Lapin. In early-August comments, the trainer [predicted](https://www.boxingscene.com/anthony-joshua-will-prove-you-all-wrong-ko-tyson-fury-says-joshuas-trainer--190087) that Joshua will knock out Fury.

"Joshua will knock out Fury. He's not just a powerful puncher — he's a machine that delivers devastating blows. Tyson has never faced an opponent with this combination of power, speed and physical pressure," Lapin said.

According to the source, Joshua found himself in a difficult position in the first round several times but ultimately knocked out Christian Prenga. After the win, the boxer was criticized for his form, and Lapin responded: "There have always been doubts, and there probably always will be. Throughout our journey, our decisions, methods and the team's level have been questioned. We don't answer with words — we answer with results."

Organizers have not yet named the official venue or exact date of the fight.
