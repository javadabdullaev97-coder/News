The Center for Traffic Organization of Tashkent (CODD) synchronized five traffic lights on a section of the Little Ring Road (Kichik halqa) from Bogibuston to the Chilanzar metro station on August 3 — travel time was cut by almost half.

## What changed on the Little Ring Road

CODD continues to roll out the "green wave" system on the city's streets in stages, the center [said](https://t.me/codd_tashkent/289) in a Telegram post. In the new stage, specialists calculated and mutually coordinated the operation of five traffic light installations on a 1.7-kilometer section of the Little Ring Road (Kichik halqa) — from the intersection with Bogibuston Street to the Chilanzar metro station.

On this stretch, a unified "green wave" mode [worked](https://t.me/codd_tashkent/289) around the clock and in both directions simultaneously for the first time. This was possible because traffic intensity in both directions is nearly equal, CODD explained.

Before synchronization, crossing the section took about 5 minutes on average. After the system launched, the time dropped to 2 minutes 45 seconds — [almost half, a 45% reduction](https://t.me/codd_tashkent/289). CODD recommends drivers travel at 40–60 km/h — at that speed, intersections on the section can be crossed without stopping at a red light.

## Not the first stage

The Little Ring Road is the second section in the city program. CODD [launched](https://tashkent.uz/ru/news/7362) the first one on July 29 on Buyuk Ipak Yoli — from the Tura Buva mosque to Salom Street, synchronizing 13 intersections. Travel time there dropped from 8.5 to 6 minutes (29%) toward the center and from 10 to 8 minutes (20%) in the opposite direction. Unlike the Little Ring Road, the mode on Buyuk Ipak Yoli changes by time of day: in the morning, priority goes to traffic heading toward the center, in the evening — to the opposite direction.

With the new section, CODD has now synchronized 18 traffic light installations under the "green wave" system. The center [operates under](https://tashkent.uz/ru/news/7306) the Tashkent hokimiyat and is responsible for organizing traffic in the capital.

## What's next

CODD has not yet announced the timing or location of the next "green wave" stage.
