Tashkent region prosecutor Azamat Mirzayev held an outreach reception for citizens and legal entities on August 8 — 11 people came with complaints about loans, land, electricity and housing. The Prosecutor General's Office [said](https://t.me/uzbprokuratura/87928).

## What issues were raised

The reception was held to communicate openly with the public, identify systemic problems facing residents of the region and resolve them quickly — that was the stated goal of the meeting, according to the [Prosecutor General's Office](https://t.me/uzbprokuratura/87928). Eleven individuals and legal entities, along with their representatives, attended.

The complaints covered several areas:

- allocation of loans and subsidies;
- land issues and power supply;
- investigative and operational search actions;
- housing and material assistance;
- property disputes.

## Outcome of the reception

The prosecutor's office resolved 2 of the 11 complaints on the spot. The remaining 9 [were accepted for review](https://t.me/uzbprokuratura/87928) — according to the prosecutor's office's wording, they require further examination and time. No timeline for reviewing these 9 complaints was given in the statement.
