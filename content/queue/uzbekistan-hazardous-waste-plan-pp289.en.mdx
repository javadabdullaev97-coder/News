President Shavkat Mirziyoyev signed Resolution PP-289 on August 3 — starting January 1, 2027, hazardous waste owners must annually coordinate their waste management plans with the Waste Management Agency.

## What Hazardous Waste Owners Must Do

The resolution ["On Measures to Improve the Efficiency of Hazardous Waste Management"](https://t.me/huquqiyaxborot/20217) requires organizations that own hazardous waste to develop annual waste management plans. The plans are drawn up based on environmental impact assessments and coordinated with the regional offices of the Agency for Waste Management and Circular Economy Development. That's according to a [report](https://t.me/huquqiyaxborot/20217) from the Ministry of Justice's Telegram channel "Huquqiy axborot."

The plan must include targets for reducing the volume of hazardous waste generated and increasing the share of waste recycled and safely disposed of. Organizations must hand over recycled or disposed waste to specialized licensed enterprises — the Ministry of Justice does not specify whether self-disposal is permitted.

A separate requirement concerns decommissioned facilities and land plots that previously housed hazardous waste. These must be transferred to the Waste Management Agency along with funds for environmental land reclamation.

## Reporting and Deadlines

Organizations must begin keeping annual statistical reporting as early as October 1, 2026 — before the rest of the resolution's provisions take effect. The main requirements — annual plans, their coordination with the Agency, and the transfer of waste to licensed processors — take effect on January 1, 2027.

The Ministry of Justice's published summary of the resolution does not specify the amount of fines or other liability for violating the new requirements.

## Context: Waste Management System Reform

Resolution PP-289 continues a reform launched by Presidential Decree [UP-5 of January 4, 2024](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/6733912), "On Measures to Improve the Waste Management System and Reduce Its Negative Impact on the Environmental Situation." That document introduced circular economy principles and secondary raw material exchanges for household waste in Uzbekistan. The new resolution extends the planning and permitting approach to hazardous waste, which was previously regulated separately from household waste.

On April 30, 2026, Mirziyoyev was [presented](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9163) with a project for an integrated hazardous waste management platform in the Navoi region, valued at $260 million with a processing capacity of 330,000 tons per year. According to its authors, it would be the first platform of its kind in the CIS. The project includes laboratory classification of hazardous waste, its physicochemical and thermal treatment, a specialized landfill, and production of RDF fuel from waste.
