Typhoon Dolphin hit Japan's Okinawa island on August 8, injuring at least six people, AP [reported](https://www.nbcnews.com/world/china/typhoon-dolphin-hits-japans-okinawa-china-shuts-ports-ahead-landfall-rcna591465), while Chinese authorities evacuated more than 420,000 people ahead of the storm's expected landfall on the east coast overnight on Monday, August 10, Vedomosti [reported](https://www.vedomosti.ru/politics/news/2026/08/08/1219761-kitaya-evakuirovali).

## Japan: six injured, tens of thousands of homes without power

Five elderly residents of Okinawa sustained non-life-threatening injuries — three of them fell due to strong winds — and one more person was injured in Kagoshima Prefecture, AP [reported](https://www.nbcnews.com/world/china/typhoon-dolphin-hits-japans-okinawa-china-shuts-ports-ahead-landfall-rcna591465), citing local authorities. Japan's Meteorological Agency recorded sustained winds of up to 162 km/h with gusts of up to 216 km/h.

As of Saturday morning, about 39,000 homes remained without power in Kagoshima Prefecture and more than 12,000 in Okinawa — over 50,000 households combined. Airlines ANA and Japan Airlines canceled flights to and from Okinawa.

The day before, on August 7, authorities had already ordered evacuations for nearly 260,000 residents of Kagoshima and Okinawa prefectures, Reuters [reported](https://www.thestar.com.my/news/world/2026/08/07/japan-orders-evacuations-as-typhoon-dolphin-approaches-cancels-500-flights) — the order remains in effect while the cyclone moves through the Ryukyu archipelago.

## China: over 420,000 evacuated, ports and airport closed

Chinese authorities evacuated more than 420,000 people in coastal provinces — more than 390,000 of them in the city of Taizhou, Zhejiang Province, and over 30,000 in coastal areas of Shanghai, Vedomosti [reported](https://www.vedomosti.ru/politics/news/2026/08/08/1219761-kitaya-evakuirovali), citing China Central Television (CCTV) and the China Meteorological Administration. The agency raised its typhoon response level from fourth to third on August 8 — under the national four-tier scale, where the first level is the highest, Vedomosti [reported](https://www.vedomosti.ru/politics/news/2026/08/08/1219761-kitaya-evakuirovali). Zhejiang provincial authorities raised their own response level to the maximum, Al Jazeera [wrote](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/8/typhoon-dolphin-hits-japan-heads-towards-china).

The Yangshan port near Shanghai has been cleared of vessels, port operations in Zhejiang have been suspended, and 162 passenger ferry routes have been halted, Al Jazeera [wrote](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/8/typhoon-dolphin-hits-japan-heads-towards-china). Ningbo Lishe Airport stopped flights as of Saturday evening, part of rail service in the Yangtze River Delta will be suspended from Sunday, and schools in Ningbo are closed.

The typhoon is forecast to make landfall between late Sunday and early Monday morning, in the area between the city of Zhoushan in Zhejiang and the city of Fuding in Fujian. Sustained winds are expected to weaken to 38–45 m/s by the time of landfall.

## Region: canceled flights and record heat

In Taiwan, 78 flights were canceled due to the typhoon, most of them international, AP [noted](https://www.nbcnews.com/world/china/typhoon-dolphin-hits-japans-okinawa-china-shuts-ports-ahead-landfall-rcna591465). Dolphin is approaching the region amid record heat in East Asia. In Japan, three lions died at a zoo from suspected heatstroke, while in South Korea more than 20 people died from the heat, Al Jazeera [wrote](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/8/typhoon-dolphin-hits-japan-heads-towards-china).

## What's next

Zhejiang and Fujian provinces and the city of Shanghai remain on emergency typhoon response. Rain in these regions is forecast by Chinese meteorologists to continue through August 12, with more than 600 mm of precipitation in some areas. In Japan, the typhoon will continue moving along the Kyushu and Ryukyu islands, keeping the risk of flooding and structural damage until it reaches China overnight on August 10.
