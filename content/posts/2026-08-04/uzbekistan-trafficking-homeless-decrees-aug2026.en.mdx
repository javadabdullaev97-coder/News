President Shavkat Mirziyoyev signed Resolution PP-288 and Decree UP-146 on August 3, turning into concrete rules and deadlines the social protection reform proposals [approved the same day](https://leap.uz/en/2026/08/04/social-protection-reform-2026).

## Homeless people will no longer be sent to centers by court order

Resolution PP-288, ["On improving the system of providing social services to persons without a fixed place of residence"](https://t.me/huquqiyaxborot/20212), abolishes the mandatory court order for placing homeless people in social institutions — the rule takes effect on September 1, 2026. Existing rehabilitation centers run by the Ministry of Internal Affairs (MVD) will be officially renamed temporary accommodation and social reintegration centers.

From September 1, people will be placed voluntarily and with their consent: they will receive temporary housing, food and hygiene items, and an individual social service plan will be drawn up for each person, [the statement says](https://t.me/huquqiyaxborot/20212). From October 1, 2026, homeless people staying at the centers will receive official unemployed status and the right to vouchers for professional retraining through district and city employment and poverty reduction bodies. Temporary registration at the center's address and the related state duty will be financed from the "Sahovat va Ko'mak" ("Mercy and Assistance") fund.

## Decree UP-146: hotline and travel compensation for trafficking victims

Decree UP-146, ["On additional measures to fundamentally improve the system for identifying persons affected by human trafficking and their integration into society"](https://t.me/huquqiyaxborot/20213), introduces the "1146" consultation line from September 1, 2026, offering psychological, social and legal support to victims. The document also provides for migration consultation bureaus at the airports of Tashkent and Samarkand — their launch date is not specified in the announcement, [according to the publication](https://t.me/huquqiyaxborot/20213).

The document sets deadlines for identifying victims: a preliminary identification within one month, a final one within one year. Travel to the Republican Center for the Rehabilitation of Women and Children will be reimbursed at one base calculated value, and travel to regional centers at half that amount; the costs will likewise be covered by the "Sahovat va Ko'mak" fund.

## What's next

Both documents implement the social protection reform proposals that Mirziyoyev [approved on August 3](https://leap.uz/en/2026/08/04/social-protection-reform-2026) — the same day also saw plans presented for moving subsidized transit fares into an app and for the education of children with disabilities. No timeline has yet been given for adopting final acts on these two areas.
