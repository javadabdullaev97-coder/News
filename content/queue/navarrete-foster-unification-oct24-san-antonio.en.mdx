Mexican Emanuel Navarrete and American O'Shaquie Foster will meet in a junior lightweight (up to 58.97 kg) unification fight on October 24 at the Alamodome in San Antonio, Texas, according to [BoxingScene](https://www.boxingscene.com/articles/emanuel-navarrete-oshaquie-foster-unification-fight-set-for-october-24-in-san-antonio).

The 31-year-old Navarrete (40 wins, 2 losses, 1 draw, 33 knockouts) brings the World Boxing Organization (WBO) and International Boxing Federation (IBF) belts into the fight. He unified both titles in February, stopping Eduardo Nunez in the 11th round. The 32-year-old Foster (25 wins, 3 losses, 12 knockouts) defends the World Boxing Council (WBC) title — in May he won a decision over Raymond Ford. The winner will leave San Antonio with three of the division's four major belts, according to [BoxingScene](https://www.boxingscene.com/articles/emanuel-navarrete-oshaquie-foster-unification-fight-set-for-october-24-in-san-antonio). DAZN will broadcast the fight; both boxers are promoted by Top Rank.

Negotiations took several months: an agreement on the fight was reached in mid-August, according to [BoxingScene](https://www.boxingscene.com/articles/emanuel-navarrete-oshaquie-foster-unification-fight-set-for-october-24-in-san-antonio).

## Question Over the WBO Belt

In March, [the WBO](https://wboboxing.com/wbo-official-documents/wbo-orders-negotiations-for-mandatory-jr-lightweight-championship-navarrete-vs-suarez/) ordered Navarrete to negotiate a mandatory title defense against challenger Charlie Suarez. If the Mexican fights Foster in San Antonio instead of Suarez, the organization could require him to vacate the WBO belt. The status of that requirement was not clarified at the time the fight was announced.
