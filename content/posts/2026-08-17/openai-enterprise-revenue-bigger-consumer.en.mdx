OpenAI Chief Financial Officer Sarah Friar told investors on August 14 that revenue from corporate clients surpassed revenue from consumer ChatGPT subscriptions for the first time, [CNBC reported](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/14/openai-cfo-friar-tells-investors-that-enterprise-bigger-than-consumer.html).

At the meeting, Friar cited the following figures: the company's annualized revenue run rate (ARR) reached $40 billion. In July, growth came in at 20% month-over-month. According to CNBC, the number of enterprise customers grew 32% over the same month. "We started the year at a 60-40 split, but the enterprise segment grew much faster than expected, and the lines crossed," CNBC quoted Friar as saying. Earlier in the year, the consumer segment accounted for 60% of revenue and the enterprise segment for 40%. Separately, according to the same data, advertising on ChatGPT came up: testing began in February. The annualized revenue run rate from advertising is approaching $1 billion.

## CNBC's Figures Diverge From Bloomberg's

[Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-13/openai-s-revenue-run-rate-tops-40-billion-ahead-of-ipo) had also written about OpenAI's revenue a day earlier, on August 13, and confirmed the $40 billion ARR figure. But the outlet cited a different share for the enterprise segment — "more than 40% of total revenue" — and did not report that enterprise revenue had overtaken consumer revenue.

The statement about the "lines crossing" is known only from CNBC's account. OpenAI has not published a separate official statement on its revenue breakdown, and there is no independent confirmation of the exact figures as of publication.

## Personnel Changes Ahead of IPO

Friar's statement came a day after OpenAI announced the departure of Chief Revenue Officer Denise Dresser. She held the position for [about eight months](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/14/openai-cfo-friar-tells-investors-that-enterprise-bigger-than-consumer.html), CNBC reports. The company [said](https://openai.com/index/dali-rajic-chief-revenue-officer/) Dresser is leaving "to pursue other opportunities," and that her position was taken by Dali Rajic, formerly president and chief operating officer of Wiz.

Two days earlier, Chief Operating Officer Brad Lightcap announced his departure after [eight years](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/14/openai-cfo-friar-tells-investors-that-enterprise-bigger-than-consumer.html) at OpenAI, CNBC reports.

The personnel changes come as the company prepares for a possible initial public offering. In June, OpenAI [confirmed](https://openai.com/index/openai-submits-confidential-s-1/) that it had submitted a confidential registration application on Form S-1 to the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission.
