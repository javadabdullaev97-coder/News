Fly Khiva twice postponed its charter flight from Termez to Medina on August 12–14, leaving 189 passengers waiting more than a day to depart from Termez, and also canceled a backup flight to Tashkent.

## How the flight was postponed

The Fly Khiva flight from Termez to Medina was originally scheduled to depart on the night of August 12–13. The airline then postponed it to the night of August 13–14, according to [a statement](https://t.me/uzbairportsuz/9355) from Uzbekistan Airports, the state enterprise that operates Termez airport.

Because of the schedule change, the 189 passengers awaiting departure to Medina were booked onto a backup charter flight to Tashkent, scheduled to depart at 9:00 a.m. on August 14. That flight was also canceled — due to technical reasons, according to Uzbekistan Airports.

## What the airport says

Termez airport said it is ensuring all necessary services are operating and is assisting passengers within its authority. Uzbekistan Airports recommends checking directly with Fly Khiva representatives or the airline's official website for the new departure time and passenger transport arrangements.

Fly Khiva itself had not issued a separate statement about the delay as of publication — neither on its website, flykhiva.uz, nor on its official Telegram channel.

## What's next

The exact time of the new departure from Termez to Medina has not yet been announced. Uzbekistan Airports recommends checking directly with Fly Khiva for up-to-date information on flight status and passenger accommodations.
