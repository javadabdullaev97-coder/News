Uzbekistan has introduced a "business without a license" regime: a law adopted August 13 allows entrepreneurs to conduct certain types of activity without a license for up to three months after notifying the authorized body electronically.

## How the New Regime Works

The [law ZRU-1164 of August 13](https://t.me/huquqiyaxborot/20256) introduces a special "business without a license" regime. An entrepreneur who needs a license or permit to operate can start their activity without waiting to obtain it — it is enough to notify the authorized body free of charge through a special electronic system or the unified portal of interactive government services.

The regime lasts three months. Throughout this period the entrepreneur bears no liability for operating without a license or permit, the Telegram channel Huquqiy axborot, run by the Ministry of Justice of Uzbekistan, reported.

## What Must Be Done Within Three Months

During the three-month period, the entrepreneur must either bring their activity into compliance with licensing or permit requirements and obtain the necessary document through the established procedure, or discontinue the activity, [according to the Ministry of Justice's explanation](https://t.me/huquqiyaxborot/20256).

The law does not specify a list of particular activities that will fall under the new regime — the published explanation describes only the mechanism itself.

## Part of a Broader Package

The law on the license-free regime is one of two documents previously approved by the Senate of the Oliy Majlis as part of a business support package. The second was the law "[Mechanisms to Support Entrepreneurship Are Being Improved](https://senat.uz/ru/plenary-sessions/post-5874)," also approved by the upper chamber of parliament.

The law on the "business without a license" regime itself was titled in the Senate "[The Law Creates Conveniences for the Population and Business Entities](https://senat.uz/ru/plenary-sessions/post-5876)."
