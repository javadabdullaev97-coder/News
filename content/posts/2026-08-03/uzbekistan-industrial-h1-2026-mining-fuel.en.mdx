The National Statistics Committee of the Republic of Uzbekistan reported in a press release on July 28 that the country's mining industry produced 42.4 trillion soums worth of output in January–June 2026.

The committee repeated these figures in two Telegram posts on August 3 — the second of which also noted growth in gasoline production, to 614.2 thousand tons.

## Manufacturing Accounts for 86% of Output

Both [National Statistics Committee posts on Telegram](https://t.me/uzstataxborot/69164) are based on the agency's [press release](https://stat.uz/img/press-relizlar/press-reliz-sanoat-07-2026-rus_p46063.pdf) of July 28: in January–June 2026, the country's industrial enterprises produced 637.2 trillion soums worth of output (≈$53.15 billion at the [Central Bank of Uzbekistan exchange rate](https://cbu.uz/ru/arkhiv-kursov-valyut/json/USD/2026-07-28/) on the release date — 11,988.92 soums per dollar). That is 8.0% more than in the same period of 2025.

Output structure across the four sections of economic activity:

- manufacturing — 549.7 trillion soums (≈$45.85 billion), 86.3% of total output, up 8.8%;
- [mining](https://t.me/uzstataxborot/69164) and quarrying — 42.4 trillion soums (≈$3.54 billion), 6.7%, up 2.1% — the weakest growth among the four sections;
- electricity, gas, steam and air conditioning supply — 41.7 trillion soums (≈$3.48 billion), 6.5%, up 7.9%;
- water supply, sewerage, waste collection and disposal — 3.3 trillion soums (≈$275 million), 0.5%, up 15.9% — the fastest rate, but on the smallest base.

By region, Navoi region delivered the largest volume of industrial output — 155.0 trillion soums (physical volume index of 105.4%), followed by the city of Tashkent — 109.6 trillion soums (108.0%) and Tashkent region — 100.0 trillion soums (108.4%).

## Mining Lags Behind Due to Gas, Oil, and Coal

The mining section's weak growth is explained by declining output of raw commodities that the National Statistics Committee classifies under this section. According to [the agency's data](https://t.me/uzstataxborot/69162) for January–June 2026:

- natural gas — 18.3 billion cubic meters, down from 21.9 billion a year earlier (−16.4%);
- coal — 2.5 million tons, down from 3.0 million (−16.7%);
- gas condensate — 462.1 thousand tons, down from 575.2 thousand (−19.7%);
- oil — 313.8 thousand tons, down from 323.9 thousand (−3.1%).

LEAP News covered the causes and dynamics of the decline in gas output in recent years in a [separate article](/article/uzbekistan-gas-output-h1-2026-drop), published after the same National Statistics Committee press release. In its new Telegram posts on August 3, the committee repeated the same physical-volume figures without additional explanation.

## Gasoline and Diesel Output Rose Year on Year

Unlike raw material extraction, fuel refining grew over the first half of the year. According to [the National Statistics Committee's data](https://t.me/uzstataxborot/69162):

- gasoline — 614.2 thousand tons, up from 579.7 thousand in January–June 2025 (+6.0%), but still below the 675.9 thousand tons recorded in January–June 2024;
- diesel fuel — 568.0 thousand tons, up from 556.4 thousand (+2.1%) and 505.1 thousand tons two years earlier.

Portland cement was another manufacturing product to post growth: 10.1 million tons, up from 9.9 million a year earlier (+2.0%).

## Car Engine Output Rose Nearly 9%

The National Statistics Committee separately reported car engine output — 126,671 units, up from 116,543 a year earlier (+8.7%). The press release notes that JSC "UZAUTO MOTORS POWERTRAIN" provided revised data on engines separately from cylinder blocks for January–June 2026 and the same period of 2025.
