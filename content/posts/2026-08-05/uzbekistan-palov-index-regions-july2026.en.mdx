The National Statistics Committee of Uzbekistan on August 5 [published](https://t.me/uzstataxborot/69172) the cost of ingredients for 1 kg of palov by region for July — from 107,400 soums in Karakalpakstan to 128,700 soums in Tashkent.

## Price Spread by Region

Palov is cheapest in the Republic of Karakalpakstan — [107,396 soums](https://t.me/uzstataxborot/69172) (≈$9.03 at the Central Bank's August 6 exchange rate) per kilogram of ingredients. It is most expensive in Tashkent: [128,730 soums](https://t.me/uzstataxborot/69172) (≈$10.83).

Data for other regions, soums per kg of ingredients:

- Syrdarya — 113,707
- Andijan — 116,554
- Khorezm — 117,907
- Namangan — 118,315
- Jizzakh — 118,191
- Bukhara — 118,694
- Navoiy — 119,331
- Qashqadaryo — 120,065
- Surkhandarya — 120,303
- Samarkand — 122,320
- Tashkent region — 122,346
- Fergana — 125,283

A simple average of the National Statistics Committee's data across all 14 regions comes out to about 119,200 soums (≈$10.03) per kilogram — a figure the agency does not publish separately.

The National Statistics Committee [clarifies](https://t.me/uzstataxborot/69172) that the calculation covers only the cost of raw ingredients, not the price of the finished dish at cafes and restaurants. The standard set of ingredients for 1 kg of palov used by the agency for the calculation: 700 grams of beef, 1 kilogram of rice, 1 kilogram of carrots, 300 grams of onion, 0.3 liters of sunflower oil, 100 grams of chickpeas, 10 grams of cumin and 20 grams of salt.

## Monthly Increase Amid Overall Food Deflation

Compared with June, palov prices rose in every region. In Karakalpakstan, ingredient costs increased [by 2,181 soums](https://t.me/uzstataxborot/69059) — from 105,215 to 107,396 soums (+2.1%). In Tashkent — [by 1,923 soums](https://t.me/uzstataxborot/69059), from 126,807 to 128,730 soums (+1.5%).

The rise in palov prices comes against a backdrop of overall food deflation. According to the National Statistics Committee, July 2026 [recorded deflation](https://t.me/uzstataxborot/69173) of 0.1% for the consumer basket as a whole, while food products became 0.91% cheaper. The annual consumer price index stood at 106.4% — 2.5 percentage points lower than in July 2025. Palov is getting more expensive despite the overall decline in food prices. The National Statistics Committee does not separately explain the reasons for this discrepancy — the agency does not break down the contribution of meat and rice to the cost of the set.
