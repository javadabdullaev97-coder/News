Accelerator Y Combinator unveiled Speko on July 30 — a router for voice AI models that joined the Summer 2026 batch (S26) and was founded by Karakalpakstan-born Bekhnazar Abdikamalov.

## What Speko Does

The product combines three components of a voice agent — speech recognition (speech-to-text), a language model and speech synthesis (text-to-speech) — under a single API key, [Y Combinator says](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/speko). The system continuously tests [more than 50 providers and over 140 models](https://speko.ai/) by language and use case, and publishes the results — recognition accuracy, latency and cost per minute — at [benchmarks.speko.ai](https://benchmarks.speko.ai). For each language, the service automatically switches the client to the model with the best current performance.

The company is registered in San Francisco with a team of four people, [according to Speko's listing](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/speko) in the YC directory. Every company admitted to the accelerator receives $500,000 in investment in exchange for equity under Y Combinator's [standard deal terms](https://www.ycombinator.com/deal).

## Who Is Bekhnazar Abdikamalov

Before Speko, Abdikamalov worked as an engineer at Amazon and was co-founder and chief technology officer of the startup Hupo, which was backed by DST Global, Meta, Goodwater Capital and Collaborative Fund, [YC says](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/speko). He earned his bachelor's and master's degrees in computer science at UNIST, a university in South Korea.

In August 2024, President Shavkat Mirziyoyev [met](http://www.it-park.uz/ru/itpark/news/president-shavkat-mirziyoyev-visited-it-park-branch-in-karakalpakstan) with Abdikamalov during a visit to the IT Park branch in Nukus — the second-largest after the Tashkent branch — and endorsed his project at the time, Hupo, which had by then raised $4 million in investment. The [Jokargy Kenes of the Republic of Karakalpakstan](https://t.me/joqargikenes/32258) recalled that visit on July 31, reporting that Y Combinator had unveiled Abdikamalov's new project, Speko.

## IT Park in Karakalpakstan

By August 2024, the number of residents at the IT Park branch in Nukus had tripled from the end of 2023 to 108 companies with 310 employees. Authorities also announced plans to allocate $50 million to fund AI startups in the republic, [IT Park Uzbekistan reported](http://www.it-park.uz/ru/itpark/news/president-shavkat-mirziyoyev-visited-it-park-branch-in-karakalpakstan). There is no official data on the residency status of Speko or Hupo at the IT Park branch as of publication.
