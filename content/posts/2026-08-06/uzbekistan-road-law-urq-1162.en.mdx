On August 5, 2026, President Shavkat Mirziyoyev signed the Law on Automobile Roads (No. ZRU-1162) — the document directly regulates toll roads within its own text for the first time, rather than deferring the topic to a separate law. This was [reported](https://t.me/huquqiyaxborot/20223) by the Ministry of Justice's Telegram channel "Huquqiy axborot".

## Roads now divided into three categories instead of four

The new law replaces the [2007 version](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/1254492) (ZRU-117), which divided roads into four categories — public roads, city streets, farm roads and inter-farm rural roads (articles 3, 9, 10 and 11). Law No. ZRU-1162 reduces them to three:

- **public roads** — state property, under the jurisdiction of the Automobile Roads Committee under the Ministry of Transport (also known as "Uzavtoyul"), open to all road users;
- **intra-city and rural streets** — roads within the boundaries of populated areas;
- **farm roads** — used by individual enterprises and organizations for their own needs.

Public roads are further divided into technical categories, which determine design standards and the width of the land allotted for the road. The law leaves the specific parameters of each category to subordinate legislation still to be adopted by the Cabinet of Ministers.

## Toll roads allowed only if a free alternative exists

The main innovation is that the law directly regulates toll roads within the text of the document itself for the first time. Until now, [article 2 of the 2007 law](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/1254492) left the topic to a "separate law" that was never adopted.

Now a toll road can only be established by a decision of the president or the Cabinet of Ministers, and the law requires maintaining a free alternative route for it.

The law allows toll sections to be financed through public-private partnerships — it leaves the specific mechanism for recovering investments to separate government decisions. Payment for travel is envisaged both directly at toll collection points and through pre-purchased passes.

A similar version of the law passed through the Senate in April. As Kutbiddin Burkhonov, chairman of the Senate committee on defense and security, [said](https://uza.uz/kz/posts/odobren-zakon-o-sozdanii-platnyx-dorog_841472) at a plenary session on April 7, 2026, "the law clearly defines the basic principles mandatory for compliance in the field of automobile roads," including road design based on scientific calculations and transparency of decisions made.

## Who is responsible for the roads

The Automobile Roads Committee under the Ministry of Transport remains the specially authorized body in the sector. The law delineates levels of responsibility for road maintenance.

A separate provision of the law concerns oversized and heavy vehicles: such vehicles are permitted to travel on roads only with special permits from authorized bodies.

## What's next

The law does not name specific toll routes or tariffs — that is left to future decisions by the president and the Cabinet of Ministers for each specific section. Subordinate legislation with technical standards for road categories has also not yet been published. LEAP News will continue to follow which road sections are the first to receive toll status.
