Uzbekistan's Ministry of Economy and Finance said on August 3 that citizens opened 338 targeted mortgage deposit accounts worth 23.9 billion soums during the first month of the pilot project.

## How the Targeted Mortgage Deposit Works

The mechanism has been operating on an experimental basis from July 1 to December 31, 2026 — [as established](https://lex.uz/docs/8298141) by a joint resolution of the Ministry of Economy and Finance and the Central Bank (Ministry of Justice registration No. 3874 dated June 29, 2026), adopted pursuant to a presidential decree of February 24, 2026 on the progress of housing construction.

A citizen who has received a notification of a mortgage loan subsidy for purchasing an apartment on the primary housing market can open a deposit. They apply to a bank, which opens a special savings account within three business days. First, the depositor accumulates the portion of the down payment they must contribute out of pocket. Once that amount is collected, the bank submits an application via the subsidyplatform.mf.uz platform within two business days, and the Ministry of Economy and Finance transfers the budget subsidy — the remaining part of the down payment — to the account within five business days.

The bank pays interest at the market rate on the entire account balance — both the depositor's own funds and the subsidy. The maximum accumulation period is 12 months, after which the bank transfers the funds along with the mortgage loan to the developer. If a citizen decides not to buy housing, is denied a loan, or force majeure circumstances arise, the depositor's own funds plus interest are returned to them, while the budget subsidy plus interest goes back to the state budget.

## 338 Deposits in a Month

As of August 1, 338 targeted mortgage deposits had been opened under the pilot for a total of 23.9 billion soums (≈$2 million at the Central Bank's exchange rate for August 3). Of that amount, 13.8 billion soums (≈$1.15 million) were depositors' own funds, and 10.1 billion soums (≈$0.84 million) were budget subsidies, [according to](https://t.me/minecofinuz/16168) the Ministry of Economy and Finance's publication.

The average deposit size was about 70.7 million soums (≈$5,900).

## Agrobank and Border Regions Lead

Agrobank accounts for 41% of all deposits opened — more than any other bank participating in the pilot. Annual deposit rates range from 14% to 18%, with Hamkorbank offering the highest rate, according to the Ministry of Economy and Finance's publication.

By region, Fergana and Khorezm lead, each accounting for 13% of deposits opened. The rest of the country's regions show a smaller share of participation in the pilot.

## What's Next

The pilot project will run until December 31, 2026. The Ministry of Economy and Finance has not yet announced plans to extend or expand the mechanism to other regions or banks after the experiment concludes.
