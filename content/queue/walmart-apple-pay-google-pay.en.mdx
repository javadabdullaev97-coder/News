Walmart and the Sam's Club chain will begin accepting contactless smartphone payments — Tap to Pay technology — at some U.S. stores starting August 24, 2026, [the company said](https://corporate.walmart.com/news/2026/08/21/more-ways-to-pay-tap-to-pay-is-coming-to-walmart-and-sams-club). Supported services will include Apple Pay and Google Pay, according to [MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/21/walmart-to-begin-accepting-apple-pay-in-us/) and [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/08/21/walmart-finally-launching-apple-pay-support-next-week/). Tap to pay is a technology in which a shopper taps a smartphone or watch to a terminal instead of using a card or cash.

According to [Walmart](https://corporate.walmart.com/news/2026/08/21/more-ways-to-pay-tap-to-pay-is-coming-to-walmart-and-sams-club), the new payment method will supplement existing options, including the company's own Walmart Pay service — the chain will keep accepting it alongside the new options. "We want customers and members to have choice in how they pay..." Walmart said in a statement cited by [MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/21/walmart-to-begin-accepting-apple-pay-in-us/).

Walmart had not accepted Apple Pay or Google Pay for more than a decade, [report](https://9to5mac.com/2026/08/21/walmart-finally-launching-apple-pay-support-next-week/) [9to5Mac and MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/21/walmart-to-begin-accepting-apple-pay-in-us/). The retailer had favored its own Walmart Pay service. According to [MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/21/walmart-to-begin-accepting-apple-pay-in-us/), the company had remained one of the few major U.S. chains not supporting either service.

Google confirmed the launch via its [official Google Pay blog](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/platforms/google-pay/tap-to-pay-google-pay-walmart/). "Starting next week, shoppers will be able to pay with a single tap using Google Pay at select Walmart and Sam's Club stores in the U.S.," the post said. No direct statement from Apple about the partnership was found among the sources reviewed.

## What's Next

Tap to Pay will reach the entire U.S. chain by the end of 2026 — the service will work at all Walmart and Sam's Club stores, the company [said](https://corporate.walmart.com/news/2026/08/21/more-ways-to-pay-tap-to-pay-is-coming-to-walmart-and-sams-club). Apple Pay will be among the supported services, according to [MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/21/walmart-to-begin-accepting-apple-pay-in-us/) and [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/08/21/walmart-finally-launching-apple-pay-support-next-week/). Google Pay support is confirmed by [Google's blog](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/platforms/google-pay/tap-to-pay-google-pay-walmart/). Contactless smartphone payment will be added at the chain's gas stations by mid-2027 — a timeline confirmed by both [Walmart](https://corporate.walmart.com/news/2026/08/21/more-ways-to-pay-tap-to-pay-is-coming-to-walmart-and-sams-club) and [Google](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/platforms/google-pay/tap-to-pay-google-pay-walmart/).
