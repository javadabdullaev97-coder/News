The Senate of the Oliy Majlis of Uzbekistan [approved](https://uza.uz/ru/posts/informaciya-o-vosemnadcatom-plenarnom-zasedanii-senata-oliy-mazhlisa-respubliki-uzbekistan_892436) the constitutional law "On the Administration of the President of the Republic of Uzbekistan" on August 7 at its eighteenth plenary session, chaired by Senate Chairwoman Tanzila Narbayeva.

The document passed through both chambers of parliament: the Legislative Chamber adopted it in its first reading on August 4 and in its third reading on August 6, sending it to the Senate. For details on the law's content and the course of its discussion, see the [article LEAP News published](https://leap.uz/en/2026/08/07/administration-law-sent-to-senate) after the third reading.

## What the Senate Said

Abdulhakim Eshmurodov, chairman of the Senate Committee on Judicial and Legal Affairs and Anti-Corruption, [said](https://uza.uz/uz/posts/prezident-administraciyasining-huquqiy-maqomiga-oid-qonun-maqullandi_892437) the law establishes the legal foundations for an effective system of state power and administration. The document defines the status of the Presidential Administration, the principles of its work, its structure and the procedure for interacting with government bodies.

At the same session, senators also [approved](https://uza.uz/ru/posts/informaciya-o-vosemnadcatom-plenarnom-zasedanii-senata-oliy-mazhlisa-respubliki-uzbekistan_892436) five other bills — on market surveillance, on general product safety, on state pension provision for military personnel, on the procedure for awarding pensions, and on amendments to the law on advertising.

## What's Next

To take effect, the constitutional law approved by the Senate must be signed by the president of Uzbekistan. No signing date has been announced yet — [LEAP News reported earlier](https://leap.uz/en/2026/08/07/administration-law-sent-to-senate) that this is the final formal step after passage through both chambers of parliament.
