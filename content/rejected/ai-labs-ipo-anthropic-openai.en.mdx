On August 20, [Bloomberg reported](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-20/anthropic-expects-to-match-spacex-s-record-ipo-size-or-top-it), citing people familiar with the matter, that Anthropic expects an IPO — an initial public offering — the size of SpaceX's record or larger. The information comes from several outlets; there is no official confirmation as of publication.

OpenAI, Anthropic's rival, is taking a more cautious timeline. According to [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/19/open-ai-ipo-timing-2027-friar.html), OpenAI Chief Financial Officer Sarah Friar told employees on August 19 that the company could go public in 2027 or sooner if growth continues.

Anthropic's annualized revenue, based on current monthly figures, rose to $65 billion in July. That is roughly seven times higher than a year earlier, [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-17/anthropic-revenue-run-rate-surpasses-65-billion-ahead-of-ipo) and [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/17/anthropic-says-annualized-revenue-climbed-to-65-billion-in-july.html) reported. LEAP News covered this growth in detail on [August 18](https://leap.uz/en/2026/08/18/anthropic-revenue-ipo-2026-08). Anthropic itself has not officially confirmed the figures as of publication.

## Anthropic's IPO: The Benchmark Is SpaceX's Record

This refers to the largest stock offering to date — SpaceX's IPO. Anthropic [filed](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-01/anthropic-files-confidentially-for-ipo-as-claude-demand-surges) its confidential application back in June, in a race with OpenAI.

Anthropic's investors previously told the Financial Times that the company could be valued at $2 trillion or more in an October offering, [TechCrunch reports](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/17/anthropics-annualized-revenue-surges-to-65b/). That is nearly double the $965 billion valuation the company received in May, after a [Series H round](https://www.anthropic.com/news/series-h) of $65 billion. Anthropic has not officially confirmed either the offering date or the target valuation.

## OpenAI: No IPO Before 2027

According to Sarah Friar, an IPO for OpenAI is not a finish line but "another funding round." The CFO told employees that in March 2026 the company [closed a round](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/03/31/openai-funding-round-ipo.html) of $122 billion at a valuation of about $852 billion. That cushion, she said, gives the company flexibility and there is no need to rush an IPO. OpenAI has not named a specific offering date — only the target of "2027 or sooner."

## What's Next

Anthropic, according to Bloomberg, plans to file publicly by the end of August and list its shares in October. OpenAI's only stated target is 2027, with the caveat "sooner, if business growth allows."
