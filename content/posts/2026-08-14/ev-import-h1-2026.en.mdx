Uzbekistan imported 37,874 electric vehicles worth $447.7 million in January–June 2026 — 2.6 times more vehicles than a year earlier, the [Statistics Agency under the President of Uzbekistan](https://t.me/uzstataxborot/69209) reported.

## Growth in Numbers

In the first half of 2025, the country imported 14,356 electric vehicles, according to data from the [Statistics Agency](https://stat.uz/ru/press-tsentr/novosti-goskomstata/62830-o-zbekistonga-qancha-yengil-avtomobil-import-qilingan-2026). Over the year to June 2026, the number rose by 23,518 units, or 2.6 times — import value reached $447.7 million.

China remains the main supplier — 37,560 electric vehicles, or 99.2% of the total volume, came from there in the first half of 2026, the [Statistics Agency reported](https://t.me/uzstataxborot/69209).

## Costlier Than All Car Imports a Year Earlier

Spending on electric vehicle imports alone in the first half of 2026 exceeded the value of **all** passenger car imports — combining internal combustion, hybrid and electric vehicles — in the first half of 2025.

At that time, according to the [Statistics Agency](https://stat.uz/ru/press-tsentr/novosti-goskomstata/62830-o-zbekistonga-qancha-yengil-avtomobil-import-qilingan-2026), the country imported 24,355 passenger cars of all types worth $422.1 million. Of those, electric vehicles accounted for 14,356 cars — about 59% of the total. The difference between the value of EV imports in the first half of 2026 and total car imports in the first half of 2025 is $25.6 million.

China's share of total car imports in the first half of 2025 was 21,399 vehicles, or 87.8%.
