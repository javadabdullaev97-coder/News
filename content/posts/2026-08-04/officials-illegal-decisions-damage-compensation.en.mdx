Uzbek President Shavkat Mirziyoyev signed a [law](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/8354256) on July 27, 2026, establishing a unified procedure for compensating citizens and legal entities for damage caused by unlawful decisions, actions or inaction of state bodies.

## What the law provides

Law No. ZRU-1161 "On Compensation for Damage Caused by an Unlawful Decision, Action or Inaction of a State Body or Its Official" establishes a unified compensation mechanism. It replaces the previously scattered set of norms, [the text of the document states](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/8354256).

Both individuals and legal entities can claim compensation. The law divides damage into two types: material — costs of restoring violated rights, loss of property, lost profit — and moral damage, related to mental and physical suffering.

The basis for payment is a court ruling that has entered into legal force. The Ministry of Justice of the Republic of Uzbekistan (Ministry of Justice) [announced](https://t.me/huquqiyaxborot/20209) the fact of the law's adoption on the "Huquqiy axborot" Telegram channel on August 3. The publication was limited to a headline and an infographic without details — the full text of the law is available on the lex.uz portal.

## Territorial funds and recourse against those at fault

Payments will be made not by the state body that committed the violation, but by specially created **territorial compensation funds** — under the Council of Ministers of Karakalpakstan, the hokimiyats of the regions and the city of Tashkent. Money will flow into the funds from several sources: at least 15% of the reserve funds of local budgets, additional budget sources, proceeds from the sale of land plots, revenue from extra-budgetary funds, charitable contributions and other sources not prohibited by law, [according to the law](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/8354256).

If a court identifies a specific official at fault, the fund first pays compensation to the victim from its own funds. The fund then recovers this amount from the official at fault through recourse.

Among the principles stated in the law are legality, priority of human rights and freedoms, prevention of conflicts of interest, and transparency and openness of the procedure.

## When it takes effect

The law was adopted by the Legislative Chamber and approved by the [Senate of the Oliy Majlis](https://senat.uz/ru/post/post-4837), after which the president signed it on July 27, 2026. The document takes effect on January 28, 2027 — six months after signing.
