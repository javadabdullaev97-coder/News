Tesla has discontinued production of Solar Roof, its roofing shingle with built-in solar cells, and since August 20, 2026, has been supplying independent contractors with regular solar panels only, according to Electrek.

Two sources close to the program, cited by Electrek, [reported](https://electrek.co/2026/08/20/tesla-discontinues-solar-roof-panels-only/) the decision. Tesla has not issued an official statement. According to one of the sources, the company internally considered the Solar Roof shingles economically unviable. An independent check by LEAP News found that the [tesla.com/solarroof](https://www.tesla.com/solarroof) page, which previously had a separate section on the shingles, now redirects to an offer to lease regular solar panels.

## 1,000 Roofs a Week Promised, 21-32 Delivered

Tesla founder Elon Musk [unveiled](https://electrek.co/2026/08/20/tesla-discontinues-solar-roof-panels-only/) Solar Roof in 2016 — the shingles were meant to protect the roof and generate electricity at the same time. The product was used to justify Tesla's [acquisition](https://electrek.co/2026/08/20/tesla-discontinues-solar-roof-panels-only/) of SolarCity for $2.6 billion that same year.

Musk promised to install 1,000 roofs a week, quoting a price of about $22 per square foot. According to Electrek, the peak installation rate never exceeded 21-32 systems a week, and only about 3,000 Solar Roof installations appeared across the US over seven years. One customer, according to the outlet, received a final bill of $146,000 instead of the $72,000 originally quoted. Tesla settled the resulting dispute for $6 million in 2023.

Tesla's contractors will now continue installing regular solar panels, which the company supplies, among other things, through its leasing program.
