The unified public services portal my.gov.uz [reminded](https://t.me/MyGovUz/3973) applicants on August 7 that the second stage of the 2026 admissions campaign — choosing a university, field of study and study format — ends on August 8 inclusive.

## What Applicants Need to Do Before August 8

To complete their selection, applicants need to go to the my.gov.uz portal, open the service «Abituriyentni onlayn ro'yxatdan o'tkazish» ("Online Registration of Applicants") and click «Xizmatdan foydalanish» ("Use the Service"). Then they need to go to the «OTM tanlash» section (university selection), [the portal's post says](https://t.me/MyGovUz/3973). The same section is available directly on the my.uzbmb.uz platform.

The second stage of the 2026 admissions campaign runs from July 25 through August 8 — 15 days are allotted for the whole process, [according to a post by the admissions committee of Tashkent Medical Academy](https://admissions.tma.uz/2026/07/%E2%9D%97%EF%B8%8F25-iyuldan-oliy-talim-muassasalari-va-talim-yonalishlarini-tanlash-boshlandi/). The official Telegram channel of the Agency for Assessing Knowledge and Qualifications [reminded](https://t.me/BaholashUz/11301) on August 6 that two days remained until the end of the second selection stage.

## How the Selection Works

Applicants can choose up to five higher education institutions and fields of study, specifying the study format, [the TMA admissions committee says](https://admissions.tma.uz/2026/07/%E2%9D%97%EF%B8%8F25-iyuldan-oliy-talim-muassasalari-va-talim-yonalishlarini-tanlash-boshlandi/). First, the priority order of the choices is indicated, along with participation in targeted admission if applicable, followed by the universities and fields of study themselves, matching the set of subjects the applicant has taken and the language of instruction. The selection is confirmed with an SMS code, after which the applicant's registration sheet is generated. Data can be changed and resaved until the deadline expires.

## What's Next

After the selection process ends, final results — enrollment recommendations — will be announced within a week, [according to a post by the TMA admissions committee](https://admissions.tma.uz/2026/07/%E2%9D%97%EF%B8%8F25-iyuldan-oliy-talim-muassasalari-va-talim-yonalishlarini-tanlash-boshlandi/).
