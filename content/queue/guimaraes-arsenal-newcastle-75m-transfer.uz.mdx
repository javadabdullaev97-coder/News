"Arsenal" 2026-yil 8-avgustda "Nyukasl" kapitani, braziliyalik yarim himoyachi Bruno Gimaraynshni transfer qilganini e'lon qildi — [BBC Sport](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/articles/c1719r079rjo) va [Sky Sports](https://www.skysports.com/football/news/12040/13570096/bruno-guimaraes-arsenal-sign-midfielder-in-75m-transfer-as-newcastle-surpass-240m-in-player-sales-this-summer) ma'lumotlariga ko'ra, transfer summasi 75 million funt-sterlingni tashkil etdi — bu "Nyukasl"ning bu yozdagi eng yirik sotuvlaridan biri.

## O'tish shartlari

28 yoshli braziliyalik Angliya chempioni bilan uzoq muddatli shartnoma imzoladi va 39-raqamli formani oldi — buni "Arsenal"ning rasmiy sayti [xabar qildi](https://www.arsenal.com/news/bruno-guimaraes-joins-arsenal-assZJ0i5HUEi). [Sky Sports](https://www.skysports.com/football/news/12040/13570096/bruno-guimaraes-arsenal-sign-midfielder-in-75m-transfer-as-newcastle-surpass-240m-in-player-sales-this-summer) va [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/sports/2026/8/8/arsenal-complete-signing-of-newcastle-midfielder-bruno-guimaraes) ma'lumotlariga ko'ra, shartnoma to'rt yilga tuzilgan bo'lib, yana bir mavsumga uzaytirish opsiyasiga ega — demak, kelishuv 2030-yilgacha amal qilishi mumkin. Ikkala klub ham o'z bayonotlarida bitim summasini aniq ko'rsatmadi: "Nyukasl" faqat [shunday izoh berdi](https://www.newcastleunited.com/en/news/bruno-guimaraes-leaves), o'yinchi "oshkor etilmagan summaga" ketgan, bu summa 28 yosh va undan katta o'yinchi uchun to'langan eng yirik transfer to'lovlari o'ntaligiga kiradi hamda uning yoshi va o'ynash o'rniga ko'ra rekord hisoblanadi. 75 million funt-sterlinglik summani Sky Sports va Al Jazeera tasdiqlaydi; oxirgisining ma'lumotiga ko'ra, bu taxminan 101 million dollarga teng.

"Arsenal"ning sport direktori Andrea Berta klubning rasmiy saytida shunday dedi: "Bruno Gimaraynshni klubimizga qabul qilganimizdan xursandmiz. Bruno — ajoyib xarakterga va yuqori o'yin sifatiga ega, jamoamizga kuchli yetakchilik olib keladigan futbolchi".

Gimaraynsh 2022-yil yanvar oyida frantsuz "Lion" klubidan "Nyukasl"ga o'tgan va bu klub safida 195 ta o'yin o'tkazib, 31 ta gol urgan edi. 2025-yilda u Angliya Liga kubogini yutgan jamoaning kapitani bo'lgan, deb [xabar beradi](https://www.aljazeera.com/sports/2026/8/8/arsenal-complete-signing-of-newcastle-midfielder-bruno-guimaraes) Al Jazeera.

## "Sotishni xohlamagan edik"

"Nyukasl"ning sport direktori Ross Wilson klub kapitani bilan ajralishni rejalashtirmaganini [ma'lum qildi](https://www.aljazeera.com/sports/2026/8/8/newcastle-say-they-didnt-want-to-sell-guimaraes-as-arsenal-move-looms): "Biz Brunoni sotishni xohlamagan edik. Uni sotish rejalarimizda yo'q edi". Wilsonning so'zlariga ko'ra, futbolchining o'zi klub rahbariyatiga ketishni xohlashini bildirgan, klub esa shu holatga munosabat bildirgan.

Gimaraynshning o'zi bu qarorni hayotidagi eng qiyin qarorlardan biri deb [atadi](https://www.newcastleunited.com/en/news/bruno-guimaraes-leaves): "Men bu joyga oshiq bo'lib qoldim. Men chindan ham shunday deb o'ylayman". U PIF investitsiya fondiga, direktorlar kengashi raisi Yasir Al-Rumayyanga va klubning hammuassisi Jamie Reubenga minnatdorchilik bildirdi.

## "Nyukasl" bu yoz davomida 240 million funt-sterlingdan ortiq o'yinchi sotdi

Gimaraynshning sotilishi "Nyukasl"ning bu yozdagi uchinchi yirik bitimi bo'ldi. [Sky Sports](https://www.skysports.com/football/news/12040/13570096/bruno-guimaraes-arsenal-sign-midfielder-in-75m-transfer-as-newcastle-surpass-240m-in-player-sales-this-summer) ma'lumotlariga ko'ra, klub o'yinchilarni sotishdan 240 million funt-sterlingdan ortiq mablag' tushirgan: Gimaraynshdan (75 million funt-sterling, "Arsenal") tashqari, klub yarim himoyachi Sandro Tonalini "Tottenxem"ga 100 million funt-sterlingga va hujumchi Entoni Gordonni "Barselona"ga 69,3 million funt-sterlingga sotgan.
