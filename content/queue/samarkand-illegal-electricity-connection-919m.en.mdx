The Department for Combating Economic Crimes under the Prosecutor General's Office of the Republic of Uzbekistan reported on August 4 an illegal connection of a workshop to the power grid in Samarkand region — the damage was estimated at 919.8 million soums.

## What the investigation found

According to the [Department's report](https://t.me/Department_uz/12942), a pre-investigation inquiry was conducted by the agency's division for Samarkand district. According to the investigation, officials of "M.B." LLC connected a sand-processing workshop directly to the public power grid for commercial purposes, without proper authorization. According to the agency, this resulted in the illegal use of electricity worth 919.8 million soums.

A criminal case was opened over the incident under Article 169 of the Criminal Code (theft). Investigative actions are ongoing.

According to the [Central Bank of Uzbekistan exchange rate](https://cbu.uz/ru/arkhiv-kursov-valyut/json/USD/2026-08-04/) for August 4 — 11,989.46 soums per dollar — 919.8 million soums amount to about $76,700.

## How damage from unauthorized connections is calculated

The procedure for connecting consumers to power grids and calculating the cost of unaccounted electricity in case of violations is governed by the [Rules for the Use of Electric Energy and Natural Gas](https://lex.uz/docs/6954979). The document was approved by Cabinet of Ministers Resolution No. 319 of May 31, 2024. It is this methodology that agencies use to determine the volume and cost of electricity consumed without a contract or metering devices — as in the case of the workshop in Samarkand region.

## What's next

The Department did not name the individuals or their positions mentioned in the report, nor the exact period during which the illegal connection took place. Until a court verdict takes effect, all parties to the case are presumed innocent. The agency did not specify deadlines for completing the investigation or referring the case to court.
