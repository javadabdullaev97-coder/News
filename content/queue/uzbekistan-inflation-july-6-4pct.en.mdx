The Central Bank of Uzbekistan said annual inflation reached 6.4% in July 2026, compared with 12.3% in 2022, when price growth was at its highest in recent years.

The data appear in the [regulator's Telegram post](https://t.me/centralbankuzbekistan/13436), which notes a steady decline in price growth since 2022.

Official statistics from the [Central Bank at cbu.uz](https://cbu.uz/ru/monetary-policy/annual-inflation/indicators/) show the rate has held at 6.4% for a second consecutive month. Annual inflation was the same in June 2026.

That means prices in the country are rising twice as slowly as at the 2022 peak.
