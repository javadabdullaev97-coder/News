Apple increased iPhone shipments in India, Europe and Latin America amid an overall smartphone market slowdown, according to four [Counterpoint Research](https://counterpointresearch.com/en/insights/india-smartphone-weekly-sales-decline-for-three-consecutive-weeks) reports published August 19–20, 2026.

In India, smartphone sales fell 14% year-on-year during weeks 14 through 31 of 2026 — from late March to early August, [Counterpoint Research reported](https://counterpointresearch.com/en/insights/india-smartphone-weekly-sales-decline-for-three-consecutive-weeks). Apple's shipments over the same period grew 15% — the highest growth rate among all manufacturers in the market.

A similar picture emerged in Latin America: the market contracted 10% year-on-year in the second quarter of 2026. Apple's shipments rose 5%, [the report says](https://counterpointresearch.com/en/insights/samsung-apple-growth-latam-market-q2-2026). Regional leader Samsung added 6%. In the premium segment above $600, Apple accounts for about 51% of the market.

## Europe and China

In Europe, smartphone shipments fell 10% year-on-year in the second quarter, to 35 million units — the worst result in three years. According to AppleInsider and MacTech, citing a [Counterpoint Research report](https://counterpointresearch.com/en/insights/europe-smartphone-shipments-drop-10-percent-yoy-in-q2%202026), Apple's share in Europe grew from 31% to 34%, matching Samsung's share.

China is the only one of the four markets where the new Counterpoint report does not cite Apple's growth rate. Over the first 30 weeks of 2026, smartphone sales in the country fell 8.6% year-on-year. Since July, Apple has entered a seasonal slowdown: some demand was pulled forward during the "618" shopping festival, and prices have returned to normal levels, [the report says](https://counterpointresearch.com/en/insights/china-smartphone-market-saw-sharper-contraction-after-618). Counterpoint analysts expect demand to recover closer to the launch of the new iPhone lineup.
