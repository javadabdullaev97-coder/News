Hokim of Fergana Region Khayrullo Bozorov on August 6 received a delegation from South Korean company AHA Co., Ltd, led by its head Ku Gi Do, along with representatives of Chinese partner companies. The plant in [Ekoshahar will launch in September](https://gov.uz/uz/news/view/202901) — investment in the project totals $20 million.

## What is being built

AHA Co., Ltd [is investing](https://gov.uz/uz/news/view/202901) $20 million in the project in Fergana Region. The new plant in Ekoshahar will set up local production of interactive flat panel displays (IFPDs), electric vehicle chargers and medical equipment — the products will be supplied both to the domestic market and for export.

Ku Gi Do said construction and installation work has entered its final stage, and production will launch in September. He also said the company, together with its Chinese partners, will set up production of components and spare parts. In addition, the company will attract grant funding for education and information technology.

## Who attended the meeting

The meeting covered the progress of the plant's construction, joint projects in IT and education, and new cooperation plans. The hokim of Fergana Region praised the Korean investor's activity and responsibility in implementing the project. He also noted the investor's initiatives to attract new partners and expand the region's investment climate.

The parties discussed specific issues related to completing and launching the project — the officials responsible were given relevant instructions. The hokim stressed that the region will support all promising proposals and initiatives from foreign partners.

## What's next

Following the meeting, the parties agreed on further steps to develop cooperation. The exact launch date of the plant in September, the number of jobs to be created, and the names of the Chinese partner companies [are not specified in the government portal's publication](https://gov.uz/uz/news/view/202901).
