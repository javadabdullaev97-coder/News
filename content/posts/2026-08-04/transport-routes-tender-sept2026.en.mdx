The Ministry of Transport of Uzbekistan has announced a tender for the allocation of 19 passenger transport routes in eight regions — applications are being accepted until September 1, 2026, the agency [said](https://t.me/Mintrans_uz/28535) on its Telegram channel.

## How Many Lots, and Where

The routes have been put up for competition with the following regional breakdown: Andijan region — 8 routes; Namangan, Kashkadarya, Syrdarya regions and Karakalpakstan — 2 routes each; Bukhara, Fergana and Surkhandarya regions — 1 route each. These are urban and suburban routes within the regions, not interregional or international lines.

Winners will receive the right to operate on the routes under long-term contracts.

## How and Where to Apply

Applications are accepted through the electronic system [e-tender.uztrans.uz](https://e-tender.uztrans.uz) — a platform that has been used to allocate passenger transport routes by region, including interregional routes, since March 2022. The legal basis for the tender is Cabinet of Ministers Resolution No. 738 of December 6, 2021.

Applications are accepted until September 1, 2026, 12:00 noon. The tender itself — the opening of bids and selection of winners — is scheduled for September 2, 2026, also at 12:00 noon.

## Context

Uzbekistan's electronic tender system for routes has been in operation since March 1, 2022. Through the same system, the Ministry of Transport regularly announces separate tenders by region — the latest batch of lots covers eight regions at once.
