The Interagency Tariff Commission under the Cabinet of Ministers of the Republic of Uzbekistan approved regulated tariffs for the generation, transmission and distribution of electricity for settlements between the country's energy companies on July 15, in Minutes No. 37.

## How Much Electricity Costs at Each Link in the Chain

The minutes set settlement tariffs for state-owned energy companies — from power plants to regional grid operators. It was [signed](https://api-portal.gov.uz/uploads/3/2026/07/23/04ce7cca-04b6-c4f5-6cf8-6af33a1ded88_media_.pdf) by the commission's chairman, Deputy Prime Minister and Minister of Economy and Finance Jamshid Kuchkarov.

The cost of generating 1 kWh varies by power plant: [522 soums](https://api-portal.gov.uz/uploads/3/2026/07/23/04ce7cca-04b6-c4f5-6cf8-6af33a1ded88_media_.pdf) at Uzbekgidroenergo — the cheapest source on the list. Next come 579 soums at Talimarjan Thermal Power Plant, 828 at Novo-Angren Thermal Power Plant, 947 at Angren Thermal Power Plant and 957 at Takhiatash Thermal Power Plant. Electricity from Tashkent Thermal Power Plant is the most expensive — 1,126 soums per kWh. The tariff at Navoi Thermal Power Plant varies by buyer: 887 soums for Uzenergosotish and 832 soums for Navoiazot.

Between producers and consumers in the chain stands the sole electricity buyer. It resells power to the supply company Energosavdo at [704 soums](https://api-portal.gov.uz/uploads/3/2026/07/23/04ce7cca-04b6-c4f5-6cf8-6af33a1ded88_media_.pdf) per kWh, and compensates distribution and transmission grid operators for losses — at 843 and 735 soums, respectively.

Transmission via the trunk networks of the National Electric Grids of Uzbekistan costs [98 soums](https://api-portal.gov.uz/uploads/3/2026/07/23/04ce7cca-04b6-c4f5-6cf8-6af33a1ded88_media_.pdf) per kWh — a single tariff with no regional breakdown.

Distribution costs vary most across regional networks: from [171 soums](https://api-portal.gov.uz/uploads/3/2026/07/23/04ce7cca-04b6-c4f5-6cf8-6af33a1ded88_media_.pdf) in Kashkadarya Region to 410 soums in Karakalpakstan. In Tashkent, the city grid company charges 276 soums per kWh; in Tashkent Region — 254; in Fergana Region — 366; in Samarkand Region — 278; in Andijan Region — 243.

## Why the Tariffs Were Revised Now

LEAP News [reported on this decision](https://leap.uz/en/2026/08/07/energy-tariff-rab-methodology-enterprises-aug2026) on August 7 — at the time, only the estimated percentage increases in costs previously announced by the Ministry of Economy and Finance were known. Minutes No. 37, with the final tariff amounts for each link in the energy chain that this article is based on, became available later.

The commission explained the revision by new prices for fuel and energy resources that took effect on June 1, 2026. They were [approved](https://lex.uz/uz/docs/8195731) by the Cabinet of Ministers through Resolution No. 243 of May 15. After that, the existing tariffs for the generation, transmission, distribution and sale of electricity [required recalculation](https://api-portal.gov.uz/uploads/3/2026/07/23/04ce7cca-04b6-c4f5-6cf8-6af33a1ded88_media_.pdf), the minutes say. The calculations were prepared by the Ministry of Economy and Finance as the commission's working body. It relied on the regulations for setting electricity and gas tariffs approved by the Cabinet of Ministers in June 2024.

All seven commission members present voted for the decision; no one voted against or abstained.

## Who the New Figures Affect

The approved tariffs are not what households and businesses pay for electricity. They are settlement rates between the energy companies themselves: producers (Uzbekgidroenergo, thermal power plants), the national grid operator, the sole buyer, regional distribution networks and the supply company Energosavdo. [Resolution No. 243](https://lex.uz/uz/docs/8195731) sets retail tariffs for end consumers separately. For households with a centralized electric stove, the rate ranges from 325 to 1,100 soums per kWh depending on consumption volume; for other household consumers — from 650 to 2,200 soums. For consumers outside the household category, the tariff is 1,100 soums per kWh.

The same minutes also [revised](https://api-portal.gov.uz/uploads/3/2026/07/23/04ce7cca-04b6-c4f5-6cf8-6af33a1ded88_media_.pdf) wholesale natural gas tariffs between UzGasTrade, Uztransgaz and regional gas supply organizations. The range is from 195,000 to 1,600,000 soums per thousand cubic meters.
