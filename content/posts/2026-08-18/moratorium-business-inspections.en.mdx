President Shavkat Mirziyoyev on August 18 announced a three-year moratorium on small business inspections at the sixth open dialogue with entrepreneurs in the Khorezm region, with exceptions for cases involving threats to people's health or damage to other entrepreneurs.

These exceptions will apply throughout all three years of the moratorium, while other types of small business oversight will be suspended for that period. [The Telegram channel of presidential press secretary Sherzod Asadov reported this](https://t.me/Press_Secretary_Uz/7721).

## Rules for Medium and Large Businesses

A different oversight mechanism is being introduced for medium and large businesses. A company will be able to undergo a voluntary preventive audit: if it corrects the identified deficiencies, the tax authority will accept the audit's findings and no fine will follow. The Commissioner for the Protection of the Rights and Legitimate Interests of Business Entities under the President of the Republic of Uzbekistan (business ombudsman) will issue permits for repeat inspections of the same company within a year. If an entrepreneur violates a rule for the first time and causes no harm to people's life, health or property, they will be given ten days to fix the deficiencies without a fine. [The Business Ombudsman's channel reported this](https://t.me/biznes_ombudsman/13042).

## Presumption of Innocence and Licenses

The same source states that an entrepreneur will be presumed innocent until guilt is proven in court. The oversight body will pay the state fee at every stage of appeal after the first.

The business ombudsman will gain the right to review the validity of every license denial. Officials' liability for unjustified denials will be tightened, [the agency's channel said](https://t.me/biznes_ombudsman/13036).

The moratorium and the new inspection rules are among the initiatives announced at the president's sixth open dialogue with entrepreneurs.
