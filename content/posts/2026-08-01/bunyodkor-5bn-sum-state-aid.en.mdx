Football club Bunyodkor said on August 1 that the government allocated it 5 billion soums on July 31; on July 30, the Professional Football League had announced the club could not travel to Navoi for lack of money.

## 5 Billion Soums Under Resolution PP-107

The club [announced](https://t.me/fcBunyodkorOfficial/80270) the allocation on the morning of August 1 on its official page. According to Bunyodkor's wording, the funds were allocated under Presidential Resolution PP-107 of the Republic of Uzbekistan, dated March 24, 2026, "On Measures to Improve the Mechanisms for Financing the Sports Sector and Expand the Involvement of Business Entities in the Industry." The amount is 5 billion soums, or about $416,000 at the Central Bank of Uzbekistan's exchange rate of 12,006.39 soums per dollar, which was in effect [on July 31](https://cbu.uz/ru/arkhiv-kursov-valyut/json/USD/2026-08-01/).

The Ministry of Sports of the Republic of Uzbekistan has not published any statement about the transfer on its official page. The club itself is the only source of information about it.

Two days earlier, on July 30, the Professional Football League (PFL) [said](https://t.me/uzpfl/78925) that Bunyodkor would be unable to travel to Navoi because of financial difficulties. PFC Qizilqum [reposted](https://t.me/pfc_qizilqum/44926) the league's message on its own channel the same day.

Bunyodkor itself [published](https://t.me/fcBunyodkorOfficial/80264) a separate statement about its situation on July 30. The club confirmed it was going through economically difficult days and said it was working to find a sponsor. It also asked for the second tranche of 10 billion soums (about $833,000) from state funding already approved for 2026 to be disbursed sooner.

## The Mechanism: 35 Billion Soums per Super League Club in 2026

Resolution PP-107, which Bunyodkor cites, [created](https://www.lex.uz/uz/docs/8097905) a Sports Initiatives Support Fund under the Ministry of Sports — without legal-entity status, managed through a board of trustees. In 2026 the fund receives 700 billion soums from the State Budget (about $58 million at the same rate), of which 200 billion soums are due by April 1 and the rest quarterly.

The document sets direct funding for professional football clubs on a declining scale: each Super League club gets 35 billion soums in 2026 (about $2.9 million), 30 billion soums in 2027, and 25 billion soums in 2028.

## What's Next

Round 15 of the Super League [kicked off](https://t.me/uzpfl/78945) on August 1. As of publication, the PFL had not announced any decision on postponing Bunyodkor's away match or awarding a technical result.

On July 31, the league [said](https://t.me/uzpfl/78938) it would cooperate with Bunyodkor to help organize the match against AGMK.

On August 1, the club [thanked](https://t.me/fcBunyodkorOfficial/80271) the fans, bloggers, sports websites, and clubs that had supported it during these days — Pakhtakor, Surkhan, Bukhara, Navbahor, Lokomotiv, and Olimpik-Mobiuz.
