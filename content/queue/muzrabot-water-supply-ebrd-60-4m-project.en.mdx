O'zsuvta'minot JSC reported August 9 on progress building a $60.4 million water pipeline that will connect 56,000 residents of Muzrabad district in Surkhandarya region to centralized water supply for the first time by the end of 2026.

## Where the water will come from

The pipeline is being built from the Oqtosh and Poshxo'rt underground sources, located in neighboring Sherabad district. From there, water will flow through a trunk pipeline to towns in Muzrabad district, where some residents currently lack a connection to the centralized network. The project was approved by [Presidential Resolution PP-342](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/-6139620) of August 2, 2022 — the same document also launched a parallel water supply project in Namangan region. It is being implemented by O'zsuvta'minot JSC and Surkhandarya Suv Ta'minoti LLC together with the regional administration.

## Who is paying for the project

The project's total cost is $60.38 million. Of that, [$50 million](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/-6139620) is a loan from the European Bank for Reconstruction and Development (EBRD) with an 18-year term and a four-year grace period, while the remaining $10.38 million is Uzbekistan's share. The EBRD's board of directors approved the financing back on [November 18, 2020](https://www.ebrd.com/home/work-with-us/projects/psd/50979.html) as part of the broader "Surkhandarya Water Project," with an environmental impact category of B. The 2022 resolution set the implementation timeline in Uzbekistan at 2022–2026.

## What the district will get

President Shavkat Mirziyoyev held a [meeting on the development of Surkhandarya region](https://president.uz/oz/lists/view/8980) on February 27, 2026. Following the meeting, authorities plan to complete the connection of 56,000 Muzrabad district residents to centralized water supply by the end of this year. The same meeting separately addressed plans to build sewage systems in the district centers of Muzrabad, Oltinsoy, Angor, Qiziriq and Boysun — this work is not part of the EBRD project.

The Muzrabad project is part of a broader program to upgrade water supply in Surkhandarya region. According to a [presidential meeting in Termez on February 26, 2026](https://president.uz/oz/lists/view/8974), a separate project based on the Tupalang reservoir raised centralized water supply coverage in Termez and ten districts of the region from 47% to 67.5%, providing water to 563,000 people.
