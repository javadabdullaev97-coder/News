[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/20/openai-is-gaining-on-anthropic-with-business-users-new-data-indicates/) reported on August 20, citing data from Ramp, a corporate card operator, that OpenAI's share of U.S. business customers is growing faster in the third quarter of 2026, though Anthropic still leads.

Ramp processes payments and expenses for more than 70,000 companies in the United States and tracks which AI services they pay for. The company does not disclose dollar amounts and publishes only customer shares.

According to [Ramp's index from August 12](https://ramp.com/data/ai-index-august-2026), Anthropic led among AI-paying customers in July 2026 with 43.5%, against 39.7% for OpenAI. Over the month, Anthropic added 1.1 percentage points, while OpenAI added 0.23 percentage points.

In May 2026, according to [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/20/openai-is-gaining-on-anthropic-with-business-users-new-data-indicates/), Anthropic's share stood at 41%, OpenAI's at 39%. Anthropic's lead widened by July. Since the start of the third quarter, OpenAI has been growing faster than Anthropic in this segment, [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/20/openai-is-gaining-on-anthropic-with-business-users-new-data-indicates/) reports, citing Ramp data not yet published on the index's website.

## GPT-5.6 Sol vs. Fable 5

Ramp economist Ara Kharazyan told [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/20/openai-is-gaining-on-anthropic-with-business-users-new-data-indicates/) that OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Sol model is becoming an increasingly popular choice among developers. According to him, Anthropic's Fable 5 model has disappointed both in adoption and in practical use, due to its price and data storage requirements.

The share of Ramp companies paying for at least one AI service rose from more than 50% in March to nearly 56% by July 2026, TechCrunch notes. The outlet points out that businesses are willing to switch between providers following the release of new models. This volatility raises questions about the sustainability of corporate AI spending for investors in both companies.
