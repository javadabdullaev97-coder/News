Residents of 21 mahallas (neighborhood communities) in Andijan city owe 34 billion 674 million 108 thousand 255 soums for drinking water, JSC Andijon Suv Ta'minoti, a regional subsidiary of JSC Ozsuvta'minot, reported on August 7. The debt was accumulated by 95,241 customers.

## What Was Discussed at the Meeting

The debt was discussed at a meeting at JSC Andijon Suv Ta'minoti on August 7. According to the company, the enterprise's director, Dilshodbek Kuldashev, analyzed the debt structure and set tasks for [reducing the debt, working directly with consumers, and improving payment discipline](https://t.me/uzsuv/45466).

The company's regional Telegram channel also reported the same debt amount — [34 billion 674 million soums](https://t.me/uzsuvandijon/20708) — and specified that it had accumulated among residents of 21 mahallas in Andijan. The meeting set tasks for addressing the problem, but the company did not disclose specific measures against debtors — repayment deadlines, possible penalties, or a suspension of water supply — in its publications.

## How Much Is That in Dollars

34.7 billion soums is about $2.9 million [at the Central Bank of Uzbekistan's exchange rate for August 7, 2026](https://cbu.uz/ru/arkhiv-kursov-valyut/json/USD/2026-08-07/) (11,915.64 soums per dollar). Per debtor, that averages about 364,000 soums, or roughly $30 per customer.

## How to Check Your Debt

JSC Ozsuvta'minot maintains a [personal account](https://cabinet.uzsuv.uz/) for customers, where they can check charges and pay for water supply and sewerage services online.
