WBC welterweight champion Ryan Garcia accused Conor Benn on August 18 of looking for excuses before their September 12 fight in Las Vegas, [Boxing News Online reported](https://boxingnewsonline.net/news/garcia-on-weights-concerns-for-benn/). The fight will be the Briton's last at 147 pounds — Benn made that decision himself because of difficulties making weight. Benn is the WBC's mandatory challenger.

"We know you can't make the weight," Garcia said in a video posted by Ring Magazine. That, he said, is why Conor Benn called this his last fight at 147 pounds. "My opponent is looking for excuses before he's even lost — who even says that: 'this is my last fight at 147,'" the champion added. "Are you really going to move back just to get a beating?" he asked, addressing Benn.

Garcia won the WBC welterweight title in February 2026 by beating Mario Barrios on points, [Sky Sports reports](https://www.skysports.com/boxing/news/12183/13561973/conor-benn-vs-ryan-garcia-wbc-welterweight-title-fight-announced-in-las-vegas-on-september-12). The September 12 fight will be his first defense of the belt. Benn last fought at 147 pounds in April 2022, when he knocked out Chris van Heerden, according to Boxing News Online. Sky Sports, however, reports that in April 2026 Benn beat Regis Prograis on the undercard of Tyson Fury's fight.

On the same night's undercard at T-Mobile Arena, cruiserweight champion Jai Opetaia will face WBC cruiserweight champion Noel Mikaelian. Opetaia defends his Ring and Zuffa Boxing titles, while it remains unclear whether Mikaelian will still hold the WBC belt by fight time, [CBS Sports reports](https://www.cbssports.com/boxing/news/jai-opetaia-next-fight-noel-mikaelian-ryan-garcia-vs-conor-benn-undercard/).
