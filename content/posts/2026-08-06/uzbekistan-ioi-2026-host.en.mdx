Uzbekistan will host the 38th International Olympiad in Informatics (IOI 2026) for the first time in its history on August 9–16 — the competition will take place in Tashkent, the Telegram channel of the Ministry of Preschool and School Education [reported](https://t.me/uzedu/44664).

## Venue and organizers

The olympiad's venue is the Central Asian Expo (CAEx) exhibition center in Tashkent's Yangi Uzbekiston (New Uzbekistan) residential area, [according to a report](https://gov.uz/en/digital/news/view/130997) on the gov.uz portal about a visit by members of the IOI international committee, who assessed Uzbekistan's readiness to host guests — including the congress hall and 12 hotels in the district.

The organizer on the Uzbek side is the Ministry of Digital Technologies together with the Ministry of Preschool and School Education. A press conference on preparations for the olympiad, [according to the ministry's Telegram channel](https://t.me/uzedu/44663), was attended by First Deputy Minister of Preschool and School Education Sherzod Karimov, Deputy Minister of Digital Technologies Rustam Karimjonov, Director of the Center for Scientific Olympiads Turaboy Shermatov, and Director of the Center for Digital Education Development Azamat Eshankulov.

## Scale

The olympiad is expected to draw 745 participants from more than 97 countries, the ministry [reports](https://t.me/uzedu/44664). Uzbekistan will act not only as host but also as a participant — the country will be represented at IOI 2026 by its national team.

## Uzbekistan's previous results at IOI

Uzbekistan has taken part in the IOI regularly since [2015](https://stats.ioinformatics.org/countries/UZB). The country won its first medals at IOI 2023 in Budapest, when Dilyorbek Valijonov and Dilshodbek Khodjaev, students at a school in Tashkent's Almazar district, took bronze, [IT Park Uzbekistan reported](https://www.it-park.uz/en/itpark/news/the-international-olympiad-in-informatics-in-2026-will-take-place-in-uzbekistan). The national team's best result came at IOI 2025 in Sucre, Bolivia: Asilbek Sunnatov won silver and Ulugbek Rakhmatullaev won bronze, according to official IOI statistics on [stats.ioinformatics.org](https://stats.ioinformatics.org/results/2025).
