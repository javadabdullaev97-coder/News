Uzsuvtaminot JSC on August 8 [reminded](https://t.me/uzsuv/45497) customers that the company's controllers have no right to demand cash from them for drinking water — all payments are accepted only in cashless form.

## What the Company Reminded

Uzsuvtaminot JSC published the message in the channel's regular segment "#Mutaxassis_minbari" ("Specialist's Tribune"), a format the company uses to regularly address typical customer questions. In the [August 8 post](https://t.me/uzsuv/45497), the company states directly: the company's controllers (nozoratchi) have no authority to accept cash from a customer on the spot.

## Why Paying a Controller in Cash Is Not Allowed

The rule has been in place for some time. Uzsuvtaminot JSC [announced](https://t.me/uzsuv/25057) a full switch to cashless payments for drinking water supply and sewerage on December 1, 2024. Since that date, cash desks no longer accept cash, and payments go through the personal account [cabinet.uzsuv.uz](https://cabinet.uzsuv.uz) and other cashless payment channels. Among the goals of the switch, the company cites fighting corruption among controllers and reducing accounts receivable.

This rule has a direct implication for customers: cash handed to a controller "hand to hand" does not enter the company's official billing database. It does not guarantee that a debt or meter reading will be recorded.

## Where to Report If a Controller Demands Money

If someone identifying themselves as an Uzsuvtaminot controller demands cash for the company's services, customers should not pay. Uzsuvtaminot recommends reporting this through the official channels listed in the [August 8 post](https://t.me/uzsuv/45497):

- hotline — phone **1255**;
- Telegram complaints bot — [@suvmuammobot](https://t.me/suvmuammobot);
- company website — [uzsuv.uz](https://uzsuv.uz);
- personal account — [cabinet.uzsuv.uz](https://cabinet.uzsuv.uz).
