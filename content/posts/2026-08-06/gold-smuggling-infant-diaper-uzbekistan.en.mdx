The State Customs Committee of Uzbekistan [said](https://t.me/customschannel/70803) on August 5 it had stopped two attempts to illegally smuggle gold and foreign currency at the Navoi border checkpoint — in both cases, valuables were hidden with children.

## Gold in an infant's belongings

In the first case, a woman hid gold bars weighing a total of about 200 grams in an infant's belongings. According to the [customs committee](https://t.me/customschannel/70803), the seized gold was valued at approximately 450 million soums. That is about $37,700 at the [Central Bank of Uzbekistan exchange rate](https://cbu.uz/ru/arkhiv-kursov-valyut/json/USD/2026-08-05/) for August 5 (11,942.21 soums per dollar). The committee did not disclose the name or age of the woman detained with the gold.

## Dollars hidden under a teenager's clothing

In the second case, according to the same customs committee data, a woman attempted to smuggle out $40,000 hidden under the clothing of a 14-year-old girl. The agency did not disclose the relationship between the woman and the teenager, nor did it name the country of which the detained woman is a citizen.

The customs committee did not specify what penalties the women could face following the two inspections or whether the cases were referred to law enforcement.
