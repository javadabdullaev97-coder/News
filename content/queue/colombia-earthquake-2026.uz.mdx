Qo'shiqchi Shakira 18 avgustda Kolumbiyaning g'arbidagi Choko departamentida — 10 avgust zilzilasidan eng ko'p zarar ko'rgan hududda — maktablarni tiklash uchun 1,25 million dollar yig'ish haqida e'lon qildi, deb xabar beradi [AP News](https://www.wral.com/news/ap/24d2e-shakira-makes-surprise-visit-to-earthquake-damaged-colombian-schools-and-says-she-will-help-rebuild/).

Qo'shiqchi shaxsan Choko markazi Kibdo shahriga tashrif buyurdi. Mablag'lar FIFA Global ta'lim jamg'armasi va prodyuser Live Nation'dan keladi — har biridan 500 000 dollardan. Yana 250 000 dollarni Lotin Amerikasi va Karib havzasi taraqqiyot banki (CAF) yo'naltiradi, deb xabar beradi AP. Pul maktablarni tiklash va bolalarni sinflarga qaytarishga sarflanadi.

«Meni eng ko'p tashvishga soladigan narsa — favqulodda vaziyat tugaganda maktablar tiklanmay qolishi», — dedi Shakira.

Loyihaga amerikalik investor Uorren Baffetning o'g'li Xovard Baffet jamg'armasi qo'shildi — u Shakiraning o'zining Pies Descalzos xayriya jamg'armasi bilan hamkorlik qiladi, deb aniqlashtiradi AP.

7,4 magnitudali zilzila Kolumbiyada 10 avgustda Choko departamentida markazlashgan holda yuz berdi, deb [xabar bergan edi](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/10/7-4-magnitude-earthquake-hits-colombia-killing-at-least-20) Al Jazeera. 17 avgust holatiga ko'ra halok bo'lganlar soni 289 nafarga yetdi, deb [yozgan edi](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/17/as-rubble-clears-colombia-begins-focusing-on-recovery-after-the-earthquake) Al Jazeera — zarar va hokimiyat munosabati haqida batafsil LEAP News [ilgari yozgan edi](https://leap.uz/ru/2026/08/18/colombia-earthquake-toll-289-damage).

## Keyingi qadamlar

Shu yakshanba, 23 avgustda, Shakira Bogotada xayriya konsertida Maluma va Sebastyan Yatra bilan birga chiqish qiladi, deb xabar beradi AP.
