Six Anthropic investors expect the company to be valued at more than $2 trillion at its IPO in October 2026, [Ars Technica reports](https://arstechnica.com/ai/2026/08/anthropic-could-be-worth-2-trillion-when-it-goes-public/), citing the Financial Times. That would surpass SpaceX's June debut.

## Who's Behind the Number, and Why

Anthropic itself has not confirmed the target valuation: according to investors, the company's leadership has not settled on a figure for the IPO even in internal talks, [Ars Technica writes](https://arstechnica.com/ai/2026/08/anthropic-could-be-worth-2-trillion-when-it-goes-public/). The $2 trillion figure is a calculation by the company's own backers, based on revenue projections.

Investors expect Anthropic's annualized revenue to reach $100–120 billion by the end of 2026 — more than a tenfold increase since the start of the year, [Ars Technica reports](https://arstechnica.com/ai/2026/08/anthropic-could-be-worth-2-trillion-when-it-goes-public/). One investor called $2 trillion an undervaluation: at an annual growth rate of 800% and a multiple of 30 times annual revenue, the company could be worth $3 trillion.

Anthropic's current valuation is $965 billion, set in May 2026 after a $65 billion Series H round with participation from Altimeter Capital, Dragoneer, Greenoaks, Sequoia Capital, Capital Group, Coatue and D1 Capital Partners, [TechCrunch reported](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/01/anthropic-files-to-go-public/). On June 1, 2026, Anthropic filed confidential IPO paperwork with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) — at the time of filing, the company was valued at about $1 trillion, [TechCrunch wrote](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/01/anthropic-files-to-go-public/).

## Decart Acquisition and Competition with OpenAI

Alongside its IPO preparations, Anthropic is in talks to acquire Israeli startup Decart AI for $6 billion — which would be the largest acquisition in the company's history, [Bloomberg reported on August 13](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-13/anthropic-said-in-talks-to-buy-ai-startup-decart-for-6-billion). Decart is developing the DOS stack for accelerating neural network training and inference, as well as the Lucy and Oasis "world" models for simulating physical environments. The startup raised $300 million in a Series B round at a valuation of about $4 billion in May 2026, according to Bloomberg. The deal has not closed and may not go through, the agency notes.

Anthropic's main competitor in the market is OpenAI, which raised a $122 billion round at an $852 billion valuation in March 2026, [TechCrunch reported](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/01/anthropic-files-to-go-public/). Both companies are preparing to go public around the same time window.

## What Comes Next

Anthropic has disclosed neither the size of the offering nor an exact IPO date — the "October" target comes from investors, not the company itself, [Ars Technica notes](https://arstechnica.com/ai/2026/08/anthropic-could-be-worth-2-trillion-when-it-goes-public/). The company already filed confidential paperwork with the SEC in June 2026, [TechCrunch reported](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/01/anthropic-files-to-go-public/); the offering price and number of shares have yet to be determined.
