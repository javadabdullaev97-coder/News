Egypt captain Mohamed Salah flew into Istanbul on August 5 wearing Trabzonspor's kit — the Turkish club expects to sign him at a ceremony in Trabzon on Thursday, August 6.

## Club still says "expected," not "signed"

Salah stepped off the plane in Trabzonspor's maroon-and-blue kit and was met by a crowd of fans at Atatürk Airport, [Al Jazeera reports](https://www.aljazeera.com/sports/2026/8/5/ex-liverpool-star-salah-arrives-in-turkiye-ahead-of-move-to-trabzonspor). The forward addressed club fans with a video message: "Trabzon, are you ready? I can hear you. See you very soon."

There had been no official announcement from the club or the player confirming the deal was complete by the time Salah arrived in Turkey. Trabzonspor chairman Ertuğrul Doğan said the club expects to sign the 34-year-old forward on a free transfer — meaning no transfer fee paid to Liverpool, [Al Jazeera writes](https://www.aljazeera.com/sports/2026/8/5/ex-liverpool-star-salah-arrives-in-turkiye-ahead-of-move-to-trabzonspor). The signing ceremony is scheduled for Thursday in Trabzon, a city on Turkey's Black Sea coast. Contract terms — length and salary — had not been disclosed by either the club or the player at the time of publication.

## Parting ways with Liverpool

Salah left Liverpool at the end of last season — the two sides terminated his contract by mutual agreement ahead of the term agreed in April 2025, [Sky Sports reports](https://www.skysports.com/football/news/12040/13569969/mohamed-salah-former-liverpool-forward-set-to-join-turkish-club-trabzonspor-on-free-transfer-after-leaving-anfield). Over nine seasons at Anfield, the forward scored 257 goals in 442 matches — the third-highest tally in club history — and won seven trophies with the team.

According to Sky Sports, Salah had previously turned down offers from Saudi Arabia, including a £150 million bid from Al-Ittihad three years ago. The exact terms of his prospective Trabzonspor contract remain unconfirmed: former English Premier League player John Curtis, speaking on Sky Sports, suggested the forward's salary is rumored to be around £17 million a year — less than he earned at Liverpool. That figure comes not from an official club statement but from an expert's estimate.

## What's next

Trabzonspor qualified for the Europa League playoff round after finishing third in last season's Turkish Süper Lig. The contract-signing ceremony with Salah is scheduled for Thursday, August 6, in Trabzon. No official confirmation from Liverpool about the details of the contract termination had been received at the time of publication.
