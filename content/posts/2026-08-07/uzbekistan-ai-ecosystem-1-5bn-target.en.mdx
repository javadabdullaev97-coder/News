Uzbekistan President Shavkat Mirziyoyev on August 7 set a target to bring the country's AI products and services output to $1.5 billion and implement more than 200 new projects. The tasks were set following a [presentation](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9488) on the development of the national artificial intelligence ecosystem.

## Uzbekistan's position in the global ranking

In recent years, Uzbekistan has [risen](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9488) 25 positions to rank 62nd in the international artificial intelligence readiness index. The goal is to enter the top 50 countries in the world on this index by 2030.

"Artificial intelligence technologies are developing at a rapid pace," the head of state said. He noted the need to analyze global and regional trends, quickly adopt advanced developments, and put forward new initiatives.

## Computing infrastructure and data

In July, Uzbekistan [launched](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9488) a supercomputer based on NVIDIA technology, which ranked 321st in the global ranking. Within a month of operation, the cluster's load reached 90%.

To date, computing infrastructure worth $24 million has been put into operation. The presentation proposed allocating another $70 million to expand the cluster.

"The effectiveness of AI-based solutions directly depends on the quality of the database," the presentation noted. The president instructed that data in ministries and agencies be inventoried and its completeness and accuracy ensured. The presentation separately emphasized the need to increase the number of AI projects in the economy and social sphere to more than 100 this year — solutions for healthcare, energy, transport, and customs administration were presented.

## IT Park, personnel, and specialist training

The importance of engaging with IT Park residents, startups, and more than 20 AI laboratories was noted. According to [expectations presented](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9488), the number of IT Park residents will reach 300, the volume of their services will total 2&nbsp;trillion soums (≈$168&nbsp;million at the Central Bank of Uzbekistan's exchange rate as of August 7), and exports will reach $160 million.

More than 1.5 million users have already received AI certificates. Training has been completed by 900 executives, more than 3,000 civil servants, 1,000 bank employees, and 200 hokim advisers. Over the next two years, the task has been set to implement the "Five Million AI Leaders" project and develop educational and methodological materials.

Industry hackathons were held in eight cities, and the "Best Artificial Intelligence Startup" competition was launched. The presentation separately discussed the work of hokim advisers on AI issues: 222 advisers were selected out of 500 candidates. The president instructed that a digital platform be launched to monitor their activities and the projects they implement.

## International cooperation

Uzbekistan [became a member](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9488) of WAICO, the World Artificial Intelligence Cooperation Organization. The presentation emphasized that participation in the organization should help advance initiatives to adopt advanced technologies and implement joint projects.

Following the presentation, the president gave instructions on expanding computing capacity, scaling up practical projects, building a quality database, supporting youth, and developing international cooperation. The official release does not specify exact implementation deadlines or responsible agencies.
