Turkish club Trabzonspor announced on August 5 that it had signed Egyptian forward Mohamed Salah, who joined the team as a free agent after leaving Liverpool, [CBS Sports reports](https://www.cbssports.com/soccer/news/mohamed-salah-trabzonspor-liverpool-free-agent/).

The contract runs for two years, through summer 2028. At Trabzonspor, the 34-year-old forward received the number 61. Salah left Liverpool in May 2026, after nine seasons at the club, 257 goals and 442 appearances for the English team.

## Why Salah left Liverpool

Salah's relationship with Liverpool head coach Arne Slot deteriorated in late 2025: in December, he was left on the bench three times in a row. The player then said the coach had "set him up," and said he had no contact with him, [CBS Sports writes](https://www.cbssports.com/soccer/news/mohamed-salah-trabzonspor-liverpool-free-agent/). After returning from the Africa Cup of Nations in January, Salah did not regain his place in the starting lineup. In March 2026, he announced his departure from the club. Besides Trabzonspor, his transfer was discussed by MLS clubs and Saudi Pro League clubs, but those talks did not result in a deal.

## Trabzonspor president: 'Salah was entrusted to us'

On August 16, Trabzonspor president Ertuğrul Doğan commented on the transfer on Egyptian channel ON Sport. According to [TRT Spor](https://www.trtspor.com.tr/haber/futbol/dogan-salah-bize-emanet-32661828), Doğan called Salah a player important to world football and linked the move to relations between Turkey and Egypt. "Mohamed Salah was entrusted to us," he said.

Trabzonspor finished last season third in the Turkish Süper Lig and is competing in Europa League qualifying rounds this season.
