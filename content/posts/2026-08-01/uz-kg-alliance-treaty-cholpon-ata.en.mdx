The presidents of Uzbekistan and Kyrgyzstan, Shavkat Mirziyoyev and Sadyr Japarov, [signed](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9472) a Treaty on Allied Relations in Cholpon-Ata on July 30 — the outcome of the Uzbek leader's state visit to Kyrgyzstan.

## The Treaty and the Package of Agreements

The official welcoming ceremony was held at the [Ruh Ordo cultural center named after Chingiz Aitmatov](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9470) in Cholpon-Ata. Talks were also held there — first in a narrow format, then with members of the official delegations, the press service of the president of Uzbekistan [said](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9471).

Together with the alliance treaty, the two sides [exchanged](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9472) a package of bilateral documents. It included a treaty on sections of the Uzbek-Kyrgyz state border, an agreement on the joint use of the Chashma spring, an agreement on state social insurance and pension provision, and an agreement on the mutual recognition of higher education credits. The same package included protocols on border crossing points and mutual travel by citizens, as well as joint action plans in culture, tourism and the agro-industrial sector for 2026–2027.

Separate documents [concern](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9472) resort and recreational facilities in the Issyk-Kul region and the Buston resort in Ton district.

The sides agreed to establish an Intergovernmental Council chaired by the presidents of the two countries. According to the official wording, it [will become](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9474) "the main mechanism for implementing allied relations and determining long-term priorities of cooperation."

Mirziyoyev [called](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9474) the visit "historic" and said: "Our main task now is to fill our cooperation with new content, projects and initiatives."

## Trade Turnover: $1.2 Billion and a Target of $3 Billion

Over recent years, trade turnover between the two countries has grown almost tenfold and [exceeded](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9474) $1.2 billion last year. The sides set a target of raising the figure to $3 billion.

450 joint ventures have been established, and the countries are connected by 15 flights and 5 bus routes, a [statement](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9471) on the results of the narrow-format talks said.

New projects in energy, electrical engineering, pharmaceuticals, agriculture and textiles were agreed at a business forum [held the day before](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9474). A Joint Development Fund will be used to support them.

The sides identified energy as a strategic area. The Ministry of Energy of the Republic of Uzbekistan [said](https://t.me/minenergy_uz/20639) that the talks underscored the importance of signing an agreement on the Kambar-Ata HPP-1 construction project as soon as possible. The same station number appears in a [statement](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9471) from the president's press service on the narrow-format talks. In the final press service statement on the results of the talks, the station is named without a number — the Kambar-Ata HPP. Signing the agreement on it [will be expedited](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9474).

The transport section of the agenda is the China–Kyrgyzstan–Uzbekistan railway. The presidents [agreed](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9474) to jointly develop trade and logistics infrastructure along this route and to introduce digital solutions into trade and transport procedures.

## Order of Manas, 1st Degree

On July 30, Japarov [presented](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9473) Mirziyoyev with the Order of Manas, 1st degree. Mirziyoyev said he viewed the award as "a high appreciation of joint efforts to strengthen the centuries-old bonds of true friendship and good neighborliness between the two peoples."

The Order of Manas was [established](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9473) in 1996 and ranks among Kyrgyzstan's highest state awards. It is given to citizens of the republic and to foreigners for contributions to strengthening statehood and developing international cooperation.

## Summit of Six Presidents in Cholpon-Ata

On July 31, Mirziyoyev [took part](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9476) in an informal consultative meeting of the heads of state of Central Asia and Azerbaijan in Cholpon-Ata. The meeting was also attended by the presidents of Azerbaijan, Ilham Aliyev; Kazakhstan, Kassym-Jomart Tokayev; Kyrgyzstan, Sadyr Japarov; Tajikistan, Emomali Rahmon; and Turkmenistan, Serdar Berdimuhamedov.

Following the meeting, the [Cholpon-Ata Declaration](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9476) was adopted. The press service of the president of Uzbekistan limited itself to stating the fact and did not disclose the content of the document. The Kazinform agency [reported](https://qazinform.com/news/leaders-of-central-asia-and-azerbaijan-sign-cholpon-ata-declaration-7c10aa) that the presidents discussed regional cooperation, trade and economic ties, transport and logistics, and strengthening strategic partnership; the agency does not disclose the content of the declaration itself. The wording of the two reports diverges: on president.uz the declaration was "adopted," while Kazinform says it was "signed." The full text of the document had not been posted on president.uz at the time of publication.

In his remarks, Mirziyoyev [proposed](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9477) forming a unified space for development encompassing Central Asia, the South Caucasus and Afghanistan, strengthening coordination within intergovernmental organizations, and developing the region as a center of education, science and innovation. He devoted a separate section to adapting to climate change. He also [congratulated](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9477) Kyrgyzstan on its election as a non-permanent member of the UN Security Council. Mirziyoyev had [named](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9474) the mandate's term — 2027–2028 — the day before, following the bilateral talks.

The day's program included the opening of the five-star Baku Hotel on the shore of Issyk-Kul. The hotel [is located](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9478) on a 17.2-hectare plot and includes a seven-story building of about 30,000 square meters with more than 120 rooms. The day before, the presidents [opened](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9475) Kyrgyzstan's first international-level golf club in Cholpon-Ata.

## What's Next

The first forum of regions of Uzbekistan and Kyrgyzstan in the new format [will take place](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9474) in Uzbekistan by the end of 2026. The press services did not name a date for signing the agreement on the Kambar-Ata HPP.

The timing for submitting the Treaty on Allied Relations for ratification to the Oliy Majlis has not been officially announced. As of August 1, the texts of the treaty and the Cholpon-Ata Declaration had not been published on president.uz.
