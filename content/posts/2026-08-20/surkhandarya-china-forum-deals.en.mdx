The international ["Uzbekistan (Surkhandarya) — China"](https://t.me/axborot_xizmati/58834) forum ran in Termez on August 14-16 and concluded with preliminary agreements on 63 investment projects. Their total value is $6.25 billion, or more precisely $6,253.5 million, [the Surkhandarya Region hokimiyat said on August 17](https://t.me/axborot_xizmati/58834).

The region presented 95 investment projects worth $10 billion in construction materials, textiles, mining, services, tourism, agriculture, oil and gas, chemicals, electrical engineering and mechanical engineering. Preliminary agreements were reached on 63 of them, [the hokimiyat's statement said](https://t.me/axborot_xizmati/58834).

More than 350 representatives of over 120 Chinese companies took part in the forum. It was also attended by about 100 representatives of 46 Afghan companies and more than a thousand local officials and entrepreneurs, [the hokimiyat said](https://t.me/axborot_xizmati/58834).

The opening ceremony on August 14 was led by Deputy Prime Minister Jamshid Khodjaev and Surkhandarya Region hokim Ulugbek Kosimov. China's ambassador to Uzbekistan Yu Jun and Afghanistan's Minister of Industry and Commerce Nooruddin Azizi also attended the ceremony, [the hokimiyat's statement said](https://t.me/axborot_xizmati/58795).

Hokim Kosimov held separate meetings with representatives of Chinese companies on August 16, [the hokimiyat said](https://t.me/axborot_xizmati/58825). Kenfulei discussed setting up pump manufacturing. Anhui Zhuochuang technical school discussed training staff in modern trades on the basis of a local vocational college. The Guangdong Jiangnan agricultural investment group discussed building infrastructure for collecting, storing and exporting fruit and vegetable produce to China.

Officials separately discussed expanding cooperation with "Surxondaryo Huamian Taifeng Technology Industrial Cluster," a company already operating in the region. They also discussed preparing new investment contracts with it, [the hokimiyat's statement said](https://t.me/axborot_xizmati/58825).
