Italiyalik Yannik Sinner, dunyo birinchi raketkasi, 21-avgustda 2026-yilgi US Opendan rasman chetlashdi — o'ng tizza jarohati unga Nyu-Yorkda chiqishga imkon bermadi, [deb xabar berdi](https://www.skysports.com/tennis/news/12040/13575443/jannik-sinner-world-no-1-withdraws-from-us-open-with-knee-injury-and-misses-out-on-final-grand-slam-of-the-season) Sky Sports.

«Biz jamoa va tibbiy shtab bilan ko'p ishladik, ammo qiyin qarorga keldik — bu yil US Openda chiqisha olmayman», — [deyilgan](https://www.tennis365.com/tennis-news/jannik-sinner-speaks-us-open-2026-withdrawal) Sinnerning bayonotida. Tennis365 bayonot matnini 106 so'zdan iborat deb baholaydi. «So'nggi ikki kun Monte-Karloda mashq qildik va o'ng tizzamni tiklash uchun ko'proq vaqt kerakligini angladik», — deb aniqlashtirdi tennischi. U, Nyu-York yuragida alohida o'rin tutishini aytib, «juda xafa va hafsalasi pir bo'lganini» qo'shimcha qildi.

Ayni kuni ilgariroq LEAP News [xabar bergan edi](https://leap.uz/uz/2026/08/21/sinner-us-open-uncertain), Sinnerning turnir oldidan holati noaniqligicha qolganini. Sportchi iyulda Uimbldondagi g'alabasidan beri kortga chiqmagan va xuddi shu jarohat tufayli Monreal va Sinsinnatidagi turnirlarni o'tkazib yuborgan, [deb ta'kidlaydi](https://www.tennismajors.com/us-open-news/breaking-jannik-sinner-withdraws-from-2026-us-open-due-to-knee-injury-858970.html) Tennis Majors.

Sinnerning chetlashishi 2026-yilgi US Opendan voz kechgan sportchilar orasida o'n beshinchi va erkaklar orasida beshinchi holat bo'ldi, [deb hisobladi](https://www.tennis365.com/tennis-news/jannik-sinner-speaks-us-open-2026-withdrawal) Tennis365. Sinner — 2024-yilgi US Open amaldagi chempioni; davolanishdan so'ng u Pekin va Shanxaydagi Osiyo turnirlar seriyasida qaytishni rejalashtirmoqda, [deb xabar beradi](https://www.tennismajors.com/us-open-news/breaking-jannik-sinner-withdraws-from-2026-us-open-due-to-knee-injury-858970.html) Tennis Majors.

## Turnirdan yana kimlar chetlashdi

Sinnerdan tashqari 2026-yilgi US Open asosiy setkasida ispaniyalik Alehandro Davidovich Fokina (dunyo reytingida 27-o'rin), amerikaliklar Itan Kuin (58-o'rin) va Sebastyan Korda (64-o'rin) ham o'ynamaydi. Shuningdek, kanadalik Gabriel Diallo (105-o'rin) va amerikalik Emilio Nava (108-o'rin) ham turnirni o'tkazib yuboradi, [deb xabar beradi](https://www.tennis365.com/tennis-news/jannik-sinner-speaks-us-open-2026-withdrawal) Tennis365. Turnirning asosiy setkasi 30-avgustda boshlanadi.
