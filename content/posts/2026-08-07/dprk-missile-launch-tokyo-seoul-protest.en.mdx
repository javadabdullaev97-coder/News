North Korea fired a short-range ballistic missile from Wonsan toward the East Sea on Thursday, August 6 — Japan's Ministry of Defense lodged a protest, and South Korea's National Security Council called the launch a provocation.

## Reaction From Tokyo and Seoul

South Korea's Joint Chiefs of Staff detected the launch around 5:00 p.m. local time (09:00 GMT), [AP reports](https://abcnews.com/International/wireStory/north-korea-launches-short-range-ballistic-missile-sea-135413600). The missile was launched from the Wonsan area in eastern North Korea and flew toward the East Sea (Sea of Japan).

Japan's Ministry of Defense described the projectile as "a suspected ballistic missile"; it did not strike Japanese territory or waters, AP reports. Japanese Defense Minister Shinjiro Koizumi said "North Korea's series of actions threatens the peace and security" of Japan, the region and the international community, and that Tokyo lodged a strong protest with Pyongyang through its embassy in Beijing, [Jiji Press reports](https://www.nippon.com/en/news/yjj2026080600860/).

South Korea's presidential National Security Council convened an emergency meeting with the Ministry of National Defense and the Joint Chiefs of Staff, called the launch "a provocation that violates UN Security Council resolutions," and demanded an immediate halt to such actions, [Yonhap reports](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/foreignaffairs/northkorea/20260806/n-korea-fires-short-range-ballistic-missile-toward-east-sea-military).

## The June 25 Launch and the "Special Warhead"

The August 6 launch was North Korea's first ballistic missile test since June 25, [Yonhap reports](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/foreignaffairs/northkorea/20260806/n-korea-fires-short-range-ballistic-missile-toward-east-sea-military). On June 25, 2026, North Korean leader Kim Jong Un observed tests in which the military displayed an upgraded 240mm 24-tube multiple rocket launcher, a "special warhead" for a tactical ballistic missile, and an extended-range round for a 155mm self-propelled howitzer, [AP and Reuters report in an Al Jazeera article](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/6/26/north-korea-conducts-major-weapons-tests-south-training-drone-warriors), citing the North Korean state news agency KCNA.

According to KCNA, the new warhead is designed "to deal a deadly blow to the enemy's key targets, including airfields, ports and energy facilities." Kim Jong Un demanded that the military ensure "the enemy constantly feels anxiety and fear" and shift to a "lethal, destructive and offensive combat posture."
