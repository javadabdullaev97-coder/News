The Main Directorate for Emergency Situations (Tashkent Emergency Situations Department) of Tashkent city partially restricted operations at the Kuylyuk farmers' market in Bektemir district on August 5, cutting off power to part of the territory over fire safety violations.

## What inspectors found at the market

A fire safety inspection at Kuylyuk market took place on August 5. Emergency Situations Department specialists [found a number of violations](https://t.me/ToshkentFVBB/18214) of fire safety requirements. According to the department, the deficiencies found could have caused a fire and posed a threat to people's lives, health and property. Following the inspection, part of the market's territory was cut off from the power supply.

The department did not provide a list of specific violations or name the affected rows and pavilions — the post only mentions "cutting off part of the market from the power supply."

## When the market will resume operations

Tashkent's Emergency Situations Department did not name a specific timeline for resuming operations. [According to the department](https://t.me/ToshkentFVBB/18214), power supply will be restored and the market will resume operations once Kuylyuk's management fully addresses the violations found.

The Tashkent Emergency Situations Department is a territorial subdivision of the [Ministry of Emergency Situations of the Republic of Uzbekistan](https://gov.uz/ru/fvv/pages/about) and is responsible for state oversight in the field of fire safety.

## What's next

Tashkent's Emergency Situations Department has not yet reported the results of a follow-up inspection or the exact date the market will fully resume operations.
