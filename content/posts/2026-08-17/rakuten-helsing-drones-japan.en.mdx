Rakuten Group [brokered](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/articles/german-drone-maker-helsing-enlists-082115162.html) German defense startup Helsing's deal with Japan's military — the Ground Self-Defense Force is testing the HX-2 strike drone through the end of September 2026.

Rakuten [confirmed](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/articles/german-drone-maker-helsing-enlists-082115162.html) its role through a company representative — Hideaki Mukai, chief of staff to Rakuten's chief executive. According to him, startups' involvement in Japan's defense industry is "an important opportunity to strengthen the country's defense capabilities." At Helsing, a company representative confirmed the cooperation with Rakuten as a local broker.

Munich-headquartered Helsing was founded in 2021. The company grew amid Russia's invasion of Ukraine, where it supplies drones and software with the backing of the German government.

According to Helsing's own statements, the company's products — including the HX line, which includes the HX-2 — are designed for autonomous target search and recognition without a continuous link to an operator. Details specific to the Japan contract have not been independently confirmed.

## How the Deal Came Together

Helsing and the Japanese government reached a preliminary agreement in early 2026. At the time, negotiations were led by an unnamed "local partner-broker" — now confirmed by both companies to have been Rakuten. According to Nikkei Asia, this is [one of the first](https://asia.nikkei.com/business/aerospace-defense-industries/japan-s-rakuten-to-partner-with-german-ai-defense-drone-startup) partnerships for Helsing in the Asia-Pacific region. Japan's Ground Self-Defense Force did not respond to Reuters' request for comment.

## Rakuten's Defense Pivot

Rakuten had limited experience in the defense sector. But in 2026, the holding company brokered a deal between foreign military-technology companies and Japan's military for the second time. Earlier this year, Rakuten [helped](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/articles/german-drone-maker-helsing-enlists-082115162.html) Ukrainian drone software developer Swarmer showcase its technology to Japan's military.

Field trials of the HX-2 are due to wrap up by the end of September 2026. Neither Rakuten, nor Helsing, nor Japan's defense ministry has announced a timeline for a possible procurement decision.
