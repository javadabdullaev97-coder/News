Joint-stock company Uzbekneftegaz increased net profit 110% year-on-year in the first half of 2026, according to a report on financial and business activity published on July 27.

## First-Half Financial Results

Uzbekneftegaz's commodity output for January–June [totaled](https://teletype.in/@uzbekneftegaz_ung_uz/noKLW_ii_ZB) 25.3 trillion soums (≈$2.1 billion at the Central Bank exchange rate for July 27) — 106% more than a year earlier. Exports reached $438 million, and investment totaled $475 million.

The company said cost optimization delivered savings of 1.2 trillion soums (≈$100 million). Separately, interest payments on loans saved 219 billion soums (≈$18.2 million), owing to a reduction in borrowing rates from 9.5–12% to 5.5–7.5%.

Of the company's 14 oil depots, 11 ended the half-year profitable, with combined profit of 12.3 billion soums (≈$1 million). A year earlier, this group of facilities had recorded a loss of 36 billion soums (≈$3 million).

## Plan to Curb Falling Output at Mubarek

Uzbekneftegaz Chairman Abdugani Sanginov [instructed](https://teletype.in/@uzbekneftegaz_ung_uz/2F5mVjrP-Z4) staff to develop a specific plan of measures to slow the decline in output at wells of the Mubarek Oil and Gas Production Department. The instruction came at a meeting on priority tasks in the company's production and investment activity, where participants discussed, among other things, the results of a first-half report presented to the president on August 3.

Separately, Sanginov set a task to ensure small compressor stations at the Shimoliy Berdak and Sharqiy Berdak fields are fully ready by the end of September. The company describes its overall goal as "stable maintenance and growth of natural gas production volumes" and preventing a decline in output at existing fields.

Other tasks discussed at the meeting included increasing production of jet fuel and diesel fuel, ensuring uninterrupted diesel fuel supply to farms during the cotton harvest season, and introducing digital technologies in the industry.

## Industry-Wide Decline: Gas, Oil and Coal Output Down Nationwide

According to a [press release](https://stat.uz/img/press-relizlar/press-reliz-sanoat-07-2026-rus_p46063.pdf) from the National Statistics Committee of the Republic of Uzbekistan, natural gas production across Uzbekistan fell 16.4% in January–June 2026, to 18.3 billion cubic meters from 21.9 billion a year earlier. Oil production declined 3.1%, to 313,800 tons; gas condensate output fell 19.7%, to 462,100 tons; and coal production dropped 16.7%, to 2.5 million tons.

At the same time, production of a number of refined and other industrial products rose: gasoline output grew 6%, to 614,200 tons; diesel fuel rose 2.1%, to 568,000 tons; portland cement rose 2%, to 10.1 million tons; and electricity generation grew 5.7%, to 44.14 billion kWh. Total industrial output in the country grew 8% for the half-year, to 637.2 trillion soums (≈$53 billion at the Central Bank exchange rate for July 27).

The National Statistics Committee's industry-wide figures cover production by all producers in the country, including private operators and joint ventures, and do not match the scale of Uzbekneftegaz's own figure — 11.5 billion cubic meters of gas for the same half-year, [as LEAP News previously reported](https://leap.uz/posts/2026-08-03/uzbekneftegaz-h1-2026-report), citing the company's report to the president.

## What's Next

Compressor stations at the Shimoliy Berdak and Sharqiy Berdak fields are to be ready for operation by the end of September. The company's published materials do not specify a deadline for approving the plan itself to curb the decline in output at Mubarek.
