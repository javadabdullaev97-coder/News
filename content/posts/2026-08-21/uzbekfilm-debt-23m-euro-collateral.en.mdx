Film studio Uzbekfilm has 22.9 million euros in loan obligations, and its land and property have been pledged to creditors. First Deputy Minister of Culture and Tourism and director of the Cinematography Agency of Uzbekistan Jakhongir Akhmedov said this on August 21 in a [post](https://t.me/uzbekkinopress/30519) that the agency's official Telegram channel reposted.

Besides the loan debt in euros, the studio has a tax debt of more than 1 billion soums (about $84,300 at the Central Bank exchange rate for August 18), Akhmedov said. Because of it, 12 of Uzbekfilm's vehicles could be put up for auction within the next 12 days. Akhmedov also mentioned wage arrears owed to employees without giving an exact figure.

The Cinematography Agency of Uzbekistan oversees Uzbekfilm's operations; the agency itself reports to the Ministry of Culture and Tourism. Akhmedov did not specify in his statement who exactly extended the 22.9-million-euro loan to the studio or when the debt arose.

To remain solvent, Uzbekfilm needs 60 billion soums (about $5.06 million at the Central Bank exchange rate for August 18) in state orders every year, Akhmedov said. According to him, without such support the studio cannot cover its current obligations from its own revenue alone.

The agency's post does not include a comment from Uzbekfilm itself.
