In the night of August 5, an explosive-laden drone was found near a Ukrainian cargo plane at Germany's Leipzig/Halle airport; police called in bomb disposal experts and opened an investigation, and Germany's interior minister went to the scene.

## What Was Found Near the Runway

An airport employee spotted a drone just before midnight near the runway, close to where a Ukrainian An-124 Antonov cargo plane was parked. The drone was carrying an unspecified explosive device, according to [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/5/germany-investigates-explosive-device-attached-to-drone-at-leipzig-airport).

Police deployed a bomb disposal squad with a robot to defuse the device — the detonator was removed from the explosive. A NATO official, [BBC reports](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cyvlg4q48l3o), confirmed that the drone found was fitted with explosives and a detonator.

The northern runway was closed for about two hours and reopened Wednesday morning; the southern runway had been closed for scheduled maintenance unrelated to the investigation. Several flights, including one passenger flight, were [diverted](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/5/germany-investigates-explosive-device-attached-to-drone-at-leipzig-airport) to other airports.

## A Separate Incident Involving a DHL Plane

A DHL cargo plane, which had to abort its landing approach because of the runway closure, collided in mid-air with an unidentified object. The aircraft sustained minor damage to its nose and made a safe emergency landing at Hanover airport; no one was injured. A link between this object and the drone found on the airfield [has not yet been established](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/5/germany-investigates-explosive-device-attached-to-drone-at-leipzig-airport).

## Saxony Prosecutors Are Leading the Investigation

The case is being handled by prosecutors in the state of Saxony who deal with politically motivated and extremist crimes. No suspects have been identified so far, and no one has been held responsible. Published photos show police technicians working near the Ukrainian An-124 Antonov cargo plane, but authorities have not said the aircraft was the target of an attack, [Al Jazeera notes](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/5/germany-investigates-explosive-device-attached-to-drone-at-leipzig-airport).

Germany's government confirmed the discovery of the drone. Interior Minister Alexander Dobrindt, [according to BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cyvlg4q48l3o), cut short his vacation and traveled to the airport to meet with security officials, including Saxony's state interior minister, Armin Schuster.

## Germany and Ukraine Point to Russia

Germany — one of Ukraine's largest military donors — has previously said it suspects Russia of orchestrating a series of similar incidents at sensitive sites as part of a campaign of sabotage, espionage and disinformation against European countries, [Le Monde writes](https://www.lemonde.fr/en/international/article/2026/08/05/german-airport-on-alert-after-explosive-drone-found-near-ukrainian-plane-and-mid-air-collision_6756188_4.html). Authorities have not yet presented evidence tying Moscow specifically to the Leipzig incident.

Ukraine's ambassador to Germany, Oleksii Makeiev, said on the Welt television channel that he suspects Russia: "Who else could it be but Russia?" [BBC reports](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cyvlg4q48l3o). There has been no official response from Moscow to the incident as of the morning of August 6.
