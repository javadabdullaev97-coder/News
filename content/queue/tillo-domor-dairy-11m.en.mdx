President Shavkat Mirziyoyev on August 17 [visited](https://t.me/shmirziyoyev/35064) a new production complex of the Tillo Do'mor enterprise in the Urgench district of Khorezm region — an $11 million dairy plant that has created 120 jobs.

The plant's annual production capacity is 100 billion soums (about $8.4 million at the Central Bank's exchange rate for August 17, 2026 — 11,891.18 soums per dollar), the state news agency UzA [reported](https://uza.uz/ru/posts/prezident-oznakomilsya-s-deyatelnostyu-predpriyatiya-po-proizvodstvu-molochnoy-produkcii_895804). The plant purchased its technological equipment in Bulgaria.

The president [reviewed](https://t.me/shmirziyoyev/35071) the plant's products — cheese, cream, sour cream, cottage cheese, qatiq (a fermented milk drink) and kefir. In total, the plant produces more than 70 types of dairy products. More than 100 farms and local households supply raw materials for processing daily.

The plant has created 120 jobs, and 100 kilowatts of solar panels have additionally been installed. According to UzA, the plant plans to create another 100 jobs and expand its product range.

The head of state emphasized the need to increase production of high value-added goods through deeper processing of local raw materials. He instructed responsible officials to support entrepreneurial initiatives in this area.

About 1,200 enterprises processing agricultural products operate in Khorezm region.
