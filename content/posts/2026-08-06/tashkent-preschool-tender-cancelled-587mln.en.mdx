The Committee for Development of Competition and Protection of Consumer Rights of the Republic of Uzbekistan on August 4 [announced](https://raqobat.gov.uz/raqobat-qomitasi-sorovi-bilan-maktabgacha-va-maktab-talim-vazirligining-boshlangich-narhi-5872-mln-somlik-tender-savdosi-bekor-qilindi/) the cancellation of a tender by the Ministry of Preschool and School Education worth 587.2 million soums. The amount is equivalent to approximately $49,000 at the Central Bank's exchange rate on August 4, and the cancellation was due to a violation of the law "On Competition".

## What happened

The Ministry of Preschool and School Education announced the tender for centralized event-organizing services on July 27, with a starting price of 587.2 million soums. The trigger for the Competition Committee's review was a critical social media post titled "One Address, One Phone Number — One Tender," [the agency said in its statement](https://raqobat.gov.uz/raqobat-qomitasi-sorovi-bilan-maktabgacha-va-maktab-talim-vazirligining-boshlangich-narhi-5872-mln-somlik-tender-savdosi-bekor-qilindi/).

Following the review, the Committee found a violation of Article 29 of the law "On Competition" — actions that restricted or could have restricted competition among tender participants. The agency did not disclose what specifically these actions involved.

The Committee sent the ministry a request to comply with competition law requirements. Following this request, the ministry cancelled the tender results in accordance with established procedure, [the Committee said](https://t.me/RaqobatGovUz/12535) on its Telegram channel.

## The Committee's position

The Committee called the tender's cancellation a measure that ensures efficient and transparent spending of public funds and creates equal conditions for businesses. The agency did not name the companies that had submitted bids for the tender and did not say whether a new tender for the same services would be announced.

## What's next

The Competition Committee did not specify a deadline by which the ministry must announce a new procurement for event-organizing services, should the need for one remain.
