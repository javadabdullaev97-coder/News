U.S. President Donald Trump signed an executive order imposing tariffs of up to 100% on imported drones and their components, [the White House said](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2026/08/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-bolsters-national-security-and-strengthens-u-s-supply-chains-by-imposing-tariffs-on-drones-and-their-parts-and-components/) on August 13, 2026.

## Rates by category and country

The maximum 100% rate will apply to drones that Washington classifies as sensitive to national security. These are aircraft with a takeoff weight of more than 25 kilograms or equipped with thermal imaging cameras, as well as their docking stations and critical components, [the White House document says](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2026/08/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-bolsters-national-security-and-strengthens-u-s-supply-chains-by-imposing-tariffs-on-drones-and-their-parts-and-components/). Smaller models and other components face a 25% tariff.

Preferential rates apply to U.S. allies. Drones and components from the European Union, Japan, South Korea, Switzerland, Liechtenstein and Taiwan face a 15% tariff. For British manufacturers, the rate is cut to 10% — [Euronews confirms these figures](http://ru.euronews.com/business/2026/08/14/trump-imposes-drone-tariffs-of-up-to-100-with-a-lower-rate-for-the-eu-and-uk). The preferential rate applies only if the hardware, software and technology were produced predominantly in these countries or in the United States. Otherwise, the shipment automatically falls under the 25–100% rate.

## Reasons and legal basis

The tariffs were imposed under Section 232 of the U.S. Trade Expansion Act of 1962, a provision that allows the president to impose trade restrictions on national security grounds, [Euronews notes](http://ru.euronews.com/business/2026/08/14/trump-imposes-drone-tariffs-of-up-to-100-with-a-lower-rate-for-the-eu-and-uk). The White House attributed the decision to American industry's dependence on foreign drone suppliers — technology that, in the administration's wording, has changed the nature of modern warfare.

Most tariffs take effect 21 days after the executive order was signed on August 13. For components the authorities don't classify as especially sensitive, the delay will be 180 days, [the White House said](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2026/08/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-bolsters-national-security-and-strengthens-u-s-supply-chains-by-imposing-tariffs-on-drones-and-their-parts-and-components/). The U.S. Department of Commerce has been instructed to develop a program to support investment in companies that produce drones and components domestically.

## Context: trade standoff with China

China remains the main target of the tariffs — one of the world's largest producers of civilian drones and their components. [Bloomberg notes](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-13/trump-imposes-100-tariffs-on-certain-drones-countering-china) that the new tariffs could accelerate the technological decoupling of drone supply chains between the U.S. and China. This could strain relations between the two countries ahead of Chinese President Xi Jinping's visit to the U.S., planned for September.

On August 5, before the tariff order was signed, China's Ministry of Commerce tightened export controls on drones, their components and related technologies destined for the United States, [Bloomberg notes](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-13/trump-imposes-100-tariffs-on-certain-drones-countering-china). The restrictions were part of a package of trade countermeasures that also included sanctions against several American companies. Beijing had not issued an official response specifically to the August 13 tariffs as of publication.

## What's next

Tariffs on sensitive drone categories and their components are set to take effect in early September — 21 days after the order was signed. The Department of War has 20 days to approve a list of components eligible for exclusions from the Federal Communications Commission's Covered List (FCC Covered List). For those, the delay will be 180 days, [the White House document says](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2026/08/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-bolsters-national-security-and-strengthens-u-s-supply-chains-by-imposing-tariffs-on-drones-and-their-parts-and-components/).
