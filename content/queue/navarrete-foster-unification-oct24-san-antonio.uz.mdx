Meksikalik Emanuel Navarrete va amerikalik O'Shaquie Foster 24-oktabrda Texas shtatining San-Antonio shahridagi Alamodome arenasida birinchi yengil vazn (58,97 kg gacha) toifasida birlashtiruvchi jangda uchrashadi, deb xabar beradi [BoxingScene](https://www.boxingscene.com/articles/emanuel-navarrete-oshaquie-foster-unification-fight-set-for-october-24-in-san-antonio).

31 yoshli Navarrete (40 g'alaba, 2 mag'lubiyat, 1 durang, 33 nokaut) jangga Jahon boks tashkiloti (WBO) va Xalqaro boks federatsiyasi (IBF) kamarlarini olib kiradi. U ikkala unvonni fevral oyida Eduardo Nunesni 11-raundda to'xtatib birlashtirgan edi. 32 yoshli Foster (25 g'alaba, 3 mag'lubiyat, 12 nokaut) Jahon boks kengashi (WBC) unvonini himoya qiladi — mayda u Raymond Fordni ochkolar hisobida mag'lub etgan edi. G'olib San-Antoniodan divizionning to'rtta asosiy kamaridan uchtasini olib ketadi, deb xabar beradi [BoxingScene](https://www.boxingscene.com/articles/emanuel-navarrete-oshaquie-foster-unification-fight-set-for-october-24-in-san-antonio). Jangni DAZN namoyish etadi, ikkala bokschini Top Rank promouter kompaniyasi ilgari suradi.

Muzokaralar bir necha oy davom etdi: jang bo'yicha kelishuvga avgust oyining o'rtalarida erishildi, deb xabar beradi [BoxingScene](https://www.boxingscene.com/articles/emanuel-navarrete-oshaquie-foster-unification-fight-set-for-october-24-in-san-antonio).

## WBO kamari atrofidagi savol

Martda [WBO](https://wboboxing.com/wbo-official-documents/wbo-orders-negotiations-for-mandatory-jr-lightweight-championship-navarrete-vs-suarez/) Navarretedan da'vogar Charli Suares bilan majburiy himoya bo'yicha muzokara olib borishni talab qilgan edi. Agar meksikalik boksschi San-Antonioda Suares o'rniga Foster bilan chiqsa, tashkilot undan WBO kamaridan voz kechishni talab qilishi mumkin. Jang e'lon qilingan vaqtda bu talabning holati aniqlanmagan.
