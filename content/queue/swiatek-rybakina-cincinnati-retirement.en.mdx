Iga Swiatek commented on Elena Rybakina's retirement from the Cincinnati Open quarterfinal on August 20. The Kazakhstani player withdrew from the match trailing 4-1 in the first set due to a left ankle injury.

Swiatek was leading 4-1 in the first set when Rybakina took a medical timeout and subsequently retired from the match, [the WTA reported](https://www.wtatennis.com/news/4563969/swiatek-reaches-cincinnati-semifinals-as-rybakina-succumbs-to-left-ankle-injury). The tournament did not disclose an official diagnosis.

According to Rybakina, the ankle problem had been bothering her for a while: "It's been bothering me for quite a long time. I was managing it before, but after a run of matches in Canada and two matches in a row here, it got worse," [the WTA quoted her as saying](https://www.wtatennis.com/news/4563969/swiatek-reaches-cincinnati-semifinals-as-rybakina-succumbs-to-left-ankle-injury). The player added: "One wrong movement, one landing, and something happened."

Commenting on her opponent's withdrawal, Swiatek said: "I was worried just watching the situation. I hope Elena will be fine. Health is a priority, the next tournament is a priority, US Open is US Open, and I hope she'll be ready. She's really close to becoming world No. 1. It's a shame it happened this way," [Swiatek said](https://www.tennismajors.com/wta-tour-news/swiatek-into-cincinnati-semi-finals-as-injured-rybakina-retires-858950.html).

Rybakina holds the second spot in the [current WTA rankings](https://www.wtatennis.com/rankings/singles). A win over Swiatek and a place in the Cincinnati Open final would have brought the Kazakhstani player close to the top ranking spot, Tennis Majors notes.

Swiatek will face American Jessica Pegula in the Cincinnati Open semifinal, [the WTA reported](https://www.tennismajors.com/wta-tour-news/swiatek-into-cincinnati-semi-finals-as-injured-rybakina-retires-858950.html).
