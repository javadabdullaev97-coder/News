The Unified Portal of Interactive Public Services my.gov.uz has opened applications for testing in five foreign languages — [registration](https://t.me/MyGovUz/3971) runs through August 12, and the exams will be held August 24–25, 2026.

## Which Languages and When the Tests Will Be Held

Testing covers five languages — English, German, French, Turkish, and Arabic, [according to a post](https://t.me/MyGovUz/3971) on the my.gov.uz Telegram channel. The organizer is the Agency for Assessment of Knowledge and Qualifications under the [Ministry of Higher Education, Science and Innovation](https://gov.uz/ru/uzbmb/pages/about) of the Republic of Uzbekistan, the successor to the State Testing Center, which had operated in the country since 1992.

The exams themselves will be held on August 24–25. Registration of applications closes on August 12, and payment must be completed by August 13.

## Cost and How to Register

The "Foreign Language Proficiency Assessment" service on the my.gov.uz portal [costs](https://my.gov.uz/ru/service/559) 556,200 soums (1.35 times the base calculation value) — about $47 at the [Central Bank of Uzbekistan](https://cbu.uz/ru/arkhiv-kursov-valyut/) exchange rate for August 6 (11,886.72 soums per dollar). Payment is made within the portal.

To submit an application, users need to log in to my.gov.uz (via OneID or an existing Unified Portal of Interactive Public Services account) and file a request in the service section — registration or logging into a personal account is mandatory, and no separate documents need to be attached in advance. The my.gov.uz Telegram channel separately [asks](https://t.me/MyGovUz/3971) applicants to enter their personal data completely and without errors.

The service on the portal has [already been used](https://my.gov.uz/ru/service/559) more than 2.5 million times — counting repeat requests since the service launched.

## What's Next

After the application period ends on August 12 and payment is completed by August 13, participants will take the test on August 24–25. The Agency for Assessment of Knowledge and Qualifications and my.gov.uz have not yet published the dates or procedure for announcing results — updates can be followed on the [portal's Telegram channel](https://t.me/MyGovUz).
