A working group of JSC Uzbekneftegaz inspected polymer production units at Shurtan Gas Chemical Complex in Kashkadarya region on August 8 and proposed measures to develop infrastructure, the complex's press service [said](https://teletype.in/@uzbekneftegaz_ung_uz/H_RtJwQEHs4).

## What the working group inspected

The working group was headed by the first deputy chairman of the management board for finance, transformation and privatization. The group traveled to the complex on the instruction of Uzbekneftegaz's board chairman, [the publication said](https://teletype.in/@uzbekneftegaz_ung_uz/H_RtJwQEHs4) on the company's official blog.

The working group first inspected the Karshitermoplast unit, where it [reviewed](https://teletype.in/@uzbekneftegaz_ung_uz/H_RtJwQEHs4) the organization of production processes, the quality of polymer products and the utilization level of production capacity. Following the inspection, the working group discussed tasks for using capacity more efficiently, improving product quality and increasing output volumes.

The second site was the joint venture NASAF XCP TECHNO, established with the Chinese company Yunnan Xinchengpeng Polymer Technology — [the working group reviewed](https://teletype.in/@uzbekneftegaz_ung_uz/H_RtJwQEHs4) the operations of this production facility.

## Infrastructure: health center and hotel

The group paid special attention to the Geolog health center, where it [inspected](https://teletype.in/@uzbekneftegaz_ung_uz/H_RtJwQEHs4) existing infrastructure facilities and the current condition of buildings and structures.

The group also discussed the renovation of a hotel operated by the joint venture Gissarneftegaz and the modernization of its infrastructure to meet current requirements. Gissarneftegaz is an Uzbek-Swiss joint venture registered in 2005. It develops hard-to-recover oil and gas fields in Kashkadarya region, including Severny Nishan, Kamashi and Beshkent, [according to](https://gissarneftgaz.uz/en/о-компании/) data on its official website.

## What is known about the joint venture NASAF XCP TECHNO

The joint venture NASAF XCP TECHNO was registered on February 17, 2025, in Karshi, [according to](https://orginfo.uz/organization/f7c291269ff5/) the company's profile in the register of legal entities. Its founders are the Chinese company Yunnan Xinchengpeng Polymer Technology (60% stake), Shurtan Gas Chemical Complex (20%) and Techno Cable Group (20%). The company's charter capital is 27.89 billion soums (≈$2.3 million at the Central Bank of Uzbekistan's exchange rate on August 7 — 11,915.64 soums per dollar). The company is registered under the activity "manufacture of other types of electric wire and cable," and its head is Elamon Gapporovich Nazarov.

The publication by Shurtan Gas Chemical Complex's press service does not provide specific figures on production plan fulfillment and does not specify deadlines for implementing these recommendations.
