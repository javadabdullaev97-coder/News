The Ministry of Higher Education, Science and Innovations of the Republic of Uzbekistan opened applications from students for dormitory places via the my.gov.uz portal on August 1 — dormitory placement and temporary registration are now processed as a single service.

## How to apply

The ministry accepts applications through the [Single Portal of Interactive Public Services my.gov.uz](https://my.gov.uz/ru/service/870); the service is called "Applying for Placement in a Student Dormitory." It is free, and no documents need to be attached. The portal's trust-line phone number is 1006, and the call center is +998 (71) 230-64-64; the service page also lists the number 1242.

The admission procedure is established by regulations approved by [Cabinet of Ministers Resolution No. 376](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/-6568679) dated August 14, 2023. The document is in effect as amended on February 2, 2026: it was amended by Resolution No. 272 dated May 8, 2024, Resolution No. 32 dated January 28, 2026, and Resolution No. 35 dated January 29, 2026. Under the regulations, applications are accepted from August 1 through the end of the academic year.

After the application is reviewed, the student receives an electronic contract and a placement referral. Paying the amount specified in the contract [triggers the composite service](https://t.me/MyGovUz/3959) — after that, temporary registration is processed automatically.

## What changed

Previously, dormitory placement and registration at the place of stay were two separate procedures: after moving into a dormitory, a student had to gather documents and apply to other agencies. Now both procedures have been combined into a composite service, [the my.gov.uz portal reported](https://t.me/MyGovUz/3959).

After paying the registration fee, the student is issued a registration slip confirming registration at the place of stay. The temporary registration period runs from the date of payment and remains valid through the date specified in the housing contract.

Registration at the place of stay itself is provided by the Ministry of Internal Affairs of the Republic of Uzbekistan (MIA). Neither the ministry nor the portal disclosed the amount of the payment within the composite service. The standalone "Registration at Place of Stay (Temporary)" service on the portal [costs 7,416 soums](https://my.gov.uz/ru/service/187) (0.018 of the base calculation value); the processing time is one business day, and the result is a barcode-protected registration slip. The legal basis is the Law "On the Registration of Citizens of the Republic of Uzbekistan, Foreign Citizens, and Stateless Persons at Place of Residence and Place of Stay" dated July 10, 2025, No. ZRU-1074.

## Who gets housing first

The regulations divide applicants into a preferential list and a general list. Places are first allocated among students with benefits and priority status, [the ministry reported](https://t.me/eduuz/49951).

The regulations classify as the preferential category: residents of "Mehribonlik" homes, orphans and students left without parental care; children from families included in the social register and in the "Temir Daftar," "Ayollar Daftari," and "Yoshlar Daftari" registers; students with category I and II disabilities; foreign-national students; and children from families with two or more members enrolled full-time in bachelor's programs on a paid-contract basis.

A separate list is housed by order of the university rector: winners of the ministry's national subject olympiads; winners of the national stage of the "Zakovat" quiz game, the "Student of the Year" competition, and student theater-studio competitions; chairs of dormitory student councils and floor monitors.

## How the remaining places are divided

Places remaining after preferential-category students are distributed among general-list students on a first-come, first-served basis. The ministry [gave the following proportions](https://t.me/eduuz/49951): first-year students receive 55–65% of places, second-year students 15–20%, and third-year students and above up to 20–25%.

## Who the regulations do not cover

Clause 1 of the regulations sets the procedure for housing students of **state** higher education institutions. Clause 2 excludes from its scope dormitories of foreign, non-state, and military higher education institutions. It also does not apply to those dormitory places built under public-private partnership terms that are allocated to the private partner for commercial purposes. As a result, the regulations do not govern dormitory placement at branches of foreign universities or private universities.

## What's next

Applications will be accepted through the end of the academic year. Records are kept in a dedicated information system maintained by the ministry: it is updated annually by August 1, while data on available places is updated as places become free.

Neither the ministry nor the portal specified in their July 31 and August 1 announcements from what point applicants recommended for first-year admission for the 2026/2027 academic year can submit applications.
