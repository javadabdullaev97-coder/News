U.S. special envoy Jared Kushner held a rare meeting in Cairo on August 16 with Hamas chief Khalil al-Hayya on the 15-point road map for Gaza, [PBS NewsHour reports](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/kushner-has-rare-meeting-with-hamas-leader-on-gaza-road-map-a-meeting-with-netanyahu-is-next).

At the Cairo meeting, Kushner was one of three U.S. representatives — PBS NewsHour does not name the other two. Officials from Egypt, Qatar and Turkey, the countries mediating between Israel and Hamas, also attended, [PBS NewsHour reports](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/kushner-has-rare-meeting-with-hamas-leader-on-gaza-road-map-a-meeting-with-netanyahu-is-next).

The 15-point road map calls for the phased disarmament of Hamas and a withdrawal of Israeli troops from the Gaza Strip. "The group is committed to the plan and is waiting for mediators to start a 14-day negotiation period," a Hamas representative said, [PBS NewsHour reports](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/kushner-has-rare-meeting-with-hamas-leader-on-gaza-road-map-a-meeting-with-netanyahu-is-next).

A meeting with Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu is set for Monday, August 17. Kushner, Blair and Nikolai Mladenov, head of the Peace Council for the Gaza ceasefire, will take part in the talks, [PBS NewsHour reports](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/kushner-has-rare-meeting-with-hamas-leader-on-gaza-road-map-a-meeting-with-netanyahu-is-next).

Netanyahu rejected the plan on August 9: Israel will not withdraw its troops until Hamas is fully disarmed. The prime minister also ruled out the creation of a Palestinian state, [Kommersant writes](https://www.kommersant.ru/doc/8890852), citing The Jerusalem Post. On August 10, Israel's Channel 13 reported that the United States had demanded Israel end its military operations in Iran, Lebanon and the Gaza Strip. The demand was conveyed by CENTCOM commander Brad Cooper, according to the channel, [Kommersant writes](https://www.kommersant.ru/doc/8890852).

## Eight countries accuse Israel of derailing the plan

The foreign ministers of the UAE, Qatar, Jordan, Indonesia, Pakistan, Turkey, Saudi Arabia and Egypt issued a joint statement on August 16. In it, the ministers condemned Israel's refusal to implement the road map, [the statement on the UAE Foreign Ministry's website says](https://www.mofa.gov.ae/en/mediahub/news/2026/8/16/uae-egypt).

"Israel now bears responsibility for obstructing efforts to achieve peace in Gaza," the joint statement says, [Kommersant writes](https://www.kommersant.ru/doc/8890852), citing The Jerusalem Post.

The ministers also called on the United States to press Israel to fully comply with the terms of the plan. Separately, they insisted on the creation of an independent Palestinian state within the 1967 borders with its capital in East Jerusalem, [the statement on the UAE Foreign Ministry's website says](https://www.mofa.gov.ae/en/mediahub/news/2026/8/16/uae-egypt).

Talks on a Gaza ceasefire had stalled for more than ten months before the current round of meetings, [PBS NewsHour notes](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/kushner-has-rare-meeting-with-hamas-leader-on-gaza-road-map-a-meeting-with-netanyahu-is-next).
