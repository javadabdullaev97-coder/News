Surxondaryo Suv Ta'minoti JSC has been gradually restoring drinking water supply in eight mahallas of Termez since 2 p.m. on August 5 — the outage was caused by repairs to a 630-millimeter main water pipeline.

Residents of eight mahallas in Termez — Do'stlik, Saxovat, Navro'z, Eski Shahar, Majnuntol, Ma'rifat, A. Jomiy and Farhod — [were left](https://t.me/uzsuvsurxondaryo/17038) without centralized water supply. The cause was repair work on the 630 mm main pipeline that supplies these districts of the city.

The company did not specify what necessitated the repair — a technical fault or scheduled maintenance.

The company [said](https://t.me/uzsuvsurxondaryo/17038) that, due to ongoing repair work, water supply is being restored later than initially expected. Water supply in the listed mahallas began being restored in stages from 2 p.m. on August 5.

The national operator O'zsuvta'minot [reposted](https://t.me/uzsuv/45361) the same message on the morning of August 5, confirming the list of mahallas, the pipeline diameter and the time restoration would begin.

The company apologized to consumers for the inconvenience and promised to inform residents again once water supply is fully restored. The operator did not specify the exact time repairs would be completed or the number of affected households.
