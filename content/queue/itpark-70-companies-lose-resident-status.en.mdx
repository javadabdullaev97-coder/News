IT Park Uzbekistan stripped 70 companies of resident status after they failed to submit activity reports for the second quarter of 2026, the organization [said](https://t.me/itpark_uz/20575) on its Telegram channel in early August.

## Why companies lost their status

"IT Park resident status is not only about benefits — it also comes with an obligation to meet established requirements. One of them is submitting a quarterly activity report on time," IT Park [said](https://t.me/itpark_uz/20575) in the post.

Following a review conducted after the end of the second quarter of 2026, the organization identified companies that had not filed their reports on time. It revoked resident status from 70 of them. Timely and complete reporting is one of the main conditions for keeping resident status, along with operating within the approved list of business activities and undergoing an annual audit, IT Park [explains](https://www.it-park.uz/ru/itpark/news/kak-it-park-lishaet-kompaniy-rezidentstva) in a separate note on its website.

## What companies lose

Once stripped of resident status, companies lose the entire package of IT Park tax incentives. According to the organization's [data](https://www.it-park.uz/ru/itpark/news/kak-it-park-lishaet-kompaniy-rezidentstva), residents are exempt from corporate profit tax and social tax — the latter stands at 12% for non-residents. Residents also pay a reduced personal income tax rate of 7.5%, compared with 12% for other companies, and are exempt from VAT on imported services. IT Park forwards information on excluded companies to the tax authorities, which reinstate their obligations to pay these taxes to the state budget.

IT Park does not publish a separate public list of the 70 excluded companies — information on violators is sent only to the tax authorities.

## Can the status be reinstated

Resident status can be reinstated if a company corrects the violation, IT Park [says](https://www.it-park.uz/ru/itpark/news/kak-it-park-lishaet-kompaniy-rezidentstva). The organization does not detail the mechanism or deadlines for resubmitting documents on its website.

In its post about the 70 companies, IT Park recommends that residents regularly check notifications in their personal accounts and track reporting deadlines in advance. Data must be submitted in full and on time to avoid losing the status.

## Not the first case this year

Excluding companies from the resident register over reporting violations happens regularly. In July 2026, IT Park [stripped](https://www.it-park.uz/uz/itpark/news/iyul-oyida-152-ta-kompaniya-it-park-uzbekistan-rezidenti-maqomidan-mahrum-bo-ldi) 152 companies of resident status, mainly over late submission of 2025 audit reports. According to the same source, 768 companies have lost resident status since the start of 2026. Besides missed reporting deadlines, the reasons include overdue mandatory payments, inactivity for more than six months, discrepancies between actual operations and the approved business plan, and bankruptcy.
