Israel on Wednesday, August 5, launched a series of strikes on southern Lebanon and ordered the evacuation of the Lebanese village of Al-Mansouri, accusing Hezbollah of violating the ceasefire after two Israeli soldiers were killed.

## What happened in southern Lebanon

Israeli aircraft struck the roof of a prayer hall at a cemetery in the town of Tebnin in southern Lebanon. According to Lebanon's health ministry, cited by [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/aug/05/israel-strikes-southern-lebanon-deadliest-ceasefire), one person was killed and at least 11 were injured; [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/5/at-least-one-lebanese-person-two-israeli-soldiers-killed-in-south-lebanon), citing the same ministry, puts the number of injured at 12. The Guardian called the strike one of the deadliest since the ceasefire took effect in June.

At the same time, but in a separate incident, in the village of Majdal Selim — in a buffer zone in southern Lebanon held by Israeli troops — two Israeli soldiers were killed and one more was severely wounded, with several others suffering lesser injuries, when an improvised device exploded in a booby-trapped building, [Al Jazeera reported](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/5/at-least-one-lebanese-person-two-israeli-soldiers-killed-in-south-lebanon). Al Jazeera correspondent Zeina Khodr said no Hezbollah presence had been recorded in Majdal Selim and described the situation in the area as highly unstable.

According to the [Financial Times](https://www.ft.com/content/45df9a1f-7400-4ef8-85e5-aa519a627e66), the Israeli military ordered residents of Al-Mansouri to immediately leave their homes and move at least 1,000 meters north into open ground; the paper called it the first such order in Lebanon in the past month.

## What the sides are saying

The Israeli military described the strikes as a "targeted" response to a "flagrant violation of the ceasefire by the terrorist organization Hezbollah," [Al Jazeera quoted](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/5/at-least-one-lebanese-person-two-israeli-soldiers-killed-in-south-lebanon) the military as saying.

Hezbollah Secretary-General Naim Qassem reiterated that direct talks with Israel would bring Lebanon "shame, humiliation and constant concessions," [Al Jazeera reported](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/5/at-least-one-lebanese-person-two-israeli-soldiers-killed-in-south-lebanon) — the group continues to refuse direct talks on surrendering its weapons.

## Deadlock in the Rome talks

The strikes came on the second day of a resumed round of Lebanon-Israel talks in Rome — the seventh since the start of the US-mediated process, [LEAP News reports](https://leap.uz/posts/2026-08-05/lebanon-israel-rome-talks-round7-2026). The sides are discussing implementation of the framework agreement signed on June 26 in Washington — the withdrawal of Israeli troops from southern Lebanon and the disarmament of Hezbollah.

According to [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/5/lebanon-and-israel-still-split-over-disarming-hezbollah-at-rome-talks), the Israeli delegation for the first time backed off its demand to discuss Hezbollah's disarmament before the border issue — but the sides remain divided over the timeline and verification procedure for the withdrawal of Israeli troops. A separate sticking point remains the Ali-Taher hill near Nabatieh: Israel is demanding that the Lebanese army take control of it and dismantle Hezbollah infrastructure there, while Beirut argues there are no Israeli units on the hill that would need to be replaced.

Lebanon again proposed including the Khiam or Bint Jbeil area in the next troop-withdrawal "pilot zone," the outlet noted. In the assessment of Al Jazeera's correspondent, the current round of talks is unlikely to lead to a further withdrawal of Israeli troops from southern Lebanon. No date has yet been announced for the next round.

## Context

The Israeli military last ordered an evacuation in southern Lebanon more than a month ago, before the latest wave of strikes, the [Financial Times reports](https://www.ft.com/content/45df9a1f-7400-4ef8-85e5-aa519a627e66). The Rome talks, running from August 4 to 6, are a continuation of the process launched by talks in April, while the ceasefire between the sides was reached in early June and formalized by the June framework agreement, [LEAP News notes](https://leap.uz/posts/2026-08-05/lebanon-israel-rome-talks-round7-2026).
