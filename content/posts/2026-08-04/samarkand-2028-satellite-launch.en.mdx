The Uzcosmos agency and China's STAR.VISION will [launch](https://t.me/uzcosmos_official/2663) the hyperspectral satellite Samarkand-2028 into orbit on August 5, together with the Lampung-1 satellite, from a sea launch platform off the coast of China's Shandong province.

## Launch Details

The agency has not yet specified the exact launch time within the day on August 5. According to the agency, the carrier rocket is already aboard the launch vessel heading to the launch point off Shandong province. The launch itself is being carried out by STAR.VISION, the Chinese company that develops hyperspectral satellites with onboard AI modules together with Uzcosmos. Indonesia's Lampung-1 will reach orbit alongside the Uzbek satellite.

## AI Module Built by Uzbek Developers

Samarkand-2028 carries an artificial intelligence module [developed](https://t.me/uzcosmos_official/2663) by Uzcosmos specialists. The module will process hyperspectral data directly in orbit, speeding up results and reducing the volume of information that needs to be transmitted to Earth for further processing.

According to [the Ministry of Digital Technologies of Uzbekistan](https://t.me/mitcuz/23916), onboard data processing will help produce more accurate information faster about the state of agricultural land, water resources and the environment.

## Memorandum With STAR.VISION

The project resulted from a strategic memorandum that Uzcosmos and STAR.VISION [signed](https://gov.uz/ru/uzspace/news/view/104736) on November 24, 2025. The document covers joint projects in satellite technology and strengthening scientific and technical cooperation. The agreement also envisages involving universities and technology companies from both countries, including using hyperspectral data to create national maps of cotton and wheat fields.

Under the same agreement, STAR.VISION backed Samarkand's bid to host the International Astronautical Congress (IAC) in 2028 — the company named the satellite Samarkand-2028 in honor of that decision.

## What's Next

The Ministry of Digital Technologies independently [confirmed](https://t.me/mitcuz/23916) the launch date. Uzcosmos plans to announce the exact launch time and confirm the satellite has reached orbit separately after the mission is complete.
