A Uzbekistan-India business forum was held in New Delhi on August 4, 2026: the two sides agreed to raise bilateral trade turnover from $1.3 billion to $2 billion, the state portal gov.uz [reported](https://gov.uz/ru/news/view/201784).

## Forum agreements

According to [gov.uz](https://gov.uz/ru/news/view/201784), trade turnover between the two countries grew 30% last year and exceeded $1.3 billion for the first time. The new target set by the two sides is to raise the figure to $2 billion. A Memorandum of Understanding on trade facilitation is being prepared to that end.

About 400 enterprises with Indian capital operate in Uzbekistan, and the portfolio of joint projects exceeds $5 billion, according to the [report](https://gov.uz/ru/news/view/201784) on the government portal.

The two sides named metallurgy and mining, chemicals and pharmaceuticals, energy and renewables, digital infrastructure, and mechanical engineering and agro-industry as priority areas for cooperation.

## Who attended

The Uzbek delegation at the forum was headed by Laziz Kudratov, Minister of Investments, Industry and Trade of the Republic of Uzbekistan. Piyush Goyal, Minister of Commerce and Industry of India, took part on the Indian side. More than a hundred representatives of business circles from both countries attended the event, along with the Federation of Indian Chambers of Commerce and Industry (FICCI) and the state agency Invest India, gov.uz [reports](https://gov.uz/ru/news/view/201784).

## In parallel: meeting with India's president

The business forum took place as part of a four-day visit to India by Uzbekistan Foreign Minister Bakhtiyor Saidov. A day earlier, on August 3, Saidov met with Indian President Droupadi Murmu at her Rashtrapati Bhavan residence. This was [reported](https://newsonair.gov.in/india-uzbekistan-agree-to-expand-strategic-partnership/) by India's state service Akashvani/DD News, citing the President's Secretariat of India.

At the meeting, Murmu noted the potential for cooperation in rare earth metals mining, agriculture, pharmaceuticals and IT. According to Akashvani/DD News, more than 3,000 Uzbek officials, specialists and students have trained under India's Indian Technical and Economic Cooperation (ITEC) program. In addition, more than 16,000 Indian students are studying at universities in Uzbekistan.

Saidov also held talks with Foreign Minister of India Subrahmanyam Jaishankar, and the two sides discussed cooperation in energy, infrastructure, trade, defense, education and culture. India thanked Uzbekistan for supporting its 2026 BRICS chairmanship. India also voiced support for Uzbekistan's candidacy for chairmanship of the Non-Aligned Movement for 2027-2029, Akashvani/DD News notes.
