The Cabinet of Ministers, by Resolution No. 418 of July 30, 2026, which took effect on July 31, introduced a cash subsidy for coal for families in the Social Registry who complete their purchase through the [mycoal.uz](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/8366663) portal.

## How much families get and how the amount is calculated

The subsidy applies in 2026-2027. For every ton of coal a Social Registry family buys within a calendar year, [the state will reimburse 300,000 soums (about $25 at the Central Bank's exchange rate as of July 31), but no more than 600,000 soums (about $50) per family a year](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/8366663). The subsidy cap is calculated for a purchase of up to two tons of coal.

The only condition for receiving the payment is that the family buy coal through the interactive mycoal.uz portal or its mobile app and confirm the purchase in the system.

## How to complete a purchase through mycoal.uz

The mycoal.uz portal and its mobile app [handle registration of residents, publish coal suppliers' offers with delivery addresses and timelines, and let buyers choose between delivery and pickup](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/8366663). Once the purchase is confirmed, the subsidy is transferred to the family's social or bank card.

The list of enterprises that will supply coal to the population and budget organizations in the regions is compiled by the Council of Ministers of the Republic of Karakalpakstan and the hokimiyats (regional administrations) of the regions.

## Why the subsidy appeared

The measure is part of the coal market's shift to market-based pricing. Since June 1, 2026, coal has been removed from the list of strategically significant social goods with state-regulated prices, and its cost is now set by supply and demand. President Shavkat Mirziyoyev [reviewed plans to introduce targeted support for families in need during the transition period at a meeting on May 26, 2026](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9253). Resolution No. 418 fixed the exact calculation mechanism and payment terms.

For budget organizations, the resolution provides for three channels of coal supply: state procurement at auction, a special trading section of the commodity exchange, and guaranteed supply from Kumir Taminot LLC. The last channel applies if an organization has not chosen a supplier on its own. Kumir Taminot's own assets may be sold at auction, but only on condition that the buyer continues supplying coal to the population and budget organizations.
