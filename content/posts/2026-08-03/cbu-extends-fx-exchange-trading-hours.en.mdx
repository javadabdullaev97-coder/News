The Central Bank of Uzbekistan will extend trading sessions on the foreign exchange market starting August 10, 2026 — the closing time will shift from 3 p.m. to 5 p.m., the [Central Bank of Uzbekistan](https://t.me/centralbankuzbekistan/13392) said in its Telegram channel. The session start time — 10 a.m. — remains unchanged, and trading will continue every banking business day.

## Why the Trading Session Is Being Extended

According to the [Central Bank's press center](https://cbu.uz/uz/press_center/adverts/4257108/), the extended session serves three purposes: improving banks' management of foreign currency liquidity, ensuring the timeliness of client payments, and maintaining the continuity of foreign transfers.

## How the Official Exchange Rate Is Set

The official exchange rate of the soum against foreign currencies will still be determined as the weighted average of transactions concluded on the foreign exchange market and the over-the-counter interbank currency market, the [Central Bank](https://t.me/centralbankuzbekistan/13392) said. The regulator announces the rate daily at 5:30 p.m.

Based on the trading results, the official exchange rates of 74 foreign currencies against the soum are [published](https://cbu.uz/uz/press_center/adverts/4257108/) — they are used in accounting, statistics, customs duties and other mandatory payments.

## What's Next

The new schedule takes effect on August 10, 2026. Until then, foreign exchange trading sessions will continue to close at 3 p.m., as they do now.
