President Shavkat Mirziyoyev announced a two-year extension of the hotel construction subsidy program at an open dialogue with entrepreneurs in Khiva on August 18, presidential press secretary Sherzod Asadov [said](https://t.me/Press_Secretary_Uz/7724) on his Telegram channel. The measure covers three-, four- and five-star hotels.

The requirement to confirm a hotel's star rating with a certification from a foreign company in order to qualify for the subsidy is being scrapped, according to the post.

The tax break on land tax and property tax for hotels, set at a rate 10 times lower than the standard one, has been extended through 2030, the president's official website [reports](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9511). $50 million will be allocated for the construction, renovation and equipping of hotels.

Services that tour operators provide to foreign tourists will be treated as exports and exempted from VAT. The customs break on imports of tourist buses, electric buses and minibuses has been extended for another year, and a recycling fee will not be charged on such vehicles.

Banks will install cash currency exchange machines in hotels with more than 50 rooms.

The meeting in Khiva is Mirziyoyev's latest open dialogue with entrepreneurs. As of publication, no separate regulation based on the meeting's outcomes has been issued.
