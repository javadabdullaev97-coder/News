SpaceXAI on August 14 [closed a deal](https://cursor.com/blog/joining-spacex) to acquire the AI coding tool Cursor for $60 billion — two days after releasing its flagship Grok 4.6 model.

The company was formed in February 2026, when SpaceX [announced the acquisition](https://x.ai/news/xai-joins-spacex) of Elon Musk's xAI, along with the Grok chatbot and the X social media platform. Since then, SpaceX's AI division has operated under the SpaceXAI brand.

## Grok 4.6: claims of parity with GPT-5.6 Sol

SpaceXAI [unveiled](https://x.ai/news/grok-4-6) Grok 4.6 on August 12 — a model built for long-horizon agentic tasks: coding, working with unfamiliar codebases and building prototypes. According to the company, Grok 4.6 matches OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Sol in intelligence and trails Anthropic's Claude Fable 5 only slightly.

The claim rests on the independent [Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index](https://artificialanalysis.ai/evaluations/artificial-analysis-intelligence-index) ranking. According to SpaceXAI, citing that ranking, Grok 4.6 scored 61 points — the same as GPT-5.6 Sol and one point below Claude Fable 5. Claude Fable 5 scored 62 points, which the ranking's page confirms directly. By SpaceXAI's own data, the model trails Claude Fable 5 on some other benchmarks — CursorBench and FrontierCode — but outperforms GPT-5.6 Sol on the APEX-SWE test.

Grok 4.6's standard pricing is $2 per million input tokens and $6 per million output tokens. That undercuts GPT-5.6 Sol ($5 and $15) and Claude Opus 5 ($5 and $25), [AIBusiness writes](https://aibusiness.com/generative-ai/grok-4-6-out-undercutting-ai-prices-rivals).

## The Cursor acquisition

SpaceX and Cursor [announced a partnership](https://9to5mac.com/2026/08/14/spacex-lands-deal-to-likely-purchase-claude-code-and-openai-codex-competitor/) on April 21, 2026. The agreement gave SpaceX the right to buy all of Cursor for $60 billion within a year.

On August 14, the parties [announced the deal had closed](https://cursor.com/blog/joining-spacex) — in SpaceX stock. Cursor's blog reported the deal's completion and the company's move under SpaceXAI. [TechCrunch confirmed](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/15/spacex-officially-closes-its-cursor-acquisition/) the closing.

Cursor is an AI code editor used by developers worldwide. The service competes with Anthropic's Claude Code and OpenAI's Codex. Cursor uses SpaceX's Colossus supercomputer — access began under the April partnership, before the deal's formal closing.
