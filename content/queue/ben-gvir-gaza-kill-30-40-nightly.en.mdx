Israeli National Security Minister Itamar Ben Gvir said on August 16 on the podcast of former Hamas hostage Rom Braslavsky that the army should kill "between 30 and 40" Gaza residents every night, Kommersant [reported](https://www.kommersant.ru/doc/8890893).

The statement came in a podcast episode released on Sunday that circulated in Palestinian media, Al Jazeera [reported](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/16/israeli-minister-calls-for-killing-30-to-40-palestinians-in-gaza-nightly?traffic_source=rss). "I think targeted killings need to happen in Gaza — wiping out 30 to 40 people every night," Ben Gvir said, the outlet [quoted him as saying](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/16/israeli-minister-calls-for-killing-30-to-40-palestinians-in-gaza-nightly?traffic_source=rss). According to the minister, this applies not only to those who pose an immediate threat: "There are people there who don't deserve to live. They shouldn't live. They're not even human."

In the same episode, Ben Gvir called "all of Gaza" Israeli territory, Al Jazeera [quoted him as saying](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/16/israeli-minister-calls-for-killing-30-to-40-palestinians-in-gaza-nightly?traffic_source=rss). He proposed building new Jewish settlements not only in Gush Katif but across the entire enclave, encouraging Palestinians toward "maximum emigration," Kommersant [reported](https://www.kommersant.ru/doc/8890893). Podcast host Braslavsky asked the minister to personally authorize him to execute Palestinian militants; Ben Gvir said he would do everything he could to make that happen, Al Jazeera [wrote](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/16/israeli-minister-calls-for-killing-30-to-40-palestinians-in-gaza-nightly?traffic_source=rss).

Ben Gvir sits on Israel's security cabinet and takes part in shaping military policy in Gaza, Al Jazeera [noted](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/16/israeli-minister-calls-for-killing-30-to-40-palestinians-in-gaza-nightly?traffic_source=rss). In May 2026, Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu and Israeli Foreign Minister Gideon Saar already condemned the minister over his treatment of detained pro-Palestinian activists, Kommersant [wrote](https://www.kommersant.ru/doc/8890893). The United Kingdom and some EU countries have previously imposed sanctions on the minister — without consequences for his position in government, Al Jazeera noted.

The Hamas-run Gaza health ministry [said](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/16/israeli-minister-calls-for-killing-30-to-40-palestinians-in-gaza-nightly?traffic_source=rss) more than 1,250 people have died in the enclave since the truce took effect in October 2025. The figures are as of August 16, Al Jazeera noted. Israel has a death penalty law that applies only to Palestinians convicted of terrorism, the outlet noted.
