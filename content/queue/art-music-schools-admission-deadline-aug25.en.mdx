The Ministry of Culture of the Republic of Uzbekistan has [announced](https://t.me/madaniyatvazirligi/60957) the start of admissions to children's music and art schools for the 2026/2027 school year — applications are accepted from August 1 to August 25, 2026.

## Only through my.gov.uz

Applications can be submitted exclusively online, through the [Unified Portal of Interactive Government Services my.gov.uz](https://my.gov.uz/uz/service/1264), under the service "Admission of documents to children's music and art schools." The Ministry of Culture stresses that paper applications are not accepted. Parents need to register or log in on the portal — after that, the authorized body verifies the data, and the applicant receives an electronic notification of the result.

According to the service page on my.gov.uz, processing an application takes three business days, and the service itself is free. For consultations, the Ministry of Culture lists the phone number +998 (55) 502-33-77, extension 1325.

## Required documents

The list of documents required for admission is set out in the [Regulation on Children's Music and Art Schools](https://lex.uz/docs/2947562), approved by the Cabinet of Ministers on May 5, 2016 (Resolution No. 144). Parents or the child's legal representatives must submit:

- an application specifying the field and specialty of study;
- the child's birth certificate;
- a medical certificate of the established form;
- two 3×4 cm photographs.

If the documents are submitted by someone other than a parent, a document confirming that person's status as a legal representative is additionally required.

## Creative admission tests

Admission to the schools is conducted through creative admission tests — the admissions board assesses the child's abilities in the chosen field:

- musical ear and sense of rhythm — for music specialties;
- physical attributes and coordination — for choreography;
- drawing ability — for fine arts;
- acting ability — for theater departments.

The procedure and results of the tests are determined by the current regulation, the Ministry of Culture notes in its statement.

## What's next

The application period closes on August 25, 2026. The Regulation on Children's Music and Art Schools sets a deadline for creative admission tests of no later than August 31, 2026 (clause 17). The exact dates of the tests and the enrollment procedure are determined by the admissions boards of individual schools.
