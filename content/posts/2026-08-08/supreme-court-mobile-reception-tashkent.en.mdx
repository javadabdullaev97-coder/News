The chairman of the Supreme Court, his deputies and the heads of courts in Tashkent and Tashkent region will hold a mobile public reception on August 8 to address procedural complaints about court rulings, the Supreme Court's Telegram channel [said](https://t.me/oliysuduz/51380).

## Who Is Holding the Reception and on What Issues

Besides the chairman of the Supreme Court and his deputies, the chairman and deputy chairman of the Supreme Judicial Council are also [taking part](https://t.me/oliysuduz/51360) in the reception. The chairmen and deputy chairmen of courts in Tashkent and Tashkent region are participating as well. Individuals and legal entities, as well as their legal representatives, who live or are registered in Tashkent and Tashkent region, may apply.

The Supreme Court [accepts](https://t.me/oliysuduz/51360) procedural complaints about court rulings and other appeals that fall within the competence of judicial bodies. Appeals are reviewed in accordance with current legislation and established procedure.

## Where and When

Registration of participants [begins](https://t.me/oliysuduz/51360) at 9:00, with the reception itself starting at 10:00. The venue is the Uzexpocentre complex at 107 Amir Temur Shokh Street, Yunusabad district, Tashkent.

Those applying need to [have with them](https://t.me/oliysuduz/51360) an identity document — a passport or ID card. Representatives need a document confirming their authority, as well as court rulings and other materials relevant to the substance of the appeal. The announcement does not mention advance registration — participants are asked to arrive on the day of the reception with their documents. For additional questions, the Supreme Court [provided](https://t.me/oliysuduz/51360) a phone number: 71 207-97-37.
