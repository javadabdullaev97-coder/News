Another "Car-Free Day" campaign will take place across all regions of Uzbekistan on August 10, [announced](https://t.me/ecogovuz/49497) by the Ministry of Ecology, Environmental Protection and Climate Change — on this day, civil servants are barred from using official vehicles under [Presidential Decree UP-46](https://lex.uz/docs/8101212).

## What exactly is banned, and for whom

The ban applies to official vehicles, not personal cars. It was established by [Presidential Decree UP-46](https://lex.uz/docs/8101212) of March 25, 2026, on implementing the nationwide "Clean Air" project aimed at improving air quality. Since May 1, 2026, "Car-Free Day" and "Car-Free Week" campaigns have been held on the 10th and 25th of each month. On these days, civil servants are strictly barred from using official vehicles. If the date falls on a working day, drivers' salaries are paid in full.

This requirement is part of the decree's broader enforcement mechanism, not a separate sanction specifically for driving a car on August 10.

The decree does not ban the use of personal vehicles. The [Ministry of Higher Education, Science and Innovation](https://t.me/eduuz/50302) clarified in its Telegram channel that heads and employees of subordinate organizations are recommended, on this day, to forgo personal cars and use public transport, a bicycle or another eco-friendly mode of transport. The channel's wording describes this as a recommendation, not a ban.

## Why the campaign is not a one-off

"Car-Free Day" is not being held for the first time: since May 1, 2026, the campaign has been organized regularly, twice a month — on the 10th and 25th. August 10 is a routine, not an exceptional, campaign under the decree's rule that has been in effect since spring.

## What's next

The next "Car-Free Day" campaign will take place on schedule on August 25. Decree UP-46 also provides for other measures under the "Clean Air" project, which agencies report on separately.
