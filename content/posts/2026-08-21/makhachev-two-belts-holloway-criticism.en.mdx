Former UFC champion Max Holloway on Monday, August 17, criticized Islam Makhachev on his Kick livestream for posing with two championship belts at once.

"Why the hell do you have two belts? For the aura? I mean, it is what it is — you're kind of a champion and kind of not," Holloway [said](https://www.mmafighting.com/ufc/505465/max-holloway-questions-islam-makhachev-walking-around-with-2-belts). He added: "That was temporary, but now you're only defending one belt, bro."

The trigger was Makhachev's win at UFC 330 on August 15 in Philadelphia — his 17th straight win in the UFC and a [new organization record](https://www.ufc.com/news/ufc-330-islam-makhachev-vs-ian-machado-garry-results). LEAP News covered the fight in detail in an earlier report — [Makhachev defended his UFC welterweight title and broke Silva's record](https://leap.uz/ru/2026/08/17/makhachev-welterweight-record-ufc330).

After the win, Makhachev appeared at the press conference with two belts — the current welterweight belt and his former lightweight belt, which formally no longer belongs to him.

## Why Makhachev formally holds two belts

Makhachev vacated the lightweight belt in May 2025, when he decided to move up to welterweight. Ilia Topuria won the vacant title by knockout over Charles Oliveira at UFC 317 in June 2025. In June 2026, at the UFC Freedom 250 event near the White House, Justin Gaethje took the title, stopping Topuria by technical knockout late in the fourth round. Gaethje remains the current lightweight champion. Formally, Makhachev has defended only one title since May 2025 — the welterweight belt.

Neither Makhachev nor his team has publicly responded to Holloway's remarks.
