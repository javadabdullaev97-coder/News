At the Centro Caribe Sports General Assembly in Santo Domingo on July 23, organization president Luis Mejía Oviedo introduced participants to International Kurash Association (IKA) vice president and Pan American Kurash Union president Jaime Casanova Martínez. The assembly also announced an upcoming visit to Santo Domingo by IKA acting president Ziyoviddin Khodjiniyazov — [the Ministry of Sports of Uzbekistan reported](https://t.me/Minsportuz/89933) that the parties are discussing prospects for including kurash in the program of the 2030 Central American and Caribbean Games.

## What Is Known About the Meeting

The meeting took place on the sidelines of the [Centro Caribe Sports General Assembly](https://kurashpanam.org/ika-vice-president-participates-in-the-centro-caribe-sports-general-assembly/) — an organization that unites the national Olympic committees of Central American and Caribbean countries and is responsible for holding the Central American and Caribbean Games. The assembly was held on July 23, 2026, a day before the start of the XXV Games in Santo Domingo, which run from July 24 to August 8.

According to the Pan American Kurash Union, the meeting was "an important step toward strengthening kurash's position in Latin America, expanding international cooperation and advancing sports diplomacy." As of publication, no final decision has been made on including kurash in the 2030 Games program — the parties continue discussions, and no next round of talks has been scheduled.

In addition to leading Centro Caribe Sports, Mejía Oviedo is a member of the International Olympic Committee (IOC).

## How Kurash Is Being Promoted in the Region

The Pan American Kurash Union operates within the IKA structure and holds Pan American championships for youth, juniors and adults in the region.

Ziyoviddin Khodjiniyazov was elected IKA's first vice president in April 2026 and had become the association's acting president by July 2026. Before his election, he served as IKA vice president for international cooperation and had worked in kurash organizations since 2000, including as secretary general of the Kurash Confederation of Asia.

## What's Next

Organizers of the Central American and Caribbean Games typically finalize the next cycle's program several years before the opening, so discussions on the sports lineup for the 2030 Games may continue in the coming months. The exact date the list of disciplines will be announced is not yet known.
