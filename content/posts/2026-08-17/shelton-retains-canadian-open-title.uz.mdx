Amerikalik Ben Shelton 13-avgustda Monrealda o'tkazilgan National Bank Open finalida vatandoshi Brendon Nakashimani — 6:3, 7:6(4) — [mag'lub etdi](https://www.atptour.com/en/news/shelton-nakashima-montreal-2026-thursday-final) va ATP Masters 1000 turnirini ketma-ket ikkinchi yil yutib oldi. Bir yil oldin u Torontoda g'olib chiqqan edi, endi — Monrealda.

Shelton turnir davomida birorta ham setni yo'qotmadi va finalda birorta ham breyk-pointni bermadi, buni Tennis Majors [qayd etadi](https://www.tennismajors.com/atp/shelton-defends-montreal-crown-joins-elite-open-era-club-with-gritty-win-over-nakashima-858490.html). O'zining zarbasida ochkolarning 83 foizini yutib oldi, 22 ta vinner zarba uyushtirdi va 12 ta majburiy bo'lmagan xato qildi; Nakashimada — 19 ta vinner zarba va 21 ta xato qayd etildi.

Bu g'alaba Shelton uchun karerasidagi yettinchi unvon va Masters 1000 turnirlaridagi ikkinchi unvon bo'ldi. Nakashima bilan shaxsiy uchrashuvlar hisobi 6:0 ga yetdi. Turnirdan so'ng Shelton to'rt pog'ona ko'tarilib, ATP reytingida 6-o'ringa qaytdi, buni ATP Tour [xabar beradi](https://www.atptour.com/en/news/shelton-pif-atp-rankings-14-august-2026). Monrealdagi g'olib 1 151 420 dollar (≈997 000 yevro, o'yin sanasidagi taxminiy kursda) mukofot oldi, buni ATP Tour [ma'lum qiladi](https://www.atptour.com/en/news/montreal-2026-champion-prize-money).

## Nadaldan keyin yettinchi

Shelton Ochiq era davrida Canadian Open unvonini himoya qila olgan yettinchi o'yinchi bo'ldi, buni Tennis Majors [yozadi](https://www.tennismajors.com/atp/shelton-defends-montreal-crown-joins-elite-open-era-club-with-gritty-win-over-nakashima-858490.html). Undan oldin bu Ivan Lendl, Jon Makkenro, Andre Agassi, Endi Marrey, Novak Jokovich va Rafael Nadalga muyassar bo'lgan edi. Nadal bunday dublni oxirgi marta amalga oshirgan o'yinchi bo'lib, u turnirni 2018 va 2019-yillarda yutgan edi.

Monrealdagi final 2003-yildan buyon Masters 1000 turnirlaridagi birinchi barcha-amerikalik final bo'ldi — o'shanda Endi Roddik Sinsinnatida Mardi Fishni mag'lub etgan edi, buni The Guardian [xabar beradi](https://www.theguardian.com/sport/2026/aug/13/ben-shelton-brandon-nakashima-canadian-open-final-tennis). Shelton Roddikning 2003-yildagi g'alabasidan keyin Kanada Masters 1000 turnirini yutgan birinchi amerikalik bo'ldi.

## Keyin nima bo'ladi

Shelton uchun keyingi turnir — 13–23 avgust kunlari Sinsinnatida o'tkaziladigan ATP Masters 1000 seriyasidagi Cincinnati Open, buni ATP Tour [xabar beradi](https://www.atptour.com/en/news/cincinnati-2026-atp-masters-1000-history-draw-schedule).
