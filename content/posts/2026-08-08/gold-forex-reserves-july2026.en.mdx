Uzbekistan's gold and foreign exchange reserves reached $64.34 billion as of August 1, 2026 — in July, the figure [grew by $589.75 million, or 0.92%](https://cbu.uz/ru/statistics/intlreserves/4320433/), the Central Bank of the Republic of Uzbekistan said.

## What Grew

The value of gold holdings rose by $365.3 million in July — to [$56.12 billion](https://cbu.uz/ru/statistics/intlreserves/4320433/). In physical terms, the volume of gold barely changed. As of August 1, it stood at 13.85 million troy ounces (about 430.8 tons) — against 13.88 million ounces (431.7 tons) [at the start of the month](https://cbu.uz/ru/statistics/intlreserves/4124475/). The increase in value came primarily from the price of the metal, not new purchases.

The foreign currency portion of reserves grew by $208.8 million, to [$7.65 billion](https://cbu.uz/ru/statistics/intlreserves/4320433/). Within it, the structure shifted more noticeably than the total sum. The volume of cash and deposits increased by $1.31 billion — to $5.89 billion. Holdings of foreign securities, by contrast, fell by $1.13 billion (-38.4%) — to $1.76 billion. In other words, the securities portfolio in the Central Bank of Uzbekistan's reserves shrank markedly over the month, while the volume of currency deposits grew at the same time.

## Reserves at the Start and End of July

According to [Central Bank data as of July 1, 2026](https://cbu.uz/ru/statistics/intlreserves/4124475/), the country's total reserves stood at $63.75 billion. Gold was valued at $55.75 billion, the foreign currency portion at $7.44 billion. Of that, $4.58 billion was cash and deposits, and $2.89 billion was securities. Over the month, these figures rose; at the same time, the structure of the foreign currency portion shifted toward deposits, while the securities portfolio shrank.

A month earlier, in June, reserves had been declining — the July data mark a reversal of that trend.
