The Central Bank of Uzbekistan (CBU) set the official dollar rate for August 10 at [11,952.10 soums](https://cbu.uz/ru/arkhiv-kursov-valyut/json/USD/2026-08-10/) — 36.46 soums above the previous trading day's level.

## Second Consecutive Day of Growth

The dollar rate has risen for a second consecutive trading day. On August 7, the CBU [recorded](https://cbu.uz/ru/arkhiv-kursov-valyut/json/USD/2026-08-07/) a rate of 11,915.64 soums — up 28.92 soums from the previous trading day. The same rate held over the weekend, August 8–9: the exchange does not revise quotes on non-working days. On August 10, the rate rose by another 36.46 soums. Over the two trading days combined, the dollar gained 65.38 soums against the soum, or 0.55%.

## Rate Still Below Week-Ago and Month-Ago Levels

Despite the past two days' gains, the August 10 rate remains below levels from early August and mid-July. A week earlier, on August 3, the CBU [set](https://cbu.uz/ru/arkhiv-kursov-valyut/json/USD/2026-08-03/) the rate at 11,960.18 soums — 8.08 soums above the current level. A month earlier, on July 10, the official rate stood at [12,012.21 soums](https://cbu.uz/ru/arkhiv-kursov-valyut/json/USD/2026-07-10/) — 60.11 soums, or 0.5%, above the current figure.
