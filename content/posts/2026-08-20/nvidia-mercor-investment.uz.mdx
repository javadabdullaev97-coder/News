Nvidia belgilangan ma'lumotlar yetkazib beruvchisi Mercor'ning 20 milliard dollarlik baholanishidagi moliyalashtirish raundida ishtirok etishni muhokama qilmoqda — bu haqda nomsiz manbalarga tayanib The Information nashri xabar berdi, buni [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/news/investment-tracker/2026/nvidia-weighs-investment-in-round-valuing-mercor-at-20-billion/) chorshanba, 19 avgust kuni yetkazdi.

Mercor Nvidia o'zining ochiq Nemotron oilasidagi modellarini ishlab chiqishda foydalanadigan belgilangan ma'lumotlarni yetkazib beradi. O'tgan chorakda Mercor'ning Nvidia buyurtmalaridan olgan daromadi o'nlab million dollarga yetdi, [PYMNTS yozadi](https://www.pymnts.com/news/investment-tracker/2026/nvidia-weighs-investment-in-round-valuing-mercor-at-20-billion/).

PYMNTS Nvidia'ning mumkin bo'lgan investitsiyasining aniq miqdorini va raundning umumiy hajmini oshkor qilmaydi. 20 avgust holatiga ko'ra, Nvidia yoki Mercor tomonidan muzokaralarning rasmiy tasdig'i yo'q.

Mercor'ning avvalgi baholanishi 10 milliard dollarni tashkil etgan edi. Bu ko'rsatkichga kompaniya 2025-yil oktyabrida 350 million dollarlik C seriyali raund yakunlari bo'yicha erishgan, [PYMNTS ta'kidlaydi](https://www.pymnts.com/news/investment-tracker/2026/nvidia-weighs-investment-in-round-valuing-mercor-at-20-billion/). Yana 9-iyul kuni [TechCrunch xabar bergan](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/09/mercor-is-in-talks-for-a-20b-valuation/)ki, Mercor 20 milliard dollarlik baholanishdagi raund bo'yicha muzokaralar olib bormoqda, biroq ishtirokchilar orasida Nvidia'ni tilga olmagan.
