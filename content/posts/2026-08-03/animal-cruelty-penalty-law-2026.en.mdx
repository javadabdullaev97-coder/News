Starting June 28, 2026, administrative fines for animal cruelty doubled in Uzbekistan — amendments to Article 111 of the Code on Administrative Liability [took effect](https://t.me/poytaxt_uz/51242), the Telegram channel Poytaxt.uz reported.

## What changed in the law

Article 111 of the [Code on Administrative Liability](https://lex.uz/docs/97661?ONDATE=28.06.2026+00) establishes liability for torturing animals or causing them suffering out of self-interest or other base motives. The article also provides for a stricter fine or administrative arrest of up to 15 days.

The Telegram channel of Saida Mirziyoyeva, head of the Administration of the President, [states](https://t.me/SShMirziyoyeva/4155) that as of June 28, "administrative fines... have been doubled."

The Base Calculation Value (BCV), used to calculate fines under Article 111, has [stood at](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/8180) 412,000 soums since August 1, 2025 (about $34 at the Central Bank's exchange rate [as of August 3](https://cbu.uz/ru/arkhiv-kursov-valyut/?date=03.08.2026) — 11,960.18 soums per dollar).

Criminal liability for animal cruelty operates separately and was not changed by the June 28 amendments. Under [Article 202-1 of the Criminal Code](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/5601207), introduced in 2021, liability applies to torturing an animal that results in its death or injury, committed after an administrative penalty has already been imposed. The punishment is a fine of 25 to 50 BCV, up to 240 hours of mandatory community service, up to a year of correctional labor, restriction of liberty, or imprisonment for up to 6 months.

## Saida Mirziyoyeva's comment

In the same post, Mirziyoyeva linked the tougher penalties to public safety and society's intolerance of violence.

"The safety of our society directly depends on intolerance toward violence. Tougher penalties for animal abuse help build an environment where respect for all life is an absolute norm," the post on Mirziyoyeva's channel states.

Parents were urged to teach children not to hurt the weak and not to prove strength through cruelty, with the post describing raising respect for other lives as a family responsibility. The post included a video of two cats, jokingly referred to as "employees" of the Administration of the President, with their names given as Yalpiz and Darkhan.

The statement belongs personally to Mirziyoyeva as the author of the post on her personal Telegram channel — the Administration of the President did not publish this position separately.
