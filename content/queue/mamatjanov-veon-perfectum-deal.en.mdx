Perfectum co-owner Farkhod Mamatjonov asked Mirziyoyev on August 18, at a dialogue with entrepreneurs in Khorezm, to speed up a VEON deal worth roughly $200 million — according to reports cited by several outlets; there is no official confirmation as of publication.

Several outlets quote Mamatjonov as saying: "In June we reached all the agreements. They are ready to sign a contract to attract $200 million. Our only question is whether you could help us obtain a ruling from the Competition Development Committee."

According to the same reports, Mirziyoyev replied that he would take personal control of the matter.

VEON is an international telecom holding registered in the Netherlands and listed on Nasdaq. It owns the operator Beeline Uzbekistan. Perfectum is a trademark of Rubicon Wireless Communication, an Uzbek mobile operator and digital platform.

## What led to the request

Mamatjonov is the majority shareholder of InfinBank and a co-owner of Perfectum. According to several outlets, the Competition Development Committee allegedly approved the transfer of a 50% stake in Rubicon Wireless Communication to Mamatjonov's ownership in July. There is no official confirmation or decision number.

In August 2024, Mamatjonov, according to the same reports, announced a partnership with Britain's Vodafone worth 250 million euros. Talks later shifted to VEON.

The committee is a government body that approves deals involving foreign participation in Uzbekistan's telecom market. The current status of the application's review is unknown as of publication.
