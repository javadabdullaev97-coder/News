Nvidia disclosed a stake in SpaceX worth nearly $21 billion as of June 30, 2026, in a filing with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) on August 14. The exact figure, according to [SEC data](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000065/0001045810-26-000065-index.htm), is $20.98 billion; the stake includes 122,764,805 Class A shares.

Nvidia received the shares through its investment in Elon Musk's xAI, which merged with SpaceX in February. The stake became the second-largest in the company's disclosed portfolio — only the $29.99 billion Intel stake is larger.

## How the stake emerged

In January 2026, Nvidia invested $10 billion in xAI's $20 billion Series E round. Valor Equity Partners, Qatar Investment Authority and Fidelity also joined the round, [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-14/nvidia-has-21-billion-spacex-stake-30-billion-in-intel-shares) reported.

In February, SpaceX acquired xAI in a $1.25 trillion stock deal, [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-14/nvidia-has-21-billion-spacex-stake-30-billion-in-intel-shares) reported — each xAI share converted into SpaceX Class A shares. SpaceX held its IPO on Nasdaq on June 12 — the largest offering in history, [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/14/nvidia-discloses-21-billion-stake-in-spacex-at-end-of-second-quarter.html) reported. Afterward, SpaceX shares became a publicly traded security, and Nvidia's stake appeared in a quarterly holdings report for the first time.

## A $63.44 billion portfolio

The total value of Nvidia's disclosed holdings reached $63.44 billion as of June 30, according to [SEC data](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000065/0001045810-26-000065-index.htm). The largest position is Intel: 214,776,632 shares worth $29.99 billion.

Besides SpaceX and Intel, the portfolio includes CoreWeave ($4.7 billion), Coherent ($3.07 billion) and Nokia ($2.21 billion). The list also includes Synopsys ($2.15 billion), Nebius ($328.8 million) and Generate Biomedicines ($14.06 million). Together, the Intel and SpaceX stakes account for more than 80% of the portfolio.

By August 14, the day the filing was published, SpaceX shares had fallen to $140 — down from $170.86 at the end of June. The market value of Nvidia's stake had dropped to about $17.2 billion by the time of disclosure, [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-14/nvidia-has-21-billion-spacex-stake-30-billion-in-intel-shares) estimated.

## What's next

Nvidia will present its full second-quarter fiscal year 2027 financial results on August 26, 2026, during an investor conference call, [the company said](https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2026/NVIDIA-Sets-Conference-Call-for-Second-Quarter-Financial-Results/default.aspx).
