Uzbekistan's phygital football team beat Spain 5-3 in the football stage of the III Games of the Future in Astana — the team's first win of the tournament, the Ministry of Sports of the Republic of Uzbekistan [said](https://t.me/Minsportuz/90003) on August 5.

## First Win of the Tournament

According to the Ministry of Sports, the Uzbekistan team scored its first tournament win precisely in the match against Spain. The ministry did not specify the exact date of the match, the team lineup or the goal scorers, nor did it say whether the match was part of the group stage or a separate qualifying round. As of publication, this is the only official statement on the Uzbekistan team's participation in the tournament; the team's status as a national squad could not be independently confirmed on the tournament organizer's website (gofuture.games) — the list of Phygital Football participants published there consisted, at the time of checking, of club teams.

## How Phygital Football Works

Phygital football is a discipline in which the match result is made up of two parts. In the digital part, under the [World Phygital Community's rules](https://worldphygital.org/phygital-football/), each team fields four players on consoles — two players per half. The teams then take the field: four outfield players and a goalkeeper on each side play under mini-football rules. Goals scored in both parts are added together — the team with the higher combined score wins.

## Games of the Future 2026

The III International Games of the Future are being held in Astana from July 29 to August 9, 2026, and include eight disciplines that combine traditional sport and esports, [according to](https://worldphygital.org/news/dates-and-disciplines-announced-for-the-games-of-the-future-2026/) World Phygital Community, the international organization that oversees phygital sport. They include phygital football, phygital basketball, phygital shooting and phygital dance.

## What's Next

The Ministry of Sports has not disclosed the schedule of Uzbekistan's remaining phygital football matches in the tournament. Competition in Astana continues through August 9.
