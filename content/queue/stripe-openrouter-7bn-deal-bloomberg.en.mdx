Payments company Stripe has agreed to buy U.S. artificial intelligence startup OpenRouter for more than $7 billion. [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-08-17/stripe-to-buy-ai-firm-openrouter-in-7-billion-deal-video) reported the deal on August 16, citing people familiar with the matter.

Stripe and OpenRouter have not officially confirmed the deal. A Stripe spokesperson said the company does not comment on "rumors and speculation," [PYMNTS reports](https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/stripe-finalizes-7-billion-deal-for-ai-firm-openrouter/), citing Bloomberg.

OpenRouter gives developers a single access point to more than 400 artificial intelligence models. They include models from more than 60 companies, including OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta and DeepSeek, [PYMNTS writes](https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/stripe-finalizes-7-billion-deal-for-ai-firm-openrouter/). The service does not build its own models — it selects which one handles a given request based on price, speed and quality, [PYMNTS notes](https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/stripe-finalizes-7-billion-deal-for-ai-firm-openrouter/). In return, OpenRouter charges developers a commission — 5% of what they spend on processing requests through the models, [SiliconANGLE reports](https://siliconangle.com/2026/08/16/stripe-reportedly-finalizes-deal-to-buy-ai-model-router-openrouter-for-more-than-7b/).

The service has about 8 million users worldwide, SiliconANGLE notes. OpenRouter was founded in 2023 by Alex Atallah and Louis Vishi. Atallah described his company as "the Stripe for AI" — a reference to the payments company that is now buying it.

## Valuation Quintupled in Three Months

OpenRouter raised $113 million in a Series B round in May 2026, led by CapitalG LP, Alphabet's venture arm. Andreessen Horowitz and Menlo Ventures also took part in the round. The company was valued at $1.3 billion at the time, [SiliconANGLE reports](https://siliconangle.com/2026/08/16/stripe-reportedly-finalizes-deal-to-buy-ai-model-router-openrouter-for-more-than-7b/). Stripe is paying more than $7 billion for it — more than five times the May valuation.

Weekly request volume on the platform grew to 25 trillion tokens by May 2026 — five times more than in November 2025. Annual revenue rose from about $19 million at the end of 2025 to about $50 million in March 2026, SiliconANGLE writes.

According to PYMNTS, negotiations in July were still based on a valuation of about $10 billion — the final deal size came in lower.

## Why Stripe Wants This

Stripe has served as OpenRouter's payments provider since January 2026. Together with OpenAI, the companies developed the Agentic Commerce Protocol — a settlement standard for AI agents that make purchases on their own, SiliconANGLE notes.

Stripe previously bought Metronome, a startup that calculates the cost of AI usage for billing clients. OpenRouter decides which model handles a given request. Both acquisitions cover different parts of the same task for Stripe — billing and AI traffic distribution, PYMNTS writes.
