Gulmira Nuralieva, deputy director of School No. 42 in Kumkurgan district, Surkhandarya region, underwent an interview with the Council of Directors this week — under the school leadership selection mechanism reported by the [Ministry of Preschool and School Education](https://t.me/uzedu/44631).

## How the Selection Worked in Kumkurgan

Nuralieva held the position of deputy director for spiritual and educational work at School No. 42. According to the [ministry](https://t.me/uzedu/44631), candidates for vacant director positions apply through an open competition on the manager.uzedu.uz platform. During the interview, the regional Council of Directors assesses a candidate's managerial potential, strategic thinking and problem-solving ability — the same criteria applied to Nuralieva.

## What the Council of Directors Is and When It Was Introduced

The mechanism has been in effect since 2023. [Presidential Decree No. PF-79](https://lex.uz/docs/-6476175) "On Measures to Effectively Organize the Activities of the Ministry of Preschool and School Education and Its Subordinate Organizations" took effect on May 31, 2023. It mandated the creation of councils of directors in Karakalpakstan, the regions and Tashkent — composed of the most experienced heads of general secondary schools in the region.

Under the decree, the appointment of school directors is now carried out by territorial departments of preschool and school education, but only on the recommendation of the regional Council of Directors, issued following an open competition. Previously, appointment decisions were made without this selection procedure.

## How This Differs From the Previous Procedure

The same decree abolished the practice under which deputy directors for spiritual and educational work were recommended by district (city) centers of spirituality and enlightenment. That was the exact position Nuralieva held before entering the competition. These appointments now also go through the new procedure.

The decree also introduced mandatory certification of sitting school directors once every five years, effective January 1, 2024. A director who fails certification is dismissed in accordance with established procedure, [the decree states](https://lex.uz/docs/-6476175).

## What's Next

Under the decree, councils of directors are meant to operate in all regions of the country since 2023. This means Kumkurgan district is not a separate pilot site but one of the districts where the nationwide mechanism is already applied as standard practice. The ministry did not provide figures on how many director positions nationwide have been filled through the new system.
