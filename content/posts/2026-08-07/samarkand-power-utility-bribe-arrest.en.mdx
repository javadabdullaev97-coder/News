The head of a power supply utility was arrested in one of the districts of Samarkand region — according to investigators, for accepting a $5,000 bribe. This was reported on August 6 by the Telegram channel "Xavfsizlik," which is linked to the State Security Service.

## What the money was demanded for

According to [the channel](https://t.me/xavfsizlik_uz/2453), the head of the utility demanded money from a local entrepreneur who owns a food-service business. The entrepreneur had illegally connected to the public power grid — the channel estimated the damage to the state from this connection at 393 million soums (≈$33,062 at the [Central Bank of Uzbekistan exchange rate](https://cbu.uz/ru/arkhiv-kursov-valyut/json/USD/2026-08-06/) for August 6 — 11,886.72 soums per dollar). For $5,000, the official promised not to pursue criminal charges against the entrepreneur and instead to understate the amount of damage and refer the case to court under administrative rather than criminal proceedings.

In late July, according to the publication, the head of the utility received the first installment — $4,000 — and demanded that the entrepreneur quickly hand over the remaining $1,000. According to the channel, the arrest was carried out by State Security Service officers together with the Department for Combating Economic Crimes under the Prosecutor General's Office — he was detained while receiving the last part of the sum through an intermediary.

## Criminal case opened, investigation continues

According to the channel, a criminal case has been opened over this incident. The publication does not name the suspect, the exact district where the utility is located, or the article of the Criminal Code under which the act was classified. It is separately noted that the head of the utility had previously been convicted. As of publication, no official statement on this case had come from the Department for Combating Economic Crimes, the Prosecutor General's Office, or the State Security Service. Until a court verdict takes legal effect, the person involved is presumed innocent.

## Context: other cases of energy-sector violations in Samarkand region

This week, the Department for Combating Economic Crimes [reported](https://t.me/Department_uz/12942) a separate, unrelated case in Samarkand region: officials at a company illegally connected to the public power grid at a sand-processing workshop and consumed 919.8 million soums' worth of electricity. A criminal case has also been opened over this incident.
