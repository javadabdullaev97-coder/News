AQSH prezidenti Donald Tramp 19-avgustda Kanadaning $20,2 mlrdlik eksportiga nisbatan 50 foizli bojlarni uch kunga to'xtatdi va muddat tugashidan sal oldin Kanada bilan savdo bitimiga erishilganini e'lon qildi. Bu haqda [PBS NewsHour](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/trump-says-us-and-canada-reached-last-minute-deal-to-delay-50-us-tariffs-on-canadian-imports) va [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/8/19/us-canada-reach-trade-deal-to-avert-steep-tariffs-trump-says) xabar beradi.

Bojlar [20-avgust kechasi](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/trump-says-us-and-canada-reached-last-minute-deal-to-delay-50-us-tariffs-on-canadian-imports) kuchga kirishi kerak edi, ammo Tramp muddat tugashiga ikki soatdan kamroq vaqt qolganda pauzani e'lon qildi. «Men Kanadaga qarshi 50 foizli bojlarni... uch kunlik muddatga to'xtatdim, chunki Kanada va AQSH o'rtasida... BITIM bor!» — [dedi Tramp](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/trump-says-us-and-canada-reached-last-minute-deal-to-delay-50-us-tariffs-on-canadian-imports). Al Jazeera ma'lumotlariga ko'ra, [bojlar 22-avgustgacha to'xtatilgan](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/8/19/us-canada-reach-trade-deal-to-avert-steep-tariffs-trump-says).

Kanada bosh vaziri Mark Karni muzokaralarda «sezilarli muvaffaqiyatga erishilgani»ni [tasdiqladi](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/trump-says-us-and-canada-reached-last-minute-deal-to-delay-50-us-tariffs-on-canadian-imports), biroq «muhim ishlar hali qolganini» ta'kidladi. Tomonlar bitimning to'liq shartlarini oshkor qilmadi.

PBS NewsHour ma'lumotlariga ko'ra, bojlar tahdidi ostida Kanadaning AQShga eksportining qariyb 5 foizi — xokkey tayoqlaridan tortib yog'och kurakchalargacha bo'lgan taxminan [$20 mlrd](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/trump-says-us-and-canada-reached-last-minute-deal-to-delay-50-us-tariffs-on-canadian-imports)lik mahsulotlar bor edi. Al Jazeera esa [boshqa ro'yxatni keltiradi](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/8/19/us-canada-reach-trade-deal-to-avert-steep-tariffs-trump-says) — elektronika, sanoat uskunalari, mebel, sut mahsulotlari va vino, jami $20,2 mlrd qiymatida.

PBS NewsHour ma'lumotlariga ko'ra, Kanada AQSh tomoni amerikalik alkogol, sut mahsulotlari va avtomobillarga nisbatan kamsituvchi deb hisoblagan choralarni [bekor qilishga majburiyat oldi](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/trump-says-us-and-canada-reached-last-minute-deal-to-delay-50-us-tariffs-on-canadian-imports).

Tramp, shuningdek, Jo Bayden ma'muriyati davrida yopilgan Keystone XL quvuri «qabrdan tirilishi mumkinligini» aytdi. Bu loyihaning taqdiri bitim shartlariga kirganini anglatadimi, prezident aniqlik kiritmadi.

Al Jazeera ma'lumotlariga ko'ra, pauza 22-avgustgacha amal qiladi — shu muddatgacha tomonlar bitimning qolgan tafsilotlarini kelishib olishlari kerak.
