Negotiations over the fight between Tyson Fury and Anthony Joshua have entered a "critical week," Matchroom Boxing chief Frank Smith said on August 18 in an interview with [Sky Sports](https://www.skysports.com/boxing/news/12040/13574297/tyson-fury-vs-anthony-joshua-negotiations-enter-critical-week-with-ongoing-talks-about-staging-fight-in-new-york).

According to Smith, the two sides have seven to ten days to finalize the remaining details of the bout. Asked whether the coming week would truly be decisive, he said: "I'd say one hundred percent."

The contract the sides have already signed originally called for the fight to be held in Britain. The move to stage the bout at Madison Square Garden in New York is now under discussion, [Sky Sports reports](https://www.skysports.com/boxing/news/12040/13574297/tyson-fury-vs-anthony-joshua-negotiations-enter-critical-week-with-ongoing-talks-about-staging-fight-in-new-york). New York is the preferred venue for promoter Turki Alalshikh, Joshua's promoter Eddie Hearn told [Sky Sports](https://www.skysports.com/boxing/news/12183/13567060/tyson-fury-vs-anthony-joshua-everything-you-need-to-know-about-the-proposed-heavyweight-fight), and Netflix, which holds the broadcast rights, is involved in choosing the venue.

If the negotiations move quickly, the fight could take place in November 2026. The boxers need an eight-to-ten-week training camp, so organizers must decide on the venue in the coming days, Sky Sports notes.

## Two Previous Collapses

The sides have already tried twice to agree on this fight — and both times the deal fell through. In August 2021, the bout was announced for Saudi Arabia, but the agreement collapsed. In December 2022, the sides again accepted terms for the fight, but it never took place, [Sky Sports reports](https://www.skysports.com/boxing/news/12183/13567060/tyson-fury-vs-anthony-joshua-everything-you-need-to-know-about-the-proposed-heavyweight-fight).

Both fighters have already had tune-up bouts. On July 25, Fury beat Mariusz Wach by technical knockout in the seventh round in Thailand. On July 26, Joshua knocked out Christian Prenga in Jeddah, dropping him twice in the first round.

On August 18, Fury posted a video on Instagram teasing an upcoming announcement, calling it "the one you've all been waiting for." He will reveal the details soon, [Sky Sports reports](https://www.skysports.com/watch/video/13574370/tyson-fury-teases-huge-fight-announcement-the-one-youve-all-been-waiting-for). The clip does not specify what the announcement is about.

## What's Next

Organizers need to agree on a venue and announce the fight within the next 7-10 days to have time for a full training camp ahead of the tentative November date, Sky Sports reports.
