Tashkent Hokimiyat announced on August 7 that reconstruction of School No. 290 was complete, duplicating the report on the city portal tashkent.uz — a new academic building for 750 students has raised the school's total capacity to 1,714 students.

With the construction of a new academic building for 750 students, the total capacity of School No. 290 [has risen to 1,714 seats](https://t.me/poytaxt_uz/51282). Before the reconstruction, the school was designed for roughly 964 students — a figure obtained by subtracting the new building's 750 seats from the final total of 1,714 and not stated outright by the agency.

The hokimiyat did not disclose the cost of the reconstruction, the school's exact address and district, or the layout of the new building — for example, whether it includes a gymnasium, cafeteria or assembly hall.

According to [the hokimiyat](https://tashkent.uz/ru/news/7384), the reconstruction has reached its final stage: exterior landscaping of the grounds and final interior finishing are currently underway. Builders are expected to complete the work by the start of the new school year, which traditionally begins in Uzbekistan on September 1. The hokimiyat did not give an exact date for the building's handover.
