Uzbekistan imported 72,000 tons of beef worth $359.5 million in January-June 2026 — up 9.1% year-on-year, the National Statistics Committee of the Republic of Uzbekistan [reported](https://stat.uz/ru/press-tsentr/novosti-goskomstata/69662-zbekistonga-mol-g-shti-ajsi-davlatlardan-import-ilinmo-da-3).

## Where the beef comes from

India is the main supplier of beef to the republic, at 33,900 tons, the National Statistics Committee [reported](https://t.me/uzstataxborot/69175) in its Telegram channel. That accounts for 47.1% of total imports for the half-year. Belarus ranks second with 19,600 tons (27.2%), followed by Kazakhstan with 10,600 tons (14.7%). Pakistan supplied 4,000 tons of beef (5.6%), and the remaining 4,000 tons (5.6%) came from other countries.

## Growth over last year

Beef import volume increased by 6,000 tons, or 9.1%, compared to the same period in 2025, the National Statistics Committee notes. The agency did not provide import value data broken down by individual supplier countries in the publication, nor did it specify the exchange rate used to calculate the dollar figure.
