The My.Gov.Uz public services portal this week [reminded](https://t.me/MyGovUz/3968) car owners that an application for a window tint permit can be submitted online in just two minutes — without visiting an office or using intermediaries; the portal itself issues the permit within two hours of payment.

## How to Get the Permit Through My.Gov.Uz

According to the [portal's](https://t.me/MyGovUz/3968) description, the process works as follows: log in to my.gov.uz or the MyGov app and find the service "Changing the Color of Vehicle Windows" (in the Uzbek-language interface — "Avto oynalari tusini o'zgartirish"). Next, select the vehicle — your own or one you hold power of attorney for — and pay for the service online. The permit, with a QR code, is generated automatically and stored in the personal account; there is no need to print it.

The service is provided by the [Road Traffic Safety Service (RTSS) of the Ministry of Internal Affairs of the Republic of Uzbekistan](https://my.gov.uz/uz/service/1058). According to the service description on my.gov.uz, no additional documents are required, and the permit is issued within [two hours](https://my.gov.uz/uz/service/1058) of payment. Both individuals and legal entities can apply.

## How Much the Permit Costs

The state fee is calculated in base calculation values (BCV), which for August 2026 stands at [412,000 soums](https://lex.uz/bhm). According to [my.gov.uz](https://my.gov.uz/uz/service/1058), when paid through the portal, the permit costs:

- for passenger cars — 2,966,400 soums (7.2 BCV, about $250 at the Central Bank of Uzbekistan exchange rate for August 6 — [11,886.72 soums per dollar](https://cbu.uz/ru/arkhiv-kursov-valyut/json/USD/2026-08-06/));
- for buses, minibuses and class-three trucks — 14,832,000 soums (36 BCV, about $1,250 at the same rate);
- for foreign legal entities — 5,191,200 soums (12.6 BCV, about $440 at the same rate).

## Not a New Service, but Timely in Summer

The option to obtain a tint permit through the unified portal has been available since 2021: [Cabinet of Ministers Resolution No. 465](https://lex.uz/docs/-5533443) of July 28, 2021, required the Ministry of Internal Affairs to automate the process by October 1 of that year. When paid through the portal, the permit costs 10% less than when processed offline.

This week's My.Gov.Uz post is not an announcement of a new feature but a reminder of an already existing service: the portal emphasizes that in the summer heat, motorists still lose time in lines or turn to intermediaries, even though the permit can be obtained independently from a phone.
