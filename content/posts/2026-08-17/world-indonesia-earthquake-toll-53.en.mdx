The death toll from the earthquake on Indonesia's Flores Island rose to at least 53 as of the evening of August 16 to the morning of August 17, Indonesia's National Disaster Management Agency (BNPB) [said](https://www.antaranews.com/berita/5697401/bnpb-umumkan-total-korban-meninggal-dunia-gempa-ntt-53-jiwa).

LEAP News earlier [reported](https://leap.uz/ru/2026/08/15/flores-earthquake-toll-38) that on the evening of August 15, BNPB put the death toll at 38 — it has since risen by another 15.

Manggarai district recorded the most deaths — 25 — with another 20 killed in East Manggarai, BNPB [said](https://www.antaranews.com/berita/5697401/bnpb-umumkan-total-korban-meninggal-dunia-gempa-ntt-53-jiwa). Eight more people died in Sikka, Ende, West Manggarai and Nagekeo districts. 135 people were injured, the AP [reported](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/rescuers-in-indonesia-recover-more-bodies-in-earthquake-aftermath-raising-death-toll-to-53). The magnitude 7.7 earthquake struck early Saturday, August 15, off the northern coast of Flores, in East Nusa Tenggara province.

## Aid on Independence Day

Indonesian authorities brought the total volume of humanitarian aid sent to affected areas to 52 tons, Cabinet Secretary Teddy Indra Wijaya [said](https://en.antaranews.com/news/427375/govt-sends-52-tons-of-aid-to-quake-hit-areas-of-e-nusa-tenggara). The latest shipment, weighing 25 tons, included 5 tons of ready-to-eat food, 4 tons of drinking water and 16 tons of grain. The cargo [arrived](https://en.antaranews.com/news/427375/govt-sends-52-tons-of-aid-to-quake-hit-areas-of-e-nusa-tenggara) in Labuan Bajo, Maumere and six other districts. Medicine and tents for the affected areas were sent as a separate shipment and are not included in this weight, Antara News [reported](https://en.antaranews.com/news/427375/govt-sends-52-tons-of-aid-to-quake-hit-areas-of-e-nusa-tenggara). Separately, the Indonesian Air Force airlifted 13 tons of cargo aboard two Hercules planes and one Boeing from El Tari Air Base in Kupang, Antara News [reported](https://en.antaranews.com/news/427349/ntt-quake-indonesia-air-force-airlifts-13-tons-of-emergency-aid).

The humanitarian operation coincided with Indonesia's 81st Independence Day celebrations on August 17. Residents of the affected areas complained about delays in aid delivery while festivities were underway in Jakarta, Euronews [reported](https://www.euronews.com/2026/08/17/thousands-in-indonesia-await-aid-after-quake-kills-53-overshadowing-independence-day).

## Aftershocks Continue

Since the main shock, seismologists have recorded 995 aftershocks, 46 of which were felt by residents, the AP [reported](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/rescuers-in-indonesia-recover-more-bodies-in-earthquake-aftermath-raising-death-toll-to-53). On Monday, August 17, a magnitude 5.9 aftershock struck near Flores, SCMP [reported](https://www.scmp.com/news/asia/southeast-asia/article/3364225/magnitude-59-aftershock-strikes-indonesias-flores-quake-death-toll-rises-53).

Rescuers still cannot reach parts of the hard-to-access areas of Manggarai, East Manggarai and Nagekeo, where landslides have blocked roads. Search operations continue there, and there have been no reports of missing persons so far, the AP [reported](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/rescuers-in-indonesia-recover-more-bodies-in-earthquake-aftermath-raising-death-toll-to-53). By the morning of August 17, the number of evacuees had risen to nearly 13,000, according to Euronews. An earthquake of the same magnitude, 7.7, struck Flores in 1992 — that quake and the tsunami it triggered killed about 2,500 people.
