Islam Makhachev's coach Javier Mendes and Irish fighter Conor McGregor backed Ian Machado Garry after he lost to Makhachev by unanimous decision at UFC 330 in Philadelphia on [August 15](https://www.ufc.com/event/ufc-330).

LEAP News covered the fight in detail in an earlier report — [Makhachev defended his UFC welterweight title and broke Silva's record](https://leap.uz/en/2026/08/17/makhachev-welterweight-record-ufc330).

## Reaction

Coach Mendes [publicly backed Garry](https://www.sherdog.com/news/news/Islam-Makhachevs-coach-backs-Ian-Garrys-No-1-UFC-ranking-after-loss-202405) after the fight, saying Garry had proven he deserved to remain the No. 1-ranked welterweight. "Ian Garry came out to show how tough he is and how much he deserves the No. 1 spot — and he proved it," Mendes said.

Garry's fellow Irishman Conor McGregor [praised his mindset](https://www.sherdog.com/news/news/Conor-McGregor-applauds-Ian-Garrys-response-to-UFC-330-defeat-202410) after the loss, commenting: "Champion mindset!" McGregor had earlier predicted Garry would knock out Makhachev.

## What's next

Makhachev named Brazilian fighter Carlos Prates as the most deserving next title challenger and [said he wants to fight again before Ramadan begins](https://www.sherdog.com/news/news/Islam-Makhachev-mocks-Max-Holloway-following-twobelt-jab-202407).

Prates, who was in attendance at UFC 330 in Philadelphia, [vowed to knock out the champion](https://www.sherdog.com/news/news/Carlos-Prates-has-a-chilling-promise-for-Islam-Makhachev-202411): "I'm going to knock you out, brother. I'm bringing that belt to Brazil — the first welterweight champion from Brazil." According to Prates, a fight with Makhachev could take place in December. In April, he plans to face undefeated Michael Morales, who served as the backup fighter at UFC 330.
