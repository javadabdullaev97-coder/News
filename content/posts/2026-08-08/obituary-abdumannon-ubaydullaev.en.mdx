Actor, director, teacher and professor at the State Institute of Arts and
Culture of Uzbekistan Abdumannon Ubaydullaev has died at the age of 86 — the
Ministry of Culture of Uzbekistan [announced](https://t.me/madaniyatvazirligi/60972)
on its Telegram channel on the evening of August 7.

## What is known

In its statement, the ministry described Ubaydullaev as an Honored Youth
Educator of Uzbekistan, a director and an actor. The agency
[offered condolences](https://t.me/madaniyatvazirligi/60972) to his family
and loved ones.

The institute where Ubaydullaev taught [added](https://t.me/dsmi_uz/58926) in
its own condolence message that he was a professor and held a Candidate of
Sciences degree in art history.

Ubaydullaev appeared in the films "Vatan," "Omad," "So'nggi lahza,"
"Hijron," "Nazar," "Yigit yig'lamasin," "Aldangan ko'ngil" and "Mehr,"
according to the ministry's [post](https://t.me/madaniyatvazirligi/60972).

Besides acting, he spent many years teaching at the institute's department
of sound engineering and camera work — where, according to
[the institute itself](https://t.me/dsmi_uz/58926), he trained a number of
specialists for Uzbek film and television.

## Date and details of the farewell

The Ministry of Culture did not specify the date, place or cause of death in
its statement, nor the time and place of the farewell ceremony. This
material will be updated as soon as this information appears in official
sources.
