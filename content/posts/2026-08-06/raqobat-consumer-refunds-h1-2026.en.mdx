The Competition Development and Consumer Rights Protection Committee of the Republic of Uzbekistan [reported](https://t.me/RaqobatGovUz/12538) on August 1 that it had processed 23,200 consumer complaints and protected the rights of 10.5 million people in the first half of 2026. Consumers were refunded around 59 billion soums — about $4.96 million at the [Central Bank of Uzbekistan exchange rate](https://cbu.uz/ru/arkhiv-kursov-valyut/json/USD/2026-08-06/) for August 6 (11,886.72 soums per dollar).

## Which areas the inspections covered

According to the committee's [publication](https://t.me/RaqobatGovUz/12538), the complaints concerned consumer rights protection, competition legislation, advertising, trade and services, utilities, communications, transport, and construction.

## Regional breakdown

The committee also publishes H1 results for individual regions. The Republic of Karakalpakstan received 827 complaints, and consumers were refunded more than 6.2 billion soums (≈$521,600), [according to a publication](https://t.me/RaqobatGovUz/12533) by the regional department. Andijan Region registered 1,935 complaints and refunded more than 761 million soums (≈$64,000), the committee [reported](https://t.me/RaqobatGovUz/12536).

Bukhara Region received 868 complaints, with refunds totaling more than 819.2 million soums (≈$68,900), [according to a post](https://t.me/RaqobatGovUz/12540) by the regional department. In Navoi Region, with more than 850 complaints, the refund amount reached about 15 billion soums (≈$1.26 million), the committee [reported](https://t.me/RaqobatGovUz/12541). Among the four published regional results, this is the largest refund amount.
