The Prosecutor General's Office of the Republic of Uzbekistan will inventory courtyard and playground construction across Tashkent, Saida Mirziyoyeva, head of the Presidential Administration of the Republic of Uzbekistan, announced on July 31.

## Visit to the Lolazor Mahalla

The trigger was a visit to Lolazor, a mahalla (neighborhood community) in the capital's Mirobod district. The post [states](https://t.me/SShMirziyoyeva/4148) that trees planted by residents had been destroyed there, and a business center was planned for the site of a playground.

"Urban spaces set aside for people's needs must serve only their intended purpose, not be used for the construction of offices or residential buildings," the post on Mirziyoyeva's channel says.

In the same post, she noted that residents of the capital are "rightly outraged by construction work that destroys their courtyards, playgrounds and sports grounds."

## What Will Be Checked

The inventory will [cover](https://t.me/SShMirziyoyeva/4148) similar sites across Tashkent. Mirziyoyeva listed three areas of review: the legality of issued construction permits, compliance with urban-planning norms, and respect for residents' rights.

"And if violations are found, those responsible must be punished, up to and including imprisonment," the post says.

The timeline for the inventory and the list of sites it will cover have not been disclosed. At the time this article was published, the Telegram channels of the Prosecutor General's Office and the Tashkent City Prosecutor's Office had not posted separate updates on the inventory's progress.

## What Was Planned for Lolazor in December 2025

In December 2025, the Tashkent City Hokimiyat press service [reported](https://t.me/poytaxt_uz/44868) that the Lolazor mahalla would get workout areas, football pitches and spaces for children's intellectual development.

The same work plan for Mirobod district included reconstructing the grounds around residential buildings in the At-Termiziy mahalla, building playgrounds, and improving the grounds around apartment blocks. According to the report, all work was to be carried out in stages through 2026 under the continuous oversight of responsible agencies.
