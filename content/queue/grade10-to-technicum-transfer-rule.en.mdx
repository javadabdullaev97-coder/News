The Cabinet of Ministers of the Republic of Uzbekistan adopted Resolution No. 450 on August 20, covering the transfer and reinstatement of technicum students, including 10th-grade schoolchildren — according to several media outlets; there has been no official confirmation as of publication.

The document, according to these reports, establishes a unified transfer and reinstatement procedure for all vocational education institutions — colleges and technicums. This includes 10th-grade schoolchildren, who will be able to switch to vocational training without waiting to finish 11th grade. Before Resolution No. 450, transfer rules varied among different vocational education institutions, according to media reports.

Applications for transfer or reinstatement, according to the same reports, will be accepted electronically — through Public Service Centers or the Unified Portal of Interactive Public Services. Government agencies, together with the Ministry of Digital Technologies, are reportedly required to set up application processing through the portal within two months of the resolution's date. If the deadline holds, applications will be accepted nationwide by the end of October 2026.

LEAP News will send an inquiry to the Cabinet's press service to confirm the details of Resolution No. 450; the article will be updated upon receiving a response.
