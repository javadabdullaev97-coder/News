US President Donald Trump on August 19 announced the "most crushing economic operation" ever against Iran and threatened massive consequences for any country that helps Tehran, [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/19/trump-announces-most-crushing-economic-operation-ever-against-iran) reports.

Trump published the statement on Truth Social. Iran faces "economic warfare and isolation on an unprecedented scale," the post said. Trump listed what must stop: "Oil smuggling, swap lines, money transfers, exchange offices, ship registries, shell companies — all of this must stop immediately." He called the operation itself an "economic D-Day": "This will be an economic D-Day, and we need all of our allies to stand with the United States."

Separately, Trump addressed countries helping Tehran: "Any country that allows its financial institutions, companies, airports or government entities to provide any support to Iran will itself face tremendous economic consequences," the post said, according to [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/19/trump-announces-most-crushing-economic-operation-ever-against-iran).

Central Bank of Iran Governor Abdolnasser Hemmati said on August 19 that the country's oil exports have fallen amid the war and sanctions, [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/20/tremendous-costs-can-trump-stop-other-countries-from-trading-with-iran) reports.

The naval blockade of Iranian ports, [as LEAP News reported](https://leap.uz/en/2026/08/17/iran-oman-hormuz-escalation), has been in place since late February 2026 — since the start of the US and Israeli war against Iran. On August 17, Trump threatened to bomb Oman if it interfered with the blockade.

## US calls on China to join the operation, Beijing urges diplomacy

US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent on August 20 called on China to join the operation. "Keep in mind that the Chinese get 50 percent of their energy from inside the Gulf. So it would behoove them to get on board with the program," he said. Bessent called the operation "the greatest coordinated economic isolation in the history of the world," [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/20/us-treasury-secretary-says-new-economic-measures-will-collapse-iran) reports.

Chinese Foreign Ministry spokesperson Lin Jian said: "China calls on relevant parties to take responsible measures and solve the issues through diplomatic and political means," [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/20/how-china-and-russia-could-hobble-trumps-plans-to-isolate-iran) reports.
