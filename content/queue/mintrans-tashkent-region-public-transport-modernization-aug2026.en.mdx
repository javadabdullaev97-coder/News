The Ministry of Transport on August 6 presented Tashkent region hokim Zoir Mirzayev with a comprehensive plan to modernize the public transport system in six cities of the region — Yangiyul, Angren, Akhangaran, Almalyk, Nurafshan and Chirchik.

## What Was Proposed

Priority tasks [outlined](https://t.me/Mintrans_uz/28590) in the presentation: upgrade public transport based on modern management principles, optimize the route network, efficiently organize transport corridors, and create modern stops and transport interchange hubs.

A separate area is the digitalization of management: the ministry plans to launch a unified digital registry that will track routes, bus movement and passenger flow in the six cities.

## Expected Impact

According to the Ministry of Transport's calculations, the comprehensive reorganization of the transport system should increase the number of daily public transport users in the six cities from 298,600 to 533,000 people. That is an increase of 234,400 passengers, or 78.5%.

The Ministry of Transport prepared projections for 2026, 2030 and 2035, but did not specify a start date for the work or the amount of funding.

## National Context

The presentation for Tashkent region comes amid a nationwide reform of urban passenger transport. The Cabinet of Ministers, by [Resolution No. 276](https://lex.uz/docs/-8244019) of June 3, 2026, introduced a zero recycling fee rate nationwide on imports of new buses, electric buses and trolleybuses of Euro-5 class or higher — the exemption is in effect through December 31, 2027.

The same resolution introduces, from September 1, 2026, an automated fare payment system — transport cards and contactless payments via NFC, QR codes and biometrics — and reclassifies as urban any routes extending 2 km or more beyond city limits. At the same time, the upper boundary of an "urban" route under approved city master plans has been raised to 12 km (up from 5 km).

## What's Next

The Ministry of Transport did not name a date for approving the plan and did not specify who will finance the purchase of vehicles and the construction of interchange hubs in the six cities. The Tashkent region hokimiyat has not yet commented on the outcome of the presentation.
