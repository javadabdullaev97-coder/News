Yemen's Houthi movement, Ansar Allah, launched a series of massive missile and drone strikes on August 7 against pro-government forces' positions in the eastern provinces of Marib and Hadramawt and on Saudi Arabian territory.

## Conflicting Death Tolls

Casualty figures for the strike on the "Sahn al-Jin" camp in Marib province differ among all sides. According to AFP data, [reported by CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/live-updates/iran-war-yemen-saudi-arabia-houthis-donald-trump-deadly-attacks/), at least 58 government troops were killed. Yemen's Joint Operations Command, however, confirmed only 17 troop deaths. According to an official statement from Yemeni authorities [cited by Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/7/houthi-attacks-on-govt-forces-hint-that-a-major-battle-in-yemen-is-brewing), 30 troops were killed and another 15 wounded. The Houthis themselves, through movement spokesman Yahya Saree, claimed "hundreds" of pro-government fighters were killed — a figure not independently confirmed by any side.

[Vedomosti](https://www.vedomosti.ru/politics/news/2026/08/07/1219676-husiti-atakovali-bazu) and [Kommersant](https://www.kommersant.ru/doc/8875175), citing Saree's statement carried by the Houthi-run Al-Masira TV channel, report that missiles and drones were launched at the camp in Marib.

## Strikes on Residential Areas of Marib

In addition to military targets, the Houthis shelled residential neighborhoods and camps for internally displaced people in the city of Marib — Yemen's largest refugee hosting center. According to Yemen's government and Health Minister Qasem Bahaiba, [cited by Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/7/two-civilians-killed-in-houthi-strike-on-marib-says-yemens-government), two civilians were killed and another 14 wounded. Yemen's Ministry of Defense said several Houthi drones were shot down over the city.

Bahaiba called the Houthis a "terrorist group." Yemen's government said "the deliberate shelling of safe residential neighborhoods and camps for displaced people... constitutes a grave violation of international humanitarian law."

## Shelling Continued the Next Day

According to Al Jazeera and CBS News, shelling of Marib resumed on August 8 as well. At least 10 deaths are reported (8 troops and 2 civilians) along with 12–14 wounded. At the same time, Saudi Arabia's border province of Najran came under attack from Yemeni territory. Eleven civilians were wounded, including a four-year-old child with second-degree burns, [CBS News reports](https://www.cbsnews.com/live-updates/iran-war-yemen-saudi-arabia-houthis-donald-trump-deadly-attacks/).

Houthi spokesman Yahya Saree said the strikes were a response to an "observed large-scale military mobilization" by the Saudi side, describing the operation itself as a "large-scale and special military operation."

## A Fragile Truce and the US-Israel War on Iran

A UN-brokered truce between Yemen's government and the Houthis has been in place since April 2022 and had largely held until the latest escalation, [Al Jazeera reports](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/7/houthi-strikes-hit-yemen-as-saudi-arabia-braces-for-attacks-what-we-know). The situation deteriorated sharply after the United States and Israel went to war against Iran on February 28, 2026. The Houthis, allies of Tehran, have since ramped up their own attacks on Saudi Arabia and in the Red Sea.

On July 20, the Houthis declared a naval blockade of Saudi Arabia. That same day, according to Al Jazeera, they struck Saudi Aramco facilities in Jizan and Yanbu. The Houthis also reported strikes on oil infrastructure — including the Saudi tanker NCC Ghazal, which movement spokesman Yahya Saree announced last week, [Al Jazeera notes](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/7/houthi-strikes-hit-yemen-as-saudi-arabia-braces-for-attacks-what-we-know). Analyst Ahmed Nagi of the International Crisis Group told Al Jazeera that Iran's support for the Houthis has "become far more visible" than before.

Gulf Cooperation Council Secretary-General Jasem Mohamed Albudaiwi called the strikes a "flagrant violation of international law," [Al Jazeera reports](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/7/houthi-strikes-hit-yemen-as-saudi-arabia-braces-for-attacks-what-we-know). A source close to the Saudi military said the coalition "will not stand idle" amid the escalation, [CBS News reports](https://www.cbsnews.com/live-updates/iran-war-yemen-saudi-arabia-houthis-donald-trump-deadly-attacks/).

## What Comes Next

On August 7, Saudi Arabia signed a trilateral defense pact with Turkey and Pakistan amid the escalating situation, CBS News reports. Analysts interviewed by Al Jazeera do not rule out that the confrontation could escalate into a major battle: according to expert Ahmed Nagi, "the threat of a full-scale war between the sides no longer looks distant." Sheikh Osman Majali of Yemen's Presidential Leadership Council said achieving peace requires "confronting and overthrowing" the Houthi movement.
