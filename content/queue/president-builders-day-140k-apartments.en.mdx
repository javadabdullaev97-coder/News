President Shavkat Mirziyoyev congratulated construction workers on their professional holiday on August 8 and said that Uzbekistan plans to [commission 140,000 new apartments](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9489) in 2026.

## What the greeting says

Over the past nine years, the country has [built structures and facilities with a total area of more than 210 million square meters](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9489), including residential buildings comprising 647,000 apartments. Of these, 135,000 apartments were completed in 2025.

The construction industry, building materials production and related sectors currently employ more than 3.5 million people, the greeting says. According to the president, the industry is undergoing reforms: [the number of procedures for obtaining construction permits has been cut threefold, and the time required to process them has been cut fourfold](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9489). The greeting also says that 24 electronic platforms for the digital management of construction processes have been launched.

## Plans through 2040

The president said that by 2040 authorities intend to raise the annual volume of housing completions to [280,000 apartments](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9489) — twice the figure expected in 2026.

A year earlier, in his [greeting to builders on August 9, 2025](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/8369), the president cited different targets. Construction output grew to nearly 234 trillion soums in 2024, and 135,000 apartments were planned for completion in 2025. The same figure now appears in the 2026 greeting as the actual figure for 2025.

[Builders' Day in Uzbekistan is celebrated annually on the second Sunday of August](https://t.me/Mintrans_uz/28617). The [Ministry of Transport](https://t.me/Mintrans_uz/28617) and the [Tashkent hokimiyat](https://t.me/poytaxt_uz/51311) also published greetings to builders on this day.
