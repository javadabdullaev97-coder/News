Tashkent's Center for the Organization of Road Traffic (TCORT) said it plans to complete the first stage of construction of a dedicated lane for accelerated bus movement — bus rapid transit, or BRT — on Shahrisabz Street by August 31, 2026.

The 1.2-kilometer section runs from the ring junction at the intersection of Shahrisabz, Mirobod and Shota Rustaveli streets to Amir Temur Avenue, [TCORT said](https://t.me/codd_tashkent/305). Work on the section has been underway since July 24.

Crews are fully rebuilding the road surface rather than simply repaving it. Soil is being excavated to a depth of more than 2 meters, and geotextile — a material that separates the base layers — is being laid between them. This is meant to protect the pavement from deformation under the weight of buses, the agency said. Six bus stops will be built along the section.

This is the city's second dedicated BRT bus lane. The first opened in October 2025 on Shota Rustaveli Street. On the reconstructed section, which spans nearly 7 kilometers, passenger traffic rose from 2,500 to 6,000 people per hour, [the presidential press service said](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/8570).
