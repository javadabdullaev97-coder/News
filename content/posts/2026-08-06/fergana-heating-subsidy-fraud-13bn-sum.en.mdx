The Department for Combating Economic Crimes under the Prosecutor General's Office of the Republic of Uzbekistan reported on August 3 that it had opened a criminal case over inflated volumes of heat energy reported as supplied to residents of Fergana region. As a result, the region's heat supply company received 12.9 billion soums in excess subsidies from the budget.

## What the Investigation Found

According to [the Department's statement](https://t.me/Department_uz/12937), officials at Fergana region's Issiqlik Taminoti heating company reported to the regional Department of Economy and Finance that it had supplied residents with 212.3 thousand Gcal of heat energy in 2024–2025 and January–May 2026. The audit found that residents actually received only 85.7 thousand Gcal — almost 2.5 times less than reported.

The discrepancy allowed the company to receive an additional 12.9 billion soums in subsidies from the budget. At the [Central Bank of Uzbekistan's exchange rate](https://cbu.uz/ru/arkhiv-kursov-valyut/json/USD/2026-08-03/) for August 3 — 11,960.18 soums per dollar — that is about $1.08 million. The Department opened a criminal case over the incident but did not name the specific articles of the Criminal Code, or the names and positions of the suspects. The investigation is ongoing.

## Context: Other Department Cases in July–August

The Fergana case is not the first the Department has reported in recent days. On July 31, the agency [reported](https://t.me/Department_uz/12928) the embezzlement of 19.9 billion soums (about $1.66 million at the Central Bank of Uzbekistan's exchange rate for July 31 — 12,006.39 soums per dollar) in gas meter servicing in Tashkent, and on August 3 — the [detention](https://t.me/Department_uz/12938) of a branch head of JSC Andijonsuvtaminot on suspicion of taking an $8,500 bribe for a sewage connection.

## What's Next

The Department did not give a timeline for completing the review of the Fergana case and did not say whether specific officials have been held accountable. Until a court verdict takes legal effect, all those involved in the case are presumed innocent.
