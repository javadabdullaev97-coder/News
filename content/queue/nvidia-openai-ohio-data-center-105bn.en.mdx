Nvidia on August 17 [guaranteed](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-guarantees-sb-energy-s-ports-pike-technology-campus-in-ohio-to-exclusively-host-nvidia-ai-compute) up to $105 billion in financial support for OpenAI's data center at the PORTS-Pike campus in Pike County, Ohio, and will invest $1.5 billion in developer SB Energy, [TechCrunch reported](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/17/nvidia-investing-1-5b-in-softbank-data-center-developer-behind-openai-project/).

The guarantee does not cover the full cost of construction. Nvidia [is taking on](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/securing-the-infrastructure-of-intelligence/) part of the payments for land lease and electricity, as well as an obligation to compensate for the residual value of the first-phase capacity. The mechanism is known as a residual value guarantee — it reduces risks for the project's lenders. It applies to the facility's first phase — 4.25 gigawatts of IT capacity, meaning electricity that goes directly to servers, not counting cooling and distribution networks.

Besides the guarantee, Nvidia will directly invest $1.5 billion in SB Energy, the campus developer controlled by Japan's SoftBank Group. In exchange, [Nvidia becomes](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-guarantees-sb-energy-s-ports-pike-technology-campus-in-ohio-to-exclusively-host-nvidia-ai-compute) the exclusive supplier of computing infrastructure across the entire site.

OpenAI [leased](https://the-decoder.com/openai-signs-record-ohio-data-center-lease-with-nvidia-backing-up-to-105-billion/) the campus's capacity for 20 years. The site's total capacity could grow from 4.25 to 8 gigawatts, TechCrunch reported. The campus is being built on the site of the former [Portsmouth Gaseous Diffusion Plant](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-guarantees-sb-energy-s-ports-pike-technology-campus-in-ohio-to-exclusively-host-nvidia-ai-compute), a uranium enrichment facility that belonged to the U.S. Department of Energy. The project is being carried out jointly with utility AEP Ohio and the U.S. Department of Commerce. The first phase is expected to come online in stages starting in 2028.

## What Pike County Gets

OpenAI and SB Energy will [direct](https://openai.com/index/openai-joins-ports-pike-project) $80 million to a local community support fund — $40 million from each company. Building the campus will create 35,000 jobs over six years, through 2032, OpenAI said. Once operational, the site will employ 2,500 people permanently.

## The AI Industry's Hidden Liabilities

The deal fits into a broader picture. According to Wall Street Journal data cited by [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/openai-signs-record-ohio-data-center-lease-with-nvidia-backing-up-to-105-billion/), nine technology companies carry about $3 trillion in off-balance-sheet AI-related liabilities. They include Alphabet, Meta, Microsoft and Nvidia itself. Of that amount, $1.2 trillion is tied to leases that have not yet begun — four times more than a year earlier, the outlet noted.
