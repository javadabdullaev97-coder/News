Colombian President Abelardo de la Espriella on August 17 put direct damage from the August 10 earthquake at 30 trillion pesos, about $9.6 billion — he made the statement at a press conference in Bogotá, [Reuters reports](https://www.freemalaysiatoday.com/category/world/2026/08/18/colombia-s-president-pegs-early-quake-losses-at-us-9-6bil).

The magnitude-7.4 earthquake struck Colombia's western Chocó department on August 10. By August 17, the death toll had risen to 289, with 4,187 injured, [Al Jazeera reports](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/17/as-rubble-clears-colombia-begins-focusing-on-recovery-after-the-earthquake), citing an official government tally. A day earlier, authorities had cited the same 289 dead and 143 missing, with the number of injured estimated at 3,937, [PBS NewsHour noted](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/colombian-president-asks-trump-to-suspend-tariffs-to-help-earthquake-recovery). LEAP News previously reported that [the death toll had exceeded 280 as of August 15](https://leap.uz/en/2026/08/15/colombia-earthquake-death-toll-280).

Of the 30 trillion pesos announced by the president, 24.5 trillion went to buildings and 5.5 trillion to infrastructure, [Reuters reports](https://www.freemalaysiatoday.com/category/world/2026/08/18/colombia-s-president-pegs-early-quake-losses-at-us-9-6bil). "This is a preliminary figure that will be adjusted as new information comes in and damage is assessed in each region," de la Espriella said. A week earlier, the finance minister had put damage at no less than $6.4 billion, [Al Jazeera reported](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/17/as-rubble-clears-colombia-begins-focusing-on-recovery-after-the-earthquake); the estimate has since grown by almost half. More than 14,700 homes were destroyed and over 81,500 more damaged, [PBS NewsHour reported on August 16](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/colombian-president-asks-trump-to-suspend-tariffs-to-help-earthquake-recovery), citing Colombian authorities.

## President Asked Trump to Suspend Tariffs

De la Espriella on Saturday, August 15, asked US President Donald Trump to temporarily suspend high tariffs on Colombian goods. The goal is to help businesses hit by the earthquake, [PBS NewsHour reports](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/colombian-president-asks-trump-to-suspend-tariffs-to-help-earthquake-recovery). The Trump administration did not immediately respond. The US has already allocated $26.5 million to Colombia for food, temporary shelter and medicine, the outlet notes.

## Housing Minister's Vacation Sparks Outrage

Housing Minister Jaime Andrés Beltrán spent the weekend with his family at the Caribbean resort of Santa Marta — beach photos circulated on social media, [South China Morning Post reports](https://www.scmp.com/news/world/americas/article/3364351/colombian-ministers-caribbean-holiday-amid-earthquake-crisis-stokes-fury). The left-wing opposition said it intends to initiate a no-confidence vote against the minister, calling the trip inappropriate amid the ongoing crisis.
