IT Park Uzbekistan is accepting applications for the "Digital Uzbekistan: Tech Mission Germany 2026" program until August 3 — startups will travel to Germany from September 15 to 18.

## About the Mission

IT Park Uzbekistan — a technology park overseeing the development of the country's IT industry — has [announced](https://t.me/itpark_uz/20556) recruitment for a business trip to Germany. The delegation will visit Berlin, Wiesbaden and Frankfurt am Main, where it will take part in four business events. These include the dedicated Digital Uzbekistan forum, the Schwarz Digits Partner Summit and STACKIT's Cloud X Summit, IT Park [said](https://www.facebook.com/itparkuzb/posts/meet-germanys-biggest-b2b-tech-players-in-personit-park-uzbekistan-is-taking-a-s/1940128580528883/) on its Facebook page.

## Who Can Apply and How

Companies ready to enter the European Union market are invited to participate: startups with an already validated product, service companies looking for clients in Europe, and teams with a ready foreign market entry strategy and marketing plan, the organizer's [announcement](https://www.facebook.com/itparkuzb/posts/-ready-to-scale-across-the-eu-start-with-germany-applications-are-now-open-for-d/1937682754106799/) said. Applications are accepted through the [Startup Base](https://startupbase.uz/uz/events/digital-uzbekistan-germany) platform — the deadline is August 3.

## What Participants Will Get

Selected companies will give presentations at the four business events, hold B2B meetings with potential clients and partners, and gain access to networking with the European tech community. According to IT Park, more than 200 corporations, investors and venture funds will gather at the mission's venues in Germany.

## Context

The trip to Germany is not IT Park's first business mission in 2026. In June, the Uzbek delegation [took part](https://www.facebook.com/itparkuzb/posts/it-park-uzbekistan-concludes-its-business-mission-in-poland-at-absl-summit-2026o/1910600793481662/) in the ABSL Summit 2026 in Poland and held meetings with at least eight companies, including Proservartner and Brave AI Enterprise, as part of a week-long business tour of Gdańsk and Warsaw.
