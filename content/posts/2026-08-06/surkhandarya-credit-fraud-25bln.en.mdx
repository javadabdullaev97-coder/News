The Department for Combating Economic Crimes under the Prosecutor General's Office of Uzbekistan reported on August 5 that it had uncovered a scheme in Surkhandarya region to steal a 25 billion soum bank loan.

## How the Scheme Worked

According to [the Department's statement](https://t.me/Department_uz/12949), the entrepreneur's buildings and structures were already pledged as collateral at one of the commercial banks in Surkhandarya region under an active loan agreement with another company. According to the investigation, the entrepreneur, together with accomplices, produced forged documents. Using them, he pledged the same property a second time — this time at a bank in Andijan region. Based on these documents, the bank issued a loan of 25 billion soums — about $2.09 million at the [Central Bank of Uzbekistan exchange rate](https://cbu.uz/ru/arkhiv-kursov-valyut/json/USD/2026-08-05/) for August 5 (11,942.21 soums per dollar). According to the investigation, the funds received were stolen.

## Criminal Case Opened, Investigation Ongoing

A criminal case has been opened over the incident. The Department did not name the suspects or their positions, nor did it specify the exact articles of the Criminal Code under which the act was classified. The investigation is ongoing; until a court verdict enters into legal force, all persons involved in the case are presumed innocent.

## Context: Other Department Cases in July–August

The Surkhandarya case is not the first the Department has reported in recent days. On August 3, the agency [said](https://t.me/Department_uz/12937) that a company in Fergana region had overstated the volume of thermal energy it supplied. This allowed it to obtain 12.9 billion soums (≈$1.08 million at the Central Bank of Uzbekistan exchange rate for August 3 — 11,960.18 soums per dollar) in excess subsidies from the budget. And on July 31, the Department [reported](https://t.me/Department_uz/12928) the theft of 19.9 billion soums (≈$1.66 million at the Central Bank of Uzbekistan exchange rate for July 31 — 12,006.39 soums per dollar) during gas meter servicing in Tashkent.
