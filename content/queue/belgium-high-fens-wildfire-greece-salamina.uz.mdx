Belgiyaning sharqidagi Yuqori Fenn qo'riqxonasidagi yong'in mamlakat tarixidagi eng yirik yong'inga aylandi va 16-avgustda Germaniya chegarasiga yetib keldi — taxminan 600 kishi evakuatsiya qilindi.

Yong'in qo'riqxonaning 27 kvadrat kilometrdan ortiq vereska dashtlari va torfzorlarini qamrab oldi, [Al Jazeera xabar beradi](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/16/belgiums-largest-wildfire-on-record-spreads-towards-german-border). Bu 2011-yilgi yong'in maydonidan katta — Belgiyaning o'sha hududdagi avvalgi rekordi 14 kvadrat kilometrni tashkil etgan edi.

Yong'in 14-avgust, juma kuni boshlangan. Shanba kuniga kelib hokimiyat Byutgenbax shaharchasi va Vaymes jamoasi aholisini evakuatsiya qilishga qaror qildi — ular Malmedidagi sport zaliga joylashtirildi, [Euronews xabar beradi](http://ru.euronews.com/my-europe/2026/08/16/fires-europe-belguim-germany-france).

Belgiya Yevropa Ittifoqining fuqarolik muhofazasi mexanizmini ishga soldi. Yong'inni o'chirish uchun Belgiya, Germaniya, Lyuksemburg va Niderlandiyadan 250 dan ortiq o't o'chiruvchi va qutqaruvchi yuborildi, [Al Jazeera xabar beradi](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/16/belgiums-largest-wildfire-on-record-spreads-towards-german-border).

«Yuqori Fenn yonmoqda, Belgiya bunga yolg'iz qarshi turmaydi», — dedi YEIning inqirozlarni boshqarish bo'yicha komissari Hadja Lahbib, [Associated Press xabar beradi](https://www.news4jax.com/news/world/2026/08/16/firefighters-battle-a-massive-belgian-wildfire-that-forced-hundreds-to-evacuate/).

Belgiyaga Shvetsiya, Niderlandiya va Chexiyadan ikkita suv tashiydigan samolyot va uchta vertolyot yetib keldi, [Euronews xabar beradi](http://ru.euronews.com/my-europe/2026/08/16/fires-europe-belguim-germany-france). Shvetsiya ikkita sisterna-samolyot, Niderlandiya ikkita vertolyot, Chexiya esa bittasini yubordi, [Associated Press aniqlashtiradi](https://www.news4jax.com/news/world/2026/08/16/firefighters-battle-a-massive-belgian-wildfire-that-forced-hundreds-to-evacuate/).

Yong'in Germaniyaning Monshau shahriga taxminan 3 kilometr masofagacha yetib keldi. Tunda hokimiyat tutun tufayli uchta chekka tuman aholisiga vaqtincha uylarini tark etishni tavsiya qildi, [Al Jazeera xabar beradi](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/16/belgiums-largest-wildfire-on-record-spreads-towards-german-border). Ilgari evakuatsiya qilingan Gay qishlog'ining taxminan 2 ming aholisi shanba kuni kechqurun uylariga qaytdi, [Euronews xabar beradi](http://ru.euronews.com/my-europe/2026/08/16/fires-europe-belguim-germany-france).

Belgiya ichki ishlar vaziri Bernard Kanten yong'in xizmati xodimlari olovning Germaniya chegarasi tomon yurishiga yo'l qo'ymagani va Monshauni himoya qilganini aytdi, [Associated Press xabar beradi](https://www.news4jax.com/news/world/2026/08/16/firefighters-battle-a-massive-belgian-wildfire-that-forced-hundreds-to-evacuate/).

## Salamindagi yong'in: ikki kishi halok bo'ldi

Afinaning g'arbidagi Gretsiyaning Salamin orolida 16-avgustda yong'in ikki kishining hayotiga zomin bo'ldi — ularning jasadlari Peristeriya qishlog'idan topildi, [Associated Press xabar beradi](https://www.news4jax.com/news/world/2026/08/16/firefighters-battle-a-massive-belgian-wildfire-that-forced-hundreds-to-evacuate/). Halok bo'lganlar, ko'rinishidan, er-xotin bo'lgan, yana kuygan besh kishi tibbiy punktga yetkazildi, [Reuters xabar beradi](https://whtc.com/2026/08/16/two-die-as-wildfires-sweep-greek-island-of-salamina/).

Salamindagi yong'inni o'chirishga o't o'chiruvchilar, samolyotlar va vertolyotlar jalb qilindi. [AP](https://www.news4jax.com/news/world/2026/08/16/firefighters-battle-a-massive-belgian-wildfire-that-forced-hundreds-to-evacuate/) va [Reuters](https://whtc.com/2026/08/16/two-die-as-wildfires-sweep-greek-island-of-salamina/)ning turlicha ma'lumotlariga ko'ra, ularning soni 192 dan 260 tagacha bo'lgan (16-avgust, yakshanba kuni kechki holat bo'yicha).

Selini hududida ikkinchi yong'in o'chog'i paydo bo'ldi — u yerdan sohil qo'riqlash va o't o'chirish qayiqlari plyajlardagi dam oluvchilarni evakuatsiya qildi, [Associated Press xabar beradi](https://www.news4jax.com/news/world/2026/08/16/firefighters-battle-a-massive-belgian-wildfire-that-forced-hundreds-to-evacuate/). Gretsiya o't o'chirish xizmati vakili brigada generali Vasilios Vatrakoyannis kuchli shamolda olov yetti daqiqada turar-joy kvartallariga o'tib ketganini aytdi, [Associated Press xabar beradi](https://www.news4jax.com/news/world/2026/08/16/firefighters-battle-a-massive-belgian-wildfire-that-forced-hundreds-to-evacuate/).

Yong'inlar Gretsiyada yozgi turistik mavsum cho'qqisiga to'g'ri keldi. Yakshanba 15-avgustdagi pravoslav Muqaddas Bibi Maryamning vafoti (Uspeniye) bayramidan keyingi dam olish kuniga to'g'ri keldi — bu yozning eng band dam olish kuni edi, [Reuters xabar beradi](https://whtc.com/2026/08/16/two-die-as-wildfires-sweep-greek-island-of-salamina/).

Belgiya va Gretsiyadagi yong'inlar Yevropa bo'ylab kengroq yong'inlar to'lqinining bir qismi. Frantsiyaning Land mintaqasida yong'in davom etmoqda (1700 gektardan ortiq maydon, 550 o't o'chiruvchi), Ispaniyaning Ueska provinsiyasida esa 16 600 gektargacha maydon kuygan, [Euronews xabar beradi](http://ru.euronews.com/my-europe/2026/08/16/fires-europe-belguim-germany-france).
