Chairperson of the Senate Tanzila Narbaeva and Deputy Prime Minister and Chairperson of the Committee for Family and Women's Affairs Dilnozakhon Kattakhanova [held](https://gov.uz/oz/news/view/203221) an open dialogue in Tashkent on August 7 on the outcomes of work with families and women.

## Outcomes: door-to-door talks in 1,137 mahallas

Following five priority areas previously identified by the head of state, authorities [held](https://gov.uz/oz/news/view/203221) door-to-door talks in 1,137 mahallas across the country, involving more than 2.3 million women. Representatives of the Federation of Trade Unions and women activists from the capital also took part in the open dialogue.

Participants discussed protecting women's rights, boosting their socio-economic activity and providing targeted assistance to women in difficult life situations, the Government Portal of Uzbekistan [reported](https://gov.uz/oz/news/view/203221).

## New initiatives

Participants at the dialogue [proposed](https://gov.uz/oz/news/view/203221) integrating the system for registering women in need of support with healthcare, education and statistics databases, which should speed up the identification of such cases at the local level.

The Committee also announced a retraining system for women returning from parental leave and the creation of an electronic platform for enrolling in training courses. Separately, participants [announced](https://gov.uz/oz/news/view/203221) plans to organize the "Zulfiyaxonim izdoshlari" forum and to more actively involve girls in sports.

## Instructions following the dialogue

Tanzila Narbaeva [instructed](https://gov.uz/oz/news/view/203221) officials to strengthen divorce prevention efforts and to work on reintegrating women who have returned from labor migration. The Senate chairperson also proposed extending Tashkent's approach to working with families and women to other regions of the country.
