Zoir Mirzayev, hokim of Tashkent region, and Radiy Khabirov, head of the Republic of Bashkortostan, held an online meeting on August 3 and discussed speeding up implementation of seven joint investment projects worth a combined $235.7 million.

## What Was Agreed

The parties discussed priority areas of trade-economic, investment and industrial cooperation, [according to a post on the Tashkent Region Hokimiyat's Telegram channel](https://t.me/toshvilpressa/64031). According to the hokimiyat, Tashkent region and Bashkortostan are currently implementing seven investment projects worth a combined $235.7 million, which are meant to boost the economic potential of both regions and create new production capacity and jobs.

Meeting participants separately discussed development of the technopark in Bekabad, the creation of joint industrial enterprises, the expansion of trade ties, and cooperation in agriculture, tourism and sports. The parties agreed to speed up implementation of ongoing projects.

## The Bekabad Technopark

One of the cooperation projects is a technopark in the Bekabad district of Tashkent region, which is meant to host Bashkir companies as residents.

The technopark's management company — Bashkir Investment Management Company LLC — [had stated](https://tp-bekobod.uz/novosti/bashkortostan-i-tashkentskaya-oblast-uzbekistana-pristupayut-k-realizaczii-sovmestnyix-proektov-v-sootvetstvii-s-porucheniyami-glavyi-respublik.html) that construction work on the technopark grounds was planned to begin in April 2026. The parties did not disclose the current status of construction as of the August 3 meeting.

## Context

Mirzayev and Khabirov hold regular online meetings on joint projects: the previous one took place on December 29, 2025, the Tashkent Region Hokimiyat [reported](https://gov.uz/oz/toshvil/news/view/115239) at the time.
