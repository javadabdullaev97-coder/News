The Department for Combating Economic Crimes under the Prosecutor General's Office of Uzbekistan said on August 5 that it had shut down an underground production facility for fake cosmetics and household chemicals in the Zangiata district of Tashkent region.

## What Was Found at the Site

According to [the department's statement](https://t.me/Department_uz/12968), the underground workshop operated in the Nayman mahalla of Zangiata district. The inspection seized 2 tons of liquid disguised as the cleaning product "AZELIT" and 200 liters of liquid disguised as "CLEAR" shampoo. Inspectors also found 4,800 counterfeit bottles labeled "Elseve," 2,040 containers labeled "AZELIT," and 1,400 empty "CLEAR" bottles. In addition, they discovered 79 sheets of fake labels — according to the department, they imitated six trademarks, including the brands listed above.

## Who Is Behind the Workshop

The workshop was organized by two citizens — the department's statement gives only their initials, N.Sh. and Q.N. According to the agency, one of them has a prior conviction. The department opened a criminal case under Article 276-11 of the Criminal Code. According to the department, this article establishes liability for producing, storing, transporting or selling products that do not meet safety requirements ([text of the code](https://lex.uz/docs/111457)). The department did not disclose the suspects' full names or occupations; the investigation continues.

According to investigators, the finished products were disguised as well-known cosmetics and household chemical brands and could have reached retail sale. The department did not specify how much of the product had already been sold or for what amount.

## What's Next

The department did not specify a timeframe for completing the inquiry. Until a court verdict takes effect, all individuals involved in the case are presumed innocent.
