Tashkent has been switching on bus air conditioners in phases since May 7: whenever the outdoor temperature exceeds +28°C, cooling systems are required to run, and the temperature inside the bus must stay at +26°C.

## What the Rule Requires

The rule was [set](https://t.me/toshdeptransport/7615) by the Tashkent Main Department of Transport and Road Infrastructure Development: whenever the outdoor air temperature exceeds +28°C, bus cooling systems must run, and the indoor temperature must stay at +26°C.

A driver may switch off the air conditioner temporarily, at the request of elderly passengers or passengers traveling with small children. Inspectors from the state agency monitor compliance with the rule.

## Who Checks and What They Find

Monitoring is handled by the state agency for monitoring and control of passenger transport in Tashkent (the agency has not published an official English name; this is a working translation from Uzbek). On July 15, the Main Department of Transport [reported](https://t.me/toshdeptransport/7706) that inspections of cooling systems and compliance with the minimum requirements are being carried out regularly.

The monitoring found cases of violations of technical and sanitary standards; reports were filed requiring the violations to be corrected. The department called on carriers to take service standards more seriously.

## Where to Report Issues

Toshshahartranshizmat JSC operates a hotline — [1062](https://t.me/tshtxuz/8101).

The second channel is the transport department itself: phone +998 71 202-05-00 (extension 204) and email toshkent@mintrans.uz, [listed](https://toshkent.mintrans.uz/about) on the agency's official website. The department runs the [Telegram channel @toshdeptransport](https://t.me/toshdeptransport), where it posts tender announcements and inspection reports.

## What's Next

The department is holding open electronic tenders to assign carriers to city routes. The next tenders are on [August 15](https://t.me/toshdeptransport/7710) for routes 79 (TTZ bus station — Beruniy metro station) and 102 (Chorsu shopping center — Chilanzar metro station), and on [August 18](https://t.me/toshdeptransport/7714) for route 84 (Obod Turmush mahalla — Gulobod Street bus station). Applications are submitted through the e-tender.uztrans.uz platform.
