South Korea's Samsung Electronics on August 21 disclosed a plan to return 90 trillion to 110 trillion won ($65 billion to $80 billion) to shareholders in 2026, [CNBC reported](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/21/samsung-shareholder-return-package-sk-hynix-buyback-ai-chip-boom.html), citing a company statement.

The company will pay about 30 trillion won in cash dividends for the third quarter of 2026, [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/21/samsung-shareholder-return-package-sk-hynix-buyback-ai-chip-boom.html) said. That is roughly $22 billion — here and elsewhere, LEAP News converts figures using an exchange rate of about 1,360 won per dollar as of August 21. The new figure is part of Samsung's three-year program for 2024-2026. According to media reports, the program calls for directing up to half of cumulative free cash flow — the funds remaining after operating and capital expenses — to shareholders. Several outlets estimate the company has already returned about 29 trillion won through dividends and share buybacks over 2024-2025. The final amount for 2026 will depend on the company's financial results, CNBC noted.

CNBC's headline describes the figure as payouts of "up to $80 billion" — the upper end of the 90-110 trillion won range. [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-20/samsung-plans-as-much-as-79-billion-in-shareholder-returns) puts the program's ceiling at $79 billion.

## Context: SK Hynix's Record Buyback

Samsung's decision followed an announcement from its memory chip market rival, SK Hynix. The company's board of directors on August 19 approved a buyback and full retirement of treasury shares worth 40 trillion won, [SK hynix said](https://news.skhynix.com/en/share-buyback-and-retirement/) in a statement. That is about $29 billion at the same exchange rate. The company calls it the largest treasury share retirement in the history of South Korean public companies.

The buyback will cover about 24.07 million shares — roughly 3.3% of SK Hynix's total outstanding shares. The calculation is based on the 1,662,000 won closing price on the eve of the board's decision. The program will run for about three months starting August 20. At the same time, SK Hynix expanded its shareholder return target: instead of "no more than 50%" of cumulative free cash flow for 2025-2027, the company now intends to direct "more than 50%." SK Hynix's net cash position stood at about 69 trillion won at the end of the second quarter of 2026.

In its statement, the company attributes the decision to the market undervaluing its business. "The company's intrinsic value — reflecting the competitiveness of the business, its sustained ability to generate cash flow and its medium-term growth potential — is not fully reflected in the current share price," the statement said. SK Hynix shares rose more than 12% on the Seoul exchange after the buyback announcement, [CNBC reported](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/20/sk-hynixs-south-korean-shares-surge-stock-buyback-.html) on August 20.

## What's Next

SK Hynix's share buyback will run for about three months from August 20 — the program is expected to conclude around mid-November 2026.
