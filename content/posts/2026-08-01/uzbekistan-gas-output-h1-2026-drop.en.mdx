The Statistics Agency under the President of the Republic of Uzbekistan said on July 28 that natural gas output in January-June 2026 totaled [18.3 billion cubic meters](https://stat.uz/img/press-relizlar/press-reliz-sanoat-07-2026-rus_p46063.pdf) — 16% less than a year earlier.

## Output Down 4.2 Billion Cubic Meters Over Two Years

In January-June 2025, the country produced 21.9 billion cubic meters of gas, and in the same period of 2024 — [22.5 billion](https://stat.uz/img/press-relizlar/press-reliz-sanoat-07-2026-rus_p46063.pdf). The difference with the current half-year is 3.6 billion cubic meters against the 2025 level and 4.2 billion, or 18.7%, against the 2024 level.

The Statistics Agency does not publish monthly data. The June figure is the difference between the January-June and January-May releases: 18.3 billion cubic meters minus [15.8 billion](https://stat.uz/img/news/press-reliz-sanoat-05-2026-rus_p20150.pdf) gives 2.5 billion cubic meters. A year earlier, the same calculation gives 3.5 billion (21.9 minus 18.4). That means output fell by almost 29%, or 1 billion cubic meters, in June. [Interfax cites](https://www.interfax.ru/business/1106324) the same two monthly figures, referencing the statistics agency.

Overall industrial production in January-June 2026 [rose 8%](https://stat.uz/img/press-relizlar/press-reliz-sanoat-07-2026-rus_p46063.pdf) compared with the same period last year.

## Gas Imports Up 1.4 Times, Propane Imports Up 3.6 Times

In the first six months of 2026, Uzbekistan imported natural and manufactured gas worth [$971.7 million](https://stat.uz/img/press-relizlar/press_release_rus_p53810.pdf) — 141.4% of the January-June 2025 figure. Liquefied gas (propane) appears as a separate line in the same release: [$86.3 million](https://stat.uz/img/press-relizlar/press_release_rus_p53810.pdf), up 3.6 times.

The International Energy Agency (IEA) [forecasts](https://www.interfax.ru/business/1101932) that Russian gas supplies to Uzbekistan transiting through Kazakhstan will rise from 7 billion cubic meters in 2025 to more than 10 billion cubic meters in 2026. Domestic output in January-May, according to the IEA's estimate, fell 14%, or 2.6 billion cubic meters. The agency attributes this to the "deteriorating condition of the production base."

## Three Years of Decline, Then a Rise in 2025

According to [open data from the Statistics Agency](https://api.siat.stat.uz/media/uploads/sdmx/sdmx_data_2680.xlsx), annual natural gas output stood at 66.0 billion cubic meters in 2010, 60.7 billion in 2019 and 49.8 billion in 2020. In 2021, the figure rose to 53.8 billion, then declined for three consecutive years: 51.7 billion in 2022, 46.7 billion in 2023 and 41.5 billion in 2024. In its [release for January-December 2025](https://stat.uz/img/news/press-reliz-sanoat-21_01_2026-j_-rus_p46166.pdf), the agency gives a revised figure for 2024 — 41.3 billion cubic meters.

For full-year 2025, the country [produced 42.3 billion cubic meters](https://stat.uz/img/news/press-reliz-sanoat-21_01_2026-j_-rus_p46166.pdf). This is preliminary data, but it represents growth from 2024 rather than a continuation of the decline.
