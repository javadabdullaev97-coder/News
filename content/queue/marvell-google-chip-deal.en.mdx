Marvell Technology issued Google a warrant to purchase nearly 59 million shares under an expanded deal to design AI chips — the company's stock rose 9.8% on Wednesday, August 19.

The companies signed a commercial agreement on July 29, 2026, under which Marvell will help Google design custom semiconductor products. This is [stated in a disclosure](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001835632/000119312526356217/d412696d8k.htm) filed with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC). The deal covers AI inference accelerators, storage controllers, network interface controllers, memory interface controllers and near-memory computing devices. All of them connect to Google's tensor processing unit (TPU) ecosystem.

On August 18, Marvell [issued a warrant](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001835632/000119312526356217/d412696d8k.htm) to purchase up to 58,970,907 shares at $206.58 per share. That is about $12.2 billion at the exercise price, based on SEC filing data. The warrant is valid until August 18, 2033.

1,360,867 warrant shares vest in equal quarterly tranches over the first year after the agreement was signed. The remaining shares transfer to Google as chip purchases occur — in 240 equal tranches. One tranche corresponds to every $500 million in Marvell's revenue from custom products. Vesting will continue through the company's fiscal year 2033.

Marvell shares closed at $237 on Wednesday, SiliconANGLE [reported](https://siliconangle.com/2026/08/19/marvell-shares-jump-9-8-on-google-chip-design-deal/), citing trading data. Marvell's competitor Broadcom has a similar agreement with Google to supply AI accelerators that has been in effect since April 2026 and runs through 2031.
