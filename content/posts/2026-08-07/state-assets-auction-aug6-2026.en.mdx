The State Assets Management Agency of the Republic of Uzbekistan on August 6 [sold at an online auction](https://t.me/DAVAKTIVUZ/6194) 340 of 1,101 listed lots for a total of 25.2 billion soums at a starting price of 21.5 billion soums.

## What was sold

The final transaction amount exceeded the starting value of the lots by 3.7 billion soums and totaled 25.2 billion soums (≈$2.12 million at the Central Bank's August 6 exchange rate of 11,886.72 soums per dollar), against a starting price of 21.5 billion soums (≈$1.81 million). A total of 939 legal entities and individuals took part in the bidding, the agency [said](https://t.me/DAVAKTIVUZ/6194) on its Telegram channel.

By category, the agency [provided](https://t.me/DAVAKTIVUZ/6194) the following breakdown: state enterprise property — 77 lots for 11.1 billion soums, starting at 10.1 billion; property sold by court decision — 61 lots for 8.6 billion soums, starting at 8 billion; land plots — 16 lots covering a total of 0.9 hectares for 2.8 billion soums, starting at 1.2 billion; agricultural land — 130 plots covering a total of 594.3 hectares for 1 billion soums, starting at 0.9 billion; state assets — 6 lots for 1 billion soums, starting at 0.9 billion; other assets — 48 lots for 0.7 billion soums, starting at 0.3 billion; lease of state property — 2 lots for 21 million soums, starting at 11 million.

## Specialized equipment in Fergana saw the sharpest price jump — 2.6 times

Among individual lots, the agency [recorded](https://t.me/DAVAKTIVUZ/6194) the sharpest price increase for XCMG-brand specialized equipment in Kuvasay, Fergana region: the starting price of 600 million soums rose to 1.6 billion soums — more than 2.6 times higher. The agency did not name the winning bidder.

A land plot for trade and services in Tashkent's Mirabad district sold for 1.8 billion soums against a starting price of 800 million — 2.25 times the starting price. A T-7060 tractor on the balance sheet of a Xalq Banki branch in the Romitan district of Bukhara region sold for 800 million soums against a starting 500 million — a 1.6-fold increase. A former machine and tractor station building in the Jomboy district of Samarkand region sold for 641 million soums against a starting 583 million — an increase of about 10%.

## For comparison

At the previous auction on August 5, the agency [listed](https://leap.uz/en/2026/08/06/state-assets-auction-aug5-2026) 619 lots, of which 237 sold for 54.2 billion soums against a starting price of 53.2 billion soums — the sharpest increase then was the lease of a school kitchen in Fergana region, which sold for more than 31 times its starting price, LEAP News reported.

The August 6 auction nearly doubled the number of listed lots — 1,101 versus 619 — but the share sold fell to 30.9% from 38.3% the day before, and the final transaction amount was almost half as much: 25.2 billion soums against 54.2 billion. At the same time, unlike the previous auction, no lot on August 6 rose by an order of magnitude — the largest price increase was 2.6-fold, against 31-fold the day before.

The State Assets Management Agency regularly holds online auctions through its own electronic platform.
