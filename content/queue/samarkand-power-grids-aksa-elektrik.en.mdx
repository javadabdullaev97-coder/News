President Shavkat Mirziyoyev on August 13 ordered a faster handover of Samarkand region's power grid to Turkey's Aksa Elektrik — the two sides signed an agreement on private management of the grid in December 2025.

## Terms of the Aksa Elektrik Deal

The Uzbek government finalized the agreement to hand over Samarkand's territorial power grid to private management by Turkey's [Aksa Elektrik](https://gov.uz/ru/news/view/108357) on December 5, 2025. Under the agreement, the company takes over operational management of the grid, invests in its modernization, and commits to halving technical power losses. The government expects average savings of $20 million a year.

The region's grid is currently managed by [Regional Electric Networks JSC](https://www.het.uz/ru), a company responsible for power transmission and distribution, commercial metering, and connecting consumers.

## What Was Discussed on August 13

A meeting with the president on August 13 [addressed ways to speed up](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9496) the project to hand over Samarkand's grid to a foreign investor, the presidential press service said. The meeting also discussed construction of the Surkhan–Puli-Khumri high-voltage line. The press service did not specify a completion date for the handover or the total investment amount.

## The Grid Management Model Will Expand to Other Regions

The government announced back in December 2025 that it would [extend](https://gov.uz/ru/news/view/108357) the private grid management model to other regions. In 2026, it will be introduced in Jizzakh and Syrdarya regions, and in 2027, in Namangan and Tashkent regions.

## The "Yangiyul Experience" Will Be Extended Nationwide

Separately at the August 13 meeting, Mirziyoyev ordered the so-called "Yangiyul experience" to be extended to all districts of the country. It is a system of energy passports and energy consumption balances by mahalla (neighborhood community) that has been piloted since July in 14 districts — one per region. According to [the presidential press service](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9487), the pilot revealed shortages of electricity and gas supply in some mahallas. Relevant agencies have been instructed to organize the same work — energy passports and targeted assessment of grid conditions — in all regions of the republic.
