The State Statistics Committee of the Republic of Uzbekistan reported on July 30 that the country's non-observed economy reached 245.5 trillion soums, or 22.9% of GDP, in January–June 2026, down from 26.1% a year earlier.

## The informal sector and the shadow economy are not the same thing

In its [press release](https://stat.uz/img/press-relizlar/analitika-vvp_rus_p99487.pdf), the Statistics Committee divides the non-observed economy into two parts with different definitions.

The first is the activity of households and individuals: production of goods and services without registration in the established procedure, i.e., the informal sector. In January–June 2026, its gross value added amounted to 165.5 trillion soums (≈$13.7 billion at the Central Bank's exchange rate of July 30 — 12,035.26 soums per dollar) — 15.4% of GDP.

The second is the shadow economy proper: activity that is not prohibited by law but is intentionally concealed from the state for the purpose of tax evasion. It amounted to 80 trillion soums (≈$6.6 billion) — 7.5% of GDP.

By industry, the shadow and informal sectors in agriculture, forestry and fisheries accounted for 60.5% of the entire non-observed economy. The share in services was 23%, in construction — 17.8%, and in industry — 6.7%.

For comparison, Uzbekistan's total GDP for January–June 2026 amounted to 1,072.7 trillion soums (≈$89.1 billion at the Central Bank's exchange rate of July 30 — 12,035.26 soums per dollar). It grew in real terms by 8.5% compared with the first half of 2025.

## The share has declined for the second year in a row

The share of the non-observed economy in GDP fell from 26.1% in the first half of 2025 to 22.9% in the first half of 2026 — a decline of 3.2 percentage points.

For the full year 2025, according to [data from the Statistics Committee](https://stat.uz/img/news/analitika-vvp_rus_p44660.pdf), the share of the non-observed economy was 26.9% of GDP: 19% came from the activity of households and individuals, and 7.9% from the shadow sector. The first-half 2026 figures are lower than both of these values.

The Statistics Committee publishes its calculation methodology as a separate document — the "Methodological Provisions for the Statistical Assessment of the Non-Observed Economy" — a link to which is provided in the press release itself.
