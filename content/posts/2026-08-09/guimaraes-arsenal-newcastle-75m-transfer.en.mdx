Arsenal announced the signing of Newcastle captain, Brazilian midfielder Bruno Guimarães, on August 8, 2026 — according to [BBC Sport](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/articles/c1719r079rjo) and [Sky Sports](https://www.skysports.com/football/news/12040/13570096/bruno-guimaraes-arsenal-sign-midfielder-in-75m-transfer-as-newcastle-surpass-240m-in-player-sales-this-summer), the transfer fee was £75 million — one of Newcastle's biggest sales of the summer.

## Terms of the Deal

The 28-year-old Brazilian signed a long-term contract with the English champions and was given the number 39 shirt, [Arsenal's official website reported](https://www.arsenal.com/news/bruno-guimaraes-joins-arsenal-assZJ0i5HUEi). According to [Sky Sports](https://www.skysports.com/football/news/12040/13570096/bruno-guimaraes-arsenal-sign-midfielder-in-75m-transfer-as-newcastle-surpass-240m-in-player-sales-this-summer) and [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/sports/2026/8/8/arsenal-complete-signing-of-newcastle-midfielder-bruno-guimaraes), the contract runs for four years with an option to extend for a further season — meaning the deal could run until 2030. Neither club named the exact fee in its own statement: Newcastle [limited itself to saying](https://www.newcastleunited.com/en/news/bruno-guimaraes-leaves) that the player left for "an undisclosed fee" that ranks among the ten largest transfer fees ever paid for a player aged 28 or over, and is a record for a footballer of his age and position. The £75 million figure is confirmed by Sky Sports and Al Jazeera; according to the latter, that equates to roughly $101 million.

Arsenal's sporting director Andrea Berta said on the club's official website: "We are delighted to welcome Bruno Guimarães to our club. Bruno is a player with excellent character and a high level of quality, who will bring strong leadership to our team."

Guimarães joined Newcastle in January 2022 from France's Lyon and made 195 appearances for the club, scoring 31 goals. In 2025 he became captain of the team that won the League Cup, [Al Jazeera reports](https://www.aljazeera.com/sports/2026/8/8/arsenal-complete-signing-of-newcastle-midfielder-bruno-guimaraes).

## 'We Didn't Want to Sell'

Newcastle's sporting director Ross Wilson [said](https://www.aljazeera.com/sports/2026/8/8/newcastle-say-they-didnt-want-to-sell-guimaraes-as-arsenal-move-looms) the club had not planned to part with its captain: "We didn't want to sell Bruno. It wasn't part of the plan to sell him." According to Wilson, the player himself made clear to management that he wanted to leave, and the club responded to those circumstances.

Guimarães himself [called](https://www.newcastleunited.com/en/news/bruno-guimaraes-leaves) the decision one of the hardest of his life: "I fell in love with this place. I really mean that." He thanked the PIF investment fund, chairman Yasir Al-Rumayyan and club co-owner Jamie Reuben.

## Newcastle Sells More Than £240 Million in Players This Summer

Guimarães's sale was Newcastle's third major deal of the summer. According to [Sky Sports](https://www.skysports.com/football/news/12040/13570096/bruno-guimaraes-arsenal-sign-midfielder-in-75m-transfer-as-newcastle-surpass-240m-in-player-sales-this-summer), the club has raised more than £240 million from player sales: besides Guimarães (£75 million, Arsenal), the club sold midfielder Sandro Tonali to Tottenham for £100 million and forward Anthony Gordon to Barcelona for £69.3 million.
