The Department for Combating Economic Crimes under the Prosecutor General's Office of Uzbekistan said on August 14 that it had detained a Pension Fund specialist in Namangan who, according to investigators, took money for processing pensions.

## What happened, according to investigators

Officers from the department's Namangan city unit [detained](https://t.me/Department_uz/12971) the chief specialist of the city branch of the Extra-Budgetary Pension Fund, identified in the statement only by the initials J.A. According to the agency, he promised two citizens he would process their old-age pensions and increase their pension payments. In exchange, he received $200 and 4.8 million soums from them — a combined equivalent of about $603 at the [Central Bank of Uzbekistan's exchange rate](https://cbu.uz/ru/arkhiv-kursov-valyut/json/USD/2026-08-07/) for August 7, 2026 (11,915.64 soums per dollar).

According to the department, the specialist was detained red-handed — at the moment he received the money from the complainants — and physical evidence was seized.

## Criminal case

A criminal case was opened under Articles 25 and 168 of the Criminal Code of Uzbekistan (fraud). The department did not disclose the suspect's full name or exact age. The investigation is ongoing; the suspect is presumed innocent until a court verdict takes legal effect.

The department did not specify whether the specialist has been suspended from his position or what measures have been taken at the Namangan branch of the Pension Fund.
