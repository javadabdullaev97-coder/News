The Central Bank of Uzbekistan on July 30 named Westminster International University in Tashkent the winner of its 2026 research grant competition — the university will study behavioral factors behind loan demand in the country. The results were [announced](https://cbu.uz/uz/press_center/releases/4265314/) by the Central Bank's Research Council, and the decision was also [published](https://t.me/centralbankuzbekistan/13385) on the regulator's Telegram channel.

## Research Topic

The winning project is titled "Behavioral Factors Driving High Loan Demand in Uzbekistan." Under the competition terms [published on the Central Bank's website](https://cbu.uz/uz/research/hub/grants/), the team must identify and assess the role of behavioral factors in shaping loan demand. Based on the findings, the researchers will develop practical recommendations, including soft regulatory policy measures.

The grant for the research is 120 million soums (about $10,000 at the Central Bank's exchange rate on July 30 — 12,035.26 soums per dollar).

## How the Winner Was Chosen

The Central Bank announced the competition on June 10, 2026, with applications accepted until June 30, [according to the Central Bank's release](https://cbu.uz/uz/press_center/releases/4265314/). Two teams competed — Westminster International University in Tashkent and the Tashkent University of Information Technologies (TUIT).

The Central Bank's Research Council evaluated the applications based on their relevance to the topic, methodology, team composition and researchers' qualifications, including their prior publications. The council voted on July 14: 11 members supported Westminster University's application, one voted against, and there were no abstentions.

## Competition Requirements

The competition was open to higher education institutions and research organizations. A mandatory requirement was a team of at least two candidates or doctors of science (PhD or equivalent). At least one participant had to have experience in behavioral or experimental economics.

Applications were accepted by email together with a 3–5-page research proposal — a literature review, methodology and work schedule. The application also required résumés of the team members and a list of their scientific publications, [according to the competition page](https://cbu.uz/uz/research/hub/grants/).

## What's Next

The Central Bank of Uzbekistan plans to publish the research results on its official website once the work is complete. The published materials do not specify a deadline for the final report.
