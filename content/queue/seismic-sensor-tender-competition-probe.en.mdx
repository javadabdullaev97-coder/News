The Competition and Consumer Rights Protection Committee of the Republic of Uzbekistan [said](https://t.me/RaqobatGovUz/12545) on August 7 that it had opened a case against the Republican Center for Seismic Prognostic Monitoring under the Ministry of Emergency Situations (MES) and two tender participants. The case concerns a tender for the purchase of seismic sensors worth 5.7 billion soums.

## What the Committee Found

The trigger for the review was a critical social media post titled "One Address, a Shared Past and a Billion-Soum Tender," the Committee [said](https://t.me/RaqobatGovUz/12545) in its statement.

The Center for Seismic Prognostic Monitoring announced the tender on August 4 for the purchase of 475 sets of seismic sensors with a starting price of 5.7 billion soums. That is about $475,000 at the Central Bank of Uzbekistan's exchange rate for August 4 (11,989.46 soums per dollar). The Committee [checked](https://t.me/RaqobatGovUz/12545) whether the tender complied with the requirements of the Law "On Competition."

Following the review, the Committee found that the Center for Seismic Prognostic Monitoring itself, along with LLC "Drilltechpro" and LLC "Edulab," had failed to comply with the requirements of [Article 29](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/6518383) of the Law "On Competition." The article is titled "Requirements Against Restriction of Competition in Tenders." The agency [said](https://t.me/RaqobatGovUz/12545) that the actions of all three parties showed signs of restricting or potentially restricting competition in the tender.

## Case Status

The Committee [opened a case](https://t.me/RaqobatGovUz/12545) against the Center for Seismic Prognostic Monitoring, "Drilltechpro" and "Edulab" under the established procedure. The agency did not disclose the specifics of the signs of restricted competition among the three parties or name a timeline for the case review.

## What's Next

The Competition Committee [promised](https://t.me/RaqobatGovUz/12545) to provide additional information once the case is resolved. The final decision — whether the tender will stand, be canceled, or other measures will follow — has not yet been announced.
