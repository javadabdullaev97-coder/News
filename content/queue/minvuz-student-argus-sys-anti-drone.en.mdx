Inventor Ikromjon Moydinjonov presented a project for the ARGUS-SYS anti-drone system at the "Kelajak muhandislari" ("Engineers of the Future") competition — the Ministry of Higher Education, Science and Innovations of Uzbekistan [said](https://t.me/eduuz/50281) in a Telegram channel post.

## How the system works

According to the [Ministry's description](https://t.me/eduuz/50281), ARGUS-SYS automatically detects and locks onto drones using the YOLOv8 artificial intelligence algorithm and optical transceivers. Once it detects a target, the system neutralizes it with a 500-watt laser beam at a wavelength of 405 nanometers — at a distance of more than 1,000 meters and in less than 2 seconds.

The Ministry describes the system as designed for the "rapid and effective neutralization" of unmanned aerial vehicles. Other technical details — the emitter's design, power source, the unit's weight and dimensions, and whether it has been tested on real drones — are not disclosed in the publication.

## About the competition

"Kelajak muhandislari" ("Engineers of the Future") is an international engineering festival held by the Ministry of Higher Education, Science and Innovations. It includes a national contest-review: [organizers](https://km.edu.uz/en) have set three categories — "best idea," "best project" and "best invention"; school students, university students and practicing specialists can take part.

The Ministry's Telegram channel does not name the university where Moydinjonov studies, nor the specific date and location of the ARGUS-SYS presentation at the competition, nor the status of the development — whether it is a prototype, a working model or still a conceptual project.

## What's next

The Ministry has not published plans to refine, test or deploy ARGUS-SYS. It has also not yet announced the results of the "Kelajak muhandislari" competition — the winners' rankings and awards.
