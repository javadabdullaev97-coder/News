A court in the Khojaobod District of Andijan Region sentenced a woman to three years of restriction of liberty for violating traffic safety rules, the Telegram channel of the Supreme Court of the Republic of Uzbekistan [said](https://t.me/oliysuduz/51361) on August 6.

According to the channel, the woman was found guilty of violating rules governing traffic safety and vehicle operation. The sentence has already been handed down: the court ordered restriction of liberty for a term of three years.

The convicted woman's name is not disclosed in the publication — the Supreme Court used only the word "woman," without giving a surname, age or other details that could identify her. Also not named are the specific court that issued the sentence, the exact date the case was heard, and the article of the Criminal Code under which the violation was classified.

Restriction of liberty is a standalone type of punishment under the [Criminal Code of the Republic of Uzbekistan](https://lex.uz/docs/111457) (Article 48-1) that does not involve sending the convicted person to a place of imprisonment. Unlike imprisonment, it does not involve isolation from society but imposes a number of restrictions on the convicted person for the entire term of the sentence.

The Supreme Court did not specify the circumstances of the violation that led to the sentence.
