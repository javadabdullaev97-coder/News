Payments company Stripe officially confirmed on August 19 that it had bought AI startup OpenRouter, which routes requests between AI models — two days earlier LEAP News [reported on the pending deal](https://leap.uz/en/2026/08/18/stripe-openrouter-7bn-deal-bloomberg), citing Bloomberg.

The company published a statement about the deal, [SiliconANGLE reports](https://siliconangle.com/2026/08/19/stripe-buys-ai-model-router-openrouter-in-reported-7-5b-deal/). OpenRouter routes developers' requests among hundreds of AI models and charges a commission for it. Stripe declined to comment on the deal's financial terms, [Payments Dive notes](https://www.paymentsdive.com/news/stripe-openrouter-finally-strike-a-deal/828326/).

Stripe CEO Patrick Collison called the deal a step toward building "economic infrastructure for AI," [SiliconANGLE reports](https://siliconangle.com/2026/08/19/stripe-buys-ai-model-router-openrouter-in-reported-7-5b-deal/). According to him, the company intends to help businesses allocate model spending more efficiently through request routing. OpenRouter's founders emphasized the platform's neutrality toward different AI models in their statement, [Payments Dive reports](https://www.paymentsdive.com/news/stripe-openrouter-finally-strike-a-deal/828326/). They also noted the service's tenfold annual growth since it was founded in 2023.

## How Much the Deal May Be Worth

According to the New York Times, the deal is worth $7.5 billion — [Payments Dive cites this estimate](https://www.paymentsdive.com/news/stripe-openrouter-finally-strike-a-deal/828326/), attributing it to the NYT. Of that amount, $1.5 billion will go to OpenRouter founders Alex Atallah and Louis Vishi. Axios values the deal at more than $8 billion and says most of the payment will come in Stripe stock — [SiliconANGLE reports](https://siliconangle.com/2026/08/19/stripe-buys-ai-model-router-openrouter-in-reported-7-5b-deal/), citing Axios. Bloomberg had previously estimated the figure at more than $7 billion — [LEAP News covered that figure on August 18](https://leap.uz/en/2026/08/18/stripe-openrouter-7bn-deal-bloomberg).

OpenRouter provides access to more than 400 models from over 80 providers and serves more than 10 million developers, [SiliconANGLE reports](https://siliconangle.com/2026/08/19/stripe-buys-ai-model-router-openrouter-in-reported-7-5b-deal/). Daily request volume exceeds 10 trillion tokens, of which the service retains about 5% of spending. The deal is expected to close within a few weeks, though no exact date has been given, [SiliconANGLE notes](https://siliconangle.com/2026/08/19/stripe-buys-ai-model-router-openrouter-in-reported-7-5b-deal/).
