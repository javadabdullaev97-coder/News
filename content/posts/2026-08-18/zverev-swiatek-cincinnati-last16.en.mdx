Alexander Zverev beat Frenchman Terence Atmane 7:6(4), 7:6(6) on August 18 to reach the Round of 16 at the ATP Masters 1000 tournament in Cincinnati, [Al Jazeera reported](https://www.aljazeera.com/sports/2026/8/18/cincinnati-open-williams-sisters-fall-in-doubles-while-zverev-advances). It was the German's 46th win of the season.

Zverev is the tournament's top seed. He arrives in Cincinnati after an uneven summer. On June 7, the German [won his first Grand Slam title](https://www.rolandgarros.com/en-us/article/2026-edition-zverev-cobolli-final-report) at Roland Garros, beating Italy's Flavio Cobolli in a five-set final 6:1, 4:6, 6:4, 6:7(5), 6:1. On July 12, he reached the Wimbledon final but [lost](https://www.atptour.com/en/news/sinner-zverev-wimbledon-2026-final-sunday) to Jannik Sinner. Then on August 5 in Montreal, at the National Bank Open, Zverev unexpectedly [lost](https://www.atptour.com/en/news/griekspoor-zverev-montreal-2026-wednesday) in the second round to the Netherlands' Tallon Griekspoor 6:7(3), 6:2, 6:4.

In Cincinnati, the top seed opened with a win over Cameron Norrie 3:6, 6:3, 6:3, then beat Atmane. "He played very aggressively, forehands were flying all over the court, which made it hard for me to play aggressively myself," Zverev said after the match. Atmane was a semifinalist at Cincinnati in 2025.

## Swiatek Defends Her Title

Iga Swiatek beat Maria Sakkari 4:6, 6:1, 6:1 on August 17 to reach the last 16, [WTA Tennis reported](https://www.wtatennis.com/news/4561453/from-a-set-down-swiatek-wins-12-of-last-14-games-vs-sakkari-in-cincinnati). The seventh-seeded Pole dropped the first set but won 12 of the next 14 games.

She is defending her title: in 2025, Swiatek [won Cincinnati for the first time](https://cincinnatiopen.com/news/its-right-back-to-work-for-defending-champ-swiatek/), beating Jasmine Paolini in the final 7:5, 6:4. That made her the first Pole in the Open Era to win the tournament. This season, she arrived in Cincinnati straight after winning the National Bank Open in Toronto, where she beat Elena Rybakina in the final 6:2, 6:3. It is her first WTA 1000-level title of 2026. The flight from Toronto to Mason, where the tournament is held, took less than a day. "We just packed up, went to the airport — and here I am," Swiatek said.

In the next round, Swiatek will play Frenchwoman Diane Parry.

Cincinnati is a combined ATP Masters 1000 and WTA 1000 tournament, [held](https://cincinnatiopen.com/news/cincinnati-open-releases-2026-schedule/) on hard courts in Mason, Ohio, from August 11 to 23. Both finals will be played on Sunday, August 23.
