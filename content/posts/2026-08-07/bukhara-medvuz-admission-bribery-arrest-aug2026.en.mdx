The Department for Combating Economic Crimes under the Prosecutor General's Office of Uzbekistan on August 7 [reported](https://t.me/Department_uz/12966) the detention in the Bukhara region of a man who had promised to secure a university applicant's admission to a medical program in exchange for 26 million soums.

## What the Department Found

According to [the agency](https://t.me/Department_uz/12966), the man, identified by the initials Kh.T., gained the trust of a regional resident identified by the initials D.O. He produced forged documents claiming the resident had completed years 1–3 at a university in Kazakhstan, and, through acquaintances he said held senior positions, promised to enroll him directly into the 4th year of the medical program at Bukhara Innovative University of Education and Medicine (BITU).

The detention took place as 26 million soums — about $2,180 at the Central Bank of Uzbekistan's exchange rate of 11,915.64 soums per dollar on August 7 — changed hands. The department said the money was seized as material evidence.

The operation was carried out by the Karakul district department together with other law enforcement agencies in the region, [the agency's post said](https://t.me/Department_uz/12966).

## Criminal Case Opened

A criminal case has been opened over the incident under Articles 25 and 168, as well as 28 and 211 of the Criminal Code of Uzbekistan — fraud and bribery. The department said investigative actions are underway.

The department did not disclose the detained man's full name or additional details of the case. His guilt has not been established — under the law, only a court can determine guilt, and only after a verdict takes effect.
