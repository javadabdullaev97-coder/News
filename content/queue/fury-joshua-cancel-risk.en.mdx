Promoter Frank Warren said on August 17 that the Tyson Fury-Anthony Joshua fight, scheduled for November, may not happen if the sides fail to agree on a venue — [Boxing News reports](https://boxingnewsonline.net/news/frank-warren-on-fury-joshua-ongoing/). Earlier that day, LEAP News reported that Joshua and his promoter Eddie Hearn are insisting the fight take place in the United Kingdom — now Warren has publicly acknowledged for the first time that the fight could be canceled entirely if the sides fail to agree.

"He [Joshua] has his contract, and Tyson has his agreement, which says something different. The fight will happen. I've heard what Eddie [Hearn] said — either they agree [to a U.S. venue] or they don't. If they don't agree, there won't be a fight," [Boxing News quoted](https://boxingnewsonline.net/news/frank-warren-on-fury-joshua-ongoing/) Warren as saying.

The dispute stems from differing contract terms. According to [Boxing News](https://boxingnewsonline.net/news/frank-warren-on-fury-joshua-ongoing/), Fury's agreement allows for a fight in the United States, while Joshua's contract calls for a venue in the United Kingdom. Joshua's promoter Eddie Hearn says organizers are under pressure to stage a Netflix show at Madison Square Garden in New York. But he insists his boxer's contract requires a British arena. Hearn warned that announcing a venue prematurely, without both sides' agreement, could derail the fight.

Both boxers have already fought warm-up bouts. Fury stopped 46-year-old Mariusz Wach in the seventh round in Thailand. Joshua was knocked down twice in the first round against Christian Prengi. He won by technical knockout in the second round in Saudi Arabia — these details are also reported by [Boxing News](https://boxingnewsonline.net/news/frank-warren-on-fury-joshua-ongoing/).

## British Boxers Insist on a Home Arena

Former world champion Carl Froch publicly spoke out against moving the fight to the United States on August 15. "We've already talked about it possibly happening in the States. I said that's a disrespect to British fans, it's wrong. This is a purely British rivalry, potentially the biggest fight in British boxing history. It should take place on British soil," Froch said on his YouTube channel, [Boxing News reports](https://boxingnewsonline.net/news/froch-on-joshua-vs-fury-being-in-the-us/).

According to [Boxing News](https://boxingnewsonline.net/news/froch-on-joshua-vs-fury-being-in-the-us/), it is Netflix that is pushing for Madison Square Garden to be chosen — the platform is broadcasting the fight. The outlet also suggests that if negotiations drag on, the fight could shift from November to early 2027. Joshua and Hearn, for their part, continue to insist on holding the fight in the United Kingdom — as [BoxingScene](https://www.boxingscene.com/articles/anthony-joshua-and-eddie-hearn-remain-firm-on-tyson-fury-showdown-taking-place-in-uk) previously reported.
