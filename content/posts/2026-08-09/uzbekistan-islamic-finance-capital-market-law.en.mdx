The National Agency for Prospective Projects (NAPP) on August 4 [discussed](https://t.me/napp_uz/2364) prospects for cooperation on Islamic finance under the draft Capital Market Law with the Islamic Development Bank and the United Nations Development Programme. NAPP's counterparts at the meeting were the Islamic Development Bank (IsDB) and the United Nations Development Programme (UNDP) office in Uzbekistan.

## What was agreed

The parties [discussed](https://t.me/napp_uz/2364) further cooperation on developing Islamic finance within the draft Capital Market Law and separately considered the possibility of attracting technical assistance from the Islamic Development Bank to support these initiatives. NAPP did not name the specific participants from the Uzbek side, the timeline for next steps, or the scope of possible technical assistance in its publication.

NAPP is the [authorized state body](https://napp.uz/uz/pages/about) that regulates the capital market, crypto-assets, insurance, e-commerce and gambling in Uzbekistan, including the licensing of participants in these markets.

## Context: legislation already changing

The meeting took place against the backdrop of the launch of Islamic banking in the country. On March 27, 2026, [Law ZRU-1126](https://lex.uz/docs/8109485) "On Introducing Amendments and Additions to Certain Legislative Acts of the Republic of Uzbekistan Aimed at Introducing Islamic Banking Activity in Uzbekistan" was adopted. The law took effect on June 29, 2026, and amended the laws on the Central Bank and on banks and banking activity, as well as the Civil Code.

The document introduced definitions of Islamic banking and Islamic financial standards into legislation, established a separate type of license for Islamic banking activity, and provided for the creation of an Islamic Finance Council under the Central Bank.

The draft Capital Market Law discussed by NAPP, IsDB and UNDP is meant to supplement this framework with rules for the securities market that operate on Islamic finance principles. The exact text of the relevant chapter of the draft law was not publicly available on official portals at the time of publication.
