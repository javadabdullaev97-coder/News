The my.gov.uz Unified Portal of Interactive Government Services has accepted applications for residence registration in mortgaged or seized housing without the consent of the mortgagee bank since July 28, 2026.

## What Changed

Previously, registering in housing under encumbrance — a prohibition on disposal or a seizure (Uzbek: *taqiq* or *xatlov*) — required the consent of the mortgagee, usually the bank that issued the mortgage loan. [That consent is no longer required](https://t.me/MyGovUz/3951): applications can now be filed through my.gov.uz without visiting a passport office or standing in line, according to a post on the my.gov.uz Telegram channel.

The new procedure was introduced by a resolution of the President of the Republic of Uzbekistan dated July 15, 2026, No. PQ-265, according to the post on the my.gov.uz channel. Applications are accepted through two portal services — [residence registration (permanent)](https://my.gov.uz/ru/service/363) and [residence registration (temporary)](https://my.gov.uz/ru/service/187).

## What Remains Restricted

A citizen's registration in encumbered housing can be annulled — based on an application from the owner, the mortgagee, or the authority responsible for imposing or lifting the prohibition, submitted to the internal affairs authorities, [according to the my.gov.uz post](https://t.me/MyGovUz/3951). In other words, registration without bank consent is possible, but the bank or the owner retains the right to cancel it later.

## How to Apply

The state fee for registration through the portal is [7,416 soums (0.018 of the base calculation value)](https://my.gov.uz/ru/service/363), with the service provided within one business day. The legal basis for both services is [Law of the Republic of Uzbekistan No. ZRU-1074 dated July 10, 2025](https://my.gov.uz/ru/service/363), "On the Registration of Citizens of the Republic of Uzbekistan, Foreign Citizens, and Stateless Persons at Their Place of Residence and Stay."

The registration and deregistration procedure itself is governed by [the Regulation approved by Cabinet of Ministers Resolution No. 224 of May 5, 2026](https://lex.uz/docs/8177297).
