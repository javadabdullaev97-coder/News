On August 3, 2026, Uzbekistan Airways [launched](https://t.me/uzbekistanairways/8751) installment payments for airline tickets on uzairways.com and in its mobile app through Yandex Split, a service that sets installment terms individually for each customer.

## How to buy in installments

The airline describes the purchase in three steps: choose a flight on uzairways.com, click "Pay in installments" at checkout, and sign in with a Yandex account. After that, [Yandex Split calculates](https://t.me/uzbekistanairways/8751) a personal payment schedule on its own — no income statements and no long wait.

Uzbekistan Airways did not specify in its announcement the exact number of installments, the fee amount, the minimum or maximum purchase amount, or the list of routes covered by the installment plan — the service determines these parameters individually for each customer at checkout.

## How Yandex Split works

Under the [service's terms](https://yandex.uz/support/split/ru/conditions), Yandex Split is not a loan: installment data is not passed to credit history bureaus. The first payment is charged immediately at checkout, and the rest follow a fixed schedule that cannot be changed after the purchase is confirmed. Users can pay off part of the installments or the full amount early. The installment limit is set individually for each customer of the service.
