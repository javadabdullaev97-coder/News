Google, according to [Nikkei Asia](https://asia.nikkei.com/spotlight/supply-chain/exclusive-google-plans-to-stop-making-pixel-products-in-china-in-2027), has notified suppliers of plans to stop making Pixel smartphones, watches and wireless earbuds in China starting in 2027. Sources familiar with the company's plans told the outlet that production will move to Vietnam and India.

Google itself has made no official statement as of publication. The outlet writes that the plans cover the entire Pixel lineup — smartphones, smartwatches and wireless earbuds.

## Why Now

Google has spent several years expanding production capacity in Vietnam and India, but most Pixel assembly is still concentrated in China, [Nikkei Asia notes](https://asia.nikkei.com/spotlight/supply-chain/exclusive-google-plans-to-stop-making-pixel-products-in-china-in-2027). Earlier, in January 2026, [Nikkei Asia reported](https://asia.nikkei.com/spotlight/supply-chain/google-to-develop-and-make-high-end-phones-in-vietnam-this-year) that Google planned to set up production of its flagship Pixel model in Vietnam.

The move comes amid continuing trade tensions between Washington and Beijing, the outlet writes.
