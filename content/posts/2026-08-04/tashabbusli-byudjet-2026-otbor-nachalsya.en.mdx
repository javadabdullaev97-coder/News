Intake of initiatives for the second season of the Initiative Budget 2026 program (Tashabbusli byudjet) closed on August 3, with residents of Uzbekistan filing 23,551 proposals, the Ministry of Economy and Finance [said](https://t.me/minecofinuz/16174).

## What happened

Projects were accepted for about three weeks, from July 15 to August 3. Over that period the [openbudget.uz](https://openbudget.uz/boards/initiatives) platform received [23,551 proposals](https://t.me/minecofinuz/16174) from citizens across the country.

The submitted initiatives go through a selection stage from August 4 to 18, [according to the ministry's post](https://t.me/minecofinuz/16174). The ministry did not say in the post who carries out the selection at this stage or by what criteria projects will be cleared for the next round.

## How to take part

The Initiative Budget is a participatory budgeting program. Under it, citizens propose projects to address local problems — landscaping, repairs to infrastructure, social initiatives in their district or mahalla (neighborhood community). Proposals are submitted on the [openbudget.uz](https://openbudget.uz/boards/initiatives) portal.

The ministry has not yet named the date or format of the program's next stage.
