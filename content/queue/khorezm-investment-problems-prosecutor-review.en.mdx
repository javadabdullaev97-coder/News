Deputy Prosecutor General Dilmurod Kasimov and Deputy Prime Minister — Minister of Investment and Foreign Trade Jamshid Khodjaev this week reviewed 46 problems facing investment projects and exporters in Khorezm region, the [Prosecutor General's Office](https://t.me/uzbprokuratura/87760) reported.

## What was discussed

The meeting was held with members of the Government Commission on Attracting Investment, Industrial Development and Trade Regulation. According to the [Prosecutor General's Office](https://t.me/uzbprokuratura/87760), participants reviewed unresolved cases involving businesses in the region across four areas:

- **10 cases** — bank lending for investment projects;
- **8 cases** — infrastructure issues and the search for foreign partners;
- **4 cases** — taxation and working capital;
- **2 cases** — customs clearance;
- **22 cases** — other obstacles to business, including idle equipment imported from abroad.

The Prosecutor General's Office did not name specific banks, companies or equipment brands — the agency's statement does not disclose these details.

## What was decided

Following the meeting, relevant ministries and agencies were instructed to eliminate the identified barriers, the Prosecutor General's Office [said in its post](https://t.me/uzbprokuratura/87760). The agency did not publish deadlines or a list of the instructions.
