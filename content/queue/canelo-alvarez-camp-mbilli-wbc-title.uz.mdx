Meksikalik boksyor Saul «Kanelo» Alvares 19 avgustda [Butunjahon boks kengashi](https://wbcboxing.com/saul-canelo-alvarez-alista-campamento-en-busca-de-reconquistar-el-campeonato-mundial-supermedio-del-wbc-ante-christian-mbilli/) (WBC) ma'lumotlariga ko'ra, supero'rta vazn toifasidagi jahon chempioni unvoni uchun mag'lubiyatsiz chempion Kristian Mbilliga qarshi jangga tayyorlanmoqda.

Jang [31-oktabrda Er-Riyodda](https://wbcboxing.com/en/canelo-alvarez-vs-christian-mbilli-rescheduled-for-october-31/) (Saudiya Arabistoni) bo'lib o'tadi: WBC sanani ilgari e'lon qilingan 12-sentyabrdan ko'chirdi — buning sababi Alvaresning tayyorgarlik boshlanishini kechiktirgan shaxsiy holatlari bo'ldi.

Mbilli — WBCning supero'rta vazn toifasidagi mag'lubiyatsiz chempioni: Alvares ringga chiqmagan davrda u vaqtinchalik chempionlikdan to'liq huquqli chempionlikka ko'tarilgan. 31-oktabrdagi jang uning uchun [unvonni birinchi marta himoya qilishi](https://www.skysports.com/boxing/news/12183/13544900/canelo-alvarez-mexican-superstar-will-return-on-september-12-to-fight-christian-mbilli-for-wbc-super-middleweight-championship) bo'ladi.

Alvares uchun bu amerikalik Terens Kroufordga qarshi [mag'lubiyatidan](https://www.skysports.com/boxing/news/12183/13544900/canelo-alvarez-mexican-superstar-will-return-on-september-12-to-fight-christian-mbilli-for-wbc-super-middleweight-championship) keyingi birinchi jang bo'ladi — o'sha mag'lubiyat meksikalik boksyorni supero'rta vazndagi mutlaq chempion maqomidan mahrum qilgan edi.
