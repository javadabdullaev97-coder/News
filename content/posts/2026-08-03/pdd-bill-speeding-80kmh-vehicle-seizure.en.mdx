Factions of the Legislative Chamber of the Oliy Majlis on August 3 discussed a bill toughening penalties for traffic violations — from letting minors drive to speeding by more than 80 km/h, the UzA news agency [reported](https://uza.uz/uz/posts/noqonuniy-poyga-uchun-mamuriy-zhavobgarlik-belgilanadi_890250). The report covers the outcome of a meeting of the People's Democratic Party of Uzbekistan faction.

## Which violations were singled out

The bill amends several of the country's legislative acts as part of [improving the road safety system](https://uza.uz/uz/posts/voyaga-etmagan-shaxsga-transportni-berish-zhavobgarlikka-sabab-boladi_890243), including the Criminal Code, the Criminal Procedure Code and the Administrative Code.

The document [provides](https://uza.uz/uz/posts/noqonuniy-poyga-uchun-mamuriy-zhavobgarlik-belgilanadi_890250) separate administrative liability for four types of violations:

- letting a minor take control of a vehicle;
- organizing or taking part in illegal street racing;
- stopping or leaving trucks parked on the roadside at night in a position that endangers other road users;
- exceeding the speed limit by more than 80 km/h.

## Imprisonment and vehicle seizure — not for every violation

At a meeting of the Ecological Party of Uzbekistan faction, [held](https://uza.uz/uz/posts/voyaga-etmagan-shaxsga-transportni-berish-zhavobgarlikka-sabab-boladi_890243) the same day, lawmaker Noiba Inoyatova clarified that the toughest sanction — imprisonment and seizure of the vehicle for six months — does not apply to all four violations. It applies to severe cases where a crash resulted from letting a minor drive, as well as situations where a truck is left parked on the roadside at night in a position that endangers other road users.

Lawmakers cite crashes involving underage drivers as the reason for the tougher penalties: the law does not allow imprisoning a minor, and relatives of those killed in such accidents complain that those responsible go unpunished.

## What's next

Members of the PDPU faction recommended studying [the experience of other countries](https://uza.uz/uz/posts/noqonuniy-poyga-uchun-mamuriy-zhavobgarlik-belgilanadi_890250) before introducing the new rules, ensuring uninterrupted operation of traffic lights in remote areas, and installing warning signs ahead of speed control devices.

The discussion took place at the faction level of the Legislative Chamber — a preliminary stage of review. A date for bringing the bill to a plenary session of the chamber has not yet been announced.
