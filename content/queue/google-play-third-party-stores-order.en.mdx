US District Judge James Donato on August 13 gave Google one week to remove barriers to third-party app stores in Google Play, in the Epic Games case before the Northern District of California.

The hearing is recorded in the [case docket](https://cand.uscourts.gov/cases-e-filing/cases/320-cv-05671-jd/epic-games-inc-v-google-llc-et-al) as a "Motion Hearing." According to [a report by 9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/08/14/google-play-third-party-android-app-stores-changes-judge/), the judge demanded three changes to Google Play. Search must surface third-party stores for queries like "store for apps." The "View" button shown before installation must be replaced with "Install," and the banner asking "did you mean this?" must disappear. Donato called the current search setup "unacceptable" and demanded that third-party stores appear "for any query phrasing that matches at least 70%." Google was ordered to make all the changes by August 20.

## Background

A San Francisco jury in December 2023 found Google liable for illegally monopolizing Android app distribution and its in-app payment system. Donato issued an injunction on October 7, 2024, that runs for three years. Under the injunction, Google was required to allow third-party app stores inside Google Play, [Al Jazeera reported](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2024/10/7/us-judge-orders-google-to-open-up-app-store-to-competition).

Google launched the [Play Catalog Access Program](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/15582165?hl=en) on June 22, 2026, giving developers a month to opt out of sharing app data with third-party stores. [The program took effect](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/17187609?hl=en) on July 22, 2026.

Aptoide Games became the first major third-party store inside Google Play on August 10, 2026. The service has a catalog of more than 400,000 apps and 25 million monthly active users, [the company said](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/aptoide-returns-to-google-play-after-more-than-a-decade-opening-a-new-chapter-for-competition-on-android-302847083.html) in a press release. Aptoide CEO Paulo Trezentos said: "More than a decade ago, Android users lost the ability to find alternative app stores through Google Play — today that is changing."

## What's next

Google must implement all three fixes by August 20. As of publication on August 17, the company had not reported on progress, and no decision on appealing this part of the order has been announced.
