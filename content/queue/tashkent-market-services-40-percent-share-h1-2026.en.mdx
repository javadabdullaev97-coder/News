Uzbekistan's National Statistics Committee [said](https://stat.uz/ru/press-tsentr/novosti-goskomstata/69693-bozor-khizmatlarining-arijb-yarmi-pojtakht-issasiga-t-ri-kelmo-da-3) on August 7 that market services across the country reached 664.9 trillion soums in January–June 2026, with Tashkent accounting for 41.3% of that volume. In dollar terms, that is about $55.1 billion at the exchange rate of the Central Bank of Uzbekistan (CBU).

## The capital's share and the leading regions

Tashkent provided 274.4 trillion soums (≈$22.8 billion) worth of market services in the first half of the year — more than any other region in the country, [the National Statistics Committee said](https://stat.uz/ru/press-tsentr/novosti-goskomstata/69693-bozor-khizmatlarining-arijb-yarmi-pojtakht-issasiga-t-ri-kelmo-da-3). The capital is followed by Samarkand region (47.3 trillion soums), Tashkent region (45.5 trillion soums) and Fergana region (44.2 trillion soums). Combined, these three regions accounted for less than Tashkent alone.

## How services were distributed across the other regions

The full list of market services volume by region for January–June 2026, [according to the National Statistics Committee](https://t.me/uzstataxborot/69183), in trillion soums:

- Andijan region — 34.0
- Kashkadarya region — 31.3
- Namangan region — 30.7
- Bukhara region — 25.6
- Khorezm region — 23.2
- Surkhandarya region — 20.8
- Republic of Karakalpakstan — 19.7
- Navoi region — 16.1
- Jizzakh region — 14.7
- Syrdarya region — 9.5

The dollar figures are calculated using the CBU exchange rate of 12,060.84 soums per dollar on June 30, 2026. The National Statistics Committee did not provide data on the change in market services volume compared with the first half of 2025, so growth rates cannot be compared with the previous period based on the published materials.
