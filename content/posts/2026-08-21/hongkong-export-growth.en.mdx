Uzbekistan's exports to Hong Kong rose 20.3-fold in January-June 2026, to $543.1 million, the National Statistics Committee of the Republic of Uzbekistan said.

Hong Kong entered the top six markets for Uzbek goods and services exports for the first time, overtaking Turkey, which posted $516.2 million over the same period. Only Russia, China, Afghanistan, Kazakhstan and France rank ahead of Hong Kong.

A year earlier, in January-June 2025, Uzbekistan sold only $26.8 million worth of goods and services to Hong Kong. Total trade turnover between the two countries — including imports — grew from $72 million to $594.7 million over the year. The National Statistics Committee included Hong Kong on its list of Uzbekistan's main export partners for goods and services, with a 3.4% share.

A month-by-month breakdown shows the growth started gradually but accelerated sharply from March. Uzbekistan sold $17.8 million worth of goods and services to Hong Kong in January; by June, the monthly figure had reached $157.9 million.

## What Drove the Export Growth

The National Statistics Committee does not publish the commodity breakdown of exports by individual country, so there is no way to confirm directly what exactly was sold to Hong Kong. But the timing of the jump coincided with a sharp rise in the group "miscellaneous manufactured articles not elsewhere classified." Under the SITC (Standard International Trade Classification), this group also includes jewelry. Exports of this group for the country as a whole grew 3.7-fold, to $682.5 million.

At the same time, exports of non-monetary gold fell 4.3-fold, to $1,503.8 million. That dragged down the overall figure: Uzbekistan's total exports for the half-year declined 8.8%, to $15.9 billion. Hong Kong is one of the world's centers for the gold and jewelry trade, which makes the jewelry industry a probable explanation for the rise in shipments there. But the statistics agency does not disclose exact data on the commodity structure of this specific destination.

## Tashkent and Hong Kong Discuss Cooperation

On June 4, 2026, President of Uzbekistan Shavkat Mirziyoyev met in Tashkent with Hong Kong Chief Executive Lee Ka-chiu (John Lee), the president's website said. The guest was accompanied by a large business delegation. The two sides agreed to expand cooperation in green energy, agriculture, biotechnology, the digital economy, fintech and artificial intelligence. A trade house of Uzbekistan has opened in Hong Kong.
