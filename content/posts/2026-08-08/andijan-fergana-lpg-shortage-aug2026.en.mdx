Deputy Prosecutor General Salom Samadov chaired a coordination meeting of the republican fuel and energy resources control task force on August 7 — [reviewing implementation of instructions](https://t.me/uzbprokuratura/87859) that President Shavkat Mirziyoyev gave to relevant agencies on July 27.

## What the Prosecutor General's Office Reviewed

The meeting was attended by heads of relevant ministries and agencies, as well as members of territorial and district task forces, [according to the publication](https://t.me/uzbprokuratura/87859) of the Prosecutor General's Office. The task force critically reviewed the state of payments for energy resources, the reduction of accounts receivable, improvements in energy efficiency, and cuts to grid losses. Officials separately heard reports on the pilot model being run in Yangiyul district, which authorities plan to extend to another 13 districts of the republic. Following the meeting, agency heads and local task forces were assigned additional tasks, and the question of holding managers accountable for negligence in their work was raised.

## Where the Figures Come From: The President's July 27 Meeting

The instructions reviewed by the prosecutor's office stemmed from the president's videoconference meeting on July 27. At that meeting, Mirziyoyev dismissed the energy minister and the head of JSC Regional Electric Networks (RES) "for serious shortcomings in the system," and placed the heads of National Electric Networks, Thermal Power Stations, Transgaz, and Hududgaz on probation until the end of the year — [the president's press secretary said](https://t.me/Press_Secretary_Uz/7665). Sherzod Khodjayev was appointed the new energy minister, Sardor Isakulov was named head of RES, and Akhad Isokjonov was named head of Uzenergoinspektsiya; all three were instructed to introduce a new working style starting the same day.

At the meeting itself, the president cited figures illustrating the scale of the problem: power grid losses across the country stand at 17.2%, totaling 4.8 billion kWh over the first six months of the year, [according to the publication](https://t.me/Press_Secretary_Uz/7664) of the press secretary. In individual districts — the city of Andijan and Asaka district in Andijan region, Koshrabot and Tashlak districts in Fergana region, and a number of others — losses exceed 25%. Nationwide, 300,000 gas meters and 234,000 electricity meters in households do not transmit data to the automated metering system. Around 4,000 grid failures occurred in June and July, leaving residents and businesses without electricity for 8–10 hours. The debt of Yangiyul district alone for energy resources reached 43 billion soums (about $3.6 million at the Central Bank's exchange rate on August 7 — 11,915.64 soums per dollar), of which 9.4 billion soums (about $0.79 million) has been written off as uncollectible; total nationwide gas debt stands at 188 billion soums (about $15.8 million).

## RES Inspects Grids in the Field

According to posts on RES's feedback channel, the company conducted targeted inspections in the regions in early August: an [audit of the electricity metering system](https://t.me/hetmuloqot/26455) in Fergana district, a [review of the handling of resident complaints](https://t.me/hetmuloqot/26462) in Yangiyul and Tashkent districts, and an [inspection of energy facilities](https://t.me/hetmuloqot/26467) in Urgut district — the latter with the personal participation of board chairman Sardor Isakulov. The company has not published overall statistics on grid readiness for winter on its channel — only reports on individual site visits are available.

Separately from RES, the Ministry of Energy [announced](https://t.me/minenergy_uz/20625) on July 30 the creation of special task forces in 15 districts of the country for an "in-depth analysis" of electricity and gas supply — the list includes Andijan and Fergana districts, selected due to high debt, elevated grid losses, and frequent complaints about worn-out infrastructure.

The president's website [published a report](https://president.uz/oz/lists/view/9487) on August 6 on interim measures following the July meeting: particular attention is given to preparing mahallas with limited gas supply capacity for winter and expanding private-sector participation in liquefied gas deliveries.

## What's Next

Following the August 7 meeting, the Prosecutor General's Office instructed agencies and local task forces to communicate additional tasks and warned of accountability for managers found negligent in their work — the publication contains no exact deadlines or sanctions. The pilot model from Yangiyul district is planned to be extended to 13 districts nationwide; a Center for Digitalization of the Fuel and Energy Sector and Artificial Intelligence Implementation has been established under the Ministry of Energy.
