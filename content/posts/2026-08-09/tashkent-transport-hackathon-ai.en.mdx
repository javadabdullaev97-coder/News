Uzbekistan's Ministry of Transport held the country's first National Transport Hackathon in Tashkent on August 6-8 with a prize fund of 320 million soums and, after three days, [named](https://t.me/Mintrans_uz/28618) nine winning teams across three tracks.

## Three tracks, nine winning teams

The hackathon [ran](https://t.me/Mintrans_uz/28601) for 72 hours and was dedicated to developing the artificial intelligence ecosystem in the transport sector, introducing digital technologies and supporting young specialists. Teams competed in three tracks:

- **"Special services and applications to improve public mobility"** (air, rail and road transport). Winners: VorteX with the QATNOV project, AlmaCode with the EasyPay project, AYSLATTE with the "YaSamokat" project.
- **"Integrating artificial intelligence into the Ministry of Transport's information systems"**. Winners: Dominify with the Aibus project, 5dev with the Permit Flow project, TransportBrain with the TransportBrain Studio project.
- **"Big transport data analysis"**. Winners: Komanda 21 with the Vector project, Qamrov AI with a project of the same name, Developers with the Oqim project.

The Ministry of Transport's Telegram channel does not specify what underpins each of the nine projects — the ministry did not disclose specific technical solutions.

## Prize fund and organization

The hackathon's prize fund totaled 320 million soums (≈$26,855 at the [Central Bank's August 7 exchange rate](https://cbu.uz/ru/arkhiv-kursov-valyut/json/USD/2026-08-08/) of 11,915.64 soums per dollar). The figure is [confirmed](https://t.me/Mintrans_uz/28601) both by the Ministry of Transport's Telegram channel and by the [official LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/the-ministry-for-development-of-itc-of-the-republic-of-uzbekistan_nationaltransporthackathon-activity-7485672089077645312-PjOC) of the Ministry for Development of Information Technologies and Communications, which announced participant registration.

Minister of Transport Ilkhom Makhkamov opened the hackathon. Neither the Ministry of Transport nor the Ministry for Development of Information Technologies and Communications published the number of registered teams and participants.

## What's next

The Ministry of Transport [said](https://t.me/Mintrans_uz/28601) it plans to hold the National Transport Hackathon annually and organize the transport sector's first "ideathon" by the end of 2026. What will happen to the winning teams' projects — whether they will be implemented in the ministry's work — is not addressed in the published posts.
