Uzbekistan's President Shavkat Mirziyoyev on August 13 [appointed](https://t.me/Press_Secretary_Uz/7698) Olim Davlatov as adviser on representative bodies, non-governmental non-profit organizations (NGOs) and youth affairs, removing Ruslanbek Davletov from the post.

## What was officially announced

The personnel decisions [are described](https://t.me/Press_Secretary_Uz/7698) in a post on the official Telegram channel of the president's press secretary: "By the relevant decrees of the President of the Republic of Uzbekistan: Davlatov Olim Davlatovich has been appointed adviser to the President of the Republic of Uzbekistan on representative bodies, non-governmental non-profit organizations and youth affairs; Davletov Ruslanbek Kuroltayevich has been removed from the post of adviser to the President of the Republic of Uzbekistan on representative bodies, non-governmental non-profit organizations and youth affairs." The press service did not publish the individual decree texts or their numbers.

## What is known about the previous post

The Institute for Social and Spiritual Research under the Republican Center for Spirituality and Enlightenment was, until recently, [headed](https://uza.uz/uz/posts/milliy-qadriyatlar-va-manaviy-merosning-zhamiyat-taraqqiyotidagi-orni_659259) by Olimjon Davlatov — that is how he is named in a November 20, 2024, article by the National Information Agency of Uzbekistan. There is no direct confirmation in open data that this is the same person as the newly appointed presidential adviser Olim Davlatovich Davlatov — the sources match only in surname and a close variant of the first name.
