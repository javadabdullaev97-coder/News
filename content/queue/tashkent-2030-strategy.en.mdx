The Tashkent City Council of People's Deputies on August 20 reviewed the draft «Tashkent — 2030» strategy, [the Tashkent hokimiyat (city administration) said](https://tashkent.uz/ru/news/7413). The city's economy is to grow 2.1-fold — to $60.4 billion.

The draft was discussed at the city council's 26th session. The information was presented by Abdulla Abdukadirov, first deputy director of the Agency for Strategic Development and Reforms under the President of the Republic of Uzbekistan, [according to a statement on the hokimiyat's website](https://tashkent.uz/ru/news/7413). Following the session, decisions were adopted and assignments given to responsible officials; separately, deputies reviewed issues of winter utility preparations, investment projects and appeals submitted through the «Xalq nazorati» platform.

## Economy and revenue

GRP (gross regional product) per capita is to grow from $9,300 to $17,700, [the Agency for Strategic Development and Reforms said](https://asdr.gov.uz/ru/rassmotren-proekt-strategii-razvitiya-stoliczy-do-2030-goda/). The inflation target is 5% a year. Under the draft, absolute poverty is to fall to zero and unemployment is not to exceed 3.5%. The number of women in need of social support is planned to be cut 3.5-fold.

## Transport and roads

The metro network will grow from 50 to 79 stations, line length from 71 to 103 km, and train intervals will shorten to 2-3 minutes. The share of residents using public transport is to rise from 40% to 60%. The city plans to repair 1,000 km of roads and install 700 "smart" intersections.

## Housing and environment

Annual housing commissioning is to reach 54,574 apartments, and housing provision is to grow from 16.6 to 24.4 square meters per person. The share of green spaces is planned to increase from 14.1% to 30%, adding 4,450 hectares of new green zones. Household waste collection is to cover 100% of the city.

## Health care, education, exports and tourism

Life expectancy for city residents is to rise from 75.2 to 78 years. Preschool education coverage is set at 81.2%, and higher education coverage at 50%. The IT sector is planned to employ 23,000 young people, with another 461,000 covered by the «Ibrat farzandlari» foreign language learning project.

The city's exports are to reach $7.5 billion. Of that, IT services exports are to grow from $775 million to $4 billion (5.2-fold) and cover 120 countries. The draft estimates the number of foreign tourists at 7.5 million a year and domestic tourists at 20 million.

## Basis and status of the project

The strategy is being prepared in accordance with presidential decree UP-21 of February 16, 2026, [the Agency for Strategic Development and Reforms said](https://asdr.gov.uz/ru/rassmotren-proekt-strategii-razvitiya-stoliczy-do-2030-goda/). The document is part of the nationwide «Uzbekistan — 2030» strategy. According to the agency, following the discussion the draft was approved by the city council, and a decision was made on its implementation. Sources have not yet published a separate decree or resolution on the document's final approval.
