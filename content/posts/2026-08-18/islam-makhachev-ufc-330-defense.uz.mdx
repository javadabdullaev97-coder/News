Islam Makhachevning murabbiyi Javier Mendes va irlandiyalik Conor McGregor Ian Machado Garrini [15-avgustda](https://www.ufc.com/event/ufc-330) Filadelfiyada UFC 330 turnirida Makhachevga hakamlar hay'atining bir ovozdan qarori bilan mag'lub bo'lganidan so'ng qo'llab-quvvatladi.

Jang tafsilotlarini LEAP News ilgari alohida materialda yoritgan edi — [Makhachev UFC'ning yarim o'rta vazn toifasidagi unvonini himoya qildi va Silva rekordini yangiladi](https://leap.uz/uz/2026/08/17/makhachev-welterweight-record-ufc330).

## Reaksiya

Murabbiy Mendes jangdan so'ng [ochiqchasiga Garrini qo'llab-quvvatladi](https://www.sherdog.com/news/news/Islam-Makhachevs-coach-backs-Ian-Garrys-No-1-UFC-ranking-after-loss-202405), uning yarim o'rta vazn reytingida birinchi o'rinda qolish huquqini isbotlaganini aytdi. «Ian Garri o'zining qanchalik chidamli va birinchi o'rinni qanchalik loyiq ekanini ko'rsatish uchun chiqdi — va buni isbotladi», — dedi Mendes.

Garrining vatandoshi, irlandiyalik Conor McGregor mag'lubiyatdan so'ng [uning ruhiy holatini maqtadi](https://www.sherdog.com/news/news/Conor-McGregor-applauds-Ian-Garrys-response-to-UFC-330-defeat-202410), «Champion mindset!» (chempion tafakkuri!) izohini qoldirib. Ilgari McGregor Garri Makhachevni nokaut qilishini bashorat qilgan edi.

## Keyingi qadam

Makhachev braziliyalik Carlos Pratesni unvonga eng munosib keyingi da'vogar deb atadi va [Ramazon boshlanishidan oldin yana bir jangga chiqishni xohlashini aytdi](https://www.sherdog.com/news/news/Islam-Makhachev-mocks-Max-Holloway-following-twobelt-jab-202407).

Filadelfiyadagi UFC 330 turnirida hozir bo'lgan Prates [chempionni nokaut qilishga va'da berdi](https://www.sherdog.com/news/news/Carlos-Prates-has-a-chilling-promise-for-Islam-Makhachev-202411): «Men seni nokaut qilaman, birodar. Men bu kamarni Braziliyaga olib boraman — Braziliyadan chiqqan birinchi yarim o'rta vazn chempioni bo'laman». Pratesning so'zlariga ko'ra, Makhachev bilan jang dekabr oyida bo'lishi mumkin. Aprel oyida u UFC 330'da zaxira bo'lib turgan mag'lubiyatsiz Michael Moralesga qarshi chiqishni rejalashtirmoqda.
