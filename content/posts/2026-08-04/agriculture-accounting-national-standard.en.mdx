The Ministry of Economy and Finance put draft National Financial Reporting Standard (NFRS) No. 70, "Accounting for Agricultural Activity," up for public discussion on August 3 — the first standalone accounting standard for the agricultural sector in Uzbekistan's history.

## What the New Standard Covers

The document sets uniform rules for the recognition, measurement, financial-statement presentation and disclosure of information on [biological assets, agricultural produce, bearer (perennial) plantations and government grants related to biological assets](https://gov.uz/en/imv/news/view/201504). The ministry expects the unified approach to simplify the classification of accounting objects in the sector and improve the comparability and reliability of financial reporting.

The standard covers the practices of farms and agro-clusters, cotton and grain production, horticulture, viticulture, vegetable growing, livestock farming, poultry farming, fish farming, beekeeping and sericulture.

## Based on International Standard IAS 41

NFRS No. 70 was developed [based on the principles of International Accounting Standard (IAS) 41, "Agriculture"](https://gov.uz/en/imv/news/view/201504), adapted to the specifics of Uzbekistan's agricultural sector. The Ministry of Economy and Finance says the final document is designed for practical application — simple accounting rules for entrepreneurs, farmers and agribusiness.

## Part of a Broader Accounting Reform

The development of sector-specific standards is part of the transition to the NFRS system, which is replacing the existing National Accounting Standards (NAS). The reform's foundation was laid by [Presidential Resolution No. PP-282 of September 15, 2025](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/7728927), "On Measures to Improve the Financial Accounting System in Line with International Requirements and Standards," which took effect on December 17, 2025. It required publicly significant organizations — which the Ministry of Economy and Finance adds to a registry annually by July 1 — to keep accounts under IFRS starting January 1 of the year following their inclusion.

As part of this reform, the Ministry of Economy and Finance has already developed and approved two basic standards: [the Conceptual Framework for the Preparation of Financial Statements (reg. No. 3854 of June 10, 2026) and NFRS No. 2 on accounting policy (reg. No. 3810 of April 11, 2026)](https://gov.uz/en/imv/news/view/201504). Both take effect January 1, 2027.

## What's Next

Draft NFRS No. 70 is currently at the public discussion stage on the [regulation.adliya.uz portal](https://regulation.adliya.uz/project/10841/33854?contentLang=uz). The Ministry of Economy and Finance has invited businesses, ministries and agencies, public organizations, accountants, auditors and industry specialists to take part in the discussion. The ministry's statement does not specify an exact date for the standard's approval and entry into force.
