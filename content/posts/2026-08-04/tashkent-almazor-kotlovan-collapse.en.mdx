The edge of an excavation pit at a future hotel construction site in Tashkent's Almazar district collapsed on August 2 due to negligence by the contractor during earthworks — one person was injured, and a special commission has been formed to investigate the cause.

## What Happened

The collapse occurred on Sunday, August 2, at a construction site near house No. 3 in the Orzu residential area (Orzular mahalla) of Tashkent's Almazar district. The Inspectorate for Control in Construction and Housing and Utilities Sector [reported](https://t.me/OZR_QSN/18245) that part of the excavation pit collapsed during earthworks and attributed the incident to negligence by the contractor.

After information from the scene spread on social media, emergency services and inspectorate staff went to the site. The press service of the Tashkent hokimiyat [reported](https://t.me/poytaxt_uz/51238) that a special commission has been formed to investigate the cause, including the emergency situations department, the construction inspectorate, the prosecutor's office and district representatives. The hazardous area has been fenced off, and officials are checking whether nearby infrastructure was damaged.

The Tashkent City Prosecutor's Office [circulated](https://t.me/toshkentprokuraturasi/132648) the same text the same day — as a repost of the statement from the hokimiyat's press service.

## Casualties: From Denial to Confirmation

The initial statement from the Tashkent hokimiyat and prosecutor's office read: "At present, there are no injured persons from the incident who have sought medical help." With this wording, city authorities [denied](https://t.me/toshkentprokuraturasi/132648) rumors about casualties circulating on social media.

Later, [the same post](https://t.me/poytaxt_uz/51238) by the Tashkent hokimiyat's press service was edited and updated: according to the updated information, one person was injured in the incident and received timely medical assistance. The Tashkent prosecutor's office's version remained unchanged, however — its post, being a repost of the original hokimiyat statement, does not update automatically and still shows the earlier wording stating there were no casualties.

The special commission formed on the day of the incident continues to investigate the cause of the collapse.
