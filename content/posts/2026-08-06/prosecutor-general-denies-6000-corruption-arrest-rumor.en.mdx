The Prosecutor General's Office of Uzbekistan on August 6 [denied](https://t.me/uzbprokuratura/87737) social media reports claiming that the head of an anti-corruption unit at one of the ministries had been detained with $6,000.

## What the Statement Says

In an official statement headlined "Rasmiy bayonot" ("Official Statement"), the agency [said](https://t.me/uzbprokuratura/87737) that some social media accounts were spreading reports claiming that the head of an anti-corruption unit at one of the ministries had been detained with $6,000 in cash. The Prosecutor General's Office called the information "completely untrue" and described the reports themselves as "unsubstantiated and unconfirmed."

The agency did not name the specific ministry, the individual involved, a criminal case number, or the date of the events described in the reports it was denying.

The Prosecutor General's Office [urged](https://t.me/uzbprokuratura/87737) social media users to rely only on information published by official sources and not to spread unverified reports.
