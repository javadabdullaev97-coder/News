IT Park Uzbekistan resident studio Grimoire Entertainment presented its game in development, Echo of Souls, at the international ChinaJoy 2026 exhibition in Shanghai — the IT Park Telegram channel [reported](https://t.me/itpark_uz/20564).

## What was shown at ChinaJoy

At ChinaJoy 2026, Grimoire Entertainment presented Echo of Souls — a cyberpunk platformer about a corporate fighter named Amir who has lost his memory, and a hacker named Aurora. The two team up against corporate power in a dystopian megacity. Players switch between the two characters, who have different combat abilities — one excels in melee combat, the other attacks from a distance and manipulates the environment, [according to the game's description on Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3363800/Echo_of_Souls/). No exact release date has been announced — on Steam the game is listed as "Coming soon."

According to IT Park, at the exhibition the studio's founder held meetings with foreign publishers, investors and technology companies to discuss potential partnerships. ChinaJoy is an annual digital entertainment exhibition in Shanghai, one of the largest in the world.

## About the studio and the game

Grimoire Entertainment is based in Tashkent and is among the residents of IT Park Uzbekistan. [According to the studio's website](https://grimoire.uz/), the company handles the full game development cycle — from concept to release — and uses the Unity engine for Android and iOS projects. Besides Echo of Souls, the studio's portfolio includes the children's chess game UzChessKids.

The studio was founded by Otabek Baratov. In December 2025, he spoke on a panel about player retention mechanics at the Moscow International Video Games Week forum. There, Echo of Souls went through more than 50 testing sessions with positive feedback from players and experts — [IT Park Uzbekistan reported](https://www.it-park.uz/en/itpark/news/export-investment-and-new-jobs-outcomes-of-it-park-uzbekistan-s-visit-to-moscow) following the delegation's visit to Moscow.

## Game dev in Uzbekistan

IT Park calls Grimoire Entertainment's participation in ChinaJoy confirmation of the growth of Uzbekistan's game development industry. The organization provides residents with tax benefits and acceleration programs, and promotes them at specialized international exhibitions alongside other IT companies in the country.
