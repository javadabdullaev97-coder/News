The Institute for Shadow Economy Reduction and Fiscal Analysis under the Ministry of Economy and Finance of the Republic of Uzbekistan [confirmed](https://ifs.imv.uz/ru/press/news/rasmiy-bayonot-bank-omonatlari-foizlarini-soliqqa-tortish-taklifi-xususida) on July 30 that it is proposing to introduce a tax on interest income from individuals' bank deposits at the dividend tax rate.

## What exactly the institute proposed

The institute [said](https://ifs.imv.uz/ru/press/news/rasmiy-bayonot-bank-omonatlari-foizlarini-soliqqa-tortish-taklifi-xususida) that the proposal — "the result of analytical and academic research conducted by the institute's researchers" — was presented on July 30 at the "[Fiscal Dialogue](https://ifs.imv.uz/ru/press/news/toshkentda-ekspertlar-va-oav-vakillari-ishtirokida-fiskal-muloqot-bolib-otdi)" event "as an idea for professional discussion among experts and specialists" and "does not have the status of an official decision, document, or draft law."

The institute separately [stressed](https://ifs.imv.uz/ru/press/news/rasmiy-bayonot-bank-omonatlari-foizlarini-soliqqa-tortish-taklifi-xususida) that "at this stage, this proposal does not mean any changes are being made to current legislation." According to the institute's wording, the tax would apply only to interest income received, not to the deposit amount itself. The institute [compared](https://ifs.imv.uz/ru/press/news/rasmiy-bayonot-bank-omonatlari-foizlarini-soliqqa-tortish-taklifi-xususida) the proposed rate to the tax rate on dividend income without naming a specific percentage. The statement contains no timeline for a possible introduction, no thresholds by deposit amount, and no named authors of the proposal — no names or titles of specific staff members.

## How deposit interest is taxed now

Income of resident individuals in the form of dividends and interest in Uzbekistan [is taxed at a rate of 5%](https://gov.uz/ru/advice/70/document/1325), according to data from the portal of the Government of the Republic of Uzbekistan. Interest on individuals' bank deposits does not currently fall under this rule — the institute explicitly states that current legislation has not yet been changed, meaning the tax exemption remains in place.

## Context: the institute has already proposed changing tariffs

This is not the first initiative the institute presented at the "Fiscal Dialogue" on July 30. According to the institute's follow-up clarification of July 31, on the same day the institute also put forward for discussion a [proposal](https://ifs.imv.uz/ru/press/news/toshkentda-ekspertlar-va-oav-vakillari-ishtirokida-fiskal-muloqot-bolib-otdi) to shift from universal subsidizing of heating tariffs to targeted support — a topic the institute [separately explained](https://ifs.imv.uz/ru/press/news/issiqlik-taminoti-tariflari-va-ijtimoiy-meyor-taklifi-xususida) on July 31, after media reports circulated an interpretation about the tariff rising "fivefold."

The institute describes both proposals as the result of its own research, not draft regulatory acts. The institute [said](https://ifs.imv.uz/ru/press/news/rasmiy-bayonot-bank-omonatlari-foizlarini-soliqqa-tortish-taklifi-xususida) that "the essence of the institute's work is precisely to conduct scientific research and exchange views."
