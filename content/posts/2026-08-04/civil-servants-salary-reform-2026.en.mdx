President Shavkat Mirziyoyev on August 4 reviewed a presentation of proposals to reform the pay system for civil servants — the new pay scale is meant to cover [54,000 employees](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9484).

## What's Wrong With the Current System

According to the [presentation shown to the president](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9484), base salary today accounts for no more than 15% of a civil servant's pay structure. Up to 70% of the wage fund goes to various allowances, bonuses and incentive payments. There is no single document governing the pay system — most government agencies operate under more than 50 separate regulatory acts.

The staffing levels of administrative personnel in government agencies have already been streamlined — from 73,000 in 2016 to 54,000 today, the [materials say](https://gov.uz/ru/news/view/201961).

## How the Proposed Pay Scale Is Structured

Drawing on international experience, the authors of the proposals advocated for a [new pay scale with 11 grades and 6 steps](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9484). Instead of more than ten types of allowances and bonuses, the system would be reduced to three main types of incentive payments:

- a qualification and KPI allowance, assessed by functional duties, discipline and initiative;
- bonuses for completing complex tasks and implementing innovative approaches;
- payments for mastering information technology and artificial intelligence, and for professional development.

Government agencies are proposed to be divided into five levels — national, republican, regional, district and city — according to the [presentation](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9484).

## The Goal of the Reform

Mirziyoyev stressed the need to ensure fairness and transparency in the pay system and to link employees' earnings to their qualifications and performance, according to [materials](https://president.uz/ru/lists/view/9484) published on the president's website. The stated principles include equalizing pay for staff at territorial units and employees in comparable positions, as well as a transparent salary calculation for every civil servant.

The published materials do not specify a timeline for transitioning to the new system. At this point, the president has only reviewed the proposals — no separate decision on their approval or an implementation schedule has been announced.
