The Bailiff Bureau under the Prosecutor General's Office recovered 52 billion soums (≈$4.36 million at the Central Bank's exchange rate as of August 7) in favor of Tashkent residents and legal entities in January–July 2026. This was [reported](https://t.me/uzbprokuratura/87764) by the Prosecutor General's Office Telegram channel.

## How the requests broke down

In January–July, the Tashkent City Department of the Bailiff Bureau (MIB) and its territorial divisions received 15,180 requests from citizens and legal entities, [the publication states](https://t.me/uzbprokuratura/87764). Of these, 13,517 requests were reviewed.

5,664 requests were granted — recovering 52 billion soums for claimants. Another 7,852 requests received legal clarifications, 330 requests were left without consideration, and 950 were closed without the outcome specified in the publication.

The same text with identical figures was also [published](https://t.me/mibtoshahahar/8146) by the Telegram channel of the Tashkent City Department of MIB itself.

## What the Bailiff Bureau is

The Bailiff Bureau under the Prosecutor General's Office was established by presidential decree UP-5059 dated May 29, 2017. Its operating procedure is set out in the [presidential resolution on the implementation of the decree](https://president.uz/uz/lists/view/583) dated May 31, 2017 — the agency handles the enforcement of court rulings and other enforcement documents, including those related to debt recovery.

The publication does not break down which categories of disputes most often involved recovered funds — utility debts, alimony, unpaid wages, or other claims. The Prosecutor General's Office noted that the review of requests is ongoing.
