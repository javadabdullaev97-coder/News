SpaceX published its first quarterly earnings report on August 4 since going public in June — the company's revenue for April–June grew 92%, to $7.8 billion, the Financial Times [reported](https://www.ft.com/content/41f7963b-dd50-4f27-a085-771ddec4a8ca).

That same day, August 4, SpaceX [announced](https://www.vedomosti.ru/technology/news/2026/08/04/1218895-spacex-nvidia) a strategic partnership with Nvidia to develop a computing module for the Starmind AI1 satellites. Each satellite will be equipped with Nvidia Rubin graphics processing units and Nvidia Vera central processing units — a combination meant to deliver "data-center-level computing power" directly in orbit.

SpaceX went public on Nasdaq on June 12, 2026 — the largest IPO in history: the company [raised](https://www.kommersant.ru/doc/8740690) $75 billion, or up to $85.7 billion including the overallotment option. The company's valuation at the time of the offering was $1.77 trillion, and founder and CEO Elon Musk became the world's first dollar trillionaire after the IPO.
