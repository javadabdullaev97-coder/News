JSC Almalyk Mining and Metallurgical Combine (AGMK) posted 8.69 trillion soums in net profit for the first half of 2026 — nearly double the figure a year earlier, according to the [combine's financial report](https://agmk.uz/uploads/docs/d6679b543a0182737cea6dc1c80de1ba.pdf) dated July 29.

## Revenue up 40%, profit up 93%

Net sales revenue for January–June 2026 totaled 30.19 trillion soums, up from 21.59 trillion soums in the same period of 2025 — a 39.8% increase. This is according to the [financial results report](https://agmk.uz/uploads/docs/d6679b543a0182737cea6dc1c80de1ba.pdf), published by the combine in its [open data section](https://agmk.uz/ru/opendata/4-019-0008). The half-year revenue is equivalent to about $2.50 billion at the Central Bank of Uzbekistan's exchange rate for June 30 — 12,060.84 soums per dollar. The combine does not disclose the reasons for the revenue growth in its report.

The data is disclosed under national accounting reporting forms, not International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS). The combine did not publish an auditor's opinion on the half-year figures.

Net profit rose from 4.49 trillion to 8.69 trillion soums (≈$720.7 million) — up 93.4%. Pre-tax profit increased from 8.01 trillion to 15.30 trillion soums, and income tax rose from 3.52 trillion to 6.61 trillion soums.

According to LEAP News calculations, AGMK's half-year profit equals 74.9% of its [result for all of 2025](https://agmk.uz/uploads/docs/ba6015a27ced5bb3d95d7f34a604df60.pdf) — that year the combine earned 11.61 trillion soums in net profit on revenue of 50.43 trillion soums.

## Cost of sales grew three times slower than revenue, exchange-rate losses shrank

The cost of goods sold for the half-year rose 11.8% — from 8.46 trillion to 9.46 trillion soums. With revenue up 39.8%, this produced gross profit of 20.73 trillion soums (≈$1.72 billion), up from 13.13 trillion a year earlier, and operating profit of 15.83 trillion soums, up from 9.67 trillion.

The second factor was foreign exchange differences. Income from them in the first half of 2026 rose to 2.27 trillion soums, while losses fell to 2.36 trillion soums. According to LEAP News calculations, the net loss from exchange-rate differences narrowed from 1.26 trillion soums a year earlier to roughly 0.1 trillion soums.

## Production, exports and budget payments

According to [data from the combine](https://agmk.uz/uz/news/okmk-birinchi-yarim-yillik-natijalari-sarhisobi), AGMK produced marketable output worth 30.9 trillion soums (≈$2.56 billion) in January–June 2026 and fulfilled its production forecast at 133.3%. The forecast for cathode copper output was met at 100.0%.

Half-year exports totaled $493.4 million, or 148.6% of the forecast. Of the $294.6 million 2026 investment program covering seven projects, $142.8 million was disbursed over six months (101.9% of the plan). Under the localization program, 35 of 40 projects worth 1,511.1 billion soums (≈$125.3 million) have been implemented, worth 649.8 billion soums.

The combine transferred 16.0 trillion soums (≈$1.33 billion) to the budget in the first half. The 2026 production cost-reduction program envisages savings of 2,301.5 billion soums, or 13.2%; at the half-year mark, the expected effect was 1,148.2 billion soums, or 14.2%.

## IPO planned for the first half of 2027

AGMK is on a list of 12 state-owned enterprises for which [Presidential Resolution PP-145 of April 21, 2025](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/7488169) approved a schedule of initial and secondary public offerings on international and domestic stock markets. For the combine, Annex No. 2 to the document specifies an IPO of 10–15% of shares in the first half of 2027.

The resolution does not name the listing venue. The combine has not published separate announcements on the IPO's parameters in its [2026 information disclosure section](https://agmk.uz/ru/menu/2026-yil).
