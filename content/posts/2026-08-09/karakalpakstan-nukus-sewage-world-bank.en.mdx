O'zsuvta'minot said on August 9 that construction and reconstruction of sewage networks and pumping stations is underway in the northern part of Nukus as part of a project involving the World Bank.

## What is being built

The work is part of the project titled "Reconstruction of the sewage system in the city of Nukus and the district centers of Takhiatash and Khojayli" — that is how it is named in the [O'zsuvta'minot post](https://t.me/uzsuv/45516). According to the post, the project envisions building four sewage pumping stations and 30.4 km of new sewage networks in total. The goal is to provide residents with modern sewage services, upgrade existing infrastructure and improve the region's environmental sustainability.

The [post](https://t.me/uzsuv/45516) notes that construction work is proceeding at a fast pace and that sewage service quality for Nukus residents will improve once the project is completed.

## Context: a $239 million World Bank loan

The World Bank's Water Services and Institutional Support Project has been underway in Nukus, Takhiatash and Khojayli since 2020 — it covers the same Karakalpakstan localities, though O'zsuvta'minot's post about the current construction does not mention the World Bank project by name. The World Bank's Board of Executive Directors [approved](https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2020/03/12/uzbekistan-water-services-and-institutional-support-project) a $239 million loan from the International Development Association for it on March 12, 2020. In addition, the State Secretariat for Economic Affairs of Switzerland allocated a $7.8 million grant to strengthen planning and the regulatory framework of Uzbekistan's water supply and sewage utilities.

Besides Karakalpakstan, the loan covers the Syrdarya region and the city of Kattakurgan in the Samarkand region. According to World Bank estimates, more than 500,000 residents of the three regions will have access to improved water supply and sewage services once the project is completed.

Other project goals include reducing water and energy losses, creating an energy efficiency financing program for utility companies, and installing metering devices.
