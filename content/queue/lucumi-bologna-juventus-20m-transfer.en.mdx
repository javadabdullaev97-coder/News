Colombian defender Jhon Lucumi passed a medical at the JMedical center in Turin on August 16, 2026, completing his move from Italian club Bologna to Juventus, [Football Italia reports](https://football-italia.net/video-lucumi-arrives-for-juventus-transfer/).

The deal is worth €20 million in a base fee and could rise to €22 million with bonuses, [Football Italia writes](https://football-italia.net/juventus-agree-22m-deal-for-bologna-lucumi/). Lucumi will sign a contract with the Turin club running until June 30, 2030, [Juventus's official website confirmed](https://www.juventus.com/en/news/articles/jhon-lucumi-joins-juventus). The defender's salary will be about €2.5 million a year before bonuses, according to [Football Italia](https://football-italia.net/juventus-agree-22m-deal-for-bologna-lucumi/).

The 28-year-old defender spent four seasons at Bologna, which bought him from Belgium's Genk in 2022. He has been called up to Colombia's national team and played at the 2026 World Cup.

According to Football Italia, Lucumi turned down offers from several clubs, including England's Nottingham Forest and Sunderland, while waiting for a move to Juventus specifically.
