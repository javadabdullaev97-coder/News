Uzbekistan's National Agency for Perspective Projects (NAPP) [published](https://regulation.adliya.uz/project/11003/33917?contentLang=ru) a draft regulation on August 3 on the portal for discussing legal acts, setting fines for foreign legal entities that provide illegal crypto-asset circulation services to Uzbekistan residents.

## What NAPP is proposing

The document is titled "[Regulation on the procedure for imposing fines on foreign legal entities providing illegal crypto-asset circulation services to residents of the Republic of Uzbekistan](https://regulation.adliya.uz/project/11003/33917?contentLang=ru)" and is drafted as an annex to an order of the agency's director — meaning it is still a draft, not an operative rule. The text does not specify the signing date of the order itself.

The draft introduces four categories of violations and fines for foreign companies:

- operating in crypto-asset circulation without a license — up to 25,000 base calculation values (BCV);
- violating personal data protection requirements — up to 15,000 BCV;
- violating anti-money laundering and counter-terrorism financing legislation (AML/CFT) — up to 15,000 BCV;
- violating advertising requirements — up to 10,000 BCV.

Starting September 1, 2026, the base calculation value will [rise](https://www.lex.uz/ru/docs/8283680) to 440,000 soums under Presidential Decree No. UP-115. At that rate, the maximum fine for operating without a license (25,000 BCV) would reach 11 billion soums — about $917,000 at the Central Bank of the Republic of Uzbekistan (CBU) exchange rate on August 4.

## How the collected fines would be distributed

The draft stipulates that collected fines would be split evenly between Uzbekistan's state budget and NAPP's treasury personal accounts — 50% each.

## Why the regulator can fine foreign companies at all

Under the current [NAPP rules](https://napp.uz/ru/pages/service-providers), licensable activities in crypto-asset circulation — crypto exchange, crypto store, crypto depository, mining pool — can be carried out only by legal entities resident in Uzbekistan. Foreign companies are not issued such licenses at all, so any crypto services they provide to the country's residents are formally already a violation. The current licensing procedure in this area was registered by the agency on August 15, 2022.

## Status of the document

The draft is currently at the public discussion stage on the regulation.adliya.uz portal. The posted text does not specify the date the agency director's order takes effect or the procedure for its further approval. Until the order is signed, the proposed rules do not apply.
