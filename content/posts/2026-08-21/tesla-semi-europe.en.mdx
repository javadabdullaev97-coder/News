According to reports cited by several outlets, Tesla is preparing to launch its electric Semi truck in the European market; there has been no official confirmation as of publication. The company will present details at the IAA Transportation show in Hannover on September 15-20, 2026.

Tesla's plans are [described in a post](https://x.com/tesla_semi/status/2090333014576488877) on the official Tesla Semi account on X, cited by several industry outlets including [electrive.com](https://www.electrive.com/2026/08/20/tesla-semi-truck-heads-to-iaa-as-european-launch-takes-shape/). The post itself had not loaded for LEAP News editors as of this article's publication. IAA Transportation is Europe's largest commercial vehicle show; it [will run](https://www.iaa-transportation.com/en) in Hannover from September 15 to 20, 2026, with a press day set for September 14. The organizer's website does not confirm Tesla's participation as an exhibitor — only the company's post and trade press report it.

In the United States, Tesla already sells two versions of the Semi: the Standard Range, with a 523-kilometer range, and the Long Range, with 800 kilometers. Both are equipped with three electric motors delivering up to 800 kW combined and batteries of 548 or 822 kWh, [according to](https://www.electrive.com/2026/08/20/tesla-semi-truck-heads-to-iaa-as-european-launch-takes-shape/) electrive.com. The truck supports 1.2 MW charging and recovers most of its range in about half an hour.

Tesla has not yet disclosed the specifications or price of the European Semi — it will present them only at the September show.

## What's next

Tesla is expected to present the specifications and launch terms for the European Semi at IAA Transportation from September 15 to 20, 2026. We have sent a request to Tesla's press office; any response will be added to this article.
