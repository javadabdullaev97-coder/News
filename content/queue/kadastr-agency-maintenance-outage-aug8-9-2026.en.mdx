The Cadastre Agency under the Ministry of Economy and Finance of the Republic of Uzbekistan has warned of possible disruptions to cadastral e-services on the my.gov.uz portal from 12:00 on August 8 to 20:00 on August 9 due to scheduled maintenance, the official my.gov.uz Telegram channel [said](https://t.me/MyGovUz/3975).

## What Won't Work

Scheduled preventive maintenance will take place across all information systems of the Cadastre Agency, [the post said](https://t.me/MyGovUz/3975) on the my.gov.uz channel. During that period — nearly 32 hours straight — disruptions are possible when applying for cadastral e-services through the my.gov.uz portal. One example of such a service is [generating a cadastral passport for a residential property and registering rights to it](https://my.gov.uz/uz/service/939).

The agency did not specify the exact list of affected services or say whether its own online resources outside the my.gov.uz portal would also be unavailable. The announcement does not list alternative ways to obtain cadastral services during the maintenance window — the agency only apologized for the inconvenience. As of publication, there is no independent confirmation from the Cadastre Agency itself outside the my.gov.uz channel.

## Not the First Outage This Year

On July 28, the Cadastre Agency had already suspended part of its services: for five hours, from 15:00 to 20:00, only the database of the agency's Tashkent city department went offline due to scheduled maintenance, the same my.gov.uz channel [reported](https://t.me/mygovuz/2861) at the time. The August 8-9 outage covers all of the agency's information systems nationwide and will last considerably longer.

## What to Do Before the Maintenance

Applicants who need cadastral services through my.gov.uz — registering real estate objects, registering rights, obtaining cadastral passports — should complete their requests before 12:00 on August 8. The portal is expected to resume normal operation after 20:00 on August 9.
