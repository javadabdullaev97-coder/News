Uzbekistan received 21,042 foreign nationals arriving to study in January–June 2026 — 2.2 times more than in the first half of 2025, the [National Statistics Committee of the Republic of Uzbekistan](https://stat.uz/ru/press-tsentr/novosti-goskomstata/69689-zbekistonga-ish-ma-sadida-kelgan-khorizhliklar-soni-2-2-barobarga-oshdi-3) said.

## Where the Students Come From

According to [the committee's data](https://stat.uz/ru/press-tsentr/novosti-goskomstata/69689-zbekistonga-ish-ma-sadida-kelgan-khorizhliklar-soni-2-2-barobarga-oshdi-3), most foreign nationals who arrived to study in the first half of 2026 came from neighboring and regional countries:

- Turkmenistan — 9,207 people
- India — 3,723
- Tajikistan — 1,915
- China — 951
- Kyrgyzstan — 834
- Pakistan — 689
- Egypt — 603
- South Korea — 526
- Philippines — 367
- United States — 302
- other countries — 1,925

Turkmenistan and India together account for about 62% of the total flow — 12,930 of 21,042 people.

## A 2.2-Fold Increase From Last Year

The National Statistics Committee says the number of arrivals grew by 11,500 people compared with the same period of 2025 — the basis for the 2.2-fold increase. The release does not include a breakdown by university, academic program, or level of education (bachelor's, master's, language courses), nor does it include a separate comment from a committee representative.
