Butun jahon boks kengashi (WBC) prezidenti Mauricio Sulaiman 19-avgustda tashkilot Tyson Fury va Anthony Joshua jangini chempionlik unvoni uchun final saralov sifatida tasdiqlashini aytdi. Bu haqda [sports.ru xabar beradi](https://www.sports.ru/boxing/1117339067-sulejman-o-poedinke-fyuri-i-dzhoshua-wbc-odnoznachno-odobrit-etot-boj-.html).

«WBC bu jangni final saralov jangi sifatida albatta tasdiqlaydi. Tyson Fury — bo'lim reytingidagi birinchi da'vogar, WBC bu promouterlikning bir qismi bo'lganidan albatta faxrlanadi» (asl nusxada: «The WBC would absolutely approve that as a final eliminator... Tyson Fury is the No. 1 contender of the division»), — Sulaimanning so'zlarini [MiddleEasy keltirdi](https://middleeasy.com/boxing/anthony-joshua-tyson-fury-wbc-eliminator-agit-kabayel).

Tashkilot prezidenti Fury'ni «WBC tarixidagi eng buyuk elchilardan biri» deb atadi va Joshua'ning maqomini alohida ta'kidladi: «Anthony Joshua karerasini boshlaganida WBC reytingida birinchi o'rinda edi. Ehtimol, Anthony Joshua'ni WBCga olib kelish vaqti keldi» (asl nusxada: «Anthony Joshua, he was the first ranked by the WBC when he started boxing... Maybe this will be the time to bring Anthony Joshua to the WBC»), — [MiddleEasy xabar beradi](https://middleeasy.com/boxing/anthony-joshua-tyson-fury-wbc-eliminator-agit-kabayel).

Agar WBC jangni rasman saralov jangi sifatida tasdiqlasa, g'olib WBCning amaldagi og'ir vazn chempioni Agit Kabayel bilan unvon uchun uchrashadi, [MiddleEasy xabar beradi](https://middleeasy.com/boxing/anthony-joshua-tyson-fury-wbc-eliminator-agit-kabayel).

Sulaimanning pozitsiyasi jang qayerda va qachon o'tkazilishi haqidagi savolga javob bermaydi — jangning o'zi hali tomonlarning birortasi tomonidan rasman tasdiqlanmagan. LEAP News 20-avgustda [xabar bergan edi](https://leap.uz/uz/2026/08/20/fury-joshua-venue-dispute-escalation), Joshua'ning promouteri Eddie Hearn Zuffa Boxing rahbari Dana White'ga qarshi sud da'vosi ochishi mumkinligini aytgan. Sabab — jangni Nyu-Yorkka ko'chirish bo'yicha muzokaralar. Joshua va Hearn'ning o'zi Buyuk Britaniyadagi maydonchani afzal ko'radi, Medison-skver-garden bo'yicha muzokaralar esa davom etmoqda.
