President Shavkat Mirziyoyev on August 14 signed the constitutional law "On the Administration of the President of the Republic of Uzbekistan" — the status, tasks, structure and powers of the Administration are for the first time set out in a [standalone law of 34 articles](https://t.me/Department_uz/12984), rather than in internal acts.

## What the law changes

The document defines the Administration as a state body providing [organizational, legal and analytical support](https://t.me/Department_uz/12984) for the powers of the head of state. It is assigned the drafting of presidential decrees, resolutions and orders, organizing their implementation, coordinating the work of state bodies subordinate to the president, and organizing open dialogue with the public and entrepreneurs.

According to the [presidential portal](https://president.uz/uz/lists/view/9497), the law for the first time explicitly provides that instructions from the head of the Administration on matters within its competence are mandatory for execution by state bodies, enterprises, institutions, organizations and officials. The Administration also receives the right to initiate disciplinary action against officials, including dismissal, following inspections of the implementation of presidential decisions.

The law separately establishes guarantees for Administration employees — social, economic and legal protection while performing their official duties. According to the [presidential portal](https://president.uz/uz/lists/view/9497), the leadership and officials of the Administration are granted inviolability and immunity, and initiating a criminal case against management employees is subject to a special procedure.

## How the law was passed

According to reports, the Legislative Chamber of the Oliy Majlis adopted the bill in its first reading on August 4, in its second and third readings on August 6, and the Senate approved the law on August 7. According to the presidential press service, the experience of the United States, Finland, the Czech Republic, Slovakia, Romania, Latvia and Kazakhstan was studied while drafting the document.

Presidential press secretary Sherzod Asadov [said](https://t.me/Press_Secretary_Uz/7699) that the law closes an existing legal gap in regulating the Administration's activities and strengthens the legal foundations of the presidential institution in line with international practice.

## What's next

The law takes effect on the day of its official publication on the national legislation database lex.uz — as of this article's publication, the full text with the act's official details had not yet been posted on lex.uz.
